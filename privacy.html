What we collect, why we use it, and how to contact us.

Updated November 7, 2026

Systemic Error is an independent publication operated by Paulo Santos. You can read the website without creating an account. This policy covers the website, email publication, and the internal Editorial Studio Facebook integration.

Email subscriptions

When you subscribe, we ask for your email address and, if you choose to provide it, your name. Zoho Campaigns processes the signup, email confirmation, subscription preferences, delivery, and unsubscribes. Signup and delivery records can include confirmation and consent information, source, timestamps, technical request information, and delivery status. These records help administer the publication and prevent abuse. Zoho’s form script may use cookies or similar identifiers to operate the form and measure signup activity.

The existing mailing list was reconstructed from notification emails received when people subscribed to Systemic Error on Substack. Those addresses were carried over for the same publication. This website's signup form is a separate way for new readers to subscribe; it does not automatically enrol people who only visit the site.

Zoho reports newsletter deliveries, bounces, unsubscribes, opens, and link clicks. Opens and clicks are indicators of email activity, not proof that someone read an article. Your subscription details are used to deliver and manage Systemic Error emails, not sold or added to unrelated mailing lists.

Advertising

When advertising is enabled, Systemic Error uses Google AdSense. Google and other advertising vendors may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, and device identifiers to serve and measure ads. Advertising cookies can use visits to this and other websites to personalize ads, subject to your consent and applicable settings. See how Google uses information from partner sites and Google My Ad Center to learn more and manage personalized advertising.

Website analytics and technical information

The article website uses Google Analytics to measure visits, page views, traffic sources, device/browser information, approximate geography, and engagement. Google may process cookies and network or device information as part of that service. Google Signals and advertising-personalization signals are disabled in our website configuration. Analytics does not require you to enter a name or email address.

GitHub Pages hosts the public website, Cloudflare provides domain/network services, and Microsoft Azure hosts the service that renders newsletter content. These providers, Zoho, Google, and Meta may process technical information needed to operate their services under their own policies. The retired Azure subscriber database is not used for the current signup form; subscription records are managed in Zoho.

Editorial Studio's Facebook connection

Systemic Error Studio is an internal tool used by an authorized administrator to share article links to the Systemic Error Facebook Page. Readers do not need to sign in with Facebook. During authorization, Meta provides the approved identity and Page access. Studio uses the Page ID, Page name, and Page access token, and sends the administrator's chosen post text and article URL to Meta.

The connection requests permission to list managed Pages, read Page content and engagement, and create, edit, or delete Page posts. Access is restricted to the Systemic Error Page. The current publishing feature verifies that Page and creates article-link posts. It does not read private messages, collect a follower directory, or post to personal profiles.

The token is encrypted for the administrator's Windows account using Windows Data Protection. Local publishing receipts contain the article ID, URL, attempt time, status, and Facebook post ID when available, helping prevent duplicate sends. The token is not included in the public website or application source. Published Facebook posts and their article links are public.

Unsubscribe, access, correction, and deletion

Every newsletter includes an unsubscribe link. Use it to stop future publication emails or manage your topic preferences. Subscription records remain in Zoho while needed to provide the service; unsubscribed addresses or minimal suppression records may be retained to avoid accidentally sending again. Messages already in transit may still arrive.

For a copy, correction, or deletion of subscription information, email newsletter@systemicerror.media. Please identify the subscribed address so the request can be verified. We do not publish subscriber lists in the website repository.

To revoke Studio's Facebook access, open Facebook Settings → Business integrations → Systemic Error Studio and remove the connection. The Page administrator can remove the saved local token and publishing receipts and delete public posts through Facebook. Revoking access does not itself delete public posts or local files. For Facebook-integration data requests, email the address above or message the Systemic Error Facebook Page.

Provider policies and changes

See the privacy policies of Zoho, Google, GitHub, Cloudflare, Microsoft, and Meta for their handling of information. We update this page when the publication's practices change.
