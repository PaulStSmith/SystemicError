Trump: ‘Not thinking about Elon’ – but privately asked about his drug use

U.S. President Donald Trump speaks to members of the media aboard Air Force One, in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates, May 16, 2025. REUTERS/Brian Snyder

President Donald Trump downplayed his fiery feud with Elon Musk that exploded this week, telling reporters aboard Air Force One on Friday that he wasn’t giving the tech billionaire much thought – even as their public split made international headlines.

But behind closed doors, the president is actively asking aides about Musk’s recent behavior, including whether it may be tied to his alleged ketamine use, a source told CNN.

“Honestly, I’ve been so busy working on China, working on Russia, working on Iran, working on so many things, I’m not thinking about Elon,” Trump told reporters Friday as he flew to New Jersey. “I just wish him well.”

The new comments came days after Musk criticized what he called the “big, beautiful bill” Republicans on Capitol Hill have been vigorously pushing, which triggered a back-and-forth social media war and the official end of their MAGA alliance.

The Times last week cited multiple associates of Musk in an explosive report detailing the billionaire Tesla owner’s alleged drug use during his time on the campaign trail. Musk has publicly admitted to using legally prescribed ketamine in the past but denied the Times report.

Meanwhile, Trump said Friday he didn’t have “any plans” to speak to Musk, his former DOGE head.

Our Analysis:

Trump vs. Musk: A Feud Wrapped in Contradictions and Allegations

In the latest spectacle to grip the nation, the tiff between President Donald Trump and Elon Musk has escalated from a simmering disagreement to a full-blown public feud, replete with accusations, denials, and, of course, a generous dose of media frenzy. Despite President Trump's attempts to downplay the situation to reporters aboard Air Force One, claiming he's too engrossed in international affairs to spare a thought for Musk, reports from CNN suggest a starkly different behind-the-scenes reality. Let's dissect the layers of this unfolding drama, highlighting the hypocrisy, manipulation, and distractions it unveils.

The Public Dismissal vs. The Private Obsession

On the surface, Trump's public statements paint a picture of a leader too consumed with matters of state to concern himself with a tech billionaire's antics. "Honestly, I’ve been so busy working on China, working on Russia, working on Iran, working on so many things, I’m not thinking about Elon," Trump declared, projecting an image of unwavering dedication to his presidential duties. Yet, this carefully crafted image crumbles with revelations that, away from the public eye, Trump is actively probing his aides about Musk's behavior and its potential links to alleged ketamine use.

The question arises: Why the dual narrative? Trump's public indifference contrasts sharply with his private inquiries, suggesting a calculated attempt to maintain a presidential facade while indulging in personal vendettas behind closed doors. This duality not only undermines the sincerity of his public statements but also raises concerns about the prioritization of personal over national interests.

The Alleged Ketamine Use: A Red Herring?

The focus on Musk's alleged drug use, particularly ketamine, serves as a perfect red herring, diverting attention from substantive issues to salacious gossip. While Musk has admitted to legally prescribed ketamine use in the past, the fixation on this detail amidst a barrage of significant policy discussions and international concerns is telling. It's a classic case of character assassination, a strategy aimed not at engaging with Musk's criticisms on their merits but at undermining his credibility through personal attacks.

What's lost in this spectacle is a meaningful discourse on the "big, beautiful bill" that initially sparked Musk's ire and Trump's subsequent animosity. Instead of addressing the substance of Musk's criticisms, the dialogue has devolved into a mire of accusations and character smears, sidelining any potential for constructive debate.

The End of a MAGA Alliance

The public dissolution of the Trump-Musk alliance underscores a broader theme of conditional loyalty and the volatile nature of political alliances. The once-celebrated camaraderie, symbolized by Musk's former title as Trump's "DOGE head," has disintegrated under the weight of disagreement and personal affronts. This fallout is a stark reminder of the fragility of political relationships, which are often built on the shaky ground of mutual benefit rather than shared principles or enduring commitments.

Conclusion

The Trump-Musk feud is more than just a clash of titans; it's a microcosm of the larger issues plaguing our political discourse. It reveals how easily substance can be overshadowed by scandal, how quickly alliances can turn to animosities, and how personal vendettas can distract from pressing national concerns. As this saga unfolds, it serves as a cautionary tale of the dangers of allowing personal grievances and sensationalism to steer the ship of state. In the end, it's not just about Trump or Musk; it's about the health of our public discourse and the priorities of our leaders.
