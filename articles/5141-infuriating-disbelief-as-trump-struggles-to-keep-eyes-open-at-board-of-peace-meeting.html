'Infuriating': Disbelief as Trump struggles to keep eyes open at Board of Peace meeting

U.S President Donald Trump speaks at the inaugural Board of Peace meeting at the U.S. Institute of Peace in Washington, D.C., U.S., February 19, 2026. REUTERS/Kevin Lamarque TPX IMAGES OF THE DAY

Observers of the inaugural Board of Peace gathering in Washington were in disbelief as President Donald Trump appeared to nod off while hosting delegates from more than two dozen countries to discuss Gaza’s reconstruction and security.

Trump announced at the meeting that member states pledged roughly $7 billion toward rebuilding Gaza and thousands of personnel for an international stabilization force aimed at enforcing the ceasefire and helping disarm Hamas.

The three‑hour meeting included typical Trump rhetoric and more formal speeches from key figures such as Secretary of State Marco Rubio, Jared Kushner, Tony Blair, and UN Ambassador Mike Waltz.

But one moment in particular generated buzz on social media - and not in a good way.

As another foreign leader spoke, Trump struggled to keep his eyes open. When the official finished his thoughts, applause appeared to jolt Trump, who joined in clapping.

The moment earned immediate scorn from internet critics.

“Trump is not well,” chided the Republicans against Trump account on X.

Author and blogger Carl Potak wrote on X, “SLEEPY DONNY, again showing he CANNOT handle the rigors of the PRESIDENCY. He is THE GREATEST... at being a HYPOCRITE. He charged A BILLION DOLLARS for countries TO JOIN his silly PEACE COUNCIL and CAN’T EVEN STAY AWAKE. What a DISGRACE.

Comedian Justin Martindale joked on X, “’Bored of Peace.’”

Cameron Niven, who does communications for Rep. John Garamendi (D-CA), wrote on X, “For everyone criticizing AOC for barely stumbling on a few words on a complex policy, I raise you the actual president of the United States sleeping at this meeting. The fact that there’s not the same coverage is both depressing and infuriating.”

Richard Hall, a former Middle East correspondent, wrote on X, “People mistakenly believed the Board of Peace was Trump’s attempt to subvert the UN, but it’s clear from these long speeches and elderly leaders falling asleep that this is the Arab League 2.0.”

James Cibella, co-host of the Pool Scene Podcast, wrote on X, “If this was Biden, this would be the lead.”

He added that Trump has been accused of rape and sexual abuse, including in the Epstein files. Trump has denied wrongdoing.

Independent journalist Aaron Rupar, who shared the clip of Trump on X, remarked, “Trump continues to struggle to stay awake as this interminable ‘Board of Peace’ meeting drags on. We’re two and a half hours into this!

”

Our Analysis:

The Snooze at the Peace Table: Trump’s Nap Heard Around the World

In what was meant to be a landmark moment for international diplomacy and reconstruction efforts in Gaza, the inaugural Board of Peace gathering in Washington instead turned into a spectacle of somnolence, thanks to none other than President Donald Trump. As the world watched, Trump’s inability to stay awake during critical discussions not only marred the event’s significance but also provided a stark illustration of his often criticized approach to governance.

$7 Billion Pledge Overshadowed by a Nod

While Trump announced a substantial $7 billion pledge from member states for Gaza’s reconstruction and the formation of an international stabilization force, this significant commitment was quickly overshadowed by his apparent struggle to remain conscious. With key figures like Secretary of State Marco Rubio, Jared Kushner, Tony Blair, and UN Ambassador Mike Waltz delivering important speeches, one would assume the gravity of the situation might have kept Trump alert. Yet, as social media buzzed not with discussions on Gaza but with critiques of Trump’s nap time, the moment became emblematic of his presidency’s broader issues.

The Hypocrisy of Sleepiness

Critics were quick to jump on the irony of “Sleepy Donny” — a moniker Trump has gleefully applied to his adversaries — now being the one unable to stay awake during a crucial international meeting. Carl Potak’s scathing remark on X underscores a pervasive sentiment: Trump, who has positioned himself as a titan of industry and politics, showed a glaring incapacity to handle the very responsibilities he boasts of mastering. Charging a billion dollars for countries to join this so-called Peace Council, yet failing to engage actively, is not just hypocritical; it’s a disgrace of international proportions.

The Double Standards of Media Coverage

The incident also highlighted the glaring double standards in media and political circles. Cameron Niven’s commentary points out the disproportionate criticism aimed at figures like AOC for minor slip-ups, compared to the leniency Trump enjoys despite more significant blunders. If the tables were turned, and it was Biden caught snoozing, the narrative would likely be far different, as James Cibella pointedly noted. This discrepancy in treatment is not just depressing; it’s a vivid illustration of the biases that pervade our media landscape.

A Missed Opportunity for Meaningful Dialogue

Beyond the immediate ridicule and criticism lies a deeper issue: the missed opportunity for meaningful dialogue on Gaza’s future. The Board of Peace, potentially a significant platform for advancing peace and stability in the region, was reduced to a sideshow. Instead of dissecting the speeches and commitments made, the world focused on Trump’s lapse in decorum. Richard Hall’s comparison to the Arab League 2.0 hints at the underlying fear that this initiative, too, might fall into the trap of long speeches and little action, with elderly leaders at the helm.

Conclusion: A Wake-Up Call Needed

Trump’s snooze at the Board of Peace gathering is more than just an embarrassing moment; it’s a metaphor for his presidency’s broader failings. It underscores a lack of seriousness towards critical issues, a hypocrisy in self-presentation versus reality, and a media environment fraught with double standards. As the world grapples with complex crises, the need for vigilant, engaged leadership has never been more apparent. Perhaps this incident will serve as a wake-up call — both for Trump and for those who continue to underestimate the importance of consistent, coherent, and active engagement in global diplomacy.
