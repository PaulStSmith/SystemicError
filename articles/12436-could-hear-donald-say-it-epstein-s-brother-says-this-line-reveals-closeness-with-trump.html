'Could hear Donald say it': Epstein's brother says this line reveals closeness with Trump

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosJuly 24, 20252 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Donald Trump partying with NFL cheerleaders at his Mar-a-Lago club with Jeffrey Epstein (Image via NBC / Screengrab)

Convicted pedophile Jeffrey Epstein's brother Mark Epstein said Tuesday President Donald Trump and Jeffrey Epstein were "very good friends" and "very close."

Speaking to CNN's Erin Burnett on her show Tuesday evening, Mark Epstein said: "I know Donald was in Jeff's office a lot back in the 90s. It's documented that Donald flew on Jeff's plane a number of times. I don't know if they ever checked, but I know Jeff flew on Donald's plane."

He continued: "Also, I don't know if they've ever looked at his flight logs. And they were they were good friends."

Mark Epstein claimed that Trump was with him and Jeffrey Epstein on the latter's plane in 1999. "I was on a plane in 1999, flying up from Florida, and Donald was with us and we were all joking around. They were good friends," he said.

The disgraced financier's brother also recalled an alleged conversation between Trump and Jeffrey Epstein, where Epstein asked Trump, "how come you sleep with so many married women?"

"I was talking to Jeffrey, and he told me that he was talking to Donald, and he asked Donald, 'how come you sleep with so many married women?' and Donald's answer was, 'because it's so wrong now amongst guys.' It was a funny line," Mark Epstein told CNN.

He claimed his brother asked the same question in front of him. "And then when we were on the plane a week or two later, Jeffrey asked Donald the same question. I know he did that for my benefit, so that I could hear Donald say it."

He added: “That’s not the kind of question you ask a casual acquaintance… that’s a question you ask a good friend — someone you can get away with asking those kinds of questions. So that gives you an idea of how friendly they were.”

When the host asked him about the White House claim that Trump had never been to Epstein's office, Mark Epstein said: "That's just another blatant lie because he was there. The people that worked for Jeffrey in his office, you can find them."

"They can testify that they saw trump in Jeffrey's office on numerous occasions."
