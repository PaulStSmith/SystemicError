Dick Morris, the well-known political consultant who helped former President Bill Clinton win his 1996 re-election bid, came to President Donald Trump’s defense Saturday by launching into a racist rant directed at Muslims, who he said immigrate to the United States to “open up colonies” and take “over the country.”

“We see this situation in Minnesota with Somalians,” Morris said Saturday during an appearance on Newsmax, where he was introduced as an advisor to Trump.

“The effective massive Muslim immigration is really not designed to find homes for people who would like to live better, it’s designed to invade the United States, and they open up colonies like the 80,000 people in Minnesota. [They] elect their own congressmen, elect their own senators, elect their own governor.”

Somali migrants make up less than 1.4% of Minnesota’s population, but nevertheless, Morris insisted that their influence in the country was strong enough to nearly decide the position of the vice president in the 2024 election, given Minnesota Gov. Tim Walz’s spot near the top of the Democratic ticket last year.

Morris’ comments came in response to new immigration policies announced by Trump recently, which include ending deportation protections for Somalis in Minnesota, which itself was followed by Trump calling Somali immigrants “garbage.” They were also made in response to Trump’s remarks Friday that Europe was facing “civilizational erasure” if it didn’t change its immigration policies.

“His view is – and I think he’s right – that European policies [are dooming European competitiveness],” Morris said.

‘Gigantic time off, huge benefits for workers, no great emphasis on productivity, no great emphasis on working very hard; short work days, short work weeks. [It’s] dooming European competitiveness not only economically, but it’s making Europe weak in the face of massive Muslim immigration.”

Morris was championed for his remarks by Newsmax host Rita Cosby as an “interesting way to look at it,” with Morris going on to blame recent shootings in the United States on what he called the “phenomenon of colonization of America” by Muslims.

“You’re talking about a lawless element that is very self interested in basically taking over the country and using immigration as a way of doing it,” he said. “It’s a whole new chapter in the fight against illegal immigration.

Our Analysis:

Dick Morris’s Racist Rant: A Deep Dive into Prejudice and Misinformation

In a recent appearance on Newsmax, Dick Morris, a political consultant notorious for his role in Bill Clinton’s 1996 re-election and now an advisor to Donald Trump, unleashed a racist tirade against Muslims. Morris, devoid of any factual basis, claimed that Muslims immigrate to the United States with the sinister intent of “opening up colonies” and “taking over the country.” This alarming statement, grounded in prejudice rather than reality, represents a dangerous escalation in the rhetoric surrounding immigration and cultural integration.

The Misuse of Statistics and Fearmongering

Morris specifically targeted the Somali community in Minnesota, stating that their presence and political participation are indicative of a broader strategy to “invade” the United States. By focusing on the Somali migrants, who constitute less than 1.4% of Minnesota’s population, Morris not only grossly exaggerates their influence but also singles out a vulnerable group already facing significant challenges in their quest for a better life.

This kind of rhetoric is not just misleading; it’s dangerously inflammatory. Labeling the entire Muslim immigration as a plot to “invade” the U.S. is a classic example of fearmongering, designed to stir up xenophobia and racial hatred. It’s a tactic that has been used throughout history to justify discrimination and violence against minority groups.

Support from Newsmax - A Platform for Disinformation

It’s equally concerning that Morris’s comments were met with approval from Newsmax host Rita Cosby, who described his views as an “interesting way to look at it.” This endorsement from a media platform adds a layer of legitimacy to Morris’s baseless claims, potentially spreading this harmful narrative to a wider audience.

The responsibility of the media is to inform the public and foster a well-informed citizenry. When platforms like Newsmax choose instead to amplify racist and xenophobic rhetoric, they betray this responsibility, contributing to a more divided and hateful society.

Linking Immigration to Violence: A Dangerous Leap

Morris didn’t stop at vilifying immigrants for political participation; he also blamed recent shootings in the United States on what he called the “phenomenon of colonization of America” by Muslims. This leap to connecting immigration with criminality is not just baseless; it’s a harmful stereotype that has been debunked repeatedly by research showing immigrants are less likely to commit crimes than native-born citizens.

Trump’s Policy and Rhetoric: Fueling the Fire

Morris’s comments were a defense of Donald Trump’s recent immigration policies and remarks, which included ending deportation protections for Somalis in Minnesota and derogatorily referring to Somali immigrants as “garbage.” Trump’s framing of European immigration policies as leading to “civilizational erasure” due to “massive Muslim immigration” echoes Morris’s unfounded fears and perpetuates a narrative of cultural and racial purity that has no place in modern society.

Conclusion: The Real Threat to American Values

Dick Morris’s rant on Newsmax is a stark reminder of the dangerous rhetoric that is being normalized in some quarters of political discourse. It’s not just an attack on the Muslim community; it’s an assault on the very fabric of American values that celebrate diversity and the contributions of immigrants to society.

As a society, it’s imperative to challenge these narratives, emphasizing facts over fear and unity over division. The real threat to America is not the imagined “colonization” by immigrants but the acceptance of racism and xenophobia as legitimate forms of political discourse. It’s time to reject these narratives, holding those who spread them accountable, and reaffirming our commitment to a diverse and inclusive society.
