'Total loser!' Trump melts down in late night rage over ex-GOP star

Donald Trump on the campaign trail in Las Vegas, Nevada June 18, 2016. REUTERS/David Becker

Donald Trump lost his cool late Sunday after a former White House star criticized him live on Fox News.

Karl Rove, who was central in George W. Bush’s administration, called the president “Mr. Scrooge” during an interview on the network.

“We elected him for a variety of reasons that were important: inflation, the border, DEI, the military, respect for America,” Rove said.

“[But] things like tweeting out a picture of you as the pope is deeply offensive to a great many people.”

The criticism followed an AI-generated selfie Trump shared of himself wearing the papal robes after he joked that he’d make a great pope.

Rove also criticized other aspects of the president’s term in office so far, including the mistaken deportation of a man to El Salvador and his handling of the economy, specifically tariff. He focused on comments that American children may have to make do with two dolls rather than 30.

“It sounds like Mr. Scrooge,” Rove said on Sunday. “The ordinary American is like, ‘Wait a minute, I thought you were on my side. I didn’t think you were on the side of saying I need to do with less. You’ve got plenty of money, [but] I’ve got to make mine stretch as far as I can?’”

Trump wasn’t impressed.

“I don’t need to have Karl Rove of Fox News to tell me what to do,” he wrote on Truth Social.

“The guy’s a total Loser who’s been wrong about almost everything!”

Our Analysis:

The Meltdown of a Presidency: Trump vs. Rove

In a spectacle that would be hilarious if it weren't so deeply troubling for the state of American politics, Donald Trump has once again showcased his hallmark fragility and penchant for petulance. This time, his ire was directed at Karl Rove, a strategist synonymous with George W. Bush's tenure, who dared to criticize Trump on his erstwhile favorite network, Fox News.

Rove's offense? Calling Trump "Mr. Scrooge" and critiquing a series of presidential actions and attitudes that, frankly, are ripe for scrutiny. Let's dissect the absurdity and the gravity of this debacle, piece by piece.

Trump as the Pope: The Height of Hubris

First off, the AI-generated selfie of Trump in papal robes. The fact that this needs to be discussed at all is a testament to the surreal circus American politics has devolved into. Trump's joke about being a great pope is not just in poor taste; it's a glaring exhibition of the man's boundless ego and disrespect for religious figures and institutions. That Rove finds it "deeply offensive" is hardly surprising. What's surprising is that more people aren't outraged by such blatant blasphemy from someone who once held the highest office in the land.

Economic Mismanagement and the Scrooge Comparison

Rove's critique extends to Trump's handling of the economy, including misguided tariffs and a bizarre stance on consumerism—implying Americans should settle for less in a time when economic inequality is a pressing issue. This "Mr. Scrooge" analogy strikes a chord not because it's a witty jab, but because it uncannily captures the essence of Trump's presidency: a tenure marked by a glaring disconnect from the everyday struggles of ordinary Americans.

The Art of the Tantrum

Trump's response to Rove's criticisms is, as always, predictably juvenile. Resorting to his platform of choice, Truth Social, Trump lambasted Rove as a "total Loser" who's "been wrong about almost everything." This knee-jerk defensiveness and inability to engage with criticism constructively is emblematic of Trump's tenure and persona. It's a behavior pattern that speaks volumes about his suitability (or lack thereof) for any position of leadership.

The Bigger Picture

What this latest spat between Trump and Rove highlights is not just a rift within the conservative camp; it's a glaring spotlight on Trump's pathological inability to tolerate dissent or criticism. His penchant for personal attacks over substantive debate undermines the very fabric of democracy and respectful discourse.

The saddest part of this entire affair is its predictability. We've seen this behavior from Trump time and time again. Yet, it continues to shock and dismay. That a former president would engage in such pettiness and deflect instead of reflecting on valid critiques is not just disappointing; it's dangerous.

In conclusion, the Trump vs. Rove saga is more than a political squabble; it's a microcosm of the systemic issues plaguing American politics. It's about a former president who refuses to grow, learn, or accept responsibility. And in the process, he drags down the office he once held and the country he once led.
