'He fantasizes!' Trump accused of exposing 'breathtaking depravity' with made-up tale

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosDecember 17, 20254 min read

Donald Trump’s Truth Social post commenting on the death of film director Rob Reiner highlights the president’s “thirst for political violence,” an analyst claimed Tuesday.

Writing in The Bulwarks’ Morning Shots newsletter, Mona Charen suggested Trump had a lust for political violence which had exposed itself in his Truth Social post. The president had reacted to the death of Reiner and wife Michele Singer Reiner after the pair were found dead at their home on Sunday.

Trump’s first statement regarding Reiner’s death read, “A very sad thing happened last night in Hollywood. Rob Reiner, a tortured and struggling, but once very talented movie director and comedy star, has passed away, together with his wife, Michele, reportedly due to the anger he caused others through his massive, unyielding, and incurable affliction with a mind crippling disease known as TRUMP DERANGEMENT SYNDROME, sometimes referred to as TDS.”

“He was known to have driven people CRAZY by his raging obsession of President Donald J. Trump, with his obvious paranoia reaching new heights as the Trump Administration surpassed all goals and expectations of greatness, and with the Golden Age of America upon us, perhaps like never before. May Rob and Michele rest in peace!”

Charen wrote, “So powerful is his thirst for political violence that he not only fails to recoil from it when it breaks out; he fantasizes that it’s taking place even when it isn’t.”

“There’s zero reason to believe Reiner’s murder had anything to do with his political opposition to Trump. But the president says this is true for the same reason he says many false things are true: because he wants it to be true.”

The political commentator would go on to suggest Trump’s comment on the death of Reiner highlighted how he had a “breathtaking moral depravity” to him.

Charen added, “Has there ever been a simpler encapsulation of the Trump era? We suffer through daily reminders of the weakness of the president’s mind, but the United States has muddled along with other dim presidents. Where Trump occupies a category all his own is in his breathtaking moral depravity.”

Our Analysis:

The Macabre Dance of Trump’s Commentary

In a display that can only be characterized as grotesquely insensitive, Donald Trump took to Truth Social to comment on the death of film director Rob Reiner, manifesting what can only be described as a disturbing penchant for intertwining the somber with the politically vindictive. The analysis by Mona Charen in The Bulwark’s Morning Shots newsletter dares to peel back the layers of Trump’s rhetoric, exposing not just a thirst for political violence, but a staggering moral vacuity at the heart of his persona.

Trump’s statement on Reiner’s death, laden with references to a so-called “TRUMP DERANGEMENT SYNDROME,” not only trivializes a tragic event but seeks to politicize it in the most macabre manner imaginable. His words, “A very sad thing happened last night in Hollywood...” quickly devolve into a bizarre self-congratulation and mockery of a perceived political adversary even in death. This isn’t just bad taste; it’s a glaring exhibition of breathtaking moral depravity.

The Lust for Political Violence

Charen’s assertion that Trump harbors a lust for political violence is chillingly supported by his own words. By suggesting, without a shred of evidence, that Reiner’s death was due to his political stances, Trump not only indulges in baseless conspiracy theories but actively promotes a narrative where political dissent is met with fatal consequences. This isn’t merely irresponsible; it’s a calculated attempt to sow division and fan the flames of political animosity, potentially inciting violence.

A Breathtaking Moral Depravity

Charen hits the nail on the head when she speaks of Trump’s breathtaking moral depravity. It’s one thing for a political figure to engage in partisan bickering; it’s entirely another to exploit the deaths of individuals for political gain. Trump’s commentary doesn’t just cross the line of political decency; it obliterates it. His incapacity to offer sympathy without injecting a venomous political jab is not just a failure of empathy; it’s a stark revelation of his character, or the lack thereof.

The Trump Era’s Simple Encapsulation

The Trump era, as Charen succinctly puts it, is a daily reminder of not just the weakness of the president’s mind but of a deeper, more concerning moral void. To exploit death for political point-scoring is not just the mark of a dim politician; it’s indicative of a profound moral failure. Trump’s commentary on Reiner’s death isn’t just an isolated incident; it’s emblematic of his tenure’s moral and ethical bankruptcy.

In conclusion, Trump’s response to Rob Reiner’s death is more than just a political misstep; it’s a glaring testament to his character, showcasing a disregard for decency that transcends political rivalry. Charen’s analysis in The Bulwark doesn’t just critique a singular event; it lays bare the moral crisis at the heart of Trump’s political persona. It’s a stark reminder that in the theatre of Trump’s political machinations, no tragedy is too sacred to be spared from his macabre dance of politicization.
