Former Trump associate Lev Parnas, who says he used to be "deep inside the Trump machine" and now reports from the outside, recently flagged a news story that he was sure would make Trump "flip." Parnas has described himself as a former "trusted operative" of the president.

Now, Parnas is saying Trump's carefully calculated moves with Putin in Alaska were merely a distraction.

"But that’s the illusion. That’s the game. While they chatter about body language and handshakes, the real story is buried beneath the surface — exactly where Putin wanted it," Parnas wrote in a piece for Substack. "And now, even before the dust settles in Alaska, I hear from my sources that Trump is summoning Zelensky to the White House on Monday. The media will sell it as progress, as hope. But I’m telling you, it’s a trap."

He continued to outline what he sees as Trump's "plan" for the leader of Ukraine.

"And now, with Zelensky summoned to Washington, the danger is clear. They will try to corner him into a deal he cannot accept — a land swap, a false 'peace' carved out of Ukraine’s sovereignty. And when he refuses, they will paint him as the obstacle, the problem, the reason peace failed."

"That’s the plan. That’s what Alaska was really about," according to Parnas.

He added:

"I’m risking everything to bring you this truth. My sources are risking everything too. Every day, Trump, Putin, and their enablers are tightening their grip, cracking down on leakers, truth-tellers, and independent voices like ours. They want silence. They want obedience. They want you in the dark while they divide nations like real estate and rewrite history as if the dead, the stolen, and the broken never mattered."

Our Analysis:

Analyzing the "Trap": Trump, Zelensky, and the Art of Distraction

In the labyrinth of modern political maneuvering, few tales are as laden with intrigue and potential deceit as the one Lev Parnas, a self-professed former insider of the Trump administration, brings to light. Parnas, once entangled within what he terms the "Trump machine," emerges as a whistleblower of sorts, casting a stark light on a purported plot involving Donald Trump and Volodymyr Zelensky, the President of Ukraine. This narrative isn't just a mere sideshow; it's a masterclass in political distraction and manipulation, if Parnas's allegations hold water.

The Alleged Trap

According to Parnas, Trump's recent rendezvous with Vladimir Putin in Alaska wasn't about diplomatic niceties or geopolitical posturing. No, it was a smokescreen, a diversionary tactic aimed at misdirecting the global audience's attention from the real play: setting a trap for Zelensky. The claim that Trump is engineering a scenario to force Zelensky into an untenable position over a "land swap" or a so-called peace deal that would compromise Ukraine's sovereignty is a grave assertion. Such a maneuver would not only undermine Ukraine's autonomy but would also place Zelensky in a no-win situation: accept a deal that erodes his country's integrity or be painted as the antagonist blocking the path to peace.

The Illusion of Diplomacy

The narrative Parnas paints is one of illusion and misdirection. The public's gaze is directed towards the superficial—handshakes and body language between Trump and Putin—while the substantive issues are obscured, buried "beneath the surface" as Parnas puts it. This tactic of diversion is a common one, yet its repeated success underscores a worrying trend of public and media susceptibility to spectacle over substance.

The Risk of Speaking Out

Parnas doesn't mince words about the personal risk involved in disclosing these alleged plans. The environment he describes is one of increasing hostility towards dissenters, with Trump, Putin, and their associates purportedly "tightening their grip" on the narrative by stifling whistleblowers and independent voices. This depiction of a political landscape hostile to transparency and truth is alarming, to say the least. It paints a picture of leaders not as public servants but as autocrats, intent on silencing opposition and controlling the narrative at all costs.

Conclusion: A Call for Vigilance

Parnas's revelations, while yet to be corroborated, serve as a stark reminder of the intricate games of power and manipulation that play out on the world stage. They underscore the importance of critical engagement with political narratives and a vigilant, questioning media. The potential "trap" for Zelensky, as described by Parnas, is not just a diplomatic skirmish—it's indicative of a broader strategy of manipulation and control. As observers, participants, and victims of global politics, the onus is on us to look beyond the illusion, to question the presented narratives, and to demand accountability from those in power.

In the shadowy corridors of power, where the stakes are nations' sovereignty and peoples' lives, the truth is often a casualty. Parnas's account, steeped in the dramatic flair of insider knowledge and dire warnings, may read like a political thriller. Yet, the underlying message is clear: in the age of information warfare, skepticism is a crucial tool, and transparency, a rare but indispensable ally.
