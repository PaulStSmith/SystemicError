Donald Trump on Saturday took to Truth Social, his own social media site, to rage against Seth Meyers, and Commissioner of Federal Communications Commission Brendan Carr raised eyebrows by amplifying the attack.

“NBC’s Seth Meyers is suffering from an incurable case of Trump Derangement Syndrome (TDS),” Trump wrote over the weekend. “He was viewed last night in an uncontrollable rage, likely due to the fact that his ‘show’ is a Ratings DISASTER.”

Our Analysis:

A Scandalous Amplification: FCC Chairman’s Dubious Endorsement of Trump’s Tirade

In a move that blurs the already murky lines between political partisanship and official duty, FCC Chairman Brendan Carr has taken a step that can only be described as both astonishing and deeply troubling. By sharing a post from Donald Trump—without comment, but loaded with implications—Carr has effectively endorsed a call to action against NBC’s Seth Meyers, stemming from nothing more than personal animosity and a perceived slight.

Trump’s tirade on his platform, Truth Social, is nothing new. His attack mode against anyone who dares to criticize, mock, or disagree with him has been a hallmark of his persona. This time, however, the former president labeled Meyers as suffering from an “incurable case of Trump Derangement Syndrome (TDS),” attributing an “uncontrollable rage” to the comedian’s supposedly disastrous ratings. But let’s not get sidetracked by Trump’s predictable behavior; the real story here is the complicity of Brendan Carr.

The Inexplicable Act of an FCC Chairman

It’s bewildering to see the Chairman of the Federal Communications Commission—a regulatory body tasked with overseeing interstate and international communications—repost such a blatantly partisan attack. The FCC’s mandate includes promoting competition, innovation, and investment in broadband services. It’s difficult to see how Carr’s actions align with these objectives. By amplifying Trump’s call, Carr has not only ventured into the murky waters of censorship but has also raised serious questions about his impartiality and the integrity of the FCC under his chairmanship.

The Dangerous Implications

Carr’s decision to share Trump’s post, especially without any form of commentary, sends a chilling message about the potential use of regulatory power to silence political opponents or critics. This act is especially alarming considering the FCC’s significant influence over the broadcasting landscape. It hints at a willingness to exploit this power for partisan purposes, undermining the very principles of free speech and press that the commission is supposed to protect.

The Backlash

The backlash from various quarters has been swift and severe. From attorneys and public policy professionals like Gregg Nunziata questioning Carr’s appropriateness for his role, to journalists like NPR’s David Folkenflik highlighting the troubling implications for broadcast regulation, the consensus is clear: Carr’s actions are indefensible.

Norman Ornstein’s condemnation of Carr as an “utter disgrace” and “unfit for any office” echoes a broader concern about the erosion of democratic norms and the politicization of federal agencies. Meanwhile, the reactions from entities like Protect Kamala Harris and OSINTdefender underscore the gravity of the situation, with the latter explicitly stating Carr’s actions as an endorsement of Trump’s demand.

A Call to Action

This is not merely a faux pas that can be brushed aside or excused as a lapse in judgment. This is a deliberate action by a high-ranking official, one that has far-reaching implications for free expression and the impartiality of media regulation. It’s a stark reminder of the need for vigilance and accountability, especially when those in power show a readiness to cross the lines of propriety and fairness.

The silence from Brendan Carr following the backlash is deafening. It is incumbent upon him to clarify his stance and for the FCC to reaffirm its commitment to nonpartisanship and the upholding of democratic values. Anything short of this would only serve to deepen the mistrust and cynicism towards a body that should stand as a bulwark against censorship and the suppression of dissenting voices.

In a time when the independence of regulatory bodies is more critical than ever, the actions of Brendan Carr serve as a stark warning of how quickly and easily these institutions can be compromised. It’s a systemic error that demands immediate correction.
