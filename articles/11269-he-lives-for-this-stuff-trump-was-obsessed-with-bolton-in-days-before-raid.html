'He lives for this stuff': Trump was obsessed with Bolton in days before raid

Donald Trump (Photo via Reuters)

Despite disavowals from Donald Trump that he was not aware of a planned raid on the home of John Bolton, White House insiders claim he had been obsessed with his former national security adviser for the past few weeks.

Questioned about the raid afterward, the president told reporters he was out of the loop and knew nothing about it, however, in interviews with the Washington Post, insiders said he not only was focusing on Bolton but that he dropped hints in the days before the raid.

As the Post’s Natalie Allison and Michael Birnbaum wrote, “People close to Trump have privately noted, however, that the president was bothered by Bolton’s recent deprecation of his attempts at peacemaking. And both Trump and some of his top appointees have talked openly about using the criminal justice system against his perceived enemies.”

The precipitating event appears to be Bolton’s very public rebuke of the Putin summit with the president watching TV news to see how his negotiations were being portrayed. He was particularly stung by Bolton’s criticism in multiple TV appearances, including his comment that the Russian strongman “...clearly won.”

The Post report notes, “Until Aug. 13, Bolton had not been a target of Trump’s social media attacks for seven months — since before Trump’s inauguration in January. That changed just over a week ago, when Trump became angry at a quote from Bolton and fired off a post on Truth Social expressing his frustration.”

According to the Post, “The president probably watched some of those appearances,” with one adviser admitting, “He lives for this stuff, so he’s watching it all day long.”

“The Justice Department’s decision to launch a probe into Bolton comes as top officials there have faced pressure from Trump’s base to take more aggressive action against so-called deep-state corruption,” the Post is reporting.

Our Analysis:

The Unveiling of a Thinly-Veiled Vendetta

The Washington Post's recent revelations paint a picture of a scenario that could be torn right out of a political thriller, yet it's not fiction—it's the unsettling reality of today's White House under Donald Trump. Despite vehement denials from Trump that he had no knowledge of a planned raid on John Bolton's home, credible insiders have spilled the beans, showing us yet again that the truth is often stranger, and more disturbing, than fiction.

Trump's Obsession: A Prelude to Retaliation?

It's hardly surprising to hear that Trump had been "obsessed" with his former national security adviser, John Bolton, in the weeks leading up to the raid. This obsession, as reported, wasn't just a passing whim. It's described with a fervor that suggests a premeditation, a calculated focus on Bolton, who had become a thorn in Trump's side, especially after Bolton's public criticism of Trump's diplomacy, or lack thereof, at the Putin summit. This criticism, where Bolton stated that Putin "...clearly won," seems to have struck a nerve with Trump, igniting his ire.

The Thin Veil of Denial

Trump's insistence that he was "out of the loop" about the raid crumbles under the weight of the evidence presented. His pattern of behavior, a mix of public denials and behind-the-scenes machinations, is a song and dance we've become all too familiar with. The claims of ignorance are laughable—if not downright insulting—given the detailed accounts of his focus on Bolton and his not-so-subtle hints in the days leading up to the raid.

Weaponizing the Justice System

Perhaps one of the most chilling aspects of this saga is the revelation that Trump, along with his top appointees, have discussed using the criminal justice system against perceived enemies. This isn't just an abuse of power; it's a brazen attempt to undermine the very foundations of justice and democracy. The Justice Department's probe into Bolton, under the pressure from Trump's base for more aggressive action against "so-called deep-state corruption," highlights a dangerous politicization of what should be impartial and fair.

The Unmistakable Pattern of Retaliation

The timeline of events leading to the raid on Bolton's home cannot be ignored. The sudden shift in Trump's social media attacks on Bolton, resuming after a seven-month hiatus, is a clear indicator of Trump's pettiness and vindictiveness. It's a pattern we've seen repeated: Trump becomes "angry" at a statement or action, and suddenly, the machinery of his administration swings into action to target the source of his ire.

Conclusion

The unfolding of events as reported by the Washington Post is not just a story about Donald Trump's vendetta against John Bolton. It's a stark reminder of the dangerous path the current administration is willing to tread, weaponizing the justice system to serve personal grudges and political ambitions. It's a scenario where the principles of justice, fairness, and democracy are not just at risk—they're under direct attack. And it's a situation that we, as a nation, must confront with the seriousness it deserves.
