The Silence That Screamed: When Immigration Policy Betrayed Humanity

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosAugust 5, 20253 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

A System Built to DehumanizeNot About Safety. Not About Law. About Power.The Myth of “Cutting Waste”ICE Must Be AbolishedFinal Word: Humanity Cannot Be Optional

There are moments in political history when a single question exposes more than hours of grandstanding ever could. One such moment arrived when NBC’s Peter Alexander asked a simple, piercing question at a White House press briefing: “So… a six-year-old girl fleeing cartel violence, crossing the border with her grandmother… is a criminal?”

The question wasn’t rhetorical. It was surgical.

Karoline Leavitt, the GOP mouthpiece on duty, froze. Her armor of rehearsed talking points, her media-trained smirk, her combative stance—all shattered in real time. Her only defense? “I’m not here to debate hypotheticals.”

But it wasn’t hypothetical. It was happening. It is still happening.

Every. Damn. Day.

Children ripped from their families. Students abducted after court-approved hearings. Elderly women kidnapped from courthouses. This isn’t law enforcement. It’s institutionalized cruelty under the color of law. It’s ICE—a rogue agency drunk on unchecked power and politically fueled vindictiveness.

A System Built to Dehumanize

ICE doesn’t just deport. It terrorizes. It doesn’t distinguish between drug traffickers and grandmothers. Between asylum seekers and gang members. It doesn’t prioritize. It pulverizes.

The agency's directive is clear: arrest everyone. Violent or not. Child or not. Cancer patient or not. If you're undocumented, you are a target.

That directive leaves no room for nuance, no consideration for context, and no mercy for the human condition.

And it leads to scenes like those witnessed by Stephanie Spellers and dozens of others at 26 Federal Plaza. A Korean-American student, Yeonsoo Go, 20 years old, daughter of a New York priest, was detained by ICE after appearing for a routine student visa hearing. She complied with every legal expectation and was still thrown into a "mass warehousing room" — a sanitized term for a dehumanizing hellscape.

Ketty Elizabeth De Los Santos, a 59-year-old woman who fled Peru, had just been granted work authorization by a judge. That didn’t stop ICE. They waited until the hearing ended, then abducted her too. Now no one knows where she is.

What do you call a government agency that overrides judicial rulings with brute force?

You call it what it is: authoritarian.

Not About Safety. Not About Law. About Power.

Mass deportation is not about making America safer. It's about making America whiter, more fearful, and more submissive. It's about feeding the right-wing media machine red meat: cruelty packaged as policy.

The administration line — “They're all criminals” — is the laziest kind of propaganda. It ignores the foundational premise of justice: the ability to distinguish.

A six-year-old girl fleeing narco-violence is not a threat. A mother working three jobs to support her kids is not a danger. A theology student trying to extend her visa is not an enemy of the state.

But when your policies lack a soul, you start calling everyone an enemy. And when your spokesperson stumbles on camera, it’s not just a PR fail. It’s a mask slipping.

Karoline Leavitt wasn’t defeated in a debate. She was exposed. The silence between her talking points roared with the sound of moral emptiness.

The Myth of “Cutting Waste”

The same ideology that fuels ICE's dragnet logic also freezes aid programs and slashes public services under the pretense of “cutting waste.” But it isn't waste that's being cut. It's lives. It's futures. It's dignity.

Removing a child from a classroom, a parent from a hospital, or a family from a heating assistance program doesn’t enhance safety. It ensures suffering. It enshrines cruelty as governance.

That’s not fiscal responsibility. That’s state-sponsored sadism.

ICE Must Be Abolished

ICE isn’t reformable. You cannot train away its core mission. You cannot tame an agency designed to treat human migration as a threat to be neutralized.

ICE is not broken. It’s functioning exactly as intended.

And that’s the problem.

It must be dismantled. Its funding cut. Its practices exposed. Its survivors honored. Its architects held accountable.

The United States doesn’t need a deportation squad. It needs a humane immigration system built on due process, proportionality, and the recognition of shared humanity.

Until then, every ICE raid is a moral failure. Every detention is an indictment. Every silenced question is a wound we keep ignoring.

Final Word: Humanity Cannot Be Optional

Peter Alexander didn't “own” Karoline Leavitt. He illuminated the rot. He punctured the illusion that this policy is about keeping anyone safe.

In that moment, the political theater dissolved, and what remained was the raw, searing truth:

A six-year-old isn’t a criminal. But treating her like one is.

And we’re all complicit if we let that truth go unanswered.

Abolish ICE. Before it buries more lives in silence.
