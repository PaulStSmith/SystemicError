Roger Stone, a longtime ally to President Donald Trump, officially challenged former White House chief strategist Steve Bannon to a bare-knuckle boxing match late Saturday, calling the MAGA influencer a “gutless piece of human garbage” and highlighted his past ties to Jeffrey Epstein.

“The folks at Bare Knuckle Fighting Championship have suggested a match between me and Epstein Associate Steve Bannon tentatively at the Hard Rock Casino in Hollywood, Florida. I am in and waiting on Stinky Steve,” Stone wrote in a social media post on X.

“I have been training for months now three days a week on the heavy bag. Unless Steve Bannon is a gutless piece of human garbage he’ll accept the challenge. The first round won’t last two minutes.”

Both Stone and Bannon were key fixtures in Trump’s 2016 presidential campaign – Stone as a political advisor and Bannon as campaign CEO – and both ended up serving prison sentences related to their connections with Trump. Both were also pardoned by Trump, and have gone on to have a hostile relationship with one another.

That hostility appeared to come to a head on Saturday with Stone’s challenge, where he pledged to donate his winnings from the fight to the White Coat Waste Project, a government watchdog group that works to expose taxpayer-funded animal abuse. In the event that Bannon won the fight, Stone already had a recommendation for where he should direct his winnings.

“In the unlikely event that Steve wins, he can pay back the Epstein estate for the millions they paid him to rehabilitate his Pedo buddy’s image,” Stone wrote.

Bannon had long maintained a relationship with Epstein, more details of which were unveiled this past week as part of the 20,000 pages of documents that were released from Epstein’s estate. Messages from the documents – mostly emails – showed that Bannon had advised Epstein on how to rehabilitate his reputation in the wake of his conviction over child prostitution.

The recent release of Epstein files has alsorenewed interestin a lengthy interview Bannon had shot and teased of Epstein in 2019 prior to his death in prison, an interview that to date has yet to be released. A photo of Bannon was also prominently displayed in Epstein’s New York home,revealed for the first timein August by The New York Times

Our Analysis:

The Absurdity of Political Theater: Stone vs. Bannon

In the latest episode of the political circus that refuses to leave town, Roger Stone, a figure whose notoriety is matched only by his loyalty to former President Donald Trump, has thrown down the gauntlet in a manner so ridiculous, it would be comedic if not for the gravity of the individuals involved. Stone has officially challenged Steve Bannon, another alumnus of the Trump administration with a similarly controversial reputation, to a bare-knuckle boxing match. Yes, you read that right. Amidst a world plagued by climate change, economic disparities, and global conflicts, these two political operatives have decided that the best use of their time and platform is to engage in a fistfight.

Let’s dissect this farce piece by piece:

The Challenge

Stone, in a move that reeks of desperation for relevancy, took to social media to call Bannon a “gutless piece of human garbage” while simultaneously highlighting Bannon’s past ties to Jeffrey Epstein, a convicted sex offender. The spectacle is proposed to take place at the Hard Rock Casino in Hollywood, Florida, under the umbrella of the Bare Knuckle Fighting Championship. Stone’s choice of venue and platform for this challenge is as tactless as it is tragically fitting, marrying the world of political scandal with the unabashed brutality of bare-knuckle boxing.

The Background

Both Stone and Bannon were instrumental in Trump’s 2016 campaign, with Stone serving as a political advisor and Bannon as the campaign CEO. Their paths post-campaign have been marred by legal troubles, with both serving prison sentences related to their activities before receiving presidential pardons. The fact that these individuals, who once held significant political power, are now resorting to playground tactics is a testament to the surreal post-presidency life of Trump’s inner circle.

The Cause?

Stone asserts that he will donate his winnings to the White Coat Waste Project, a noble cause aimed at exposing taxpayer-funded animal abuse. However, the sincerity of this gesture is undercut by the spectacle of the proposed event. It’s hard to take seriously a pledge to fight animal abuse when it’s tied to a proposal for a bare-knuckle brawl as a means of settling personal vendettas.

The Bannon-Epstein Connection

Stone’s challenge also serves as a vehicle to shine a light on Bannon’s relationship with Jeffrey Epstein, a relationship that has come under renewed scrutiny with the release of 20,000 pages of documents from Epstein’s estate. These documents reveal Bannon’s advice to Epstein on rehabilitating his image post-conviction, a fact that should not be overshadowed by the absurdity of a boxing match challenge.

Conclusion

The proposed Stone vs. Bannon match is emblematic of the current state of political discourse: a spectacle devoid of substance, where personal vendettas overshadow critical issues. While it’s easy to dismiss this challenge as a mere sideshow, it’s essential to recognize the implications of giving such theatrics a platform. This event, should it occur, is not a testament to the strength or resolve of either individual but rather a sad commentary on the depths to which political discourse has sunk.

In a world where there are genuine battles to be fought for justice, equality, and the preservation of our planet, the Stone vs. Bannon match is a stark reminder of the need to refocus our attention on what truly matters. Let’s not get distracted by the spectacle. Let’s demand more from those who seek to lead or influence public opinion. The fight against systemic issues is far more deserving of our time and energy than any bare-knuckle brawl could ever be.
