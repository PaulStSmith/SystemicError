Donald Trump posted a bizarre video of himself playing Journey’s “Don’t Stop Believing” on a stage before devoted fans.

The clip, which was produced by artificial intelligence, shows the president sitting on a stage in front of a piano festooned with an American flag.

In front of the stage is an adoring patriotic crowd.

Trump then changes from a suit to a red, white and blue outfit and is seen rocking a guitar riff, before moving to a drum kit.

The famous song was played for Trump at Mar-a-Lago by the band's keyboard player in 2022, which resulted in him getting a cease-and-desist order from a former bandmate who called the performance a “harmful use of the brand.”

Our Analysis:

The Art of Distraction: Trump's Latest Foray into AI-Generated Fantasy

In a move that can only be described as bizarre, Donald Trump has once again managed to divert the public's attention from pressing issues with a video that toes the line between fantasy and farce. This latest spectacle involves a piece of artificial intelligence-generated content that depicts him in a scenario that feels ripped straight from a patriot's fever dream.

Firstly, let's dissect the setting: Trump is shown seated on a stage, an American flag draped over a piano, a gesture that seems to scream patriotism louder than fireworks on the Fourth of July. But the spectacle doesn't stop there. Before an "adoring patriotic crowd," Trump undergoes a miraculous transformation, shedding his suit for a red, white, and blue outfit, and miraculously mastering not just the guitar but the drum kit as well. The choice of Journey’s “Don’t Stop Believing” as the soundtrack to this fantastical journey is, in itself, a masterstroke of irony—perhaps a subconscious plea to his base to cling to the remnants of his faltering political relevance.

Secondly, the legal entanglements arising from his unsanctioned use of the song are glossed over with the casual mention of a cease-and-desist order from a former bandmate, who condemned the performance as a “harmful use of the brand.” This blip on the legal radar serves as a stark reminder of Trump's recurrent disregard for intellectual property laws, a pattern all too familiar to those who have followed his career with any degree of scrutiny.

The implications of this AI-generated stunt are twofold. On one hand, it speaks to the alarming potential for artificial intelligence to be misused by individuals in positions of power to create misleading or completely fabricated content. On the other, it underscores Trump's relentless pursuit of the spotlight, employing any means necessary to stay relevant in a political landscape from which he is increasingly alienated.

In conclusion, this latest episode in the Trump saga is more than just a quirky anecdote for the news cycle. It's a glaring testament to the former president's propensity for spectacle over substance, his readiness to lean into the absurd to maintain his hold on the public's imagination. As we stand at the crossroads of reality and AI-generated fiction, one can't help but wonder: in Trump's America, is seeing still believing? Or have we entered a new era where belief is all about what we want to see, facts be damned?
