Whack-a-Mole Governance: Trump’s Tariff Tactics and the Illusion of Legality

A Pattern of Defiance

In a typical display of defiance, Donald Trump has responded to a Supreme Court decision that struck down his tariffs by introducing new import taxes under different legal pretenses. This maneuver is a classic example of how Trump manipulates legal systems to serve his agenda, regardless of judicial rulings. Here, Trump’s real power lies not in his adherence to the law but in his relentless pursuit to bend it to his will. Rachel Cohen, a featured attorney and activist on the “Legal AF” podcast, has rightly pointed out this recurring strategy of legal evasion, likening it to a game where the rules are perpetually rewritten to favor one player: Trump himself.

Misdirection and Consumer Impact

The real losers in this game are U.S. consumers. The temporary import taxes, like the tariffs they replace, are likely to increase the cost of goods, burdening everyday Americans. Cohen’s insight reveals a tragic redirection of funds—money collected by the government that should ideally benefit public welfare is instead funneled to corporations. This maneuver is not just a legal shuffle; it’s an economic strategy that prioritizes corporate interests over the needs of American citizens.

Trump 2.0: Contempt for the Public

Trump’s current approach to governance, as Cohen notes, shows a clear contempt for the welfare of ordinary Americans. Unlike his first term, which saw at least some attempts to distribute benefits like stimulus checks, his current tactics include no such measures to alleviate economic pressures on the public. This shift suggests a strategic choice to ignore the economic wellbeing of voters in favor of opaque financial maneuvers that benefit a select few.

Electoral Implications

This pattern of behavior is particularly concerning in an election context. Cohen’s observations suggest a strategy where Trump has no interest in policies that could genuinely win over the American public. Instead, there’s a focus on obscuring real economic conditions and pushing narratives that ignore the detrimental impacts of his policies on everyday people. Such tactics not only mislead the public but also erode trust in the economic governance of the country.

Systemic Insight: Governing as a Game of Power

Trump’s repeated circumvention of legal setbacks to impose economically damaging policies under new guises is emblematic of a broader political pathology: the treatment of governance as a game of power rather than a responsibility to the public. This approach undermines democratic norms and legal standards, setting a dangerous precedent for executive behavior. It’s critical to recognize and challenge these tactics, as they reveal a governance style that prioritizes personal and political gain over public good and legal integrity.
