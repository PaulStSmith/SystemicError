“...AND SO IT BEGINS!” Trump wrote on his social media platform Truth Social shortly before midnight Tuesday.

Tuesday’s elections – seen as the first bellwether for the 2026 midterm elections – saw Democratic candidates secure two governorships in Virginia and New Jersey, and secure a win for the progressive wing of the party with the victory of Zohran Mamdani in the New York City Mayoral race.

Democrats also secured a win in California with the passage of Proposition 50, a ballot measure that will see California redraw its congressional districts to combat the redistricting in Texas pushed by Trump to bolster Republican’s majority in the House.

Democrats’ victories also come in the wake of the Trump administration having deployed poll monitors from the Justice Department to both California and New Jersey, a move that critics have decried as blatant “voter intimidation.”

The victories also come despite Trump having issued a slew of endorsements over the weekend just ahead of the election, though he notably did not endorse the Republican gubernatorial candidate in Virginia, Winsome Earle-Sears, with one GOP operative offering the New York Post a possible explanation for the non-endorsement: “Trump doesn’t endorse losers,” they told the outlet.

Our Analysis:

A Closer Look at the Democratic Tsunami and Trump’s Cryptic Foreboding

In an era where political subtlety is as rare as a bipartisan bill passing Congress, President Donald Trump’s late-night proclamation, “...AND SO IT BEGINS!,” on Truth Social, serves as a chilling prelude to what one can only assume is a series of ominous political maneuvers yet to unfold. This cryptic message followed a series of Democratic triumphs that not only marked a significant shift in the political landscape but also a clear repudiation of Trump’s influence on the electorate.

The Democratic Sweep: A Clear Mandate

The recent elections have been nothing short of a referendum against the Trump-era policies and tactics, with Democratic candidates securing pivotal victories in Virginia and New Jersey governorships. Furthermore, the election of Zohran Mamdani as the New York City Mayor underscores a pronounced tilt towards the progressive agenda, signaling a broader appetite for substantial political reform amongst voters.

Perhaps more telling is the passage of Proposition 50 in California, a direct countermeasure to Trump’s redistricting efforts in Texas aimed at cementing a Republican majority in the House. This isn’t just a victory; it’s a battle cry against gerrymandering practices that have long skewed political representation in favor of those in power, particularly the Republicans under Trump’s command.

The Specter of Voter Intimidation

Amidst these electoral battles, the Trump administration’s decision to dispatch Justice Department poll monitors to California and New Jersey reeks of desperation and a blatant attempt at voter intimidation. This maneuver, widely criticized, highlights a dangerous trend towards undermining the electoral process, a cornerstone of democratic governance.

Trump’s Endorsement Games

Trump’s selective endorsements, or lack thereof, especially his notable abstention from endorsing the GOP gubernatorial candidate in Virginia, Winsome Earle-Sears, speaks volumes. The justification, as per a GOP operative’s words to the New York Post, that “Trump doesn’t endorse losers,” is a stark admission of the transactional nature of Trump’s political support, seemingly devoid of principle or loyalty.

Conclusion: The Beginning of What?

Trump’s ominous warning, “...AND SO IT BEGINS!“, begs the question: what exactly is beginning? Is it a new era of Democratic dominance, a progressive wave ready to reshape the American political landscape? Or perhaps, more darkly, it signals Trump’s descent into further divisive and potentially destructive tactics as his influence wanes.

What remains clear is the undeniable shift in the political winds, a shift that Trump, with all his bluster and cryptic messaging, appears powerless to stop. The recent elections are not just a defeat for Trump and his brand of politics; they are a beacon of hope for those advocating for a more equitable, representative, and just political system.

As we analyze these developments, it’s essential to remain vigilant. The fight for democracy and against voter intimidation, gerrymandering, and political manipulation is far from over. But for now, the message from voters is unmistakable: the era of Trump’s unchecked influence is drawing to a close, and a new chapter, however uncertain, is beginning.
