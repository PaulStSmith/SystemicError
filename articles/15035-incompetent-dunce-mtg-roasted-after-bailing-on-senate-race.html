'Incompetent dunce': MTG roasted after bailing on Senate race

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosMay 11, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Marjorie Taylor Greene (Photo Via Reuters)

Rep. Marjorie Taylor Greene (R-GA) finally settled weeks of speculation by taking her name out of consideration for Senate, in a lengthy post that railed against Republican lawmakers and insisted she could win the race but wouldn't be able to accomplish anything because her GOP colleagues would just roll over for Democrats anyway.

The announcement, which followed Gov. Brian Kemp also declining to run, was met with widespread mockery on social media, as observers said they knew for all her bravado, there was no way she could actually win.

"In her announcement that she’s not running for senate MTG delivers some thinly veiled blows to many Republicans both in Washington and back home in Georgia," wrote New York Times congressional correspondent Robert Jimison.

"Long winded way of saying she’s scared to run against Jon Ossoff," wrote political commentary account Blue Georgia.

"Incompetent dunce @mtgreenee claims that she'd definitely win the #GASen primary & general if she ran, but doesn't want to run because the Senate is stupid," wrote former Massachusetts state representative candidate Mathew Helman. "I'm not even exaggerating or oversimplifying. She knows she'd lose to Jon @ossoff. Otherwise, she'd run."

"In short, you could run but you know you’d lose and you’re trying to cover it up by saying the Senate sucks," wrote Georgia Young Dems treasurer and state representative candidate Zae Brewer. "Yet just weeks ago you were flattered by the idea of running for Senate? A complete joke and an EMBARRASSMENT to our state of Georgia."

Our Analysis:

Marjorie Taylor Greene Bows Out: A Dive into the Political Theater

In an act that can only be described as a masterclass in political equivocation, Rep. Marjorie Taylor Greene (R-GA) has decided to grace the political stage with a performance that veers between comedy and tragedy, depending on your seat in the auditorium. Her recent declaration of non-intent regarding the Senate race is a spectacle of contradictions, fear-mongering, and, let's be frank, a clear exhibition of political cowardice wrapped in bravado.

The Fearful Warrior

Greene, in what can only be termed as a lengthy soliloquy of self-praise and blame-shifting, essentially argues that while she could easily claim victory in both the primary and general elections, she chooses not to. Her reasoning? A bizarre claim that her GOP colleagues would simply capitulate to Democrats, rendering her efforts futile. This thinly veiled excuse does little to mask the fear of a probable defeat at the hands of Jon Ossoff, despite her bold assertions to the contrary.

Mockery and Criticism: The Social Media Verdict

The response from social media was swift and merciless, highlighting a general consensus that Greene's decision was less about a strategic political maneuver and more about avoiding a public and embarrassing defeat. Observers, including political analysts and commentators, have not held back in their criticism. The mockery ranges from calling her announcement a "long-winded way of saying she’s scared to run against Jon Ossoff" to outright labeling her an "incompetent dunce" who is acutely aware of her slim chances against a formidable opponent.

A Divisive Figure in Her Own Party

What stands out in Greene's rant against her decision not to run is her thinly veiled contempt for her own party members. This internal discord within the GOP, showcased by Greene's scathing remarks, reveals a fracturing party, struggling to unite its base. Her attack is not just on the perceived weakness of her colleagues but a reflection of the ideological rifts that plague the Republican Party.

The Real Embarrassment

Zae Brewer, Georgia Young Dems treasurer and state representative candidate, hits the nail on the head, calling Greene's flip-flop on the Senate race a "complete joke and an EMBARRASSMENT to our state of Georgia." This sentiment seems to echo across the political spectrum, highlighting a shared understanding of Greene's actions as a self-serving political maneuver designed to save face.

In Conclusion: A Performance to Remember

Greene's announcement and the subsequent fallout is a stark reminder of the current state of political discourse in the United States. It's a blend of personal ambition, fear of failure, and a divisive strategy that undermines the very essence of public service. While Greene may have intended her statement to be a powerful mic drop, it landed with a thud, resonating as a testament to political cowardice and a glaring inability to face the electorate's verdict.

In the end, this episode will likely be remembered not for its political acumen but as a cautionary tale of how not to engage in the political arena. As the curtains close on this particular act, one can only hope for a future where political figures strive for unity and progress rather than division and retreat.
