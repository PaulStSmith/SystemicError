Trump Flips Off His Base — Again: The Collapse of 'America First'

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosJune 16, 20252 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Donald Trump has once again revealed the hollow core of his political brand—this time by metaphorically flipping the bird to his most loyal supporters. In a breathtaking display of audacity and betrayal, the self-proclaimed founder of the "America First" doctrine is now dragging the United States into yet another Middle East conflict, despite widespread opposition from his own base.

Let's be absolutely clear: this isn’t a pivot. It’s a betrayal.

In the wake of growing backlash from within MAGA circles demanding non-interventionism, Trump made it perfectly known—he couldn’t care less. A leaked phone call captured him boasting, "Considering that I'm the one that developed America First, and considering that the term wasn’t used until I came along, I think I'm the one that decides." The arrogance would be comical if the consequences weren’t so deadly.

"America First" was always more bumper sticker than policy doctrine, but for many of Trump’s followers, it was the ideological glue that justified years of chaos, cruelty, and corruption. Non-interventionism was central to that identity. Staying out of foreign entanglements was supposed to be the antithesis of the Bush-era neoconservative doctrine. That promise has now been shattered—publicly, loudly, and unapologetically.

This latest about-face isn’t surprising to anyone who's been paying attention. Trump has always been a man untethered to principles. Whether it's bragging about grabbing women without consent, inciting an insurrection, or now marching toward another Middle East disaster, he governs (when he governs at all) by whim and wounded ego.

The true damage here isn’t just geopolitical—it’s cultural and psychological. His loyalists now face a painful reckoning: the man they saw as a savior from globalist wars is just another narcissist willing to sacrifice them at the altar of his own vanity. And worse, he’s gaslighting them into thinking they were never truly part of the mission to begin with.

This is the authoritarian playbook in action: redefine the terms, erase the past, punish dissent. By claiming sole ownership of "America First," Trump isn’t just rewriting history—he's setting the stage for a movement that revolves entirely around his whims. Today, America First means staying out of war. Tomorrow, it might mean bombing Tehran. Whatever he says goes. It’s not a doctrine. It’s a personality cult.

And let’s not forget the enabling infrastructure behind this: a Republican Party that’s lost any capacity for moral judgment, a right-wing media machine that continues to whitewash his every blunder, and a base that—until now—has eagerly lapped up every contradiction.

But maybe this time, the emperor’s nakedness is too obvious to ignore.

When even the most dyed-in-the-wool MAGA loyalists are grumbling, it’s worth asking: how many more betrayals will it take before they realize they were never part of the plan?

In the end, Trump’s middle finger isn’t just for the left, the press, or foreign adversaries. It’s for anyone naive enough to believe he ever stood for anything other than himself.

And he’s not even hiding it anymore.

This isn’t a glitch.
It’s the design.
