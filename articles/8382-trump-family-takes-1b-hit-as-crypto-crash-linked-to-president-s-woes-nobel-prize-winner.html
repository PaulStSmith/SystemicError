An expert economist believes the Crypto slump is due to it being tied to Donald Trump’s approval rating.

Paul Krugman says the Trump trade is “unraveling” after the president went all-in on the digital currency. The Trump family made their way into meme coins and cryptocurrency earlier this year and it appears to have backfired. The Daily Beast reported the Trump family has lost upwards of $1 billion in trading crypto.

Further from that, the volatility of cryptocurrency seems to be tied with Trump’s approval rating, as Krugman notes a clear link between the administration’s failures and the decline of digital currencies like Bitcoin.

He explained in his Substack post, “Bitcoin’s price surged after Donald Trump won last year, and its recent plunge coincides with a series of Trump political setbacks. So how should we understand Bitcoin’s recent crash? Think of it as the unraveling of the Trump trade.”

“Trump remains as determined as ever to reward the industry that made his family rich, and those around him are as determined as ever to make America safe for predators of all kinds. But Trump’s power is visibly diminishing, so the price of Bitcoin, which has in effect become a bet on Trumpism, has plunged.”

But when Trump looks weaker, as recent polling cited by Krugman suggests, the knock-on effect to cryptocurrencies heavily backed by the Trump administration and his family is clear.

He added, “Is it a stretch to link Trump’s political woes to the price of crypto? No. As Josh Marshall often emphasizes, power is unitary. A weakened Trump is less able to work his will on all fronts, including his efforts to promote crypto.”

Analysis from Bloomberg News suggests Trump and his family has dragged their estimated collective wealth from $7.7 billion in early September to $6.7 billion in November. Trump’s power base appears to be weakening as the president faces resilience from a split GOP on issues of healthcare and economics.

Trump’s slip on power has been noted by The New Republic, who suggested the midterms next year could be a massive shift for the Democrats. Experts believe Trump’s age will be a major factor in voter turnout and voting intent in the 2026 elections.

Our Analysis:

The Trump-Crypto Conundrum: A Fable of Misplaced Faith and Financial Folly

In the bizarre financial fable of our times, the intertwining of Donald Trump’s political fortunes with the volatile world of cryptocurrency stands as a stark illustration of the dangers of conflating political fervor with economic investment. According to Paul Krugman, a move that not only showcases a staggering misunderstanding of digital currencies but also highlights the perilous pitfalls of political investments.

The Trump Trade: A Misguided Venture

The narrative that the Trump family, diving headfirst into the treacherous waters of meme coins and cryptocurrency, could somehow turn the tide in their financial favor is as flawed as it is fascinating. The notion that the Trump brand could extend its Midas touch to the digital currency market has proven to be a spectacular miscalculation. The reported loss of upwards of $1 billion in trading crypto by the Trump family is not just a financial blow; it’s a stark emblem of the perils of mixing politics with market speculation.

The Krugman Analysis: Linking Politics with Price Fluctuations

Krugman’s analysis, suggesting a direct correlation between Trump’s waning approval ratings and the plummeting prices of cryptocurrencies, is a biting commentary on the fickle nature of political investments. This assertion, that Bitcoin’s price movements could be seen as a bet on Trumpism, is a scathing indictment of the speculative frenzy that has often characterized the crypto market. It’s a marketplace that, as Krugman rightly points out, becomes a reflection of political volatility rather than economic fundamentals.

The Fallacy of Power and Wealth

The decline in the Trump family’s estimated collective wealth, from $7.7 billion to $6.7 billion, as reported, is indicative of a broader lesson in the illusory nature of power and wealth. Trump’s diminishing influence, as evidenced by a split GOP on pivotal issues like healthcare and economics, serves as a cautionary tale of how political fortunes can be both a boon and a bane for financial endeavors.

The Broader Implications

This tale of misplaced faith in the convergence of political power and financial markets extends beyond the Trump saga. It’s a stark reminder of the inherent risks involved in allowing political allegiances to dictate investment decisions. The speculation that Trump’s age might influence voter turnout and intent in the 2026 elections further underscores the precarious intersection of politics, power, and the financial markets.

Conclusion: A Cautionary Tale

The story of the Trump family’s foray into cryptocurrency, as analyzed by Krugman, is not just a narrative of financial loss. It’s a broader commentary on the volatile interplay between political fortunes and financial markets. As the crypto market continues to evolve, the lessons from this saga remain clear: mixing political fervor with financial speculation is a gamble that can lead to unpredictable, often disastrous, outcomes. The unraveling of the Trump trade is a cautionary tale, reminding us that in the volatile world of cryptocurrency, political winds are unpredictable, and their financial implications, profound.
