'He was very involved': Sources call out Trump’s big slush fund lie

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosMay 24, 20263 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

The Unsupervised Slush Fund: Trump’s Direct Hand in a $1.8 Billion Payout

The Unsupervised Slush Fund: Trump’s Direct Hand in a $1.8 Billion Payout

Master of Manipulation and Misdirection

Once again, former President Donald Trump has been caught in a web of his own deceit. Reports from Zeteo reveal that contrary to Trump’s public denials, he was significantly involved in orchestrating a $1.8 billion IRS settlement that conveniently benefits his allies and potentially himself. Trump’s direct involvement was confirmed by three administration sources, emphasizing that he was as engaged in this process as any major corporate leader would be in their own business dealings.

Power Plays and Presidential Influence

Donald Trump’s influence over the settlement is undeniable. As the sitting president at the time, Trump had the ultimate authority over his administration’s actions and the Department of Justice (DOJ). His claim of non-involvement flies in the face of the operational reality of presidential power. Moreover, the settlement ended a lawsuit Trump had initiated against his own government, which suggests a conflict of interest so glaring it could only exist in an administration that plays fast and loose with ethical guidelines.

Convenient Legal Outcomes

The details of the settlement further expose the manipulative tactics at play. Not only does this agreement establish a questionable fund, but it also includes a clause wiping out any audits and investigations into Trump and his business empire regarding tax discrepancies. This addendum, signed off by Acting Attorney General Todd Blanche, conveniently shields Trump from potential financial liabilities estimated at around $100 million. It’s a clear case of Trump using his presidential power to shield himself from legal and financial repercussions, under the guise of settling a lawsuit.

Judicial Concerns Overridden

The judicial oversight was effectively bypassed in this scenario. The judge overseeing Trump’s case against the IRS expressed concerns about the legitimacy of the lawsuit, given the lack of adversity between Trump and the government entities he controlled. However, before any judicial ruling could be made, the Trump DOJ hastened to announce the settlement. This rush to settle circumvents the necessary judicial scrutiny that could have prevented the establishment of this controversial fund.

Systemic Implications: Autocracy in Action

This incident is not merely about a legal settlement; it’s a glaring example of how Trump’s administration operated with autocratic impulses, bending governmental power for personal and political gain. The creation of a $1.8 billion fund under dubious circumstances and without proper oversight or transparency is indicative of a broader pattern of governance where rules are seen as obstacles rather than safeguards.

Conclusion: A Pattern of Personal Gain at Public Expense

The creation of this $1.8 billion IRS settlement fund under Trump’s direct influence is a stark reminder of the dangers posed by leaders who place personal gain over public good. It underscores the urgent need for robust mechanisms to ensure transparency and accountability in government, particularly when the lines between personal benefit and public duty are blurred. As we move forward, it is crucial to recognize and challenge these manipulative practices to protect the integrity of our democratic institutions.
