Former Trump associate Lev Parnas, who says he used to be "deep inside the Trump machine" and now reports from the outside, recently flagged a news story that he was sure would make Trump "flip."

Now, Parnas is saying Trump's language gave away some of the GOP's plan in Texas.

"I used to be one of them. Deep inside the Trump machine. I know the language, the strategy, the playbook. I know how they move, when they pivot, and what comes next," Parnas wrote in an article called "Trump Says 'We’re Entitled to Five More Seats'—Texas Turns into a Battlefield."

Summarizing the ongoing Texas controversy with Republicans abruptly trying to change the map, Parnas said, "More than fifty lawmakers fled the state, denying Republicans the quorum they needed to ram the maps through. Some went to New York. Others to Massachusetts. Many to Illinois and New Hampshire. Anywhere but Texas."

"Governor Greg Abbott responded by issuing civil arrest warrants. But those only carry legal weight inside Texas. Outside state lines, they’re little more than headlines," the ex-associate added. "So they escalated. Senator John Cornyn wrote to the FBI, asking the federal government to help locate and detain state legislators. And Donald Trump—again—made clear what he wanted. He told reporters the FBI 'may have to get involved.' He said the quiet part out loud."

Here's what Parnas says is the real plan "behind the scenes":

"Here’s what my sources are telling me, behind the scenes: the FBI’s involvement isn’t just about locating lawmakers. It’s about building a case. Officials inside the Bureau are now quietly exploring whether figures like Beto O’Rourke or Illinois Governor J.B. Pritzker can be tied to a broader conspiracy—accusations of bribery, of influencing public officials to abandon their sworn duties," he wrote. "If they can make that stick, they’ll say it crosses state lines. And suddenly, it’s a federal case."

Parnas goes on to say the plan goes beyond Texas.

"This isn’t just about Texas. This is about 2026. This is about the future of the American political map, and who controls it," he said, adding that Florida has launched similar operations. "This is Project 2025 in motion. The map is part of the mission. And if you think it ends at gerrymandering, you’re not paying attention. They’re not just shifting lines. They’re shifting laws. Deploying federal agencies. Elevating partisan loyalty over constitutional norms. Every lever of power is being pulled."

Our Analysis:

The Texas Redistricting Ruse: Trump's Transparent Tactics

In an era where political subterfuge seems as common as morning coffee, former President Donald Trump's latest maneuvers in the redistricting battles, particularly in Texas, are as subtle as a sledgehammer at a tea party. Lev Parnas, once a cog in the Trump machinery, now turned commentator, has shone a light on what appears to be a not-so-covert operation under the guise of redistricting efforts in the Lone Star State. Let's dissect this latest scheme with the precision of a scalpel, cutting through the obfuscation to reveal the marrow of the matter.

Trump's Not-so-secret Secret

According to Parnas, Trump's recent pronouncements on Texas redistricting aren't just off-the-cuff remarks but a deliberate reveal of the GOP's broader strategy. "We’re entitled to five more seats," Trump is reported to have said, turning Texas into a veritable battleground not just for control of its legislative map but for the soul of American democracy itself. This entitlement mentality towards legislative seats is a glaring testament to the audacity at play, suggesting a brazen attempt to manipulate political power through redistricting.

The Texas Tango

The drama unfolds with over fifty lawmakers fleeing Texas in a bid to deny the Republicans the quorum needed to force through their preferred maps. This act of defiance prompts a response worthy of a political thriller, with Governor Greg Abbott issuing civil arrest warrants that, outside Texas borders, are as toothless as a newborn. The escalation continues with Senator John Cornyn's plea to the FBI, transforming a state issue into a potential federal spectacle. Trump, ever the master of saying the quiet part out loud, suggests FBI involvement, unwittingly (or wittingly) signaling the real play here.

The Bigger Picture: Project 2025

Parnas peels back the layers to reveal a grander scheme aptly named Project 2025, which, according to him, aims to redraw not just the physical but the legal landscape of American politics. The alleged involvement of the FBI to build cases against political figures like Beto O’Rourke and Illinois Governor J.B. Pritzker under the guise of investigating bribery and abandonment of duty crosses a dangerous line. This isn’t mere gerrymandering; it's an orchestrated attempt to weaponize federal agencies against political adversaries, an egregious overreach that threatens the very foundations of our democratic norms.

The Shift Beyond Lines

The narrative doesn't stop at redrawing district lines; it's about a systematic shift in how laws are manipulated, how loyalty is placed above constitutional obligations, and how every lever of power is pulled to cement control. This isn't just about ensuring a Republican stronghold in Texas or Florida; it's about setting the stage for a political landscape in 2026 and beyond that favors one party, not through the will of the people but through the twisting of the system.

Conclusion: A Call to Vigilance

The revelations by Parnas, whether entirely accurate or tinged with the bias of a former insider turned outsider, should serve as a clarion call for vigilance. The use of federal agencies to influence state politics, the bending of laws to fit a particular political agenda, and the overt manipulation of the redistricting process are symptoms of a democracy in peril.

As observers, participants, and defenders of democratic principles, it is our duty to scrutinize these maneuvers with a critical eye. The battlefront isn't just in Texas; it's in every district, every state, and at every level of government where the principles of democracy are being challenged. The transparency of Trump's tactics in Texas provides us with a window into a much larger strategy at play. It's up to us to ensure that this window doesn't become a mirror reflecting a future where such strategies have succeeded in undermining the very essence of democratic governance.
