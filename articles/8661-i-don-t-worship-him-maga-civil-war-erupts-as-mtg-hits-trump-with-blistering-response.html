'I don't worship him': MAGA civil war erupts as MTG hits Trump with blistering response

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosNovember 16, 20255 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Our Analysis:A Deep Dive into the MAGA Meltdown: Trump vs. Greene

Less than an hour after President Donald Trump lashed out at one of his most ardent MAGA allies, Rep. Marjorie Taylor Greene, the Georgia Republican fired back with a blistering statement of her own.

In a post to X, Greene continued the public MAGA feud by accusing Trump of lying, panicking over the looming release of the Jeffrey Epstein files, and trying to intimidate GOP lawmakers.

“President Trump just attacked me and lied about me,” Greene wrote Friday night. “I haven’t called him at all, but I did send these text messages today. Apparently this is what sent him over the edge. The Epstein files.”

Greene claimed that Trump is “coming after me hard to make an example to scare all the other Republicans before next weeks vote to release the Epstein files.”

“It’s astonishing really how hard he’s fighting to stop the Epstein files from coming out that he actually goes to this level,” she told her followers Friday.

Greene added: “But really most Americans wish he would fight this hard to help the forgotten men and women of America who are fed up with foreign wars and foreign causes, are going broke trying to feed their families, and are losing hope of ever achieving the American dream.”

She went on in her lengthy social media response to Trump that she has given the president “too much of my precious time, too much of my own money,” and has defended him even when “almost all other Republicans turned their back and denounced him.”

“But I don’t worship or serve Donald Trump,” she concluded. “I worship God, Jesus is my savior, and I serve my district GA14 and the American people. I remain the same today as I’ve always been and I will continue to pray this administration will be successful because the American people desperately deserve what they voted for.”

Along with her statement, Greene posted what she said was the text message that prompted the fallout. In the message – timestamped 12:23 p.m. – Greene urged Trump to “Check the flight logs of the Epstein plane. Bill Clinton is on there like 26 times! Hillary too!!” She pushed him to lean into the release of the records, writing: “Epstein was a spider that wove the web of the deep state. Lean into it.”

The one-time MAGA loyalist ended her statement on X by insisting: “For me, I remain America First and America Only!!!”

Our Analysis:

A Deep Dive into the MAGA Meltdown: Trump vs. Greene

In the latest episode of the MAGA saga, the rift between former President Donald Trump and Rep. Marjorie Taylor Greene escalates into a public spectacle, shedding light on the tumultuous nature of political alliances within the Republican Party. The feud, ignited by Trump’s attack and Greene’s fiery rebuttal, encapsulates the chaos and internal conflicts that have become synonymous with the MAGA movement.

Trump’s Accusation and Greene’s Retaliation: The Spark That Lit the Fuse

Less than an hour after Trump unleashed his fury on Greene, she was quick to retaliate with a statement that was nothing short of incendiary. Greene’s response on X not only accused Trump of lying but also suggested that his actions were motivated by panic over the potential release of the Jeffrey Epstein files. This accusation is particularly explosive, hinting at underlying fears and secrets that could shake the foundations of the MAGA world.

Greene’s assertion that Trump is attempting to “intimidate GOP lawmakers” to prevent the release of the Epstein files speaks volumes about the power struggles and the dark underbelly of political maneuvering. It’s a bold claim, suggesting that the quest for transparency is being stifled by those who once championed the cause of “draining the swamp.”

The Irony of MAGA Infighting

The irony of this infighting cannot be understated. MAGA, a movement heralded for its united front against the so-called “deep state” and its promise to champion the forgotten Americans, is now mired in its own web of conflicts and contradictions. Greene’s lament that Trump should focus more on helping Americans rather than engaging in petty squabbles highlights a significant deviation from the movement’s proclaimed values.

Greene’s Declaration of Independence

Perhaps most striking is Greene’s declaration that she does not “worship or serve Donald Trump.” This statement, coupled with her insistence that she remains “America First and America Only,” signifies a pivotal moment of departure from unwavering loyalty to Trump. It underscores a growing recognition that allegiance to an individual can sometimes conflict with the broader principles and commitments to one’s constituents.

The Text Message Heard Around the MAGA World

The text message Greene claims to have sent Trump, urging him to investigate the Epstein flight logs, is a fascinating insight into the priorities and strategies within the MAGA camp. The mention of high-profile figures like Bill and Hillary Clinton in connection with Epstein is a reminder of the movement’s fixation on uncovering and combating elite corruption. However, the focus on such allegations also raises questions about the consistency and focus of their efforts to address the myriad of other pressing issues facing Americans.

Conclusion: The MAGA Meltdown as a Microcosm

This public spat between Trump and Greene is more than just a political sideshow; it’s a microcosm of the larger issues plaguing the MAGA movement and, by extension, the Republican Party. The clash of egos, the prioritization of personal vendettas over policy, and the deep fissures within the movement are symptomatic of a broader identity crisis. As the MAGA world turns its battle inward, one must wonder about the future direction of this political force and its impact on American politics.

In essence, the Trump-Greene feud is a glaring reminder that in the volatile arena of MAGA politics, alliances are fragile, loyalty is fleeting, and the quest for power often overshadows the proclaimed mission to serve the American people.
