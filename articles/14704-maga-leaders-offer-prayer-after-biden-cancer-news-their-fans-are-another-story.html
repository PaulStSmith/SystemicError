MAGA leaders offer prayer after Biden cancer news — their fans are another story

Joe Biden's cancer diagnosis went wide Sunday, resulting in MAGA leaders offering their prayers, but their followers weren't so kind.

Raw Story flagged earlier in the day reports from New York Times and CNN about Biden's "aggressive" prostate cancer diagnosis.

It wasn't long before Marjorie Taylor Greene weighed in.

"I’m sorry to see this news. Cancer is truly awful," the MAGA lawmaker wrote. "My Dad passed away in 2021 with cancer. Prays for Joe Biden and his family."

One MAGA account, Here for the News, replied, "I’m sorry but I do not feel for him. He’s a PURE TRAITOR! Despicable! Should be serving his last years behind bars…along with his corrupt family!"

Another self-identified conservative, Max McAdams, responded to Greene with, "I could not be happier about it."

Another account with MAGA in their bio, TimF, chimed in, "No prayers for this man."

Similarly, Donald Trump Jr. on Sunday said he "agreed 100%" with an image wishing Biden a "speedy recovery."

The Trump son's fans weren't quite as generous.

Butch Connors, who frequently shares right-wing conspiracy theory accounts, asked, "Are we just going to forget that he trafficked kids across the boarder(sic)? Or was that a lie too?"

One popular right-wing account, RedpillGoku, simply replied, "The Biden Administration was the worst ever."

Yet another MAGA conspiracy account, Meme War Veteran, had this to say:

"He’s welcome to seek treatment in Gitmo."

Our Analysis:

The Stark Contrast Between MAGA Leaders and Their Followers

In the swirling vortex of American politics, the recent announcement of Joe Biden's aggressive prostate cancer diagnosis has unveiled a stark, unsettling contrast within the MAGA (Make America Great Again) community. On one side, we have leaders like Marjorie Taylor Greene offering prayers and condolences, highlighting the universal struggle against cancer that transcends political divides. On the other, a venomous underbelly of MAGA followers has surfaced with reactions ranging from apathy to outright malice.

The Hypocrisy of Political Decency

Greene's response, while seemingly sincere, underscores a broader issue of performative decency often displayed by political figures. "Cancer is truly awful," she writes, a statement few would contest. Yet, this is the same Greene known for her controversial and often divisive rhetoric. The juxtaposition of her personal empathy against her public persona is a striking reminder of the complex human behind the politician, a nuance frequently lost in the binary world of American politics.

The Disturbing Echoes of MAGA Followers

The reactions from MAGA followers, as reported, range from cold indifference to disturbing schadenfreude. Phrases like "PURE TRAITOR" and "should be serving his last years behind bars…along with his corrupt family" not only dehumanize Biden in the face of a life-threatening diagnosis but also reflect a deep-seated animosity that has been cultivated over years of political division. The sentiment of not offering prayers, or worse, expressing happiness over the diagnosis, illuminates a severe lack of empathy permeating parts of the MAGA base.

The Dangerous Game of Dehumanization

This phenomenon isn't just about Biden or the MAGA community; it's a symptom of a larger, more dangerous game of dehumanization playing out across the political spectrum. When political opponents are no longer seen as human, but as caricatures of evil or corruption, it paves the way for a society where empathy is conditional, based on political allegiance rather than shared humanity.

The Role of Leaders and Followers

Donald Trump Jr.'s agreement with a message wishing Biden a "speedy recovery" stands in awkward solidarity with Greene's sentiments, yet their followers' responses starkly deviate from this script. This raises questions about the role of leaders in shaping or moderating the attitudes of their followers. Are MAGA leaders simply out of touch with the base, or is there a more complex dynamic at play where leaders provide a veneer of decency that barely conceals the vitriol beneath?

The Misinformation Menace

The mention of trafficking and Gitmo by MAGA followers points to another significant issue: the role of misinformation and conspiracy theories in fueling hatred and division. These baseless accusations serve as a reminder of the power of misinformation to dehumanize and demonize political opponents, further entrenching divisions within society.

Conclusion

The reaction to Biden's cancer diagnosis within the MAGA community encapsulates the broader societal challenge of political dehumanization. While some leaders offer prayers, the cruel reactions of many followers reveal a deeply entrenched animosity that transcends political disagreements. This episode serves as a stark reminder of the urgent need for empathy, understanding, and a recognition of shared humanity in the realm of political discourse. Without it, the American political landscape risks devolving into an arena where not even life-threatening illnesses can bridge the chasm of division.
