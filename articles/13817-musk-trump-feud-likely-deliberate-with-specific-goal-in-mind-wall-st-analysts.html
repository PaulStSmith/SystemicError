Musk-Trump feud likely deliberate with specific goal in mind: Wall St analysts

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosJune 11, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Our Analysis:The Calculated Circus of Elon Musk and the Political Arena

A newspaper front page featuring U.S. President Donald Trump and Elon Musk is displayed at a shop at Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport in Arlington, Virginia, U.S., June 6, 2025. REUTERS/Carlos Barria

Financial analysts with Wall Street firm Morgan Stanley believe that Elon Musk's very public feud with President Donald Trump was calculated to gain "maximum" attention for his opposition to the "big, beautiful bill," according to Investing.com.

Market reporter Sam Boughedda wrote Tuesday, "Morgan Stanley analysts believe that Elon Musk’s recent public communication 'campaign'...is 'likely part of a planned strategy by Elon to achieve a specific goal with his approach designed to bring maximum public attention to the issue.'"

Before the friendship completely imploded last week, Musk wrote on his social media platform, "This massive, outrageous, pork-filled Congressional spending bill is a disgusting abomination."

The Investing.com report continued that "Despite the potential for 'negative sentiment around Tesla (NASDAQ:TSLA)’s products/brand' due to Musk’s political involvement, Morgan Stanley suggests this 'would not have come as a surprise to company management.'"

The firm argued that despite his promise to "focus solely on Tesla" from now on, Musk's vision of business and politics are "very much inseparable" for the Tesla CEO, with the "financial adequacy of the United States (budget deficit, national debt, etc)...elevated to a top priority."

Regardless, Morgan Stanley said it retained "sufficient confidence in the long-term vision of the company to reiterate the name as our ’Top Pick’ in U.S. autos," Investing.com reported.

London-based Data Center Dynamics offered a perspective on the Morgan Stanley/Elon Musk connection.

"Elon Musk's split with President Donald Trump was on the same day that Morgan Stanley tried to help [Musk's] generative AI company xAI raise $5 billion," the site reported. "The Wall Street Journal reports that xAI executives were speaking to Wall Street about the company’s data center buildout and AI chatbot Grok, this past Thursday."

Data Center's editor-in-chief continued, "Morgan Stanley had been hoping to sell the debt at around 100 cents on the dollar, with the close relationship between President Trump and White House adviser Musk having helped boost stocks in Musk's businesses, and reverse a decline in the valuation of social platform X (which is merging with xAI)."

Our Analysis:

The Calculated Circus of Elon Musk and the Political Arena

In the latest drama to unfold on the grand stage of corporate-political theater, we find Elon Musk, the ever-controversial tech mogul, at the center of a very public feud with none other than President Donald Trump. According to a report by Investing.com, financial analysts at Morgan Stanley have suggested that Musk's recent outbursts are not the random ravings of a billionaire on a social media spree, but a calculated move aimed at drawing "maximum" attention to his opposition against what he describes as a "massive, outrageous, pork-filled Congressional spending bill."

Musk’s Strategy: A Spotlight or a Spotlight Hog?

Musk's approach, as dissected by Morgan Stanley analysts, appears to be a meticulously planned campaign to leverage his vast social media influence to sway public opinion—or at least to shine a glaring spotlight on it. Before his friendship with Trump "completely imploded," Musk took to his platform to lambaste the congressional spending bill as a "disgusting abomination." This move, while bold, begs the question: Is Musk genuinely concerned about fiscal responsibility, or is this just another chapter in the saga of Musk's ego?

The analysts suggest that Musk's political involvement, while potentially detrimental to Tesla's brand, was a calculated risk. They argue that Musk's vision of business is "very much inseparable" from politics, elevating the "financial adequacy of the United States" to a top priority. This intertwining of business acumen and political maneuvering showcases Musk as a figure who refuses to be pigeonholed into the role of a silent corporate leader. Instead, he opts for the path of maximum disruption—for better or worse.

The Musk-Morgan Stanley-Trump Triangle

Adding another layer to this complex narrative is the report from London-based Data Center Dynamics, highlighting a peculiar coincidence: Musk's public split with Trump occurred on the same day Morgan Stanley was attempting to help Musk's AI company, xAI, raise a staggering $5 billion. This revelation casts a shadow of strategic maneuvering over Musk's actions, suggesting that his political posturing could also serve as a lever to bolster his business ventures.

It's crucial to note that Musk's ventures, particularly in the realm of generative AI and data center buildouts, stand to gain significantly from such high-profile exposure. The Wall Street Journal's report on xAI executives speaking to Wall Street about the company's ambitious projects only thickens the plot. Morgan Stanley's attempt to sell debt at around 100 cents on the dollar, amidst Musk's fluctuating relationship with Trump, paints a picture of a canny business magnate who knows how to play the political game to his advantage.

Conclusion: The Musk Method—Innovation or Instigation?

As Morgan Stanley retains its faith in Tesla, reiterating the company as their ‘Top Pick’ in U.S. autos, one must ponder the implications of Musk's actions. Is Elon Musk a visionary using his platform to advocate for fiscal responsibility and innovation, or is he a master manipulator, adept at using political controversies to further his business interests?

What is clear is that Musk's foray into the political arena is neither coincidental nor without consequence. As the lines between business leadership and political influence continue to blur, figures like Musk remind us of the power—and the peril—of having a platform. Whether his actions are seen as a bold stance against fiscal irresponsibility or a calculated move to serve his interests, one thing is undeniable: Elon Musk knows how to keep the world watching.
