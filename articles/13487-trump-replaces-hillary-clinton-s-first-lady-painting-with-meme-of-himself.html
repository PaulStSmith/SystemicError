Trump replaces Hillary Clinton's first lady painting with meme of himself

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosJune 19, 20251 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Former first lady Hillary Clinton’s official White House portrait hanging in the hallway outside The Vermeil Room during President Barack Obama’s second term (Photo: Sarah Burris/RawStory.com)

Reuters White House Correspondent Jeff Mason posted a photo to X of a wall in the White House where former first lady Hillary Clinton's portrait once hung.

The wall features paintings of first ladies in chronological succession, with Laura Bush and Barbara Bush, and between them would commonly be Clinton's painting. That has now been replaced with a meme of President Donald Trump turned into an unframed painting.

The paintings appear to be hanging outside the East Colonnade, the glass-enclosed hallway that connects the first lady's East Wing to the main White House residence.

Each first family chooses which paintings to display in the White House. The East Wing typically features a number of first ladies' portraits. Clinton's, from the 1990s, features the former first lady and Secretary of State in a dark pant suit.

The Trump picture is a meme in which an American flag has been superimposed as skin over Trump's face.
