Trump’s Cult Fractures Over Epstein—and He’s Furious

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosJuly 16, 20252 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

“I Don’t Want Their Support Anymore!”The “Jeffrey Epstein Hoax”The Bondi Problem“We Have a PERFECT Administration”Even Mike Johnson Is Backing AwayFinal Thought

Donald Trump is now turning on the very cult he built, torching the MAGA faithful for daring to question his administration’s ludicrous handling of the Jeffrey Epstein files. This is rich. The man who rose to power by peddling conspiracy theories like birtherism and “deep state” paranoia is now raging that his base is too conspiratorial—over Epstein, no less, a man Trump once partied with and praised.

Let’s dissect this slow-motion car crash of narcissism and gaslighting:

“I Don’t Want Their Support Anymore!”

Trump lashes out at his own followers like a toddler throwing blocks at the wall because they won’t stop talking about Epstein. These aren’t Democrats or “fake news media” he’s whining about—these are his own sycophants. People radicalized by his rhetoric, stewing for years in the QAnon sewage he tolerated, encouraged, and sometimes winked at.

And now? He’s mad they won’t drop the Epstein bone? Spare us.

The “Jeffrey Epstein Hoax”

According to Trump, the Epstein controversy is a “hoax” now. Remember when he called Russia a hoax? Ukraine? Covid? The 2020 election being legitimate? His go-to playbook is to label anything inconvenient as fake and expect his followers to fall in line. But now that some are questioning the glaring inconsistencies in the Epstein story—and Trump’s own proximity to it—they’re suddenly traitors?

He even calls them “selfish people.” Imagine that. The grifter-in-chief, who used the Oval Office as a personal ATM, now calling others selfish for wanting transparency about the most infamous sex trafficking case of the 21st century.

The Bondi Problem

Former Florida AG Pam Bondi—loyal Trump bootlicker—is at the center of this mess. She told Fox News in February that Epstein’s “client list” was on her desk. This predictably sent the MAGAverse into a frenzy. Then, when the DOJ and FBI suddenly declare there’s no client list, no evidence Epstein was murdered, and no real reason to ask questions... they expect the conspiracy crowd to just sit down and shut up?

They fed this fire, and now they’re surprised it’s burning down their tent.

“We Have a PERFECT Administration”

Trump writes in all caps that his administration is “THE TALK OF THE WORLD” and “PERFECT.” The delusion is stunning. Perfect administrations don’t bleed staff, botch pandemic responses, fumble insurrections, or misplace classified documents like they’re loose Tic Tacs in a glove compartment. But sure, Don. Keep shouting it. Maybe it’ll become true if your echo chamber believes it hard enough.

Even Mike Johnson Is Backing Away

When Mike Johnson—Trump loyalist and Bible-thumping empty suit—says, “we should put everything out there,” you know the dam is cracking. He’s not a truth-teller by any stretch. He’s a canary in the MAGA coal mine. And if he is calling for transparency, it means the stench is too strong to cover with the usual “witch hunt” nonsense.

Final Thought

This isn’t just a tantrum. It’s a slow fracture. The MAGA base was built on distrust, paranoia, and vengeance. Trump rode that wave into power. But when you build a movement on conspiracy, eventually they come for you too.

And now they are. And Trump is flailing. Good.

Let the MAGA cult eat its tail.
