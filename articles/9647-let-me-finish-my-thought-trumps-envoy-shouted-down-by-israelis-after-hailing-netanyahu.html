‘Let me finish my thought!’ Trump’s envoy shouted down by Israelis after hailing Netanyahu

By Paulo Santos

REUTERS/Al Drago

Steve Witkoff, President Donald Trump’s special envoy to the Middle East, was shouted down in Israel Saturday after hailing Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu as being instrumental in the Gaza peace plan that was agreed to, in part, by both Israel and Hamas.

“This moment was made through the tireless dedication of leaders who would not rest until the world saw what could be achieved, one of them standing right here with me, Jared Kushner,” Witkoff said to a crowd of thousands in Tel Aviv, Israel.

Witkoff then proceeded to mention Netanyahu’s name in his round of thanks only to be met with loud boos and jeers from the crowd.

“Okay, okay,” Witkoff said, trying to thank Netanyahu again before being interrupted by a second round of jeers from the crowd.

“I, I, okay, let me just finish my thought. I was, I was in the trenches... guys, let me just finish my thought,” Witkoff said, as he struggled to tamper down the jeers from the crowd. “I was in the trenches with the prime minister – believe me, he was a very important part here.”

Boos continued to ring out from the audience at every mention of Netanyahu, whose popularity among Israelis has plummeted since Hamas’ attack in 2023, with many Israeli critics accusing Netanyahu of deliberately undermining a hostage deal.

“The prime minister and his staff, Ron Dermer included, have both, have both…” Witkoff said, before again being interrupted by thunderous boos and jeers.

“Okay – have both sacrificed so much for this country and devoted their lives to the service of Israel. Their dedication to the history and destiny of this nation stands out tonight. They’ve given everything – their time, their energy and their hearts – to building a safer, stronger future for the Jewish people. Their commitment to this country has never wavered and it never will.”

Witkoff then invoked Trump’s name, which immediately sparked unanimous cheers among the crowd of thousands, who began to break out in chants, shouting “thank you, Trump!”

Our Analysis:

The Unpopular Praise: Netanyahu’s Controversial Legacy

In a recent spectacle that unfolded in Tel Aviv, Israel, Steve Witkoff, President Donald Trump’s special envoy to the Middle East, found himself in a verbal quagmire when he attempted to shower praises on Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu. Despite Netanyahu’s involvement in a Gaza peace plan agreed upon, in part, by both Israel and Hamas, the crowd’s reaction was anything but peaceful.

The Boos That Spoke Volumes

Witkoff’s attempt to commend Netanyahu was met with a storm of boos and jeers, a vivid demonstration of the public’s sentiment. This uproar wasn’t merely a disagreement but a potent symbol of Netanyahu’s plummeting popularity among Israelis. The crowd’s reaction is a testament to the deep-seated frustration and disillusionment with Netanyahu, especially in the wake of Hamas’ attack in 2023. Critics accuse Netanyahu of deliberately undermining a hostage deal, a serious allegation that colors the public’s perception of his leadership.

A Tale of Two Reactions

What’s particularly striking in this scenario is the stark contrast between the reception of Netanyahu’s mention and the unanimous cheers for Donald Trump. This dichotomy highlights a polarized response to political figures who, ostensibly, were working towards the same goal. While Trump is hailed with gratitude, Netanyahu is scorned, suggesting a complex interplay of local sentiments and international politics.

The Irony of Unwavering Commitment

Witkoff’s speech, despite the interruptions, emphasized the “sacrifice” and “dedication” of Netanyahu and his staff to the service and future of Israel. Yet, this narrative of unwavering commitment falls flat in the face of the public’s vocal disapproval. It’s an irony that underscores a disconnection between political rhetoric and public sentiment. The assertion of dedication and sacrifice, intended to bolster Netanyahu’s image, instead becomes a hollow echo amid the cacophony of disapproval.

The Political Fallout

This incident is not merely a footnote in a public gathering; it’s a microcosm of Netanyahu’s eroding support base. It raises critical questions about the effectiveness of his leadership and the future political landscape in Israel. The public’s reaction in Tel Aviv could very well be a bellwether for Netanyahu’s political destiny and, by extension, the direction of Israeli politics.

Conclusion: The Sound of Discontent

In the end, the boos that drowned out Witkoff’s praises for Netanyahu speak louder than the words themselves. They represent a growing discontent among Israelis, a systemic error in leadership that cannot be ignored. As Israel navigates its complex geopolitical challenges, the voices of its people, as heard in Tel Aviv, will undoubtedly shape its path forward. The incident is a stark reminder that in the realm of politics, popularity is fleeting, and the court of public opinion can be the most unforgiving judge.
