'Hoping he gets canned': MAGA allies reportedly pushing out Trump's trusted confidant

U.S. Secretary of Commerce Howard Lutnick stands next to U.S. President Donald Trump as he speaks with journalists onboard Air Force One en route to Miami, Florida, U.S., April 3, 2025. REUTERS/Kent Nishimura

A new report in Politico claims that one of President Donald Trump's closest confidants, Commerce secretary Howard Lutnick, is considered a pariah among MAGA supporters who "are openly trash talking him to the boss, hoping he gets canned."

Reporters Rachael Bade and Megan Messerly wrote, "talk to almost any Trump ally outside the White House and you’ll hear scant praise for the Commerce secretary. He’s brash and impulsive, catering to Trump’s worst instincts on tariffs that could jeopardize the economy, they whisper. His loud mouth is equally matched by his bad judgment, they steam."

But the complaints amount to little more than "wishful thinking," the report said, due to Lutnick's unusually close relationship with the president. Lutnick, himself, has referred to the 30-year friendship with Trump as his "superpower."

In essence, Lutnick is protected by a shield of loyalty to Trump, even after a debacle like the "chaotic rollout of the president’s 'Liberation Day' tariff policies that might have doomed any other Commerce secretary," the reporters wrote.

The ride or die relationship between the two New York billionaires revolves around "the two things Trump arguably values most: loyalty and money," the report said.

"When Trump turned into a pariah after Jan. 6, 2021, Lutnick still flew down to golf with him in Palm Beach," the report said. "During rocky parts of the 2024 campaign, people close to Trump say Lutnick 'stuck by the president' even on the grimmest days. Not to mention, he’s one of Trump’s biggest donors. He donated more than $10 million to Trump’s re-election, raising an additional $75 million for him by leaning on his Wall Street connections."

Lutnick's critics describe him as the worst kind of sycophant: "a sounding board who won’t curb [Trump's] most reckless instincts" who "marches into the Oval Office and tells Trump whatever he wants to hear rather than providing an accurate accounting of possible blowback," the report said.

However, "For all the kvetching among others close to Trump, Lutnick is the one who has a regular ice cream date with the president," which doesn't look like it will end anytime soon, the report concluded.

Our Analysis:

The Paradox of Power and Loyalty: Dissecting the Politico Report on Howard Lutnick

In a political landscape that often resembles a high-stakes drama more than a functioning democracy, the recent Politico report on Howard Lutnick, Commerce Secretary under President Donald Trump, reads like yet another episode in the saga. The article paints a vivid picture of a man who has become a pariah among MAGA supporters, yet remains untouchable due to his close personal relationship with Trump. This analysis delves into the underlying currents of this relationship, exposing the fragile balance between loyalty, power, and governance.

The Cult of Personality and the Price of Loyalty

At the heart of the Politico report is an uncomfortable truth that has defined much of Trump's presidency and post-presidency: the elevation of loyalty over competence. Lutnick's described "superpower" is not his acumen as a Commerce Secretary but his 30-year friendship with Trump. This is not governance; it's a feudal allegiance. The description of Lutnick as brash, impulsive, and a purveyor of Trump's "worst instincts on tariffs" underscores a governance style driven by personal loyalty rather than policy efficacy or expert advice.

The Sycophant's Dilemma

Lutnick is depicted as the epitome of a sycophant, a term that carries with it the stench of disingenuity and servility. Critics within the MAGA camp describe him as someone who "won’t curb Trump's most reckless instincts" and "marches into the Oval Office and tells Trump whatever he wants to hear." This behavior is not merely disappointing; it is dangerous. It represents a fundamental failure to uphold the duties of a public servant, whose allegiance should be to the welfare of the country, not the whims of an individual, no matter how powerful.

The Economics of Loyalty

The article highlights the financial underpinnings of Lutnick's relationship with Trump, noting his significant contributions to Trump's re-election campaign and his ability to leverage Wall Street connections for fundraising. This intertwining of money and politics is a stark reminder of the transactional nature of power in Trump's orbit. It's not just about being a loyal friend or an agreeable cabinet member; it's also about the financial contributions that cement one's place within the inner circle.

The Consequences of Echo Chambers

Lutnick's role as a "sounding board who won’t curb [Trump's] most reckless instincts" is indicative of a broader issue within Trump's administration: the creation of an echo chamber. This environment, where dissenting voices are silenced, and affirming ones are amplified, is antithetical to the principles of democratic governance. It leads to policy decisions that are not only poorly conceived but dangerously myopic, as they fail to account for the complex realities of governance and global economics.

Conclusion: The Cost of Unchecked Loyalty

The Politico report, through its examination of Howard Lutnick's position within Trump's circle, exposes the dangerous precedence of loyalty over competence, integrity, and the greater good. The narrative surrounding Lutnick is a microcosm of a larger issue plaguing American politics: the valorization of personal allegiance at the expense of public service and accountability.

As we move forward, it is crucial to remember the lessons of this era and the figures like Lutnick, who serve as cautionary tales. The path to a more accountable, transparent, and effective governance requires a rejection of the cult of personality and a recommitment to the principles of democracy. Only then can we hope to avoid the pitfalls of leadership that prioritizes loyalty over all else.
