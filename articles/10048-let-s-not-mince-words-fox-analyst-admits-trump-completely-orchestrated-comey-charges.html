'Let's not mince words': Fox analyst admits Trump 'completely orchestrated' Comey charges

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosSeptember 27, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Fox News‘ Howard Kurtz stopped for a moment on Thursday evening to acknowledge the hard reality for the generally Donald Trump-supportive network: the president’s personally-influenced Justice Department indictment of former FBI Director James Comey flies in the face of all standards of how federal prosecutors are supposed to operate, or in fact would have been tolerated in any prior administration.

Comey, who was initially fired near the beginning of Trump’s first term in office for refusing to intervene to shut down the Russia investigation, was indicted earlier in the day in Virginia, following Trump’s demands for it to happen. The charges, of false statements and obstruction, appear to be based on a discrepancy between his and his deputy’s accounts in Senate questioning several years ago, which was already investigated by the inspector general and determined likely not to have been a lie.

But Trump insisted on the prosecution anyway, and took a victory lap on his Truth Social platform after it was filed, writing, “JUSTICE IN AMERICA! One of the worst human beings this Country has ever been exposed to is James Comey, the former Corrupt Head of the FBI.”

None of this is normal, Kurtz pointed out to his fellows on Fox News’ “Special Report with Bret Baier.”

“Let’s not mince words here — I say this as a former Justice Department reporter,” said Kurtz. “This was completely and totally orchestrated by President Trump. He says he’s leaving it to Justice, but he very much out in the open — remember, he fired his own appointee as U.S. attorney in that part of Virginia, because he didn’t think there was enough evidence to bring this case. Brought in a White House aide, his former defense lawyer, whose job was to get this done, and apparently that has worked.”

“I think we’ve become inured to this, but in previous administrations, any hint of contact with a criminal case would be a scandal on its own,” he added. “And so President Trump, by the way he pulled the strings, has gotten his way with this initial indictment.”

Our Analysis:

The Orchestration of Injustice: Trump’s Vendetta Against Comey

In a moment of rare acknowledgment from Fox News, a network notoriously aligned with Donald Trump’s political ethos, Howard Kurtz has laid bare a disturbing truth. Trump’s personally-influenced Justice Department indictment of former FBI Director James Comey is not just an anomaly; it’s a glaring affront to the principles of justice and a stark deviation from the norms that have historically governed federal prosecution. The indictment, driven by Trump’s vendetta and executed under his direct influence, marks a chilling departure from democratic standards.

The Charges: A Pretext for Political Revenge

The charges against Comey, rooted in alleged false statements and obstruction, stem from a discrepancy in testimonies—a matter previously scrutinized and dismissed by the inspector general. This resurrection of charges by Trump’s Justice Department, following his explicit demands, is nothing short of a political witch hunt. The act of celebrating this indictment on Truth Social as “JUSTICE IN AMERICA!” starkly illustrates Trump’s manipulation of the justice system for personal vendetta. It’s a manifestation of his disdain for the legal and democratic principles that underpin the American system.

The Unprecedented Nature of Trump’s Influence

Kurtz’s commentary sheds light on a disturbing reality: Trump’s direct intervention in the Justice Department’s operations is unprecedented and dangerous. The dismissal of a U.S. attorney for lack of evidence against Comey and the subsequent appointment of a White House aide and former defense lawyer to ensure the indictment’s success is a blatant abuse of power. Trump’s actions, as Kurtz rightly points out, have orchestrated a scenario where the lines between the executive branch and the justice system are not just blurred—they’re obliterated.

The Silence and the Scandal

What is equally alarming is the normalization of such egregious oversteps. In any previous administration, mere hints of such interference would have sparked outrage and been deemed scandalous. Yet, in the era of Trump, the bar for what constitutes an abuse of power seems to have been lowered to the ground. Kurtz’s assertion that we’ve become inured to this level of manipulation is a damning indictment of not just Trump’s presidency but of the societal and political milieu that allows for such actions to pass without significant reprisal.

Conclusion: A Wake-Up Call

Kurtz’s moment of clarity on Fox News serves as a crucial wake-up call. The indictment of James Comey is not merely about settling scores; it’s a symptomatic display of Trump’s broader assault on the judiciary and the principles of justice. This incident underscores the urgent need for a collective reckoning with the ways in which political power is wielded and the paramount importance of safeguarding the independence of our judicial system. As we navigate these tumultuous waters, let us not lose sight of the foundational values that are being eroded—values that we must vigilantly protect and preserve.
