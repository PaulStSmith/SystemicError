Roger J. Stone Jr., a longstanding ally of President Donald Trump, has been lobbying for a prominent cryptocurrency investor nicknamed "Bitcoin Jesus," who is currently facing federal fraud and tax-related criminal charges, the New York Times reported Friday.

According to a report by the Times, congressional records reveal that Stone provided documents indicating he was engaged by Roger Ver, an early Bitcoin investor who was charged last year for allegedly hiding his cryptocurrency holdings to avoid paying $48 million in taxes.

Ver, who previously lived in California and gave up his U.S. citizenship in 2014, was arrested in Spain last year, per the Justice Department.

In a recent filing, Stone mentioned that he received $600,000 from Ver since early February to assist with his client's case. Ver reportedly worked to eliminate the tax provisions central to the allegations.

In January, Ver posted a video on social media in which he refuted the allegations against him, claiming that he was facing threats of a potential prison term exceeding 100 years due to his political beliefs and his involvement in advocating for cryptocurrency.

The video was a plea to Trump in which Ver connected his situation to the president's concerns regarding the alleged misuse of the justice system.

“They’re doing the exact same thing to me that they’ve done to you,” he said in the video.

The Allegations: A Closer Look

Roger Ver, once a prominent figure in the world of cryptocurrency, allegedly went to great lengths to hide his cryptocurrency holdings, thereby evading a whopping $48 million in taxes. This act alone should raise eyebrows — not just for the sheer amount but for the audacity. Ver, who renounced his U.S. citizenship in 2014, found himself arrested in Spain, with the U.S. Justice Department breathing down his neck. Yet, what does he do? He enlists Stone, a man no stranger to controversy himself, paying him $600,000 since early February to whip up some form of political defense.

Stone's Role: The Middleman of Influence

Roger J. Stone Jr.'s involvement brings to light the disturbing reality of how political connections are exploited. Stone, leveraging his longstanding alliance with Trump, has been working behind the scenes, attempting to sway the legal outcomes for Ver. This isn't just about lobbying; it's about manipulating the justice system, using every possible connection to dodge accountability. Stone provided documents indicating his engagement by Ver — a move that smacks of desperation and manipulation, aiming to use political influence as a shield against legal repercussions.

Ver's Defense: A Cry for Presidential Intervention

In a bold move, Ver took to social media, posting a video that sought to appeal directly to President Trump. His message? That he, like Trump, is a victim of the justice system's alleged misuse. This isn't just a defense; it's a calculated plea, drawing parallels between his situation and Trump's grievances against the justice system.
