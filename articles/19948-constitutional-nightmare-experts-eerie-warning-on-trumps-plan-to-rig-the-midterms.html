'Constitutional nightmare': Expert’s 'eerie warning' on Trump’s plan to rig the midterms

True dictators who want to take over a country don’t wait until there’s an election. They plot and plan and strike when the moment is right.

That’s what The Bulwark’s Will Saletan said when speaking to once-Republican commentator Matt Lewis on his Substack. Saletan said he was researching historical analogs to Trump’s efforts to overturn the U.S. election results.

Director of National Intelligence Tulsi Gabbard was recently photographed lurking in an evidence truck as the FBI raided a Fulton County elections hub. As AlterNet reported last week, the administration’s story for why she was there has changed at least five times.

The truth, said Saletan, is that during a conversation with Trump, Gabbard was indeed sent to Atlanta at Trump’s urging, despite Trump claiming otherwise in a subsequent interview.

“Trump literally said to her in a meeting about — she was talking about an unrelated matter, something that was actually part of her job. And he tells — he says to her, and these are witnesses in the room. ‘You go do that. You get it done.’ He’s saying that to the DNI. And so that’s why she shows up a couple of days later on an evidence truck,” said Saletan, relaying the New York Times reporting about the raid.

He called it “scary as hell” to have the FBI doing something like this, adding that the DNI’s inclusion makes it worse.

Saletan remarked on a key provision of the warrant: the FBI could only take copies of the ballots. They couldn’t take possession of literally every document that Fulton County had.

“Trump’s FBI goes down with Tulsi Gabbard, this political hack,” Saletan explained. “They seize these ballots, and I forget what else, what other equipment. They put it in the truck and they take it away. If I’m a juror, I’m not believing anything about this. The chain of custody is broken. I do not trust that those ballots that that those have have not been messed with.”

Lewis noted that taking the ballots gives the administration the opportunity to now claim that Trump won 100 percent of Fulton County’s votes and there would be no way to prove him otherwise. The former Republican said he’s more concerned about the 2026 and 2028 elections. He asked Saletan whether the raid is about 2020 or if it’s Trump digging into future elections.

Saletan said he fears this is more about our future before noting he was unable to find an analogous situation to compare because dictators don’t wait around until they lose an election to overthrow the government.

“Real dictators, if you study history they make sure they get it right the first time,” he said, using Nicolás Maduro as an example. “They make sure that when they lose an election, they won it. That the numbers come out there.”

Lewis clarified that they don’t try to change the results; they simply rig it or shut it down altogether.

Saletan said Trump likely learned from 2020 and now knows to first control the counting of the votes.

“This is a constitutional nightmare,” said Saletan.

“That’s what Trump is trying to do: Go and use Tulsi Gabbard and others to claim that the 2020 election was stolen by the other side. So, now ‘my people’ are going to go in and make sure we do this clean. It’s going to be a fair election. How do we know it’s fair? Cuz we’re going to win it,” he added.

It made Lewis wonder: “If Tulsi Gabbard can go in last week and seize the 2020 ballots, she could presumably go in this November and seize the 2026 ballots.”

A Close Examination: Trump, Gabbard, and the Erosion of Democratic Norms

In an era teeming with political subterfuges and attempts to undermine democratic institutions, the latest episode involving Donald Trump and Tulsi Gabbard marks a chilling escalation in the assault on electoral integrity in the United States. The article under scrutiny, detailing a conversation between Will Saletan and Matt Lewis, unveils a sinister plot to overturn U.S. election results, a scheme bearing the hallmark of authoritarian maneuvers rather than actions befitting a democratic nation.

Trump’s Playbook: A Dictator in Disguise?

First and foremost, it’s crucial to underscore the gravity of Saletan’s assertion: True dictators don’t wait for elections; they preemptively seize power. This premise sets the stage for a comprehensive analysis of Trump’s tactics, which alarmingly resemble those of a would-be autocrat rather than a former president of a democratic republic. The involvement of Tulsi Gabbard, ostensibly acting under Trump’s behest, in an FBI raid to seize ballots, is not just an egregious breach of protocol but a stark manifestation of the lengths to which Trump is willing to go to subvert the electoral process.

The Alarming Role of Tulsi Gabbard

The article delineates a concerning narrative about Tulsi Gabbard, the Director of National Intelligence, who ostensibly acted as Trump’s emissary in this dubious endeavor. Gabbard’s presence in an evidence truck during the raid, as reported, raises serious questions about the abuse of power and the weaponization of intelligence services for political gain. The shifting explanations for her involvement only serve to deepen the suspicion surrounding the motives and actions of those involved.

Saletan’s analysis is particularly noteworthy for its clarity and the dire implications it draws attention to. The potential for manipulating the custody of ballots – and by extension, the outcome of elections – poses a “constitutional nightmare” that threatens the very foundations of American democracy.

The Specter of Future Elections

The dialogue between Saletan and Lewis further illuminates the long-term objectives behind this raid. It’s not merely about contesting past election results; it’s a calculated move aimed at undermining future elections. The audacity to seize ballots under dubious pretenses, as Saletan suggests, is indicative of a strategy to control the narrative and, ultimately, the result of future electoral contests.

The comparison to real dictators, such as Nicolás Maduro, serves as a poignant reminder of the slippery slope the United States finds itself on. The manipulation of electoral processes to ensure a predetermined outcome is a tactic commonly associated with authoritarian regimes, not democracies.

A Constitutional Nightmare Unfolding

In essence, what Saletan and Lewis’s conversation reveals is a scenario that should alarm every advocate of democratic governance. The actions of Trump and Gabbard, as described, not only breach ethical and legal standards but also erode public trust in the electoral process.

The notion that elections can be “fair” only if a particular side wins, as jestingly suggested by Saletan, is antithetical to the principles of democracy. It underscores a Machiavellian approach to governance where the ends justify the means, regardless of the damage inflicted upon democratic norms and institutions.

Conclusion: A Call to Vigilance

This analysis isn’t merely an academic exercise; it’s a clarion call for vigilance and action. The events described, and the implications thereof, highlight a concerted effort to undermine the electoral process in the United States. It’s a reminder that the preservation of democracy requires constant vigilance and an unwavering commitment to safeguarding the integrity of elections.

In conclusion, the article sheds light on a disturbing episode that should serve as a wake-up call for all who cherish democratic values. The involvement of high-ranking officials in schemes that threaten to distort electoral outcomes is not only reprehensible but should be met with the fullest force of accountability. As we move forward, it’s imperative that such attempts to subvert democracy are not only exposed but decisively thwarted.
