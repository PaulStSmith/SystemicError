Dr Phil faces 'total collapse' after MAGA alignment backfires massively: report

Dr. Phil McGraw’s attempt to transition from daytime television personality to a politically aligned media mogul during Donald Trump’s second presidential term has been marked by spectacular failure and financial turmoil, according to a new report.

Since the start of Trump’s presidency, ex-psychologist and famed TV doctor McGraw positioned himself as an active participant in the administration. He became a firsthand witness to ICE raids, a critic of protests, and an ever-present volunteer for the executive branch — attending events like RFK Jr.’s swearing-in and appearing with Trump after the Texas flooding.

But his media venture, Merit Street Media, launched in 2024 with grand ambitions and alignment with MAGA media, has been crumbling since, Slate reported Tuesday. McGraw declared at the time, “American families and our core values are under attack. Together we are going to stand strong and fight for the very soul and sanity of America.”

However, the network quickly unraveled. Within months of launching, Merit Street Media experienced multiple setbacks.

Dozens of employees have been laid off as it averaged just 27,000 weekly viewers, broadcasting partnerships floundered and, in July, it filed for bankruptcy.

It also got hit by a lawsuit from distributor Trinity Broadcasting Network accusing McGraw of fraud and misrepresentation, Slate reported.

“A total collapse finally kicked off this summer,” Slate reported. “In late June, Dr. Phil Primetime went on another “summer hiatus,” and 40 more Merit Street Media staffers were laid off.”

Undeterred, McGraw quickly launched Envoy Media Company with Steve Harvey, proposing a “citizen journalism” platform. However, this move was viewed skeptically, with Professional Bull Riders — which claims Merit Street Media owes it $3.5 million — accusing him of orchestrating the bankruptcy to avoid paying debts, Slate reported.

McGraw’s transformation from a relatively neutral TV personality to a politically charged media figure seems to have backfired, Slate reported. As the “anti-woke” British outlet UnHerd noted, his “reinvention as a culture warrior isn’t working.” There are already numerous MAGA-aligned media personalities, and McGraw’s approach lacks the authenticity of those who have been consistently politically engaged, Slate reported.

“McGraw decries politics in one breath, then repeatedly pops up at the White House and films videos about ‘Cowardly Democrats’ who ‘Spit in the Face of All Texans,” Slate reported.

“How’s that workin’ out for ya?”

Our Analysis:

Dr. Phil’s Political Ambitions: A Spectacular Flop

Dr. Phil McGraw’s audacious leap from daytime TV guru to a would-be titan of the MAGA media landscape is nothing short of a spectacular faceplant. The man who once dished out advice on family squabbles and personal dilemmas with a folksy charm has found himself embroiled in a quagmire of financial woes and credibility crises. His foray into the political arena, aligned with Donald Trump’s second presidential term, has been a disaster that would make for a juicy episode on his own show—if only it weren’t so pitifully tragic.

The Fall from Grace

McGraw’s transition into politics and media moguldom has been marked by a series of missteps and miscalculations that can only be described as jaw-dropping. His venture, Merit Street Media, launched with the bold claim of fighting for the “soul and sanity of America,” has instead limped along, hemorrhaging staff and credibility alike. Averaging a paltry 27,000 weekly viewers, it’s a wonder the network managed to stay afloat as long as it did before declaring bankruptcy. This isn’t just a failure; it’s an implosion of epic proportions.

A Litany of Errors

McGraw’s attempt to rebrand himself as a political heavyweight and cultural warrior is as laughable as it is lamentable. Aligning with MAGA media and diving headfirst into the administration’s activities, he seemed to believe his own press a bit too readily. His presence at ICE raids and criticism of protests not only alienated a significant portion of his erstwhile audience but also highlighted a staggering lack of self-awareness. The subsequent lawsuit for fraud and misrepresentation is just the cherry on top of a disastrous sundae of poor judgment and even poorer execution.

The Misguided Venture of Envoy Media Company

Undeterred by the catastrophic collapse of Merit Street Media, McGraw’s pivot to launch Envoy Media Company with Steve Harvey, under the guise of “citizen journalism,” smacks of desperation. This endeavor, viewed with rightful skepticism, appears to be nothing more than a thinly veiled attempt to sidestep financial obligations. The accusation of orchestrating bankruptcy to dodge debts is particularly damning, casting a long shadow over McGraw’s already tarnished reputation.

A Lack of Authenticity

The crux of McGraw’s failure lies in his jarring lack of authenticity. Entering the political fray with no real political pedigree, his flip-flopping and opportunistic appearances smack of insincerity. Slate’s report underscores a critical misstep in McGraw’s strategy—assuming that a politically charged media venture could succeed on bluster and bravado alone, without the genuine conviction that underpins the efforts of more established political commentators.

Conclusion: A Cautionary Tale

Dr. Phil McGraw’s foray into political commentary and media ownership is not just a failure; it’s a cautionary tale for celebrities eyeing the political arena. It’s a stark reminder that political commentary and media influence require a foundation of genuine belief and consistent engagement, not just the whims of fame seeking a new outlet. McGraw’s spectacular fall from grace serves as a painful lesson that in the world of politics and media, authenticity and credibility are currency—and once bankrupt, are nearly impossible to regain.

“How’s that workin’ out for ya?” Indeed, it seems not too well.
