'It was a signal': Stephen Miller statement seen as clue for ICE's next big target

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosSeptember 27, 20255 min read

White House deputy chief of staff for policy Stephen Miller attends the annual White House Easter Egg Roll with his family, including his wife, Katie Miller, left, on the South Lawn of the White House in Washington, D.C., U.S., April 21, 2025. REUTERS/Leah Millis

Steve Bannon, a longtime ally of President Donald Trump and his chief of staff Stephen Miller, reportedly suggests Miller’s statement Thursday was a clue for ICE’s next big target.

Bannon said that Trump and his administration would use the shooting at a Dallas ICE facility as “the hook to unleash law enforcement on liberal immigration groups by linking political violence to anti-ICE rhetoric. This is going to get ugly,” according to Greg Sargent of The New Republic.

“Take all that together, and it’s likely the administration is gearing up to use this shooting—and other alleged violence against ICE—as a pretext to launch precisely that crackdown on the liberal-left,” Sargent writes. “This would work as follows: The administration would seek to investigate liberal or left-leaning groups—either criminally or through other agencies like the IRS—if their rhetoric or other activities can be somehow blamed for violence and hostility toward ICE.”

Following the shooting in Dallas at an ICE facility that left one detainee dead and two others critically injured, Trump, Miller, and Vice President JD Vance all pushed to blame “anti-ICE” rhetoric on the “radical left” and “Antifa.”

Trump posted this on Truth Social after the shooting:

“This violence is the result of the Radical Left Democrats constantly demonizing Law Enforcement, calling for ICE to be demolished, and comparing ICE Officers to ‘Nazis.’”

Trump’s executive order designating Antifa a terrorist organization and reports that the FBI could label transgender people or groups “Nihilistic Violent Extremists” are “game changers,” Bannon says.

“Miller specifically said Thursday in the Oval Office that ‘calling ICE is ‘authoritarian’ is incitement to violence, I asked Bannon if he thinks this means law enforcement should/will now investigate groups who call ICE ‘authoritarian.’ ‘Yes,’ Bannon said. ‘Miller is correct—more importantly he’s in charge,’” Sargent writes.

“This is a very historic and significant day,” Miller said. “This is the first time in American history that there is an all-government effort to dismantle left-wing terrorism, to dismantle Antifa, to dismantle violence and terrorism.”

Potential investigations and prosecutions against these groups are “designed to achieve Trump/Stephen Miller’s goals” and that might not be easy for Trump, who is trying to lay out the groundwork for potential litigation.

“The threat to do it is itself a form of intimidation. It’s intended to chill lawful political expression,” Jameel Jaffer, executive director of the Knight First Amendment Institute, told Sargent.

Sargent said of Miller’s latest comment, “it was a signal” about the next target.

Our Analysis:

Analysis: The Strategic Weaponization of Law Enforcement Against Political Dissidents

The recent comments by Steve Bannon, a notorious figure closely aligned with the darkest corners of Trump’s administration, illuminate a chilling strategy aimed at stifling dissent under the guise of national security. Bannon’s interpretation of Stephen Miller’s statements, as reported by Greg Sargent of The New Republic, reveals a calculated plan to leverage a tragic shooting at a Dallas ICE facility as a pretext to launch a crackdown on liberal and left-leaning groups. This strategy exemplifies a disturbing trend of weaponizing law enforcement against political adversaries, a tactic that should alarm any observer of democratic norms.

Manipulating Tragedy for Political Gain

The shooting incident, which resulted in one detainee’s death and critical injuries to others, is being twisted into a narrative designed to vilify and suppress voices critical of ICE and its practices. Trump, Miller, and Vice President JD Vance’s immediate pivot to blame “anti-ICE” rhetoric on the “radical left” and “Antifa” is not just opportunistic; it’s a calculated move to associate legitimate political dissent with acts of violence.

Trump’s post-shooting comments on Truth Social, calling for the demonization of law enforcement by “Radical Left Democrats,” and his alarmist comparison of ICE officers to Nazis, are nothing short of fearmongering designed to delegitimize criticism of ICE. By painting a broad brush over the entire left as inciters of violence, the administration aims to silence its critics through intimidation.

The Dangerous Precedent of Labeling Dissent as Terrorism

The executive order designating Antifa a terrorist organization, coupled with Bannon’s revelation that transgender groups could be labeled as “Nihilistic Violent Extremists,” signals a dangerous shift in how the government views dissent. It’s a stark reminder of how easily the tools of law enforcement can be twisted to serve the political interests of those in power, rather than the principles of justice and equity.

Stephen Miller’s assertion that calling ICE ‘authoritarian’ constitutes incitement to violence is not only an egregious misinterpretation of free speech but also a clear indication that the administration is willing to use any means necessary to quash opposition. Bannon’s confirmation that law enforcement could investigate groups critical of ICE underlines the administration’s intent to leverage the full weight of the government against its critics.

The Threat to Free Expression

Jameel Jaffer’s comments highlight the real danger of these tactics: the intimidation and chilling of lawful political expression. The potential investigations and prosecutions against liberal and left-leaning groups are not about justice; they are about consolidating power by instilling fear among those who dare to speak out.

Miller’s declaration of an “all-government effort to dismantle left-wing terrorism” is a historic overreach that threatens the very foundations of free expression and assembly. By conflating criticism with terrorism, the administration sets a dangerous precedent that could have far-reaching implications for political discourse in the United States.

Conclusion: A Call to Vigilance

The strategy outlined by Bannon and implicitly endorsed by Miller and Trump is a stark reminder of the lengths to which this administration is willing to go to suppress dissent. It’s a Machiavellian maneuver that seeks to exploit tragedy for political gain, weaponizing law enforcement against those who challenge the status quo.

This calculated attempt to silence opposition under the guise of national security is not just an attack on the left; it’s an assault on the democratic principles that allow for a vibrant and robust political debate. As observers and participants in this democracy, it is our duty to remain vigilant against such tactics and to defend the rights of all individuals to express dissent without fear of government retribution. The weaponization of law enforcement against political dissidents is a systemic error that must be corrected to preserve the integrity of our democratic institutions.
