'Poor lil incel': Charlie Kirk slammed after blaming Jewish Dems for CO terror attack

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosJune 4, 20252 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Turning Point USA’s Charlie Kirk on March 6, 2025 (Sua Sponte Photography/Shutterstock.com)

At least 12 people were injured in Boulder, Colorado when, on Sunday, June 1, a group of demonstrators were attacked with Molotov cocktails. The demonstrators were calling for the release of Israelis being held hostage by Hamas terrorists in Gaza.

Turning Point USA's Charlie Kirk is drawing a great deal of criticism for his response to the attack. On his radio show, the far-right MAGA Republican argued that Americans Jews are encouraging antisemitic violence by voting for Democrats.

Kirk told listeners, "To the Jews of America: How are you still voting Democrat, and why are you still giving money to the institutions and the charities and the political parties that hate you and want you dead? My tolerance with the American Jews that are giving money and playing footsie with the American left is nothing. In fact, my patience has completely run out. So you want the borders to stay wide open so more young Jews could be hunted down in the streets? And I have to ask the Jews of America: Do you have a death wish?"

Kirk's comments are getting a lot of responses on X, formerly Twitter.

X user Matthew C. posted, "It's because of Candace Owens and Nick Fuentes and Dave Smith and Theo Von and Ian Carroll and Jake Shields and Charlie's good friend Tucker Qatarlson. It's because of Charlie going out of his way to defend Glenn Greenwald, a man who has spread more Blood Libel than anyone."

Another X user, Tony Sabra, wrote, "Are you paying attention to the news in the USA? FEMA and many other organizations having thousands fired when they are needed. This is all the GOP saving money for the rich. Why not ask why so many Republicans left the GOP from 2016 to present?"

Lynn Carter tweeted, in all caps, "BECAUSE OF YOUR ALTRIGHT NAZI FRIENDS."

@Penipulation said of Kirk, "Poor lil incel. Like tRump, he'll spew anything when he doesn't understand."
