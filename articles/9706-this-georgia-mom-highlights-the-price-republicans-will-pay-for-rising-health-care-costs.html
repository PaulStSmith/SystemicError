This Georgia mom highlights the price Republicans will pay for rising health care costs

It has taken some time, but the American public is finally beginning to understand what Republicans in Washington are doing to our nation’s health care system, including the Affordable Care Act that now covers an estimated 24 million people.

“When the tax credits expire this year my own adult children’s insurance premiums for 2026 are going to DOUBLE,” one North Georgia mother exclaimed this week on social media. “Not a single Republican in leadership talked to us about this or has given us a plan to help Americans deal with their health insurance premiums DOUBLING!!!

“WE HAVE TO DO SOMETHING ABOUT THE ABSOLUTELY INSANE COST OF INSURANCE FOR AMERICANS,” she concluded, in all capital letters.

That exasperated mother is Marjorie Taylor Greene, the Republican representing Georgia’s 14th Congressional District. And her adult children are not the only ones experiencing sticker shock because tax credits are expiring at the end of this year.

For example: “On average, a 60-year-old couple making $85,000 would see yearly premium payments rise by over $22,600 in 2026, after accounting for an annual premium increase of 18 percent,” according to health care analysts at KFF. “This would bring the cost of a benchmark plan to about a quarter of this couple’s annual income.”

If you’re wondering what the government shutdown is all about, this explains a lot of it. Democrats have been warning for months that the ACA subsidies that keep health insurance affordable will expire at the end of the year without congressional action, but as Greene notes in her typically flamboyant fashion, Republican leaders have done nothing to address the problem.

Democrats say that if the subsidies are reinstated, they’ll provide enough Democratic votes in the Senate to fund the government. In response, Republicans say nothing.

Current projections are that without such action, 450,000 Georgians will be forced to drop their insurance coverage through the ACA because they can no longer afford it, and that in turn will send ripples throughout the state’s health care infrastructure.

According to a second analysis, this one by the Robert Wood Johnson Foundation, that would mean a loss of $1.6 billion in revenue to hospitals and other health-delivery systems in Georgia, with particular impact on struggling rural hospitals.

But if you believe Republican leadership in Washington, none of this is happening. They continue to claim that Democrats are demanding free health care for illegal immigrants as their price for reopening the government, while Democrats continue to insist that is not true.

Democrats point out, correctly, that longstanding federal law bars illegal immigrants from coverage under Medicaid, Medicare and the Affordable Care Act, and Democrats say they have no intention of trying to change that.

Essentially, Republicans are caught in a trap of their own making. Polls continue to show stronger and stronger support for the Affordable Care Act, also known as Obamacare. Two-thirds of Americans now say they support the program, compared to just 33 percent who are still opposed. Last month, a KFF poll found that 78 percent of Americans support extending the tax credit subsidies, while just 22 percent oppose it.

That 22 percent, however, is largely the MAGA base. With Republicans in charge of the House, Senate and White House, any move to extend the Obamacare subsidies would have to come with Republican approval and Republican votes. That would be seen as a deep, intolerable act of betrayal by the GOP base, which has been taught that Obamacare is the work of the devil.

So they do nothing, and doing nothing will come with a price, both for Americans who will lose their health insurance and for the politicians who allowed it to happen.

Our Analysis:

The Hypocrisy of Political Inaction: A Closer Look at the GOP’s Health Care Debacle

In a recent revelation, the American public has been given a front-row seat to the unfolding drama orchestrated by Republicans in Washington regarding the nation’s health care system, specifically the Affordable Care Act (ACA). The act, which provides coverage for an estimated 24 million people, is under threat as tax credits are set to expire by the end of the year. This looming crisis has prompted outrage, even from within Republican ranks, highlighting a dire need for action that remains unaddressed by GOP leadership.

Marjorie Taylor Greene’s Social Media Outburst: A Symptom of a Larger Problem

The frustration of the American people found an unlikely spokesperson in Marjorie Taylor Greene, a Republican representing Georgia’s 14th Congressional District. Greene’s social media outburst about the expected doubling of her adult children’s insurance premiums by 2026 serves as a stark illustration of the widespread impact of the GOP’s inaction. Her surprise and anger mirror the sentiments of many Americans facing similar predicaments. Yet, it’s ironic, if not outright hypocritical, that a vocal critic of the ACA is now lamenting the consequences of policies she and her party have supported or failed to act upon.

The Staggering Cost of Inaction

The consequences of allowing the tax credits to expire are nothing short of catastrophic. KFF health care analysts have painted a bleak picture, with a 60-year-old couple making $85,000 potentially seeing their yearly premium payments rise by over $22,600 in 2026. This would allocate a quarter of this couple’s annual income to health insurance alone. Such a drastic increase underscores the urgency of the situation, a sentiment seemingly lost on Republican leaders.

A Government Shutdown Over Health Care

The current government shutdown sheds light on the core of this issue. Democrats have long warned about the expiration of ACA subsidies, ready to provide enough votes in the Senate to fund the government if these subsidies are reinstated. Republicans, however, have remained eerily silent, seemingly indifferent to the plight of 450,000 Georgians who stand to lose their insurance coverage through the ACA. This silence extends to the potential loss of $1.6 billion in revenue to Georgia’s health-delivery systems, with rural hospitals facing the brunt of the impact.

Republican Leadership: Denial and Distraction

Amidst this crisis, Republican leadership clings to misleading narratives, accusing Democrats of demanding free health care for illegal immigrants as a condition for reopening the government. This claim not only distracts from the issue at hand but also spreads false information, as federal law explicitly bars illegal immigrants from coverage under Medicaid, Medicare, and the ACA.

The Political Quagmire and Public Opinion

Republicans find themselves in a political quagmire of their own making. Public support for the ACA has grown significantly, with two-thirds of Americans now in favor of the program. A KFF poll further revealed that 78 percent of Americans support extending the tax credit subsidies. Yet, the GOP base, largely consisting of MAGA supporters, remains vehemently opposed. Fearful of backlash, Republican leaders are paralyzed, choosing inaction over the potential political fallout.

Conclusion: The Price of Political Cowardice

The GOP’s failure to address the health care crisis is a betrayal of American values and the needs of its citizens. As insurance premiums skyrocket and millions risk losing coverage, the consequences of political cowardice become increasingly clear. It’s not just about the financial toll on American families; it’s about the erosion of trust in a system that seems more concerned with political gamesmanship than the welfare of its people. The GOP must confront the reality of its choices and the lasting impact on American health care, lest they face the judgment of those they’ve sworn to serve. The time for action is now; the health and well-being of millions depend on it.
