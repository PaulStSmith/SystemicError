Author Michael Wolff claimed Monday that President Donald Trump is growing increasingly paranoid following a political rift with tech billionaire Elon Musk, who has positioned Vice President JD Vance as a major rival to Trump’s authority.

Wolff, known for chronicling Trump’s presidency in his book Fire and Fury, said during a Daily Beast podcast that Musk's move shook Trump, intensifying his mistrust of Vance.

Last week, Musk, who recently exited the Trump administration, publicly called for Trump’s removal from office and endorsed Vance as his replacement.

In his comments on the podcast Monday, Wolff went on to say that Trump has never been able to fully trust Vance, who he views as an unconventional Republican with most of his backing coming from Silicon Valley circles.

Trump's decision to select Vance as his vice president, Wolff noted, was influenced by Musk — who allegedly tied his substantial financial support to Vance’s nomination.

In a post on the social platform X following Musk's attacks against Trump last week, Vance said: “I’m the vice president to President Trump. My loyalties are always going to be with the president.

“I think it’s a huge mistake for (Musk) to go after the president like that," he added.

But Wolff said it’s unclear whether Vance’s statement that he stands with the president will be enough to earn Trump’s trust following this fallout.

Last week, the previously friendly relationship between Trump and Musk took a sharp turn, evolving into a widely publicized clash following Musk's departure from the government. Tensions escalated when the Tesla CEO slammed Trump’s flagship legislative effort, the "One Big Beautiful Bill Act," calling it a “disgusting abomination” on his social media site, X.

In response, Trump voiced his frustration and warned that he might cancel federal contracts with Musk’s companies, including Tesla and SpaceX, both of which benefit significantly from government funding.

Tensions rose further on Thursday when Musk, in a now-deleted post on X, claimed that Trump’s name appears in government documents connected to convicted child predator Jeffrey Epstein. He suggested this could be the reason why some of the documents have yet to be released to the public.

Our Analysis:

The Trump-Musk-Vance Triad: A Political Drama

In the latest episode of what might as well be dubbed The Real Politicians of America, Michael Wolff throws a spotlight on an increasingly paranoid President Donald Trump, caught in a political whirlwind with tech magnate Elon Musk and Vice President JD Vance. Wolff, who has made a career out of chronicling the tumultuous Trump administration in works like Fire and Fury, reveals on a Daily Beast podcast the deepening rift between these powerful figures. Let's dissect the elements of this political soap opera, where loyalty, power, and paranoia intertwine.

The Musk Effect

Elon Musk, once a member of Trump's administration, has taken a dramatic exit, not just stepping back but leaping into the political fray with a call for Trump's ousting. Musk's endorsement of Vance as a preferable alternative is akin to igniting a powder keg beneath Trump's presidency. Musk's departure and subsequent denouncement of Trump are not merely a personal betrayal in Trump's eyes; they represent a seismic shift in the political landscape. Musk's actions have not only intensified Trump’s inherent mistrust of Vance but have also spotlighted Vance as an unconventional Republican, buoyed by Silicon Valley rather than traditional GOP support.

Vance: The Loyal Lieutenant?

Vance's response to Musk's barrage against Trump was textbook loyalty — at least on the surface. Declaring his allegiance to Trump, Vance positions himself as the steadfast vice president. Yet, Wolff questions whether this avowal of loyalty is sufficient to bridge the chasm of mistrust that has opened between Trump and Vance. In the intricate dance of political alliances, words are but a veneer over the murky depths of realpolitik.

The Specter of Paranoia

Trump's reaction to Musk's critique and the subsequent public spat is emblematic of a deeper paranoia. Musk’s scathing assessment of Trump's "One Big Beautiful Bill Act" as a "disgusting abomination" and the threat of canceling federal contracts with Musk’s companies underscore a volatile relationship that has implications far beyond personal grievances. It's a battle of titans, with government contracts and legislative agendas hanging in the balance.

The Epstein Allegation: A Wild Card

Musk's insinuation connecting Trump to Jeffrey Epstein via government documents is a low blow in the political boxing ring. This allegation, whether baseless or not, adds a layer of intrigue and danger to the already fraught relationship between Trump and Musk. It's a reminder of the potent mix of politics, power, and scandal that defines this era.

Conclusion: A Political Maelstrom

The saga of Trump, Musk, and Vance is a microcosm of broader political turmoil. It's a tale of shifting alliances, where trust is ephemeral and loyalty is contingent on the ever-changing winds of political fortune. In this narrative, paranoia is not a symptom but a survival mechanism. The only certainty in this unfolding drama is the relentless pursuit of power, with each player maneuvering for advantage in the unforgiving arena of public opinion and political influence.

As this story develops, one thing remains clear: In the high-stakes game of politics, today's ally can become tomorrow's adversary with a single tweet.
