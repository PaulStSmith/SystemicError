Trump is being 'played for a fool': conservative columnist

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosMay 21, 20255 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Our Analysis:Trump's Illusion of Diplomacy: A Critical Analysis

U.S. President Donald Trump gestures as he attends the National Republican Congressional Committee (NRCC) dinner at the National Building Museum in Washington, D.C., U.S., April 8, 2025. REUTERS/Nathan Howard

President Donald Trump is being "played by a fool" in negotiations over the Russian invasion of Ukraine, conservative columnist Max Boot warned in The Washington Post — and he has no idea how effortlessly he's being used.

Trump has been tough to pin down on the Ukraine war. He has spent much of his political career sympathetic to Russia's Vladimir Putin after his government interfered in the 2016 presidential election, and has frequently suggested Ukraine should give up territory to appease Russian aggression, but has more recently acted aware that Russia isn't interested in peace — yet, Boot wrote, he keeps giving Putin far more chances than he is entitled to.

"President Donald Trump is not known for being patient or forgiving with those who defy his will," wrote Boot. "In just the past few days, he has lashed out at New York Attorney General Letitia James, Bruce Springsteen, Beyoncé and former FBI director James B. Comey — all of whom have incurred his wrath for various reasons. Yet when it comes to dealing with Russian dictator Vladimir Putin — who continues to sabotage Trump’s showcase efforts to end the war in Ukraine — the president seems to have an endless supply of patience, goodwill and understanding."

Russia has rejected all ceasefire proposals Ukraine has agreed to, making clear they will not stop until the world recognizes their annexation of Ukrainian territory. But despite Trump saying he was "very angry" with Putin sabotaging peace talks, "that was 50 days ago, and there is still no indication of any additional sanctions. Instead, on Monday, the U.S. president had a two-hour phone call with his Russian counterpart that failed to yield any appreciable progress." And Trump's reaction was to brag to the public that “Russia and Ukraine will immediately start negotiations toward a Ceasefire and, more importantly, an END to the War.”

Trump, Boot noted, had repeatedly promised to end the war on his first day in office. Now, "Behind closed doors, the Wall Street Journal reports, Trump is kvetching that ending wars is harder than it seems. (Who knew?)" — but he seems unwilling to show the same anger toward Putin he does to any domestic political critic who annoys him.

"While Trump’s lack of success in peacemaking might not doom Ukraine, it certainly dispels the president’s pretensions to being a world-class dealmaker," Boot concluded. "In the face of Russian intransigence, he keeps violating the prime rule of successful negotiating: You must apply leverage. Putin is playing him for a fool, and Trump doesn’t even seem to realize it."

Our Analysis:

Trump's Illusion of Diplomacy: A Critical Analysis

In an era where political maneuvers are scrutinized more than ever, the recent observations by Max Boot in The Washington Post shed light on a concerning pattern of naivety and inefficacy emanating from the highest office in the United States. The subject at hand is President Donald Trump's handling of the Russian invasion of Ukraine, a geopolitical crisis that not only tests the mettle of international diplomacy but also the resolve and acumen of those in leadership positions. Boot's analysis, while scathing, unveils a series of missteps and miscalculations by Trump that warrants a deeper examination.

Trump's Perplexing Patience with Putin

At the heart of Boot's critique is the paradox of Trump's disposition towards Vladimir Putin. Trump, a figure known for his quick temper and swift retribution towards critics and adversaries alike, displays an uncharacteristic patience and leniency towards the Russian dictator. This is particularly baffling given Putin's blatant undermining of Trump's efforts to resolve the conflict in Ukraine. The inconsistency in Trump's behavior raises questions about his understanding of the dynamics at play and his ability to effectively counter Putin's maneuvers.

The Mirage of Diplomatic Success

Trump has vocally expressed his intent to end the war in Ukraine, a promise that remains unfulfilled and appears increasingly illusory. Despite public declarations of progress and imminent negotiations, the reality on the ground tells a different story. Russia's continued rejection of ceasefire proposals and the absence of any significant move towards peace negotiations expose a glaring gap between Trump's rhetoric and the tangible outcomes. The president's claim of an impending ceasefire, following a lengthy conversation with Putin, not only lacks credibility but also underscores a failure to recognize or admit the futility of his approach.

A Strategy Marred by Inaction

One of the critical points Boot makes is Trump's apparent reluctance to leverage the tools at his disposal. The absence of additional sanctions or any concrete actions to pressure Russia reveals a fundamental misunderstanding of negotiation dynamics. Successful diplomacy, especially in the face of aggression, requires the astute application of leverage. Trump's failure to escalate consequences for Russia's intransigence suggests a disconnect from this reality, casting doubt on his prowess as a dealmaker and leader on the world stage.

Conclusion: A Leadership Deficit

Boot's analysis, while focused on Trump's handling of the Ukraine crisis, points to a broader issue of leadership and strategic acumen. The president's inability to effectively navigate this complex international challenge not only jeopardizes the prospects for peace in Ukraine but also diminishes the United States' standing and influence on the global stage. The portrayal of Trump as a leader being unwittingly manipulated by Putin is a damning indictment of his tenure, highlighting a profound misjudgment and incapacity to safeguard and promote the interests of his country and its allies.

In conclusion, the situation as depicted by Boot is not merely a diplomatic failure; it is a stark reminder of the critical importance of leadership that is informed, decisive, and grounded in a clear understanding of international relations. As the crisis in Ukraine continues to unfold, the need for such leadership has never been more apparent.
