The Audacity of Corruption: Trump’s IRS “Settlement” and the Institutionalization of Lawlessness

Who Holds the Power?

The recent IRS “settlement” under the Trump administration, orchestrated by Acting Attorney General Todd Blanch and his cronies, is not just a scandal—it’s a masterclass in the abuse of power. The move, which sets up a $1.776 billion fund ostensibly for victims of governmental weaponization, actually serves as a slush fund for Trump loyalists and insurrectionists. This fund, misleadingly dubbed the “Anti-Weaponization Fund,” is controlled by Blanch and is answerable to virtually no one, bypassing both congressional oversight and public accountability.

Decisions and Enablers

Blanch, appointed by Trump, steers this ship directly into the iceberg. He has the authority to appoint a commission to distribute these funds and Trump holds the power to dismiss any dissenting members. The decision to create a perpetual fund that rewards past and potentially future misconduct showcases a blatant disregard for legal norms and sets a dangerous precedent. This isn’t just about rewarding those charged in the Capitol riots; it’s about incentivizing future lawlessness under the guise of fighting against governmental overreach.

Misdirection and Propaganda

Calling this corrupt endeavor an “Anti-Weaponization Fund” is a stroke of Orwellian genius. It redirects the narrative from the corruption of the fund itself to a fictitious war against a politicized government. The administration’s narrative is clear: they are the victims, and they are fighting back. In reality, this fund is a weapon itself, aimed at the heart of democratic accountability and rule of law. It’s a cunning misdirection, one that not only obscures the true nature of the fund but also paints any opposition as a defense of government weaponization against citizens.

A Pattern of Impunity

This scandal is part of a broader, more disturbing pattern in Trump’s presidency: the erosion of institutional integrity and a blatant move towards autocracy. The DOJ’s decision to bar any pursuit of tax claims or legal actions against Trump or his entities forever cements this pattern of impunity. It’s a clear message that not only will corrupt activities be financed, but they will also be legally untouchable. The rule of law has been replaced by rule by law—where laws are manipulated to serve those in power.

Systemic Decay and the Way Forward

The “Anti-Weaponization Fund” is not merely a scandal; it is a symptom and a catalyst of the decay of American democracy. It’s a fund that effectively institutionalizes corruption and sets a price on loyalty and lawlessness. This isn’t just about Trump or Blanch; it’s about a failure of our entire system to check the power of those at the top. As we approach the 250th anniversary of the Republic, the real crisis isn’t external threats but the internal subversion of our democratic values.

Moving forward, the challenge will be not only to address this egregious abuse of power but to restore faith in a system that allows such abuses to occur. Transparency, accountability, and a recommitment to democratic principles are essential. However, as long as figures like Trump and Blanch manipulate the system unchecked, the path forward remains perilously unclear. The first step is recognizing this not as an isolated incident but as a deliberate strategy to undermine the very foundations of democratic governance.
