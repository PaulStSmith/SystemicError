Rep. Thomas Massie (R-KY), a leading, bipartisan advocate in the U.S. House of Representatives for forcing the Department of Justice to release all of its unredacted files related to late convicted sex offender Jeffrey Epstein, says that President Donald Trump’s push to investigate Democrats allegedly involved with Epstein is one big ruse, Politico reports.

Speaking to ABC’s Jonathan Karl on “This Week” on Sunday, Massie says the Justice Department’s investigation into Epstein’s ties to notable Democrats coming from Trump after the release of explosive emails and documents mentioning him is “a last-ditch effort to prevent the release of the Epstein files.”

“If they have ongoing investigations in certain areas, those documents can’t be released,” Massie says. “So, this might be a big smokescreen, these investigations, to open a bunch of them to, as a last-ditch effort to prevent the release of the Epstein files.”

Explosive emails referencing Trump from Epstein’s private correspondence have been publicly released by the House Oversight Committee. These emails were obtained from Epstein’s estate as part of a congressional probe into the federal investigation of Epstein.

In one of those emails, an April 2011 exchange with Epstein’s convicted sex trafficker co-conspirator Ghislaine Maxwell, Epstein referred to Trump as the “dog that hasn’t barked,” noting that a victim (later identified by Republicans as Virginia Giuffre) “spent hours at my house with him , he has never once been mentioned” in the ongoing legal issues.”

Upon the release of these emails, Trump directed his Attorney General Pam Bondi to begin investigations into former President Bill Clinton, among other Democrats, writing on Truth Social, “ “This is another Russia, Russia, Russia Scam, with all arrows pointing to the Democrats.”

On Friday, Bondi promised to pursue the investigation “with urgency and integrity to deliver answers to the American people” after assigning Manhattan U.S. Attorney Jay Clayton to lead the probe, Politico reports.

Although just three of Massie’s Republican colleagues signed on to the discharge petition (Reps. Marjorie Taylor Greene (R-GA), Nancy Mace (R-SC) and Lauren Bobert (R-CO)),Massie says hopes to “secure a veto-proof majority on his legislation, which would require a two-thirds vote in both the House and the Senate.”

Massie says he anticipates “a deluge of Republicans” — perhaps even more than a hundred — to support the bill.”

Massie, Politico reports, says he urged Senate Majority Leader John Thune (R-SD) to “do the right thing” and bring the legislation up for a vote in the Senate.

“Not only the speaker, but the attorney general, the FBI director, and the president himself, and the vice president, they’re taking a big loss this week, because after months of fighting, I am winning this week with Ro Khanna,” Massie says. “We’re forcing this vote, and it’s going to happen.”=

Our Analysis:

The Political Theater of Absurdity: Unmasking the Epstein Files Saga

In a recent revelation that reads more like a script from a House of Cards season discarded for being too outlandish, Rep. Thomas Massie (R-KY) has thrown a rather incendiary accusation into the already blazing fire surrounding the late Jeffrey Epstein’s dark legacy. According to Politico, Massie, a self-proclaimed bipartisan crusader for transparency, alleges that President Donald Trump’s calls for investigations into Democrats linked with Epstein are nothing more than a strategically placed smokescreen.

The Smokescreen Strategy

Massie’s assertion hinges on the notion that by initiating new investigations, the Trump administration seeks to stall the release of the unredacted Epstein files under the guise of ongoing investigative processes. This tactic, as Massie suggests, is a desperate attempt to keep the Epstein skeletons in the proverbial closet. Given the historical penchant of administrations for using the judiciary as a shield or sword as it suits their agenda, this claim, though shocking, fits snugly into the pattern of political maneuvering that prioritizes optics over accountability.

The Trump-Epstein Email Bombshell

Adding fuel to this firestorm are newly publicized emails that tie Trump to Epstein, with one particularly damning exchange from April 2011, where Epstein refers to Trump as the “dog that hasn’t barked.” This metaphor, likely alluding to Trump’s silence or unproblematic stance in Epstein’s sordid affairs, raises eyebrows and questions in equal measure. The unsettling coziness between convicted sex trafficker Ghislaine Maxwell, Epstein, and Trump, as suggested by these communications, paints a picture that many in Trump’s camp would prefer remained buried.

The Counter-Offensive

In what can only be characterized as a tit-for-tat, Trump, never one to take allegations lying down, directed his Attorney General to launch probes into former President Bill Clinton among other Democrats. This move, couched in the rhetoric of exposing the “Russia, Russia, Russia Scam,” smacks of retaliatory politics at its most transparent. Pam Bondi’s promise to pursue this investigation “with urgency and integrity” would be laudable if not for the murky motivations and the shadow of partisanship that looms large over it.

The Legislative Battle and Massie’s Crusade

Massie, undeterred by the political circus, is pushing forward with legislation that seeks to compel the release of the Epstein files. Despite the lukewarm support from his Republican colleagues, with only three signing the discharge petition, Massie’s optimism about securing a “veto-proof majority” borders on the quixotic. Yet, in the context of the current political landscape, where stranger things have happened, Massie’s determination might just pay off, especially if he manages to rally the anticipated “deluge of Republicans.”

Conclusion: A Political Quagmire

The Epstein saga, with its labyrinthine twists and turns, underscores the depths to which political entities will sink to protect their interests and narratives. Massie’s allegations, if proven true, reveal a troubling strategy of obstruction and misdirection at the highest levels of power. The pursuit of truth, especially when it concerns individuals of such high profile and the heinous crimes they are associated with, should transcend partisan politics. The American public deserves transparency, accountability, and justice, not a never-ending cycle of political gambits designed to obfuscate and delay.

As this theatrical performance unfolds, one can only hope that the curtain will finally be lifted on the Epstein files, shedding light on the dark corners of power and influence. Until then, we remain spectators in a political drama that seems to have no end in sight.
