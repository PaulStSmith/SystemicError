Countries forced to 'grin and bare teeth' as 'washed-up' Trump official tries to get clout

The new U.S. ambassador to the United Nations is forcing other countries to “grin and bare their teeth” as he attempts to show his loyalty to the Trump administration amid reported criticism that he’s “washed-up.”

Mike Waltz — still recovering his reputation from the Signalgate scandal, where he added a journalist from The Atlantic to a Signal chat with several Trump appointees discussing an impending military strike — was confirmed just three days before the high-level diplomatic week at the General Assembly, according to Politico on Friday.

“There’s a lot of personal diplomacy involved in New York, and it’s a face-to-face job more so than in a bilateral relationship — and an ambassador’s cachet is their ability to speak for the president,” a former U.S. official with knowledge of the U.S. mission in New York, told Politico. “When you have a washed-up ambassador who was so visibly shunted, other countries will grin and bare their teeth hoping that Waltz can convey messages on their behalf.”

Over the last several days, the former national security advisor has issued a stern warning to Russia following its breaches of NATO airspace and argued against a Palestinian state in his speeches at the U.N. Security Council sessions.

His main job is to represent President Donald Trump, whose speech to the U.N. earlier this week (and triple sabotage claims) left diplomacy experts cringing.

Waltz’s new role was seen as a demotion by some, and it even took months to confirm him, but Trump administration officials reportedly cited that he is a “key player” in Trump’s team. Vice President JD Vance, Secretary of State Marco Rubio and White House spokesperson Karoline Leavitt have all reportedly rallied around him.

“The president still likes Mike Waltz. He wasn’t fired. Security didn’t remove him from the building. He stayed on the executive payroll,” a former Trump administration official told Politico. “Mike Waltz is out of central casting for the administration at the U.N. with his warrior-statesman ethos.”

Our Analysis:

The Waltz of Diplomacy: A Scathing Review

The appointment of Mike Waltz as the U.S. ambassador to the United Nations is a classic example of the Trump administration’s modus operandi: controversy, cronyism, and a blatant disregard for the nuances of international diplomacy. The Politico article detailing Waltz’s rocky start and the challenges he faces serves as a perfect microcosm for the broader dysfunctions within Trump’s foreign policy apparatus.

A “Washed-Up” Warrior in the Diplomatic Arena

The phrase “grin and bare their teeth” paints a vivid picture of the international community’s reaction to Waltz’s appointment. It’s a thinly veiled diplomatic smile, concealing a grimace of discomfort or disdain. This reaction is hardly surprising given Waltz’s recent embroilment in the Signalgate scandal, an episode that reads like a subplot from a poorly written spy novel. His involvement in a clandestine discussion about a military strike, inadvertently exposed to a journalist, would be farcical if it weren’t so deeply concerning.

The article suggests that Waltz’s primary mission is to demonstrate his loyalty to Trump, an endeavor that seems to overshadow the more pressing needs of global diplomacy and multilateral cooperation. His efforts to recover his reputation and assert his relevance at the United Nations come off as a desperate attempt to salvage a career marred by scandal and criticism.

Dubious Support and Questionable Qualifications

The backing of high-profile figures within the Trump administration, including Vice President JD Vance, Secretary of State Marco Rubio, and White House spokesperson Karoline Leavitt, does little to assuage concerns about Waltz’s suitability for the role. Their support is presented as a testament to his importance within Trump’s circle, yet one cannot help but question the criteria for such endorsement. Is it loyalty over competence? The article hints at a troubling possibility: that Waltz’s appointment is less about his diplomatic acumen and more about his alignment with Trump’s political agenda.

Furthermore, the notion that Waltz is “out of central casting” for the Trump administration, with his “warrior-statesman ethos,” raises eyebrows. It reflects a superficial approach to diplomacy, suggesting that image and bravado are valued over substance and expertise. In the complex and nuanced world of international relations, such attributes are hardly sufficient.

The Implications of Misplaced Priorities

Waltz’s stern warnings to Russia and his opposition to a Palestinian state, as highlighted in his speeches at the U.N. Security Council sessions, underscore a confrontational stance that seems more attuned to domestic political posturing than to fostering global cooperation and peace. His role, ostensibly to represent President Trump’s interests, further underscores the administration’s inward-looking and often isolationist approach to foreign policy.

The article, while detailing Waltz’s challenges, inadvertently exposes a deeper, systemic issue within the Trump administration: a fundamental misunderstanding of diplomacy. Diplomacy requires tact, empathy, and the ability to navigate complex international landscapes with finesse — qualities seemingly in short supply.

Conclusion: A Misstep on the Global Stage

In sum, Mike Waltz’s appointment as U.S. ambassador to the United Nations is emblematic of the Trump administration’s flawed approach to diplomacy and international relations. It’s a move that prioritizes loyalty and optics over expertise and integrity. As the world watches, one can only hope that the damage to America’s diplomatic standing can be contained.

In the grand scheme of things, Waltz’s tenure may be a footnote in the annals of diplomatic history. Yet, it is a telling one, indicative of an era where the art of diplomacy is often overshadowed by the theatrics of domestic politics. The global community deserves better.
