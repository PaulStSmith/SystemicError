'Quit now': MAGA freaks out on GOP leader after he blocks Trump from recess appointments

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosAugust 4, 20255 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Our Analysis:The Art of Political Sabotage: Thune’s Masterstroke and MAGA’s Meltdown

Republican Senators Lisa Murkowski, John Thune and Susan Collins (C-SPAN screenshot)

A decision by Senate Majority Leader John Thune (R-SD) to schedule pro forma sessions throughout the August recess which will, in effect, stop Donald Trump from making any recess appointments, has set off a firestorm among the president's MAGA followers.

According to CSPAN's Craig Caplan, "Senate just agreed by unanimous consent at the request of Majority Leader Thune to hold pro forma sessions every 3 days following today’s session and not return for votes until Tuesday, Sept. 2nd. This agreement will block President Trump from making recess appointments."

For Trump's supporters, insult was added to injury because occasional Trump critics, Senators Lisa Murkowski (R-AK) and Susan Collins (R-ME), were sitting behind Thune and smiling as he made the motion.

That led one admirer of the president to post on X, "JUST IN: John Thune blocks President Trump from making recess appointments using pro-forma sessions. RINOs Susan Collins smirks behind him as Lisa Murkowski nods her head in approval. These anti-American people do not have Trump’s back."

That was followed by a wave of outrage with all three GOP lawmakers labeled "Republicans in Name Only" or "RINO" in shorthand.

Rush Limbaugh's brother, David Limbaugh complained, "I know Thune says he has 'institutional concerns' about recess appointments, but I am no longer that concerned about such things when only one party even tries to honor them AND when all the other preceding presidents have done it -- sometimes in great numbers."

Maga Voice wrote, "This is what a sellout Traitor RINO looks like."

Frequent X poster and Trump supporter Paul A. Szypula, added, "Leader John Thune on Saturday refused to adjourn the Senate and allow President Trump to make recess appointments. Susan Collins and Lisa Murkowski were pleased. Everyone who warned you Thune was a RINO was right. Primary Thune in 2028. He’s a traitor."

Bob and Eric Save America podcaster Eric Matheny wrote, "John Thune says Trump can make no recess appointments. Look who are seated behind him - smiling ear to ear. Collins and Murkowski. Thune is a useless RINO!"

"Those faces. Americans who voted overwhelmingly to install President Trump to do the will of the people - we mean nothing to these weak sad pompous “i know whats better for you than you do” individuals. Disgusting that they have this power," Carol Tisdale stated.

Libertarian Mabel Lynn, added, "Thune has no principles, watching him pretend to now is infuriating. We’ve watched this man violate every conservative principle over the last several years, but now, today, he must draw the line. He is a spineless loser and I’m so sick of seeing his face."

"It’s bad enough that Thune has deliberately moved at a snail’s pace to confirm Trump’s nominees. But the fact that both he and Mike Johnson are doing pro forma sessions—which exist entirely to block recess appointments—shows that permanent Washington is deliberately obstructing Trump, his nominees, and his agenda," argued Sean Davis of The Federalist.

Our Analysis:

The Art of Political Sabotage: Thune’s Masterstroke and MAGA’s Meltdown

In a move that has sent shockwaves through the MAGA echo chamber, Senate Majority Leader John Thune (R-SD) has adeptly employed pro forma sessions to essentially handcuff President Donald Trump, preventing any recess appointments during the Senate's August recess. This strategic play, unanimously agreed upon in the Senate, has ignited a fury among Trump’s most ardent supporters, casting Thune and his occasional allies, Senators Lisa Murkowski (R-AK) and Susan Collins (R-ME), as the newest villains in the MAGA narrative.

The Pro Forma Shield

At its core, the decision to hold pro forma sessions every three days is a legislative maneuver, one that operates within the boundaries of the Constitution to maintain a semblance of Senate activity, thereby denying the President the opportunity to make unilateral recess appointments. This is not a novel strategy; it has been previously utilized to thwart potential appointments from presidents of both parties. Yet, the MAGA contingent's response is as if Thune has committed an unfathomable betrayal of conservative values.

The MAGA Outrage Machine

The backlash from Trump's followers was as predictable as it was swift, with figures like David Limbaugh and entities like Maga Voice decrying Thune as a "sellout Traitor RINO." The vitriol spilled over onto social media platforms, with supporters calling for Thune's primary challenge in 2028 and labeling him and his compatriots as "anti-American." This outrage underscores a glaring hypocrisy within the MAGA movement: the call for strict adherence to constitutional principles appears to be a one-way street, only to be followed when it aligns with their political objectives.

A Question of Principle or Power?

David Limbaugh's lamentation that he is "no longer that concerned about such things" as institutional concerns when it comes to recess appointments is particularly telling. It reveals a naked truth about the current state of political discourse within certain factions of the Republican Party: the ends justify the means, and long-standing institutional norms and principles can be discarded if they impede the MAGA agenda.

The RINO Hunter’s Dilemma

Labeling Thune, Collins, and Murkowski as "RINOs" (Republicans in Name Only) for their allegiance to Senate protocol rather than to Trump's whims adds a layer of irony so thick it could be cut with a knife. The term "RINO" has been weaponized to such an extent that it no longer holds any real meaning, other than signaling anyone who dares to deviate from Trump’s line. It’s a McCarthy-esque tactic used to root out perceived disloyalty, ignoring the fact that political parties are, and should be, big tents with a diversity of thought.

The Bottom Line

The MAGA movement's reaction to Thune's scheduling of pro forma sessions reveals much about the current state of the Republican Party. It showcases a faction so devoted to the cult of personality surrounding Trump that it is willing to forsake long-held conservative principles and Senate traditions. Thune’s maneuver, whether seen as a defense of institutional integrity or a political play, has laid bare the fault lines within the GOP, pitting party traditionalists against the MAGA loyalists.

In the grand scheme, this episode is but a snapshot of a larger battle for the soul of the Republican Party. It poses a fundamental question: Will the party choose to embrace the rule of law and constitutional governance, or will it continue down the path of blind allegiance to one man’s agenda, at the expense of its own principles? The MAGA outrage over Thune's actions provides a clear indication of where many within the party currently stand.
