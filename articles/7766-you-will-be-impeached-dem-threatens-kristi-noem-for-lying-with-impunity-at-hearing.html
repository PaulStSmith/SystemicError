'You will be impeached': Dem threatens Kristi Noem for 'lying with impunity' at hearing

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosDecember 12, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Our Analysis:The Theatrical Dance of Political Accountability

U.S. Homeland Security Secretary Kristi Noem attends a House Homeland Security hearing entitled “Worldwide Threats to the Homeland,” on Capitol Hill in Washington, D.C., U.S. December 11, 2025. REUTERS/Elizabeth Frantz

Rep. Delia C. Ramirez (D-IL) threatened to impeach Department of Homeland Security Secretary Kristi Noem after accusing her of lying at a congressional hearing.

“I want to be very clear,” Ramirez told Noem at a Homeland Security Committee hearing on Thursday. “You have misused resources appropriated by Congress. You’ve engaged in unethical behavior. You have repeatedly made false and misleading statements to Congress and to the press, and you’ve undermined the separation of powers and disregarded the role of Congress on the courts.”

Our Analysis:

The Theatrical Dance of Political Accountability

In a performance that could only be described as a high-stakes political drama, Rep. Delia C. Ramirez (D-IL) has thrown down the gauntlet in a confrontation that underscores the friction between the legislative and executive branches of the U.S. government. The target of her ire, Department of Homeland Security Secretary Kristi Noem, stands accused of a litany of offenses that read like a rap sheet for political malfeasance: misuse of resources, unethical behavior, and a flagrant disregard for the truth.

Misuse of Resources & Unethical Behavior

Ramirez’s allegations against Noem are severe and multifaceted, starting with the misuse of resources appropriated by Congress. This is no petty accusation; it strikes at the heart of the legislative branch’s power of the purse, a fundamental aspect of American democracy designed to ensure that no single branch of government holds too much power. Ramirez couples this with a charge of engaging in unethical behavior, a broad accusation that, while lacking in specifics, suggests a pattern of conduct unbecoming of a public official.

False and Misleading Statements

Perhaps the most damning of Ramirez’s accusations are those related to Noem’s alleged propensity for falsehoods. To lie to Congress and the press is to undermine the very foundation of accountability and transparency that democracy requires. It’s a bold claim, implying not just a lapse in judgment but a systematic disregard for the truth.

Undermining Separation of Powers

Ramirez’s accusation that Noem has undermined the separation of powers and disregarded the role of Congress and the courts is arguably the most constitutionally significant. It suggests a breach of the fundamental principles that govern the interactions between the branches of government, hinting at a usurpation of power that threatens the balance designed to prevent tyranny.

The Ultimatum: Resign, Be Fired, or Be Impeached

The options Ramirez lays out for Noem are stark and final. Resignation would be an admission of guilt, while being fired by Trump would signal a loss of favor with the executive branch. Impeachment, however, is the nuclear option, a drawn-out process that would drag Noem’s alleged indiscretions into the public sphere for a detailed and likely divisive examination.

The Call for Investigation

By urging the Judiciary Committee to open an investigation, Ramirez is not just making a statement; she’s initiating a process that could have serious implications for Noem’s career and for the Department of Homeland Security as a whole. An investigation of this nature is both a probe into the allegations at hand and a public spectacle that could further strain the already tense relations between the legislative and executive branches.

In conclusion, Ramirez’s confrontation with Noem is more than just political theater; it’s a critical test of the mechanisms of accountability that underpin the U.S. government. The allegations, if true, represent a serious breach of ethical and constitutional norms. However, as with all such accusations, the principles of due process must be upheld. The coming investigation will not only determine Noem’s fate but also serve as a litmus test for the health of American democracy itself.
