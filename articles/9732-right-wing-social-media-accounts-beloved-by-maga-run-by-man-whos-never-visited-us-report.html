Right-wing social media accounts beloved by MAGA run by man who’s never visited US: report

A pair of pro-MAGA social media accounts promoted by prominent Republican politicians and that push election fraud conspiracy theories in the United States has been exposed as being run by an Eastern European man who has never visited the United States, and was accused of violating campaign finance laws.

According to Rolling Stone, the X accounts Defiant L’s and Resist the Mainstream “both [offer] takes on American politics, often alleging corruption and election fraud by Democrats, to a combined over 2 million followers. Republican lawmakers like Mike Lee, Nancy Mace, Andy Biggs, Eli Crane, and Dan Crenshaw, along with major political figures like Elon Musk, share the accounts’ posts on X, some claiming that ‘illegals’ or undocumented immigrants are fraudulently voting in American elections.” Musk went so far as to call Defiant L’s “one of the best accounts on X.”

But it turns out both of those accounts are run by a North Macedonian national named Rumen Naumovski, through a media company registered in Florida.

“Filings from a 2024 copyright lawsuit brought by a photojournalist who alleged Resist the Mainstream repeatedly stole his work show that Naumovski claims 100 percent ownership of the Florida company that operates the two X accounts, Raww Digital LLC,” reported Jacqueline Sweet. Naumovski contributed over $3,000 to the 2022 congressional campaign of Paul Watkins, and those donations on the Federal Election Commission site “both list the St. Petersburg, Florida, registered agent’s office where Raww Digital Inc. is located and Raww Digital LLC as Naumovski’s employer.”

Raww Digital “was first registered in 2021, with Naumovski as sole owner with an address in Veles, North Macedonia. A month later, an amendment with a new principal address was filed, at the registered agent’s office in St. Petersburg,” said the report.

It is a federal crime for a foreign national to contribute to a U.S. election campaign. Watkins later returned those donations, and Naumovski, who in a Daily Wire op-ed stated “I’ve never set foot in the United States,” says he did not know at the time his donation was illegal. According to experts, most illegal foreign donations are obscured through the use of straw donors, and it’s very rare for a foreign national to actually list themselves as a donor with the FEC.

Our Analysis:

The Puppeteer from Eastern Europe: Unveiling the Man Behind Pro-MAGA Social Media Facade

In an era where the digital world blurs the lines between reality and fiction, the revelation that a pair of pro-MAGA social media accounts have been operated by an Eastern European individual who has never stepped foot in the United States serves as a stark reminder of the pervasive and insidious nature of misinformation online. Rumen Naumovski, a North Macedonian national, emerges from the shadows, not as a fervent supporter from the American heartland as one might expect, but as a foreign actor accused of violating U.S. campaign finance laws, orchestrating his influence operations from thousands of miles away.

A Web of Deception

At the heart of this saga are the X accounts Defiant L’s and Resist the Mainstream, platforms that have become conduits for election fraud conspiracy theories and anti-Democratic rhetoric, amassing a staggering collective following of over 2 million. These accounts, bolstered by endorsements from prominent Republican figures and even tech mogul Elon Musk, have played a significant role in shaping the political discourse, all the while concealing their true origins.

The Unlikely Influencer

Naumovski’s orchestration of these platforms through his media company, Raww Digital LLC, registered in Florida, unveils a troubling reality of foreign influence in American politics. Despite his claims of ignorance regarding the illegality of his actions, Naumovski’s financial contributions to a 2022 congressional campaign, albeit returned, underscore the ease with which foreign entities can attempt to sway U.S. elections — a federal crime that seems to have been committed with alarming nonchalance.

A Tangled Legal Web

The saga thickens with the revelation of a copyright lawsuit against Resist the Mainstream for allegedly pilfering a photojournalist’s work, further tarnishing the credibility of Naumovski’s operations. This legal entanglement offers a glimpse into the murky waters of content creation and copyright infringement that often accompany such covert online activities.

The Irony of Ignorance

Naumovski’s assertion in a Daily Wire op-ed of his unfamiliarity with U.S. soil juxtaposes sharply with the profound impact his accounts have had on the American political landscape. This dissonance raises critical questions about the authenticity and integrity of the sources from which millions of Americans receive their political information.

A Call to Action

As this episode unfolds, it beckons a serious reflection on the mechanisms in place to safeguard the U.S. political process from foreign interference. The rarity of direct foreign donations, as highlighted by experts, speaks not to the ineffectiveness of such actors but to the sophistication of the strategies employed to mask their involvement.

In conclusion, the case of Rumen Naumovski and his pro-MAGA social media empire is not merely a tale of political subterfuge; it is a clarion call for vigilance in an age where digital personas wield unprecedented influence. For democracy to thrive, there must be a concerted effort to pierce through the veil of anonymity that the internet affords, ensuring that the voices shaping our political discourse are as authentic as the values they purport to champion.
