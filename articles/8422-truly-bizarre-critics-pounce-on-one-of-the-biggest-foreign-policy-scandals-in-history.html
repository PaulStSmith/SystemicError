“BREAKING: After talking to @SecRubio, @SenatorRounds announces that the 28 point plan was a Russian document, not a US document,” he wrote.

Sen. Angus King, one of the senators who broke with the majority of the Democratic caucus to support a deal to end the federal government shutdown, confirmed that Rubio report, saying, “According to Secretary Rubio, this plan is not the administration’s position — it is essentially the Russians’ wish list that is now being presented to the Europeans and to the Ukrainians.”

That sparked outrage.

Former Lincoln Project veterans affairs adviser Fred Wellman, an ex-Republican and current Democratic campaign consultant, said, “This is the most incompetent and idiotic Administration in history,” and then added, “Who is in charge?”

Political scientist Norman Ornstein responded to Wellman, saying simply, “Putin.”

But there’s a twist, because a State Department spokesman, Tommy Pigott, issued the following statement:

“This is blatantly false. As Secretary Rubio and the entire Administration has consistently maintained, this plan was authored by the United States, with input from both the Russians and Ukrainians.”

That led to senior congressional reporter Andrew Desiderio to chime in with, “A truly bizarre series of events.”

“Senators from both parties said in Halifax that Rubio told them via phone today that the Ukraine peace plan is actually a Russian document, not a U.S. proposal,” he added. “State Department spox says that’s not true, it’s a U.S.-authored proposal. ???”

MeidasTouch also added, “Holy s---: here’s a tweet where Vance pushed the Ukraine ‘peace plan’ that Marco Rubio just admitted was written entirely by Russia. This may be one of the biggest foreign policy scandals in history.”

Our Analysis:

The Unraveling of a “Peace Plan”: A Closer Look

In an episode that reads more like a script from a poorly written political thriller than reality, the so-called peace plan for Ukraine has become a center of controversy, confusion, and outright farce. The revelations, as reported, bring to light a tangled web of misinformation, geopolitical manipulation, and a stunning display of incompetence—or worse, complicity—at the highest levels of political leadership. Let’s dissect the layers of this debacle with the precision of a scalpel cutting through the fog of political obfuscation.

The Core of the Controversy: According to reports, what was initially thrust upon Ukraine as a peace plan was, in reality, a document authored by Russia. This alone sets off alarm bells, considering Russia’s aggressive invasion of Ukraine and its longstanding history of undermining Ukrainian sovereignty. The notion that a peace plan beneficial to Ukraine could be penned by its aggressor is not just absurd; it’s a mockery of the concept of peace itself.

Marco Rubio’s Messaging Mishap: Senator Marco Rubio finds himself at the center of this storm, with his reported confusion or miscommunication exacerbating an already volatile situation. For a figure of Rubio’s experience and stature in foreign affairs to be caught in such a bewildering narrative raises serious questions about the clarity and coherence of US foreign policy messaging. When the lines between friend and foe, peace and capitulation, become blurred, the consequences can be dire.

A Bipartisan Breakdown: The bipartisan confusion—with senators from both parties sharing conflicting accounts of the plan’s origins—highlights a disturbing lack of unity and clarity on matters of national and global importance. Senator Angus King’s revelation that the plan is essentially a “Russian wish list” being presented to Europeans and Ukrainians is damning. It suggests not just a failure of American leadership to stand firm against autocratic aggression, but an alarming willingness to entertain the ambitions of a hostile power.

The Administration’s Denial: The State Department’s spokesman, Tommy Pigott, vehemently denies the Russian authorship of the plan, asserting it was “authored by the United States, with input from both the Russians and Ukrainians.” This statement, intended to quell the firestorm, only adds fuel to the flames, raising further questions about the nature of the “input” from Russia and the true intentions behind this plan.

The Reaction from the Political and Analytical Community: The responses from figures like Fred Wellman and Norman Ornstein capture the collective disbelief and outrage at the situation. The utterance of “Putin” in response to questions of leadership underscores a chilling narrative: the perception of Russian manipulation at the highest levels of international diplomacy.

The Bizarre Unfolding: Andrew Desiderio’s remark about the “truly bizarre series of events” might be the understatement of the year. The conflicting statements, the back-and-forth between senators and the State Department, and the revelation of the plan’s Russian roots paint a picture of a foreign policy establishment in disarray.

A Foreign Policy Scandal of Historic Proportions?: The suggestion by MeidasTouch that this could represent “one of the biggest foreign policy scandals in history” is not hyperbole. If it indeed turns out that a peace plan for Ukraine—a nation fighting for its very survival against Russian aggression—was crafted by its aggressor and peddled by US officials, the implications are profound and deeply disturbing.

In conclusion, this episode is a stark reminder of the necessity for transparency, coherence, and principled leadership in foreign policy. The stakes are too high for anything less. The idea that a peace plan, so crucial to the future of Ukraine and the stability of the region, could become mired in such controversy is a testament to the systemic errors plaguing our political and diplomatic processes. As the dust settles, one can only hope that clarity emerges from the chaos, and that a genuine path to peace for Ukraine can be found—one that respects its sovereignty, dignity, and the aspirations of its people for freedom and security.
