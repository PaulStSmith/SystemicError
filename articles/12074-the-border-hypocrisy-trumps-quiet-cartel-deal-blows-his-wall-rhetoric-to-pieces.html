The Border Hypocrisy: Trump’s Quiet Cartel Deal Blows His Wall Rhetoric to Pieces

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosAugust 4, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

For years, Donald Trump thundered about border security. "Build the Wall!" was more than a slogan—it was a cult chant. His base didn’t just want a physical barrier; they wanted vengeance against the people they were told were "invading" America. Trump painted immigrants as gangsters, rapists, cartel mules, and disease vectors. And all the while, he positioned himself as the tough, no-nonsense law-and-order president who alone could stop the madness.

Then, in May 2025, something extraordinary happened. Mexico’s Secretary of Security, Omar García Harfuch, confirmed that 17 relatives of Sinaloa cartel figures crossed into the United States in a deal coordinated with U.S. authorities. Among them were direct family members of Joaquín "El Chapo" Guzmán—including his ex-wife. They were met at the Tijuana-San Ysidro port of entry by American agents, carrying luggage and walking into what appeared to be a pre-negotiated arrangement.

The cartel kingpins’ families weren’t smuggled. They didn’t sneak through tunnels or stash themselves in tractor trailers. They walked into the country openly. With federal blessing.

Let’s not dance around this: Trump’s administration orchestrated a secret entry deal with the Sinaloa cartel while still publicly vilifying immigrants and promising law and order. The stench of hypocrisy is overwhelming. And yet, few in the GOP seem to care.

The Facts They Can’t Deny

According to multiple verified reports from the Associated Press, CBS News, and even Fox News, the crossing happened in mid-May 2025. The Mexican government was caught off-guard. President Claudia Sheinbaum’s administration admitted it was not informed of the deal ahead of time. The move blindsided Mexican authorities—which implies that any coordination came solely through American federal channels, likely the Department of Justice.

But here’s the kicker: Trump loyalists and right-wing propagandists immediately tried to spin it. Some claimed it was part of a “legal strategy” to secure testimony from Ovidio Guzmán López, El Chapo’s son, who was recently extradited to the U.S. But that’s pure smoke.

You don’t need to greenlight the entire extended family of a drug lord to get legal cooperation. What we’re seeing here is not a legal necessity. It’s a favor. A concession. A deal.

And let’s not pretend the DOJ under Trump was some independent operation. This is the same administration where the Attorney General acted as the president’s personal attorney, where investigations into Trump allies were dropped like hot potatoes, and where loyalty to the man—not the law—was the price of admission.

Law-and-Order, For Show Only

The grotesque irony of this can’t be overstated.

Trump made headlines for separating migrant families at the border, caging children, and using ICE as a terror weapon against immigrant communities. He claimed it was all about "protecting Americans." About stopping drugs. About defeating cartels.

But now we know: while the kids were crying in cages, the cartel families were waltzing through border checkpoints with Trump’s nod.

What is that if not betrayal?

What is that if not proof that Trumpism is a con?

This Isn’t New—It’s the Pattern

Let’s be clear: this cartel deal may be shocking, but it’s not out of character.

Trump has always made a mockery of national security when it suited him. He:

  • Took $2 billion from Saudi Arabia for Jared Kushner's investment fund.

  • Tried to strong-arm Ukraine into smearing his political opponent.

  • Withheld aid to blue states during the pandemic based on how well their governors praised him.

  • Pushed to host the G7 at his private resort, essentially charging world leaders to stay at his hotel.

So when we hear that cartel relatives got a red carpet welcome into the U.S. under Trump’s watch, we should be disgusted—but not surprised.

GOP Silence: Complicity by Cowardice

Where is the outrage from the party of law and order? Where are the calls for accountability from the self-proclaimed defenders of American values?

Crickets.

Why? Because the MAGA movement doesn’t care about truth or principle. It’s a cult of personality where Trump’s actions don’t matter—only his posturing. He could burn a flag while hugging Putin, and his base would still chant USA.

What This Means for Immigration Policy

This isn’t just about Trump. It’s about the way immigration policy is used as a political cudgel.

Every time Republicans scream about the "border crisis," remember this: the man who led their party for eight years opened the gates for cartel families while punishing asylum seekers fleeing violence. He didn’t secure the border—he weaponized it.

This revelation obliterates any claim to moral high ground from the right. The next time you hear about fentanyl seizures, migrant caravans, or "Biden’s open border," remember who invited the cartel in without a fight: Donald J. Trump.

Final Word: A Wall to Hide the Rot

Trump never wanted to build a wall to protect America. He wanted a wall to hide the truth. To distract. To create a mythology of the strongman holding back the horde. Meanwhile, he sold out the country one deal at a time, one favor at a time, one family at a time.

This cartel deal proves it.

The border crisis wasn’t just a stage. It was a scam.

And the only thing Trump was ever serious about protecting… was himself.
