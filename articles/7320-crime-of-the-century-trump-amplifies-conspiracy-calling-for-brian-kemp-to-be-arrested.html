'Crime of the century!' Trump amplifies conspiracy calling for Brian Kemp to be arrested

U.S. President Donald Trump places a hand over his heart during a Veterans Day ceremony at Arlington National Cemetery in Arlington, Virginia, U.S., November 11, 2025. REUTERS Kevin Lamarque

President Donald Trump took to his Truth Social platform on Tuesday, reposting a long conspiracy theory screed alleging mass voter fraud in the 2020 presidential election in Georgia, and calling for the state’s Republican governor, Brian Kemp, to be jailed, alongside Secretary of State Brad Raffensperger and Atlanta poll worker Ruby Freeman.

“BREAKING: The Georgia Board of Elections just dropped this BOMBSHELL revealing that HUNDREDS OF THOUSANDS of ballots in Georgia in the 2020 presidential election were counted twice and it was NOT by human error - but by INTENTIONAL HUMAN INTERVENTION, which was in fact MASSIVE VOTER FRAUD that STOLE the election from the actual winner of Georgia, President Donald J Trump,” said the original post, made by the account @Joshua2024.

“It’s official guys and the evidence is all out after 5 long, painful years: The 2020 election was STOLEN from all of us and President Trump was the lawful winner of the state of Georgia,” said the post. “Secretary of State Brad Raffensperger, Governor Brian Kemp and other top Georgia elections officials were paid off to ‘look the other way’ and knowingly certify this FRAUDULENT election. So when do the arrests start? Ruby Freeman should have to pay Rudy back reparations and every single penny that she got off of him based on a BIG LIE AND A FALSE PRETENSE. And her, Raffensperger, Kemp and every single one of their fellow ELECTION RIGGERS MUST BE ARRESTED AND PROSECUTED TO THE FULLEST EXTENT OF THE LAW.”

“This was the POLITICAL CRIME OF THE CENTURY and they should never be allowed to show their faces in the public again without being shouted down by we the people for stealing our vote and our President from us,” the post concluded. “RESIGN NOW, YOU RINO TRAITORS. When President Trump was talking about the enemy from within, he was talking about people like them.”

“Thank you Joshua. Total Crooks!!!” Trump replied while screenshotting the post.

The claims made in the post refer to an admission made by an attorney representing Fulton County earlier this month, that some tabulator tapes — a record of voter counts used in the verification process — were not correctly signed by poll workers in accordance with state law. These tapes represented receipts for around 315,000 early votes.

There is no evidence, however, that any of the ballots on these tapes were “counted twice” or that this oversight altered the outcome of the election. Raffensperger has clarified, “Georgia has the most secure elections in the country and all voters were verified with photo ID and lawfully cast their ballots. A clerical error at the end of the day does not erase valid, legal votes.”

“Donald Trump’s 2020 election conspiracies are hogwash, and he should stop endorsing messages calling for Governor Kemp and Secretary Raffensperger’s arrest,” posted Georgia State constitutional law professor Anthony Michael Kreis on X. “It’s unfathomably stupid and irresponsible.”

Our Analysis:

Unpacking the Latest Trumpian Fantasy: A Masterclass in Misinformation

In a recent display of the ongoing saga that could be dubbed ‘The Never-Ending Story of 2020 Election Grievances’, former President Donald Trump has catapulted himself back into the spotlight with a fervor reminiscent of a Shakespearean tragedy, minus the eloquence but doubling down on the drama. On his Truth Social platform, Trump amplified a conspiracy theory screed that not only challenges the fabric of democracy but seems to weave its own alternative reality. This particular narrative accuses Georgia’s Republican governor, Brian Kemp, and Secretary of State Brad Raffensperger, among others, of orchestrating mass voter fraud in the 2020 presidential election. The post goes as far as demanding their incarceration, alongside Atlanta poll worker Ruby Freeman, for their alleged roles in this fictitious fiasco.

The Claims: A Closer Look

The original post, made by an account named @Joshua2024, boldly claims, “The Georgia Board of Elections just dropped this BOMBSHELL,” alleging that hundreds of thousands of ballots in Georgia were intentionally counted twice. This, according to the post, robbed Trump of his rightful victory in the state. It further accuses Kemp, Raffensperger, and other officials of being bribed to certify a “FRAUDULENT” election result. The solution, it suggests, is a series of arrests and prosecutions, painting this scenario as the “POLITICAL CRIME OF THE CENTURY.”

Fact vs. Fiction: The Evidence

The crux of these explosive allegations rests on an admission by an attorney representing Fulton County, acknowledging that some tabulator tapes were not correctly signed by poll workers. However, this is where the thread of truth ends and the tapestry of deception begins. The leap from this acknowledgment to the claim of double-counted ballots and a stolen election is not just a stretch; it’s a launch into fictional space. Notably, Georgia Secretary of State Brad Raffensperger, a figure often maligned by Trump, has stated, “Georgia has the most secure elections in the country,” reinforcing that a clerical error does not invalidate the integrity of the election results.

The Reckless Endorsement

Trump’s endorsement of these claims with a simple “Total Crooks!!!” adds fuel to the fire of misinformation, showcasing a blatant disregard for the truth and the democratic process. This reckless behavior not only undermines public trust in election integrity but also fans the flames of division within the country and his own party, branding officials who stand by the facts as “RINO TRAITORS.”

The Voice of Reason

Amidst this tempest of falsehoods, voices of reason emerge, such as Georgia State constitutional law professor Anthony Michael Kreis, who labeled Trump’s election conspiracies as “hogwash” and his calls for arrest as “unfathomably stupid and irresponsible.” Such statements serve as a lighthouse of logic in the fog of conspiracy, reminding us that the cornerstone of democracy is respect for the rule of law and the electoral process.

Conclusion: The Dangers of Duplicity

The narrative spun by Trump and echoed by his supporters is not just a harmless recounting of grievances; it’s a dangerous distortion of reality that threatens the very bedrock of American democracy. This latest episode, while sadly familiar, underscores the urgent need for critical thinking and media literacy among the public. As we navigate these turbulent waters of misinformation, it’s imperative to anchor ourselves to the facts and remain vigilant against the tides of falsehoods that seek to erode the foundations of our democratic society.
