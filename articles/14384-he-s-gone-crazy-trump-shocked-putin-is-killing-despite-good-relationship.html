Donald Trump on Sunday expressed shock that the leader of Russia is "killing a lot of people" without reason.

Putin waged war on Ukraine years ago, but Trump appeared to be looking at the situation with fresh eyes over the weekend.

He took to Truth Social and wrote, "I’ve always had a very good relationship with Vladimir Putin of Russia, but something has happened to him."

He continued, saying, "He has gone absolutely CRAZY! He is needlessly killing a lot of people, and I’m not just talking about soldiers. Missiles and drones are being shot into Cities in Ukraine, for no reason whatsoever. I’ve always said that he wants ALL of Ukraine, not just a piece of it, and maybe that’s proving to be right, but if he does, it will lead to the downfall of Russia!"

Trump added, "Likewise, President Zelenskyy is doing his Country no favors by talking the way he does. Everything out of his mouth causes problems, I don’t like it, and it better stop."

The president then moved to distance himself from the brutal conflict.

"This is a War that would never have started if I were President. This is Zelenskyy’s, Putin’s, and Biden’s War, not 'Trump’s,' I am only helping to put out the big and ugly fires, that have been started through Gross Incompetence and Hatred," Trump claimed.

Our Analysis:

The Paradox of Trump's Sudden Epiphany

In a recent spectacle of political theater, Donald Trump has expressed what can only be described as feigned shock at Russia's violent actions in Ukraine. His comments, made over the weekend on Truth Social, paint a picture of a man bewildered by the brutal tactics of a leader he once described as having a "very good relationship" with. This sudden shift in rhetoric prompts a closer analysis, not only of the absurdity of his supposed surprise but also of the underlying motivations and implications.

The Revelation: Trump Discovers War Is Violent

Trump's declaration that Vladimir Putin is "killing a lot of people" without reason is as shocking as it is laughable. The notion that Putin's aggression is a recent development is a farce. It's akin to waking up one morning and being surprised that the sun rises in the east. Putin's invasion of Ukraine wasn't a clandestine operation done in the dead of night; it was a globally witnessed act of aggression that has been extensively documented and condemned.

Yet, according to Trump, there's something new afoot. He states, "He has gone absolutely CRAZY!" and decries the use of missiles and drones in Ukrainian cities "for no reason whatsoever." This newfound concern for the Ukrainian plight is as perplexing as it is disingenuous. It begs the question: where has Trump been all this time?

A Convenient Amnesia

Trump's attempt to rewrite history and position himself as a peacemaker is nothing short of audacious. His claim that "This is a War that would never have started if I were President" is a masterclass in historical revisionism. One might argue it borders on fantasy. The suggestion that his presence in the Oval Office would have deterred Putin is a narrative so detached from reality, it's almost art.

Moreover, his criticism of Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy adds another layer of irony. To chastise Zelenskyy for "causing problems" with his words while simultaneously absolving himself of any responsibility is a hypocrisy of the highest order. Trump's own rhetoric, often inflammatory and divisive, apparently plays no part in the global landscape of "Gross Incompetence and Hatred" he so readily condemns.

The Grift Continues

Embedded in Trump's statements is an EXCLUSIVE accusation of a new grift, one that apparently dwarfs the infamous Qatari plane incident. Yet, in typical fashion, the details of this latest scheme are overshadowed by Trump's self-aggrandizing narrative. It's a classic Trump move: deflect, distract, and inflate one's own importance.

The Brutal Reality

This entire episode serves as a stark reminder of Trump's approach to foreign policy and leadership. It's a world where facts are malleable, history is fluid, and responsibility is always elsewhere. His comments on Putin and Ukraine are not just disconnected from reality; they're a deliberate attempt to reshape the narrative to his advantage.

In the end, Trump's sudden concern for the victims of Putin's aggression is not a sign of genuine empathy or insight. It's a calculated move, designed to distance himself from a conflict he once had the power to influence, if not prevent. His shock is not just belated; it's a performance, and a poor one at that.
