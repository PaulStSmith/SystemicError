‘We’ll make you disappear’: Dem lawmaker demands answers on disturbing FBI tip about Trump

Rep. Melanie Stansbury (D-NM) is demanding answers regarding a disturbing tip received by the FBI in 2016 that includes allegations of the “sexual exploitation of a minor” involving President Donald Trump and Jeffrey Epstein.

“Why are folks not asking more questions about this FBI complaint in the Epstein Files?” Stansbury wrote early Saturday morning in a social media post on X, alongside a link to an FBI tip that was published last month by the Justice Department.

In the tip, an individual who is identified as an attorney contacted the FBI on behalf of their client, whose name is listed as “Jane Doe.” According to the apparent attorney, their client was “enticed” to cross state lines to visit Epstein and Trump in New York “to perform sexual acts on both adult men at special parties hosted by Epstein,” a series of incidents that allegedly occurred when Doe was 13 years old.

According to the report, the attorney told the FBI that Doe had been enticed to visit Trump and Epstein under the guise of starting a modeling career, and had attended four parties hosted by the disgraced financier, all attended by Trump. Doe, according to her attorney, was asked to perform increasingly explicit “sexual acts” on Trump.

The attorney told the FBI that during the fourth party attended by their client, Doe was raped by both Trump and Epstein, and “threatened with violence by both men to not make any report of what happened.”

“We’ll make you disappear like [redacted],” Doe was told, according to the FBI write up based on the attorney’s tip.

According to the FBI report, the attorney filed a complaint in court on behalf of their client, a complaint that advanced far enough for court summons to “have been issued,” and a scheduling conference set for Sept. 2, 2016. At the time of the FBI report write up, however, the complaints had “not yet been served to Epstein or Trump.”

The report also notes that an internal FBI database search generated a case number regarding the allegation.

As a member of the House Oversight Committee, which has been among the leading congressional bodies investigating Epstein and potential co-conspirators, Stansbury is well positioned to advocate for congressional subpoenas to be issued, a tool lawmakers have at their disposal to compel witnesses to testify under oath.

While the aforementioned allegations were documented by the FBI, many of the Epstein-related documents produced by the DOJ contain unverified, uncorroborated allegations that do not constitute evidence, and do not establish wrongdoing. Trump is not facing any criminal charges or investigations related to the allegation.

Our Analysis:

The Unsettling Silence: Stansbury’s Call to Arms Against Apathy

In a world where sensationalism often overshadows substance, the chilling silence surrounding Rep. Melanie Stansbury’s (D-NM) recent outcry is as mystifying as it is inexcusable. Stansbury’s demand for answers concerning a 2016 FBI tip linking then-President Donald Trump with the sexual exploitation of a minor in connection with Jeffrey Epstein should have set off alarm bells across the political and media landscapes. Yet, the echo chamber of our public discourse seems oddly muted. Why?

A Disturbing Allegation Lost in the Noise

The Allegation: An attorney, representing someone identified only as “Jane Doe,” makes a harrowing accusation. Doe was allegedly enticed to cross state lines under the pretense of launching a modeling career, only to be thrown into a nightmarish scenario involving Trump and Epstein at parties where she was expected to perform sexual acts, culminating in a brutal rape by both men.

The Threat: Adding a layer of horror is the claim that Doe was threatened with violence and disappearance, a tactic so vile it’s the stuff of nightmares. The specific mention, “We’ll make you disappear like [redacted],” ominously suggests a pattern of intimidation and abuse that extends beyond a single incident.

The Sound of Silence: A Media and Political Blackout

Why then, in the face of such grave allegations, has there been a conspicuous lack of uproar? Rep. Stansbury, leveraging her position on the House Oversight Committee, is not just whistling into the wind. She’s demanding the deployment of congressional subpoenas, a move that could potentially unravel a tapestry of complicity and silence. Yet, the collective response has been tepid at best.

The Uncomfortable Truths

The Unverified Nature: It’s crucial to underscore that many documents emerging from the DOJ’s Epstein investigation contain allegations that are unverified and uncorroborated. This does not, however, negate the seriousness of the allegations or absolve the media and political figures from their duty to investigate and report.

No Charges... Yet: Trump, despite the severity of the accusations, faces no criminal charges or investigations linked to these allegations. This fact is often brandished like a shield by his defenders, as if the absence of charges is synonymous with innocence. But history is littered with instances where the wheels of justice grind slowly, especially when the accused wield significant power and influence.

The Implications of Our Collective Apathy

The silence and inaction that greet allegations of this magnitude reveal a systemic rot within our political and media ecosystems. When allegations involving figures of Trump and Epstein’s stature are met with a shrug, it sends a chilling message about the value we place on justice and the protection of the vulnerable.

The Call to Action: Rep. Stansbury’s call for more questions, more investigation, and more accountability is not just about this specific case. It’s a test of our societal values, of our willingness to confront uncomfortable truths, and of our commitment to justice, irrespective of the power and status of those involved.

In a just world, the allegations unearthed by the FBI would be the beginning of a rigorous investigation, not a footnote in a news cycle perennially distracted by the next shiny object. The real test now is whether we, as a society, choose to listen and act, or whether we turn a deaf ear, complicit in our silence. The time for action is now, lest we all be guilty of allowing the whispers of injustice to fade into oblivion.
