Republican senator demands Biden cancer 'cover-up' probe

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosMay 23, 20255 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Our Analysis:Dissecting Senator Johnson's Claims and the Biden Health Saga

Ron Johnson, the Republican senator from Wisconsin. REUTERS/Vincent Alban

WASHINGTON — News that former President Joe Biden has advanced prostate cancer has Sen. Ron Johnson (R-WI) demanding answers from what he calls a “cabal of radical leftists” that supposedly ran the U.S. government in Biden's stead.

“They were running the government,” Johnson claimed to Raw Story at the Capitol Wednesday. “What's worrisome is that he was incapacitated.”

Presidential and now personal physicians to Biden, 82, disagree. Republicans like Johnson don’t really care what such doctors say.

“Everybody always asks, ‘Who's running the government?’ I don't know. It’s that cabal of radical leftists that associated with [Barack] Obama, probably as far back as the Clintons,” said Johnson, who rode the hard-right, conspiracy-tinged Tea Party wave into Congress in 2010.

“It’s this group of radical leftists who have had control over the Democrat Party and their policies.”

Biden's diagnosis of advanced prostate cancer was announced Sunday. It came amid frenzied reporting and speculation concerning a book about how Biden aged in office — Original Sin: President Biden's Decline, Its Cover-up, and His Disastrous Choice to Run Again, by Jake Tapper and Alex Thompson.

The book claims close aides to Biden, nicknamed “the politburo,” effectively ran the administration.

Spokespeople for Biden have denied he was too old and infirm to govern.

After the cancer diagnosis was announced, a spokesperson for Biden said the former president had never received such a diagnosis before.

Johnson used to chair the Senate Homeland Security and Government Affairs Committee. These days he leads its Permanent Subcommittee on Investigations. This week, he plans to demand answers from Bidenworld, including a high-profile public hearing or two.

“We're going to be requesting interviews. Let's just leave it at that,” Johnson said. “I don't want to get ahead of myself.”

An official letter could be sent as early as Wednesday, according to Johnson.

“Biden was always their figurehead,” Johnson claimed. “Do you think he ever really functioned as the full president? The guy campaigned from his basement [in 2020, when he beat Donald Trump by more than 7 million votes], and the media covered up for him and Democrat elected officials covered up for him. It's obvious.”

While Johnson is demanding public hearings, he seems to have his own mind made up.

“My main concern about Biden and what happened is just [about] the completely ignoring and covering up his mental decline,” Johnson said. “It was obvious. Pretty early on in his presidency it was pretty obvious that he was unfit, just in terms of mental and physical” capabilities.

Despite such remarks, Johnson also said his heart went out to former President Biden and his family.

“It's unfortunate. There's nothing good about getting old, so I’ll say, President Biden and his family are in my prayers,” Johnson told Raw Story. “It’s not a good sign, what's happening with the cancer.”

Our Analysis:

Dissecting Senator Johnson's Claims and the Biden Health Saga

The Spectacle of Speculation: Senator Ron Johnson's comments to Raw Story regarding former President Joe Biden's health diagnosis spiral into a convoluted narrative that's more suited to a political thriller than the halls of the U.S. Capitol. Johnson's assertions that a "cabal of radical leftists" secretly controlled the U.S. government during Biden's presidency not only lack evidence but also flirt dangerously with the kind of conspiratorial thinking that undermines the very fabric of democratic governance.

Conspiracy as a Political Tool: Johnson's remarks, particularly his insinuation that Biden has been nothing more than a figurehead manipulated by shadowy forces since his campaign days, is a classic move in the conspiracy playbook. By suggesting that Biden's presidency was illegitimate, Johnson attempts to delegitimize not just Biden's administration but also the democratic process itself. It's a tactic that's as old as politics but no less dangerous for its age.

Ignoring the Experts: The narrative pushed by Johnson conveniently dismisses the opinions of Biden's personal and presidential physicians, who have publicly disagreed with the claim that Biden was "incapacitated." This selective hearing is indicative of a broader trend where inconvenient facts are dismissed in favor of more politically expedient narratives.

The Role of the Media: The article mentions a book titled Original Sin: President Biden's Decline, Its Cover-up, and His Disastrous Choice to Run Again, which has fueled much of the speculation around Biden's health and his administration's transparency. Johnson's quick leap from speculation in a book to calling for public hearings underscores the outsized role that media—especially books and reports that cater to sensationalism—play in shaping political narratives, often at the expense of nuanced understanding or truth.

The Irony of Concern: Johnson’s statement that his "heart went out" to Biden and his family over the cancer diagnosis, juxtaposed against his aggressive pursuit of a narrative that questions Biden's mental and physical fitness for office, is steeped in irony. It’s a token gesture of sympathy that feels hollow when weighed against the broader implications of his accusations and demands.

The Politics of Health and Age: The focus on Biden's health and age, while not entirely unjustified given the demands of the presidency, is weaponized in a manner that detracts from substantive policy discussions. It's a tactic that's been used against many politicians, but when entangled with unfounded conspiracy theories, it becomes particularly insidious.

What's Really at Play: Johnson's call for public hearings and investigations into the so-called "Bidenworld" is a political maneuver that seeks to keep the Biden administration on the defensive and sow doubt about its legitimacy and effectiveness. It’s a strategy that relies less on uncovering the truth and more on perpetuating a state of constant controversy.

Conclusion: Senator Ron Johnson's comments are emblematic of a political environment where conspiracy theories are increasingly used as tools to undermine opponents. Such tactics, while not new, pose significant risks to the health of the democratic process. The focus on Biden's health, underpinned by unsubstantiated claims of a secret cabal, serves as a distraction from substantive policy debates and erodes public trust in governmental institutions. It's a systemic error that demands not just scrutiny but a concerted effort to champion facts over fiction.
