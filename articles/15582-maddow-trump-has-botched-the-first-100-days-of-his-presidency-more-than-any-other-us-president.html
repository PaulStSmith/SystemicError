Maddow: Trump has 'botched the first 100 days of his presidency' more than any other US president

Image via screengrab/MSNBC.

Donald Trump is now more than three months into his second presidency, and many far-right MAGA media outlets are praising his "record of accomplishment" and echoing his "promises made, promises kept" messaging. But for President Trump's critics, there is much to criticize — from his handling of the economy to immigration to civil liberties.

One of those critics is MSNBC's Rachel Maddow. During a Saturday, April 26 appearance on colleague Katie Phang's final MSNBC show, Maddow offered a scathing assessment of Trump's first 100 days back in the White House.

"There has never been a president who has botched the first 100 days of his presidency more than Donald Trump has," Maddow told Phang. "And you don't have to take that as a subjective view. It's the view shown in scientific polling of the American public. The public is deeply, deeply, deeply against what Trump is doing his. The Pew poll that came out this week, he's minus 19 points underwater in terms of his approval rating. If you ask the American people about what they feel about his handling of every single issue that you can pull on, he's underwater on every single one of them."

Maddow argued that although it comes as no surprise that Trump has been "very radical in his first 100 days," the shocking thing is how quickly his poll numbers have plummeted.

Maddow told Phang, "He's really flailed. It doesn't change his radicalism, but I think that really, the story of the first 100 days is his failure and the American public saying no to him."

When Phang asked Maddow if reporters had a "failure of imagination" when it came to predicting how bad Trump's second president would be, she responded, "I actually look at it, I think, the other way around."

Maddow explained, "I feel like the kind of pundit common wisdom heading into Trump's second term was that the comparison with the first term, or the difference with the first term, was going to be that he was going to be more efficient. He was going to be better at doing what he wanted to do, and that's what we should brace ourselves for. And I actually don't think that's the way it's worked out."

Maddow continued, "I think that he is definitely more ambitious in terms of the kind of radical change he wants for the country. I do think we're in the middle of an attempted authoritarian overthrow of American democracy. I mean, I think everybody who predicted that was right, but I don't think he's any better at it. I mean, you just think about the litany of mistakes — things that they acknowledge even are mistakes."

Our Analysis:

Maddow's Unyielding Critique of Trump's Second Term: A Deep Dive

Donald Trump's second presidency, now over three months in, has been a magnet for polarized opinions. Far-right MAGA media outlets shower him with accolades for his so-called "record of accomplishment," parroting the "promises made, promises kept" narrative. Yet, for the critics, Trump's administration is nothing short of a disaster, with MSNBC's Rachel Maddow leading the charge with her scathing assessment during a recent appearance on Katie Phang's MSNBC show.

The Maddow Perspective: "A Botched First 100 Days"

Maddow does not mince words, stating, "There has never been a president who has botched the first 100 days of his presidency more than Donald Trump has." This isn't just a matter of opinion; Maddow points to "scientific polling of the American public" to back her claims. Trump's approval rating, as per the latest Pew poll, is "minus 19 points underwater". But it's not just the overall approval where Trump fails to swim; it's in his handling of every single issue—from the economy, immigration, to civil liberties.

Polling as a Mirror to Public Sentiment

The Pew poll serves as a stark reflection of the public's deep dissatisfaction with Trump's second term. Every issue polled shows Trump "underwater," a term that evokes imagery of a president not just failing to lead but actively sinking. Maddow's use of this imagery is a powerful indictment of Trump's inability to fulfill his presidential duties in a manner that satisfies the American populace.

The Failure of Imagination Among Reporters

Maddow's response to Phang's question about whether reporters suffered a "failure of imagination" in predicting Trump's second term is particularly illuminating. Rather than agreeing, Maddow suggests that the common wisdom was overly optimistic, expecting Trump to be "more efficient" and effective in pursuing his agenda during his second term. This expectation has been thoroughly debunked, according to Maddow. The reality is a president who is not only more radical but also more inept at implementing his vision for America.

An Attempted Authoritarian Overthrow?

Maddow doesn't shy away from the direst interpretation of Trump's ambitions: "an attempted authoritarian overthrow of American democracy." This isn't hyperbole for the sake of ratings; it's an observation grounded in the observable actions and rhetoric of Trump's administration. It's a warning that the stakes are higher than simple policy disagreements; it's about the foundational principles of American democracy.

Conclusion: A Radical Failure

Maddow's critique paints a picture of a presidency in peril—not just for Trump, but for the very fabric of American society. Her analysis, grounded in polling data and observed actions, offers a sobering reminder of the consequences of failed leadership. Trump's second term, as portrayed by Maddow, is not just a series of missteps but a radical failure to uphold the responsibilities of the presidency.

The story of Trump's first 100 days, as per Maddow, is not a narrative of success or efficiency but one of "failure and the American public saying no to him." This isn't just a critique; it's a call to action—a reminder that democracy requires vigilance, especially when faced with leaders who threaten its very core.
