Trump, writing in all caps, posted, "HAPPY MEMORIAL DAY TO ALL, INCLUDING THE SCUM THAT SPENT THE LAST FOUR YEARS TRYING TO DESTROY OUR COUNTRY THROUGH WARPED RADICAL LEFT MINDS, WHO ALLOWED 21,000,000 MILLION PEOPLE TO ILLEGALLY ENTER OUR COUNTRY, MANY OF THE BEING CRIMINALS AND THE MENTAO INSANE,THROUGH AN OPEN BORDER THAT ONLY AN INCOMPETENT PRESIDENT WOULD APPROVE, AND THROUGH JUDGES WHO ARE ON A MISSION TO KEEP MURDERERS, DRUG DEALERS, RAPISTS, GANG MEMBERS, AND RELEASED PRISONERS FROM ALL OVER THE WORLD, IN OUR COUNTRY SO THEY CAN ROB, MURDERERS, AND RAPE AGAIN, PROTECTED BY THESE USA HATING JUDGES WHO SUFFER FROM AN IDEOLOGY THAT IS SICK, AND VERY DANGEROUS FOR OUR COUNTRY. HOPEFULLY THE UNITED STATES SUPREME COURT, AND OTHER GOOD AND COMPASSIONATE JUDGES THROUGHOUT THE LAND, WILL SAVE US FROM THE DECISIONS OF THE MONSTERS WHO WANT OUR COUNTRY TO GO TO HELL."

But Trump later deleted that post and replaced it with a much shorter post that read simply, "HAPPY MEMORIAL DAY!

The original post is receiving a lot of reactions on X, formerly Twitter.

Eric Daughterty of the right-wing Florida's Voice News tweeted, "JUST IN: Trump issues glorious, classic Memorial Day message."

The far-right Catturd, who has 3.7 million followers on X, wrote, "Trump's Happy Memorial Day post - lol - love it."

But other X users weren't so favorable.

North Carolina resident Matthew Cornell posted, "This is a ridiculous statement by Trump. Totally and utterly ridiculous."

Our Analysis:

Trump's Memorial Day Meltdown: A Disgraceful Display

On a day traditionally reserved for solemn remembrance and unity, President Donald Trump chose to desecrate the spirit of Memorial Day 2025 with a tirade that can only be described as a thunderous blend of vitriol, misinformation, and sheer disrespect. His initial post on Truth Social was not just a departure from presidential decorum; it was a nosedive into the abyss of political depravity.

The Diatribe: A Breakdown

Trump's post, alarmingly composed in all caps, was not a mere deviation from the expected norms of Memorial Day messages but a comprehensive guide on how to alienate, accuse, and attack in under 280 characters. His message accused the "radical left" of allowing "21,000,000 million people to illegally enter our country," a figure so inflated it borders on the fantastical. The addition of descriptors like "criminals" and "the mental insane" to these baseless numbers is not just fear-mongering; it's a calculated attempt to stoke xenophobic flames.

The Irony of It All

The irony of Trump's call for salvation from "monsters" on a day meant to honor true heroes is as rich as it is tragic. His mention of "USA hating judges" and his apocalyptic vision of the country's future under their legal stewardship is not only a gross misrepresentation of the judiciary's role but a dangerous undermining of one of the country's foundational pillars: the separation of powers.

The Reaction: Divisiveness Personified

Predictably, Trump's post polarized the digital realm. Figures like Eric Daughtery and the user known as Catturd lauded the president's words as "glorious" and "classic," showcasing the disheartening reality that there is a market for Trump's brand of divisiveness, even on days meant for national unity.

Conversely, voices of reason emerged from the cacophony of reactions, labeling Trump's outburst as "ridiculous," "divisive," and "anti-American." The criticism wasn't just about the disrespect shown to the memory of the fallen but about the broader implications of having such a figurehead leading the national discourse.

A Day of Remembrance Hijacked

Memorial Day serves as a poignant reminder of the sacrifices made for the freedoms enjoyed today. It is a day for reflection, gratitude, and unity. Trump's decision to use this platform for a politically charged and factually dubious rant not only disrespects those who have given their lives but sows further division in an already polarized society.

Unfit to Lead?

Critics and observers have long questioned Trump's fitness to lead, citing his penchant for misinformation, divisiveness, and a seemingly innate desire to place personal grievances over national interest. This latest episode only adds fuel to that fire, with figures like Evaristus Odinikaeze condemning his actions as a "disgrace to the memory of those who gave everything for this country."

Conclusion: A Memorial Day Marred

Trump's Memorial Day message will be remembered, but not for the reasons he may have hoped. Instead of a unifying tribute to heroes past, it will stand as a testament to his continued ability to divide and demean. On a day meant for solemn remembrance, Trump chose self-aggrandizement and political theater over dignity and respect. It was, in every sense, a disgraceful display, wholly unbecoming of a leader and a stark reminder of the systemic errors that plague our political discourse.
