Jan. 6 cop makes startling prediction about Trump's post-presidency fate

A former police officer who nearly lost his life during the Jan. 6 insurrection mad a bold prediction about President Donald Trump’s eventual fate.

Former D.C. Metro police officer Michael Fanone, who was beaten and tased by Trump supporters during the U.S. Capitol riot, told journalist Tommy Christopher that he believes Trump will be forced to live out his days as a fugitive overseas after his second term ends, reported The Daily Beast.

“I foresee he’ll be hopping on that Qatari jet to Saudi Arabia and living out the remainder of his days in some guest house of the Saudi prince,” Fanone said. “I think things are gonna get a whole hell of a lot worse before they get any better.”

The 45-year-old former cop suffered cardiac arrest and a traumatic brain injury during the violent assault on the Capitol during the congressional certification of Trump’s 2020 election loss to Joe Biden, and he predicted bruising losses in next year’s midterm elections and a reckoning for the president.

“People are their most dangerous when they’re cornered and they have nothing left to lose,” Fanone said. “The likelihood — if there are free and fair elections in 2026 — of Donald Trump not being impeached, or at least investigated and having his administration investigated into oblivion, are slim to none.”

Fanone testified during the House select committee hearings on Jan. 6 that referred Trump for criminal prosecution, which he said led to threats by far-right militants in the Proud Boys, Oath Keepers and other groups, and he said Trump sought a second term for all the worst reasons.

“He doesn’t give a sh*t who he destroys, as long as he stays out of jail, gets his retribution, and makes billions of dollars off of crypto,” Fanone said.

Our Analysis:

The Fugitive Prognostication: A Bold, Yet Unsettled Vision

In a recent interview that reads more like a script for a political thriller than a sober analysis, Michael Fanone, a former D.C. Metro police officer who survived a harrowing ordeal during the January 6 insurrection, makes a bold prediction about Donald Trump’s post-presidency fate. According to Fanone, as reported by The Daily Beast, Trump is destined to become an international fugitive, seeking refuge in a Saudi Arabian “guest house” as a means to escape his legal and political troubles.

The Dramatic Plight of Michael Fanone

Fanone’s story is a stark reminder of the violence and chaos that unfolded on January 6. Suffering cardiac arrest and a traumatic brain injury, Fanone’s first-hand experience lends him a unique, albeit emotionally charged, perspective on the events and their aftermath. His assertion that things will worsen before any semblance of improvement is seen is a grim forecast that taps into the fears and tensions gripping the nation.

The Fugitive Scenario

The imagery of Trump hopping on a Qatari jet to live out his days as a fugitive in Saudi Arabia is sensational, to say the least. While it captures the imagination, it’s essential to ground such predictions in the realm of political and legal reality. The notion hinges on several speculative elements, not least the outcome of future elections and potential legal ramifications facing Trump.

The Underlying Assumptions

Fanone’s predictions rest on a series of cascading assumptions: first, that Trump will suffer “bruising losses” in upcoming elections; second, that such losses would corner him, making him “dangerous”; and third, that this scenario would lead to Trump becoming an international fugitive. Each step in this sequence is fraught with uncertainty and contingency.

A Dangerous Cornered Animal?

The metaphor of a cornered animal to describe Trump’s potential future state is a potent one. Fanone suggests that a cornered Trump would be at his most dangerous, presumably to the fabric of American democracy. This imagery is powerful but requires a careful examination of the political landscape, including the mechanisms of accountability in place to address any such threats.

The Legal and Political Reality

While Fanone’s perspective is deeply informed by his traumatic experiences, the leap to Trump as a fugitive is a significant one. It presupposes a level of legal and political failure that has yet to materialize fully. The legal system’s wheels, though slow, are turning, with investigations and potential indictments on the horizon.

Conclusion: A Caution Against Speculative Extremes

While Fanone’s prediction makes for a compelling narrative, it’s a stark reminder of the current political climate’s unpredictability and the deep divisions within the United States. Such a scenario, while within the realm of possibility, remains speculative and hinges on numerous variables that are far from certain.

In analyzing Fanone’s bold claims, it’s crucial to differentiate between the emotional weight of his experiences and the speculative nature of his predictions. The legal and political processes are complex and often unpredictable, making any definitive forecasts about Trump’s fate premature. As observers and analysts, our role is to scrutinize these processes with a critical eye, grounding our assessments in fact, even as we acknowledge the emotional and psychological toll of events like January 6.
