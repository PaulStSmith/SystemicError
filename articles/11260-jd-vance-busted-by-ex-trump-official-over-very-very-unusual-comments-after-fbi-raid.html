JD Vance busted by ex-Trump official over 'very, very unusual' comments after FBI raid

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosAugust 24, 20255 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Hours after FBI Director gave the go-ahead to his agents to raid the home and office of John Bolton, Vice President J.D. Vance was well prepared and ready to discuss it with NBC’s Kristin Welker during an extensive interview to be released in full on Sunday.

During an interview on Saturday on MSNBC, one former official from Donald Trump’s first term questioned the extent of VP’s involvement in a matter that should be outside his purview.

Speaking with Welker, Vance stated, “We are investigating Ambassador Bolton, but if they ultimately bring a case, it will be because they determine that he has broken the law. We’re going to be careful about that. We’re going to be deliberate about that, because we don’t think that we should throw people — even if they disagree with us politically, maybe especially if they disagree with us politically — you shouldn’t throw people willy-nilly in prison. You should let the law drive these determinations, and that’s what we’re doing."

After watching a clip of the Vance interview during an appearance on MSNBC’s “The Weekend,” former DHS official Miles Taylor claimed Vance’s comments should set off alarms.

“This is very, very unusual for the vice president to be this read into a criminal investigation that he should be at arm's length from,” he told the hosts. “And then in the report that you all mentioned at the top of the hour, we heard that the CIA director himself might have been the one to provide the evidence to the FBI director.”

“You know, as more of these details come out, it's starting to look completely unprecedented that all of these very top senior officials that work under Donald Trump, that know he wants to get revenge against John Bolton, are personally involved in the investigation of John Bolton,” he added. “I mean, the vice president's attestation that this had nothing to do with revenge was about as believable as what we were just talking about: [what] the administration has said on the Epstein files, because it would be a truly extraordinary coincidence that they've decided. to focus their investigative powers on a man that Donald Trump has wanted investigated for years.”

Our Analysis:

A Closer Look at the Bolton Raid: Political Machinations Unveiled

In what reads like a script torn from the pages of a political thriller, the recent FBI raid on John Bolton's home and office, following orders from FBI Director, unfurls a tapestry of intrigue, power plays, and questionable legal maneuvers. The involvement of Vice President J.D. Vance, as detailed in an NBC interview, not only raises eyebrows but sets off a cacophony of alarm bells regarding the sanctity of law and the political impartiality of criminal investigations.

Vance's Unusual Candor: A Veil Over Vengeance?

Vance's readiness to discuss the ongoing investigation into Bolton—a figure long embroiled in controversy and discord within the Trump administration—signals not a moment of transparency but a calculated move in a larger game of political chess. His comments, aimed at projecting a facade of legal prudence and fairness, instead peel back the layers on what appears to be a deeply politicized decision to target a known adversary of Donald Trump.

"We are investigating Ambassador Bolton...because they determine that he has broken the law." Vance's statement, under the guise of legal propriety, thinly veils the true nature of this operation. It's not about the law; it's about settling scores. The invocation of a deliberate and careful process by Vance does little to mask the underlying motive likely driving this investigation: revenge.

The Alarming Involvement of High-Level Officials

The revelation that senior officials, including the CIA director, could be entangled in providing evidence for the raid is not just concerning—it's downright alarming. Such direct involvement of top-tier officials in a case against a political opponent of the former president transcends the bounds of standard operational procedure. It smacks of a vendetta, executed under the cover of legal investigation, orchestrated by those in the highest echelons of power.

Miles Taylor's commentary on the situation sheds a harsh light on the reality of these developments. The notion that the Vice President, among others, is "read into" a criminal investigation of this nature reveals a startling breach of the expected separation between political leadership and law enforcement activities. This isn't just unusual; it's a glaring red flag signaling the potential erosion of democratic norms and the rule of law.

The Unprecedented Nature of the Investigation

The targeting of John Bolton—an outspoken critic of Donald Trump—does not come as a surprise. However, the extent to which the administration appears to have marshaled its resources to pursue him reeks of political retribution, not justice. To believe Vance's assertion that this investigation is devoid of any vendetta requires a suspension of reality. The facts lay bare a different truth: this is an orchestrated attack, leveraging the machinery of government against a perceived political enemy.

The comparison to the administration's stance on the Epstein files only further illustrates the disingenuous nature of Vance's claims. The parallels drawn between the two scenarios underscore a pattern of behavior that prioritizes political expediency over transparency and accountability.

Conclusion: The Erosion of Democratic Norms

What we're witnessing is not just an investigation into John Bolton. It's a symptom of a larger, more troubling trend: the use of government power to pursue personal vendettas and suppress dissent. The involvement of Vance and other top officials in this case is emblematic of a political landscape where the lines between justice and revenge are increasingly blurred.

This saga—unfolding in real-time—demands our attention and scrutiny. It's a stark reminder of the fragility of democratic institutions and the paramount importance of safeguarding them against the encroachments of those who seek to bend the rule of law to their will. In the end, it's not just about Bolton; it's about the soul of American democracy.
