Security expert blasts Trump's 'cabinet of dunces' for posting on Twitter 'like you're 12'

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosDecember 7, 20253 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

President Donald Trump holds a Cabinet meeting, Wednesday, April 30, 2025, in the Cabinet Room. (Official White House Photo by Molly Riley)

National Security attorney Bradley Moss and international affairs specialist Tom Nichols took turns dumping on the mounting idiocy of officials working under President Donald Trump as the administration closes out its first year in office.

Moss was particularly furious that the administration was dumb enough to order a second strike on an upturned boat of noncombatants in the Caribbean, claiming — without evidence — that they were transporting drugs to the U.S., which is not an act of war.

“This is not … the ‘cabinet of rivals’ you would have thought of from the Lincoln days or even from Obama’s first term. This is the cabinet of dunces. These are a bunch of childish grown adults who are way too online saying things to generals like FAFO, as if they’re my teenage daughter,” said Moss. “No, you’re a grown man. Act like a grown man being secretary of defense. Comply with the rules, comply with the law, and stop posting on twitter like you’re 12.”

Nichols said voters owe the existence of so many imbeciles under one roof to a subservient Republican Party.

“I wrote at the beginning of this term that [Secretary of Defense Pete] Hegseth was the most dangerous and incompetent of all of the dangerous and incompetent appointees, but the list goes on, not just [FBI Director Kash] Patel and [Homeland Security head Kristi] Noam, but also [Robert F.] Kennedy and [Tulsi] Gabbard and so many others. I mean, this really is just a clown car,” said Nichols. “The FBI used to strike terror into the hearts of our enemies on American soil. The soviets who were screwing around over here in the United States during the cold war really feared the FBI. Is anybody really afraid of Kash Patel?”

“These people shouldn’t have been nominated,” Nichols said. “The real fault here lies with a GOP majority in the Senate that knew these people were incompetent. They knew it, and they absolutely enabled it because they didn’t want to get sideways with Donald Trump. And now we’re stuck with the fallout.”

Moss told MS NOW host Ali Velshi that his drink “almost came out of my nose” when FBI Deputy Director Dan Bongino admitted to Fox News host Sean Hannity on Thursday that he lied for a living before Trump installed him at the FBI.

“I’m like, ‘are you serious? You just said that out loud?’ They’re admitting what they were during the Biden years: they were paid shills,” said Moss. “We know a whole lot of the social media influencers we discovered on the MAGA side who are effectively being paid by the Russians, whether or not they knew it was up for debate. But they are there to be paid liars. They are not there to base anything on facts, or even objectively reasonable interpretations of facts. They’re there to get clicks. They’re there to make a massive propaganda style clip they can repeat all over twitter, Instagram, TikTok and monetize it. And now they’re in government [whining] ‘oh, now I have to actually do things based on facts.’”
