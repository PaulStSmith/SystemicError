Musk deletes new Epstein post as battle with Trump continues

Elon Musk, Donald Trump (Photo via Kevin Lamarque)

Billionaire Elon Musk continued his battle with Donald Trump late Friday with a post on X where he once again invoked the Jeffrey Epstein files -- then deleted it a short time later.

The break-up with the president has been raging since Thursday after Trump took shots at Musk during a press availability with German Chancellor Friedrich Merz, with Musk returning fire on his social media account that led to the explosive accusation, "Time to drop the really big bomb: @realDonaldTrump is in the Epstein files. That is the real reason they have not been made public. Have a nice day, DJT!"

What has ensued is a schism within the MAGA base which has been calling for a release of government files about Epstein but also does not want to hear that the president might be tied to the accused pedophile.

In an X exchange of Friday, Musk parried complaints from a close ally who is also a supporter of Trump.

In a screenshot that was captured by multiple observers, including Mediaite, businessman James Fishbeck, wrote, "Elon, Keep tweeting about your 'new political party' and Japanese fertility rates all you want. You owe President Trump a full-throated apology, and every hour you delay, it’ll make an eventual apology less sincere. You can substantively disagree with the President on policy, but you should not have baselessly and personally attacked him."

Over three posts, since deleted, Musk responded, "What the apology for exactly?" followed by, "Be precise," which led to his mention Epstein again, where he wrote: "I will apologize profusely as soon as there is a full dump of the Epstein files."

Our Analysis:

The Spectacle of Billionaire Bickering: Musk vs. Trump

In the latest episode of what can only be described as an uber-wealthy melodrama, Elon Musk, the tech mogul whose ventures range from electric cars to space exploration, has once again thrust himself into the political arena with a series of provocative claims and deletions on the social media platform X (formerly known as Twitter). This time, Musk's target is none other than former President Donald Trump, with whom he has had a public falling out. The crux of their dispute? The infamous Jeffrey Epstein files.

The Epstein Accusation: A Bombshell or a Dud?

Musk's bold assertion that Trump is mentioned in the Epstein files is explosive, yet it is marred by a lack of substantiation. This isn't the first time high-profile names have been thrown into the Epstein saga, but Musk's claim, "Time to drop the really big bomb: @realDonaldTrump is in the Epstein files. That is the real reason they have not been made public. Have a nice day, DJT!" was made without providing any evidence. This is a dangerous game of insinuation that muddies the waters further on an already sensitive issue. The fact that Musk deleted his post shortly after only adds to the suspicion that this may be more about personal vendettas than about shedding light on the truth.

The MAGA Base: Caught in a Crossfire

What's particularly fascinating about this squabble is the position it puts the MAGA base in. This group has been vocally demanding the release of the Epstein files, presumably to uncover the truth about various elites. However, the suggestion that Trump could be implicated puts them in a precarious position of cognitive dissonance. Do they continue to push for transparency, even if it means potentially implicating their champion? Or do they back down to protect Trump's reputation? It's a revealing moment that tests the limits of their allegiance.

Musk's X Drama: An Unbecoming Stage

Musk's engagement with Trump supporters on X, including his interaction with businessman James Fishbeck, underscores a broader issue with how Musk uses his platform. His response, "What the apology for exactly?" followed by a demand for precision, and then a reiteration of his call for the Epstein files, shows Musk is playing a game. It's a mix of taunting, evasion, and demands for accountability that feels more suited to a reality TV storyline than a serious discussion about accountability and transparency.

The Real Losers

In this high-stakes game of accusations and deletions, the real losers are the public and the victims of Epstein's crimes. The serious implications of the Epstein case - which involves allegations of sex trafficking and abuse - are overshadowed by the spectacle of billionaire bickering. Musk's deletion of the posts in question only serves to detract from the gravity of the Epstein case, turning it into just another episode in the saga of Musk vs. Trump.

Conclusion

The Musk-Trump spat, with its implications of hidden truths and political fallout, is a stark reminder of how the wealthy and powerful can dominate the discourse, often at the expense of truth and justice. Musk's provocative, yet unsubstantiated claims about the Epstein files not only fail to contribute anything of value to the public discourse but also potentially detract from the real and serious investigation needed. In this circus, it's the spectacle that captivates, leaving the pursuit of truth as the ultimate casualty.
