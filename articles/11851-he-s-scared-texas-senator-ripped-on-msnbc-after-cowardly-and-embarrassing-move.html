'He's scared': Texas senator ripped on MSNBC after 'cowardly and embarrassing' move

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosAugust 10, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

John Cornyn (Shutterstock)

Rep. Greg Casar (D-TX) tore into Sen. John Cornyn (R-TX) on MSNBC's "All In" Friday evening, after the senator announced he had activated the FBI to hunt down Democratic Texas state legislators who left the state to break quorum and obstruct the GOP from passing an aggressive re-gerrymander of the state's congressional districts.

It's unclear exactly what the FBI will do and what powers they would have to track down Democratic lawmakers, as they have not been charged with either a state or federal crime.

But the mere threat is shameful, Casar, whose district is one of five targeted by the GOP redraw, told anchor Ali Velshi.

"John Cornyn, who was once sort of considered a moderate, now showing that because he's scared he might lose an upcoming primary, he's now calling on the FBI to go arrest Democratic elected officials when there's been no crime committed, when the FBI should be going after terrorists or drug traffickers," said Casar. "Instead, he wants to send them after his political opponents."

"I mean, it's just cowardly and embarrassing," he added. "And I think it would be sad if it weren't also so dangerous."

As all of this is going on, Gov. Greg Abbott and state Attorney General Ken Paxton have filed their own measures urging the state Supreme Court to declare the seats of various Democrats who left the state vacant, which attorneys representing the lawmakers have hit back on with a lengthy filing arguing this is an illegal abuse of power.

Our Analysis:

The Misuse of Power: A Deep Dive into the Texas Political Theatre

In what can only be described as a brazen display of political machination, Sen. John Cornyn (R-TX) has escalated a state political dispute to the national stage by invoking the FBI to pursue Democratic Texas state legislators. These legislators had left the state in a strategic move to prevent the GOP from passing what has been termed an "aggressive re-gerrymander" of Texas' congressional districts. This maneuver by Cornyn is not just a gross misapplication of federal resources, but it's a startling insight into the lengths some politicians will go to maintain power.

The Absurdity of the FBI Involvement

Let's break down the absurdity of using the FBI to track down lawmakers who have committed no crime. They have merely engaged in a political protest by breaking quorum - a tactic as old as democracy itself and far from a federal offense. The FBI, an agency with priorities such as combating terrorism and drug trafficking, is now being redirected towards a political scuffle. This isn’t just a misallocation of resources; it's a dangerous precedent that threatens to politicize federal law enforcement.

Cornyn's Political Desperation

Rep. Greg Casar (D-TX), whose district is directly in the crosshairs of the GOP's re-drawing efforts, has characterized Cornyn’s actions as both “cowardly” and “embarrassing”. And rightly so. Cornyn, once viewed as a moderate, seems to fear the repercussions of an upcoming primary, indicating a willingness to leverage federal power for personal political gain. This is not the hallmark of a statesman but a clear sign of political desperation.

The Legal Quagmire

On another front, Gov. Greg Abbott and state Attorney General Ken Paxton have sought the intervention of the state Supreme Court to declare the seats of the absconding Democrats vacant. This legal maneuver is not just audacious but highlights a deep-seated intent to exploit legal frameworks to disenfranchise political opposition. The response from attorneys representing the Democratic legislators, branding this as an "illegal abuse of power", underscores the precarious position democracy finds itself in when those in power resort to legal gymnastics to cling onto it.

Conclusion: Democracy in Peril

What we're witnessing in Texas is not just a state issue; it’s a national alarm bell. The actions of Cornyn and co. illuminate a worrying trend of political figures weaponizing their positions and federal resources against opponents, blurring the lines between legal authority and political vendettas. This is not merely a “sad” or “embarrassing” state of affairs, as Rep. Casar suggests. It's a glaring red flag for the health of American democracy.

The use of the FBI to chase down political opponents for a non-crime is a misuse of power that cannot be understated. It’s a dangerous path that leads to the erosion of trust in federal institutions, further polarizing an already divided nation. The implications of this saga extend beyond the borders of Texas; it's a cautionary tale of what happens when the mechanisms of law enforcement can be co-opted for political warfare.

In essence, the unfolding drama in Texas is not just a political scuffle; it's a systemic error in our democracy that requires urgent correction.
