Weaponizing Fear: Gorka’s Counterterrorism Strategy as Political Theater

A Distraction Dressed as Strategy

The recent release of a 16-page “counterterrorism strategy” crafted by Sebastian Gorka, President Trump’s counter-terrorism “czar”, is a textbook example of political distraction through bombast. The document, which targets a variety of groups and ideologies while conspicuously ignoring the rising threat of far-right domestic terrorism, serves not so much as a plan to secure the nation but as a vehicle for partisan attacks and ideological showmanship. Gorka’s strategy is not rooted in a clear assessment of threats but in a distorted vision colored by personal and political biases.

Misdirected Blame and Partisan Attacks

The strategy document is striking for its omissions and its targets. It neglects the well-documented rise of right-wing extremism, arguably the most pressing domestic terror threat in the U.S. today, as identified by numerous security experts and agencies. Instead, it fixates on “radically pro-transgender ideologies” and the nebulous category of “left-wing violent extremists,” framing them as significant threats. This misdirection serves a dual purpose: vilifying political adversaries and deflecting attention from more politically inconvenient threats.

The Real Threats Ignored

The omission of right-wing extremism in a counterterrorism strategy at this juncture of American history is not just an oversight; it is a deliberate act that potentially endangers American lives. By ignoring the real and present danger posed by these groups, the strategy fails in the most basic requirement of a security policy: to identify and counteract the most significant threats. Instead, it reads as a partisan pamphlet, prioritizing the vilification of political opponents and marginalized communities over national security.

Institutional Cowardice and Propaganda

Labeling this document a “counterterrorism strategy” is a misuse of the term. A genuine strategy would involve a sober, comprehensive analysis of threats based on data and trends, followed by actionable, detailed plans to address these threats. What Gorka delivers is instead an exercise in propaganda, echoing Trump’s broader political rhetoric that often seeks to demonize opponents and distract from his administration’s shortcomings. The strategy weaponizes the intelligence community against perceived political enemies under the guise of national security.

Consequences of Misguided Priorities

The consequences of such a strategy—if it can be called that—are profound. Misallocating resources to non-threats while ignoring burgeoning dangers undermines national security. It sows division and fear, weaponizing government power against ideological opponents while allowing actual threats to fester and grow. Furthermore, by alienating marginalized communities and democratic allies with reckless rhetoric, it erodes the very fabric of societal trust and international cooperation essential for effective counterterrorism.

Systemic Insight: National Security as Political Theater

The Gorka document is emblematic of a larger pattern within the Trump administration: the use of national security tools as extensions of political agendas. This strategy of blending security policy with partisan attack does not merely fail to protect Americans—it actively harms the nation by degrading the institutions meant to safeguard it and by promoting a skewed vision of America that sees enemies in political opponents rather than in genuine threats. As such, it serves as a stark reminder of the dangers posed when national security becomes subordinate to political expediency.
