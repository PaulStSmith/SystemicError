Senate Republican suggests 'traitor' Dem 'leaked' strike that killed Iranian leader

The Benny Show

Sen. Ron Johnson (R-WI) said he was “suspicious” that Rep. Ilhan Omar (D-MN) might have committed treason by leaking President Donald Trump’s plan to strike Iranian leadership, many of whom are now dead.

On Tuesday, Sen. Johnson spoke to MAGA influencer Benny Johnson on a broadcast titled, “Ilhan Omar LEAKED U.S. Military Attack Plans to IRAN — Treason?”

Benny Johnson alleged that Omar “leaked” Operation Epic Fury to Iran through a post on X four days before the strikes. In the post, the Minnesota Democrat said that it was “sickening to know that the US is again going to attack Iran during Ramadan.”

“Ilhan Omar told Iran exactly when we would attack,” the MAGA influencer said. “She did it in public. And it begs of the question, what did she know? When did she know it?”

“I can’t find any other tweets from any other members of Congress staying with such precise certitude when we’re going to attack Iran,” he added before introducing Ron Johnson. “Let’s jump on over to our senator. Ladies and gentlemen, Senator Ron Johnson, locked and loaded and ready to go. Is Ilhan Omar a traitor?”

“And given the fact that Rubio and the CIA director had just briefed the gang of eight about our war plans, you know, I mean, it just seems Iran — I just see the timing just seems very strange to me, and extremely reckless, of course, Americans have now died in this conflict, and it bothers us.”

Sen. Johnson agreed: “Well, it is suspicious. I certainly don’t trust the Congresswoman.”

“You know, it’s one of the reasons that we’ve given — the Constitution gives the President of the United States the authority over these foreign affairs and quite honestly kind of military action,” the senator continued. “You cannot brief Congress and expect to hold or have war plans held confidential. They’re going to leak.”

“You’re going to have people that might be traitors that way.”

The senator did not explain how the U.S. was able to kill so many Iranian leaders if they knew of the strikes in advance.

In a post on X, Elon Musk’s Grok AI chatbot disagreed with Benny Johnson’s allegation about a leak.

“No, available public records do not indicate Rep. Ilhan Omar leaked classified U.S. military attack plans to Iran or committed treason,” the chatbot said. “Her Feb 27 post stated the U.S. ‘is again going to attack Iran during Ramadan,’ framing it as politically motivated. This was public speculation/criticism ahead of reported strikes (which occurred during Ramadan), not specific details like targets or timing passed privately to Iran.”

“As a Foreign Affairs Committee member, she may have had general briefings, but no sources show she disclosed classified info or aided an enemy—treason’s narrow constitutional definition,” Grok concluded. “The accusation originates from interpretive media claims without supporting evidence of a leak.”

Our Analysis:

The Accusation: A Masterclass in Political Deflection

In a recent display of what can only be described as political theater at its most dubious, Sen. Ron Johnson (R-WI) entertained the notion that Rep. Ilhan Omar (D-MN) might have committed treason by allegedly leaking President Donald Trump’s supposed strike plans against Iranian leadership. This accusation, aired during a conversation with MAGA influencer Benny Johnson, is a textbook example of the art of deflection and unsubstantiated claims that plague our political discourse.

Let’s Break Down the Facts:

The Baseless Allegation

Benny Johnson’s claim centers on a post made by Rep. Omar on X, where she criticized the U.S. for attacking Iran during Ramadan. Johnson leaps to an extraordinary conclusion from Omar’s public statement, suggesting it was a leak of classified military plans. This leap is not only gargantuan in its logic but dangerously accuses a sitting Congresswoman of treason without a shred of hard evidence.

Sen. Ron Johnson’s Echo Chamber

Sen. Johnson, instead of bringing clarity or skepticism to the wild accusation, chooses to amplify it. His comments on not trusting Rep. Omar and questioning the timing of her remarks only serve to fan the flames of conspiracy without offering any concrete evidence. His assertion that briefing Congress on military plans is tantamount to expecting leaks is a cynical view that undermines the very foundation of our democratic oversight processes.

The Role of Grok AI

In an interesting twist, Elon Musk’s Grok AI chatbot emerges as the voice of reason. The AI, analyzing available public records, found no evidence that Rep. Omar leaked classified U.S. military plans to Iran. It points out that Omar’s post was in the realm of public speculation and criticism, far from the disclosure of specific military details constituting treason.

The Heart of the Matter

The accusation against Rep. Ilhan Omar is not just about her; it’s a reflection of a deeper malaise within American politics where sensational accusations can be hurled without evidence, and where public figures are ready to entertain conspiracy theories for political gain. This incident underscores the dangerous politicization of national security issues and the reckless bandying about of serious accusations like treason.

Conclusion

In sum, the allegations against Rep. Omar, as entertained by Sen. Johnson and propagated by Benny Johnson, lack substance and fall squarely into the realm of political fear-mongering. It’s a stark reminder of the necessity for critical thinking and the demand for evidence before making explosive claims. Our political discourse deserves better than baseless accusations and the careless undermining of democratic institutions. As the Grok AI analysis suggests, this is a case where the facts do not align with the sensationalism of the accusations—a pattern all too common in today’s political environment.
