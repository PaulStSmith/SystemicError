'Get rid of him ASAP!' Trump rages at lawyer hired by Harvard to engage with White House

By Paulo Santos

Donald Trump (Photo via Reuters)

President Donald Trump raged against Harvard University and one of its lawyers in a venomous Truth Social post.

The president ramped up his attacks on Harvard, which is being pushed by some donors to renew talks with the White House on its demands for control over the university, and he singled out an attorney hired by the university to engage with the Trump administration.

"Harvard is an Anti-Semitic, Far Left Institution, as are numerous others, with students being accepted from all over the World that want to rip our Country apart," Trump posted on his social media site. "The place is a Liberal mess, allowing a certain group of crazed lunatics to enter and exit the classroom and spew fake ANGER AND HATE. It is truly horrific!"

ALSO READ: 'Dictatorship, not a town hall': Families 'distraught' as MTG disruptors tased and jailed

The Harvard Corporation, which runs the school, refused to agree to any of the demands sent to by the White House, apparently by mistake, and the corporate board has seemingly been emboldened by public support for their resistance and has rebuffed at least three overtures from the administration seeking to negotiate and filed a lawsuit this week challenging threats to slash federal funding.

"Now, since our filings began, they act like they are all 'American Apple Pie,'" Trump posted. "Harvard is a threat to Democracy, with a lawyer, who represents me, who should therefore be forced to resign, immediately, or be fired. He’s not that good, anyway, and I hope that my very big and beautiful company, now run by my sons, gets rid of him ASAP!"

Trump is apparently referring to William A. Burck, an attorney with conservative bona fides who serves as an outside ethics adviser to the Trump Organization who, according to a New York Times report, was hired by Harvard to engage with the administration.

The university rejected Burck's advice on litigation, which he said could be risky, and filed the lawsuit anyway, but Harvard emeritus law professor Alan Dershowitz said his hiring sent different messages to different audiences.

“To the faculty, they’re saying, ‘We’re tough, we’re standing up to the government,’” Dershowitz toldBusiness Standard. “But to the government, they’re saying ‘We’re hiring your guy.’"

Our Analysis:

Trump's Tirade Against Harvard: A Closer Look

President Donald Trump's latest outburst on Truth Social against Harvard University and its legal counsel is a spectacular showcase of his characteristic blend of misinformation, paranoia, and personal vendettas. Trump's attack is not just a simple rant; it's a microcosm of his broader assault on academic freedom, legal integrity, and basic principles of democracy. Let's dissect the claims and the chaotic logic behind them.

Harvard: An "Anti-Semitic, Far Left Institution"?

Trump's labeling of Harvard as an "Anti-Semitic, Far Left Institution" is not just baseless; it's ironically contradictory. Anti-Semitism is a scourge that both the far-right and far-left, at their most extreme, can harbor. To label an entire institution like Harvard, known for its diverse and inclusive environment, as anti-Semitic without substantial evidence is not just reckless; it's dangerous. Furthermore, Trump's broad brush tarring of Harvard as "far left" is a tired tactic used to discredit academic institutions that encourage critical thinking and freedom of speech, qualities anathema to Trump's authoritarian tendencies.

The Myth of the "Liberal Mess"

Trump's portrayal of Harvard as a "Liberal mess" allowing "crazed lunatics" to foster "fake ANGER AND HATE" is a gross misrepresentation. Universities are meant to be bastions of free thought and expression, where diverse opinions, including those critical of the status quo and government policies, can be expressed safely. This demonization of dissent is a hallmark of Trumpian rhetoric, aiming to delegitimize any criticism of his administration or policies.

The Harvard Corporation's Resistance

The article highlights the Harvard Corporation's refusal to acquiesce to the White House's demands, which included control over university policies. This resistance is not just commendable; it's a necessary stance in safeguarding academic independence against political interference. Trump's insistence that Harvard's refusal to bend to his will makes it a "threat to Democracy" is an Orwellian inversion of reality. In Trump's world, defiance against his authority is tantamount to undermining democracy, a perspective that reveals a fundamental misunderstanding of what democracy entails.

The Case of William A. Burck

The targeting of William A. Burck, a lawyer with conservative credentials, for his association with Harvard, is a clear example of Trump's penchant for personal loyalty over professional integrity. Trump's assertion that Burck should be forced to resign or be fired for representing Harvard, despite his role as an outside ethics adviser to the Trump Organization, is troubling. It underscores Trump's expectation of unwavering personal allegiance, superseding the ethical obligations and professional independence of legal counsel. Moreover, Harvard's decision to reject Burck's advice and proceed with litigation underscores the university's commitment to its principles, irrespective of the political affiliations of its advisers.

Conclusion: Trump's Assault on Academic Freedom

Trump's rant against Harvard is more than just an attack on a single institution; it's indicative of his broader disdain for academic freedom, legal independence, and democratic principles. His vilification of dissent, demand for personal loyalty over professional ethics, and misunderstanding of democracy's core values are characteristics of an authoritarian mindset. As the article reveals, Trump's fury is not just about Harvard; it's about challenging any entity that dares to stand up to him. In doing so, Trump not only exposes his own vulnerabilities but also the strength of the institutions that refuse to bow to his will.
