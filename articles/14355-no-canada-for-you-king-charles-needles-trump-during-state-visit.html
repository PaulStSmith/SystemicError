'No Canada for you!' King Charles needles Trump during state visit

By Paulo Santos

Britain’s King Charles sits at the Senate of Canada Building in Ottawa, Ontario, Canada May 27, 2025. REUTERS/Blair Gable/Pool

Britain's King Charles let Donald Trump know exactly how he felt about the president's desire to absorb Canada as the 51st state of the USA.

Charles made what Reuters called "a symbolic visit" to the commonwealth nation Tuesday "to show support" in the face of Trump's threats of tariffs and annexation.

The King addressed Canada's parliament in what was dubbed "the throne speech" for the opening of the government's 45th session.

“Democracy, pluralism, the rule of law, self-determination, and freedom are values which Canadians hold dear and ones which the government is determined to protect,” Charles said, adding, “We will unleash a new era of growth that will ensure we don’t just survive the ongoing trade wars, but emerge from them stronger than ever."

Pro-Canada social media celebrated the King's remarks.

"No Canada for You: King Charles' Canada remarks seem to rebuke Trump," declared the account of Really American. "Forget about the 51st State."

Sunday Mail editor Sarah-Louise Robertson posted, "Strong words from #kingcharles at the Canadian Parliament on the importance of democracy and rule of law. He has spoken of Canada’s history and importance of its future relationship with British monarchy. A rebuff to Trump’s attempt to join it to the USA. [Canadian Prime Minister] Carney looks pleased.

"I am not by any means a royal watcher. However, King Charles visiting Canada at this point in our history is particularly important," wrote the account of @mjeso. The King of Canada giving the speech from the throne to open Parliament is a clear signal to those who advocate 51st state foolishness!"

Supporters were particularly delighted when Charles read a line from Canada's national anthem.

"King Charles III: 'As the anthem reminds us: The True North is indeed strong & free!' Canada's sovereignty is recognized! Got that Trump? Thank you King Charles & PM Carney for inviting His Majesty to address Canadians. I'm so proud of my [Canadian flag emoji]! Vive le Canada! Long live the King!" wrote Canada Strong.

Occupy Democrats wrote, "King Charles III of the United Kingdom enrages MAGA world by proclaiming that Canada will remain 'strong and free' amidst Donald Trump's efforts to turn it into the '51st state.' And it gets even better... 'The ‘True North is indeed strong and free,' Charles said during an address on the first day of Canadian Parliament."

Charles's visit marks just the third time a monarch has delivered the throne speech to Canada's parliament.

Our Analysis:

King Charles vs. Trump's Annexation Fantasies: A Royal Rebuff

In a world where the absurd too often becomes the headline, the saga of Donald Trump's aspirations to transform Canada into the 51st state of the United States reads like a plot rejected for being too far-fetched even for the most avant-garde political satire. Yet, here we are, dissecting a scenario where Britain's King Charles has publicly and pointedly rebuked such imperial fantasies.

The Scene: A "symbolic visit" to Canada by King Charles, as characterized by Reuters, ostensibly to bolster Canadian morale against Trump's annexation threats. The monarch's address to Canada's Parliament, now famously dubbed "the throne speech," was laden with not-so-subtle jabs at Trump's ambitions. Charles's rhetoric was a cocktail of democratic values—democracy, pluralism, the rule of law, self-determination, and freedom—served ice-cold to the former U.S. president's expansionist dreams.

"We will unleash a new era of growth that will ensure we don’t just survive the ongoing trade wars, but emerge from them stronger than ever," Charles proclaimed, a line that could be interpreted as nothing short of a battle cry against economic threats posed by Trump's administration.

The Public Reaction: A Symphony of Support

The response on social media was predictably jubilant. From the exclamatory praise of Really American's "No Canada for You: King Charles' Canada remarks seem to rebuke Trump," to the Sunday Mail editor Sarah-Louise Robertson's commendation of Charles's strong stance on democracy and rule of law, the chorus of approval was loud and clear.

Notably, the sentiment wasn't just confined to professional commentators. Ordinary citizens and online personalities alike heralded Charles's speech as a pivotal moment, with tweets celebrating the recognition of Canada's sovereignty and the affirmation of its enduring strength and freedom.

The Royal Symbolism

The act of a British monarch addressing Canada's Parliament is rare, with Charles's speech marking only the third instance in history. This rarity underscores the gravity of the situation, a ceremonial gesture loaded with historical weight and contemporary significance.

Charles's strategic citation of Canada's national anthem was particularly poignant, emphasizing the nation's independence and resilience. "The True North is indeed strong & free!" is not merely a line from a song but a declaration of Canada's unwavering sovereignty, a sharp retort to any and all covetous eyes.

The Underlying Message

Beneath the ceremonious veneer and the diplomatic niceties, the message here is unambiguous and stark. The world is witnessing a moment where traditional allies are forced to reaffirm boundaries and principles in the face of unorthodox challenges. King Charles's visit and speech were not merely acts of solidarity but a bold statement against the erosion of international norms and respect for national sovereignty.

Conclusion: A Royal Checkmate

In conclusion, this episode serves as a fascinating case study of international relations, domestic politics, and the unwavering spirit of nations in the face of external pressures. King Charles's visit to Canada, culminating in his parliamentary address, was a masterclass in statecraft, symbolizing a united front against Trump's annexation pipe dreams.

The real takeaway here is the reaffirmation that values like democracy, freedom, and self-determination are not antiquated ideals but living, breathing principles that nations continue to rally around. In the end, it's not just about the rejection of a preposterous proposition but a celebration of the enduring spirit of countries like Canada—forever strong, forever free.
