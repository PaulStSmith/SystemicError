Move Over Gordon Ramsay, Here Comes Timmy: Why We Need Kids Judging Cooking Shows

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosMay 17, 20251 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Let’s be honest: kids are the most unfiltered, no-holds-barred food critics on Earth. You can spend three hours perfectly searing a steak, infusing it with rosemary harvested by monks at sunrise, plating it like edible art — and a kid will stare at it like it’s radioactive sludge and ask if you have any nuggets.

Which is exactly why we need a cooking show where kids are the judges.

We already have shows like MasterChef Junior where kids cook better than most adults — but when it comes to judging, it's always white-coated professionals swirling sauces and nodding sagely. Where’s the chaos? Where’s the honesty? Where’s the seven-year-old who smells the duck confit and says, “Ew, it smells like a barf bucket!” before requesting a hot dog with no mustard or else?

Imagine an Iron Chef, having trained under Michelin stars and forged meals in kitchens hotter than the sun, presenting his pièce de résistance... only to be met with a grimace and a “This tastes like sidewalk.”

Brutal? Yes. Entertaining? Absolutely.

Kids don’t care about truffle oil or deconstructed ravioli. They want mac and cheese, cereal that turns the milk weird colors, and the culinary masterpiece known as “peanut butter on a spoon.” Their standards are chaos incarnate — which is why they'd be the most honest judges in food TV history.

Forget “Top Chef.” Give us “Tiny Tastebuds.”

It’s time the culinary world got a little humility… and a whole lot of ketchup.
