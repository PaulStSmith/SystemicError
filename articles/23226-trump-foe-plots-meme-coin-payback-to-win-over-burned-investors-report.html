Trump foe plots meme-coin payback to win over burned investors: report

Based on an article by Bennito L. Kelty

Paulo SantosSeptember 8, 20262 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

The Laptop Is a Distraction. The State Capture Is the Story.

What Happened

Hunter Biden is launching a meme coin, $LAPTOP, on Base, the Coinbase-built blockchain. The token is explicitly pitched as a jab at Donald Trump, with a chunk reserved for people who lost money on $TRUMP. The setup is petty, theatrical, and designed to feed on the same outrage economy that has already turned both families into recurring content.

The Power Is Not Symmetrical

The relevant difference is institutional power. Hunter Biden is a private citizen selling a novelty token. Donald Trump is the president, and the source says his administration has installed friendly regulators, backed stablecoin legislation, and benefited from a crypto posture that has already helped his family earn roughly $1.4 billion last year, according to financial disclosures cited by the Journal.

That is the core fact the spectacle tries to blur. One side is monetizing humiliation. The other is governing while financially entangled with the industry it is supposed to regulate.

Revenge Is Not Accountability

Hunter Biden’s coin is not serious politics. It is a brand stunt built out of grievance, irony, and speculation. The source makes that clear by describing the token’s gimmicks, including planned token destruction if a Democrat wins in 2028 or if $LAPTOP outperforms $TRUMP.

That is not a democratic strategy. It is a market play dressed up as payback. If the Democratic coalition wants to defeat authoritarian politics, it cannot confuse attention-grabbing self-dealing for leverage. A token does not become a correction simply because it annoys Trump.

Trump’s Conflict Is the Real Story

The article’s weakest move is treating Trump’s crypto profiteering as one detail among many instead of the central abuse of power. The source says watchdogs have raised conflict-of-interest warnings and a broader crypto regulation bill has stalled. That is not a side note. It is what happens when a president profits from the very sector he is shaping through appointments and legislation.

This is the more serious pattern: public office used as an asset class. The scandal is not merely that Trump likes crypto. It is that his family’s financial upside is embedded in the same ecosystem where he exerts governmental influence.

When Scandal Becomes Content

The media-friendly framing here is that the Biden and Trump families are locked in some deranged novelty feud. That makes for easy clicks and clean tribal theater. It also flattens the actual hierarchy of harm.

Hunter Biden can make noise. Trump can move policy. One is a pundit’s baited hook. The other has the machinery of the federal government, regulator access, and the power to set the terms under which crypto expands or gets disciplined. The article is most useful when it accidentally shows that difference.

The Larger Pattern

This story is not about a coin. It is about the decay of democratic standards under conditions where private enrichment, political branding, and state authority bleed into one another. The democratic problem is not that one political family has become embarrassing. It is that the presidency itself is being used to normalize self-enrichment while opponents are pushed into reactive spectacle.

That is how corruption wins twice: first through policy capture, then through distraction.
