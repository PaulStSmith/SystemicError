Right-wing Newsmax secretly lobbied to kill funding for fact-checking and ethics: report

Investigative reporter Jason Garcia, who covers corporate influence in Florida, reported Wednesday that Newsmax hired lobbyists to secretly influence state funding for companies that rate news outlets for accuracy and ethics.

Writing on his Substack, Garcia noted that Newsmax grew its network in part by being one of the few outlets willing to promote false information about the 2020 election. The network was subsequently sued by two electronic voting companies, Smartmatic and Dominion Voting Systems. Newsmax paid $40 million to settle with Smartmatic and $67 million to settle with Dominion.

Garcia obtained documents showing Newsmax’s lobbyists attempted to convince Florida House members to approve a new law that would bar all state agencies from contracting with businesses that also work with “media reliability and bias monitors.”

According to Garcia, “The measure is meant to squeeze companies like NewsGuard Technologies, a seven-year-old startup co-founded by a former Wall Street Journal publisher that evaluates news outlets based on their accuracy and adherence to journalistic standards. NewsGuard’s credibility ratings are used by clients such as search engines, AI developers, news aggregators, and brand-sensitive businesses worried about advertising on disreputable sites.”

The legislation Newsmax wanted, House Bill 1449, failed—but Republicans added a similar provision at the last minute to an appropriations bill. The language was inserted into a budget bill that wasn’t released to the public until the final Friday evening of the session and was buried on page 143 of the 154-page bill.

State House rules mandate that lobbyists disclose each piece of legislation they’re lobbying for, but Garcia reported there were no disclosures filed for House Bill 1449.

Newsmax, which is based in Boca Raton, Florida, has close ties to President Donald Trump, with several allies serving as hosts or contributors to the network.

Our Analysis:

The Covert Maneuvers of Newsmax: A Scathing Analysis

In a recent revelation that underscores the treacherous waters of media manipulation and corporate influence in politics, Newsmax's behind-the-scenes maneuvers have surfaced, showcasing a blatant attempt to skew the legislative process in Florida to its advantage. Investigative reporter Jason Garcia's exposé on Newsmax's clandestine lobbying efforts offers a disturbing glimpse into how media entities can engage in underhanded tactics to safeguard their interests, at the cost of ethical standards and the public's trust.

The Heart of the Matter

At the core of this controversy is Newsmax's aggressive lobbying to push Florida lawmakers into severing ties with firms that dare to assess the accuracy and ethics of news outlets. By hiring lobbyists to work in the shadows, Newsmax aimed to undermine companies like NewsGuard Technologies, which has the audacity to evaluate news outlets based on journalistic standards. NewsGuard, by offering credibility ratings, plays a crucial role in guiding advertisers away from investing in platforms that propagate misinformation.

The Scheme Unveiled

The mechanism Newsmax hoped to employ was a law designed to prevent state agencies from contracting with businesses associated with "media reliability and bias monitors." This not only highlights a disdain for accountability but also reflects a perverse strategy to financially cripple entities that prioritize truth and integrity. While their preferred bill, House Bill 1449, floundered, the essence of it was craftily inserted into an appropriations bill—buried deep within to evade scrutiny. This maneuver, executed with the cunning of a seasoned political operative, raises serious questions about the transparency and integrity of the legislative process.

The Skullduggery of Stealth Lobbying

Perhaps most alarming is the revelation that no disclosures were filed for the lobbying efforts behind House Bill 1449. This lack of transparency is a grave violation of State House rules and a stark illustration of the shadowy tactics Newsmax resorted to. It's a flagrant attempt to manipulate the legislative process without leaving fingerprints, underscoring the lengths to which some entities will go to protect their interests, sidelining ethical considerations and public welfare.

Complicity and Consequences

Newsmax's actions, especially given its established connections to President Donald Trump and its role in disseminating falsehoods about the 2020 election, paint a bleak picture of a media outlet unbound by the principles of journalistic integrity. The settlements with Smartmatic and Dominion Voting Systems, amounting to over $100 million, are testament to the consequences of spreading misinformation—a costly reminder of the real-world impact of Newsmax's operations.

Conclusion: The Imperative of Vigilance

This episode serves as a stark reminder of the ongoing battle for truth in an era of rampant misinformation. It underscores the necessity for vigilance and accountability, particularly when entities like Newsmax engage in covert operations to undermine the mechanisms designed to protect the integrity of information. As consumers of news, we must remain alert to the manipulations that lurk behind the scenes, threatening to distort the legislative process and compromise the very foundation of democratic discourse. The actions of Newsmax are not just an affront to journalistic standards; they are a call to arms for all who value truth, transparency, and the ethical underpinnings of our society.
