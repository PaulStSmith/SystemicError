'This man is an utter clown': Trump brutally mocked after overnight 'unhinged' Obama meltdown

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosJuly 1, 20252 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

U.S. President Donald Trump looks on during the installation of a new flagpole on the South Lawn at the White House in Washington, D.C., U.S., June 18, 2025. REUTERS/Kevin Lamarque TPX IMAGES OF THE DAY

Although former President Barack Obama left office on January 20, 2017 — almost eight and one-half years ago — President Donald Trump continues to be obsessed with him. And he is furious with Sen. Chris Coons (D-Delaware) for comparing Trump's negotiations with Israel and Iran to Obama's Iran agreement.

Early Monday morning, June 30, Trump went to this Truth Social platform to let Coons know how vehemently he resents being compared to Obama in any way.

Trump ranted, "Tell phony Democrat Senator Chris Coons that I am not offering Iran ANYTHING, unlike Obama, who paid them $Billions under the stupid 'road to a Nuclear Weapon JCPOA (which would now be expired!), nor am I even talking to them since we totally OBLITERATED their Nuclear Facilities."

Trump's post is drawing plenty of mockery and ridicule on X, formerly Twitter.

X user Khalissee tweeted, "TRUMP ON IRAN: 'I'm not offering Iran anything — unlike Obama, who gave them $ billions under the failed JCPOA. I'm not even talking to them since we OBLITERATED their nuclear facilities.' This man is an utter clown."

Another X user, Fearless, wrote, "Trump barking again: 'We obliterated Iran's nuclear facilities.' Reality check: You obliterated nothing but your own credibility. Iran never begged you, and never will. You were never open for diplomacy — only for Israel's script. Your 'maximum pressure' gave zero results, except strengthening Iran's resolve and humiliating your administration on the global stage. The harsh truth? You weren't feared — just ignored. History will remember your threats as noise, not strategy."

Logan 8094 mockingly posted, "Who in the world taught them this word 'OBLITERATED'?"

Owl-X wrote, "He violated a military standard by immediately suggesting success without waiting for intelligence level confirmation. He is truly unhinged."
