In a video captured by writer Amanda Moore on Saturday, Dana Briggs was seen participating in a protest outside an Immigration and Customs Enforcement facility in Illinois.

Moore described the incident: “After conducting another arrest, agents were backing into the facility, nearing the gate to the grounds. Just as they reached the fence, they changed course and began moving forward, yelling at protesters to get out of the road. Briggs asks why, and an agent appears to push him, knocking him to the ground.”

The Justice Department alleged that Briggs “made physical contact with an agent’s arm while the agent attempted to extend the safety perimeter around the facility.”

A charging document claimed Briggs’ actions caused the agent to “experience pain” in his wrist.

Three other protesters were also charged with felony assault. Another faced misdemeanor charges of forcibly resisting, opposing, impeding, and interfering with a federal officer.

Our Analysis:

Analyzing the Charges Against a 70-Year-Old Air Force Veteran

In a recent turn of events that can only be described as dystopian theater, the Department of Justice has charged Dana Briggs, a 70-year-old Air Force veteran, with felony assault. This charge comes after a video surfaced showing a Department of Homeland Security agent pushing Briggs to the ground during a protest outside an Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) facility in Illinois. The video, captured by writer Amanda Moore, starkly contradicts the narrative pushed by the Justice Department, highlighting a concerning trend in the criminalization of protest and dissent.

The Incident in Question

According to Moore’s account, the altercation occurred as agents, after making an arrest, were retreating into the facility. In a seemingly unprovoked response to Briggs asking a question, an agent appears to push him, resulting in Briggs falling to the ground. This video evidence raises significant questions about the use of force and the subsequent framing of the incident by the Justice Department.

The Justice Department’s Allegations

The allegations against Briggs, as outlined by the Justice Department, claim that he “made physical contact with an agent’s arm,” allegedly causing the agent to “experience pain” in his wrist. This narrative, supported by a charging document, strategically shifts the focus away from the aggressive actions of the DHS agent captured on video and onto a highly questionable claim of assault by Briggs.

The Bigger Picture: A Disturbing Trend

This case is emblematic of a broader, more disturbing trend of law enforcement agencies using charges of felony assault to stifle and punish peaceful protest. The fact that a 70-year-old veteran is being charged for what appears to be, at most, a minimal physical interaction—in response to an unprovoked shove by an agent—is deeply troubling. It underscores a systemic effort to criminalize dissent, particularly in the context of protests against immigration enforcement practices.

The Disproportionate Reaction

The charging of Briggs and three other protesters with felony assault, and another with misdemeanor charges, is a disproportionate reaction that serves as a chilling reminder of the power imbalance between individual citizens and the state. The narrative pushed by the Justice Department not only seeks to vilify peaceful protesters but also attempts to justify the excessive use of force by law enforcement under the guise of maintaining safety.

Conclusion

The charges against Dana Briggs represent a miscarriage of justice that highlights the lengths to which the state will go to suppress dissent and protect its agencies from scrutiny. The video evidence, juxtaposed with the allegations made by the Justice Department, paints a clear picture of an attempt to criminalize a veteran who, by all accounts, was exercising his constitutional right to peaceful protest.

This incident should serve as a wake-up call to all who value civil liberties and the right to dissent. The criminalization of protest, under the thin veil of maintaining public safety, not only undermines the principles of democracy but also erodes public trust in law enforcement and justice institutions. As we continue to witness the unfolding of these events, it is imperative that we maintain a critical eye on the actions of the state and its agents, ensuring accountability and justice for all citizens, regardless of the uniform they wear or the causes they support.
