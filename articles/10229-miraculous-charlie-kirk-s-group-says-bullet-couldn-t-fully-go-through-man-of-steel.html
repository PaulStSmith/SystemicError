Charlie Kirk was killed by a bullet that didn't create an exit wound because the late commentator is a "man of steel," according to Kirk's Turning Point USA.

Kirk was shot at a "prove me wrong" event he was holding on a college campus in Utah. Police have arrested a local man by the name of Tyler Robinson.

When the suspect shot Kirk, the bullet should have gone through his neck and could have even endangered others, but that's not what happened. Instead, his organization's spokesman said, Kirk was so healthy that the bullet couldn't make it all the way through to the other side.

Spokesman Andrew Kolvet said, "I want to address some of the discussion about the lack of an exit wound with Charlie. I’m usually not interested in delving into most of this kind of online chatter, and I apologize this is somewhat graphic, but in this case, the fact that there wasn’t an exit wound is probably another miracle, and I want people to know."

He continued, saying, "I just spoke with the surgeon who worked on Charlie in the hospital… He said the bullet 'absolutely should have gone through, which is very very normal for a high powered, high velocity round. I’ve seen wounds from this caliber many times and they always just go through everything. This would have taken a moose or two down, an elk, etc.' But it didn’t go through."

According to the spokesman, this is evidence of Kirk's Superman-like level of health.

"Charlie’s body stopped it. I mentioned to his doctor that there were dozens of staff, students, and special guests standing directly behind Charlie on the other side of the tent, and he replied: 'It was an absolute miracle that someone else didn’t get killed.' 'His bone was so healthy and the density was so so impressive that he’s like the man of steel. It should have just gone through and through. It likely would have killed those standing behind him too.' In the end, the coroner did find the bullet just beneath the skin," he wrote. "Even in death, Charlie managed to save the lives of those around him. Remarkable. Miraculous."

Our Analysis:

A Strange Tale of Bulletproof Health: The Charlie Kirk Incident

In an episode that reads more like a comic book fantasy than a grim slice of reality, Turning Point USA has catapulted the tragic shooting of Charlie Kirk into the realm of miraculous phenomena. According to the organization, Kirk's extraordinary health and bone density stopped a bullet that, by all accounts of physics and medical science, should have sailed right through him. This analysis peels back the layers of this bewildering narrative to examine the facts, the science, and the sheer absurdity cloaking this incident.

The Claim of Superman-like Invulnerability

Andrew Kolvet, a spokesperson for Turning Point USA, has put forth a claim that seems to defy reality. He suggests that Kirk's lack of an exit wound from a high-velocity bullet is attributable to his supreme health condition, likening him to a man of steel. This assertion not only challenges medical knowledge but ventures into a realm of idolization that borders on the mythical.

The Medical Anomaly Explained

Kolvet's recounting of the surgeon's reaction adds a layer of mystique to the incident. The surgeon allegedly remarked on the bullet's failure to pass through Kirk's body as something that should have taken down large mammals like a moose or an elk. Yet, it was supposedly halted by Kirk's remarkable bone density. While the human body does indeed vary in its resilience, the suggestion that someone's health could render them bulletproof stretches credulity to its breaking point.

The Miraculous Save

Turning Point USA's narrative doesn't stop at celebrating Kirk's supposed unique physiology; it extends into heralding him as a posthumous hero. The bullet, stopped in its tracks by Kirk's body, allegedly prevented potential casualties among those standing behind him. While the intention to honor Kirk's memory is understandable, the framing here ventures into the territory of exploiting a tragedy for the sake of elevating his legacy to legendary status.

The Skeptic's View

A skeptic would raise eyebrows at several points in this story. First, the physics of bullets and human tissue. High-velocity rounds, especially those capable of downing large game, are designed to penetrate deeply. The notion that bone density, regardless of its robustness, could stop such a round is questionable at best. Second, the use of this narrative by Turning Point USA raises concerns about the motivations behind glorifying Kirk's death in such a fantastical manner. It seems to overshadow the tragic loss of life with a veneer of superhero mythology.

Conclusion: Between Fact and Fiction

In the wake of tragedy, it's not uncommon for those affected to seek solace in various narratives. However, the story presented by Turning Point USA regarding Charlie Kirk's death straddles the fine line between honoring the deceased and venturing into the realm of incredulous myth-making. While the desire to remember Kirk as a hero is understandable, it is vital to ground such remembrances in reality and respect for the facts. The tale of the bullet that couldn't find its exit wound will likely be debated and dissected, but it should not distract from the real human costs and the ongoing debate over gun violence in America. In the end, the memory of Charlie Kirk, and the lessons drawn from his life and untimely death, deserve a narrative rooted in truth, not fantasy.
