Fear as Trump slip-up apparently posts ominous private message on Truth Social: analyst

President Donald Trump publicly directed Attorney General Pam Bondi to aggressively target his political enemies with investigations over the weekend — and MSNBC's Jonathan Lemire reported it had Democratic leadership privately sweating.

The president posted — and then deleted — a message to Bondi complaining that "nothing is being done" about former FBI director James Comey, New York Attorney General Letitia James and Democratic Senator Adam Schiff, a day after federal prosecutor Erik Siebert complied with Trump's demand for his resignation for failing to bring mortgage fraud charges against James.

"There's some speculation that this was meant as a private message to Pam Bondi," Lemire told "Morning Joe." "The White House has not confirmed that, but the writing of it might have been meant as a DM, as opposed to a Truth Social post because Trump then took that post down, tried to clean it up with a subsequent posting.

"But this is, we know President Trump campaigned on the idea of retribution. That was one of his campaign platforms last year, and this is what that appears to look like, and we have seen U.S. attorneys across this country find a lack of evidence, there has not been a charge yet against Letitia James, the New York State attorney general. There have not been charges yet against Sen. Schiff or others."

"We have also seen, though, an effort by this president to remove a U.S. attorney who wouldn't bring charges, and he makes mention of that in this post, too, saying he needs to be replaced with someone perhaps more compliant, and I think this is what Democrats have been warning about for months, that we'd get to this step," Lemire added.

"Will there be follow-through? We'll see in the days ahead, but this is, as one senior Democrat put to me over the weekend, this is a five-alarm fire."

"Morning Joe" host Joe Scarborough agreed, saying that Trump had been announcing his intention to pursue criminal cases against the prosecutors who indicted him and the lawmakers who presided over his impeachment.

"Well, yeah, this is actually what Democrats and members of the media have been worrying about for years," Scarborough said. "I mean, you know, for years, the president promised and, you know, he said, 'I am your retribution,' and then as it got closer and then when he got elected, 'Oh, I'm not going to have time for retribution, it's up to them,' and this Truth Social post laid it all bare. Then the president sort of circled back and tried to soften his comments a little bit later, but it's very interesting."

"I would guess being an attorney for Donald Trump, who understands, as Rudy Giuliani understands, as Rudy Giuliani learned, you can say one thing when you're in front of the press, outside of a federal courthouse," Scarborough added. "You go into the courthouse, you better actually stick with the law and stick with the facts. When you're talking to a judge, bad things will happen to you eventually. Maybe not that day, but eventually you will be held to account if you try to lie."

Our Analysis:

Trump's Call to Arms: A Dangerous Precedent

President Donald Trump's recent public directive to Attorney General Pam Bondi, urging an aggressive stance against his political adversaries, is a glaring testament to a presidency marred by vendettas and a blatant disregard for the principles of justice. The incident, as reported by MSNBC's Jonathan Lemire and discussed on "Morning Joe," is not merely a political maneuver but a distressing revelation of Trump's willingness to weaponize the justice system against perceived enemies—a tactic that should alarm any observer of democratic norms.

The Dangerous Implications of Trump's Actions

Trump's actions, as described, are not just an overstep; they're a leap into authoritarian tactics. Directing an Attorney General to target political enemies like former FBI director James Comey, New York Attorney General Letitia James, and Democratic Senator Adam Schiff is not only unprecedented in modern American politics but also profoundly dangerous. It represents a stark departure from the expectation that the justice system operates independently of political whims or vendettas. The fact that Trump felt emboldened to issue such directives publicly—before attempting to retract them—speaks volumes about his perception of power and governance.

Weaponizing the Justice Department

The resignation of federal prosecutor Erik Siebert, allegedly for failing to bring mortgage fraud charges against Letitia James, underscores a chilling strategy: replace those who uphold the law with those willing to bend it for political gain. This maneuver is a hallmark of authoritarian regimes, not a democracy that prides itself on the rule of law and the separation of powers. Trump's subsequent attempt to "clean it up" with a later post does little to mitigate the damage. The message has been sent: loyalty to Trump, not the law, is the prized virtue in his administration.

The Response from Democratic Leadership

The report of Democratic leadership's private concerns, described as a "five-alarm fire," is telling. It's clear that Trump's actions have far-reaching implications, potentially undermining the very fabric of the American justice system. The alarm among Democrats—and, indeed, anyone committed to the principles of democracy and justice—is not just warranted; it's necessary. As Lemire points out, the lack of evidence against Trump's targets highlights the baselessness of his directives, further emphasizing the vindictive nature of his demands.

The Media's Role in Highlighting the Threat

"Morning Joe" host Joe Scarborough's observation that Trump has long promised retribution against his enemies—only to now act upon those threats—highlights the critical role of the media in holding leaders accountable. Scarborough's reference to Rudy Giuliani's legal troubles serves as a cautionary tale: the law eventually catches up. It underscores the importance of adhering to legal and ethical standards, something Trump's directive dangerously ignores.

Conclusion: A Call for Accountability

Trump's attempt to use the Justice Department as a tool for personal vendettas is a stark reminder of the ongoing battle for the soul of American democracy. This incident should not be viewed in isolation but as part of a broader pattern of behavior that seeks to undermine democratic institutions for personal gain. It's a call to action for all who value justice and democracy to stand in opposition to such tactics. The media, the public, and elected officials must remain vigilant and uncompromising in their demand for accountability. Trump's actions are not just an affront to his political enemies but to the very principles upon which the United States was founded.
