'Totally legit and not made up!' Mockery erupts as Trump awarded new 'Peace Prize'

President Donald Trump wears a medal as he is awarded the inaugural FIFA Peace Prize by FIFA President Gianni Infantino during the FIFA World Cup 2026 Draw at the John F. Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts, Washington, D.C. on Dec. 5, 2025. REUTERS/Jonathan Ernst

Mockery erupted after President Donald Trump received the inaugural FIFA peace prize Friday.

FIFA President Gianni Infantino gave Trump the shiny gold medal on stage at the FIFA World Cup 2026 Draw at the Kennedy Center — which Trump plans to rename after himself — in Washington, D.C.

The award was created a few weeks ago after Trump did not win the Nobel Peace Prize, which he has said he deserves.

“In an increasingly unsettled and divided world, it’s fundamental to recognise the outstanding contribution of those who work hard to end conflicts and bring people together in a spirit of peace,” Gianni Infantino, resident of Fédération Internationale de Football Association, said in a statement. “Football stands for peace, and on behalf of the entire global football community, the FIFA Peace Prize – Football Unites the World will recognise the enormous efforts of those individuals who unite people, bringing hope for future generations.”

Social media users had plenty to say after the news was announced:

“FIFA gave Donald Trump the single most unenthusiastic video awards package I have ever seen,” The Blacklist Founder and producer Franklin Leonard wrote on X, paired with the Ryan Gosling laughing meme.

“FIFA inventing a peace prize to give to Trump is beyond parody…” radio host and columnist James Hanson wrote on X.

“i know it has been made fun of constantly (and, please, keep doing so) ... but it cannot be overstated how INSANE it is that FIFA have anointed themselves as the arbiters of PEACE to give out this made up peace award to trump,” Tom Bogert, MLS writer and commentator, wrote on X.

“FIFA awarding Donald Trump a ‘peace prize’ is an insult to every supporter of the beautiful game who actually believes in justice and humanity,” sports journalist Shireen Ahmed wrote on X.

“The Fifa World Cup draw becoming brazenly used as Trump propaganda is the natural conclusion of the corruption inside FIFA for over two decades now. It’s a sad day for football,” writer Camilo Moreno-Salamanca wrote on X.

“On Bloomberg this afternoon Joe Mathieu was interviewing a constitutional lawyer about the the Trump admin’s illegal double tap missile strikes against fishing boats in the Caribbean but broke into the broadcast to announce that Trump had just awarded himself the ‘FIFA peace Prize.’ 10/10 no notes,” Chris Grimsley wrote on Bluesky.

“Trump getting a phony ‘Peace Prize’ is grifting recognizing a grifter. FIFA/Trump … Olympics/Hitler vibe,” a user named Oswego Willy wrote on Bluesky.

“Congrats to Donald Trump on the FIFA peace prize! Totally legit and not made up!” former foreign service officer Matthew Gillen wrote on Bluesky.

Our Analysis:

The Farce of the FIFA Peace Prize: Trump’s Latest Mockery

In a spectacle that would be comical if it weren’t so tragically absurd, President Donald Trump has been awarded the inaugural FIFA Peace Prize. This decision has sparked a mixture of disbelief and derision across the globe, and rightly so. The award, bestowed upon Trump by FIFA President Gianni Infantino during the World Cup 2026 Draw at the Kennedy Center—a venue Trump audaciously plans to rename after himself—reeks of opportunism and a profound misunderstanding of the concept of peace.

A Prize Born of Spite

Let’s not skirt around the elephant in the room: the timing and creation of the FIFA Peace Prize reek of pettiness. Mere weeks after Trump voiced his dissatisfaction with not winning the Nobel Peace Prize—a recognition he claims he deserves—the FIFA Peace Prize emerges from the void. Gianni Infantino’s statement that “Football stands for peace... the FIFA Peace Prize – Football Unites the World will recognize the enormous efforts of those individuals who unite people” would be laudable, if it weren’t so transparently hollow in this context.

Mockery and Disbelief

The reaction on social media was swift and scathing. Franklin Leonard’s observation that FIFA gave Trump “the single most unenthusiastic video awards package” alongside a meme perfectly encapsulates the widespread mockery. Yet, beneath the humor lies a deep-seated concern: FIFA, already notorious for its corruption scandals, has further tainted its reputation by aligning itself with Trump, a figure polarizing to say the least.

Critics like Tom Bogert and Shireen Ahmed didn’t mince words, highlighting the insanity of FIFA anointing itself as a peacemaker and calling the award an insult to every true supporter of peace and justice within the football community. Camilo Moreno-Salamanca’s comment that the FIFA World Cup draw was “brazenly used as Trump propaganda” is a damning indictment of how the beautiful game is being exploited for political gains.

The Grifter Recognizing a Grifter

Perhaps the most poignant critique came from users likening the scenario to “Olympics/Hitler vibes” and categorizing the award as “grifting recognizing a grifter.” These comments underscore the dangerous precedent of using sports—a domain meant to unite and inspire—as a tool for political maneuvering and self-aggrandizement.

Conclusion: A Sad Day for Football and Peace

The FIFA Peace Prize, in its current form and context, is not just an insult to the ideals of peace and unity but also to the integrity of football itself. It stands as a stark reminder of the sport’s vulnerability to corruption and political exploitation. As fans and supporters of the beautiful game, we must demand better from its governing bodies. The game, and the world, deserves leaders who genuinely strive for peace and unity, not those who use these noble goals as stepping stones for personal glory and propaganda.

The FIFA Peace Prize awarded to Donald Trump is not a recognition of peace; it’s a farce that tarnishes the spirit of the sport and mocks the very concept of genuine peacemaking efforts. In a world craving true leadership and unity, this episode serves as a sobering example of how easily virtue can be co-opted by the powerful for their own ends.
