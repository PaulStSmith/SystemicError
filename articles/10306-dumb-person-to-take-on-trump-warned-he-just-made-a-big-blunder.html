'Dumb person to take on': Trump warned he just made a big blunder

Democrats are fighting back against President Donald Trump's threats against broadcasters and critics after ABC and Disney suspended Jimmy Kimmel, claiming the late-night host is a "dumb person to take on" — and that they're ready to challenge the administration.

Democrats are now approaching the Kimmel suspension with major attention. Rep. Ro Khanna (D-CA) Thursday motioned unsuccessfully to subpoena FCC Chairman Brendan Carr — who threatened ABC/Disney on a podcast Wednesday ahead of pulling Kimmel's show — to testify about the incident, according to an Axios report Friday.

Kimmel is a "a dumb person to take on," Khanna told Axios. "If they go after comedy or they go after sports, those are two things you can't do in America."

A new resolution introduced by Rep. Yassamin Ansari (D-AZ) calls out the Trump administration for his attempt to silence critics and people who say things he doesn't like, according to an Axios report Friday.

"No president and no political party should weaponize the power of our federal government to punish those who disagree with them," Ansari said in a statement. "Silencing critics through government power is the playbook of authoritarian regimes. We're fighting this."

Carr on Wednesday threatened immediate action against Kimmel, ABC and Disney over the comments, claiming he deliberately misled the public and said "it appears to be some of the sickest conduct possible" in the wake of Kirk's killing. He also said that Kimmel should be suspended and that ABC should lose its broadcast license.

Hours later, Kimmel was suspended.

Other Democrats have announced plans to continue investigating in the wake of Kimmel's removal.

House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries (D-NY) said his team is calling for Carr to "resign immediately" and said it was time to seek the truth, "even if that requires the relentless unleashing of congressional subpoena power."

House Oversight Committee ranking member Robert Garcia (D-CA) has launched an investigation into ABC, Sinclair Broadcasting and the Trump administration. Rep. Jamie Raskin (D-MD) also told Axios he would launch an investigation and will "leave no stone unturned."

Our Analysis:

The Kimmel Kerfuffle: A Deep Dive into Democracy's Discord

In an audacious display of power, the Trump administration has seemingly crossed a line that many thought inviolable: attacking the bastion of American freedom—satire and criticism. The suspension of Jimmy Kimmel by ABC and Disney at the behest of threats from President Donald Trump is not just a single incident of censorship; it is a blaring alarm for the state of free speech under this administration. Let's dissect this debacle with the precision it deserves.

The Unfolding of Events

At the heart of this controversy is Rep. Ro Khanna's (D-CA) characterization of Jimmy Kimmel as a "dumb person to take on," spotlighting the absurdity of the administration's targets. Khanna's attempt to subpoena FCC Chairman Brendan Carr, who had threatened ABC/Disney into compliance, though unsuccessful, underscores the Democrats' readiness to confront what they perceive as a direct assault on freedom of expression.

Carr's threats, culminating in Kimmel's suspension, evoke a chilling effect on the media landscape. His comments, referring to Kimmel’s actions as "some of the sickest conduct possible," are not just an overreaction; they are a dangerous precedent. The swift execution of these threats, leading to Kimmel's suspension, reveals a disturbing readiness to weaponize regulatory authority against perceived adversaries.

The Democratic Response

The Democratic response has been fierce. Rep. Yassamin Ansari (D-AZ) introduced a resolution condemning the Trump administration's attempt to silence critics, framing it as a tactic befitting authoritarian regimes, not democratic governments. This is not merely political posturing; it is a necessary defense of democratic principles.

House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries (D-NY) and House Oversight Committee ranking member Robert Garcia (D-CA)'s calls for investigations into the incident, along with Rep. Jamie Raskin (D-MD)'s vow to "leave no stone unturned," signify a broader concern for the integrity of free speech and the press under the current administration. Their actions are not just in defense of Kimmel but in defense of the very foundations of American democracy.

The Bigger Picture

At its core, this incident transcends Jimmy Kimmel and delves into the darker waters of government overreach and censorship. The Trump administration's actions, as reported, are emblematic of a troubling trend towards silencing dissent. When the government can dictate the permissible scope of satire and criticism, the very essence of free speech is under threat.

The role of the government, especially in a democracy as vibrant and diverse as the United States, should never veer into the suppression of lawful expression. The fact that these events are unfolding in the land of the free is both ironic and deeply concerning. It is a stark reminder that freedom of speech, a pillar of democracy, is not automatically self-sustaining but requires constant vigilance and protection.

Conclusion

The suspension of Jimmy Kimmel is not just about a late-night host or a controversial joke; it is about the broader battle for the soul of American democracy. It's a wake-up call for those who value the principles of freedom and democracy to stand up against the encroaching shadows of authoritarianism. The fight for free speech is a fight for the very essence of America.
