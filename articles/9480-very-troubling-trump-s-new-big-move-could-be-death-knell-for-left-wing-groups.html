'Very troubling': Trump's new big move could be 'death knell' for left-wing groups

As the Trump administration pushes to weaponize the IRS against the president’s political enemies, it could be a “potential death knell” for progressive organizations.

Just as President Donald Trump has used the Department of Justice to seek revenge on his foes, he’s also planning a similar move using the IRS, according to a Mother Jones report on Friday from senior editor Michael Mechanic.

And this time, he has promised to seek to revoke the tax-exempt status of liberal organizations.

The Wall Street Journal indicated this week that “sweeping changes” are coming. And Trump is lining up “a target list of progressive donors and groups, the paper reported, including, not surprisingly, billionaire George Soros, that bogeyman of right-wing conservatives, and groups with ties to his Open Society Foundations,” with Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent’s adviser Gary Shapley taking the lead.

“This is, without doubt, a very troubling development,” John Koskinen, former IRS commissioner under President Barack Obama and President Donald Trump during the first administration, told Mother Jones. He explains that partisan use of the IRS is illegal.

“Section 7217 of the US Criminal Code prohibits the president or anyone in the White House from suggesting or ordering an IRS audit,” Koskinen said. “Putting administration loyalists in charge of the IRS generally and the criminal division in particular with the expressed aim of auditing individual taxpayers or trying to eliminate the tax exemption of nonprofits the administration does not approve of certainly violates the spirit if not the letter of the Criminal Code.”

The Reagan administration also attempted to do this to Mother Jones in the 1980s, and the public won, Mechanic added.

“Such investigations could be used not only in pursuit of criminal cases, but also as a rationale for yanking a progressive organization’s tax-exempt status, eliminating the ability of its donors to take a tax deduction—a potential death knell for any nonprofit group’s ability to survive and support its mission,” Mechanic writes.

Our Analysis:

The Weaponization of the IRS: A Dangerous Precedent

In an alarming development that echoes the darkest chapters of political retribution in American history, the Trump administration’s latest maneuvers to weaponize the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) against progressive organizations represent a stark escalation in the use of governmental power to settle political scores. This disturbing trend, as reported by Michael Mechanic of Mother Jones, not only undermines the very fabric of democracy but also signals a potential death knell for the independence and survival of nonprofit groups that dare to oppose the administration’s agenda.

The Wall Street Journal’s Ominous Warnings

The Wall Street Journal’s indication of “sweeping changes” coming to the IRS should send shivers down the spine of any advocate for free speech and political independence. The administration’s plans to compile a “target list” of progressive donors and groups, with special attention to those tied to George Soros, the perennial bogeyman of right-wing conspiracy theories, is not merely an act of political vindictiveness. It is a calculated strategy to choke the financial lifelines of organizations that play crucial roles in fostering civic engagement and advocating for social justice.

A Clear Violation of Legal and Ethical Norms

The strategy to use the IRS as a cudgel against political adversaries is not just morally reprehensible; it’s potentially illegal. As John Koskinen, a former IRS commissioner under both Obama and Trump, points out, partisan use of the IRS violates Section 7217 of the US Criminal Code. This law explicitly prohibits any White House interference in IRS operations, including audit suggestions or orders. Placing administration loyalists in key IRS positions with the goal of targeting the president’s political enemies not only breaches the spirit of this law but likely the letter as well.

Historical Echoes and Future Dangers

The Trump administration’s tactics are not without precedent. The Reagan administration’s attempt to audit Mother Jones in the 1980s serves as a chilling reminder that the misuse of the IRS as a political weapon is a recurring threat to democracy. However, the public’s victory against such overreach in the past offers a glimmer of hope that similar abuses can be countered through vigilance and resistance.

The potential for these IRS investigations to strip progressive organizations of their tax-exempt status—and, by extension, their ability to operate effectively—cannot be overstated. Such actions would not only devastate these groups financially but also send a terrifying message to any who might consider opposing the administration’s policies.

Conclusion: A Call to Action

This latest development is not merely a troubling anomaly but a clarion call for all who value democratic principles. The weaponization of the IRS against political enemies is an attack on the very foundations of our political system. It requires a robust response from all sectors of society: legal challenges, public outcry, and legislative action to safeguard the integrity of our institutions.

As citizens, it is incumbent upon us to remain vigilant, informed, and ready to defend the principles of fairness and independence that define our democracy. Let us not be complacent in the face of such blatant attempts to undermine our societal norms and legal protections. The survival of our democratic institutions—and the nonprofit organizations that support our collective aspirations for a fairer and more just society—depends on it.
