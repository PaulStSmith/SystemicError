'Deeply disturbing': Lawmaker aghast as Trump admin eyes deportations to war-torn Ukraine

WASHINGTON — The Trump administration's plot to ship migrants to war-torn Ukraine alarmed a lawmaker on Capitol Hill on Wednesday who called the plan "deeply disturbing."

The Trump administration has suggested deporting certain migrants from the U.S. to Ukraine, including people who are not Ukrainian citizens.

In late January, a senior U.S. diplomat asked Ukrainian officials to accept some deportees who were nationals from elsewhere. The eyebrow-raising request comes as Ukraine fights Russia's invasion, and despite the country lacking a functioning airport due to ongoing airstrikes.

Ukrainian officials said the plan did not reach the highest levels of government and the request wasn't taken seriously. So far, Ukraine has not accepted any such deportees.

The request aligns with the Trump administration’s aggressive crackdown on immigration.

Rep. Hank Johnson (D-GA), a member of the House Judiciary Committee, slammed the Trump administration on Wednesday, telling Raw Story he saw a headline about deportees possibly being sent to Ukraine.

"It's deeply distressing and disturbing to think that there would be a time in this country where we would find the worst places to deport people to instead of places where they came from. Their homelands. Not sending them back their but sending them to the worst location we could send them to just to punish them," he said.

Johnson added it's equally as unsettling that President Donald Trump has "bandied the idea" of housing American citizens in the same "foreign gulags."

Johnson accused Trump of trying to build up a fervor to increase the incarceration population to enrich his "billionaire buddies."

But Rep. Ralph Norman (R-SC) had a different take when it comes to sending U.S. citizens to foreign prisons. While he claimed he hadn't heard Trump wanted to send migrants to countries such as Libya, Norman defended the MAGA leader.

"Whatever Trump does will make sense and will put America first," Norman told Raw Story.

Our Analysis:

Analyzing the Unthinkable: The Trump Administration's Deportation Debacle

In a revelation that sounds more like dystopian fiction than reality, the Trump administration has once again shown a bewildering disregard for human dignity and international norms. The proposal to deport migrants—not to their countries of origin, but to war-torn Ukraine—is as deeply disturbing as it is absurd.

The Plan: A New Level of Cruelty

The administration's suggestion to deport certain migrants, including those who are not Ukrainian citizens, to Ukraine, a country currently embroiled in conflict and without a functioning airport due to ongoing airstrikes, is nothing short of barbaric. This plan, floated in late January by a senior U.S. diplomat, did not merely raise eyebrows—it set off alarm bells.

Ukrainian officials have rightly dismissed the request, which reportedly did not reach the highest levels of government. This dismissal underscores the proposal's sheer impracticality and, more importantly, its cruelty. To even propose sending deportees to a war zone is a gross abdication of basic human empathy and responsibility.

The Reaction: From Distress to Disbelief

Rep. Hank Johnson's (D-GA) reaction to the news was palpable. Describing the plan as “deeply distressing and disturbing,” Johnson's condemnation speaks volumes about the Trump administration's failure to adhere to any semblance of ethical governance. The notion of deporting people to "the worst places" as a form of punishment is antithetical to the values upon which the United States prides itself.

Furthermore, Johnson's comments about the administration's broader punitive ambitions—housing American citizens in "foreign gulags" to enrich "billionaire buddies"—paint a chilling picture of a government more invested in profiteering from incarceration than in safeguarding human rights.

The Defense: Blind Loyalty Over Reason

Rep. Ralph Norman's (R-SC) defense of the indefensible is a stark reminder of the partisan blindness that has infected American politics. His assertion that "whatever Trump does will make sense and will put America first" is a dangerous abdication of critical thinking and accountability. To blindly support a leader, especially in the face of such a morally bankrupt proposal, is to forsake the very principles of democracy and justice.

Conclusion: A Call for Humanity and Accountability

The Trump administration's plot to ship migrants to Ukraine is a sobering reminder of the depths to which policy can plunge when divorced from humanity and ethical considerations. It is a policy proposal that should never have seen the light of day, indicative of a troubling disregard for international law, human rights, and basic decency.

As this news unfolds, it is crucial for lawmakers, citizens, and the international community to stand in firm opposition to such proposals. We must demand more from our leaders—policies grounded in compassion, respect for human dignity, and a commitment to the ethical treatment of all individuals, regardless of their nationality or immigration status. The time for silence and complacency is over. The stakes are simply too high.
