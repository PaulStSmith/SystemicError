'We created a monster': Trump guards build alert system to keep top ally away from him

Conservative gadfly Laura Loomer’s outsized influence over Donald Trump is raising alarms among MAGA supporters who helped her “influencer’’ career along, and dismay within the president’s inner circle which has had to establish an emergency alert protocol designed to keep Trump out of her reach.

According to a report from The New Republic’s Nina Burleigh, Loomer, a failed congressional candidate known for spreading conspiracy theories and overt racist attacks on Muslims, has a growing number critics on the far-right as she uses her growing sway with Trump to undercut her conservative rivals with the same viciousness she has used on liberals.

“In her power and profanity, in her smashing of walls between ‘influencer’ and ‘journalist’ and ‘presidential adviser,’ Loomer is a mile-marker in the descent of the American political discourse into depravity, and of our government into chaos and farce,” wrote Burleigh.

She added that Loomer has boasted of the many “scalps” — as she calls them — she has acquired among Trump administration officials who have been subsequently fired.

Looking back at the havoc Loomer has created, right-wing Christian producer Lauren Witzke, who helped create the “Loomer Unleashed” podcast, now laments, “Trump needed a pit bull, and we went all in to create his pit bull.… And we created a monster.”

One MAGA Republican, who asked not to be named over fears Loomer would target them, complained she is, “trying to eliminate competition in the dirtiest backhanded disgusting tactics.”

White House insiders told Burleigh that Loomer has become such an annoyance that the president’s comms team created an alert if she gets too near to him or starts up another ruckus that might require a response.

Case in point, a recent alert that hit the comms team’s cells that simply read: “LLP. Activate.”

“Everyone with an earpiece in the vicinity understood the acronym. Laura Loomer Protocol. According to my source, this is an actual strategy understood by White House advance teams,” Burleigh wrote.

“When a sighting is shared, the teams know to keep eyes on her and, in the patois, ‘initiate containment.’”

In one instance, the reported went on, Loomer was removed and ushered to “an outer perimeter. As she was being gently guided away, she was talking the whole time, strenuously objecting. ‘I’m supposed to be here! I have to let the president know what press to talk to! The president wants to see me, and in the presidential box.’”

According to another Trump insider, “She finds a way to get herself on the campus wherever he is, and then uses her cunning and ability to speak her way past people, to get them to get her closer to him, until he sees her. Once he recognizes her, there is a 50-50 chance he will say, ‘Hey Laura, come on over here!’”

Our Analysis:

The Loomer Conundrum: A Symptom of Political Malaise

In an era where the line between influencer and policymaker becomes alarmingly blurred, Laura Loomer’s burgeoning sway within Donald Trump’s orbit is not just a standalone spectacle of political theatre but a glaring indicator of the degenerative state of American political discourse. The revelations from Nina Burleigh’s report in The New Republic sketch a vivid portrait of a political landscape where venomous tactics and conspiracy mongering are not only tolerated but rewarded.

Influence or Infamy?

Loomer, a figure notorious for her “overt racist attacks on Muslims” and penchant for conspiracy theories, represents a disturbing evolution in the politicization of media influence. Her transition from a fringe internet personality to a key player who boasts about collecting “scalps” of ousted Trump administration officials underscores a sickening meritocracy of malice that seems to have taken hold. The establishment of an “emergency alert protocol” by Trump’s comms team, whimsically referred to as the “Laura Loomer Protocol,” is as laughable as it is terrifying. It signifies a recognition of Loomer’s disruptive potential, yet also a capitulation to the very forces of chaos she represents.

A Monster of Their Own Making

The irony of MAGA supporters and right-wing figures decrying the monster they’ve helped create cannot be overstated. Lauren Witzke’s lamentation about wanting a “pit bull” for Trump but ending up with a “monster” is a testament to the Frankenstein’s monster phenomenon within the far-right ecosystem. When political movements prioritize aggression and loyalty over principle and integrity, Loomers are not just possible; they are inevitable.

The Descent into Depravity

Burleigh’s assertion that Loomer is “a mile-marker in the descent of the American political discourse into depravity” is spot on. This is not merely about one individual’s rise to infamy but about what her ascent signifies for the broader political culture. The blending of influencer, journalist, and presidential adviser into a single, indistinguishable muddle reflects a deeply troubling erosion of public discourse and governance.

A Cautionary Tale

What this saga should prompt is not just a reflection on how American politics got to this point but a reassessment of where it’s headed. The normalization of figures like Loomer, whose tactics are “the dirtiest backhanded disgusting tactics,” according to a MAGA Republican, is a glaring red flag. It signals a political environment where the ends justify the means, where might makes right, and where truth is incidental, if not irrelevant.

Conclusion

The story of Laura Loomer’s rise is not just a narrative about one individual’s influence over a former president. It’s a broader commentary on the state of American political life, where sensationalism, extremism, and the cult of personality overshadow reasoned debate and principled leadership. As the political right grapples with the monsters it has created, one wonders whether the lessons of this saga will be heeded or if they will merely foreshadow more chaos to come.
