Profanity-laden anti-MAGA chant erupts at major pro-wrestling event

Chants attacking U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) are common at the many protests and demonstrations opposing President Donald Trump’s mass deportations policy. But on Wednesday, February 4, an anti-ICE chant broke out somewhere else: a major pro-wrestling event in Las Vegas.

The event was the All Elite Wrestling (AEW) Dynamite, held at Vegas’ T-Mobile Arena—where Maxwell Jacob Friedman (MJF) was up against “Big, Bad” Brody King. Fans, according to Newsweek immigration reporter Billal Rahman, broke out in chants of “F—— ICE.”

“During the event on Wednesday,” Rahman reports, “the anti-ICE chant drew MJF’s attention, and he appeared momentarily surprised. He paused and looked toward the audience as the chant echoed loudly throughout the arena.”

Trump’s policies have encountered both support and opposition in the professional wrestling world. The late Hulk Hogan was a major Trump supporter, but Jesse “The Body” Ventura — a former pro-wrestling star who went on to become governor of Minnesota — is a scathing critic of the ICE raids being conducted in Minnesota, describing the fatal shooting of Renne Nicole Good as a “murder” and telling reporters, “The Republican Party is a domestic enemy to our Constitution.”

The anti-ICE chant at the AEW event in Vegas are drawing a lot of responses on X, formerly Twitter.

Missouri Democrat and former wrestler Justice Horn commented, “Oh we’ve taken the AEW fan base, it’s over for MAGA.”

Horn, in a separate tweet, wrote, “Game recognize game — I watched the video and bursted out laughing. It’s like he looked at the viewer like, ‘Welp, the Midterms are cooked.’”

KrassenCast’s Ed Krassenstein tweeted, “Trump has to hate this! Last night’s AEW Dynamite wrestling match took an unexpected turn when the crowd in Las Vegas started cheering ‘F—— ICE’ over and over again just before the main event between MJF and Brody King.”

Allen Insights founder Brian Allen observed, “WOW: At AEW Dynamite in Las Vegas, the entire crowd broke into loud, unified chants of ‘F*** ICE!’ before the main event. Directed at U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement.”

Journalist Aaron Rupar posted, “Wow! What sounded like the entire AEW Dynamite audience in Las Vegas tonight chanted ‘F—— ICE! F—— ICE!’ in unison before the main event.”

Democratic activist Lucas Sanders noted how audible the chants were, posting, “WATCH: Loud F—— ICE” chants.”

Our Analysis:

The Unexpected Arena: Political Protests in Pro-Wrestling

In an era where political activism seeps into every crack and crevice of American life, the world of professional wrestling has become the latest, and perhaps most unexpected, battleground. A recent event in Las Vegas, Nevada, where a wrestling match was momentarily overshadowed by a political declaration, serves as a stark reminder of how pervasive the discourse around immigration policy has become.

During an All Elite Wrestling (AEW) Dynamite event, the air was filled not just with the usual cheers for the high-flying, body-slamming action in the ring, but with a potent political message: “F--- ICE.” This chant, breaking out among fans at the T-Mobile Arena, marks a significant moment where sports entertainment and political activism collide with the force of a heavyweight punch.

The Moment of Surprise

Maxwell Jacob Friedman (MJF), standing in the ring, was momentarily taken aback as the chant resonated throughout the arena. This reaction captured not just the surprise of one wrestler, but underscored a broader, more profound shift in the cultural and political engagement of sports fans. The wrestling arena, long considered a space for escapism and spectacle, is now a platform for vocal political expression.

The Political Divide in Wrestling

The incident in Las Vegas is a microcosm of the larger political rifts that run through the professional wrestling community. The late Hulk Hogan’s support for President Donald Trump contrasts sharply with Jesse “The Body” Ventura’s vehement criticism of ICE raids, demonstrating the deep divides not just in American politics, but within the subculture of wrestling itself.

Missouri Democrat and former wrestler Justice Horn’s reaction to the event underscores this point dramatically. “Oh we’ve taken the AEW fan base, it’s over for MAGA,” he declared, positioning the chant as a marker of political victory within the wrestling fanbase. This sentiment, while perhaps overstated, signals the increasing politicization of spaces previously deemed neutral or apolitical.

The Reaction on Social Media

The chorus of voices on X (formerly Twitter) amplifying the incident at the AEW event suggests a public eager to engage with, and indeed celebrate, the intersection of sports entertainment and political protest. From Ed Krassenstein’s gleeful observation that “Trump has to hate this!” to Brian Allen’s amazement at the unity of the crowd, the social media reaction frames the chant as a significant cultural moment.

The Bigger Picture

What transpired in Las Vegas is about more than wrestling; it’s about the increasing visibility and volume of political discourse in every corner of American life. The chant against ICE at a wrestling event is a vivid illustration of how public sentiment on immigration policy is not confined to protests or political rallies. It pervades all public gatherings, even those designed for distraction and entertainment.

In this light, the incident is a reflection of a society where the lines between entertainment, sports, and politics have blurred beyond recognition. It is a testament to the fact that in today’s America, there are no neutral corners, no spaces immune from the reach of political activism. Whether this is a positive development or a sign of heightened division is up for debate, but one thing is clear: the arena of professional wrestling has become yet another stage for the ongoing, and increasingly vocal, battle over America’s soul.
