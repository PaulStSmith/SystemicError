ICE Raids. No Warrants. Arresting Judges. This Is the Plan

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosApril 26, 20252 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Screengrab, MSNBC

A judge was arrested in a parking lot.
Not a criminal.
Not a fugitive.
A judge.

This isn’t law enforcement — it’s political theater soaked in menace.

And it’s not just about Judge Hannah Dugan. It’s about sending a message:

“We will find you. You are next.”

That isn’t a warning from the Justice Department. That’s a threat from a regime — one that sees the Constitution not as a framework, but as an obstacle to be overcome.

Rule of Law? Or Rule of Retaliation?

Let’s be clear: this judge is accused of allowing an undocumented defendant to exit her courtroom through a side door — a discretionary action in an already chaotic legal system.

But that’s not the point. The point was the cuffs in the parking lot, the media blitz, and Trump’s DOJ cronies posting about it in real time — mid-arrest, like mob bosses gloating over a hit.

“We are prosecuting you,” said the Trump administration’s AG.
“We will find you.”

Those aren’t statements from public servants.
Those are lines from a dictator’s enforcer.

The Fourth Branch Under Fire

Judges were the last line of defense during Trump’s first term.

  • They blocked his illegal travel bans.

  • They halted his funding freezes.

  • They ruled against his DEI erasures, his immigration stunts, his transgender military ban.

So now? He’s arresting them.

This isn’t a bug. This is the plan: criminalize the judiciary so the only law that remains is his.

And if you think that sounds dramatic, remember: this administration is now citing the 1798 Alien Enemies Act — a dusty relic of xenophobic paranoia — to justify warrantless home raids.

We are not at war, but they say we are.
And they are using that fantasy to burn the Fourth Amendment alive.

What They’re Tearing Down

Let’s review what they’re actively dismantling:

  • Due process

  • Judicial independence

  • The right to be free from warrantless searches

  • The ability of immigrants to testify or seek justice without fear

  • Any opposition that stands in the way of retribution politics

They don’t see the judiciary as co-equal.
They see it as competition — something to defeat, break, and humiliate in public.

Don’t Miss the Bigger Play

Trump’s team isn’t targeting this judge for her action.
They’re targeting her for her position.
This is a dry run. A proof of concept.
They want to see if America will blink when a judge is dragged out of her car for refusing to be ICE’s errand girl.

Because if we accept that?
They’ll come for the next one.
And the next.
And then you.

This Is the Line in the Sand

If a sitting judge can be arrested for respecting due process…
If warrantless searches become the new standard…
If ICE becomes a politicized shock troop and the DOJ a revenge factory…

Then we are not “debating” immigration policy.
We are not “enforcing the law.”
We are losing the republic — one spectacle at a time.

Milwaukee took to the streets.
That judge is still behind bars.

And we should all ask ourselves:
If they’re not afraid to arrest a judge, what makes you think they’ll hesitate when it’s you?

This isn’t a glitch.
It’s the design.
