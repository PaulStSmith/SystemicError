Rep. Lauren Boebert (R-CO) appeared to lose her defiant tone about sharing photos from inside a closed-door deposition with a MAGA influencer on Thursday.

Reporters caught up with Boebert near the Chappaqua Performing Arts Center in New York, where former Secretary of State Hillary Clinton appeared for a closed-door deposition related to her husband’s contacts with the disgraced financier and convicted sex criminal Jeffrey Epstein. Earlier in the day, Boebert shared images from inside the deposition with MAGA influencer Benny Johnson, which caused the hearing to be temporarily paused.

Boebert was defiant when asked by CNN’s MJ Lee why she took the photos.

“Why not? I haven’t been reprimanded.” Boebert told Lee.

The Colorado lawmaker appeared to walk back that claim when reporters asked her again why she shared the photos.

“I just returned to my hotel room and installed the BleachBit software on my cameras and iPad, so I guess in regard to the photos, I don’t recall,” Boebert said as she pushed a young child on a bicycle into the center.

Our Analysis:

The Audacity of Hypocrisy: Rep. Lauren Boebert’s Latest Stunt

In a display that can only be described as brazenly hypocritical, Rep. Lauren Boebert (R-CO) found herself entangled in a controversy of her own making. Boebert, a figure who has styled herself as a paragon of conservative values and a staunch defender of the MAGA movement, has seemingly tripped over her own standards.

During a closed-door deposition involving former Secretary of State Hillary Clinton, related to Clinton’s husband’s contacts with the disgraced financier Jeffrey Epstein, Boebert decided it was within her purview to share photos from inside the deposition with a MAGA influencer, Benny Johnson. This act alone raises a multitude of questions regarding Boebert’s understanding and respect for legal proceedings and confidentiality. But the hypocrisy doesn’t end there.

When initially confronted by CNN’s MJ Lee regarding her decision to take and share the photos, Boebert’s response was one of defiance: “Why not? I haven’t been reprimanded.” This statement is laden with arrogance and a disturbing sense of impunity. It’s as if Boebert believes that the lack of immediate consequences for her actions somehow justifies them, a mindset that is both dangerous and antithetical to the principles of accountability and rule of law.

However, this brazen attitude took a sharp turn when reporters pressed her further. Boebert’s subsequent claim—that she had used BleachBit software on her devices, thereby erasing any trace of the photos, and that she “doesn’t recall” the photos—reeks of cowardice and evasion. The reference to BleachBit, a software famously associated with Hillary Clinton’s email scandal, is a clear attempt at irony, but it falls flat, serving only to highlight Boebert’s own questionable actions.

What is truly astounding about Boebert’s behavior is the sheer audacity of her hypocrisy. This is a lawmaker who has been vocally critical of others for far less, yet when confronted with her own dubious actions, she resorts to mockery and obfuscation. The act of sharing photos from a closed-door deposition is not a trivial matter; it is a breach of protocol and, depending on the specifics, potentially illegal.

The use of a child as a prop—a young child on a bicycle no less—to deflect from her actions as she made these comments adds another layer of distaste to the whole scenario. It’s as if Boebert is attempting to shield herself from criticism by involving an innocent party, a tactic that is both cowardly and manipulative.

In summary, Rep. Lauren Boebert’s actions and subsequent responses are emblematic of a broader issue within certain political circles: a disregard for the rule of law, an embrace of hypocrisy, and a belief in personal impunity. Boebert’s conduct is not just a personal failing but a reflection of the systemic errors that plague our political discourse. The electorate deserves better than this circus of evasion and hypocrisy. It’s high time our representatives remember they are held to the same standards as those they so eagerly criticize.
