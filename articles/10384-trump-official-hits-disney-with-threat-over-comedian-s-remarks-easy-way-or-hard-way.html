Trump official hits Disney with threat over comedian's remarks: 'Easy way or hard way?'

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosSeptember 18, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Jimmy Kimmel. (Shutterstock.)

FCC Chairman Brendan Carr issued a stern threat Wednesday over comedian Jimmy Kimmel's remarks that Charlie Kirk’s assassin was a MAGA conservative.

"This is a very, very serious issue right now for Disney," Carr said in an interview with conservative podcaster Benny Johnson on the Benny Show. "We can do this the easy way or the hard way. These companies can find ways to take action on Kimmel or there is going to be additional work for the FCC ahead."

Carr threatened immediate action against Kimmel, ABC and Disney over the comments, claiming he deliberately misled the public and said "it appears to be some of the sickest conduct possible" in the wake of Kirk's killing. He also said that Kimmel should be suspended and that ABC should lose its broadcast license.

"They have a license granted by us at the FCC, and that comes with it an obligation to operate in the public interest," he said.

He said it was time for licensed broadcasters to push back on Kimmel, claiming that they should be aware that they could face fines from the FCC.

Carr, who was appointed by President Donald Trump in January, says the agency has a “strong case” to hold Kimmel, ABC, and Disney accountable over "spreading dangerous, politically motivated misinformation."

Our Analysis:

The FCC's Threat: A Closer Examination

In an act that can only be described as wielding the power of regulatory authority with a heavy hand, FCC Chairman Brendan Carr issued a stern threat against comedian Jimmy Kimmel over remarks concerning Charlie Kirk’s assassin being a MAGA conservative. This incident throws us into the murky waters of freedom of speech, censorship, and the political weaponization of regulatory bodies.

The Threat and Its Implications

Carr's comments, made during an interview with conservative podcaster Benny Johnson, were not just a casual remark. They were a clear and direct threat aimed at ABC and Disney, warning of "immediate action" over Kimmel's comments, which he deemed as a deliberate attempt to mislead the public. This raises a gargantuan red flag regarding the role of the FCC and its boundaries. The chairman's assertion that Kimmel's conduct was "some of the sickest conduct possible" in the wake of Kirk's killing is, at best, a gross exaggeration, and at worst, a dangerous precedent for censoring content that displeases those in power.

The FCC's Role and Responsibilities

The FCC, under its mandate, is indeed tasked with ensuring that broadcasters operate in the public interest. However, Carr's interpretation of this mandate seems to skew dangerously towards censorship rather than regulation. The idea that a regulatory body can threaten a broadcaster's license over the content of a comedian's remarks is chilling and smacks of authoritarianism. It's essential to remember that the FCC's role is to regulate, not to dictate the content of broadcasts based on the political winds of the day.

The Political Undercurrent

Carr's threat cannot be viewed in isolation from the political context. Appointed by President Donald Trump, Carr's actions here seem less about protecting the public from "misinformation" and more about silencing dissent and policing speech that does not align with a particular political ideology. Labeling Kimmel's remarks as "spreading dangerous, politically motivated misinformation" is a thinly-veiled attempt at politicizing what should be a non-partisan issue. The fact that this comes from an FCC chairman is not only disappointing but deeply concerning.

The Dangerous Road Ahead

If the FCC were to follow through on Carr's threats, it would set a horrifying precedent. Today, it's Jimmy Kimmel; tomorrow, it could be any broadcaster or public figure who dares to voice an opinion or joke that displeases those in power. This is not about one comedian or one broadcast; it's about the fundamental right to free speech and the dangers of allowing regulatory bodies to become political weapons.

Conclusion

The FCC's role is crucial in ensuring that broadcasters operate fairly and in the public interest. However, when threats are issued in a manner that suggests a willingness to censor and silence based on content, it's a clear and present danger to democracy and free speech. We must remain vigilant and resist any attempts to turn regulatory bodies into arbiter of acceptable speech. The implications of Carr's threats are far-reaching and must be challenged, not just for the sake of Jimmy Kimmel, ABC, or Disney, but for the sanctity of free speech and the principles upon which the United States was founded.
