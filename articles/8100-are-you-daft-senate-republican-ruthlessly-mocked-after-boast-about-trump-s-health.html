'Are you daft?' Senate Republican ruthlessly mocked after boast about Trump's health

U.S. President Donald Trump attends a cabinet meeting with U.S. Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth and U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio, at the White House in Washington, D.C., U.S., December 2, 2025. REUTERS/Brian Snyder

A day after President Donald Trump went viral after falling asleep during a cabinet meeting following an all-night Truth Social posting binge, some Republican loyalists spent Wednesday trying to spin the narrative to say that Trump is nothing short of the Energizer Bunny and the picture of perfect health.

Sen. Roger Marshall (R-KY), who also happens to be a physician, went on Newsmax saying, “I’m a pretty hard worker. I’ll work 110 hours a week. He outworks me. He’s the first person I’ve ever known that works harder than I do. He runs around that golf course. He goes through staff members, several groups of them each day. He’s in incredible shape right now. And you talk about sharp. He’s playing 4-dimensional chess right now.”

That effusiveness raised the eyebrows of several, including NewsNation contributor Chris Cillizza, who quipped on X, “This is very reminiscent of Joe Biden’s senior staff saying ‘this guy never stops working!’ sort of stuff in 2023/2024.”

“Law & Order” star Chris Meloni chimed in on X, saying, “He runs around the golf course “ Woman man camera TV. … 4 dimensional chess.”

Another X user remarked, “Senator Roger Marshall: ‘He ‘RUNS’ around the golf course’ ? Are you daft? There are countless videos and photos of half-baked Trump slumped over in the passenger seat of a golf cart. That man hasn’t ‘run’ in his life!”

“Roger Marshall is a doctor. Yet, he shamelessly lies about Trump’s stamina. Either he’s a really bad doctor [and] he should lose his license or he’s a gutless liar,” wrote another anti-MAGA account on X.

“This is some next level gaslighting. Marshall is a complete failure as a senator,” wrote X user Anna Baxter.

Chris D. Jackson, Democratic Party chair for Lawrence County, Tennessee wrote on X, “These guys are such blowhards pretending they actually do real work. It’s an insult to the actual people they represent who have real jobs.”

Our Analysis:

The Energizer Bunny Spin: A Masterclass in Political Fantasy

The latest spectacle to emerge from the political theater showcases none other than former President Donald Trump, who apparently found the strenuous task of staying awake during a cabinet meeting too Herculean after an all-night spree on Truth Social. This incident, laughable in its own right, has been twisted into a narrative so surreal, it could only find a home in the annals of political spin.

The Spin Doctors at Work

Enter Sen. Roger Marshall (R-KY), a physician turned political spin doctor, who took to Newsmax with claims that would make even the most gullible spectator blink. Describing Trump as an individual who “outworks” him, despite his own self-proclaimed 110-hour work weeks, Marshall paints Trump as a paragon of vigor, racing through golf courses and staff members with the energy of a high school track star and the mental acuity to play “4-dimensional chess.”

Let’s dissect this absurdity with the precision it deserves. The claim that Trump “runs around that golf course” is particularly hilarious. This is a man who, as countless videos and photos have shown, seems to have a closer relationship with the seat of a golf cart than with the act of running. The mental gymnastics required to leap from documented evidence of Trump’s preferred mode of golf course transportation to visions of him sprinting through the greens are Olympian in their scope.

The Echo Chamber of Hyperbole

The reaction from various quarters was swift and scathing. NewsNation contributor Chris Cillizza’s comparison to the overzealous work ethic claims made by Joe Biden’s senior staff in 2023/2024 highlights a bipartisan penchant for fabricating presidential stamina. Chris Meloni’s sarcastic echo of Trump’s infamous “Person, woman, man, camera, TV” cognitive test brag further underscores the ridiculousness of Marshall’s assertions.

The backlash on X (formerly Twitter) ranged from incredulous to outright indignant. One user’s challenge to Marshall’s medical credentials for promoting such blatant falsehoods about Trump’s physical condition and another’s labeling of Marshall as a “gutless liar” and a “complete failure as a senator” underscore the wider disbelief and frustration with the senator’s statements.

The Real Insult

The true affront here, as pointed out by Chris D. Jackson, Democratic Party chair for Lawrence County, Tennessee, is to the American populace. The comparison of Trump’s supposed Herculean work ethic to the real, tangible labor of everyday Americans is an insult of the highest order. It’s a stark reminder of the chasm that exists between the political elite’s narrative-spinning and the lived realities of the constituents they purport to represent.

Conclusion: The Spin Cycle Continues

In a political landscape increasingly dominated by the absurd, the spectacle of Trump’s somnolent escapade and its subsequent spin into a narrative of superhuman stamina is par for the course. Yet, it serves as a potent reminder of the lengths to which some will go to contort reality to fit a preferred narrative.

The Energizer Bunny analogy, while amusing, is a disservice to the intelligence of the American public. It’s high time we demand more from our political discourse than these cartoonish caricatures of leadership. In the end, one must wonder—if the leaders of today are the Energizer Bunnies of politics, are we not simply powering our way into a dystopian farce?
