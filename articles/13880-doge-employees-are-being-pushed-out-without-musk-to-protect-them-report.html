DOGE employees are 'being pushed out' without Musk to protect them: report

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosJune 9, 20255 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Elon Musk walks on the day of a meeting with House Republicans to discuss the Department of Government Efficiency (DOGE) on Capitol Hill in Washington, D.C., U.S., March 5, 2025. REUTERS/Kent Nishimura

Now that billionaire Elon Musk has stepped away from his White House job as a "special government employee," the Department of Government Efficiency (DOGE) staffers he brought on board are exiting too -- some not because they want to leave.

According to a report from the Washington Post's Faiz Siddiqui, Hannah Natanson, Cat Zakrzewski, Alex Horton and Elizabeth Dwoskin, the influence of DOGE is waning and Cabinet heads are hiring back employees who were dismissed during the mass purge initiated under Donald Trump's anti-government administration.

The Post is reporting, that Musk's influence with Trump's people has fallen apart since he and Trump have turned on each other with the billionaire going so far as to suggest there is information about the president in the Jeffrey Epstein files.

With that as a background, a notable number of prominent DOGE staffers appear to have been purged themselves.

Case in point: at the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOGE staffers were "stripped of their credentials and user accounts inside the FAA’s internal computer systems" this past week.

According to the Post, "As of June 2, the staffers — Brady Glantz, Samuel Smeal, Tom Kiernan and Theodore Malaska, all of whom are employees of Musk’s SpaceX — no longer bear the title of 'senior adviser to the administrator' on their online profiles within the agency, per the records. In fact, their profiles no longer show any job title at all — nor an affiliated organization, manager, email or phone number, the records show."

Insiders claim the "team owed its creation and power to an executive order, not an act of Congress," and with Musk gone ... so are they.

As one manager explained, "So they’re being pushed out."

According to the Post, "...some federal employees took a few moments to celebrate the diminishment of DOGE, however brief. One Interior employee said he and colleagues worked extra-hard, reveling in their government jobs as DOGE seemed to be on the way out. Then he went home and ate some ice cream," before adding, "At the FAA, a group of staffers went out for post-work drinks to toast the banishment of DOGE staff. Then they offered a more solemn toast to the more than two dozen colleagues they’d lost along the way."

Our Analysis:

The Downfall of DOGE: A Tale of Billionaire Influence and Political Purges

In what reads like a political thriller, the recent unraveling of the Department of Government Efficiency (DOGE) under the controversial stewardship of billionaire Elon Musk, as reported by the Washington Post, serves as a vivid illustration of the volatile interplay between wealth, power, and politics in Washington. This episode not only highlights the precarious nature of "special government employees" but also underscores the profound implications of political purges on the fabric of governmental institutions.

The Billionaire's Departure and the Ripple Effect

Elon Musk, a figure synonymous with both innovation and controversy, has made a dramatic exit from his White House role, triggering a cascade of departures among the DOGE staff he once championed. The narrative that unfolds is not merely about personnel changes but a reflection of a deeper, systemic issue: the wielding of billionaire influence over national governance structures.

The Purge and Its Aftermath

The mass purge initiated under Donald Trump's administration, ostensibly aimed at streamlining government efficiency, in reality, paved the way for the insertion of Musk's SpaceX employees into key advisory roles within the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) and beyond. This move, while controversial, underscores a broader trend of privatization and the outsourcing of governmental responsibilities to individuals deeply entrenched in corporate interests.

The recent stripping of credentials and user accounts of DOGE staffers within the FAA is emblematic of a broader rejection of this billionaire-led approach to governance. The fact that these individuals' association with the government hinged on an executive order rather than Congressional approval speaks volumes about the fragility of their positions and the whimsical nature of their appointment.

The Fallout of Ephemeral Alliances

Musk's falling out with Trump and the subsequent disintegration of his influence within Trump's circle is a stark reminder of the transient nature of political alliances, especially those forged not on ideological compatibility but on mutual convenience and the pursuit of power. The suggestion that Musk threatened to reveal compromising information about Trump related to the Jeffrey Epstein files adds a layer of intrigue and betrayal to the saga, hinting at the Machiavellian maneuvers that often underpin high-stakes political dynamics.

A Brief Moment of Reckoning

The celebration among federal employees at the diminishment of DOGE, as reported, while fleeting, is telling. It signifies a momentary breath of relief from the pressures of an administration intent on reshaping the government in its image. The revelry, juxtaposed with the solemn remembrance of colleagues lost in the purge, encapsulates the human toll of political vendettas and the resilience of those who remain committed to their governmental roles despite the turbulence.

In Conclusion:

The departure of DOGE staffers and the unraveling of Elon Musk's influence within the government is more than a tale of political intrigue; it is a cautionary tale about the dangers of allowing billionaires with vested interests to wield disproportionate influence over national governance structures. As the dust settles, the episode leaves us with pressing questions about the integrity of our institutions and the safeguards necessary to protect them from being co-opted by the highest bidder. This saga, while concluded, serves as a critical reflection point for the future of democratic governance and the enduring battle against the encroachment of private interests on public duty.
