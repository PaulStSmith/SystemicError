Trump unloaded on his own fans on Saturday, begging them to leave his attorney general alone and stop blaming her for a botched release of the Epstein records. MAGA threw a fit because the information didn't back up right-wing conspiracy theories, and Trump blamed Obama and Biden for whatever may be in them.

"They’re all going after Attorney General Pam Bondi, who is doing a FANTASTIC JOB! We’re on one Team, MAGA, and I don’t like what’s happening. We have a PERFECT Administration, THE TALK OF THE WORLD, and 'selfish people' are trying to hurt it, all over a guy who never dies, Jeffrey Epstein," the president wrote. "For years, it’s Epstein, over and over again. Why are we giving publicity to Files written by Obama, Crooked Hillary, Comey, Brennan, and the Losers and Criminals of the Biden Administration, who conned the World with the Russia, Russia, Russia Hoax, 51 'Intelligence' Agents, 'THE LAPTOP FROM HELL,' and more?"

The stunned reactions from observers came in quickly.

Liberal commentator Brian Krassenstein said, "HOLY SMOKES!"

"Trump just stooped for Epstein again, claiming the Epstein Files are akin to the 'FAKE Hillary Clinton/Steel Dossier'. Then he says the Epstein documents are 'radical-left inspired,' and that 'nobody cares about' Jeffrey Epstein," he said. He then asked, "MAGA are you seriously ok with this crap?"

Former GOP lawmaker Adam Kinzinger chimed in:

"So Trump is trying to now claim it was a CIA created doc which means, I don’t know, maybe he is in it."

Kinzinger also said in a separate post, "Oh he’s scareeeeeddddd."

Another liberal commentator, Brian Tyler Cohen, agreed with Kinzinger's initial assessment.

"The irony is that I was actually VERY skeptical about the files or the existence of a client list. After reading this, I'm not skeptical at all," he said on Saturday.

GOP strategist Mike Madrid wrote on X, "PAY NO ATTENTION TO THE MAN BEHIND THE CURTAIN!"

Our Analysis:

Trump's Tangled Epstein Tale: A Web of Deflection and Conspiracy

In the latest chapter of the political circus that is Donald Trump's post-presidency, the former commander-in-chief has spun a new yarn, this time involving the sordid and shadowy figure of Jeffrey Epstein. Trump's recent outburst aimed at his own supporters, imploring them to back off his attorney general, Pam Bondi, over the mishandling of Epstein's documents, is nothing short of a spectacle. But, let's dissect this scenario with the precision of a scalpel through the thick fog of obfuscation Trump so loves to create.

Trump's Misdirection Playbook

Firstly, Trump's attempt to shield Bondi, while simultaneously casting aspersions on Obama, Biden, and a veritable who's who of his favorite political adversaries, is a classic move from his misdirection playbook. By blaming these figures for the contents of the Epstein files—a maneuver as transparent as it is trite—he hopes to deflect from whatever connections or implications these documents might have in relation to him. Claiming a "PERFECT Administration" and lambasting his critics as "selfish people" is a desperate bid to rewrite the narrative in his favor.

Blame-Shifting and Conspiracy Theories

The president's claims that the Epstein files were "written by Obama, Crooked Hillary, Comey, Brennan, and the Losers and Criminals of the Biden Administration" reek of the same baseless conspiracy theorizing that has become the hallmark of his political rhetoric. By invoking the specters of the "Russia, Russia, Russia Hoax," the "51 'Intelligence' Agents," and the "LAPTOP FROM HELL," Trump is attempting to muddy the waters further, hoping to conflate unrelated or debunked conspiracies to sow doubt about the credibility of the Epstein files.

The Reaction: From Stunned to Skeptical

The reactions from observers, ranging from liberal commentators to former GOP lawmakers, underscore the incredulity and concern at Trump's statements. Brian Krassenstein's exclamation, "HOLY SMOKES!" encapsulates the collective astonishment, while Adam Kinzinger's remarks hint at a deeper fear within Trump—a fear of what truths the Epstein documents might reveal about him. The sentiment, "Oh he’s scareeeeeddddd," though perhaps flippant, cuts to the heart of the matter: Trump's bluster often belies his vulnerability.

A Skeptic's Conversion

Brian Tyler Cohen's admission of moving from skepticism to belief in the importance of the Epstein files post-Trump's tirade is particularly telling. It highlights how Trump's own words often serve to validate the concerns and suspicions he seeks to dispel. In trying to discredit the Epstein documents, Trump has inadvertently lent them a credibility they might not have otherwise possessed, at least in the eyes of some skeptics.

The Curtain Call

Mike Madrid's succinct advice on X, "PAY NO ATTENTION TO THE MAN BEHIND THE CURTAIN!" serves as a fitting summary of this entire debacle. It's a reference to the Wizard of Oz, where the "great and powerful" wizard is revealed to be nothing more than a man using elaborate devices to project an image of power and omniscience. Similarly, Trump's elaborate deflections, blame-shifting, and conspiracy-mongering are his devices to distract from his vulnerabilities and the truths he fears might emerge.

In conclusion, Trump's latest foray into the Epstein saga is a masterclass in deflection, a desperate attempt to shield himself from potential fallout by casting aspersions and spinning conspiracy theories. However, the very vehemence and absurdity of his claims only serve to amplify the scrutiny and skepticism directed at him. As observers dissect his statements, the question remains: What, if anything, is Donald Trump trying to hide?
