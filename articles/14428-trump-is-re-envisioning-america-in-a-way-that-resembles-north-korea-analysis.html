Trump is 're-envisioning America' in a way that resembles North Korea: analysis

Many critics of President Donald Trump have been attacking his immigration policies — from mass deportations to foreign students being detained in Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) facilities because of their political statements — as a blatant assault on due process and the rule of law.

One of those critics is attorney Dean Obeidallah, host of "The Dean Obeidallah Show" on SiriusXM and an opinion columnist for MSNBC's website. During a Saturday, May 24 appearance on MSNBC, Obeidallah warned that Trump's draconian immigration policies are not only a threat to immigrants — they also have disturbing implications for lifelong U.S. citizens.

The New Jersey native told MSNBC's Ali Velshi, "This is not about immigration. Regardless of (what) you feel on that issue, Democrat or Republican, it's something bigger than that…. And that word is freedom…. Donald Trump is going after everything, freedom of speech, in ways we've never seen. I mean, a judge just ruled on Friday protecting the law firms, saying you're going after dissent, going after universities. I had professor Stephen Levinsky on my show, co-author of 'How Democracies Die,' saying: Every autocrat goes after universities because they are independent centers of dissent."

READ MORE: Nicolle Wallace reveals what may finally convince Trump to 'back away from the people'

Obeidallah continued, "He's going after media outlets. He's going after Democrats. They're arresting judges, the mayor of Newark —they dropped the charges, they had no case…. They opened up an investigation into Act Blue because it's a platform to help Democrats raise money. There's now an investigation of Media Matters."

The SiriusXM host described the Trump Administration's policies as a "re-envisioning of what America is about."

Obeidallah told Velshi, "This really is a push and pull between two competing visions of America. One: that we believe in is freedom, the United States of America, with due process — and their vision, which is an autocracy. And that really is what we're dealing with — or easier than that, a dictatorship. They want Trump as the dictator of the United States, and we all have to bow down to him."

Velshi noted that when "due process is taken away…. that's where your freedoms disappear" — to which Obeidallah responded, "Absolutely. It's the only thing that keeps us (from)…. being North Korea."

Our Analysis:

Trump's Immigration Policy: A Front for Autocracy?

In an era where the line between political maneuvering and outright assault on democratic principles seems increasingly blurred, Dean Obeidallah's recent comments on MSNBC shine a harsh, yet necessary light on President Donald Trump's immigration policies. Labeling them as an assault on due process and the rule of law, Obeidallah's analysis extends beyond the surface-level implications of these policies on immigrants, touching upon a far more sinister agenda - the erosion of American freedom itself.

The Facade of Immigration Policy

Obeidallah's argument that Trump's immigration policies are a mere facade for a broader assault on freedom is compelling. The targeting of foreign students based on political statements, mass deportations, and the detention of immigrants in ICE facilities without due process are not isolated incidents but part of a concerted effort to undermine the foundational principles of democracy. The fact that these actions are justified under the guise of immigration reform is not just misleading; it's dangerously deceptive.

The Assault on Independent Centers of Dissent

Quoting Professor Stephen Levinsky, Obeidallah points out a chilling pattern common to autocratic regimes - the targeting of universities and independent centers of dissent. Trump's actions, from attacking media outlets to arresting judges and investigating platforms like Act Blue and Media Matters, mirror this pattern disturbingly well. It's a tactic aimed at silencing opposition and consolidating power by instilling fear and restricting the freedom of speech.

The Dichotomy of Visions

What we're witnessing, according to Obeidallah, is a "push and pull between two competing visions of America". On one side is the vision of America as a bastion of freedom and due process. On the other is a vision of America as an autocracy, with Trump at its helm, demanding absolute loyalty and obedience. This is not just a political debate; it's a struggle for the soul of the nation.

The True Cost of Lost Due Process

Ali Velshi's observation that the loss of due process marks the beginning of the end for personal freedoms cannot be overstated. The implications of such a loss extend far beyond immigration policy, threatening to transform the United States into a country unrecognizable to those who once hailed it as a land of freedom and opportunity. Obeidallah's comparison of a due process-less America to North Korea is hyperbolic, yet it underscores the gravity of the situation. It's a stark reminder that the erosion of democratic safeguards can lead to a precipitous decline into dictatorship.

Conclusion: A Call to Action

The analysis presented by Dean Obeidallah on MSNBC is not just a critique of Trump's immigration policy; it's a clarion call to all who value democracy and freedom. It's a reminder that silence and inaction in the face of autocratic tendencies can lead to the erosion of the very freedoms that define America. As citizens and as members of a global community that cherishes democratic principles, the responsibility falls on us to stand against these assaults on freedom, due process, and the rule of law.

In essence, Trump's immigration policies are not merely problematic in their own right; they are symptomatic of a far deeper, more alarming ambition to reshape America into an autocratic state. This is a systemic error that demands correction, not through complacency or despair, but through vigorous defense of democratic values and institutions.
