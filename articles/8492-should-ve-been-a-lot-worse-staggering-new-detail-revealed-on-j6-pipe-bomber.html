'Should've been a lot worse': Staggering new detail revealed on J6 pipe bomber

By Paulo Santos

Rep. Barry Loudermilk (R-GA) disclosed exclusive new details to Raw Story on Friday about the activities of the suspect in the pipe bombs left at the Republican and Democratic National Committees on Jan. 6.

“Based on those reports, it should have been a lot worse,” said Loudermilk, explaining that many details known to the FBI have gone unshared with the public for years.

“Why wasn’t that information shared?” Raw Story’s reporter asked.

Loudermilk went on to detail “videos that we hadn’t seen before” about the Jan. 6 pipe bomber, who has never been identified but can be seen in videos wearing a gray hoodie. “The newest revelation which we’re just getting out today is that the pipe bomber, the gray hoodie jacket person, actually made a stop ... at the Congressional Black Caucus before he placed the device at the Democratic National Committee.”

“They walked into the property ... stayed there for a minute or so, then got up and left,” Loudermilk added.

“So the FBI never released that information,” Raw Story confirmed.

The new information raises a number of additional questions about the plot, Loudermilk said, including, “Was the one at the RNC supposed to go to the CBC?”

Our Analysis:

Analyzing the Shadows of January 6th: A Closer Look at Rep. Loudermilk’s Revelations

In what can only be described as a tantalizing drip of information, Rep. Barry Loudermilk (R-GA) has decided to grace the public with his insights into the dark underbelly of the January 6th Capitol attack, specifically regarding the mysterious pipe bomber. According to an exclusive with Raw Story, Loudermilk has revealed details previously unknown to the public—details that, quite frankly, raise more questions than they answer.

First, let’s address the elephant in the room: Why has it taken years for this information to come to light? Loudermilk nonchalantly mentions that “many details known to the FBI have gone unshared with the public for years.” This lack of transparency is not just a bureaucratic oversight; it’s a glaring systemic failure. The public’s right to know is being trampled under the guise of operational secrecy. But when that secrecy shields potential threats or misleads the public discourse, one must wonder: What is the true cost of silence?

Loudermilk’s narrative takes an intriguing turn as he discusses “videos that we hadn’t seen before” showcasing the suspect, forever shrouded in a gray hoodie, making an ominous pit stop at the Congressional Black Caucus before attempting to lay siege to the Democratic National Committee. This new piece of the puzzle not only shifts the narrative but introduces a chilling prelude to what was already a harrowing day in U.S. history.

Let’s dissect the implications here: The targeting of the Congressional Black Caucus, even in a seemingly aborted attempt, signals a disturbing layer of intent and possibly a broader, more sinister objective. The calculated move to first approach a symbol of Black legislative power before moving on to the broader targets of the Democratic and Republican National Committees suggests a level of premeditation and ideological motive that goes beyond mere political disruption.

The question that Loudermilk poses—whether the device intended for the RNC was originally meant for the CBC—is not just speculative; it’s crucial. It shifts the narrative from a tale of bipartisan targeting to one that could potentially underscore the racial undertones of the January 6th insurrection. This is not about creating a hierarchy of targets but about understanding the multifaceted motivations behind the attack.

However, Loudermilk’s revelations, while illuminating, also exemplify a frustrating pattern of piecemeal disclosure. Why now? What prompted this sudden willingness to share “exclusive new details”? The timing and selective release of information do nothing but fuel speculation, hinder clear analysis, and, most importantly, delay justice.

In conclusion, the breadcrumbs dropped by Rep. Loudermilk might seem like a boon to those starved for answers regarding January 6th. However, they are but a meager offering at the feast of transparency and accountability. It’s crucial that we demand more than just sporadic revelations. Full disclosure by the FBI and other involved agencies must be prioritized to not only piece together the events leading up to and during the insurrection but to ensure the safeguarding of our democratic institutions against future threats. The shadows cast by January 6th grow longer with each withheld detail, and it’s time to bring the full truth into the light.
