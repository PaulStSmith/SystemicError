'Sharp as ever!' Trump buried in mockery after dozing off during key meeting

U.S. President Donald Trump shakes hands with Saudi Crown Prince Mohammed Bin Salman during a welcoming ceremony in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, May 13, 2025. Bandar Algaloud/Courtesy of Saudi Royal Court/Handout via REUTERS

President Donald Trump was already facing severe criticism for a Middle East "peace talks" tour immediately after the government of Qatar moved to gift him a luxury $400 million Boeing 747. But that criticism kicked into high gear after he appeared to literally doze off during a critical briefing in Saudi Arabia.

Trump running out of energy and dozing off is nothing new — he famously appeared to do so even during his own criminal trial for business fraud in New York — but the moment, captured on camera, triggered a new wave of mockery.

"BREAKING: In a surreal and frankly humiliating moment, Donald Trump just fell asleep during his own briefing in Saudi Arabia," wrote Brian Allen of the Allenanalysis podcast. "This isn’t jet lag — it’s a walking security risk with a nap schedule. The man who calls himself 'sharp as ever' couldn’t even stay conscious."

"Donald Trump appears to briefly fall asleep in Saudi Arabia," wrote podcaster Brian Krassenstein. "He's too old to be President. This is elder abuse."

In addition to mockery, many commenters expressed indignation over the fact that former President Joe Biden's cognitive ability, a major focal point of the 2024 election that ultimately forced his withdrawal from the race that is now the subject of an upcoming deep dive book, was questioned for far less on the campaign trail.

"Just imagine for a second how the media would have reacted if President Biden had dozed off like this," wrote former Long Island Obama campaign chair Jon Cooper.

"Donald Trump just fell asleep in a meeting in Saudi Arabia and Fox News is still talking about the health of Joe Biden," wrote health care activist Melanie D'Arrigo.

"BREAKING: Sleepy Don can barely keep his eyes open while representing the United States in Saudi Arabia," wrote the official account of Really American PAC. "If this were President Biden, there would be nonstop coverage of his cognitive condition and his physical fitness. Where is the media outrage?"

Our Analysis:

The Hypocrisy of Political Critique: A Scathing Analysis of Trump's Napping Incident

In an era where political theatre often overshadows substantive policy discussions, the recent incident involving former President Donald Trump dozing off during a critical briefing in Saudi Arabia serves as a stark reminder of the double standards that pervade our political discourse. While Trump's nap was undeniably newsworthy, the reaction—or lack thereof—from certain media outlets and political commentators exposes a glaring hypocrisy that merits a fierce dissection.

The Gift That Keeps on Giving: A $400 Million Boeing 747

Before diving into the snooze-fest, let's not gloss over the prelude to this episode: a luxury $400 million Boeing 747 "gift" from the government of Qatar. This gesture alone should have set off alarm bells and led to a barrage of questions regarding ethics and foreign influence. Yet, it seems to have been overshadowed by the spectacle of Trump's slumber. The ease with which such significant concerns are sidelined is alarming, suggesting a systemic failure to hold leaders accountable for their entanglements with foreign governments.

Sleepy Politics: A Double Standard

The moment Trump's eyelids closed, it was not just a lapse into sleep but a glaring illustration of the double standards that infect our political critique. Remember, this is the same Donald Trump who has frequently attacked opponents for their age and stamina. The irony of him dozing off, not in the comfort of his estates but during a critical briefing in Saudi Arabia, is palpable. Yet, the outcry seems disproportionately muted, especially when compared to the cacophony that would have ensued had the roles been reversed.

Brian Allen's remark that Trump is a "walking security risk with a nap schedule" hits a nerve not just for its wit but for spotlighting the dangerous precedent of normalizing such behavior for one individual while vilifying another for less. The elder abuse quip by podcaster Brian Krassenstein, while controversial, underscores a significant concern about the physical demands of the presidency and the selective scrutiny applied to candidates and office holders.

Media Outrage or Lack Thereof

The juxtaposition of reactions to Trump's nap versus the persistent questioning of former President Joe Biden's cognitive abilities is telling. The media's role in shaping political narratives cannot be understated, and the selective outrage—or the absence of it—reveals a troubling bias. As health care activist Melanie D'Arrigo and the Really American PAC pointedly noted, the disparity in coverage between Trump's and Biden's physical and cognitive fitness examinations is stark and indicative of a broader problem within our media ecosystem.

The Verdict

The incident in Saudi Arabia is not merely about a former president catching a few winks; it's a microcosm of the hypocritical and uneven political and media landscape that shapes public perception. The lack of consistent standards in political critique undermines the credibility of our discourse, turning what should be serious discussions about leadership and policy into sensationalized and polarized spectacles.

As the 2024 election looms, it's imperative that we demand more from both our leaders and the media that covers them. The health of our democracy depends on it.
