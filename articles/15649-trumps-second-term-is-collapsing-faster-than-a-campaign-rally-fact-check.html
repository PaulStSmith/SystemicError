Trump’s second term is collapsing faster than a campaign rally fact-check.

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosApril 26, 20252 min read

Screengrab, MSNBC

We’re not even 100 days in, and the numbers are an unbroken string of nos, hell nos, and you’ve got to be kidding mes — and not from MSNBC or liberal Twitter, but from places like Fox News and Reuters. Across every major issue — the economy, foreign policy, tariffs, crime, education, even basic competence — the public is rejecting Trumpism 2.0 with brutal clarity.

Let’s be real: this isn’t just a slump. It’s a vote of no confidence from reality itself.

Polling Hellscape

He’s:

  • -19 in Pew

  • -11 in Fox

  • Underwater on the economy by 18 to 22 points

  • And even the Fox News base says by a 49-point margin that “no, he cannot ignore the Supreme Court.”

That last one isn’t a statistic — it’s a neon billboard reading:

“We saw the authoritarian trial balloon. And we hate it.”

Trump’s Support Is Floating on Fear and Loyalty

Most Americans reject his policies more than they reject him personally, which tells us: the base isn’t voting for policies — they’re voting out of fear, identity, and habit.

He could firebomb Social Security and they’d still chant his name — not because they like it, but because they’ve been conditioned to hate everyone else more.

That’s not a movement. That’s a cultic stalemate.

Cruelty Doesn’t Poll Well (Who Knew?)

From deporting a wrongly expelled U.S. citizen (Kilmar Abrego Garcia), to snatching foreign students protesting Israel’s war in Gaza, to targeting visa holders — Americans aren’t buying the cruelty-as-leadership act anymore.

92% of Americans support due process for visa holders.
52-point disapproval of protest-based deportations.
39-point disapproval of his inflation handling among young adults.

Turns out most of the country isn’t into jackboot cosplay after all.

From “Liberation Day” to “Collapse Quarter”

He tried to brand tariff escalation as “Liberation Day.”
Polling shows he’s losing by 20 to 25-point margins on every single trade, tariff, and inflation question.

This was his big bet. His “signature move.”
And America’s response?

“Thanks, but we’d like our functioning economy back.”

And the Credits Are Already Rolling

This isn’t just an approval drop.
It’s a free fall from the illusion of inevitability.

  • The Supreme Court isn’t giving him a blank check.

  • The public isn’t buying the dictator cosplay.

  • The voters, even in friendly polling territory, are rejecting the performance.

Trump hasn’t just lost ground. He’s lost the narrative. And for someone whose power is built on illusion, that’s fatal.

We’re 100 days in.
And the emperor’s approval rating is pantsless in public.
