Appeals court rules against Trump admin in big case — and gives deadline to comply

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosAugust 11, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Our Analysis:Unveiling the Mask of Secrecy: The Court's Stand Against Trump's Administration

A federal appeals court ruled over the weekend that Donald Trump's administration's moves on government spending are an affront to the Constitution and disclosure laws, according to Politico.

According to the outlet, the three-judge D.C. Circuit Court of Appeals panel voted unanimously to "shoot down a Trump administration bid to make secret a public database of federal spending that researchers say is crucial to ensure the administration is not flouting Congress’ power of the purse." The court also imposed a deadline, according to the report.

The court reportedly gave "the administration until Friday to put the data back online."

"Two of the three appeals judges assigned to the matter also signed onto a forceful opinion declaring that the administration’s bid to conceal the data was an affront to Congress’ authority over government spending, one that threatened the separation of powers and defied centuries of evidence that public disclosure is necessary for the public good," according to the report.

Politico further noted that, "Judge Karen Henderson, a George H.W. Bush appointee, wrote in support of the decision to deny the Trump administration’s request to keep the data under wraps while litigation over the issue goes forward," and quoted her as saying, "No court would allow a losing party to defy its judgment. No President would allow a usurper to command our armed forces."

She added, “And no Congress should be made to wait while the Executive intrudes on its plenary power over appropriations.”

Our Analysis:

Unveiling the Mask of Secrecy: The Court's Stand Against Trump's Administration

In a recent ruling that can only be described as a resounding victory for transparency and a significant rebuke to the Trump administration's attempts at opacity, the D.C. Circuit Court of Appeals has laid down the law, quite literally. The unanimous decision by the three-judge panel to deny the Trump administration's efforts to obscure a public database of federal spending is a stark reminder of the enduring strength of the U.S. Constitution and the principles it enshrines.

The Essence of the Ruling

The heart of this matter lies in the administration's audacious attempt to keep a lid on a database that is not just a ledger of numbers but a testament to the government's accountability to its people. This database, as researchers and the court rightly assert, is crucial for ensuring that the administration does not sidestep Congress' power of the purse—a fundamental aspect of American democracy that guarantees a separation of powers.

A Triumph for Transparency

The court's decision to mandate the re-publication of the data by a set deadline is more than a procedural victory; it's a resounding statement in favor of transparency and public good. The opinion, particularly forceful in its language, underscores the jeopardy that such concealment poses to the separation of powers and, by extension, to the very fabric of democratic governance.

Judicial Wisdom and Historical Context

Judge Karen Henderson's words in support of the decision resonate with a deep understanding of the constitutional and historical context that governs the relationship between Congress and the Executive. Her analogy, drawing parallels between a court's judgment, a president's command over the armed forces, and Congress' power over appropriations, is both poignant and powerful. It serves as a reminder of the boundaries that define and protect the functions of each branch of government.

A Clear Message

This ruling sends a clear message to the Executive branch: attempts to undermine the constitutional balance of power and the public's right to governmental transparency will not be tolerated. The judges, through their unanimous decision and the forceful opinion, have reaffirmed a centuries-old consensus that public disclosure is not just beneficial but necessary for the public good.

The Significance of the Unanimity

The unanimity of the panel, composed of judges appointed by presidents from different political spectrums, underscores the non-partisan nature of the issue at hand. It is a powerful reminder that the principles of transparency, accountability, and separation of powers transcend political affiliations.

In Conclusion

The D.C. Circuit Court of Appeals' decision is a beacon of hope in times when the shadows of secrecy seem to loom large over the corridors of power. It is a testament to the resilience of the American judicial system and its unwavering commitment to upholding the Constitution. The Trump administration's attempt to cloak the federal spending database was not just an affront to Congress' authority but to the American people's right to know. This ruling is not just a defeat for the administration but a victory for democracy, transparency, and the rule of law.
