‘Minnesota has had it’: New shooting sparks fury as Tim Walz issues fierce demand

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosJanuary 25, 20261 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Federal agents stand guard at the scene of a shooting involving federal immigration agents in Minneapolis, Minnesota, U.S., January 24, 2026. REUTERS/Tim Evans

Minnesota Gov. Tim Walz issued a fierce demand to the Trump administration after reports of “another horrific shooting by federal agents” circulated on Saturday.

“I just spoke with the White House after another horrific shooting by federal agents this morning. Minnesota has had it. This is sickening,” Walz wrote in a social media post on X. “The President must end this operation. Pull the thousands of violent, untrained officers out of Minnesota. Now.”
