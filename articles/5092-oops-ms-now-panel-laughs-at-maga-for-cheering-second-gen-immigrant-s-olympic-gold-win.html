'Oops': MS NOW panel laughs at MAGA for cheering second gen-immigrant's Olympic gold win

It did not escape notice on MS NOW on Friday morning that the MAGA online world was thrilled by US skater Alysa Liu winning the Olympic gold medal in the Women’s Single Skating event, passing over the fact that she is the child of an immigrant.

At the same time fans of Donald Trump’s administration have been applauding the forced round-up of immigrants by masked ICE agents, MAGA world is basking in Liu’s victory, the first in that event by a US skater since Sarah Hughes won gold in 2002.

That juxtaposition was not lost on “Morning Joe” co-host Joe Scarborough and contributor Eugene Robinson since Liu is the daughter of a Chinese immigrant who came to the US in the ‘90s to escape political persecution.

As Scarborough noted, “Gene Robinson, it was really inspiring. I will say how hilarious that the sort of MAGA online MAGA world were celebrating Alysa Liu’s gold medal without a hint of any irony. Celebrating the immigrant who they were celebrating as an American.”

“You know, ‘USA! USA!,” he joked before adding, “Which of course, that’s basically proving the point that we’ve been making all along that Ronald Reagan made in his final speech to America, that we are strengthened by immigrants.”

That led Robinson to put his hand over his mouth and offer “Oops,” as the entire panel started laughing.

Our Analysis:

The Stark Irony of MAGA’s Selective Celebration

In an episode that could only be described as peak hypocrisy, the MAGA online world’s ecstatic celebration of Alysa Liu’s Olympic gold medal win in the Women’s Single Skating event is a spectacle of irony so rich it nearly defies belief. Liu, a shining example of American talent and perseverance, is also the daughter of a Chinese immigrant, a fact that seems conveniently overlooked by her newfound fans amidst their fervor. This glaring oversight was pointed out on MS NOW’s “Morning Joe,” spotlighting the dichotomy between MAGA’s usual stance on immigrants and their sudden embrace of Liu’s victory.

Selective Patriotism at Its Finest

The MAGA crowd, notorious for its hardline stance against immigration, particularly from non-Western countries, seemingly dropped its xenophobic fervor in a heartbeat to bask in the reflected glory of Liu’s victory. It’s a classic case of selective patriotism, where achievements are Americanized, and struggles or complexities are othered or outright ignored. The irony is as blatant as it is appalling, considering the Trump administration’s infamous crackdown on immigrants, characterized by the heartless round-ups by masked ICE agents.

A Convenient Amnesia

Alysa Liu’s victory is undeniably monumental, marking the first win in the Women’s Single Skating event by a US skater since 2002. However, the MAGA world’s celebration glaringly omits the acknowledgment of her heritage and the immigrant story behind her success. Liu’s father came to the US in the ‘90s to escape political persecution, a narrative that is all too familiar yet increasingly marginalized in the rhetoric of those now singing her praises.

The Hypocrisy of It All

“Morning Joe” co-host Joe Scarborough and contributor Eugene Robinson couldn’t help but highlight the irony, with Scarborough noting the MAGA world’s lack of irony in celebrating an immigrant’s achievement as quintessentially American. The situation perfectly encapsulates the double standards and selective narrative shaping that plagues much of MAGA’s discourse, proving once again that immigrant successes are celebrated only when convenient or beneficial to their cause.

A Lesson in Cognitive Dissonance

The laughter shared by the “Morning Joe” panel at this hypocrisy is a bitter reminder of the cognitive dissonance that pervades the MAGA ideology. It’s a clear demonstration of the selective embrace of immigrant success stories, conveniently ignoring the broader narrative of struggle, sacrifice, and systemic barriers faced by immigrants in America. Alysa Liu’s victory should indeed be celebrated, but not in isolation from her background or the broader context of America’s complex relationship with immigration.

In Conclusion

The MAGA crowd’s celebration of Alysa Liu, while simultaneously supporting draconian immigration policies, is a stark illustration of the selective patriotism and hypocrisy that underpins much of their ideology. It’s a reminder that achievements are often stripped of their context to suit narrative needs, ignoring the rich tapestry of immigrant stories that truly make America great. In the end, Liu’s victory is a testament to the strength and diversity of America, something that no amount of selective celebration can obscure.
