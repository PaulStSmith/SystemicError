​Trump is 'infuriating the MAGA base' as new global war threat looms: report

Donald Trump's inability to broker a continuing –– but tense –– peace between Israel and Iran has reportedly placed him on the outs with his MAGA base after Israel launched an attack that threatens a growing war in the region.

According to a report from Politico's Rachel Bade, the "Operation Rising Lion" assault on Iran that targeted nuclear facilities in Iran and reportedly ended the lives of three top Iranian military officials now puts pressure on the president who will be faced with a dilemma of how to rein in Israel while at the same time supporting a country he has backed unequivocally.

As Bade wrote, prominent MAGA supporters have been pressuring Trump to make sure what occurred on Thursday would not come to pass, and now he will have to scramble to keep all factions within his base satisfied.

According to Bade, "... the president either tried and failed, highlighting his lack of sway with Israeli Prime Minister Bibi Netanyahu — or he privately greenlit the campaign against the warnings of his base (which the administration firmly denies)" which has led to one prominent apologist for the president, Matt Boyle of Breitbart, to tell Bade, "What the president does from here could end up defining his presidency. He has to balance protecting America’s greatest ally in the region in Israel with avoiding getting the USA drawn into war.”

Trump ally Charlie Kirk also fretted over the pending schism within the MAGA base, explaining, "As you very well know, I’m very pro-Israel on this show; I’m just simply interpreting the political dynamics here. And I could tell you right now that the audience, you guys ... are not thrilled with this situation at all.”

"How does the America First foreign policy doctrine and foreign policy agenda … stay consistent with this right now?” he added.

Breitbart's Boyle agreed by suggesting, "The president listens to the base — it’s his best quality. Clearly people across the MAGA movement are watching what’s happening very closely and are concerned that any moves by globalists and neocon forces to drag the United States into another endless war in the Middle East would cause serious political damage to the president.”

Early Friday morning, Trump took to Truth Social to attempt persuade Iran to bend to Israel's will persuade Iran to bend to Israel's will, writing, "There has already been great death and destruction, but there is still time to make this slaughter, with the next already planned attacks being even more brutal, come to an end. Iran must make a deal, before there is nothing left, and save what was once known as the Iranian Empire. No more death, no more destruction, JUST DO IT, BEFORE IT IS TOO LATE. "

Our Analysis:

The MAGA Dilemma: Trump's Tightrope Walk Over Israel and Iran

The recent Politico article by Rachel Bade throws a glaring spotlight on the precarious position former President Donald Trump finds himself in, following Israel's "Operation Rising Lion" assault on Iran. This incident has not only threatened to ignite a larger conflict in the region but has also exposed a rift within Trump's MAGA base, a cornerstone of his political power.

Trump's MAGA base, a mix of staunch isolationists and ardent supporters of Israel, finds itself at a crossroads. On one hand, there is the unwavering support for Israel, an ally Trump has backed "unequivocally." On the other, there's the "America First" doctrine, which theoretically opposes any entanglement that could drag the U.S. into another endless Middle Eastern conflict. The attack on Iran, resulting in significant casualties including three top Iranian military officials, has forced Trump into a diplomatic high-wire act: maintaining his support for Israel while avoiding the quagmire of war that his base so deeply fears.

The Pressure Cooker

Bade's reporting uncovers the extent of the pressure Trump faces from key figures within his orbit. Matt Boyle of Breitbart ominously suggests that Trump's response to the Israel-Iran situation could "define his presidency," highlighting a stark choice between safeguarding America's most significant ally in the region and steering clear of another war. Similarly, Charlie Kirk voices concern over the potential for a schism within the MAGA base, questioning how Trump's "America First" policy aligns with the current crisis.

This sentiment is echoed by Boyle's assertion that Trump's attentiveness to his base is his "best quality," framing the current scenario as a test of Trump’s ability to navigate the desires of his supporters against the backdrop of global politics. The fear of neoconservative and globalist influences dragging the U.S. into conflict serves as a specter haunting Trump's base, challenging the former president's political acumen.

Trump's Response: A Plea on Truth Social

In an early morning post on Truth Social, Trump attempted to mediate the escalating situation with a blend of admonition and negotiation. He called on Iran to "make a deal" to prevent further "death and destruction," evoking the image of a once-great "Iranian Empire" and urging action "BEFORE IT IS TOO LATE." This plea, while dramatic, underscores the tightrope Trump walks between advocating for peace and supporting Israel's aggressive stance—a balance critical to maintaining his political base's support.

The Underlying Irony

The irony of Trump's predicament cannot be overstated. A president who prided himself on deal-making and strong-arming opponents on the international stage now finds himself potentially sidelined, his influence over key ally Netanyahu questioned. Was his failure to prevent "Operation Rising Lion" a sign of diminished sway, or, as some suggest, did he privately greenlight the campaign despite his base's warnings? Either scenario paints a picture of a leader caught between the rock of geopolitical strategy and the hard place of populist support.

In conclusion, the Politico article sheds light on the complex dynamics at play within Trump's MAGA base and the broader geopolitical landscape. Trump's challenge is not only about managing international relations but also about navigating the intricate web of expectations from his supporters. As the situation unfolds, Trump's legacy and the coherence of the MAGA movement hang in the balance, with the specter of war in the Middle East serving as a potent reminder of the unpredictable nature of global politics and the precariousness of populist support.
