Mockery ensues as Trump calls to ‘declassify’ Amelia Earhart records over Epstein files

President Donald Trump attends the 45th Ryder Cup golf competition at Bethpage Black Golf Course in Farmingdale, New York on Sept. 26. Mandel Ngan/Pool via REUTERS

President Donald Trump earned swift mockery on social media after calling on the government to “declassify” files on Amelia Earhart’s disappearance, with many requesting that he instead turn his attention to the still-unreleased Jeffrey Epstein files.

Trump Friday posted the following on his social platform, Truth Social:

“I have been asked by many people about the life and times of Amelia Earhart, such an interesting story, and would I consider declassifying and releasing everything about her, in particular, her last, fatal flight! She was an Aviation Pioneer, the first woman to fly solo across the Atlantic Ocean, and achieved many other Aviation ‘firsts.’ She disappeared in the South Pacific while trying to become the first woman to fly around the World. Amelia made it almost three quarters around the World before she suddenly, and without notice, vanished, never to be seen again. Her disappearance, almost 90 years ago, has captivated millions. I am ordering my Administration to declassify and release all Government Records related to Amelia Earhart, her final trip, and everything else about her.”

But the response online was mounting, with people mocking him for failing to release the long-sought-after Epstein files.

“Everyone: Release the Epstein files. Trump: Best I can do is Amelia Earhart’s flight logs,” Nick Lutsko wrote on X.

“’And what of Jeffrey Epstein, sir? Will you release the files about him?’ ‘You were talking about Amelia Earhart?’” Jorge Ribas wrote on X.

“What terrifies me is if Trump were to declassify the Amelia Earhart files. Imagine the backlash against peaceful coconut crabs?” John Bria wrote on X.

“Personally, I’d rather have the Epstein files than files about Amelia Earhart.... Just sayin’,” Brandon Collins wrote on X.

“Don’t worry about food prices or affordable housing. He’s on this Amelia Earhart situation we’ve all been worrying about,” Doc Coyle wrote on X.

“Trump just called to release the Amelia Earhart files on Truth Social. Why doesn’t he make a post like this calling for the release of all the Epstein files?” Ed Krassenstein wrote on X.

Our Analysis:

The Curious Case of Priorities: Trump, Earhart, and Epstein

In a move that could only originate from the depths of what we’ve come to expect from the political theater of absurdity, President Donald Trump has once again commandeered the national conversation, but this time, with a peculiar fixation on the decades-old mystery of Amelia Earhart’s disappearance. His announcement, made via his social platform Truth Social, to “declassify and release all Government Records related to Amelia Earhart” has not only bewildered the masses but also sparked a wave of mockery and critical commentary across social media platforms.

The Red Herring: Amelia Earhart

Firstly, let’s dissect the bizarre choice of Amelia Earhart’s files as a subject of national interest. Earhart, an admirable aviator and a historical figure whose disappearance has indeed puzzled many, last made headlines nearly 90 years ago. Trump’s sudden urge to unravel this mystery is as confounding as it is irrelevant to the pressing issues facing the nation and the world today. The decision reeks of a diversion tactic, a magician’s flourish meant to distract from more pressing, contemporary controversies.

Why Earhart, and why now? It’s a question that answers itself with the very mockery it has spawned. By focusing on a long-gone figure, Trump effectively diverts attention from the plethora of current and more damning discussions, notably, the still-undisclosed Jeffrey Epstein files.

The Elephant in the Room: Jeffrey Epstein Files

The juxtaposition of public demand for the Epstein files against Trump’s Earhart announcement is a stark and telling contrast. Epstein’s case, mired in allegations of sex trafficking and deep-seated connections with numerous high-profile figures, remains a gaping wound in the public’s trust in their leaders and the justice system. The calls for transparency and accountability through the release of the Epstein files represent a collective outcry for truth and justice in issues that are painfully current and relevant.

Yet, Trump’s priorities seem to align elsewhere, with an almost farcical focus on historical curiosities over present-day atrocities. The social media reactions underscore a profound public frustration and incredulity. Quotes like, “Everyone: Release the Epstein files. Trump: Best I can do is Amelia Earhart’s flight logs,“ and, “Don’t worry about food prices or affordable housing. He’s on this Amelia Earhart situation we’ve all been worrying about,“ serve not just as mockery, but as biting commentary on the misalignment of political focus and public need.

The Bigger Picture: Distraction and Diversion

This scenario is emblematic of a larger, systemic issue in political discourse: the strategic use of distraction to divert public attention from controversies that could harm political reputations or reveal uncomfortable truths. The choice to champion the declassification of Earhart’s files, while ignoring the clamorous calls for the Epstein files, is a calculated move. It attempts to replace a narrative fraught with political landmines with one far removed from the current century’s scandals.

In conclusion, Trump’s call to release the Amelia Earhart files, juxtaposed with the glaring omission of the Epstein files, is not just a matter of misplaced priorities. It’s a deliberate strategy, a smokescreen designed to shift the narrative away from a deeply interconnected web of power, abuse, and potential criminality that the Epstein files might unravel. The public’s response, laced with mockery and criticism, highlights a growing weariness with such tactics. As the discourse unfolds, it remains imperative to question, to critique, and to demand accountability on issues that truly matter, rather than being led astray by the spectacle of diversion.
