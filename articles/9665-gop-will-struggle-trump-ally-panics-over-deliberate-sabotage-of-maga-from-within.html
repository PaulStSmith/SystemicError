MAGA activist Laura Loomer, who has the ear of the president and has been dubbed the Trump-whisperer, melted down over the weekend about a weakening of MAGA. She specifically noted that she was worried about what would happen “after Trump.”

“Here’s what’s happening: After Trump, the GOP is going to struggle to lead because as we have seen, President Trump is the glue that keeps the party together. So what’s happening? Well, ingrate Republicans who attached themselves to Trump think they don’t need him anymore or they are retaliating because he didn’t endorse them,” Loomer wrote on X. “These people will work to undermine Trump in the last 3.5 years he’s in office and they will join forces with the radical left to smear MAGA while they give a pass to the Red-Green alliance so that our country becomes more radical and more violent. They will continue to use Israel and then they will openly embrace Islam as a wedge issue to break up the evangelical and Christian voting blocks that have strongly supported the GOP.”

She went on to name names, including Marjorie Taylor Greene, saying, “This is why you have seen a coalition of ‘conservatives’ embrace Islamists and leftists with rhetoric that is more isolationist and openly hostile to anyone who speaks out about Islamic terror. They will sit on the sidelines and allow for Islam to ravage our country while they create a toxic political environment by blaming Trump and Jews for all of their problems. You already see this in the way [Greene] and Tucker fawned over Mamdani and said he’s the ultimate ‘America First candidate’. A Muslim communist.”

She added, “These people will work as hard as they can to shatter MAGA so they can ‘save themselves’ from the wrath of the Left. Come 2026 and 2028, this coalition will work to destroy and break apart the various coalitions in our country that supported Trump because their filter for what is ‘good enough’ as a candidate will be so extreme that it pushes away moderates on the right and left.”

Loomer then concluded, “The 2028 Presidential primary will be a circus of conspiracy theories as the Woke Right attempts to derail all opportunity for MAGA to continue. If the GOP doesn’t take action to politically neutralize those on the right who seek to destroy MAGA through their resentment of Trump, the Woke Right will hand the midterms to the Dems by suppressing the vote and they will hand 2028 to a Democrat. 2028 will be won by whoever recognizes that threat now, and takes action to stop this deliberate sabotage of MAGA.”

Our Analysis:

The Loomer Diatribe: A MAGA Meltdown or a Calculated Cry?

In a recent tirade that reads more like a political thriller script than a coherent analysis, MAGA activist Laura Loomer has sounded an alarm bell that resonates with the cacophony of a conspiracy theory in overdrive. Loomer, known for her incendiary comments and having the so-called ear of former President Donald Trump, has claimed a “deliberate sabotage of MAGA” is underway, a statement that deserves a dissection not for its faint basis in reality but for its illustrative value in showcasing the current disarray within the Republican Party.

The Glue is Melting

Loomer’s assertion that Trump is the “glue” holding the GOP together is not entirely without merit. Trump’s presidency undeniably reshaped the Republican Party, creating a faction that is unwaveringly loyal to the man rather than the party’s traditional principles. However, to suggest that the GOP will crumble into oblivion “after Trump” is to ignore the historical resilience and adaptability of political parties. It’s also a convenient narrative for those who wish to position themselves as the only true heirs of the Trump legacy.

The Art of the Conspiracy

Loomer’s narrative quickly descends into a maelstrom of conspiracy theories. She speaks of a coalition of conservatives, Islamists, and leftists - an unlikely alliance if there ever was one - working together to undermine Trump and, by extension, the MAGA movement. This is a classic conspiracy theory tactic: create a nebulous enemy that is simultaneously everywhere and nowhere, making it impossible to disprove but easy to fear.

Her attack on Marjorie Taylor Greene and others for allegedly embracing Islamists is a bewildering twist in her argument. It showcases a deep misunderstanding (or deliberate misrepresentation) of political alliances and the complexities of American and global politics. Loomer’s rhetoric about Islam “ravaging our country” is not just fearmongering; it’s a dangerously xenophobic narrative that has no place in rational political discourse.

Predictions or Paranoia?

Loomer’s predictions for the future - a 2028 Presidential primary filled with conspiracy theories and the so-called “Woke Right” handing victories to Democrats - reads more like a doomsday prophecy than a grounded political analysis. Her use of terms like “Woke Right” is an attempt to frame any deviation from her brand of conservatism as betrayal, a tactic that’s become all too common in political arguments.

Her call to action, urging the GOP to “politically neutralize” those who seek to destroy MAGA, is eerily authoritarian. Political movements thrive on debate and evolution, not on purges of dissenting views. If the MAGA movement cannot withstand internal criticism or adapt to changing political landscapes, perhaps it is not as robust as Loomer believes.

Conclusion: A Fading Echo, Not a Rallying Cry

Loomer’s meltdown is symptomatic of a larger issue within the MAGA faction: an inability to plan for the future or to engage constructively with criticism. By focusing on conspiracies and perceived betrayals, Loomer and like-minded individuals are not preserving Trump’s legacy; they’re isolating themselves from the broader political discourse.

The real sabotage of MAGA, it seems, comes not from shadowy coalitions of enemies but from within, from an unwillingness to confront the realities of a changing America and a changing world. As the GOP looks to the future, it must decide whether it will continue to indulge these apocalyptic visions, or if it will chart a new course based on principles rather than personalities. Only time will tell, but if Loomer’s vision is the best the MAGA movement has to offer, its days of influence may indeed be numbered.
