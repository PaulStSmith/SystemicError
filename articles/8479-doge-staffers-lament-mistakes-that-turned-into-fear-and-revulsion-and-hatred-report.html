Some DOGE staffers lamented “mistakes” the agency made while it tried to slash federal spending, which caused the department to become hated by its colleagues, according to a new report.

One of President Donald Trump’s first actions in his second term was to create the Department of Government Efficiency, which was led by SpaceX and Tesla founder Elon Musk. The purpose of DOGE was to decrease federal spending by up to $2 trillion, according to the agency, although the amount of money the department saved the government is still unknown.

Nine former DOGE staffers spoke to Politico about their experience working for Musk’s agency. They noted multiple missteps the agency made, which put them squarely at odds with people who could have helped them fulfil their mission.

“The height of our power was the five-bullets email,” a DOGE official told Politico, referring to the “Fork in the Road” email Musk sent to government employees asking them to accept deferred resignations. The officials described the email as a “mistake” that “pitted DOGE against departments and agencies who were increasingly frustrated by Musk’s lack of communication and heavyhandedness,” according to the report.

“Then it turned into fear and revulsion and hatred,” the staffer said.

Other mistakes the agency made include allowing Steve Davis, a Musk ally, to direct DOGE’s work even after Davis left the federal government.

“It wasn’t just a couple, it was like a lot of people asking what do we do about this?” an official told Politico.

Our Analysis:

The DOGE Debacle: A Symphony of Missteps and Mismanagement

The inception of the Department of Government Efficiency (DOGE), under the Trump administration, heralded with much fanfare and the promise of slashing federal spending by an ambitious $2 trillion, has devolved into a cautionary tale of what happens when hubris and haphazard management collide. Spearheaded by none other than Elon Musk, a figure synonymous with high-tech ventures like SpaceX and Tesla, DOGE’s journey from a beacon of government frugality to an entity “hated” by its peers encapsulates a series of misguided actions and strategic blunders.

The Grand Illusion of Efficiency

At the heart of DOGE’s tumultuous saga lies a fundamental irony. An agency created to champion the cause of efficiency has been anything but. The goal was noble, if not overly ambitious: reduce federal spending by $2 trillion. Yet, the execution was flawed from the outset. The actual savings, shrouded in ambiguity, remain “unknown.” This lack of transparency not only undermines DOGE’s foundational purpose but also erodes any semblance of credibility it might have hoped to maintain.

Missteps and Miscommunications

The narrative of DOGE’s failure is punctuated by a series of missteps, most notably the infamous “Fork in the Road” email. This communication, intended to streamline operations, instead showcased Musk’s heavy-handed approach and a glaring disconnect with the very people DOGE needed to engage cooperatively. The result? A transformation of what could have been constructive collaboration into fear, revulsion, and hatred.

Moreover, the decision to allow Steve Davis, a Musk confidant, to direct DOGE’s operations post-departure from the federal government, reeks of nepotism and a lack of professional boundaries. Such actions not only compromise the integrity of DOGE’s mission but also erode trust within the broader governmental ecosystem.

The Fallout

The experiences shared by the nine former DOGE staffers with Politico paint a grim picture of an agency in disarray. An entity that could have been a symbol of governmental prudence has instead become a pariah, its potential contributions overshadowed by a series of unforced errors and a leadership style that prioritized control over collaboration.

Conclusion

The DOGE experiment serves as a stark reminder of the perils of entrusting governmental efficiency to individuals and ideologies that prioritize ambition over achievable objectives. This saga isn’t merely a critique of Musk or the Trump administration but a broader cautionary tale about the importance of communication, transparency, and genuine collaboration in the pursuit of public service. As the dust settles on DOGE’s aspirations, the lessons remain clear, serving as a guide for future endeavors aimed at government reform.
