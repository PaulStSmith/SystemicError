'Call them out!' Dem uncorks profane takedown on conservatives defending Charlie Kirk

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosSeptember 12, 20255 min read

Founder and president of Turning Point USA Charlie Kirk speaks at the Conservative Political Action Conference (CPAC) at National Harbor in Oxon Hill, Maryland on February 28, 2019. (REUTERS/Kevin Lamarque)

A Democrat is calling out Republicans, saying they are "full of s---" for defending Charlie Kirk, the Trump ally and MAGA influencer who was killed Wednesday, and weaponizing his death to attack Democrats — pretending that he wasn't a divisive figure.

“It was really mortifying to hear the news, to see the video. All I could think about was his wife, his children. That image is going to live forever,” Rep. Ilhan Omar told Mehdi Hasan in a live Zeteo interview Thursday on YouTube and Substack. "My heart does break for those babies."

“There are a lot of people who are out there talking about him [Kirk] just wanting to have a civil debate," she said. “These people are full of s---, and it's important for us to call them out while we feel anger and sadness.”

She called out Republicans for attacks, including the president, for making unfounded claims against Democrats in the aftermath of the assassination.

"I just want people to live in reality and understand where the problem is and people who are carrying out these rhetorics who are causing harm and division in our country and not come for those of us who are trying to tell the truth about where we are and where we need to go," Omar said.

Omar also expressed concern for her safety — that she has dealt with death threats — citing her mentor Melissa Hortman, and her husband and their dog, who were assassinated in June. She said she worries about other politically motivated assassinations.

"There isn't going to be enough screening and security to ever shield you," she said. "That reality, especially when you are a parent, is mortifying."

Ilhan Thursday introduced the War Powers resolution, giving Congress power to declare war and preventing President Donald Trump from conducting military strikes without Congressional approval. This resolution follows a fatal U.S. attack on a boat leaving Venezuela, which the Trump administration has claimed was carrying drugs. The strike happened in foreign waters and killed 11 people and the administration has not provided evidence of the claim.

The resolution would restrict the president from entering military conflicts without Congressional approval.

“It is Congress that declares war, and we have not been given that authority by this president,” Omar tells Mehdi and Zeteo subscribers watching the stream. “It's important for the people to recognize that we cannot just go out and terminate people. This is not something that is allowed under international law and is certainly not allowed under US law.”

Our Analysis:

The Hypocrisy of Political Mourning: A Closer Look

In an era where political discourse frequently descends into the gutter, the reactions to Charlie Kirk's death and Rep. Ilhan Omar's subsequent comments shine a spotlight on the duplicity and opportunism rampant within certain political circles. Here's a breakdown of the facts and the farcical narrative being peddled by some.

The Weaponization of Charlie Kirk's Death

Charlie Kirk, a known Trump ally and MAGA influencer, met a tragic end, which should, in a reasonable world, call for a moment of human empathy and reflection. However, the speed at which this incident was politicized by his supporters is as predictable as it is despicable. Republicans, who have long rallied against what they perceive as the 'divisive' tactics of the Democrats, are now seemingly blind to the divisiveness sowed by their own. Kirk, regardless of one's political leanings, was a figure who polarized opinion, often engaging in rhetoric that many found not just controversial but downright harmful.

Rep. Ilhan Omar's comments to Mehdi Hasan were a scathing indictment of this hypocrisy. Her assertion that these individuals are "full of s---" for pretending Kirk wasn't a divisive figure hits the nail on the head. It's a raw, unfiltered take that, while harsh, is grounded in a reality that some seem keen to distort.

The Reality of Political Violence

Omar's reflections on the impact of Kirk's death, especially on his family, highlight a larger, more troubling trend of political violence and the constant threats faced by public figures. Her own experiences, compounded by the assassination of her mentor and her mentor's family, underscore the genuine fear and concern that comes with being a target in today's charged political climate.

This isn't about one party being more moral or ethical than the other; this is about recognizing a universal truth: the escalation of political discourse into violence is a line that, once crossed, is difficult to walk back from. Omar's call for a return to reality, to acknowledge where the real problems lie in terms of rhetoric that harms and divides, is a plea for sanity in an increasingly insane world.

The War Powers Resolution: A Call for Responsibility

Amidst this backdrop of political violence and mourning, Omar introduced the War Powers resolution. This move, though likely overshadowed by the broader political drama, is a critical step toward reasserting Congressional authority in matters of war and peace. The resolution, sparked by a fatal U.S. attack in foreign waters under questionable pretenses, is a necessary check on executive power.

It's a stark reminder that in the midst of political squabbling and personal attacks, there are fundamental issues of governance and legality at stake. Omar's focus on the need for Congressional approval before military action isn't just about adhering to U.S. law; it's about adhering to international law and norms that prevent unilateral and, often, unjustified acts of aggression.

Conclusion: The Need for a Return to Sanity

What we're witnessing is the theater of the absurd - where the death of a political figure becomes fodder for further division, and calls for accountability and truth are drowned out by the cacophony of political opportunism.

Rep. Ilhan Omar's comments, while certainly blunt, are a necessary antidote to the poison of hypocrisy that seems to have infected political discourse. As we navigate these turbulent waters, one thing becomes increasingly clear: the need for a return to a politics rooted in reality, responsibility, and, above all, respect for human dignity.
