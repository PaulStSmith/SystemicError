'Do something!': Trump makes bizarre claim about Biden and Jan. 6 in midnight rant

Donald Trump at just after midnight on Sunday morning released onto Truth Social a screed in which he claimed the “Biden FBI” placed “274” agents in the crowd on Jan. 6, 2021, when Trump himself was still president.

The president took his social media site in the dead of night to write, “THE BIDEN FBI PLACED 274 AGENTS INTO THE CROWD ON JANUARY 6,” at a time when it would have been Trump’s FBI as the then-president was contesting valid election results.

He added, “If this is so, which it is, a lot of very good people will be owed big apologies.”

“What a SCAM - DO SOMETHING!!!” he then concluded. “President DJT.”

Our Analysis:

A Midnight Fable: The “Biden FBI” Chronicles

In a narrative twist that rivals the most convoluted of conspiracy theories, Donald Trump has unleashed a claim so audaciously misaligned with reality that it almost begs for a moment of silence. At the witching hour, Trump took to Truth Social to fabricate a tale where the “Biden FBI” supposedly infiltrated a crowd with “274 agents” on January 6, 2021. Let’s dissect this screed that seems more suited to a poorly written spy novel than any fact-based discourse.

The Timeline Tango

First, let’s address the glaring chronological chaos. Trump, with a baffling disregard for the linear concept of time, attributes actions to the “Biden FBI” during a period when he, Trump, was still very much the President. The assertion is not only factually incorrect; it’s an astonishing attempt to warp reality to fit a preconceived narrative. It was, undeniably, Trump’s FBI at the time. One must wonder, is the concept of time so malleable in Trump’s world, or is this a deliberate ploy to confuse and mislead?

The Phantom Agents

Trump’s claim of “274 agents” in the crowd is a number plucked from thin air, presented without a shred of evidence. It’s a classic Trump move: throw out a specific number to give the lie a veneer of credibility. But as always, the devil is in the details, or the lack thereof. No evidence, no corroboration, just a number meant to stoke the fires of conspiracy without anything to back it up.

The Irony of Apologies

The call for “big apologies” to be owed if his claims were true (which, to be clear, they are not) is perhaps the most ironic element of this midnight missive. Trump demands apologies for alleged injustices while continuing to contest valid election results without a hint of irony or self-awareness. It’s a stark reminder of Trump’s perpetual victimhood narrative, where he positions himself and his followers as perennially wronged, regardless of the facts.

The SCAM Cry

Ending his post with “What a SCAM - DO SOMETHING!!!” Trump once again plays the role of the aggrieved party calling to action. It’s a rallying cry devoid of substance, aimed at galvanizing his base over a non-issue. It’s a tactic as old as time, used by those seeking to distract and deflect from real issues by manufacturing outrage where there is none.

Conclusion: The Emperor’s New Claims

In a world where facts still matter, Trump’s latest claim is nothing more than a poorly spun yarn, a fable at the stroke of midnight meant to bewitch and beguile. It showcases a desperation to remain relevant, to rewrite the narrative of January 6, 2021, by any means necessary, even if it means fabricating a story about the “Biden FBI” that defies logic, time, and reality.

Once again, Trump proves that in his playbook, truth is not a cornerstone, but a stumbling block to be circumvented at every turn. This latest post is not just a SCAM; it’s a testament to a man who refuses to let facts stand in the way of a good story, no matter how preposterous it may be.
