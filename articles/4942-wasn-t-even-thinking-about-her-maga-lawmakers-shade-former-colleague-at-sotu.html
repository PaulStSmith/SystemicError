'Wasn't even thinking about her': MAGA lawmakers shade former colleague at SOTU

WASHINGTON — A pair of MAGA lawmakers shaded one of their former colleagues at President Donald Trump’s State of the Union address on Tuesday night.

Raw Story asked MAGA Reps. Lauren Boebert (R-CO) and Byron Donalds (R-FL) whether they were missing former Rep. Marjorie Taylor Greene (R-GA) ahead of the speech. Greene made a name for herself at former President Joe Biden’s State of the Union addresses by heckling the former president.

“Wasn’t even thinking about her,” Boebert told Raw Story.

“Oh, c’mon!” Donalds responded.

Greene retired from Congress earlier this year after she and Trump had a falling out over the administration’s handling of the Jeffrey Epstein files. Greene claimed Trump told her he was protecting some of the people mentioned in the files because he didn’t want to see his friends get hurt.

Greene was also one of the lawmakers who pushed for a bill to force the Trump administration to release all of the Epstein files in its possession. She held a press conference with multiple Epstein survivors, which caused Trump to lash out at her.

Our Analysis:

The Spectacle of MAGA Infighting: Greene’s Ghost at the State of the Union

In the latest episode of the ongoing MAGA saga, the State of the Union address served as a backdrop not just for political grandstanding, but for the petty infighting and shadowboxing that has come to define the MAGA movement. Representatives Lauren Boebert (R-CO) and Byron Donalds (R-FL) took a moment to shade their former colleague, Marjorie Taylor Greene (R-GA), in a manner that was less about policy and more about high school cafeteria politics.

“Wasn’t even thinking about her,” Boebert declared, with Donalds adding a rather theatrical “Oh, c’mon!” This interaction, as reported by Raw Story, is emblematic of the performative and often superficial nature of political discourse within the MAGA camp. It’s not about the substantive issues that affect Americans but about personal vendettas and the drama of who’s in and who’s out.

The Fall from Grace of Marjorie Taylor Greene

Marjorie Taylor Greene’s absence was noted not because of her legislative prowess or her contributions to policy, but because of her previous performances of heckling President Joe Biden during his addresses. This reduction of a lawmaker’s role to that of a provocateur speaks volumes about the state of political discourse today. Greene’s retirement from Congress earlier this year, following a fallout with former President Donald Trump over the handling of the Jeffrey Epstein files, marks a significant chapter in the MAGA narrative.

Greene’s claim that Trump wished to protect his friends mentioned in the Epstein files, rather than allow for transparency and accountability, pulls back the curtain on the MAGA movement’s oft-touted principles of draining the swamp. It’s a glaring contradiction; a movement that prides itself on combating the so-called deep state and elite conspiracies is now seen shielding its own.

Moreover, Greene’s push for a bill to release all of the Epstein files held by the Trump administration, culminating in a press conference with Epstein survivors, was met with hostility rather than support from Trump. This incident lays bare the MAGA movement’s hypocrisy. It champions whistleblowers and truth-tellers only when it’s convenient, and discards them when they pose a threat to its leading figures.

Conclusion: The MAGA Morass

The infighting, the backstabbing, and the performative allegiance to truth and transparency are all symptomatic of a deeper malaise within the MAGA movement. As Boebert and Donalds’ comments show, the focus is not on governance or on addressing the needs of the American people. Instead, it’s on personal rivalries and maintaining a facade of unity, even as the movement cannibalizes its own.

The saga of Marjorie Taylor Greene – from a vocal supporter to a sidelined critic – serves as a cautionary tale. It reveals a movement that is more concerned with loyalty to individuals than to principles, and more interested in spectacle than in substance. The State of the Union address, with all its pageantry, was merely a backdrop for these petty squabbles. It’s a stark reminder of the systemic error at the heart of the MAGA movement, where the quest for power often eclipses the pursuit of the public good.
