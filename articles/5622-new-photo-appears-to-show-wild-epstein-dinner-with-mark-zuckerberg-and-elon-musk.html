New photo appears to show ‘wild’ Epstein dinner with Mark Zuckerberg and Elon Musk

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosFebruary 8, 20264 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

A photograph appearing to depict Mark Zuckerberg (left) and Elon Musk (third from right) attending a dinner with Jeffrey Epstein on Aug. 2, 2015. (Epstein Files Transparency Act / Department of Justice)

A newly unearthed photograph shed new light on a 2015 dinner attended by Jeffrey Epstein, Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg and Tesla CEO Elon Musk, an event that Epstein described in an email as “wild.”

Published last week by the Justice Department, the photograph in question is included in an email Epstein had sent to himself on August 3, 2015. The photograph appears to show Zuckerberg and Musk sitting around a table in a dramatically lit room.

The day before Epstein had emailed the photograph to himself, he emailed celebrity doctor and CBS News contributor Peter Attia – whose deep ties to Epstein were exposed this week, sparking calls for his firing – that later that evening, he had plans for “dinner with musk thiel [and] zuckerburg.”

Epstein again referenced the dinner with Zuckerberg and Musk in an Aug. 20 email to Tom Pritzker, the Hyatt executive chairman who has been accused by a prominent Epstein accuser of sex trafficking, an accusation he “continues to vehemently deny.”

“i had dinner with zuckerburg, mu=k, thiel hoffman, wild,” Epstein wrote to Pritzker.

Zuckerberg has previously acknowledged the dinner event with Epstein, with his spokesperson saying Zuckerberg had only met Epstein “in passing, one time at a dinner honoring scientists that was not organized by Epstein.”

Musk, however, has not publicly acknowledged the dinner. He’s also claimed that Epstein had “repeatedly” tried to get him to visit his island and that he “declined,” a remark that has since been contradicted after newly revealed emails that showed Musk had “practically begged for an invitation” to Epstein’s island.

The dinner photograph Epstein emailed to himself was partially revealed in an August New York Timesreportfeaturing previously unseen images from inside his Manhattan mansion. The report showed a cabinet lined with framed photographs, including one that appears to match the image Epstein emailed himself in 2015, though it is partially obscured by another framed photograph.

Our Analysis:

The Elite’s Uncomfortable Company: Zuckerberg, Musk, and Epstein

In a revelation that reads more like a script from a disturbing drama than a page from the tech moguls’ public relations handbooks, a newly unearthed photograph has cast a long, chilling shadow over the previously disclosed connections between Jeffrey Epstein, Mark Zuckerberg, and Elon Musk. This image, a testament to the murky intersections of wealth, power, and moral ambiguity, underscores a gathering that would make any publicist break out in a cold sweat.

The Photograph and Its Implications

Embedded within an email Epstein, a convicted sex offender, sent to himself in August 2015, the photograph in question depicts Zuckerberg and Musk amidst what can only be described as a scene plucked from a film noir set, dramatically lit and all too telling. The day prior, Epstein’s correspondence boasted of his plans for a “wild” dinner with not only Zuckerberg and Musk but also Peter Thiel and Reid Hoffman, adding layers to the already complex web of high-profile associations.

The Dinner: A “Wild” Night Remembered

Epstein’s casual drop of names like Zuckerberg, Musk, and Thiel in his communications paints a picture of a network that, at best, can be accused of moral negligence. Zuckerberg, through his spokesperson, has attempted to distance himself from Epstein, framing their interaction as a brief and insignificant encounter at a dinner “not organized by Epstein.” Musk, on the other hand, has remained notably silent on the matter, despite previous claims of rejecting Epstein’s invitations to his infamous island—a narrative now contradicted by emails suggesting Musk had “practically begged” for an invite.

The Silence and Denials: A Pattern of Evasion

The silence and half-hearted denials that have emerged in the wake of these revelations are as telling as the photograph itself. Zuckerberg’s attempt to minimize his interaction with Epstein does little to assuage concerns about the judgment and integrity of those at the helm of the world’s most influential technology companies. Musk’s contradictory statements further muddy the waters, presenting a figure who is at once eager to align with Epstein’s network and later, seemingly, to distance himself from it.

The Uncomfortable Truth

What this photograph and the surrounding communications unveil is not just a moment in time but a glaring spotlight on the uncomfortable intersections between the tech elite and a convicted sex offender. It raises significant questions about the nature of the relationships between these figures and Epstein, their awareness of his criminal activities, and their moral compasses when navigating the social circles that included him.

In essence, the “wild” dinner of 2015 stands as a stark reminder of the systemic flaws within our elite circles—where the pursuit of influence, power, and connection often blinds individuals to the ethical and moral implications of their associations. The tech moguls involved might wish to dismiss or downplay their interactions with Epstein, but this photograph, and the emails that have surfaced alongside it, refuse to be swept under the rug. They demand a reevaluation of the narratives we’ve been sold about the character and judgment of some of the most powerful men in technology. In the end, the true wildness lies not in the details of the dinner itself but in the reckless disregard for ethical boundaries by those who attended.
