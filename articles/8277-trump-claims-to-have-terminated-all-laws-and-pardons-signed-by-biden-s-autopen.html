Our Analysis:

Trump’s Autopen Antics: A Masterclass in Misinformation and Ego

In an astonishing display of either profound ignorance or deliberate misinformation, President Donald Trump has proclaimed to have “terminated” all documents signed by former President Joe Biden using an autopen. This audacious claim is not just a testament to Trump’s disregard for legal norms but also highlights a troubling descent into authoritarian rhetoric, unworthy of a former leader of the free world.

The Autopen Allegation

Trump’s assertion, made in a Friday post on Truth Social, that “Any document signed by Sleepy Joe Biden with the Autopen, which was approximately 92% of them, is hereby terminated, and of no further force or effect,” is legally laughable and factually baseless. The use of an autopen for signing documents, especially in the context of a president’s duties, has been a common practice for decades, utilized by presidents from both parties to efficiently manage the voluminous demands of their office.

The claim that “The Autopen is not allowed to be used if approval is not specifically given by the President of the United States” is a misrepresentation of the process. The use of an autopen does not bypass presidential approval; rather, it is an extension of it, used after explicit consent has been given for the document in question.

Unsubstantiated Claims of Illegality

Trump’s statement that “The Radical Left Lunatics circling Biden around the beautiful Resolute Desk in the Oval Office took the Presidency away from him,” coupled with his declaration of cancelling “all Executive Orders, and anything else that was not directly signed by Crooked Joe Biden,” ventures into the realm of conspiracy theories. It’s an attempt to delegitimize not just the presidency of Joe Biden but the entire democratic process.

Moreover, the threat to bring up Biden on charges of perjury “if he says he was involved in the Autopen process” is beyond the bounds of Trump’s authority—not to mention a gross misunderstanding of what constitutes perjury.

The Legal Void

Perhaps the most glaring omission in Trump’s bombastic declaration is the complete lack of legal authority to back it up. There is no mechanism within the U.S. Constitution, federal law, or any known legal framework that would allow a former president to nullify the actions of their successor. This absence of legal grounding exposes the statement for what it truly is: a blend of wishful thinking and political grandstanding.

The Real Issue at Hand

This episode is a stark reminder of the dangers posed by misinformation, especially when propagated by individuals in positions of power. Trump’s proclamations, while devoid of legal substance, fuel divisive rhetoric and undermine public trust in the democratic process and the rule of law.

As observers and participants in a democratic society, it is our responsibility to critically evaluate the statements made by our leaders, to distinguish fact from fiction, and to hold those in power accountable for their words and actions. The fabric of democracy is weakened with every unsubstantiated claim, every unfounded accusation, and every attempt to manipulate the truth for political gain.

In sum, Trump’s claims about the autopen and his supposed authority to “terminate” Biden’s documents are not just an affront to legal and democratic norms but also a disturbing insight into the psyche of a man who once held the highest office in the land. It’s a potent reminder of the need for constant vigilance in the face of attempts to distort reality and erode the foundations of our democratic institutions.
