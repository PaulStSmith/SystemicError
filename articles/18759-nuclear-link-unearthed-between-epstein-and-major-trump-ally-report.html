‘Nuclear’ link unearthed between Epstein and major Trump ally: report

Unveiling the Shadows: Epstein, Surveillance, and Political Connections

In an era where transparency is often promised but seldom delivered, the revelations about Jeffrey Epstein’s New Mexico property, Zorro Ranch, serve as a stark reminder of the complex interplay between private interests and national security. The recent findings by Alisa Valdes-Rodriguez, drawn from public records, suggest a web of surveillance that stretches from a high-desert vacation ranch to the heart of America’s nuclear secrets.

The Strategic Location and Its Implications

Purchased by Epstein in 1993, Zorro Ranch is uniquely positioned between two pivotal U.S. nuclear weapons labs: Sandia National Laboratories and Los Alamos National Laboratory. This strategic location is not merely geographical but, as Valdes-Rodriguez points out, potentially operational. The ranch was later equipped with a “private microwave communications link,” a type of advanced communication technology typically reserved for entities like NSA field stations or the Department of Defense, not private vacation homes.

The FCC records, accessed by Valdes-Rodriguez, indicate that this system was established in 2016 with ongoing active licenses. This raises critical questions about the intended use and the current status of these communications capabilities, especially given their capacity to operate “entirely outside commercial internet infrastructure.”

The Political and Institutional Nexus

The transition of the ranch’s ownership from Epstein to Donald Huffines, a major ally to former President Donald Trump, after Epstein’s death in 2019, adds another layer of intrigue. Notably, Huffines purchased the property secretly in 2023, a fact that only came to light recently. This change in ownership could have been a point to reassess the necessity and legality of the existing surveillance infrastructure. However, the decision to maintain specific FCC licenses, while terminating others, suggests a selective continuation of the ranch’s capabilities.

Moreover, the hiring of Huffines’ son, Russell Huffines, by the Trump administration adds to the complexity. His role as associate director of agency outreach, while not directly linked to Zorro Ranch, positions the Huffines family within a sphere of substantial political influence, potentially impacting decisions about the property’s use and its communication setup.

Historical Context and Potential Motives

The backdrop of international espionage, as detailed by Valdes-Rodriguez, provides a broader context to the ranch’s capabilities. The involvement of Robert Maxwell, an alleged Israeli intelligence asset, and the use of the PROMIS software—a tool designed for surveillance—suggests a legacy of spying that could extend to the ranch’s operations. Epstein’s connection to Maxwell through Ghislaine Maxwell, coupled with the strategic positioning of communications equipment, paints a troubling picture of potential surveillance activities targeting U.S. nuclear facilities.

Current Status and Implications

The decision by the Huffines family to maintain the microwave link to Sandia Crest, while severally eliminating other FCC licenses, demands scrutiny. The retention of licenses in Epstein’s company’s name rather than transferring them to their own further clouds the transparency of their operations and intentions.

Given the sensitivity of the location and its capabilities, one must question who truly benefits from the continuation of such a surveillance setup and why it remains operational under new ownership. This scenario underscores the necessity for rigorous oversight and clarity regarding the use of such technologies on private properties, especially when positioned amidst national security interests.

Conclusion: A Call for Transparency and Accountability

This complex web of historical ties, advanced surveillance technology, and political connections calls for a reevaluation of how private properties equipped with such capabilities are regulated and monitored. The potential for misuse or unauthorized surveillance activities poses a significant risk, not just to individual privacy but to national security. The intertwining of private interests with sensitive national infrastructure necessitates a clear, accountable approach to ensure that such power is not misused or left unchecked.
