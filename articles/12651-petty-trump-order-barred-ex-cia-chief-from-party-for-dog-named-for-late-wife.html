'Petty': Trump order barred ex-CIA chief from party for dog named for late wife

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosJuly 18, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Our Analysis:The Pettiness of Power: Trump's Ban on Clapper and the Bomb-Sniffing Dog

U.S. President Donald Trump delivers remarks at the annual National Memorial Day Observance in the Memorial Amphitheater at Arlington National Cemetery in Arlington, Virginia, U.S., May 26, 2025. REUTERS/Ken Cedeno

In a move blasted by The Atlantic's Shane Harris as sheer "pettiness," President Donald Trump prohibited the former Obama administration spymaster from attending the graduation ceremony of a bomb-sniffing dog he sponsored and which was named after his late wife.

The dog, named Susan, was "an adorable yellow Lab [who] completed a CIA training program for 'detection K9s,'" and was "a natural at sniffing out concealed explosives," including suicide vests and car bombs. And she had been sponsored by James Clapper.

"Clapper, 84, was one of the longest-serving and most experienced intelligence officers in U.S. history, and was the Director of National Intelligence in the Obama administration," wrote Harris. "There was a certain professional symmetry in his pup ending up at the CIA, a place he both knew intimately and deeply admired. It was also bittersweet: Clapper named Susan after his late wife, a former National Security Agency employee who had been at his side during his five-decade career. Susan, a great animal lover, had volunteered at a local shelter and 'doted on the family dog, Augusta,' according to her 2023 obituary."

Clapper had been looking forward to the ceremony for the dog, but according to the report, "the day before the event, in late May, he received an email from the nonprofit dog-training group: Clapper’s name had been scratched from the guest list, per an executive order from the president of the United States."

The president has a special vendetta for Clapper and many other high-ranking intelligence officials in the Obama years, for their involvement in investigating Russia's interference on his behalf in the 2016 presidential election.

"In fact, Trump once compared Clapper to a canine, writing in a tweet that Clapper and another Obama-era official had begun to 'choke like dogs' during a Senate hearing about the Kremlin’s interference in the 2016 election, which Trump calls the 'Russia hoax,' a hoax he accuses Clapper and other members of the 'deep state' of concocting," the report continued. (It was not a hoax.)

Trump has also developed a reputation for disliking dogs, at one point earning mockery for refusing to touch a special forces dog that assisted operatives who took down the former leader of ISIS in 2019.

Our Analysis:

The Pettiness of Power: Trump's Ban on Clapper and the Bomb-Sniffing Dog

In what can only be described as a dramatic display of pettiness, President Donald Trump has once again showcased his penchant for personal vendettas over public interest. This time, his target was none other than James Clapper, a titan in the intelligence community with a career spanning five decades. The offense? Barring Clapper from attending the graduation ceremony of a bomb-sniffing dog he sponsored, named after his late wife. This dog, Susan, wasn't just any dog; she was a testament to the bond between human and animal, and a beacon of hope in the relentless fight against terrorism.

The Symbolism of Susan

Susan, the yellow Lab with an exceptional talent for detecting concealed explosives, represented more than just her abilities. She was a bridge between Clapper's illustrious career and his personal life, named in honor of his late wife, Susan Clapper. This connection added a layer of profound significance to her training completion at the CIA, an organization to which James Clapper dedicated much of his professional life. The barring of Clapper from her graduation ceremony was not just a snub; it was an affront to the values of recognition, respect, and basic human decency.

Trump's Vendetta: Beyond Reason

The decision to prevent Clapper from attending the ceremony underlines a disturbing trend in Trump's presidency: the weaponization of power against perceived enemies. This vendetta against Clapper—and, by extension, against other high-ranking officials from the Obama era—is rooted in their involvement in investigating Russia's interference in the 2016 presidential election. By calling these investigations a "hoax" and likening Clapper to a dog in derogatory tweets, Trump not only disrespects the integrity of the intelligence community but also undermines the very foundations of democratic accountability.

A Pattern of Disdain for Dogs

Trump's animosity isn't limited to humans; it bizarrely extends to dogs as well. His refusal to touch a special forces dog that played a crucial role in taking down the former leader of ISIS in 2019 is emblematic of his broader disdain. This incident with Clapper and Susan isn't an isolated case; it's part of a consistent pattern of behavior that reflects poorly on the character of an individual entrusted with the highest office in the land.

Conclusion: Petty Politics Over Principle

The barring of James Clapper from Susan's graduation ceremony is a microcosm of the Trump administration's approach to governance: driven by personal grievances, devoid of empathy, and dismissive of the contributions and sacrifices of public servants. This event, seemingly minor in the grand scheme of geopolitical events, is a glaring example of how petty politics can overshadow principles, respect, and decency. In the end, it's not just Clapper who's snubbed, nor Susan, nor even the intelligence community at large—it's the very notion of service and sacrifice that's disrespected.
