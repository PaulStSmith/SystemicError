'So easy to do': Trump berates 'stupid party' Republican lawmakers in early-morning tirade

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosNovember 10, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Donald Trump (Reuters)

President Donald Trump reignited his demands early Sunday morning for Republican senators to terminate the filibuster, warning GOP lawmakers that they would become “the stupid party” should they continue to defy him.

“Republicans Should Terminate the Filibuster (THE DEMS WILL DO IT THE FIRST CHANCE THEY GET!), End the Shutdown, Pass lots of Great ‘Things,’ and Win the Midterms,” Trump wrote on his social media platform Truth Social. “SO EASY TO DO - Be the Smart Party, Not the Stupid Party!”

Trump has ramped up calls in recent weeks for GOP senators to eliminate the filibuster, a Senate rule that allows a member to block a bill that receives less than 60 votes. His calls come amid the ongoing government shutdown, which reached its 40th day on Sunday and has become the single-longest shutdown in U.S. history.

The shutdown was sparked by Democrats’ refusal to back any spending bills that didn’t include an extension of Obamacare subsidies, which are set to expire this year. If allowed to expire, an estimated 4 million Americans would lose health insurance, and premiums for millions of Obamacare enrollees are projected to increase by 114%.

With there being 53 Republicans in the 100-member Senate, the party’s majority is too small to overcome the filibuster and adopt a spending bill to re-open the government.

Were the filibuster to be eliminated, however, Republicans’ simple majority would be enough to force through a spending bill without an extension to Obamacare subsidies, as well as a plethora of other agenda items from Trump, including legislation to ban “transgender for everybody,” as Trump has stated, and additional tax cuts, too.

Our Analysis:

Trump’s Filibuster Folly: A Desperate Call to Dismantle Democracy

In an early morning tirade that surprises no one, former President Donald Trump once again took to his social media platform, Truth Social, to peddle a familiar, yet dangerously flawed argument. Trump’s demand for the Republican senators to terminate the filibuster is not just a blatant attempt to bulldoze over the nuances of democratic governance; it’s a clarion call to his party to embrace autocracy over accountability. Let’s dissect the layers of irony, desperation, and outright misinformation wrapped up in Trump’s latest outburst.

The Irony of Calling for the ‘Smart Party’

Trump’s exhortation for the GOP to become “the Smart Party” by eliminating the filibuster is steeped in irony. The filibuster, a Senate rule requiring a 60-vote supermajority to pass most legislation, is a tool designed to encourage debate, compromise, and bipartisanship. To advocate for its removal is to champion a winner-takes-all approach to governance, undermining the very fabric of democratic debate. Trump’s assertion that Democrats would abolish the filibuster at the first opportunity is speculative at best and manipulative at worst, designed to stoke fear and rally his base around a false narrative.

The Misleading Crisis Narrative

Trump’s call comes amidst the longest government shutdown in U.S. history, a crisis he attributes to Democrats’ refusal to pass spending bills without an extension of Obamacare subsidies. This framing is deliberately misleading. The shutdown reflects a deeper political impasse, one where Trump’s own unyielding stance on healthcare and other contentious issues plays a central role. To blame the Democrats alone is to ignore the complexity of the situation and the role of Trump’s administration in exacerbating the deadlock.

The Real Stakes: Healthcare and Human Rights

At the heart of this debacle are the lives of millions of Americans. The potential expiration of Obamacare subsidies and the consequent loss of health insurance for an estimated 4 million Americans is not a mere political pawn; it’s a public health disaster in waiting. Trump’s cavalier attitude towards these stakes, coupled with his push for controversial policies like a ban on “transgender for everybody,” reveals a disturbing willingness to sacrifice human rights and well-being for political gain.

The Dangers of a Simple Majority

Trump’s advocacy for a simple majority to push through spending bills and other agenda items is a direct threat to the checks and balances central to American democracy. The filibuster, while frustrating at times, serves as a crucial barrier against the tyranny of the majority. Its elimination would not only pave the way for the enactment of divisive policies but would also set a dangerous precedent, potentially leading to volatile swings in policy with every shift in the majority.

Conclusion: The Folly of Short-Sighted Power Grabs

Trump’s latest demands are emblematic of his broader disregard for democratic principles. The call to eliminate the filibuster is not about making the Republican Party “smart”; it’s about making it omnipotent. It’s a short-sighted power grab that undermines the very essence of democratic governance. The GOP must resist the temptation to become “the stupid party” by falling for Trump’s dangerous, divisive tactics. Democracy thrives on debate, compromise, and the protection of minority rights. Any move away from these principles is a step towards autocracy, and that is the true folly here.
