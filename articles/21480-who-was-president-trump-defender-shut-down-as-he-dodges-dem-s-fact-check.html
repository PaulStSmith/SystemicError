'Who was president!?' Trump defender shut down as he dodges Dem's fact check

Based on an article by Travis Gettys

Paulo SantosAugust 16, 20262 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Trump Was in ChargeOversight as TheatreThe Real MisdirectionWhat Accountability Actually MeansThe Pattern

Rand Paul Is Not Chasing Truth. He Is Chasing a Cover Story

Trump Was in Charge

The argument on CNN was supposed to be about Anthony Fauci’s pandemic record and Rand Paul’s hearing. Instead, Antjuan Seawright cut through the performance with the only question that mattered: who was actually in charge when Covid hit? The answer is not subtle. Trump was president. Trump held the institutional power. Trump owned the failures.

That matters because this entire spectacle is built to obscure it.

Oversight as Theatre

Paul’s hearing is being sold as oversight, but the source makes plain what it really is: a partisan ritual designed to keep the blame moving away from the people who held power. Fauci becomes the convenient target because he is visible, technical, and easy to vilify. Trump, by contrast, is the political object that must be protected. So the story is not “accountability.” It is reputation management for the man at the top.

Bluey’s defense only deepens the deception. He talks like a prosecutor, but the case he is making depends on turning a public-health disaster into a morality play about one scientist, one diary, and one alleged lie. That is not a reckoning. It is a relocation of guilt.

The Real Misdirection

The most revealing move in the exchange is the attempt to shift the argument from Trump’s responsibility to Fauci’s alleged failure and then further outward to China. That is classic political displacement: when domestic leadership collapses, find a subordinate and then a foreign enemy. It is cleaner. It is easier. It keeps the top of the hierarchy untouched.

The problem is not that oversight exists. The problem is that Republicans are using oversight as a shield for a former president whose administration presided over the disaster they now want to reframe as someone else’s deception.

What Accountability Actually Means

If Congress wanted accountability, it would start with the person who had the power to set policy, shape messaging, and command the federal response. Instead, Paul is still building a career out of theatrical grievance against Fauci, as if a public-health official could somehow outrank the president who appointed, empowered, and benefited from the whole structure.

That is the tell. The hearing is not about learning what happened. It is about refusing to name the decision-maker.

The Pattern

This is how authoritarian politics protects itself: not only through denial, but through manufactured confusion about where authority lived when the damage was done. The strongman disappears behind a cloud of subordinate scapegoats, technical disputes, and endless hearings. The weaker target absorbs the outrage. The actual center of power gets sanitized into “legacy.”

The democratic answer is not to pretend the performance is serious. It is to name the hierarchy plainly: Trump was in charge, Republicans are trying to rewrite that fact, and Fauci is being used as a decoy to make that rewrite feel legitimate.
