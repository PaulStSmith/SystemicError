'Absolutely insane': Dem Crockett stunned by what GOP lawmakers do 'behind closed doors'

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosMay 4, 20255 min read

(Screengrab via CNN)

A progressive Texas Democrat raised some eyebrows late Friday on CNN, telling anchor Kaitlan Collins her Republican colleagues made "absolutely insane" moves behind closed doors this week.

Rep. Jasmine Crockett (D-TX) joined Collins on "The Source" late Friday to talk about the GOP's budget proposal.

"I just wonder what you make of how this is playing out among Republicans," Collins said, introducing her guest. "Because Speaker Mike Johnson endorsed this, he praised it. But we heard from several Republicans who said they didn't like it — Senator Roger Wicker said that the president's advisers were apparently not listening because of the Pentagon’s spending; Senator Mitch McConnell said it was 'material neglect.' Susan Collins called it late and expressed concern over those cuts as well. What do you think is going to happen here?"

Crockett bluntly replied she has "no idea" and said she feels lawmakers in both chambers aren't listening to their constituents, who she said are outspoken about their "pain."

She then made a notable statement about what's happening in those discussions.

"It's interesting — as we are going through reconciliation, we had a reconciliation markup in Oversight as well as Judiciary. So I had a very long night on Wednesday. And to listen to them — the things that they were not willing to say they were okay with cutting, or the things they were willing to cut — right? It's absolutely insane."

Democrats on the House Budget Committee are ensuring that every district is listed out with the number of people who will be impacted by any Medicaid cuts, as well as the number of people impacted by cuts to supplemental nutrition.

She also questioned why defense spending continues to rise despite failing the last seven audits, and as those most vulnerable are poised to get "hurt the most."

"It seems like they do whatever [Donald Trump] wants them to do at all times, right?" she later added. "Like, behind closed doors, they will talk about how these things are crazy. You can find plenty of Republicans that will tell you that these tariffs are a problem. They will tell you that. And they know that it's a problem."

Crockett asserted her Republican colleagues are actively avoiding town halls, knowing the MAGA agenda is deeply unpopular.

"This is exactly why, if you tune in and watch any of these hearings, when it comes down to marking up these bills where these cuts are supposed to be, you know who won't even defend them? The Republicans," she said.

Democrats listened for hours to amendments as they proceeded through markups. Every time, said Crockett, they called out specific Republicans and said, "'Congressman such and such, what do you think?'"

"They literally were silent. It was almost like they were told, 'Don't say anything because we can't win. We just have to vote down these common-sense amendments and get through this, because this is what we've been told to do,'" she added.

Our Analysis:

The GOP's Budget Circus: A Masterclass in Hypocrisy

In an era where the term political theater has perhaps been overused to the point of cliché, the recent antics of the GOP regarding their budget proposal serve as a stark reminder of why such metaphors persist. Rep. Jasmine Crockett (D-TX) provided a glimpse behind the curtain in a late-night interview on CNN, revealing a spectacle that can only be described as "absolutely insane."

The Illusion of Cohesion

At first glance, the GOP's stance on their budget proposal, endorsed by Speaker Mike Johnson and praised in public, might seem like a united front. However, scratch the surface, and you'll find a chasm of dissent within the ranks. Figures like Senator Roger Wicker, Senator Mitch McConnell, and Susan Collins have voiced concerns ranging from neglect of the Pentagon’s spending to the lateness and implications of the proposed cuts. This discord within the party is the first crack in the illusion of cohesion they wish to present.

Ignoring the Elephant in the Room

Crockett's candid admission of having "no idea" what will happen next is a glaring testament to the unpredictable nature of current political strategies. Yet, her observations during reconciliation markups in Oversight and Judiciary committees offer a clearer picture of the GOP's priorities—or lack thereof. The refusal to acknowledge necessary cuts, juxtaposed against the willingness to slash programs crucial to the most vulnerable, paints a damning picture of a party at war with itself and the constituents it purports to serve.

The audacity of the GOP to continue pumping funds into a defense budget that has failed seven consecutive audits, while simultaneously proposing cuts to Medicaid and supplemental nutrition, is not just a policy failure; it's a moral one. This relentless increase in defense spending, despite clear evidence of its inefficiency, is a stark reminder of where their allegiances lie—not with the vulnerable American public, but with the whims of Donald Trump and the defense industry lobbyists lining their pockets.

The Deafening Silence of Accountability

Crockett's insights into the Republicans' behavior during hearings and markups are particularly illuminating. The strategic silence of GOP members when confronted with amendments that challenge their agenda speaks volumes. It's a calculated move to avoid defending the indefensible, adhering strictly to a party line that even they seem to recognize as fundamentally flawed.

This silence is not just a tactic; it's a loud admission of guilt. By refusing to engage in meaningful debate or defend their positions, the GOP members are effectively acknowledging the weakness of their stance. It’s a cowardly abdication of their duty to their constituents and the democratic process.

Conclusion: The Grand Hypocrisy

The GOP's budget proposal saga is a microcosm of the broader issues plaguing American politics—a stark display of hypocrisy, a blatant disregard for the most vulnerable, and a desperate clinging to power at any cost. The refusal to face constituents, the reliance on Trump's unpopular MAGA agenda, and the cowardice in debates are symptoms of a party that has lost its way.

Rep. Jasmine Crockett’s revelations should serve as a wake-up call not just to her constituents, but to all Americans. The GOP's behind-the-scenes machinations reveal a party more concerned with power than with the welfare of the nation. As the budget proposal drama unfolds, one can only hope that the electorate remembers the lessons of this "absolutely insane" spectacle come the next election cycle.
