CNN host astonished by voters'  'slap in the face' to Trump in new polls

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosFebruary 5, 20265 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

U.S. President Donald Trump speaks with members of the media on board Air Force One en route to Palm Beach, Florida, U.S., January 31, 2026. REUTERS/Nathan Howard

CNN’s Sara Sidner was astonished by new polling that shows a deepening mistrust for President Donald Trump’s commitment to democracy.

The 79-year-old president proposed that Republicans nationalize and take over elections in at least 15 states, just days after the FBI and his spy chief Tulsi Gabbard raided a Georgia election facility seeking evidence of 2020 election fraud that has been repeatedly debunked, and data analyst Harry Enten told “CNN News Central” that voters don’t trust his intentions.

“They trust him about as much as they trust him to be modest,” Enten said. “I mean, just take a look at these numbers right here. I think this gives the game away. Trump committed to free and fair elections, this is among all adults. What you see is just 43 percent of Americans actually do believe that Donald Trump is committed to free and fair elections. The clear majority, 56 percent, say no, no, no, no. They do not believe that Donald Trump is committed to free and fair elections.”

“I will note it is the opposite for the Democratic Party, where you see the majority of Americans believe the Democratic Party is committed to free and fair elections,” he added, “but they do not trust Donald Trump when it comes to his commitment to free and fair elections.”

Trump has claimed for years that fraud cost him the 2020 election to Joe Biden, although numerous courts and independent investigations have found otherwise, and he was indicted in federal court and in Georgia for his efforts to overturn that loss and remain in power.

“So you see this majority right here who say that Donald Trump is not committed to free and fair elections, and among independents it is even worse,” Enten said. “Take a look here: This is a group that Donald Trump has really been struggling with, and when it comes to his commitment to free and fair elections, they absolutely believe he is not. We’re talking about two in three independents say no, Donald Trump is not committed to free and fair elections. We’re talking about just a third, just a third of independents believe that Donald Trump is committed to free and fair elections.”

“They will view his current comments very negatively, and this, of course, just builds upon their continuing distrust and their deepening distrust of Donald Trump when it comes to his job for the president overall, and specifically to this question when it comes to his commitment to free and fair elections,” Enten added.

Sidner was stunned by the poll’s findings.

“If that number doesn’t slap you in the face, I don’t know what will,” she said.

Our Analysis:

The Paradox of Trust in Trump’s Commitment to Democracy

In a recent CNN report, Sara Sidner expressed her astonishment at new polling data indicating a significant erosion of trust in President Donald Trump’s dedication to upholding the principles of democracy. This revelation comes on the heels of Trump’s controversial proposition for the Republican Party to nationalize and take control of elections in at least 15 states—a move that reeks of authoritarianism and directly undermines the foundational tenets of electoral sovereignty.

The Irony of Trust: CNN’s Harry Enten provided a stark analysis of the public’s perception of Trump’s commitment to democracy, juxtaposing it with a humorous yet poignant comparison to Trump’s well-documented lack of modesty. A mere 43% of Americans believe in Trump’s commitment to free and fair elections, a figure that is not just alarming but a clarion call to the fragility of our democratic institutions under his influence. The majority, 56%, unequivocally doubt his dedication to the integrity of the electoral process. This disparity not only highlights a crisis of confidence but underscores a broader societal skepticism towards Trump’s democratic fidelity.

Democratic Contrast: Enten’s insights further reveal a stark contrast between the public’s trust in the Democratic Party versus Trump, with the former enjoying a majority’s belief in their commitment to democracy. This dichotomy is not merely a reflection of partisan divides but a sobering indictment of Trump’s singular ability to sow discord and mistrust in the electoral process.

The Independent Perspective: Perhaps most telling is the sentiment among independent voters, a demographic critical to the sway of national elections. Two-thirds of these voters distrust Trump’s commitment to democracy, a statistic that speaks volumes about the erosion of his broader appeal and the skepticism that now shadows his political maneuvers. Only a third of independents believe in his commitment to free and fair elections, a figure that should alarm any political strategist aiming for electoral success.

The Ongoing Saga of Election Fraud Claims: Trump’s incessant and baseless claims of election fraud in the 2020 elections have not only been debunked by numerous courts and independent investigations but have also led to his indictment in federal court and in Georgia. These continued allegations, devoid of evidence, not only undermine public trust but also endanger the very fabric of American democracy by promoting a narrative of illegitimacy without foundation.

Sidner’s Rightful Astonishment: Sidner’s reaction to the polling data is a sentiment shared by any observer with a modicum of concern for the health of our democratic institutions. The erosion of trust in Trump’s commitment to democracy is not just a slap in the face but a wake-up call to the dangers of complacency in the face of authoritarian impulses.

In conclusion, the polling data presented by CNN is a stark reminder of the precarious state of democracy in the age of Trump. It’s not just about partisan politics but the very essence of what it means to be a democratic society. The deepening mistrust in Trump’s commitment to democracy is a symptom of a larger issue—the need for a robust and unwavering defense of electoral integrity and democratic principles. The road ahead requires vigilance, accountability, and an unwavering commitment to safeguarding the bedrock of our democratic ideals.
