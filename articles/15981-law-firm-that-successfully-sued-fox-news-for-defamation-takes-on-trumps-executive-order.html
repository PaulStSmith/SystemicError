Law firm that successfully sued Fox News for defamation takes on Trump’s executive order

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosApril 18, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Our Analysis:A Stark Reminder of the Dangers of Power Misuse

U.S. President Donald Trump smiles, on the day he signs energy-related executive orders, at the White House in Washington, D.C., U.S., April 8, 2025. REUTERS/Nathan Howard

Susman Godfrey, the firm that won a $787 million defamation lawsuit against Fox News for spreading lies about Dominion Voting Systems, is now suing the administration over President Donald Trump’s executive order “penalizing firms that employed his enemies or engaged in work he opposes,” the Daily Beast reports.

“No administration should be allowed to punish lawyers for simply doing their jobs, protecting Americans and their constitutional right to the legal process,” the firm wrote in a statement about the suit.

As the Beast reports, “On Wednesday, Trump signed an executive order barring Susman Godfrey from federal contracts held by the firm’s clients, removed its employees’ security clearances, and banned them from accessing federal buildings.”

According to the suit, Trump has made “no secret of its unconstitutional retaliatory and discriminatory intent to punish Susman Godfrey for its work defending the integrity of the 2020 presidential election.”

“But this goes far beyond law firms and lawyers,” Susman Godfrey said in a statement, “Today it is our firm under attack, but tomorrow it could be any of us. As officers of the court, we are duty-bound to take on this fight against the illegal executive order.”

According to the Beast, “Four firms—including the two largest firms in the country, Kirkland & Ellis and Latham & Watkins—cut deals on Friday to avoid falling victim to one of Trump’s executive orders. They agreed to provide a total of at least $500 million in pro bono work for the current administration.”

“Susman Godfrey joined the handful of firms—including Perkins Coie, Jenner & Block, and WilmerHale—that have taken action against the president, calling the president’s spree of executive orders ‘so obviously unconstitutional,’” the Beast reports.

Our Analysis:

A Stark Reminder of the Dangers of Power Misuse

In an era where the line between legal boundaries and personal vendettas seems increasingly blurred, the recent actions of President Donald Trump have yet again sparked a fierce debate on the integrity of democratic institutions and the sanctity of the legal profession. According to the Daily Beast, Susman Godfrey, a firm renowned for its victory in a $787 million defamation lawsuit against Fox News, is taking a bold stand against what appears to be a vindictive maneuver by the Trump administration. This move is not just a lawsuit; it's a clarion call for the defense of the foundational principles of American democracy.

The Executive Orders: A Dangerous Precedent

The heart of the controversy lies in the executive order signed by Trump, which essentially penalizes firms that have opposed him or his policies by barring them from federal contracts, revoking their employees' security clearances, and banning them from accessing federal buildings. This act is not merely an administrative decision; it's an unabashed attack on the legal profession and a blatant attempt to silence critics through governmental power.

Susman Godfrey's Stand: A Defense of Constitutional Rights

Susman Godfrey's response to this overreach is nothing short of heroic. Their lawsuit against the administration is a testament to their commitment to protecting the constitutional right to due process and the legal profession's integrity. Their statement, emphasizing that no administration should be allowed to punish lawyers for simply doing their job, underscores the gravity of the situation. It's a reminder that the battle here transcends individual firms or lawyers—it's about safeguarding the very pillars of democracy.

The Broader Implications: Beyond Just Law Firms

What's particularly alarming about this scenario is the broader implications it holds for the legal profession and, by extension, for anyone who dares to stand up against the administration's unconstitutional endeavors. Susman Godfrey rightly points out that today it's their firm under attack, but tomorrow it could be any entity or individual who finds themselves in the crosshairs of an administration willing to use its power to crush dissent.

The Unconstitutional Spree: An Obvious Overreach

The Daily Beast's report highlights that Susman Godfrey is not alone in its fight. Other prominent firms have either been coerced into compliance through deals, as seen with Kirkland & Ellis and Latham & Watkins, or have joined the fray against the administration's unconstitutional actions. This collective opposition from some of the country's largest law firms sends a powerful message about the egregiousness of the executive orders and the urgent need to resist such overreach.

Conclusion: The Fight For Democracy

The lawsuit by Susman Godfrey is a critical moment in the ongoing struggle to preserve the rule of law and democratic norms. It's a fight not just against a series of unconstitutional executive orders but against the very idea that an administration can wield its power to punish its adversaries with impunity. This scenario should serve as a rallying cry for all those who value democracy, reminding us of the importance of standing up against tyranny in all its forms. The legal profession, with its unique position as a guardian of justice, has shown remarkable courage. It's incumbent upon us all to support this fight, for today's defenders of democracy could be tomorrow's targets of despotism.
