The Art of the Vanish: Trump’s Library and the Missing DMs

Who Actually Holds Power Here?

The saga of the missing direct messages (DMs) from Donald Trump’s Twitter account during his presidency is not just about a few lost pieces of digital communication. It is a glaring example of how power is wielded to obscure and potentially obstruct justice. The Trump Presidential Library, controlled by Trump loyalists and backed by the Trump administration, claims ignorance of any DMs, despite court-documented evidence to the contrary. This isn’t just a clerical error; it’s a deliberate decision to defy the Presidential Records Act and maintain control over potentially incriminating records.

Decisions and Accountability

The decision to not preserve Trump’s DMs was made by the Trump administration itself, specifically when it chose to disable the capture of direct messages through ArchiveSocial software. This choice, confirmed by then-Archivist David Ferriero, was a calculated move to keep these communications out of the public record and beyond the reach of accountability mechanisms. The current administration’s attack on the Presidential Records Act, calling it unconstitutional, further reveals a strategic maneuver to weaken oversight and accountability.

Misdirection and Blame-Shifting

The Trump Library’s claim of being unable to locate any DMs is a classic example of misdirection. By pleading ignorance, the library shifts focus away from the administration’s active role in ensuring these communications were never archived. This tactic not only challenges the public’s right to know but also mocks the legal framework established to preserve the integrity of presidential records.

Pattern of Obstruction

This incident is part of a broader pattern of behavior by the Trump administration: a consistent effort to obstruct and undermine the systems designed to hold power accountable. Whether it’s firing inspectors general, flouting subpoenas, or attacking the judiciary, the playbook remains the same—erode the mechanisms of accountability to consolidate power.

The Broader Implication

The missing DMs saga is not an isolated incident but a symptomatic manifestation of a deeper political decay. It’s about how those in power believe they can manipulate the truth and the law without consequence. The ongoing legal battles and the pushback from entities like The Washington Post are crucial, but they also highlight a troubling reliance on the judiciary to manage what are essentially failures of political integrity and leadership.

A System Under Stress

The Trump administration’s handling of presidential records is a stress test for American democracy’s checks and balances. It underscores the need for robust mechanisms that are not merely dependent on the good faith of those in office. As this case progresses, it will either reinforce the rule of law or showcase its fragility in the face of power that refuses to be held accountable. The outcome will set precedents for future administrations, for better or worse.
