Trump loyalists 'declare war on the Catholic church'

Donald Trump outside St. John’s Episcopal Church in Washington, D.C. on June 1, 2020 (Official White House Photo by Shealah Craighead/Flickr)

Despite Pope Leo XIV to calling on his Catholic leadership to issue a forceful statement condemning President Donald Trump’s “villification of immigrants,” Trump loyalists, writes John Kenneth White in The Hill, have responded by declaring war on the Catholic church.

By a nearly unanimous vote, the United State Conference of Catholic Bishops issued their first special message in 12 years, saying they were “saddened by the state of contemporary debate and the vilification of immigrants,” and “concerned about the conditions in detention centers and the lack of access to pastoral care.”

They added that “we are grieved when we meet parents who fear being detained when taking their children to school and when we try to console family members who have already been separated from their loved ones.”

The bishops then put out a video denouncing the “dehumanizing rhetoric and violence” against those confronted by ICE — over 1.4 million have watched it so far, according to White.

But as clergy members continue to denounce the Trump administration’s policies, MAGA has doubled down against the church.

“Boarder czar” Tom Homan condemned the bishops’ letter and the church as “wrong,” adding “I’m saying it as not only border czar, I’ll say it as a Catholic. I think they need to spend time fixing the Catholic Church, in my opinion.”

White notes that Rep. Tim Burchett (R-TN) “accused the Catholic Church of using government grants to profit from services rendered to refugees. Gateway Pundit Jim Hoft charged that the bishops squandered more than $2.3 billion dollars received from the government, and praised Trump for terminating them.”

Laura Loomer, Trump loyalist and so-called MAGA whisperer who called Pope Leo a “woke Marxist Pope,” posted on X, “Are all of the Jew haters going to be calling out the Catholic bishops and the Marxist American Pope for condemning deportations?”

Matt Walsh, another Trump defender, White explains, “attacked the bishops, saying they didn’t make a video criticizing the Biden administration ‘for supporting, funding, and facilitating the mass slaughter of children in the womb,’ or ‘its support for the castration and sexual mutilation of children.’”

White says that these attacks are the antithesis of the church’s teachings.

“Those attacking the bishops and making reference to the sexual abuse scandals that have plagued the Catholic Church over the past decades does not diminish the bishops’ call for humane treatment of immigrants and adherence to the Gospel teachings of Jesus Christ,” he writes.

“Trump casts himself as pro-Catholic and calls himself ‘the most pro-life president ever.’ But that does not mean that the maltreatment of those living outside the womb is no less a sin,” he adds.

Actual Catholics, White says, are not happy with Trump.

“Catholics are swing voters and often determine election outcomes. Joe Biden won their votes in 2020; Donald Trump had a 12-point advantage in 2024. Today, a majority of Catholics disapprove of Trump,” he writes.

“The Catholic Church is more than 2,000 years old. Declaring war on it is hardly civilized or politically smart. Trump has three years left in office. The Catholic Church will survive condemnation by those in power; it’s hardly the first time this has occurred in its long and storied history,” he concludes.

Our Analysis:

The Unholy War: MAGA vs. The Catholic Church

In what reads like a script from an alternate reality where moral compasses spin wildly out of control, we find ourselves observing a bizarre battleground: the Trump loyalists versus the Catholic Church. John Kenneth White’s article in The Hill paints a vivid picture of this clash, sparked by Pope Leo XIV’s call for a condemnation of President Donald Trump’s treatment of immigrants. Let’s dissect the layers of irony, hypocrisy, and outright absurdity that lace this narrative.

The Bishops’ Bold Stand

Firstly, the United States Conference of Catholic Bishops’ unanimous decision to condemn the “vilification of immigrants” is nothing short of historic. It’s a rare moment of unity in a world where divisive rhetoric often drowns out calls for compassion. Their statement and the subsequent video denouncing “dehumanizing rhetoric and violence” against immigrants have clearly struck a nerve, evidenced by the 1.4 million views it garnered. Such a stance, deeply rooted in the Gospel’s teachings of love and compassion, should be commended, not condemned. Yet, commendation is far from what ensued.

The MAGA Machine’s Misguided Missiles

Enter the MAGA loyalists, armed with a barrage of accusations so misaligned with the core Christian values they purport to uphold that one can’t help but question if they’ve ever cracked open a New Testament. Tom Homan, dubbed the “Border czar,” accuses the Church of needing “fixing” rather than focusing on the plight of immigrants. This coming from a member of a faith founded on the principles of helping the “least of these” (Matthew 25:40) is not just ironic; it’s downright blasphemous.

Representative Tim Burchett and Gateway Pundit Jim Hoft’s accusations of financial misconduct by the Church in handling government grants for refugee services reek of deflection. Considering the Trump administration’s own plethora of financial inquiries, this attack is a clear case of the pot calling the kettle black.

The Woke Marxist Pope and Other Fantasies

Laura Loomer’s labeling of Pope Leo as a “woke Marxist Pope” and her insinuation of anti-Semitic sentiments within the Church is as laughable as it is lamentable. It’s a classic example of throwing everything at the wall in hopes something sticks, no matter how unfounded. Matt Walsh’s critique, meanwhile, neatly sidesteps the issue at hand—immigrant welfare—by dredging up the abortion debate, a desperate attempt to divert attention from the Church’s commendable stand on humanitarian grounds.

Trump’s Pro-Catholic Posturing vs. Reality

White’s article expertly highlights the contradiction between Trump’s self-proclaimed pro-Catholic stance and his actions that directly oppose the Church’s teachings on compassion and care for the marginalized. Trump’s claim of being “the most pro-life president ever” falls flat when his policies and rhetoric towards immigrants reflect a glaring disregard for life outside the womb.

The Political Suicide of Waging War on the Catholic Church

History is littered with the failures of those who’ve attempted to go toe-to-toe with the Catholic Church. Trump and his MAGA cohort’s decision to do so is not just uncivilized; it’s politically inept. With Catholics being pivotal swing voters, as White points out, alienating this demographic is a strategic blunder of epic proportions. The Church’s resilience through millennia of challenges is a testament to its enduring presence, one that will likely outlast the fleeting power of any political figure or movement.

Conclusion: The Gospel of MAGA vs. The Gospel of Christ

This entire debacle boils down to a fundamental misalignment between MAGA’s gospel of exclusion and the Gospel of Christ’s inclusion. The Catholic Church, in aligning itself with the latter, has chosen the side of history that values compassion over condemnation, love over loathing. For MAGA loyalists to decry this stance is not only to wage a losing war but to stand on the wrong side of morality, justice, and, indeed, Christianity itself.

In the end, White’s article doesn’t just expose a political skirmish; it reveals a profound crisis of conscience at the heart of American conservatism. One can only hope that reason, compassion, and true Christian values will eventually prevail.
