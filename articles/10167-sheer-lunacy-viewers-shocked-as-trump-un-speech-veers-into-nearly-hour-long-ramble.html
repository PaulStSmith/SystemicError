'Sheer lunacy': Viewers shocked as Trump UN speech veers into nearly hour-long ramble

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosSeptember 24, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Our Analysis:Trump’s U.N. Speech: A Theater of the AbsurdIn Conclusion: The Legacy of a Speech

U.S. President Donald Trump gestures during the 80th United Nations General Assembly at U.N. headquarters in New York City, U.S., September 23, 2025. REUTERS/Shannon STAPLETON

Donald Trump’s nearly 55-minute address to the United Nations General Assembly both appalled and raised fears about his sanity from some political observers, with notable former MSNBC host Joy Reid cutting to the chase and writing on Bluesky: “Are y’all listening to this Trump speech? This dude is out of his f—king mind.”

The address, which was slated to last only 15 minutes, was marred by a teleprompter that didn’t work initially, which led the president to bitterly complain about the inconvenience.

While speaking to the silent audience, the president lamented that he hasn’t gotten enough appreciation for his efforts, complained that allies aren’t backing him, asked why the UN even exists, and touted personal achievements in the U.S. that would be of little interest to foreign diplomats.

Former State Department spokesperson Ned Price offered, “Let’s not pretend this is a foreign policy speech or dignify it by calling it one. This is basically MAGA madlibs. Trump is speaking to his political base, hitting each of his campaign trail hits, while addressing a room of leaders who’d rather be just about anywhere else.”

“This is what giving Trump the honor of a second UK state visit achieved. Eight days later, Trump spews outright racist lies about our capital city on the world stage,” British journalist Adam Schwartz wrote.

Pointing to Trump claiming, “I’m really good at this stuff. Your countries are going to hell,” journalist Anne Applebaum drily observed, “People came from all over the world. They have serious problems to solve.”

Popular “Tom Tomorrow“ cartoonist Dan Perkins admitted, “God this is just humiliating to watch,” later adding, “really something about seeing him ramble incoherently at UNGA that drives home the sheer lunacy to which we have grown accustomed.”

“Watching Trump give this UN speech, the world thinks America has lost its mind. I do too,” columnist David Rothkopf wrote.

Former MSNBC host Joy Reid fired off a series of posts, writing, “I’m so embarrassed to be an American right now. Donald Trump is a senile old codger, standing before the United Nations, reading a speech clearly written by an alt right lunatic and a bunch of oil and gas lobbyists. Well done, U.S. voters. You really f—ked this one up,” adding, “This is the most pathetic human to ever taint the White House with his presence.”

Our Analysis:

Trump’s U.N. Speech: A Theater of the Absurd

Donald Trump’s address at the United Nations General Assembly was not merely an exercise in diplomatic discourse; it was a spectacle that veered into the realm of the absurd. Clocking in at nearly 55 minutes—well over its intended 15-minute duration—the speech unfolded like a tragicomedy, noteworthy not for its insights into global policy but for its bewildering departure from coherence and decorum.

The Spectacle of Dysfunction

The malfunctioning teleprompter was but a prelude to the chaos that ensued. Trump’s response? To bitterly complain about the inconvenience. This petulance set the tone for a speech that can only be described as a soliloquy of grievances. Lamenting the lack of appreciation for his efforts and bemoaning the supposed lack of support from allies, Trump appeared less the leader of the free world and more a jilted prom king.

A Performance for an Audience of One

Former State Department spokesperson Ned Price’s critique hits the nail squarely on the head: Trump’s speech was less about foreign policy and more akin to “MAGA madlibs.” This was not an address designed for the diverse assembly of global leaders; it was a performance aimed at pleasing his political base. The dissonance between the setting and the content of Trump’s speech could not have been more stark.

The World’s Reaction: Between Dismay and Disbelief

The international community’s response ranged from appalled disbelief to sheer embarrassment. British journalist Adam Schwartz’s commentary underscores the global dismay at Trump’s “outright racist lies” about London, while Anne Applebaum’s observation—”People came from all over the world. They have serious problems to solve.”—serves as a poignant reminder of the missed opportunity for meaningful dialogue.

The sentiment of humiliation was echoed by many, with cartoonist Dan Perkins (Tom Tomorrow) and columnist David Rothkopf articulating a collective sense of unease. The world, it seems, was left to reckon with the spectacle of a leader unmoored from the realities and responsibilities of his position.

Joy Reid: The Voice of the Disenchanted

Joy Reid’s scathing criticism encapsulates the frustration and embarrassment of many Americans. Describing Trump as a “senile old codger” and condemning the electorate for bringing this upon themselves, Reid’s words are a raw expression of disillusionment. Her characterization of Trump’s speech as the product of an “alt right lunatic and a bunch of oil and gas lobbyists” is a biting indictment of the administration’s priorities and influences.

In Conclusion: The Legacy of a Speech

Donald Trump’s U.N. address will be remembered not for its diplomatic achievements or its insights into global challenges. Instead, it will be noted as an emblem of the deep divisions within the United States and its increasingly fraught relationship with the rest of the world. The speech was a missed opportunity for leadership, a bewildering display of egotism, and a stark reminder of the systemic errors that plague our current political moment.
