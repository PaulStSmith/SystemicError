Kennedy Center board member was forcefully muted for name change vote: 'Not unanimous!'

By Paulo Santos

Joyce Beatty/X/screen grab

Rep. Joyce Beatty (D-OH), a member of the Kennedy Center board, insisted that the vote to rename the venue after President Donald Trump was “not unanimous” despite claims to the contrary.

In a statement on Thursday, Karoline Leavitt, the White House press secretary, announced that the board “just voted unanimously to rename the Kennedy Center to the Trump-Kennedy Center, because of the unbelievable work President Trump has done over the last year in saving the building.”

Beatty responded with a video walloping Leavitt’s claim.

“I’m sitting here in my congressional office in Washington, D.C., just ending a call with the Kennedy Center, where I serve as an ex officio member,” she said. “I am bringing this to you live today because what you may hear is that there was a unanimous vote to rename the Kennedy Center, the Trump Center.”

“I was on that call and as I tried to push my button to voice my concern, to ask questions, and certainly not to vote in support of this,” she continued. “I was muted. Each time I tried to speak.”

Beatty noted that “the Congress has a say in this.”

“This center, the Kennedy Center, was created by the Congress,” she said. “I think it’s important for us to know that this is just another attempt to evade the law and not let the people have a say.”

Our Analysis:

The Trump-Kennedy Center Debacle: Unmasking the Farce

In a move that can only be described as a brazen assault on democratic norms and a glaring example of the hubris that characterizes certain political maneuvers, the recent announcement concerning the renaming of the Kennedy Center has sparked outrage and disbelief. Rep. Joyce Beatty (D-OH), a figure of integrity and a board member, has come forward to challenge the preposterous claims made by the White House press secretary, Karoline Leavitt. This situation not only showcases the audacity of falsehood but also highlights a dangerous precedent in the manipulation of information.

The Illusion of Unanimity

Leavitt’s statement, asserting a “unanimous vote” to rename the prestigious venue as the Trump-Kennedy Center, is a masterclass in misinformation. The audacity to claim unanimous support for a decision so controversial is nothing short of astonishing. It’s a bold move, suggesting that the achievements of President Trump warrant such an honor, especially given the divisive nature of his tenure. The fact that this comes from the White House press secretary adds a layer of official sanction to the deception, making it all the more egregious.

Silencing Dissent

Rep. Beatty’s experience during the call throws the supposed unanimity into sharp relief. Her account of being muted—effectively silenced—whenever she attempted to voice her concerns or opposition, paints a disturbing picture of how decisions are being made. It’s a stark reminder of the lengths to which some will go to ensure their narratives remain unchallenged, even at the cost of transparency and democratic process. This isn’t just about a disagreement on naming rights; it’s a glaring example of the suppression of dissenting voices.

The Role of Congress

Beatty’s insistence on the role of Congress in the governance of the Kennedy Center is a critical point. The center, created by Congressional action, is not merely a cultural landmark but a symbol of national pride and artistic achievement. Any changes to its designation should involve a democratic process, reflecting the will of the people and not just the whims of a few. Beatty’s reminder that “the Congress has a say in this” is a powerful rebuke to those attempting to bypass legal and ethical boundaries for partisan or personal gain.

Conclusion: The Need for Accountability

This episode is symptomatic of a larger issue plaguing our political landscape: the erosion of democratic norms and the blatant disregard for truth. Rep. Beatty’s courage in speaking out provides a beacon of hope, but it also underscores the need for vigilance and accountability. The attempt to rename the Kennedy Center is not just an isolated incident; it’s a reflection of a broader attempt to rewrite history and silence opposition.

In this era of post-truth politics, it’s imperative that we stand firm in our demand for transparency, truth, and respect for democratic processes. The audacious move to rename the Kennedy Center in this manner should not just be seen as a misstep or a political maneuver, but a warning sign of the lengths some are willing to go to consolidate power and control the narrative.

The truth must prevail, and democracy must be defended — one fact, one voice, at a time.
