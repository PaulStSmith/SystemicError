Kash Patel accused of 'clear abuse' as elite FBI swat team assigned to protect girlfriend

FBI Director Kash Patel arrives for a ceremony marking the 24th anniversary of the September 11, 2001 attacks on the World Trade Center at the 9/11 Memorial and Museum in the Manhattan borough of New York City September 11, 2025. (REUTERS/Kylie Cooper)

FBI Director Kash Patel’s country music singer girlfriend has a security detail made up of elite federal agents, according to sources with knowledge of the situation.

Performer Alexis Wilkins is being protected by FBI agents usually assigned to a SWAT team in the bureau’s field office in Nashville, where she spends time for her work, and people familiar with security protocols cannot recall any instance where the girlfriends of top FBI officials enjoyed government-staffed security details, reported MS NOW.

“There is no legitimate justification for this,” said Christopher O’Leary, a former senior FBI agent and MS NOW contributor. “This is a clear abuse of position and misuse of government resources. She is not his spouse, does not live in the same house or even the same city.”

SWAT team agents assigned to protect Wilkins would presumably be unable to respond or delayed in responding to crises, such as mass shootings or terror attacks, the sources said.

The 26-year-old Wilkins has been dating Patel since 2023 and was at his side when he was sworn in as FBI director earlier this year.

Patel has faced recent criticism for using a $60 million FBI jet last month to watch Wilkins sing the national anthem at a wrestling event in Pennsylvania, despite criticizing his predecessor, Christopher Wray, two years ago for using the plane for personal travel, and he issued a lengthy response to his critics.

“I’ve always said — criticize me all you want,” Patel posted on social media. “But going after the people doing great work, my personal life, or those around me is a total disgrace. The disgustingly baseless attacks against Alexis — a true patriot and the woman I’m proud to call my partner in life — are beyond pathetic.

“She is a rock-solid conservative and a country music sensation who has done more for this nation than most will in ten lifetimes. I’m so blessed she’s in my life. Attacking her isn’t just wrong — it’s cowardly and jeopardizes our safety. My love of family will always be my cornerstone, and you will never tear that down or keep me from them.”

The criticism leveled at Patel’s use of the FBI jet did not include personal attacks on his girlfriend.

Our Analysis:

The Absurdity of Misused Power: A Critique of FBI Director Kash Patel’s Choices

In a recent revelation that sounds more like a plot from a poorly written political drama than reality, FBI Director Kash Patel has been found to allocate federal resources in a manner that raises eyebrows, to say the least. His girlfriend, country music singer Alexis Wilkins, is reportedly under the protection of an elite federal agent security detail typically assigned to SWAT teams. The situation, as detailed by sources in a report by MS NOW, not only breaches the expected norms of governmental resource allocation but also borders on a flagrant misuse of power.

Misuse of Government Resources

It’s essential to underline the unprecedented nature of this arrangement. Previous instances where the girlfriends of top FBI officials received government-staffed security details are non-existent, according to the same report. This isn’t merely a deviation from the norm; it’s a leap into uncharted territory. Former senior FBI agent and MS NOW contributor, Christopher O’Leary, aptly points out, “There is no legitimate justification for this.” The fact that Wilkins does not share a residence, or even a city, with Patel only adds to the perplexity of the situation.

The implications of having SWAT team agents assigned to protect an individual with no official government role cannot be overstated. These agents, who should be readily available to respond to crises such as mass shootings or terror attacks, are instead tied up in what many would see as a personal errand. This misallocation of critical resources not only undermines the operational effectiveness of the FBI but also potentially places public safety at risk.

The Irony of Patel’s Defense

Patel’s defense of his actions, particularly his use of a $60 million FBI jet to attend a wrestling event where Wilkins was performing, is as bewildering as the actions themselves. His statement on social media, which attempts to frame criticism as an attack on his personal life and those around him, misses the mark entirely. The criticism isn’t a baseless attack on his partner’s character but a legitimate concern over his decision-making and prioritization as the FBI Director.

His attempt to shield his actions behind a veil of patriotism and familial love, claiming that attacks on Wilkins are “cowardly and jeopardize our safety,” is a textbook example of deflection. Let’s be clear: the criticism leveled at Patel is not about his personal life per se but about his professional judgment and the misuse of government resources.

Conclusion: A Disturbing Precedent

This situation sets a disturbing precedent for the misuse of power and resources at the highest levels of law enforcement. It’s not merely a matter of optics; it’s a question of ethics, priorities, and the responsible stewardship of taxpayer-funded resources. The role of the FBI Director is one that should exemplify the highest standards of integrity and accountability. Unfortunately, Patel’s recent actions have cast a shadow over this esteemed office, prompting a reevaluation of how power and resources are wielded in the hands of those at the top.

In a world fraught with real and present dangers, the focus of our federal law enforcement’s resources should be on protecting the public and upholding the law, not on providing personal security details for the girlfriends of high-ranking officials. The misuse of power and resources in this manner is not just disappointing; it’s deeply concerning.
