'Clone': Trump shares conspiracy theory about Joe Biden being 'executed in 2020'

Donald Trump Saturday evening reposted an online message from someone claiming that former President Joe Biden was "executed in 2020" and replaced by a clone.

The president took to his own social media site, Truth Social, over the weekend, first to withdraw his own nominee to head NASA and then to repost the anonymous comment.

Trump reposted a statement by a user identified as @llijh, with a bio that reads, "Christian JesusChrist is Lord & Savior WETHEPEOPLE."

"There is no #JoeBiden - executed in 2020," the post states. "#Biden clones doubles & robotic engineered soulless mindless entities are what you see."

The user then added, "#Democrats dont know the difference."

Under those words, the user had shared a satirical article from The Babylon Bee, titled, "Biden Family Worried They May Be Running Out Of Time To Exploit Joe Biden's Health." It's not clear if Trump was sharing for the conspiracy theory or the satire article, or both.

Our Analysis:

Unpacking the Unhinged: Trump's Latest Social Media Escapade

In a move that should surprise exactly no one yet still manages to drop jaws, Donald Trump has once again catapulted into the spotlight not for a policy proposal or even a coherent political argument, but for amplifying an online theory so bizarre it borders on the absurd. This time, Trump took to his platform, Truth Social, to share a message claiming that former President Joe Biden was "executed in 2020" and replaced by a clone. Yes, you read that correctly—a clone.

The original post, crafted by a user with the handle @llijh, whose bio proudly proclaims allegiance to Christian fundamentalism and the nebulous concept of "WETHEPEOPLE," asserts not only the execution and cloning of Biden but also throws in doubles and "robotic engineered soulless mindless entities" for good measure. The claim is as outlandish as it is unhinged, yet it found its way onto the feed of a former President of the United States.

The Context of Madness

Trump's endorsement, whether aimed at the conspiracy or the satirical article from The Babylon Bee shared by the user, signals a disturbing trend in political discourse. It's not merely the act of sharing a ludicrous theory that's alarming; it's the seemingly calculated effort to blur the lines between satire, conspiracy, and reality—a tactic Trump has mastered and used to his advantage numerous times.

The article in question, "Biden Family Worried They May Be Running Out Of Time To Exploit Joe Biden's Health," from The Babylon Bee, is clearly satirical. Yet, in the context of Trump's repost, it becomes a part of a larger, more sinister narrative—a narrative that seeks to delegitimize political opponents not through debate or policy critique, but through absurdity and confusion.

The Broader Implications

This incident is more than just a former president sharing a conspiracy theory; it's a reflection of a political landscape where truth and fiction are increasingly indistinguishable. Trump's actions underscore a dangerous precedent where leaders can, without consequence, propagate falsehoods to undermine or ridicule opponents. It's a tactic that not only erodes the quality of public discourse but also deeply polarizes communities, making meaningful dialogue nearly impossible.

The role of social media platforms in this debacle cannot be overstated. Truth Social, Trump's own creation, ostensibly provides a haven for "free speech." Yet, it seems to be morphing into a breeding ground for conspiracies and unfounded claims, raising questions about the responsibility of platforms in curbing misinformation.

In Conclusion

Trump's latest social media stunt is emblematic of a broader, more disturbing trend in politics where the absurd is becoming increasingly normalized. It's not just about the ridiculous claim of Biden's execution and cloning; it's about what this represents—a willingness to sacrifice truth at the altar of political gain. As observers, participants, and victims of this political environment, we must remain vigilant, critical, and, above all, committed to distinguishing fact from fiction.

In a world where former leaders indulge in the propagation of baseless theories, the task of holding onto reality becomes all the more crucial. Let's not allow the absurd to become our accepted norm.
