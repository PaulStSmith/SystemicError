Fox News pushed Trump into a doom loop he helped create

Based on an article by Sabrina Haake

Paulo SantosJune 22, 20262 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

War by Ratings, Diplomacy by Afterthought

The Real Power Center

The source story is about Trump’s latest “outline of understanding” with Iran, but the real institutional story is simpler: the White House holds the authority, and Fox News helped shape how that authority was used. Trump started the war, announced his 39th “beginning concept” of an endgame, and now presents a shaky 60-day framework as if it were strategy. That is not statecraft. It is a president improvising after damage he already set in motion.

Who Enabled the Outcome

Fox News did more than cheerlead. According to the source, its hosts and commentators pushed Trump toward strikes, regime-change fantasies, and open escalation; some even lobbied for the ceasefire to be broken. The network’s role was not passive commentary but active political pressure, reinforced by Trump’s habit of staffing his administration with former Fox personalities. That is the actual machinery here: a presidency and a television network feeding each other, each rewarding the other’s worst instincts.

Blame Laundering

The article’s strongest point is that it refuses the usual misdirection. This was not a confused drift into war. It was a deliberate escalation sold through spectacle, then repackaged as resolve. Fox now wants credit for the same conflict it helped inflame, while Trump wants credit for partially undoing the mess he made. That is a familiar racket: cause the disaster, then claim ownership of the exit.

The False Reset

The proposed MOU, as described in the source, is not a durable settlement. It leaves core nuclear mechanisms unfinalized, defers the hardest questions for 60 days, and even includes a plan to hand Iran up to $300 billion in damages. That is not the clean, triumphant closure Trump’s allies want to advertise. It is a temporary pause after a reckless act of policy, with the bill shoved into the future and the language of success used to cover up the absence of binding results.

The Fox-Trump War Machine

The deeper pattern is not just pro-war media bias. It is the collapse of the boundary between propaganda outlet and governing apparatus. Fox sells emotional certainty, Trump performs it, and both profit from the volatility. When hosts pitch strikes, territorial seizure, and assassination-like logic as acceptable foreign policy, they are not interpreting events. They are helping make them. The network’s business model depends on rage, and war is simply rage with a flag attached.

The Larger Pattern

This is what institutional cowardice looks like when it learns to dress itself as strength. The presidency externalizes responsibility. The network monetizes the escalation. The public gets the casualties, the economic disruption, and the strategic damage. The source is right to call out the doom loop, but the larger lesson is harsher: when media power fuses with executive power, truth becomes negotiable and war becomes content.
