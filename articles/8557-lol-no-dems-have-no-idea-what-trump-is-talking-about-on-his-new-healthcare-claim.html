'Lol no': Dems 'have no idea what Trump is talking about' on his new healthcare claim

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosNovember 20, 20255 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Our Analysis:Trump’s Phantom Negotiations: A Masterclass in Political Fiction

Donald Trump (Reuters)

President Donald Trump claims he’s negotiating with Democrats on health care, but not one lawmaker from that side of the aisle was willing to own up to it.

The president said Sunday that’s “had personal talks with some Democrats” about the idea he’s floated to give cash for health care coverage directly to consumers, but Politico contacted two dozen House and Senate offices and no one confirmed they had been in contact with anyone from the White House.

“Lol no,” said one Senate Democratic spokesperson, when asked if Trump had reached out to their office.

Trump played coy about the purported talks, saying “I can’t tell you who they are, I just don’t want to do that,” and the White House did not offer specifics to back up the president’s claims about direct payments for Americans to “buy their own health insurance” instead of relying on Affordable Care Act subsidies.

“The President is committed [to] reducing health costs for everyday Americans, and he and the Administration are aggressively working to deliver. We are not going to detail the President’s private conversations, especially to a rag like Politico,” said White House spokesperson Kush Desai.

White House deputy chief of staff James Blair said Tuesday that Democrats “honestly, have not really demonstrated an interest in having serious conversations” about a bipartisan health care plan to replace ACA, but Democrats told Politico they don’t know of anyone on their side talking to the president about anything.

“I have no idea what Donald Trump is talking about,” said House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries of New York said on Monday.

“I certainly haven’t had a conversation with him,” Jeffries added. “I have no visibility into [Senate Majority] Leader [Chuck] Schumer having a conversation with him. I don’t know a single member of the House Democratic caucus who’s had a conversation with Donald Trump.”

Rep. Brian Fitzpatrick (R-PA) said he’s on a bipartisan group chat with lawmakers, including Sens. Jeanne Shaheen (D-NH) and Lisa Murkowski (R-AK), to discuss a plan to address the expiring ACA subsidies, but Democrats say there’s been radio silence from the White House.

“This administration is a black box,” said Rep. Becca Balint (D-VT). “They are not communicating with us — members of Congress that are the ones that create policy.”

Our Analysis:

Trump’s Phantom Negotiations: A Masterclass in Political Fiction

In a recent revelation that seems more at home in the realm of political theatre than actual governance, President Donald Trump has claimed to be in negotiations with Democrats over health care reform. This claim, bold as it is, appears to be as substantial as a puff of smoke, dissipating upon the slightest scrutiny. Let’s dissect the layers of this latest political charade with the precision of a scalpel.

The Claim: Trump asserts he’s had “personal talks with some Democrats” about a plan to revolutionize health care by handing cash directly to consumers, effectively bypassing the Affordable Care Act (ACA) subsidies. The premise is as tantalizing as it is revolutionary. However, this juicy tidbit of information lacks one crucial ingredient: evidence.

When Politico took the initiative to verify Trump’s claims, contacting two dozen House and Senate offices, the response was a resounding echo of disbelief and denial. The most telling was a simple, yet profoundly articulate, “Lol no,” from a Senate Democratic spokesperson. If laughter is the best medicine, this response might be the only health care solution offered in this debacle.

The Evasion: Trump, ever the master of suspense, refused to name any of the Democrats he supposedly engaged with, citing a desire not to disclose their identities. This move, reminiscent of a magician’s sleight of hand, leaves us wondering if there’s anything up his sleeve at all. The White House’s refusal to provide details further muddies the water, transforming what could have been a transparent policy discussion into a murky pond of conjecture and hearsay.

The White House’s Response: In a statement that could be mistaken for a defense in a high school debate competition, White House spokesperson Kush Desai dismisses Politico’s inquiries with disdain, while simultaneously failing to offer any concrete information. This tactic of attacking the messenger while sidestepping the message does little to inspire confidence in the administration’s commitment to transparency and accountability.

The Opposition’s Bewilderment: Democratic leaders, from House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries to Senator Chuck Schumer, have expressed confusion and denial regarding Trump’s claims. This collective amnesia among Democrats not only contradicts Trump’s assertions but also paints a picture of a presidency that is either woefully misinformed or deliberately deceptive.

The Bipartisan Group Chat: In a twist that could almost be comedic if the stakes weren’t so high, Rep. Brian Fitzpatrick mentions a bipartisan group chat, a modern-day olive branch in the age of digital diplomacy. Yet, even this attempt at cross-party cooperation seems to have been ghosted by the White House, according to Democrats.

Conclusion:

The narrative spun by Trump and his administration is a masterclass in obfuscation and political misdirection. In a world where health care remains one of the most contentious and critical issues facing Americans, the need for honest, transparent, and effective dialogue has never been greater. Yet, what we witness here is a far cry from such ideals.

The administration’s approach to this alleged negotiation process is akin to claiming the existence of a unicorn; it’s a fantastic story, but without evidence, it remains a figment of the imagination. The American people deserve better than phantom negotiations and political shadowboxing. They deserve tangible solutions, not the illusion of progress.

In the end, this episode serves as a stark reminder of the chasm between political rhetoric and reality. As we navigate the murky waters of contemporary politics, let us remain vigilant, skeptical, and always in pursuit of the truth. The health of our democracy depends on it.
