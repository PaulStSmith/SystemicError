'A war like nobody has seen' is poised to engulf Elon Musk: ex-Trump lawyer

According to one of Donald Trump's many former lawyers, the initial skirmish between the president and billionaire Elon Musk is far from over and likely to grow more vicious.

In a second segment with MSNBC host Ali Velshi on Saturday, former Trump "fixer" Michael Cohen doubled down on an earlier prediction where he stated, "...what Trump is actually doing is weaponizing the Department of Justice through his attorney general and other people — and they are going to drop the hammer on him out of nowhere when he least expects it. That's the playbook. And again, this is political guerrilla warfare at the highest level."

According to Cohen, onlookers may be on the verge of seeing a historic "war."

Cohen noted the disparity of Musk's social media reach via X, compared to Trump barely scratching the surface on Truth Social.

"On his platform which is going to really irritate and I'm sure it already has Trump on that, 172 million people viewed Elon Musk's post versus Trump's on his social media Truth Social platform: 179,000," he told the host.

'Elon Musk has massive powe and here's the problem with that, " he continued. "Trump craves relevance, Elon Musk craves dominance –– it's a very big difference. You're talking about an immovable force trying to smash into something that's indestructible. This is going to be a war like nobody has seen in maybe in all of history."

"But to your point, you just made a minute ago, most Americans are worried about other stuff, so it's fun to watch this little tennis match between them," he added.

Our Analysis:

The Trump-Musk Saga: A Spectacle of Power and Ego

In the latest chapter of what can only be described as a modern-day coliseum of clashing egos, Michael Cohen, a former lawyer for Donald Trump, has thrown in his two cents on the burgeoning feud between Trump and Elon Musk. His commentary, provided to MSNBC's Ali Velshi, suggests that we're on the cusp of witnessing not just a skirmish, but an all-out war, the likes of which history might not have seen before. But let's dissect the spectacle and the players involved, shall we?

The Weaponization of the Department of Justice: Cohen's claim that Trump is manipulating the DOJ to target Musk is a severe allegation. It's a narrative that paints Trump in the role of a political puppeteer, using the powers vested in the government to pursue personal vendettas. This isn't a new trope in the saga of Trump's presidency, but the introduction of Musk as the latest target adds a fresh layer of intrigue. The notion of "political guerrilla warfare" isn't just dramatic; it's a damning reflection of how power can be misused in political rivalries.

The Battle of the Social Media Titans: At the heart of this feud lies the battleground of social media influence. Cohen contrasts Musk's expansive reach on X (formerly Twitter) with Trump's rather modest following on Truth Social. The numbers are stark: Musk's posts can garner views in the hundreds of millions, while Trump's struggle to break two hundred thousand. This isn't just a numbers game; it's a clear indicator of where the public's interest lies, and perhaps, where the power truly resides. Musk's "massive power," as Cohen puts it, isn't just about social media metrics; it's about the ability to influence, to dominate conversations, and, by extension, public opinion.

Relevance vs. Dominance: Cohen's analysis of the motivations driving these two figures is particularly telling. Trump's craving for relevance is juxtaposed against Musk's desire for dominance. This isn't merely a fight over who can shout the loudest; it's a fundamental clash of egos and objectives. Trump seeks to maintain his grip on the public's attention, while Musk aims to assert his superiority, not just over Trump, but perhaps over the entire digital and corporate landscape.

A War Like No Other: Cohen's prediction of a historic war might sound hyperbolic, but it underscores the unprecedented nature of this conflict. Here we have two of the most controversial and influential figures of our time locked in a battle that transcends traditional political or business rivalries. It's a spectacle made for the digital age, with each volley played out in real-time for the world to see.

The Bigger Picture: Amid the spectacle, Cohen's offhand remark that "most Americans are worried about other stuff" serves as a sobering reminder of the disconnect between these high-stakes ego games and the everyday concerns of the public. This "tennis match" between Trump and Musk might provide entertainment, but it also highlights a broader issue of priorities and relevance in the face of pressing societal challenges.

In conclusion, the Trump-Musk feud, as analyzed by Cohen, is more than just a clash of titans. It's a revealing glimpse into the dynamics of power, influence, and ego in the digital age. Whether this battle will have lasting implications or merely serve as a footnote in the annals of social media history remains to be seen. But one thing is clear: in the arena of public opinion, it's not just about who wins or loses, but who can capture the imagination (or ire) of the global audience.
