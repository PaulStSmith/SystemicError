'I want this': Dem donor targeted by Trump calls his bluff over Epstein files

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosNovember 16, 20255 min read

President Donald Trump’s initial response to the latest batch of emails from deceased child predator Jeffrey Epstein has been to call for the investigations of Democrats who have been linked to Epstein. But one of the people Trump explicitly mentioned is now firing back.

In a series of posts to his official X account, LinkedIn co-founder Reid Hoffman — a prominent Democratic donor with a net worth of roughly $2.5 billion — responded to Trump’s call to investigate him with a demand to “release all of the Epstein files,” saying “every person and every document” should be known to the public.

“I want this complete release because it will bring justice to the victims,” Hoffman wrote. In a subsequent post, he wrote that releasing the full files would also prove his innocence.

“I want this complete release because it will show that the calls for baseless investigations of me are nothing more than political persecution and slander,” Hoffman tweeted. “I was never a client of Epstein’s and never had any engagement with him other than fundraising for MIT.”

The Democratic donor went on to argue that Trump’s push to investigate him was “an obvious ploy to avoid releasing the files.” He pushed for the Department of Justice (DOJ) to “simply release all the files, and expose the people who had both deep and ongoing relationships with Epstein.”

“I will do everything in my power going forward to advocate for the release of the files, to get justice for Epstein’s victims and to promote the values of truth our great country was founded on,” he added. Hoffman concluded the thread by writing: “I refuse to bend the knee to Donald Trump and his slanderous lies.”

In addition to Hoffman, Trump demanded the DOJ investigate former President Bill Clinton, former Treasury Secretary and Harvard University President Larry Summers and J.P. Morgan Chase bank.

“This is another Russia, Russia, Russia Scam, with all arrows pointing to the Democrats,” Trump wrote on his Truth Social platform. “Records show that these men, and many others, spent large portions of their life with Epstein, and on his “Island.” Stay tuned!!!”

Our Analysis:

The Art of Deflection: Trump’s Epstein Gambit

In an era where political maneuvering often resembles the plot twists of a soap opera more than the sober machinations of governance, President Donald Trump’s latest salvo in the battle of public opinion doesn’t disappoint. At the heart of this saga lies Trump’s reaction to a new batch of emails connected to the notorious Jeffrey Epstein, a move that could be seen as a masterclass in distraction and deflection.

Trump’s Call to Arms

Trump’s immediate response to the Epstein email revelations was not to address their contents or implications directly. Instead, he took to his digital bullhorn to demand investigations into Democrats linked to Epstein, a strategy as transparent as it is timeworn. Among those caught in Trump’s accusatory glare was Reid Hoffman, LinkedIn co-founder and a well-known Democratic donor. Trump’s tactic here is classic: when under fire, redirect the spotlight.

Hoffman’s Counterstrike

Reid Hoffman’s riposte to Trump’s allegations came swiftly and decisively. Through a series of posts on his official X account (formerly known as Twitter), Hoffman didn’t just refute Trump’s claims; he called for the release of “all of the Epstein files,” advocating for a level of transparency that would, in his view, exonerate him unequivocally. Hoffman’s demand is a bold move, positioning him not just as a defender of his own reputation but as an advocate for the victims of Epstein’s crimes. His assertion that the complete release of files would “bring justice to the victims” and prove his innocence is a powerful statement that shifts the narrative from political scandal to moral imperative.

The Political Ploy Unveiled

Hoffman didn’t stop at demanding transparency. He went further, labeling Trump’s calls for investigation as “nothing more than political persecution and slander.” This assertion highlights a critical aspect of Trump’s strategy: the use of allegations as weapons, regardless of their veracity. Hoffman’s argument that Trump’s maneuvers are “an obvious ploy to avoid releasing the files” paints the former president’s actions not as a quest for justice but as a cynical attempt to muddy the waters and save face.

A Call for Justice and Truth

In his concluding statements, Hoffman reiterates his commitment to advocating for the release of the Epstein files, framing it as a fight for “the values of truth our great country was founded on.” This call to action transcends personal vindication, aiming to hold those truly responsible for their connections to Epstein accountable. Hoffman’s refusal to “bend the knee to Donald Trump and his slanderous lies” is emblematic of a broader struggle against misinformation and character assassination in the service of political expediency.

The Bigger Picture

Trump’s demands for the DOJ to investigate figures like Bill Clinton, Larry Summers, and entities such as J.P. Morgan Chase, coupled with his declaration that this is “another Russia, Russia, Russia Scam,” reveal a pattern of behavior that relies on conspiracy and insinuation as tools of political warfare. His claim that “all arrows point to the Democrats” is a simplification that serves his narrative, ignoring the complex, bipartisan web of associations surrounding Epstein.

Conclusion: The Politics of Distraction

Trump’s response to the Epstein emails is not just about deflection; it’s a calculated attempt to control the narrative by sowing doubt and division. In calling for investigations into his political adversaries based on tenuous connections to Epstein, Trump seeks to recast himself as a crusader against corruption, rather than a participant in the very system he decries. Meanwhile, figures like Reid Hoffman, by demanding transparency and justice, remind us of the importance of cutting through the noise to focus on the real victims and the pursuit of truth. In this latest chapter of American political theater, the battle lines are drawn not just between individuals but between misinformation and integrity.
