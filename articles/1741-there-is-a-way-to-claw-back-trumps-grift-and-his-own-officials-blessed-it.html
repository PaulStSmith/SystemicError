There is a way to claw back Trump’s grift — and his own officials blessed it

Unmasking the Illusion of Accountability in High-Stakes Politics

The Power at Play: Disgorgement and Its Theoretical Might

The concept of disgorgement, a legal remedy intended to recover profits gained through wrongdoing, is at the heart of recent discussions by Protect Democracy leaders Justin Florence and Justin Vail. They argue that this principle can be used to reclaim wealth amassed by President Donald Trump and his circle through dubious means. This principle, which has been supported by Trump’s own government attorneys in cases unrelated directly to him, suggests a mechanism for financial restitution to the public, not contingent on proving specific victim harm.

The Irony of Government Endorsement

It’s both ironic and telling that Trump’s administration has inadvertently bolstered the argument for disgorgement. While their support was likely aimed at broader applications within securities fraud, it unwittingly applies to the myriad ways Trump’s enterprises potentially mesh personal gain with presidential power. This includes crypto profits and murky international dealings. The endorsement by Trump’s attorneys of disgorgement principles, even as a general policy, underscores a rare moment of legal clarity in an administration often marked by its attempts to bend justice.

The Mirage of Justice

Despite the robust theoretical framework for using disgorgement to rectify Trump’s alleged financial overreaches, the practical application raises significant questions about real-world efficacy. Trump’s history of evading legal repercussions—highlighted by the numerous pardons he’s issued, including potentially to himself, and the notorious ruling in Trump v. United States granting him broad criminal immunity—casts a long shadow over the optimistic view that disgorgement could serve as a tool of accountability. This paints a disheartening picture of justice: accessible in theory, yet obstructed in practice.

Misdirection and Scapegoating

The focus on disgorgement also subtly shifts the narrative from systemic corruption and abuse of power to a matter of financial penalties. This shift could be seen as a form of misdirection, framing the problem as one of financial misconduct rather than a more profound issue of governance failure. Moreover, if disgorgement is pursued without addressing the underlying power abuses, it risks being a slap on the wrist rather than a genuine correction, potentially allowing Trump and similar figures to remain political martyrs victimized by the system.

A Systemic Insight: The Gap Between Law and Justice

The discourse around disgorgement highlights a critical gap in American politics: the space between legal rights and their enforcement. Laws that exist on the books often serve more as theoretical tools than practical solutions, especially when it comes to powerful figures. The institutional power dynamics favor those with the resources and connections to navigate, if not outright manipulate, the system. This case exemplifies how even when legal mechanisms exist to address corruption, the implementation is fraught with challenges, often rendering them ineffective against well-entrenched power structures.

Conclusion: The Need for Real Change

The situation surrounding Trump and the concept of disgorgement is a microcosm of broader issues in U.S. governance. While legal tools like disgorgement can theoretically serve justice, their effectiveness is limited by the political will to apply them and the integrity of the institutions responsible for their enforcement. True accountability in politics requires not just laws that look good on paper but a systemic commitment to uphold them against all odds and interests. Without this, the cycle of corruption and impunity will continue, masked by the occasional illusion of justice.
