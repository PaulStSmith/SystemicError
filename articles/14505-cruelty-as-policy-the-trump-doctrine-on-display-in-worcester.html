Cruelty as Policy: The Trump Doctrine on Display in Worcester

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosMay 24, 20252 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

There’s a moment in every dystopian narrative when the mask slips — when authoritarianism drops the pretense and reveals itself for what it truly is: domination through fear, dressed up as law. The arrest of Rosane Ferreira de Oliveira in Worcester, Massachusetts, is that moment. Not a footnote. Not an aberration. A thesis statement.

Under the Trump administration, ICE didn’t just arrest undocumented immigrants — they hunted them. And in this case, they baited their trap with the most despicable lure possible: a baby.

Let that sink in.

They arrested a woman’s daughter and then told her she needed to come retrieve her grandchild because the underage sibling couldn’t take custody. When the woman showed up in good faith — ICE pounced. No warrant shown. No due process. Just raw, premeditated entrapment.

This isn’t immigration enforcement. This is emotional warfare waged by a state apparatus drunk on power and bereft of humanity.

And like clockwork, the Trump administration praised this operation. Of course they did. Because to them, cruelty isn’t collateral — it’s currency. It sends a message: We can get you anywhere, anytime. No law will protect you. No family bond will save you. Your rights mean nothing when we decide you don’t deserve them.

Even worse, the Worcester police, despite state law explicitly forbidding such cooperation, joined the feeding frenzy. They arrested a 16-year-old girl and a local political candidate who dared protest. This was not law enforcement. This was a public spectacle — a power display designed to humiliate, dominate, and terrify.

Let’s not forget the sickening hypocrisy. Around the same time this was happening, Trump cut deals that allowed cartel family members to enter the country. So while ICE was roughing up teenagers and trying to take babies from mothers in broad daylight, actual organized crime affiliates were waltzing through immigration thanks to a handshake deal with the cartel-connected Mexican government.

This is not national security. This is not justice. This is a state machinery used to terrorize the powerless while shielding the corrupt and well-connected.

If this isn’t the moment we wake up, when will it be? When the next mother is dragged into a van? When the next protester is arrested for daring to film state-sanctioned kidnapping?

Enough.

This country cannot claim to be a beacon of freedom while operating internment tactics in the daylight. The Trump administration didn’t just empower ICE — it transformed it into a paramilitary strike force with carte blanche to trample civil rights and use children as leverage.

And if we let this go unanswered, we are complicit.
