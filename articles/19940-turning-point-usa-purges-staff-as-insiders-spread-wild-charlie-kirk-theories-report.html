Turning Point USA purges staff as insiders spread wild Charlie Kirk theories: report

Turning Point USA has allegedly been purging staffers who bought into conspiracy theories about co-founder Charlie Kirk’s assassination.

Aubrey Laitsch, a communications staffer for the right-wing activist group, posted a video claiming that she was called into a meeting last month and summarily fired. The Bulwark reported that she’s not the only staffer cut loose for their apparent belief that Turning Point USA was involved in Kirk’s murder.

“I just have a gut feeling that I was terminated from Turning Point because I am questioning the narrative of what happened to my role model and CEO, Charlie Kirk, on the day of his assassination,” Laitsch said in the video.

Conspiracy theories about the 31-year-old Kirk’s assassination have grown in popularity on the right after influencer Candace Owens, a former TPUSA employee, spread claims that his killing was an inside job. Laitsch said she’s not the only employee who buys into the idea.

“It’s hard to say if these firings are being driven by the (very sensible) disapproval of staff talking about their company killing its founder, or paranoia about Owens having credible information about internal TPUSA activities — or both,” The Bulwark reported.

“But clearly, someone within the organization is leaking to Owens. Just this year, the highly controversial podcaster posted videos of Erika Kirk on internal videochats in the wake of Kirk’s assassination that were interpreted on the online right as insufficiently mournful.”

Owens was served a cease-and-desist order from TPUSA last month, but she continues to suggest on her YouTube show that Kirk’s 37-year-old widow, who took over the group after his fatal shooting, was involved in his death.

“On her Tuesday show, Owens quipped that, if Erika Kirk had been killed instead of Charlie — and if Charlie then acted as Erika Kirk is acting now in the wake of his assassination — he would be on trial for his wife’s murder,” The Bulwark reported.

Our Analysis:

The Unraveling: TPUSA’s Conspiracy Quagmire

In an almost cinematic twist of fate, Turning Point USA (TPUSA), a bastion of right-wing activism known for its fervent campaign against what it perceives as liberal indoctrination in American society, finds itself ensnared in a web of conspiracy theories—the very kind it has often been accused of propagating. The alleged purging of staffers who dared to question the narrative surrounding co-founder Charlie Kirk’s assassination marks a new chapter in the organization’s saga, one that reeks of irony, paranoia, and the unmistakable scent of internal discord.

The Allegations: Purges and Paranoia

According to a report by The Bulwark, TPUSA is apparently on a witch hunt, targeting employees who entertain the notion that the organization might have had a hand in Kirk’s untimely demise. Aubrey Laitsch, a communications staffer, claims she was abruptly fired for merely questioning the official account of Kirk’s assassination—a move that suggests TPUSA is not just fighting external battles but is now deeply mired in an internal struggle over its soul.

The conspiracy theories gaining traction within the organization’s ranks, fueled by former employee and influencer Candace Owens, posit that Kirk’s killing was an “inside job.” Owens, known for her controversial takes and no stranger to conspiracy theories herself, has been aggressively pushing this narrative, even implicating Kirk’s widow, Erika Kirk, in the assassination.

The Irony: A Mirror to the Organization’s Soul

The irony of this situation is as thick as it is tragic. TPUSA, an entity that has often played fast and loose with facts to suit its agenda, is now grappling with the monster it helped create—a base that is all too willing to embrace conspiracy theories, even when they are directed inward. The organization’s attempt to silence dissenting voices within its ranks, as reported, is not just a testament to its paranoia but also an indictment of its inability to apply critical thinking and openness to its operations.

The Fallout: A Question of Leadership and Integrity

The handling of this crisis raises serious questions about TPUSA’s leadership and its commitment to the very principles it claims to champion. By reportedly purging staffers for the mere act of questioning, the organization is betraying a fundamental tenet of democratic societies: the freedom of thought and expression. Furthermore, the cease-and-desist order served to Owens, while perhaps understandable from a legal standpoint, does little to address the root causes of the turmoil. Instead, it appears as an attempt to silence a troublesome narrative without confronting the underlying issues of trust and transparency within the organization.

Conclusion: The Path Forward

For TPUSA, the path forward is fraught with challenges. The organization must undertake a rigorous introspection to address not only the immediate crisis but also the systemic issues that allowed such a conspiracy-laden environment to flourish. It must strive for a culture of openness, where questioning and critical discourse are not just tolerated but encouraged. Only then can TPUSA hope to rebuild its credibility and emerge from this debacle stronger and more united.

In the end, the saga of TPUSA’s internal turmoil serves as a cautionary tale about the dangers of fostering an environment where conspiracy theories and paranoia are allowed to run rampant. It’s a stark reminder that organizations, much like individuals, must be vigilant in upholding the values they profess, lest they fall victim to the very forces they seek to combat.
