Divine Delusion: Trump’s Christ-Like Imagery and the Demon Within

Manipulation of Symbolism

The recent uproar in the MAGA community over an image shared by former President Donald Trump on his platform Truth Social, where he is depicted as a Christ-like figure, is more than just a controversy—it’s a meticulous play in visual propaganda. Trump, draped in a pseudo-religious garb healing a man, while American servicemen transformed into angels hover above, clearly stretches beyond mere admiration into the realms of messianic delusion. But the twist here is not just the blasphemous portrayal; it’s the sinister alteration of adding a horned, demonic figure in place of one of the angelic servicemen.

Who Holds the Power?

This scenario centers around Trump, an individual who despite no longer holding presidential power, still wields significant influence over public opinion and media. The decision to share this image, and specifically to alter it to include a demonic figure, was made within his camp—whether by him directly or by his staff. The responsibility for this imagery, and its implications, lies squarely with Trump and his media team, who manage his Truth Social platform and craft his public persona.

Misdirection and Blame

The White House’s inconsistent statements regarding who controls Trump’s social media posts—sometimes blaming staff for offensive content, while at other times claiming all posts are directly from Trump—serve to obfuscate accountability. This inconsistency is strategic, allowing Trump’s team to distance themselves from backlash while maintaining a direct line of communication with his base. Furthermore, the addition of a demonic figure raises questions about the intent and message being conveyed, which diverts discussion from the appropriateness of the Christ-like portrayal itself.

A Pattern of Propaganda

Trump’s use of religious iconography and elements of supernatural valor in his imagery fits a larger pattern of his political strategy: co-opting powerful symbols to bolster a personal cult of personality. This tactic is not just about flattering imagery but about positioning himself as a savior-like figure above reproach or accountability, directly appealing to the emotional and spiritual inclinations of his audience. The inclusion of a demonic figure complicates this narrative, perhaps intending to depict lurking malevolence against which only Trump can defend.

Broader Insights

This episode illustrates a broader political manipulation tactic—using visual propaganda to shape public perception and distract from real issues. Trump’s portrayal as a quasi-religious figure exploits religious sentiment, aligning his personal political ambitions with spiritual and cultural identities. This not only deepens division but also solidifies his influence by intertwining it with personal and cultural identities of his supporters.

In conclusion, Trump’s deployment of messianic imagery peppered with cryptic demonic alterations serves multiple functions: it reinforces his perceived sanctity among followers, sows confusion and intrigue, and distracts from substantive political discourse. Such tactics underscore the dangerous potency of combining political power with cult-like leader worship, a combination that remains a persistent threat to democratic norms and rational public discourse.
