Denmark creates new 'night watch' as Trump eyes Greenland

By Paulo Santos

President Donald Trump has made no secret of his desire for the U.S. to acquire Greenland as a new American territory. In response to this renewed pressure, Denmark, which currently controls Greenland as an autonomous territory, has set up a new “night watch” program to keep tabs on Trump’s public comments.

News of this program first emerged in the Danish newspaper, Politiken. The outlet reported that Denmark’s foreign ministry now has a team working a 5 p.m. to 7 a.m. night shift monitoring any public statements Trump might make about Greenland, designed to account for the time difference between the country and the U.S. Any time that Trump makes a statement containing the word “Greenland,” it is added to a report presented to officials in the morning.

This night watch program has reportedly been in operation since the spring. Speaking with Newsweek about the program, Rasmus Sinding Søndergaard, a senior researcher at the Danish Institute for International Studies, said that Danish officials remain extremely concerned about Trump’s overtures concerning Greenland, and may be looking to stall in hopes of a Democratic win in the 2026 midterms.

“Denmark is currently trying to play for time, hoping that this will fizzle out and that maybe a democratic win in the US midterms might stifle Trump’s ambitions to take Greenland,” Søndergaard explained.

Greenland is a snowy island territory situated in the Arctic Ocean northeast of Canada. Experts have suggested that Greenland’s rich supply of important minerals, including rare-earth minerals that Trump has been fixated on securing during his second term.

Danish Foreign Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen has warned U.S. officials against Trump’s Greenland posturing, and Danish officials have repeatedly insisted the territory is “not for sale.” Trump has previously attempted to claim that Greenland residents want to join the U.S., despite an overwhelming 85 percent of Greenlanders opposing the idea. The president has also stated that he does not rule out the use of military action or economic pressure to coerce Denmark into ceding control of Greenland, which put Danish officials on even higher alert.

Our Analysis:

Denmark’s Night Watch: A Vigil Against Trump’s Greenland Ambitions

In a move that reads more like a satirical twist in a political drama, Denmark has initiated a “night watch” program solely focused on monitoring President Donald Trump’s nighttime musings about Greenland. This program, as reported by the Danish newspaper Politiken, symbolizes a surreal but strategic defense against Trump’s openly expressed desire to turn Greenland into a new American territory. Let’s dissect the layers of absurdity and alarm that this situation presents.

The Night Watch: A Response to Unprecedented Times

Denmark’s foreign ministry has taken the extraordinary step of assigning a team to work from 5 p.m. to 7 a.m., tracking any public statements made by Trump regarding Greenland. The fact that a sovereign nation feels compelled to establish a monitoring program to keep tabs on another country’s president’s comments—on social media, no less—illustrates the unique and troubling diplomacy of our era. This isn’t just about international relations; it’s about the unpredictable ripple effects of one man’s social media activity.

Greenland: Not Just a Piece of Real Estate

Greenland, an autonomous Danish territory, is not a mere piece of land waiting to be acquired. Its geopolitical significance is enormous, given its location in the Arctic Ocean and its rich supply of rare-earth minerals. Trump’s fixation on these minerals underscores a broader strategic interest in the region, but his approach—treating Greenland as an asset to be purchased—reveals a troubling disregard for the norms of international sovereignty and self-determination. The overwhelming opposition from Greenlanders, with 85 percent opposing the idea of joining the U.S., speaks volumes about the deeply flawed assumption that economic pressure or military action could be legitimate tools to change the status quo.

The Danish Dilemma: Playing for Time

Rasmus Sinding Søndergaard’s comments to Newsweek about Denmark’s strategy to “play for time” in hopes of a political shift in the U.S. highlights a grim reality. It is disconcerting to witness a country having to strategize around the potential whims of foreign leadership, hoping for a change in the political winds as a defense mechanism. This situation is emblematic of the broader unease many nations feel in dealing with the current U.S. administration’s unpredictable foreign policy stance.

A Stark Warning Against Unilateral Ambitions

Danish Foreign Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen’s warnings against Trump’s posturing over Greenland should not be dismissed lightly. The mere suggestion of using military action or economic pressure to coerce Denmark into ceding control of Greenland is a stark reminder of the dangers inherent in unilateral ambitions on the global stage. Such comments not only strain diplomatic relations but also risk destabilizing an already fragile Arctic region.

Conclusion: The Seriousness Behind the Absurdity

While there’s a certain level of absurdity in the image of Danish officials huddled around monitors, waiting for Trump’s next tweet about Greenland, the underlying issues are no laughing matter. This situation underscores the volatility of international relations in the digital age and the serious implications of treating sovereign territories as commodities to be acquired. Denmark’s night watch program, though seemingly a product of bizarre times, is a rational response to an unprecedented challenge. It serves as a cautionary tale about the importance of diplomacy, respect for international norms, and the recognition of the right of peoples to self-determination.
