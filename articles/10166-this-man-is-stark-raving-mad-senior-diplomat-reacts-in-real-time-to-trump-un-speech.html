'This man is stark raving mad': Senior diplomat reacts in real-time to Trump UN speech

U.S. President Donald Trump addresses the 80th United Nations General Assembly at U.N. headquarters in New York City, U.S., September 23, 2025. REUTERS/MIKE SEGAR

A senior diplomat reacted with shock as President Donald Trump was delivering his speech to the United Nations.

While Trump was speaking to the General Assembly on Tuesday, Washington Post correspondent Ishaan Tharoor said he received a text message from a “senior foreign diplomat posted at the UN.”

“This man is stark raving mad. Do Americans not see how embarrassing this is?” the diplomat reportedly said.

During the speech, Trump claimed he had ended foreign wars and questioned the purpose of the United Nations.

“We’re the hottest country anywhere in the world,” the president insisted. “I’m really good at this stuff. Your countries are going to hell.”

Trump also said he deserved the Nobel Peace Prize while bragging about military strikes on alleged drug boats near Venezuela.

“We will blow you out of existence,” he warned.

Our Analysis:

The Spectacle at the United Nations: Trump’s Tirade

In an incident that could only be described as jaw-dropping, a senior diplomat’s reaction to President Donald Trump’s speech at the United Nations underscores the global bewilderment and concern at the highest levels of international diplomacy. The incredulity expressed by the diplomat, conveyed through a text message to Washington Post correspondent Ishaan Tharoor, encapsulates the essence of the global diplomatic community’s perception of Trump’s rhetoric. “This man is stark raving mad. Do Americans not see how embarrassing this is?” poses a question that not only critiques Trump’s demeanor but also reflects on the American public’s acceptance or normalization of such conduct.

Trump’s speech, as reported, was a blend of self-aggrandizement and contentious assertions. Claiming to have “ended foreign wars” while simultaneously threatening military action (”We will blow you out of existence”) against boats near Venezuela, Trump’s statements oscillate between the poles of peace-bringer and warmonger. This dichotomy not only muddies the waters of his foreign policy stance but also undermines the credibility of his assertions.

The Irony of Questioning the United Nations

One cannot help but marvel at the irony of President Trump questioning the purpose of the United Nations within the very walls of the institution designed to foster international peace and cooperation. The UN, with all its imperfections, stands as a testament to the collective aspiration of humanity to rise above conflict and unite for common goals. Trump’s rhetorical question, therefore, not only displays a misunderstanding of the institution’s significance but also a disregard for the principles it upholds.

“We’re the hottest country anywhere in the world”

This statement, boldly proclaimed by Trump, while perhaps intended to instill a sense of national pride, instead skirts the line of jingoism. It’s a simplistic assertion in a complex global landscape, where international respect and influence cannot be measured by such insular metrics. Furthermore, Trump’s declaration of being “really good at this stuff” juxtaposed with his subsequent derogation of other countries (”Your countries are going to hell.”) is emblematic of a diplomacy style that is both divisive and undiplomatic.

The Nobel Peace Prize Deserved?

Trump’s assertion that he deserved the Nobel Peace Prize, within the same breath as bragging about military strikes, presents a paradox that cannot be ignored. The Nobel Peace Prize, a symbol of profound contributions to peace, sits awkwardly next to the aggressive rhetoric of blowing adversaries “out of existence.” This stark contrast not only highlights a misunderstanding of the prize’s criteria but also reflects a broader dissonance within Trump’s presentation of his foreign policy achievements.

Conclusion: A Reflection on Diplomatic Decorum

The senior diplomat’s message to Tharoor serves as a poignant reminder of the expectations placed upon the president of the United States, not just as a leader within his own country, but as a figure on the world stage. The role demands not only the careful navigation of complex international dynamics but also a demeanor that reflects the gravity of the position. Trump’s speech at the United Nations, as described, deviates from these expectations, eliciting reactions that range from disbelief to concern among the global diplomatic community.

In analyzing Trump’s UN address, we are confronted with the stark reality of a presidency that often blurs the lines between nationalistic bravado and diplomatic engagement. The implications of such rhetoric on America’s global standing and relationships are profound, demanding not just scrutiny but a critical examination of the direction in which such discourse steers international relations.
