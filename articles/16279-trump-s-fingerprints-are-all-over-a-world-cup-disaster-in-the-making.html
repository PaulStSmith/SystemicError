The Strategic Alliance of Power and Spectacle: Trump, Infantino, and the 2026 World Cup

A Convenient Partnership

When the 2026 FIFA World Cup kicks off, not only will it celebrate a global sporting event, but it will also spotlight the intricate dance of power between two notable figures: former U.S. President Donald Trump and FIFA President Gianni Infantino. This partnership, which began with mutual benefits in mind, has evolved into a full display of how sports and politics intermingle, often at the expense of ethical governance and democratic values. Trump, a figure notorious for his divisive rhetoric and authoritarian leanings, has found a willing ally in Infantino, a man who has navigated his own controversies and accusations of favoritism and corruption within FIFA.

Awards as Political Tools

The awarding of FIFA’s inaugural Peace Prize to Trump by Infantino in December 2025 serves as a glaring example of how awards can be manipulated as political tools. This event, far from being a genuine recognition of peace-making efforts, appears more as a compensatory gesture to soothe Trump’s bruised ego after failing to win the Nobel Peace Prize. It underscores how Infantino uses his position to forge alliances with powerful figures, thereby securing his and FIFA’s interests by aligning with influential, albeit controversial, leaders.

Exploiting the Game

The choice of the U.S. as a host nation for the World Cup under Trump’s shadow has been fraught with controversy. Trump’s policies, which have included visa bans and hostile rhetoric towards other nations including co-hosts Canada and Mexico, paint a troubling picture of a sporting event used as a stage for nationalist and exclusionary politics. These actions by Trump, facilitated by Infantino’s accommodating stance, risk turning the World Cup from a celebration of international unity into a display of power dynamics and exclusion.

The Illiberal Trend

Infantino’s history of associating with illiberal regimes – from Russia to Qatar and Saudi Arabia – reveals a pattern: the prioritization of financial and political gain over the promotion of human rights and democratic values. This pattern is evident in his handling of the World Cup locations and his indifference to the criticisms of human rights abuses associated with these events. By granting hosting rights to countries with dubious human rights records, Infantino has shown a consistent disregard for the ethical implications, as long as it benefits FIFA’s agenda and his personal stature within the organization.

The Bigger Picture

The alliance between Trump and Infantino is not just a mutual admiration club but a strategic maneuver that benefits both by leveraging their positions for greater influence and control. This relationship exemplifies how sports organizations and political figures can collaborate to further their personal and economic interests, often at the cost of overshadowing the true spirit of the sports they represent. The 2026 World Cup, thus, is set to be not only a tournament of goals on the field but a stark display of the goals of those in power off the field.

Conclusion: Beyond the Field

The unfolding events leading to the 2026 World Cup serve as a critical reminder of the intersections between global sports, politics, and power. The Trump-Infantino alliance highlights the broader issues of how sporting events can be exploited for political gains and how leadership in these realms can profoundly impact international relations and public perceptions. As we move closer to the World Cup, it becomes imperative to scrutinize not just the games, but the larger political and economic agendas they may serve. This World Cup could either be a testament to the unifying power of sports or a chapter in the playbook of how to politicize and profit from them at a global scale.
