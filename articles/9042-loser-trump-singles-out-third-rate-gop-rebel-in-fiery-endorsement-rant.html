'Loser!' Trump singles out 'third-rate' GOP rebel in fiery endorsement rant

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosNovember 4, 20254 min read

President Donald Trump spent much of his early afternoon rattling off a long list of congressional endorsements on his Truth Social account — but he took time to conspicuously call out one particular Republican he is not endorsing.

Specifically, Trump posted a rambling manifesto against Rep. Thomas Massie (R-KY), a libertarian-leaning Republican lawmaker who has gained a reputation as something of a rebel within the caucus, often defying Trump’s more authoritarian impulses and bucking the party on various spending items.

“Third Rate Congressman Thomas Massie, a Weak and Pathetic RINO from the Great Commonwealth of Kentucky, a place I love, and won big SIX TIMES, must be thrown out of office, ASAP!” wrote Trump. “The incredible people of Kentucky’s 4th Congressional District gave us a mandate to, MAKE AMERICA GREAT AGAIN, and the person that will help us do that is Navy SEAL, Army Ranger, and Fifth Generation Kentucky Farmer, Captain Ed Gallrein, a true America First Patriot.”

Trump then went into a lengthy laundry list of praise for Gallrein, saying that he “knows how to Create GREAT Jobs, Cut Taxes and Regulations, Promote MADE IN THE U.S.A., Support our Amazing Farmers and American Agriculture, Unleash American Energy Dominance, and Champion our Nation’s Golden Age.”

“Captain Ed Gallrein has my Complete and Total Endorsement against Massie, who is now polling at about 9% because the Great People of Kentucky are wise to him — He only votes against the Republican Party, making life very easy for the Radical Left,” wrote Trump. “Unlike ‘lightweight’ Massie, a totally ineffective LOSER who has failed us so badly, CAPTAIN ED GALLREIN IS A WINNER WHO WILL NOT LET YOU DOWN!”

This is not the first time Trump has gone out of his way to target Massie. Last month, he proclaimed the congressman should be “thrown out of office“ and once again told voters to back Gallrein over him.

Our Analysis:

Trump’s Endorsement Antics: A Closer Look

In a move that surprises exactly no one, President Donald Trump has taken to his digital soapbox on Truth Social to do what he does best: stir the pot of GOP internal politics with the subtlety of a sledgehammer. This time, his target is Rep. Thomas Massie (R-KY), a figure who dares to deviate from the Trumpian line. Let’s dissect this latest episode of political theatre with the critical eye it deserves.

The Attack on Massie

Trump’s tirade against Massie isn’t just noteworthy because of its vitriol; it’s a glaring exhibition of Trump’s intolerance for dissent within his own party. Labeling Massie a “Third Rate Congressman” and a “Weak and Pathetic RINO” is classic Trump, using personal attacks rather than substantive political critique. It’s an interesting choice, given Massie’s libertarian leanings, which often align with conservative principles of limited government and fiscal restraint—principles Trump’s administration frequently sidestepped with its own spending habits.

The Praise for Gallrein

Enter Captain Ed Gallrein, Trump’s champion and the antithesis to Massie, according to the former president. Trump’s endorsement of Gallrein reads like an infomercial script for a political candidate: “Create GREAT Jobs, Cut Taxes and Regulations, Promote MADE IN THE U.S.A.,” and the list goes on. While the laundry list of promises is politically appetizing, it lacks the substance of how Gallrein plans to achieve these lofty goals. It’s the political equivalent of promising to cure all societal ailments without a hint of the medicine to be used.

The Strategy and Hypocrisy

Trump’s strategic endorsement and public shaming of Massie underscore a broader tactic: purging the party of perceived disloyalty and reinforcing a singular, Trump-centric vision of Republicanism. It’s a move that’s less about advancing conservative policies and more about solidifying personal power within the GOP. Moreover, Trump’s claim that Massie is “polling at about 9%” and his description of him as a “totally ineffective LOSER” reek of hypocrisy, considering Trump’s own tumultuous record and divisive nature, which arguably have done more to embolden the “Radical Left” than a congressman’s dissenting votes ever could.

The Underlying Irony

The irony of Trump’s rant can’t be understated. In his quest to root out so-called RINOs and enforce party purity, Trump inadvertently showcases the very authoritarian impulses Massie is accused of defying. It’s a stark reminder of how Trump’s influence continues to shape the GOP, not through a cohesive policy vision, but through loyalty tests and public spectacles.

Conclusion

Trump’s latest endorsement saga is more than just a personal vendetta against a dissenting congressman; it’s a microcosm of the current state of Republican politics. As the GOP continues to grapple with its identity in the post-Trump era, episodes like this offer a clear view of the internal tensions and the ongoing battle between principle and personality. Massie’s sin, it seems, isn’t a failure to support conservative policies, but a failure to bow to the whims of Trump. And in Trump’s GOP, that’s an unforgivable offense.
