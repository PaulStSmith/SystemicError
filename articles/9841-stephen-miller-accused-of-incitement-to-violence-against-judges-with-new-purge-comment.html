Stephen Miller accused of 'incitement to violence against judges' with new 'purge' comment

U.S. White House Deputy Chief of Staff Stephen Miller looks on as he speaks to reporters at the White House in Washington, D.C., U.S., May 30, 2025. REUTERS/Nathan Howard

U.S. White House Deputy Chief of Staff Stephen Miller Saturday was accused of “incitement to violence against judges.”

Miller, who has been accused of making his subordinates cry at work, took to social media over the weekend to make several proclamations related to “leftist” judges.

“The Democrat Party has filled our legal and judicial system with radicals who protect leftwing terrorists,” Miller wrote Saturday. “This is a very real and dire crisis for our Republican form of government.”

In a separate post, Miller accused “Democrat judges” of “shielding” terrorism.

“The issue before is now is very simple and clear. There is a large and growing movement of leftwing terrorism in this country,” he claimed. “It is well organized and funded. And it is shielded by far-left Democrat judges, prosecutors and attorneys general.”

Miller then concluded, “The only remedy is to use legitimate state power to dismantle terrorism and terror networks.”

Ex-Obama speechwriter Jon Favreau weighed in, “Is that what you call zip-tying children, smashing glass on a one-month old, shooting chemical munitions at journalists, and breaking the ribs of an 80-year old citizen?”

Former attorney Dean Obeidallah replied, “I can’t wait for Miller’s trial on state charges once Trump is out of office.”

Ex-prosecutor Ron Filipkowski said, “This seems like incitement to violence against judges.”

Journalist Jim Stewartson similarly sounded the alarm:

“Stephen Miller is going to execute a purge of the Democratic Party and the justice system based on his illegal, unconstitutional memo NSPM-7. He has collapsed Democrats, ‘far-left Democrat judges’ and ‘Antifa’ into a single domestic terror threat as a pretext for mass arrests.”

NBC News Supreme Court reporter Lawrence Hurley said Miller was ramping up the rhetoric.

“Stephen Miller escalates the harsh and conspiratorial rhetoric yet again, suggesting that federal judges, prosecutors and state attorneys general are aiding and abetting a shadowy network of leftist domestic terrorist groups,” Hurley wrote on X.

Our Analysis:

The Dangerous Rhetoric of Stephen Miller: A Critical Analysis

In a recent display of incendiary rhetoric, Stephen Miller, the U.S. White House Deputy Chief of Staff, has escalated the tension between political factions with accusations that are as dangerous as they are unfounded. Over a series of social media posts, Miller has accused “leftist” judges and the Democratic Party of protecting terrorists, an allegation that not only undermines the integrity of the United States’ judicial system but also dangerously borders on incitement to violence.

The Allegations

Miller’s assertions that the Democratic Party has infiltrated the judicial system with radicals and that there exists a “large and growing movement of leftwing terrorism” are grave. They are not, however, grounded in reality but rather in a deeply polarizing political strategy. By accusing Democratic judges of “shielding” terrorism, Miller simplifies and demonizes an entire segment of the political spectrum, without evidence, to rally his base.

The Real Crisis

The crisis Miller mentions is not, as he claims, one of leftwing terrorism being shielded by Democratic judges. The real crisis is the weaponization of language by a high-ranking government official to delegitimize and dehumanize political opponents. His call to use “legitimate state power to dismantle terrorism and terror networks” thinly veils an alarming inclination towards authoritarianism, targeting not just supposed terrorists but the very fabric of democratic dissent and judicial independence.

Responses to Miller’s Rhetoric

The responses from figures such as Jon Favreau, Dean Obeidallah, Ron Filipkowski, Jim Stewartson, and Lawrence Hurley highlight the breadth of concern over Miller’s statements. These reactions underscore the fear that Miller’s rhetoric could incite violence against judges and further polarize an already divided nation. Stewartson’s warning that Miller aims to execute a purge based on an “illegal, unconstitutional memo” amplifies the alarm over the potential for abuse of power and the undermining of democratic institutions.

Conclusion: The Dangerous Path Ahead

Stephen Miller’s rhetoric is not just reckless; it’s potentially catalytic. By conflating Democrats, “far-left Democrat judges,” and antifacist activists into a monolithic “domestic terror threat,” Miller is not just misrepresenting reality—he’s crafting a narrative that could justify draconian measures against perceived political enemies.

This is not merely a partisan issue; it is a democratic one. The implications of a government official calling for the use of state power against a fabricated enemy are chilling. It recalls the darkest chapters of history where governments have targeted political opponents under the guise of fighting terrorism or protecting the state.

Miller’s statements should not be viewed in isolation but as part of a broader, disturbing trend towards authoritarianism and away from democratic norms. The use of such rhetoric by a high-ranking official should be universally condemned, and the principles of democracy, judicial independence, and political dissent must be vigorously defended. The path Miller advocates for is not one towards security but towards division and, potentially, despotism.
