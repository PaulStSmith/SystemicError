MSNBC's Jonathan Lemire dumped cold water on the "hoax" denials of the Jeffrey Epstein scandal coming from President Donald Trump and his allies.

Trump had denied writing a suggestive birthday note decades ago to the late sex offender, and now that House Oversight Committee Democrats have obtained a copy, the president's allies are insisting his signature was forged as part of a years-long political hit campaign.

"There were real hopes in the White House that over the August recess that this story would fade away, that we would talk about other things, and we did for a time, the story lost some heat," Lemire told "Morning Joe."

"But it's come back now, and we shouldn't overlook the fact that some of the women involved here, the victims, you had that very powerful news conference a few days ago and put a real human face to this. It's not just a political story, it's a human tragedy. It's a crime, and yesterday you saw what is the familiar playbook, just an attempt to deny reality, to say, 'No, no, no, it's a lie, it's a hoax, it's a witch hunt,' whatever it is, without actually offering any evidence to support those claims, and the Wall Street Journal, let's remember, the president sued the Journal. Members of his administration attacked the Journal, basically daring them, like, show us the evidence here.

"Well, they did, but let's remember, this isn't something they conjured up."

"This was in the Epstein estate since 2003," Lemire added. "That'd be quite the long game to try to get President Trump. If this was indeed a hoax to plant a birthday card more than two decades ago, and the Journal did a very good job, one of the lines of defense was the White House said, 'Well, the president always signs first name, first and last name.' Well, that's not the case. They presented a number of letters where sometimes it's just 'Donald,' so the signature certainly would appear to match that photograph you just showed with the fake check. Certainly would raise some eyebrows, and, again, this is a story that even some Republicans are not willing to let go."

Host Joe Scarborough reminded viewers that conservative media mogul Rupert Murdoch was helping to drive the scandal with fresh reporting in the Journal, his flagship newspaper.

"It is now, again, safely the most conservative, the most powerful conservative media organization on the planet," Scarborough said. "They are the ones who have broken this story, they are the ones who have continued reporting on this story. It is Rupert Murdoch's own conservative media empire that is running this, not Democrats, not a Democratic hoax, not a left-wing CBS News edit, whatever they would want to say. This is straight out of the Murdoch empire – all of it."

Our Analysis:

The Epstein Scandal: Unwrapping the Layers of Denial and Deception

In the murky waters of political scandal, the Jeffrey Epstein saga resurfaces with a vengeance, courtesy of MSNBC's Jonathan Lemire and a suggestive birthday note that President Donald Trump fervently denies having penned. The narrative spun by Trump and his cohorts—that of a forged signature and a decades-long political hit campaign—is not just thin; it's transparently desperate.

Denials in the Face of Overwhelming Evidence

The attempts to brush off the scandal as a mere "hoax" or "witch hunt" are emblematic of a well-worn playbook of denial. Yet, the evidence, as Lemire points out, is not borne of recent fabrications. The contested note has been part of the Epstein estate since 2003, making the notion of a long-con against Trump not just implausible but laughably far-fetched.

The defense mounted by Trump's allies—that the president's signature always includes his first and last name—was quickly dismantled by the Wall Street Journal. Their presentation of instances where Trump signed with only his first name adds credence to the authenticity of the note. This isn't just an eyebrow-raiser; it's a blaring siren for anyone still clinging to the hoax narrative.

A Human Tragedy, Not Just Political Fodder

Lemire's commentary brings to the forefront the human tragedy at the heart of this scandal. The victims of Epstein's crimes, by giving a face to the suffering, remind us that this is not merely a political chess game. It's a stark revelation of crime and exploitation, a narrative that Trump and his allies seem keen to overshadow with their baseless claims of forgery and conspiracy.

The Unlikely Source of Truth

What's perhaps most striking is the origin of the renewed scrutiny: Rupert Murdoch's conservative media empire. Joe Scarborough's assertion that the Wall Street Journal—a bastion of conservative journalism—has taken the lead in reporting on this scandal is a telling deviation from the expected script. It's not the Democrats or any supposed left-wing media outlet that's keeping this story alive; it's Murdoch's own machinery.

This twist serves as a potent reminder that the truth, despite efforts to bury it under layers of political maneuvering and media manipulation, has a way of surfacing. The involvement of Murdoch's empire in driving the narrative forward underscores a significant shift in the landscape of conservative media—a shift that suggests a crack in the monolithic support Trump has historically enjoyed.

Conclusion: The Inescapable Truth

The Jeffrey Epstein scandal, with its ties to Donald Trump, is more than a political scandal; it's a mirror to the moral failings of those in power. Attempts to dismiss the mounting evidence as fabrications are not just futile; they're indicative of a deeper rot within the political establishment. As this saga continues to unfold, one thing becomes abundantly clear: no amount of denial can obscure the truth. The real hoax here is the narrative Trump and his allies are trying to sell to the American public. And like all cheap fabrications, it's bound to unravel.
