MAGA influencer uncorks bizarre theory Obamas hijacked Netflix's smash hit

MAGA influencer Jack Posobiec suggested that former President Barack Obama has been working behind the scenes to insert gay propaganda into Netflix’s hit show, “Stranger Things.”

Posobiec spoke out about his theory in a segment on Real America’s Voice on Friday, following Netflix’s announcement that it would acquire Warner Bros.

“This would become this massive behemoth taking up so much of the audience base,” Posobiec explained. “Why do they want to do that? Well, it’s very simple because all the way back in 2018, Netflix signed a huge deal with who? Barack and Michelle Obama, and then they put Susan Rice on the board of Netflix.”

“Does anyone remember around 2018?” he continued. “Where shows that were good, all of a sudden became super woke. You needed LGBT themes and everything. You needed racial themes and anything. Why did that happen? Why? All you have to do is go back and look that it was 2018 was a year when Barack Obama and Michelle Obama signed a massive deal with Netflix.”

Posobiec noted that the first two seasons of “Stranger Things” aired before the deal with the Obamas. Following that deal, the MAGA influencer outlandishly claimed the show became “pedo programming.”

“I’m angry, I’m outraged because, you know, you’re seeing this stuff where Netflix, which has just been an absolute atrocity when it’s like a war crime in terms of, you know, entertainment media pushing wokeness. It’s one of the biggest propaganda pushers of wokeness, of lies,” he remarked. “Again, another Netflix show, gayness injected in everything for kids.”

“There’s the ‘Stranger Things’ piece and the WB piece,” Posobiec said. “’Stranger Things,’ now essentially gay Darth Vader and gay Luke Skywalker. I think it is apparent that that was probably not the intention in season one, and they have gone back to have created this. That was the pre-Obama Netflix.”

“I’m sorry, like, you just can’t look at the first two seasons of ‘Stranger Things’... were done prior to Obama’s involvement with Netflix and like the Obamas and Susan Rice the whole enterprise and then suddenly season three, season four just get a little bit weirder,” he said.

Posobiec’s bizarre rant continued, claiming the show continued to get “a little bit gayer” in later seasons before ultimately comparing season five to “Hentai Japanese tentacle porn.”

Our Analysis:

The Incoherent Logic of Jack Posobiec’s Claims on Netflix and Obama

In an era where conspiracy theories often gain more traction than the truth, Jack Posobiec’s recent comments on Real America’s Voice serve as a prime example of how far-fetched and disconnected from reality these narratives can become. Posobiec suggested that former President Barack Obama has been engineering the insertion of “gay propaganda” into Netflix’s popular series, Stranger Things. This claim not only stretches credulity but also underscores a troubling trend of attributing societal shifts in representation and inclusivity to nefarious political agendas.

The Convoluted Conspiracy

Posobiec’s argument is constructed on a shaky foundation that presupposes a direct line of influence from the Obamas’ deal with Netflix to the supposed increase in LGBTQ+ themes within Netflix shows, specifically Stranger Things. The deal, inked in 2018, was indeed significant, but Posobiec’s leap to labeling subsequent content changes as “pedo programming” is not only baseless but dangerously inflammatory.

The Misinterpretation of Wokeness

The term “woke,” once a call to awareness about social injustices, has been co-opted and distorted by commentators like Posobiec to criticize any inclusion of LGBTQ+ themes or racial diversity. His assertion that Netflix has become “an absolute atrocity” and akin to a “war crime” for promoting what he perceives as “wokeness” is a gross exaggeration that ignores the broader cultural demand for more inclusive and representative media.

The Flawed Logic on Stranger Things

Posobiec’s critique of Stranger Things evolving to include “gay Darth Vader and gay Luke Skywalker” narratives is not only a bizarre misrepresentation but also highlights a misunderstanding of character development and thematic depth. To claim that the series’ exploration of identity and growth is somehow a post-Obama conspiracy is to ignore the organic evolution of storytelling and the complex world-building that the Duffer Brothers have consistently demonstrated.

The Dangerous Undercurrents of Such Claims

Beyond the absurdity of Posobiec’s claims lies a more sinister intent: to demonize LGBTQ+ representation as something manipulative and harmful. By comparing later seasons of Stranger Things to “Hentai Japanese tentacle porn,” Posobiec not only engages in hyperbolic rhetoric but also perpetuates harmful stereotypes about LGBTQ+ narratives being inherently sexual or inappropriate for younger audiences.

Conclusion

In dissecting Posobiec’s argument, it becomes clear that his narrative is less about genuine concern for media influence and more about stoking fear and animosity towards LGBTQ+ inclusivity. The transformation of Stranger Things and similar content reflects a natural progression towards more diverse and representative storytelling, not a shadowy plot orchestrated by political figures. Posobiec’s comments are a stark reminder of the importance of critical thinking and the responsibility of commentators to not distort societal progress for sensationalist or divisive ends.
