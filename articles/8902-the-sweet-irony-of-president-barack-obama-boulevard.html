The Sweet Irony of President Barack Obama Boulevard

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosNovember 8, 20251 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Picture it: a gleaming gold facade, towering over midtown Manhattan, where tourists snap photos of a name synonymous with ego and excess. Now imagine the address: 725 President Barack Obama Blvd. The poetic justice would be almost too perfect to bear.

The meme circulating online hit a nerve—and a funny bone. It suggests that New York City’s new mayor, Zohran Mamdani, should make his first official act the renaming of 57th Street in front of Trump Tower to honor Barack Obama. The idea is both hilarious and symbolically loaded: a calm, dignified counterpoint to the tantrum-prone chaos that once lived inside that gilded monument.

Think about it. Every letter, every package, every business card emanating from Trump Tower would carry the name of the man Trump spent eight years obsessing over and four years trying to erase. The irony would hum through the marble halls like a well-tuned jazz riff.

Beyond the humor, though, there’s something deeply American in this notion of symbolic reclamation. Cities rename streets all the time—to honor civil rights leaders, to right historical wrongs, to remind future generations who actually built the nation. Replacing a name tied to greed and grievance with one tied to intelligence and grace would be more than a prank; it would be an act of civic therapy.

Trump once declared Fifth Avenue his kingdom. Maybe it’s time New York declared that the city belongs to everyone else.

And so, when the GPS of history recalculates, it might just say: “Turn left onto President Barack Obama Boulevard.”
