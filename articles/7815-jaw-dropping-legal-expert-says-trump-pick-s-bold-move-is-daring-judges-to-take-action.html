A legal expert said Wednesday that interim U.S. attorney Lindsey Halligan — despite calls for her to resign — has continued her role.

Legal expert Lisa Rubin told MS NOW’s Ana Cabrera that Halligan has not left her position while pressure mounts for her to step down.

“Lindsey Halligan is still in her job and she is signing, or rather, lying,” Rubin said. “Prosecutors are signing criminal filings in the Eastern District of Virginia in her name, which is typical.”

Those filings have still said Lindsey Halligan, U.S. attorney and special attorney, but it’s not the same label Attorney General Pam Bondi gave her in late October, Rubin explained.

“When asked to justify this, the Justice Department has said that its office of legal counsel gave it an opinion that — and wait for this — because it’s kind of jaw-dropping, because Judge Cameron Curry and both of her orders in the [James] Comey and [Letitia] James case did not expressly say Lindsey Halligan needed to be removed, then it is totally fine for Lindsey Halligan to continue in her role as interim U.S. attorney, notwithstanding the fact that Judge Curry ruled that her appointment in that capacity was entirely illegitimate and unlawful,” Rubin said.

Rubin described what the bold move could mean.

“So it’s almost as if she is daring the Judges of the Eastern District of Virginia to take up their own prerogative under the statute and appoint someone of their own choosing that would force a fight, though Anna, between the judges of the district and the person that they choose on the one hand, and the Department of justice, which is still aligning itself with Lindsay Halligan on the other,” Rubin added. “And that’s why you have situations like The New York Times reported on yesterday, with Senior Judge Leonie Brinkema saying the right result here is just for Halligan to resign.”

Our Analysis:

Analysis of Legal Expert’s Commentary on U.S. Attorney Lindsey Halligan’s Position

In a recent discussion on MS NOW, legal expert Lisa Rubin provided insights into the controversial situation surrounding interim U.S. attorney Lindsey Halligan. Despite the clamor for her resignation amid legal challenges, Halligan remains steadfast in her role, a decision that not only defies expectations but also raises questions about the legal boundaries and the integrity of the Justice Department’s operations.

Continuation Amidst Controversy

Rubin’s revelation that Lindsey Halligan is “still in her job” despite the pressure to resign points to a troubling stubbornness within the Justice Department. The fact that criminal filings in the Eastern District of Virginia are being signed in Halligan’s name as if it’s business as usual, despite a cloud of illegitimacy hanging over her appointment, is a blatant disregard for legal norms and procedures. This action is not just about administrative persistence; it’s a bold statement of defiance against judicial oversight.

A Dubious Justification

The Justice Department’s reliance on a technicality—that Judge Cameron Curry’s orders did not explicitly demand Halligan’s removal—is a jaw-dropping maneuver in legal obfuscation. It’s akin to finding a loophole in a law meant to protect the public and exploiting it for personal gain. The department’s argument that Halligan’s continuance in her role is “totally fine” underlines a dangerous precedent where explicit judicial directives are required to rectify what is clearly recognized as an “illegitimate and unlawful” appointment. It’s a mockery of the legal system’s foundational principles.

Daring the Judiciary

Rubin’s analysis suggests that Halligan’s persistence in office is not just about clinging to power; it’s a challenge to the judiciary’s authority. By not stepping down, Halligan and, by extension, the Justice Department, are essentially daring the Judges of the Eastern District of Virginia to enforce their statutory prerogative and appoint a replacement. This situation sets the stage for a potential showdown between the judiciary and the Justice Department, an unprecedented conflict that could have far-reaching implications for the balance of power within the federal government.

The Call for Resignation

The call by Senior Judge Leonie Brinkema for Halligan’s resignation is not just a plea for sanity; it’s a demand for respect towards the judiciary and the legal process. Brinkema’s stance underscores the gravity of the situation and the clear path forward: Halligan’s resignation. It’s a testament to the principle that no individual’s position is above the law, and adherence to judicial rulings is not optional.

Conclusion

The saga of Lindsey Halligan is more than a legal anomaly; it’s a glaring symptom of systemic issues within the U.S. Justice Department. The willingness to sidestep judicial authority and exploit loopholes for administrative convenience erodes trust in legal institutions and undermines the very fabric of the rule of law. As this situation unfolds, it becomes increasingly clear that the resolution requires not just legal intervention but a return to ethical governance and respect for judicial oversight. The defiance against such principles is not only alarming but a call to action for those who value the integrity of the justice system.
