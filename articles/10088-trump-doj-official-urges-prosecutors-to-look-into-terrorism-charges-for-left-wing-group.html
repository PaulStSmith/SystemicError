Trump DOJ official urges prosecutors to look into terrorism charges for left-wing group

A senior official in President Donald Trump’s Department of Justice has ordered federal prosecutors around the country to investigate a group funded by billionaire Democratic donor George Soros.

Trump has demanded that Soros, a longtime villain to conservatives, be thrown in prison, and the senior DOJ official’s directive lists possible charges – from arson to material support of terrorism – that prosecutors could file, according to a copy of the document viewed by The New York Times, which noted the memo suggests department officials are targeting individuals on the president’s orders.

Aakash Singh, a lawyer in the office of Deputy Attorney General Todd Blanche, issued the directive Monday, suggesting racketeering, wire fraud and other charges against the Open Society Foundations funded by Soros, supported by evidence from a recent report by the conservative watchdog group Capital Research Center, which monitors liberal money in politics.

The watchdog report claims the group “has poured over $80 million into groups tied to terrorism or extremist violence,” citing its support for the Palestinian human rights group al-Haq, which has been critical of Israel and which the Israeli government declared in 2022 was a front for terrorist activity.

The Open Society Foundations denounced the accusations as “politically motivated attacks on civil society meant to silence speech” and defended its work as “peaceful and lawful” and aimed at “strengthening democracy and upholding constitutional freedoms.”

“Organizations should not be attacked for carrying out their missions or expressing their values in support of the communities they serve,” the organization stated. “We reject attempts to exploit political violence to mischaracterize our good work or restrict our fundamental freedoms, like freedom of speech and the freedom to give.”

A DOJ spokesman defended Singh’s directive.

“This D.O.J., along with our hard-working and dedicated U.S. attorneys, will always prioritize public safety and investigate organizations that conspire to commit acts of violence or other federal violations of law,” said Chad Gilmartin, a spokesman for the Justice Department.

The president has swiftly moved in recent weeks to pursue criminal charges against his perceived enemies, including those related to the cases against him after his first term, urging Attorney General Pam Bondi and U.S. attorneys under her direction to obtain indictments against former FBI Director James Comey and New York Attorney General Letitia James, although prosecutors have expressed doubts about evidence of their alleged wrongdoing.

“After the killing of the right-wing activist Charlie Kirk in Utah this month, the president threatened to use the levers of government to silence liberal protesters and donors to progressive groups, including Mr. Soros,” the Times reported.

“Mr. Soros began his global grant network, now known as the Open Society Foundations, decades ago to fund democratic initiatives around the world, particularly in communist and formerly communist countries,” the newspaper added. “In the 1990s, the organization expanded its work to the United States. It provides grants to groups that work for human rights, democracy and equity, but Mr. Trump and some Republicans contend, without providing evidence, that it is a shadowy network promoting civil unrest, violent protests and property destruction. Liberals say the assertions are falsehoods aimed at stifling dissent.”

Our Analysis:

The Politicization of Justice: A Critical Analysis

In a move that reeks of political vendettas and the weaponization of federal agencies, a senior official in President Donald Trump’s Department of Justice (DOJ) has blatantly ordered federal prosecutors to target a group funded by billionaire George Soros, a figure demonized by conservatives for years. This directive, as reported by The New York Times, not only exposes the dangerous precedent of pursuing legal action against political adversaries on shaky grounds but also highlights the Trump administration’s relentless attack on democratic norms and freedom of speech.

The Soros Saga: A Convenient Boogeyman

George Soros, through his Open Society Foundations, has been a significant philanthropic force, advocating for human rights, democracy, and equity globally. The work of these foundations, especially in authoritarian regimes and transitioning democracies, has been crucial in promoting freedom and justice. However, in a stark departure from these noble pursuits, the Trump administration, devoid of concrete evidence, has chosen to vilify Soros and his initiatives, painting them as orchestrators of civil unrest and violence. This narrative, unfounded and dangerously misleading, serves not just as an attack on Soros but as a broader assault on the very principles of philanthropy and activism.

A Dangerous Directive

Aakash Singh, an official in the office of Deputy Attorney General Todd Blanche, issued a memo suggesting severe charges, including racketeering and wire fraud, against the Open Society Foundations, based on a report by the conservative Capital Research Center. This directive is alarming on multiple fronts. First, it blatantly aligns DOJ actions with the president’s personal vendettas. Second, it relies on a report from a politically motivated entity known for its conservative bias, raising serious questions about the impartiality and credibility of the evidence being used to justify such severe accusations.

The Assault on Freedom

The Open Society Foundations’ response to these accusations underscores a critical point - the attacks they are facing are politically motivated efforts to stifle free speech and weaken civil society. By labeling legitimate philanthropic activities as criminal, the Trump administration is not only targeting Soros but is also sending a chilling message to all activists and organizations advocating for social justice, democracy, and human rights.

A Pattern of Political Persecution

This incident is not an isolated case but part of a disturbing trend under the Trump administration, where the president has increasingly sought to use the levers of government to pursue his political enemies. The rapid move to press charges against perceived adversaries, including former FBI Director James Comey and New York Attorney General Letitia James, without substantial evidence, is a testament to a governance style that places personal vendettas above the rule of law and due process.

The Real Victims

In this politicized crusade against Soros and the broader attempt to silence dissent, the real victims are the principles of justice, democracy, and the rule of law. When the government’s highest law enforcement agency becomes a tool for political retribution, it undermines public trust in the justice system and erodes the foundational pillars of democracy.

Conclusion

The DOJ’s directive to target George Soros and his foundations is not just an attack on one individual or entity but a frontal assault on the freedoms and democratic values that define America. It sets a perilous precedent where political affiliation and activism become grounds for legal persecution, signaling a descent into authoritarian tactics unworthy of a democratic nation. The need for a robust, independent judiciary and a vigilant civil society has never been more critical to safeguard the nation’s democratic institutions and freedoms.
