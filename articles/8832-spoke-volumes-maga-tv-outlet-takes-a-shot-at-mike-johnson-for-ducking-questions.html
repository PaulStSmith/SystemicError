'Spoke volumes': MAGA TV outlet takes a shot at Mike Johnson for ducking questions

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosNovember 11, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Our Analysis:A Symphony of Silence: Unpacking Speaker Johnson’s Walkout and LindellTV’s Critique

LindellTV, the pro-Trump, conservative online streaming and media platform founded by election-denier MyPillow CEO Mike Lindell, criticized Speaker of the House Mike Johnson (R-LA) on Monday after he refused to take questions about a possible end to the historic government shutdown, Mediaite reports.

Speaking on Capitol Hill, Johnson made a quick statement to the press.

“There’ll be long days and long nights here for the foreseeable future to make up for all this lost time that was imposed upon us,” Johnson said. “I want to close in with something that’s important. It’s important to say that this is genuine. I mean this sincerely: We applaud the seven Senate Democrats and one independent senator who did the right thing.”

Johnson left without taking questions.

LindellTV, on its official X account, posted scathing criticism of the House speaker.

The post reads: “Press Stunned as Speaker Johnson Walks Out Without Taking Questions.”

“In a rare move, [Speaker Johnson] exited his Monday morning remarks without taking a single question - leaving reporters stunned and scrambling down the hallway after him. According to gallery rules, the Speaker is expected to take at least one question. Johnson has never skipped Q&A before. After weeks of daily shutdown updates and follow up questions, the silence spoke volumes.”

CNN’s Manu Raju also noted that Johnson’s silence was rare, saying on X, “Speaker Johnson refused to take questions after advising a press conference in House radio-tv gallery where he talked shutdown. When holding pressers in the gallery, members are required to take at least one question.”

Our Analysis:

A Symphony of Silence: Unpacking Speaker Johnson’s Walkout and LindellTV’s Critique

In an era where political theater often overshadows the substantive issues at stake, Speaker of the House Mike Johnson’s (R-LA) recent press conference maneuver has lit a match under the already smoldering embers of controversy. With the historic government shutdown serving as a backdrop, Johnson’s decision to eschew the traditional Q&A session in favor of a mic drop moment has sent ripples through the media landscape. Enter LindellTV, the pro-Trump bastion helmed by the ever-controversial MyPillow CEO Mike Lindell, known more for his election denial antics than for nuanced political analysis. However, even a broken clock is right twice a day, and in this instance, LindellTV’s critique of Johnson’s press conference exit warrants a deeper dive.

The Critique Unpacked

LindellTV’s post on X (formerly known as Twitter) pulls no punches, branding Speaker Johnson’s departure as both stunning and unprecedented. The choice of words here, particularly “stunned,” evokes a sense of dramatic disbelief, painting a vivid picture of a press corps left agape as Johnson turned tail. But beyond the theatrical, the critique centers on a fundamental expectation of democratic governance: accountability. By highlighting that Johnson “exited his Monday morning remarks without taking a single question,” LindellTV taps into the broader discourse on political transparency and the duties of elected officials to engage with the press as proxies for the public.

A Break with Tradition, or a New Norm?

The article notes that, according to gallery rules, the Speaker is expected to take at least one question. This expectation isn’t merely procedural; it’s a linchpin of democratic accountability, offering a rare opportunity for the press to directly challenge and scrutinize public officials. Johnson’s departure from this norm, especially amidst a governmental crisis as severe as a historic shutdown, is not just a faux pas; it’s a signal. It suggests a willingness, or perhaps a calculated strategy, to evade direct scrutiny at a moment when the public’s need for clarity and leadership is most acute.

CNN’s Manu Raju’s commentary underscores the rarity of Johnson’s silence, reinforcing the notion that this was not business as usual. This deviation prompts a pressing question: Are we witnessing the forging of a new norm where elected officials feel empowered to bypass the mechanisms of accountability altogether?

LindellTV’s Motivations and the Bigger Picture

While LindellTV’s critique may strike a chord with advocates of press freedom and governmental transparency, it’s crucial to consider the source. LindellTV’s allegiance to pro-Trump ideologies and its foundation by a figure as divisive as Mike Lindell injects a layer of complexity into its criticisms. Is the platform genuinely concerned with the principles of accountability and transparency, or is it seizing an opportunity to lambast a figure who, in this instance, failed to play to its tune?

In dissecting Johnson’s silent exit and LindellTV’s response, we’re reminded of the multifaceted nature of political critique. It’s a reminder that valid criticisms can emerge from quarters with whom we share little ideological common ground. However, the ultimate litmus test for such critiques isn’t their source but their adherence to the principles of truth, accountability, and the relentless pursuit of a more transparent and responsive governance.

In conclusion, Johnson’s press conference walkout and LindellTV’s ensuing criticism serve as a microcosm of the broader challenges facing modern American democracy. Amidst the cacophony of the 24/7 news cycle and the polarized echo chambers of social media, the imperative for elected officials to engage openly and honestly with the press has never been more critical. As observers and participants in this unfolding drama, it’s our responsibility to demand more from those in power, scrutinizing their actions (and inactions) with a critical eye, irrespective of the political stripes from which the critique emanates.
