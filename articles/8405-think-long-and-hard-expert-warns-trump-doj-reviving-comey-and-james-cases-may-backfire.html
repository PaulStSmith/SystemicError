'Think long and hard': Expert warns Trump DOJ reviving Comey and James cases may backfire

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosNovember 25, 20254 min read

A federal judge tossed Trump’s indictments against former FBI Director James Comey and New York Attorney General Letitia James, and trying to continue pursuing these charges could backfire, a former prosecutor warned Monday.

Now that the Trump administration’s indictments have been tossed, the administration can appeal or bring the charges again under other prosecutors — but they should consider just letting it go, argued former Criminal Division Assistant Attorney General Leslie Caldwell on MS NOW. The whole case could become a much bigger mess if they try to revive it, she said.

The judge in this case ruled that Lindsey Halligan, the acting U.S. attorney hastily installed by the Justice Department after the previous U.S. attorney failed to find a credible basis to move forward with the prosecutions of Trump’s critics, was not lawfully appointed, and thus the indictments were null and void. Halligan, who made a series of blunders throughout the process, now faces the risk of disbarment in the view of some legal experts.

“So what happens next?” asked anchor Katy Tur. “I mean, we presume that the government is going to want — can we presume the government is going to want to refile these charges?”

“You know, I think it depends on what the point of filing the charges in the first place was,” said Caldwell. “They’d have to find a prosecutor who was willing to do it, which I’m sure they could probably do. They would be able to find somebody, perhaps one of the two North Carolina prosecutors, because the judge did rule that. She denied the government’s motion to make a dismissal with prejudice, so they can bring it back if they can have a valid attorney, whether they actually will or not.”

However, she continued, “there’s also a pending vindictive prosecution motion. And I think given the circumstances surrounding the case, they really should think long and hard about whether they want to bring this case back again. And they may have already achieved their main goal, which was to shame Comey and James.”

“I don’t know that they’ll actually feel shame, but I think that was certainly a large driver of the indictment in the first place,” she added.

Our Analysis:

Trump’s Legal Quagmire: A Case of Political Vendettas?

In a dramatic twist that seems ripped straight from the pages of a legal thriller, a federal judge has decisively tossed out indictments against former FBI Director James Comey and New York Attorney General Letitia James, indictments pursued by the Trump administration. This decision not only serves as a resounding rebuke of the administration’s tactics but also highlights a tumultuous saga of political vendettas masquerading as legal proceedings.

The Indictment Debacle: A Misguided Mission

At the heart of this legal debacle is the attempt by the Trump administration to weaponize the judicial system against its critics, a move that has backfired spectacularly. The judge ruled that Lindsey Halligan, the acting U.S. attorney responsible for the indictments, was not lawfully appointed, rendering the indictments null and void. This ruling isn’t merely a procedural hiccup; it’s a damning indictment of the administration’s disregard for legal norms and standards.

The Halligan Fiasco: A Catalogue of Blunders

Lindsey Halligan’s tenure, marked by a series of blunders, now faces the ignominious prospect of disbarment, according to some legal experts. This outcome underscores the peril of politicizing the appointment of key legal positions, a strategy that not only undermines the integrity of the judicial process but also jeopardizes the careers of those enlisted to carry out these misguided missions.

The Path Forward: A Legal Morass

As former Criminal Division Assistant Attorney General Leslie Caldwell articulates, the Trump administration stands at a legal crossroads. They could attempt to refile the charges, a path fraught with legal and ethical pitfalls, especially in light of the pending vindictive prosecution motion. This motion, if proven, would further expose the charges as nothing more than a thinly veiled attempt to shame and discredit Trump’s critics, a motive Caldwell suggests was the primary impetus behind the indictments.

A Political Strategy Unraveling

This entire episode raises profound questions about the use of the criminal justice system to settle political scores. The administration’s determination to pursue Comey and James, despite the dubious legal foundation for such action, speaks volumes about its priorities and its willingness to bend the rules to its will.

The Bigger Picture: A Warning Sign

This case serves as a stark warning of the dangers inherent in allowing political considerations to drive prosecutorial decisions. The implications of such actions extend far beyond the individuals involved, threatening the very principles of justice and fairness that underpin the American legal system.

In conclusion, the dismissal of the indictments against Comey and James should not be viewed merely as a legal setback for the Trump administration but as a cautionary tale about the perils of politicizing the judiciary. As this saga unfolds, it will undoubtedly serve as a litmus test for the resilience of legal norms and the rule of law in the face of political pressures.
