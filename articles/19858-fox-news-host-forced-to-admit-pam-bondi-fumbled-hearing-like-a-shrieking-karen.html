Fox News host forced to admit Pam Bondi fumbled hearing: 'Like a shrieking Karen!'

Attorney General Pam Bondi has faced extraordinary blowback over her performance at last week’s congressional hearing, including criticism from prominent conservative figures such as Fox News’ Lisa Kennedy Montgomery, who recently joined the dogpile.

“She comes off like a shrieking Karen!” Kennedy said on Friday during an appearance on Fox News‘ “The Five,” speaking of Bondi’s frequent heated exchanges with lawmakers.

Bondi testified before the House Judiciary Committee last Wednesday and faced a barrage of questions about Jeffrey Epstein. The questions largely centered around her own agency’s failure to release Epstein-related files in accordance with the law, and around her agency’s failure to prosecute a single potential co-conspirator of Epstein.

Bondi frequently shouted at lawmakers during the hearing, and on more than one occasion, awkwardly pivoted from a question about Epstein to the strong performance of the stock market, a pivot that’s gone on to receive bipartisan condemnation.

Kennedy appears to be among those on the right criticizing Bondi’s performance.

“When she’s this emotional, it looks like she’s lost a little bit of the edge, and she seems very, very unpersuasive,” Kennedy said. “It seems like the job is consuming her. She needs to turn it down a little bit.”

Bondi’s performance was viewed as so poor that one political expert – Mohamad Aly Elleithee, executive director of the Institute of Politics and Public Service at Georgetown University – suspected that she may very well be on “shaky ground” with President DonaldTrump, who he noted is acutely aware of potential political liabilities.

Our Analysis:

The Bondi Breakdown: A Study in Deflection and Desperation

In a spectacle that can only be described as both bewildering and deeply troubling, Attorney General Pam Bondi has managed to turn a congressional hearing into a circus of evasion and emotional outbursts. The subject at hand, the deeply disturbing case of Jeffrey Epstein, seemed to be the least of Bondi’s concerns as she opted for a strategy that can best be summarized as yell, deflect, and distract.

The Critique from Within

It’s not just the usual suspects from the left who are taking Bondi to task. Figures from the right, such as Fox News’ Lisa Kennedy Montgomery, have joined the fray, with Kennedy notably branding Bondi as coming off like a “shrieking Karen”. This is not a petty jab but a pointed critique of Bondi’s demeanor and approach during the hearing. Kennedy’s observation highlights a fundamental flaw in Bondi’s performance: an inability to remain composed and persuasive under scrutiny. When even those within your ideological camp can’t defend your methods, it’s a glaring sign that the wheels are coming off.

A Masterclass in Evasion

Bondi’s frequent and loud attempts to pivot the discussion from Epstein to the stock market are nothing short of astonishing. It’s a move that reeks of desperation and reveals a profound disrespect for the gravity of the situation. Epstein’s case is a matter of serious criminal justice and moral failure, yet Bondi’s strategy was to treat it like an inconvenient topic to be drowned out by unrelated economic bragging. This wasn’t just a failure to communicate; it was a failure to acknowledge the very real harm and injustice at the heart of Epstein’s crimes.

The Emotional Toll

Kennedy’s remarks about Bondi being “emotional” and appearing “unpersuasive” strike at the core of the issue. The job of an Attorney General, especially in a public and contentious setting like a congressional hearing, is to be clear, calm, and authoritative. Bondi’s approach was the antithesis of this, raising questions about her suitability for the role and the impact of such high-stakes environments on her ability to perform her duties effectively.

On Shaky Ground

Mohamad Aly Elleithee’s comments about Bondi potentially being on “shaky ground” with President Trump underscore a critical aspect of political life under his administration: performance matters. Trump, with his keen eye for public perception and loyalty, is unlikely to take kindly to an ally who becomes a liability. Bondi’s inability to handle the Epstein questions with finesse not only undermines her own credibility but also poses a risk to the wider administration’s image.

Conclusion

Bondi’s performance is emblematic of a broader issue within certain political circles: the prioritization of loyalty and deflection over accountability and transparency. Her failure to address the Epstein case adequately, coupled with her emotional volatility, not only detracts from the seriousness of the hearing but also undermines public trust in the institutions meant to protect and serve justice.

In the end, Bondi’s congressional hearing debacle serves as a stark reminder of the importance of competence and composure in public office. It’s not enough to shout down the opposition or switch topics when the heat is on. The American public deserves, and must demand, better.
