'I'm the bum?': Kimmel hits back at Trump after 'get him off the air' post from president

Jimmy Kimmel has hit back at Donald Trump after the president called for the talk show host to be taken off the air.

In a furious post made to Truth Social on November 20, Trump wrote, “Why does ABC Fake News keep Jimmy Kimmel, a man with NO TALENT and VERY POOR TELEVISION RATINGS, on the air? Why do the TV Syndicates put up with it? Also, totally biased coverage. Get the bum off the air!!!”

Kimmel has since responded to the president’s raging Truth Social post on Jimmy Kimmel Live. The president has put pressure on Kimmel previously this year, with the 58-year-old’s show briefly suspended from September 17 to September 22.

The talk show host has since addressed the Truth Social post during his opening monologue. Kimmel joked that “the angry orange posted” about his show. He added, “I’m the bum? I’m the bum? And he posted this, this is interesting, at 12:49AM, 11 minutes after the show ended on the East Coast, which is nice.”

“He watches us live, hi Mr. President. How are ya? Thanks for watching us on TV instead of on YouTube. We appreciate that, and I’ll tell ya, it’s viewers like you who keep us on the air, ironically.”

“It’s also sweet that, even in the middle of the biggest sex scandal in the history of the American presidency, he takes precious time on the toilet to post about our show. He keeps saying we have bad ratings and you should listen to him, because if anyone knows about bad ratings, it’s that guy.”

The president has made few allies in the late-night television sphere, previously posting about Stephen Colbert, whose Late Show will end next season.

Trump targeted Kimmel and the Live! show with a post to Truth Social showing the president is furious at the entertainer. Kimmel had taken a jab at Trump during the show’s opening monologue and referred to the signing of the Epstein files bill.

He said, “We are carefully following the path of Hurricane Epstein right now. It’s a category five, it’s expected to make landfall sometime very soon, we are now one step closer to answering the question ‘what did the president know?’ and ‘how old were these women when he knew them?’”

Our Analysis:

The Art of Distraction: Trump vs. Kimmel

In the latest episode of the ongoing saga between former President Donald Trump and late-night talk show host Jimmy Kimmel, we witness yet another classic maneuver from Trump’s playbook: the art of distraction. Trump’s recent outburst on Truth Social, demanding Kimmel’s removal from the airwaves, is more than just a spat between a politician and a television personality. It’s a calculated move, steeped in irony and hypocrisy, and Kimmel’s response highlights the absurdity of it all.

Trump’s Attack: A Closer Look

Trump’s rant on Truth Social, calling Kimmel a “man with NO TALENT and VERY POOR TELEVISION RATINGS,” is boldly ironic, coming from someone whose presidency was plagued by low approval ratings and who has a notorious history of failed business ventures. His call to action, “Get the bum off the air!!!”, is not just an attack on Kimmel but an attack on the freedom of speech and the press. It’s as if Trump is attempting to dictate who can and cannot speak in the public sphere, a notion that is antithetical to the very foundations of democracy.

Kimmel’s Retort: Truth Wrapped in Humor

Kimmel, never one to shy away from confrontation, addressed Trump’s late-night tirade with his signature blend of humor and truth. Mocking Trump as “the angry orange” and expressing faux-gratitude for his viewership, Kimmel turns Trump’s attempt at insult into a moment of levity. Yet, beneath the surface of these jokes lies a stinging critique of Trump’s priorities and his understanding of media ratings.

Kimmel’s reference to Trump’s timing – posting just minutes after his show ended – not only suggests Trump’s fixation on Kimmel’s content but also his peculiar priorities, choosing to attack a comedian amidst what Kimmel describes as “the biggest sex scandal in the history of the American presidency.” This juxtaposition is telling, highlighting Trump’s penchant for engaging with his critics, real or perceived, over addressing more pressing issues.

The Real Irony: Trump’s Critique of Ratings

Kimmel’s jab at Trump’s familiarity with “bad ratings” is more than just a clever retort; it’s a direct hit on Trump’s vulnerability. For a former president who was obsessed with ratings, whether it was his inauguration crowd size or approval polls, Trump’s criticism of Kimmel’s ratings reeks of hypocrisy. It’s as if Trump fails to see the irony in his obsession with television ratings mirroring his own struggles with unpopularity.

Conclusion: The Distraction Technique

Trump’s attack on Kimmel is emblematic of his broader strategy: distraction. By engaging in a public feud with a comedian, Trump diverts attention from more damning issues, like the aforementioned “biggest sex scandal.” Kimmel’s response, while humorous, serves as a reminder of the seriousness underlying this exchange. It’s not just about ratings or television shows; it’s about freedom of expression, the role of the media, and the priorities of public figures.

In this latest chapter of Trump vs. Kimmel, we are reminded that the battle is not just about laughs; it’s about highlighting truths, often hidden beneath layers of distraction and deflection. Kimmel, through his wit, manages to cut through the noise, reminding us of the importance of critical thinking and accountability, even (or especially) when it comes in the form of late-night comedy.
