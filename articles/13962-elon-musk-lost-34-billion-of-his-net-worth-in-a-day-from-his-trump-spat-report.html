Elon Musk lost $34 billion of his net worth in a day from his Trump spat: report

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosJune 7, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Our Analysis:The Billionaire Feud: Musk vs. Trump

Elon Musk speaks during the first cabinet meeting hosted by U.S. President Donald Trump, at the White House in Washington, DC, U.S., February 26, 2025. REUTERS/Brian Snyder

Tech billionaire Elon Musk's flagship businesses took a severe hit amid his public fight with President Donald Trump, as Tesla's stock slid over 14 points, and SpaceX fell almost 5 points. But it's even worse than that, Bloomberg reported, as all of this had a devastating impact on Musk's personal net worth.

"Minute by minute, post by post, Elon Musk’s very public, extremely online feud with President Donald Trump sliced into his vaunted status as the world’s richest person," reported Dylan Sloan and Tom Maloney. "The final damage at day’s end: $34 billion erased from his personal net worth, the second-largest loss ever in the history of the Bloomberg Billionaires Index of the 500 wealthiest people on the planet. The only bigger one: his own wipeout in November 2021."

Musk remains the wealthiest man on Earth at well over $300 billion. However, the losses today constitute an over 9 percent drop in his net worth, and well below his peak near half a trillion dollars at the start of the year.

The tech mogul for months has been one of Trump's closest allies, having contributed millions of dollars to his election campaign and essentially run large parts of the campaign operation himself, and heading up the administration's Department of Government Efficiency (DOGE) task force to purge the civil service and freeze government grants across the board.

However, as Musk has formally left his White House advisory position in recent weeks, tensions between him and Trump have risen.

Musk was reportedly enraged when Trump abruptly pulled the nomination of one of his close friends to serve as head of NASA, and more recently has railed against Trump's "big beautiful bill" to extend tax cuts and cut Medicaid and food stamps, asserting it makes the government even less fiscally solvent and undercuts what DOGE was trying to do.

As Trump has begun pushing back against Musk's criticism, Musk has thrown the kitchen sink at Trump, calling for his impeachment and implying that he is a child predator.

Our Analysis:

The Billionaire Feud: Musk vs. Trump

In what reads like a script out of a high-stakes political drama, tech billionaire Elon Musk finds himself in the throes of a public and costly spat with none other than President Donald Trump. The financial fallout from this feud is not just a blip on the radar; it's a storm that's wiped a staggering $34 billion off Musk's net worth, as reported by Bloomberg's Dylan Sloan and Tom Maloney. This isn't just a tale of rich men bickering; it's a saga that encapsulates the volatile intersection of tech, politics, and personal wealth.

The Financial Bloodbath

Let's dissect the ferocity of this financial hit. Musk, the mastermind behind Tesla and SpaceX, witnessed his companies' stocks plummet—Tesla by over 14 points and SpaceX nearly 5. This isn't pocket change we're talking about; it's a significant valuation erosion that underscores the high stakes of Musk's political entanglements. To put the $34 billion loss into perspective, it's the second-largest single-day drop in the Bloomberg Billionaires Index's history, trailing only behind another one of Musk's own financial decimations in November 2021. Despite this, Musk still sits atop the global wealth throne, but such volatility paints a picture of fragility at these astronomical heights of wealth.

A Political Chess Game Gone Awry

Musk's foray into the political arena, marked by his hefty contributions to Trump's campaign and his role in the so-called Department of Government Efficiency (DOGE), was a calculated move. However, this recent fallout suggests a miscalculation of epic proportions. The duo, once allies in reshaping government operations and policy, now find themselves at a bitter crossroads. Musk's departure from his White House advisory role was the first sign of trouble in paradise, but the feud escalated quickly with Musk's critical stance on Trump's policies and his audacious calls for impeachment, coupled with deeply personal accusations.

The Implications

What's at stake here goes beyond two billionaires locking horns. Musk's public rebuke of Trump's fiscal policies and the dramatic unraveling of their relationship spotlight a broader narrative of power, influence, and the repercussions of intertwining personal beliefs with business and political interests. Musk's aggressive stance against Trump, particularly his involvement in government efficiency initiatives contrasted with his criticism of fiscal irresponsibility, points to a complex interplay between ideological commitments and the practicalities of governance.

A Cautionary Tale

This saga serves as a cautionary tale about the risks of high-profile business leaders venturing too far into the political arena. The volatile nature of politics can turn allies into adversaries in the blink of an eye, with significant financial and reputational consequences. Musk's predicament is a vivid reminder that in the game of power, fortunes can change as swiftly as the direction of the political wind.

In essence, the Musk-Trump feud is more than just a clash of titans. It's a reflection of the turbulent times we live in, where the lines between technology, business, and politics are increasingly blurred. As this drama unfolds, one thing is clear: the fallout from such a public spat is not just personal, it's profoundly systemic, affecting stakeholders across the board—from investors to the average Joe watching from the sidelines. As we watch this saga unfold, the real question becomes: what will be the long-term ramifications of this high-stakes fallout on the intersection of business and politics? Only time will tell, but one thing is for certain—the ripples from this clash will be felt far and wide.
