'They're lying': Trump's niece says one GOP change is 'disastrous' for small businesses

By Paulo Santos

Donald Trump speaks to the media on board Air Force One . REUTERS/Evelyn Hockstein

A new ruling from the Donald Trump administration will be “disastrous” for small businesses, the president’s niece has warned.

Political commentator Mary Trump called out Republican representatives during an upload to her YouTube channel. The video, titled “Trump’s Global Humiliation Hits New Low”, criticised the administration for changing dates for free park entry. Trump’s birthday, June 16, is now listed as a day where members of the public can access national parks across the country for free, but MLK Day and Juneteenth were revoked, The Guardian reported.

This change in policy, Mary Trump says, is “racist”. But it also has an effect on small businesses across the country that the GOP should be doing more to protect, the political commentator noted.

She said, “Their admission prices have also been raised. Now, why is that a problem? Well, one, it makes people feel really unwelcome and it disincentivizes them to come to this country, let alone go to the national parks.”

“Also, how good do you think that is for the American economy? How good do you think that is for the local economies of these places? The small businesses in these places that rely heavily upon tourism. Whether the tourism of American citizens or people from other countries. This is a disaster for those small businesses and local communities.”

“So, once again, if any Republican tells you they care about the American working people or American small businesses, they are lying.” Mary Trump went further and suggested Trump had his attention elsewhere, with a “hostile takeover” of the Kennedy Center.

She said, “There’s a new level of humiliation that Donald engaged in last night, that would be the night that the first Kennedy Center Honors were held since his hostile takeover of the institution.”

“The entire event with honorees like Sylvester Stallone and one-hit-wonder Gloria Gaynor was a huge embarrassment’ making it infinitely worse however is that Donald chose to host the event himself.”

Trump would use the event as an opportunity to call out “miserable” people in the audience and around the world while also honoring Trump’s appointed “Hollywood ambassador” Stallone and rock band KISS.

Our Analysis:

Analysis: The Trump Administration’s Controversial Policy Change and Impacts on Small Businesses

In a move that has sparked widespread controversy and criticism, the Trump administration has decided to adjust the schedule for free entry to national parks, privileging the former president’s birthday over historically significant dates such as MLK Day and Juneteenth. This decision, as pointed out by Mary Trump, not only carries a racist undertone but also poses significant threats to the financial health of small businesses reliant on tourism.

Racial Implications and Economic Disasters

Firstly, the decision to revoke free park entry on MLK Day and Juneteenth, days commemorating critical milestones in the fight against racial injustice in America, sends a message that is both discriminatory and dismissive of America’s ongoing struggle with racial equity. Labeling this move as racist, Mary Trump sheds light on the broader implications of such policy decisions on America’s social fabric.

However, beyond the evident racial connotations, this policy shift has a dire economic dimension. By increasing admission prices and removing free entry on days that could attract a substantial number of visitors, the administration directly impacts the influx of tourists, both domestic and international. This, in turn, can have a catastrophic effect on small businesses operating in and around these national parks. These businesses, often heavily reliant on the tourism season for their survival, face an uncertain future with potentially reduced customer foot traffic.

The GOP’s Stance on American Small Businesses

Mary Trump’s critique extends beyond the immediate family to the Republican Party at large, challenging the oft-repeated claim that the GOP stands for the interests of American workers and small businesses. This policy change serves as a case in point that Republican policies under the Trump administration may not align with the welfare of small businesses as professed. It underscores a disconnect between the party’s rhetoric on economic support for small enterprises and the tangible outcomes of its policies.

The Kennedy Center “Hostile Takeover”

Adding another layer to the critique, Mary Trump lambasts what she describes as a “hostile takeover” of the Kennedy Center by Donald Trump. The reorientation of the Kennedy Center Honors to include figures like Sylvester Stallone and the rock band KISS, coupled with Trump’s personalized hosting of the event, is portrayed as an egregious misstep. This move is critiqued not just for its potential lapse in cultural judgment but also for illustrating Trump’s prioritization of personal interests and connections over merit or tradition.

Conclusion: A Pattern of Controversy and Self-Interest

The changes in national park entry policy and the manipulation of cultural institutions like the Kennedy Center highlight a broader pattern of governance under the Trump administration, characterized by controversial decisions that often serve a narrow set of interests. Mary Trump’s sharp critique underscores not only the immediate negative impacts on small businesses and the economy but also the longer-term implications for America’s social and cultural landscape.

In conclusion, these policy shifts and actions reveal a governance style marred by self-interest, exclusion, and a disregard for the economic wellbeing of small businesses. The critique laid out by Mary Trump serves as a poignant reminder of the stark contrast between public rhetoric and actual policy impact under the Trump administration.
