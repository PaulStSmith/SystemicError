Jimmy Kimmel has pointed out a problem in the MAGA fury kindled by Bad Bunny’s Super Bowl halftime show.

The Grammy winner infuriated the MAGA faithful and Donald Trump, though talk show host Kimmel believes it is ridiculous to see so much outrage over the halftime show. Speaking during the opening monologue of his talk show, Kimmel suggested the continuing anger over the Bad Bunny performance is ridiculous.

He said, “The MAGAs are still exploding with rage over the Bad Bunny halftime show. A Republican Congressman from Missouri is threatening to call in the FCC to report profanities that either may or may not have been uttered by Bad Bunny - he has no idea.”

Mark Alford, the GOP rep for Missouri, said the Bad Bunny performance could be “much worse than the Janet Jackson wardrobe malfunction” in 2004.

Kimmel hit back, saying, “If only there were some way to translate that mysterious code Bad Bunny used, we could get to the bottom of this once and for all.

“Let me see if I have this straight. First you’re angry because you couldn’t understand what Bad Bunny was saying, now you’re mad because you do? Okay.

“There are a lot of people in the MAGA news media still making a giant deal out of this halftime show, which to my eyes was just some people singing and dancing, but so many of them are so mad, I think they might need to be medicated.”

Grammy Award winner Bad Bunny sparked much derision from the president Sunday night, who signed off on a furious statement on Truth Social. Trump wrote, “The Super Bowl Halftime Show is absolutely terrible, one of the worst, EVER! It makes no sense, is an affront to the Greatness of America, and doesn’t represent our standards of Success, Creativity, or Excellence.

“Nobody understands a word this guy is saying, and the dancing is disgusting, especially for young children that are watching from throughout the U.S.A., and all over the World. This ‘Show’ is just a ‘slap in the face’ to our Country, which is setting new standards and records every single day — including the Best Stock Market and 401(k)s in History!

“There is nothing inspirational about this mess of a Halftime Show and watch, it will get great reviews from the Fake News Media, because they haven’t got a clue of what is going on in the REAL WORLD — And, by the way, the NFL should immediately replace its ridiculous new Kickoff Rule. MAKE AMERICA GREAT AGAIN! President DONALD J. TRUMP.”

Our Analysis:

The Fury Over Bad Bunny’s Super Bowl Halftime Show: A MAGA Meltdown?

In the latest spectacle that seems to have inflamed the MAGA crowd to near apocalyptic levels of rage, we find ourselves dissecting the fallout from Bad Bunny’s Super Bowl halftime performance. It’s a scenario so absurd, it could only exist in the reality-distorting echo chambers of modern political discourse. Yet, here we are, with Jimmy Kimmel pointing out the ludicrousness of the situation with the kind of biting wit that only highlights how farcical the outrage has become.

“The MAGAs are still exploding with rage over the Bad Bunny halftime show,” Kimmel quips, underscoring the seemingly endless capacity for outrage over cultural events that the MAGA faithful exhibit. It’s a phenomenon as predictable as it is bewildering. But the real cherry on top of this farce sundae is the involvement of a Republican Congressman from Missouri, Mark Alford, who is threatening to call in the Federal Communications Commission (FCC) over profanities that may or may not have been uttered during the performance. The ambiguity of his complaint is so rich, it’s almost decadent.

Alford’s comparison of Bad Bunny’s performance to the Janet Jackson wardrobe malfunction of 2004 is a masterclass in false equivalency. It’s as if the MAGA machine is so desperate for a villain that it will grasp at any straw, no matter how flimsy. Kimmel’s retort, “If only there were some way to translate that mysterious code Bad Bunny used, we could get to the bottom of this once and for all,” is a scathing reminder of the xenophobia and linguistic elitism that often underpins such criticisms.

The MAGA response to cultural phenomena they find objectionable often borders on parody, and Donald Trump’s statement on Truth Social serves as a case in point. Describing the halftime show as “a slap in the face to our Country,” Trump’s tirade against Bad Bunny’s performance is a melodramatic overreaction that would be hilarious if it weren’t so deeply indicative of the divisive rhetoric he’s known for. His diatribe touches on everything from the incomprehensibility of the lyrics to the alleged indecency of the dancing, concluding with a critique of the NFL’s kickoff rule, because why not throw that in there too?

Trump’s statement is a smorgasbord of the kind of xenophobic, puritanical, and hyperbolic language that has become the hallmark of the MAGA movement’s cultural critique. It’s a textbook example of how not to engage with art or entertainment that falls outside one’s narrow preferences or understanding.

Kimmel’s mockery of the situation is more than just late-night comedy fodder; it’s a necessary lampooning of the excessive, often irrational outrage that characterizes so much of political discourse today. The suggestion that “so many of them are so mad, I think they might need to be medicated,” might seem harsh, but in the face of such relentless and often baseless fury, it’s hard not to see his point.

In the end, the furor over Bad Bunny’s halftime show is emblematic of a larger trend in American politics and culture—a trend where outrage is often the first and only response to anything that challenges or discomforts. It’s a trend that is as unproductive as it is ubiquitous. And while Jimmy Kimmel’s monologue may not change any minds, it serves as a poignant reminder of the absurdity of it all.
