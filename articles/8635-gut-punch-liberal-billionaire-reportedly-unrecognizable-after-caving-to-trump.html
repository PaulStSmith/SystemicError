'Gut punch': Liberal billionaire reportedly 'unrecognizable' after caving to Trump

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosNovember 17, 20254 min read

Salesforce CEO Marc Benioff (Tech Crunch/Flickr Commons)

California tech billionaire Marc Benioff, once a staunch supporter of the Democratic Party, has become “unrecognizable” after endorsing President Donald Trump’s National Guard deployments and cozying up to the president, The Washington Post reported Sunday.

Benioff had initially distanced himself from Trump following his 2024 victory, pledging to maintain his company’s core values such as diversity and inclusion. But after he publicly supported Trump’s plan to deploy the National Guard to San Francisco, along with making social media posts congratulating the president, those around him begin to question whether he had caved on his principles.

“Marc Benioff stood out as someone who showed us that San Francisco values and the tech industry could coexist with and amplify each other,” said California state Rep. Matt Haney, a Democrat who represents San Francisco, speaking with the Post. “[That persona is now] unrecognizable.”

Others, like Rafael Mandelman, the president of the San Francisco Board of Supervisors, described Benioff’s apparent shift towards Trump a “gut punch.” And a message sent in his company’s internal messaging channel sharing a news article that included Benioff’s comments supporting Trump’s National Guard deployments garnered 200 negative reactions from employees using barfing-face emojis, according to a screenshot obtained by the Post.

“This seems to conflict pretty heavily with our values,” an employee at Benioff’s company Salesforce wrote, according to the Post.

While Benioff would eventually walk back his support for Trump’s proposal to deploy the National Guard to San Francisco, he continued to praise him on social media, and last month, joined Trump in Tokyo for dinner.

His relationship with Trump was apparently so strong that the president personally asked Benioff for his recommendation as to who he should replace Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell with, whom Trump has frequently tangled with and floated firing over his refusal to cut interest rates to his liking.

“Marc, do you have a suggestion?” Trump asked Benioff, who owns Time magazine, during the dinner. “You put me on the cover of Time magazine. That was a nice picture. Thank you very much for that.”

Our Analysis:

The Enigmatic Transformation of Marc Benioff: A Study in Political Fluidity

In a narrative that reads like the plot of a political thriller, Marc Benioff, the tech titan behind Salesforce and a once vocal proponent of Democratic values, has executed a public pivot that has left supporters and critics alike bewildered. According to a recent exposé by The Washington Post, Benioff’s political allegiance—or at least his public facade of one—has undergone a seismic shift, aligning more closely with President Donald Trump, a figure whose policies and demeanor have historically clashed with the ethos of the tech community, particularly that of San Francisco.

A Tale of Unlikely Bedfellows

At the heart of this controversy is Benioff’s support for Trump’s deployment of the National Guard to San Francisco, a move that starkly contrasts with the city’s and the tech industry’s liberal values. This endorsement, coupled with a series of congratulatory social media posts directed at Trump, has sent ripples through Benioff’s social and professional circles, leaving many to grapple with the question: What could cause such a drastic realignment in Benioff’s political stance?

California state Rep. Matt Haney articulately captures the bewilderment and sense of betrayal felt by many, noting that Benioff’s actions are not only out of step with San Francisco’s values but also represent a radical departure from the persona he had previously cultivated. This sentiment is echoed by Rafael Mandelman, president of the San Francisco Board of Supervisors, who describes Benioff’s apparent shift as a “gut punch.”

The Corporate Backlash

The repercussions of Benioff’s political flirtations extend into the very fabric of Salesforce, a company that prides itself on its commitment to diversity and inclusion. A leaked screenshot revealing 200 negative reactions from employees to Benioff’s endorsement of Trump’s National Guard deployment speaks volumes. It underscores a palpable sense of disillusionment within the ranks of Salesforce employees, many of whom had likely looked to Benioff as a moral compass in the often murky waters of tech and politics.

The Dinner in Tokyo

Further complicating this narrative is Benioff’s recent dinner with Trump in Tokyo, an event that not only symbolizes the cementing of their newfound relationship but also positions Benioff as an advisor of sorts to the president. Trump’s solicitation of Benioff’s recommendation for a new Federal Reserve Chair—an overture made amidst casual banter about Benioff’s ownership of Time magazine—suggests a level of influence and access that is both enviable and, in this context, deeply troubling.

The Realpolitik of Marc Benioff

What emerges from this saga is a portrait of a man navigating the treacherous terrain of power, influence, and politics in a manner that seems to prioritize personal and corporate gain over ideological consistency. Benioff’s actions, while perhaps pragmatic from a business standpoint, raise critical questions about the role of corporate leaders in public discourse and the extent to which they should, or should not, align their political engagements with their companies’ stated values.

In a world where tech giants wield power comparable to that of nation-states, the decisions made by individuals like Benioff have far-reaching implications. They shape not only the future of their companies but also the socio-political landscape in which they operate. As such, Benioff’s recent maneuvers serve as a cautionary tale about the volatile nexus of tech, politics, and power—a tale that is as indicative of our times as it is a reflection of the complex individual at its center.
