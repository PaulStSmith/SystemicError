Michigan State Police responded to a bomb threat at the Holly residence of U.S. Sen. Elissa Slotkin late Friday night, just one day after President Donald Trump publicly labeled the first-term Democrat a “traitor” and suggested she should be arrested or even put to death.

Slotkin, a former intelligence analyst who previously served three terms in the House, was not home at the time, according to a spokesperson for her office. Troopers searched the property and confirmed that no one was in danger, ABC News 12 in Michigan reported.

“Senator Slotkin is deeply grateful for the swift and professional response by Oakland County and Michigan State Police,” the spokesperson added Friday. “U.S. Capitol Police will continue to investigate and hopefully hold accountable those responsible.”

The threat came one day after Slotkin drew the president’s ire after appearing in a video with a group of Democratic veterans urging active-duty service members not to follow unlawful orders.

“No one has to carry out orders that violate the law, or our Constitution,” the video says. “Know that we have your back… don’t give up the ship.”

Trump reacted angrily, saying the lawmakers should be jailed and possibly executed. The joint statement was issued by Slotkin along with Sen. Mark Kelly (D-AZ) and Reps. Jason Crow (D-CO), Maggie Goodlander (D-NH), Chris Deluzio (D-PA), and Chrissy Houlahan (D-PA). Though it was not specified which orders would be deemed unlawful, they told troops to hold firm when put under pressure.

Friday’s development also landed on the same day Rep. Marjorie Taylor Greene (R-GA) announced she would resign from Congress. In her resignation letter, Greene cited “years of non-stop, never-ending personal attacks, death threats, lawfare, ridiculous slander and lies” from critics.

Michigan State Police and U.S. Capitol Police continue to investigate the threat, according to the Flint, Michigan, news outlet

Our Analysis:

Analyzing the Threat Against Democracy: The Bomb Threat Against Senator Elissa Slotkin

In a disturbing sequence of events that reads like a script torn from the pages of a poorly written political thriller, U.S. Senator Elissa Slotkin became the target of a bomb threat. This incident occurred in the shadow of incendiary comments made by President Trump, who labeled Slotkin a “traitor” and insinuated that she deserved arrest or execution. This chilling episode unfolded at Slotkin’s residence in Holly, Michigan, showcasing the dangerous intersection of political rhetoric and real-world violence.

The Context: A Dangerous Escalation

Senator Slotkin, a former intelligence analyst with a robust background in public service, found herself in the crosshairs not for actions deemed detrimental to the state but for advocating for the sanctity of law and the Constitution. The bomb threat against her came just one day after Trump’s outrageous comments, highlighting a direct line from inflammatory political speech to threats against public officials.

The Provocation: Advocating for Lawful Orders

The root of Trump’s ire was a video featuring Slotkin and other Democratic veterans, including Sen. Mark Kelly and Reps. Jason Crow, Maggie Goodlander, Chris Deluzio, and Chrissy Houlahan. Their message was clear and constitutionally sound: urging active-duty service members to refuse unlawful orders. This stance, though provocative to some, is grounded in the fundamental principles of military conduct and the rule of law. Yet, it was met with a response from Trump that was both disproportionate and dangerous.

The Response: Swift and Professional

In the wake of the bomb threat, the response by the Michigan State Police and U.S. Capitol Police was commendably swift and thorough. Senator Slotkin expressed gratitude for their professionalism, underscoring the importance of law enforcement in maintaining public safety amidst political turbulence. However, the incident raises grave concerns about the safety of public officials and the volatile state of political discourse in America.

The Bigger Picture: A Symptom of a Deeper Malaise

This episode is symptomatic of a larger, more insidious problem: the escalating rhetoric of violence in political discourse. When a former President labels a sitting senator a traitor and suggests execution as a reasonable consequence for political dissent, it marks a dangerous departure from democratic norms and principles. It’s a stark reminder of the power of words, especially those spoken by individuals in positions of significant influence.

The Irony: Marjorie Taylor Greene’s Resignation

Compounding the irony of the situation is the resignation of Rep. Marjorie Taylor Greene on the same day, citing “years of non-stop, never-ending personal attacks.” This juxtaposition lays bare the double standards and the selective outrage that pervades our political landscape, where one side’s call for accountability and adherence to legal principles is met with threats and vilification.

Conclusion: A Call for Accountability and Civility

The bomb threat against Senator Slotkin is not just an attack on an individual but an assault on the very foundations of democratic governance. It underscores the urgent need for a return to civility, respect for the rule of law, and the recognition that political disagreement does not warrant threats of violence or death. In a democracy, words have consequences, and it’s high time for those in positions of power to lead by example, condemning and rejecting rhetoric that endangers lives and the democratic process alike.
