Late-night Trump post fuels new speculation about his coming Kennedy Center announcement

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosAugust 14, 20251 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

FILE PHOTO: U.S. President Donald Trump speaks to the press about deploying federal law enforcement agents in Washington to bolster the local police presence, in the Press Briefing Room at the White House, in Washington D.C., U.S., August 11, 2025. REUTERS/Jonathan Ernst/File Photo

Some supporters of President Donald Trump are proposing that the Kennedy Center in Washington, D.C. be named after him.

On Tuesday night, August 12, Trump shared a photo of himself in the venue's presidential box; that followed an earlier post saluting the "GREAT Nominees for the TRUMP/KENNEDY CENTER, whoops, I mean, KENNEDY CENTER, AWARDS."

Users of X, formerly Twitter, were quick to react to Trump's Kennedy Center posts.

Writer/attorney Amee Vanderpool tweeted, "Donald Trump has just posted this: 'GREAT Nominees for the TRUMP/KENNEDY CENTER, whoops, I mean, KENNEDY CENTER, AWARDS.' The real question is can Donald Trump will get away with actually renaming a living monument?"

Filmmaker Nick van derr Graaf remarked, "Two words: Banana Republic."

X user Dion Harper posted, "I came across a post that Donald Trump posted on Truth Social; It started with the sentence 'Great nominees for the TRUMP/KENNEDY CENTRE, whoops, I mean, KENNEDY CENTRE, AWARDS'. I feel like Donald wants to make some changes re the Kennedy Center that go beyond just repairs."

Another X user, JV, commented, "Is President Trump going to rename the Kennedy Center?"
