An Open Letter to Arnold Schwarzenegger

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosAugust 16, 20252 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Dear Governor Schwarzenegger,

We write this with genuine respect for your past contributions to California and to the fight against gerrymandering. You were the rare Republican who had the courage to challenge entrenched power in Sacramento and give voters an independent redistricting commission. That reform was historic. It was meaningful. It helped California prove that democracy can function when politicians are no longer allowed to choose their own voters. For that, you deserve credit and gratitude.

But today, we fear you are standing on the wrong side of history.

You have said you oppose Governor Gavin Newsom’s proposed ballot measure to temporarily set aside the commission’s maps in favor of a Democratic-drawn one. You argue that principle demands purity—that if gerrymandering is wrong, it is always wrong, no matter the circumstances. Normally, we would agree. But these are not normal circumstances.

Governor Abbott and the Republican Party in Texas are executing one of the most brazen gerrymanders in modern history. They are not simply nudging lines for advantage—they are openly rigging maps to disenfranchise minority voters and guarantee Donald Trump’s control of the House before a single ballot is cast. This is not politics as usual. This is the systematic dismantling of democracy.

We understand your instinct to stand by your legacy, to defend the independence of California’s commission. But Governor, your very own reforms proved something crucial: when democracy functions fairly, Republicans struggle to compete, while Democrats adapt, reform, and survive. That is precisely why your party abandoned your model elsewhere. They saw what you built, and instead of copying it, they ran in the opposite direction, because fair competition does not favor them anymore.

Governor Newsom’s move is not cynical opportunism. It is not a naked partisan grab. It is a defensive measure—an emergency firebreak against an authoritarian GOP that has decided democracy is expendable. If California stays “pure” while Texas and other red states gerrymander with impunity, then we are not preserving democracy—we are handing it over.

We ask you to reconsider. Do not let your legacy be weaponized against the very ideals you fought for. By opposing Newsom’s measure, you are not strengthening democracy—you are tying one hand behind its back while the other side brings the bulldozer. This is not a stinking contest with a skunk, as you put it. This is trench warfare for the survival of democratic governance itself.

You taught Californians to believe in independent commissions. That work still matters. But in this moment, we must also confront reality: unilateral disarmament is not virtue, it is surrender. If you want to terminate gerrymandering, first help us stop those who are trying to terminate democracy.

With respect and urgency,

Systemic Error
