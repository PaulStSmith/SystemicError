'This is not what I voted for': MAGA fans blow up on Leavitt after Trump boast

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosJuly 11, 20255 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Our Analysis:The Unraveling of MAGA's Trust: A Deep Dive into the Leavitt FiascoBetrayal in the Air: The MAGA ReactionThe Underlying IronyThe Bitter Taste of DisillusionmentConclusion: A Fractured Base and an Uncertain Future

An attempt by White House press secretary Karoline Leavitt to promote a report that new voters find Donald Trump trustworthy blew up in her face with longtime MAGA supporters who feel that they have been betrayed.

Days after the DOJ claimed it has nothing new to offer about the life and death of accused pedophile Jeffrey Epstein, MAGA fans and QAnon believers are not letting it go and believe another cover-up has begun.

And they are taking out their anger on Leavitt on social media.

Leavitt posted a link on X where she wrote, "Trump seen as a rare promise keeper, especially with new voters," with a link to the conservative Washington Examiner.

That led to a flood of complaints with self-described "Christian Nationalist Populist" Muzzle Flash News demanding, "Why is the Trump administration protecting pedophiles?" which has already received over 800 likes.

QAnon fan Luke Slywalker has more than Epstein on their mind, writing, "He’s sending more money to Ukraine and failing to provide justice to Epstein’s victims, while continuing to simp for war criminal Netanyahu. This is not what I voted for."

"Ahhhh.... Now you've reduced yourself to trolling. How nice Karoline. First for full disclosure, I still support Trump. Second my trust in Trump used to be 100% while still remaining wary of some of the things he's done. I can't put a percentage on it but I can tell you that my trust in him has fallen significantly from 100%. Significantly. You are promoting a known lie concerning the Epstein debacle. Does promoting a lie somebody else has told make you a liar? I don't have an answer for that yet. When you're not professing to believe in it yourself but you're repeating it, are you a liar?" wrote the Darkest Expanse, whose pinned tweet compares Trump to Abraham Lincoln.

"Pureblood Patriot" Pamela McDaniel offered, "So why is no one in jail for Epstein, Russiagate, or the stolen election?"

"Y'all are working overtime to make us forget the Epstein List. Don't forget we also wanted no involvement in Ukraine, yet here we are continuing Biden's policies," noted Liberty Lighthouse.

American Independent cautioned, "Oh my, Karoline. Right now, any good that Trump has done is being overshadowed by the blatant lies about Epstein. Right now, any good promises he kept are all being overshadowed. The American public feels slapped in the face. This administration should not have been so."

"Funny," Scott Coner wrote. "I voted for him. But the Epstein list is horribly mishandled. Lots of points in the BBBill that aren’t looking great for some people either."

"I voted for him 3 times I knew the day that he was shot in Butler that day that he had won the election. But sister, the Epstein List is going to cling to him like skunk spray. You just can't get rid of the smell with a little soap. He must declassify those files, and somebody's got to be fired. 'The Party told you to reject the evidence of your eyes and ears. It was their final, most essential command.' - George Orwell, 1984 Republicans: There are no Epstein files and no Epstein clients," lectured Ninth Octopus.

Our Analysis:

The Unraveling of MAGA's Trust: A Deep Dive into the Leavitt Fiasco

The latest episode in the MAGA saga unfolds with Karoline Leavitt, White House Press Secretary, at its epicenter, revealing a stark and tumultuous rift within the Trump supporter base. Attempting to shine a light on Donald Trump's purported trustworthiness among new voters, Leavitt inadvertently ignited a firestorm among the long-time MAGA and QAnon loyalists. This incident, far from being a minor hiccup, underscores a broader crisis of faith festering within the ranks of Trump's most ardent supporters.

Betrayal in the Air: The MAGA Reaction

Leavitt's promotion of a report, hailing Trump as a "rare promise keeper," was met not with applause but with an avalanche of grievances on social media. The disillusionment is palpable, with accusations of protecting pedophiles and betraying foundational promises stretching the already frayed trust to its breaking point.

A Closer Look at the Accusations

  • Protecting Pedophiles? A self-described "Christian Nationalist Populist" from Muzzle Flash News confronts the administration's inaction on high-profile cases like Jeffrey Epstein, receiving widespread support.

  • Failing Epstein’s Victims? The criticism extends beyond mere protection, with allegations of failing justice for Epstein's victims, amidst broader discontent over financial aid to Ukraine and unwavering support for controversial figures like Netanyahu.

  • The Epstein List and Broken Promises: Supporters like "Pureblood Patriot" Pamela McDaniel and Liberty Lighthouse voice frustration over unaddressed scandals and perceived continuations of opposition policies, particularly in Ukraine.

The Underlying Irony

The irony in this debacle cannot be overstated. Leavitt's attempt to bolster Trump's image as trustworthy has only served to spotlight the deep-seated skepticism and disillusionment within his base. The stark juxtaposition of the administration's narrative against the supporters' grievances lays bare a profound and perhaps irreconcilable division.

The Bitter Taste of Disillusionment

The sentiment across the board, from American Independent to Scott Coner, reflects a sense of betrayal that transcends mere political disappointment. It's a feeling of being misled, where even past support for Trump is now questioned under the weight of unfulfilled promises and controversies left to fester.

A Dire Warning from Literature

Ninth Octopus’s invocation of George Orwell's 1984 is particularly telling. It serves as a grim reminder of the dangers of blind loyalty and the suppression of truth. The reference to "rejecting the evidence of your eyes and ears" encapsulates the core of the crisis — a base feeling abandoned by its leader, urged to ignore the glaring truths before them.

Conclusion: A Fractured Base and an Uncertain Future

The debacle surrounding Karoline Leavitt's post is not merely a social media mishap; it's a symptom of a deeper malaise within the Trump camp. The fierce backlash from MAGA and QAnon supporters reveals a trust so deeply eroded that even attempts to reaffirm loyalty and celebrate perceived victories are met with scorn and skepticism.

As the Trump administration navigates these turbulent waters, the path forward is fraught with challenges. Reconciling with a base that feels betrayed and lied to is no small task. The Leavitt incident, therefore, is not just a lesson in the volatility of political loyalty but a cautionary tale of what happens when the promises that bind a leader to their followers are perceived as broken. In the end, the quest for trustworthiness might have revealed the very lack of it, leaving a once-unshakeable base questioning where it stands amidst the shifting sands of Trump's America.
