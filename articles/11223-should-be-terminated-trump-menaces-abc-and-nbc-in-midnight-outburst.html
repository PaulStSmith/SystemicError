Continuing an attack from earlier in the day where the president went on a tirade aimed at ABC commentator and former rival Chris Christie, Trump doubled down in two frantic posts on Truth Social.

In his first post, he wrote, “Despite a very high popularity and, according to many, among the greatest 8 months in Presidential History, ABC & NBC FAKE NEWS, two of the worst and most biased networks in history, give me 97% BAD STORIES. IF THAT IS THE CASE, THEY ARE SIMPLY AN ARM OF THE DEMOCRAT PARTY AND SHOULD, ACCORDING TO MANY, HAVE THEIR LICENSES REVOKED BY THE FCC. I would be totally in favor of that because they are so biased and untruthful, an actual threat to our Democracy!!! MAGA.”

After making that accusation, he escalated his attack with a threat of possible action targeting the networks.

“Why is it that ABC and NBC FAKE NEWS, two of the absolute worst and most biased networks anywhere in the World, aren’t paying Millions of Dollars a year in LICENSE FEES,” he suggested. “They should lose their Licenses for their unfair coverage of Republicans and/or Conservatives, but at a minimum, they should pay up BIG for having the privilege of using the most valuable airwaves anywhere at anytime!!! Crooked ‘journalism’ should not be rewarded, it should be terminated!!!”

Our Analysis:

Trump's Tirade on Truth Social: A Dive into the Depths of Desperation

In the latest spectacle that seems ripped right from the pages of an authoritarian playbook, Donald Trump has taken to Truth Social in a late-night frenzy, targeting media giants ABC and NBC with threats that dance dangerously close to the edge of First Amendment violations. This isn't just another rant from a former president known for his social media tirades; it's a chilling glimpse into Trump's disdain for free press and a democratic principle that doesn't sing his praises.

Let's dissect the key elements of Trump's outburst and the underlying implications for media freedom and democratic health in America.

The Attack on ABC and NBC: Unpacking the Threat

Trump's accusations against ABC and NBC, labeling them as "FAKE NEWS" and "the most biased networks in history," are not just baseless but a gross misrepresentation of the media's role in a democratic society. The former president alleges these networks give him "97% BAD STORIES," a claim that smacks more of paranoia than fact. His solution? To suggest their broadcasting licenses be revoked by the FCC.

This is not merely an attack on two of the nation's leading broadcasters; it's an assault on the very foundation of free speech and press. Trump's threats to pull broadcasting licenses over unfavorable coverage reveal a misunderstanding (or perhaps a willful ignorance) of both the law and the role of a free press in a democracy.

The Misguided Call for License Fees

In a follow-up post, Trump escalates his attack with a bizarre suggestion that these networks should be paying millions in license fees, presumably as a penalty for their coverage of Republicans and/or conservatives. This notion of penalizing media outlets for their editorial content is not just undemocratic; it's fundamentally un-American.

Furthermore, Trump's call for networks to "pay up BIG" for using airwaves reveals a profound misunderstanding of how broadcasting licenses work. Licenses are issued by the FCC based on criteria that ensure broadcasters serve the public interest; they are not, and should never be, contingent on a network's political leanings or coverage.

An Actual Threat to Our Democracy

Perhaps the most ironic — and dangerous — element of Trump's tirade is his claim that these networks are "an actual threat to our Democracy." In reality, the threat comes not from the media's exercise of free speech but from attempts by powerful figures to silence dissenting voices. Trump's attack on the press is a textbook example of trying to undermine democratic institutions that hold leaders accountable.

Conclusion: A Call for Vigilance

Trump's late-night outburst on Truth Social should not be dismissed as mere venting. It's a stark reminder of the former president's willingness to challenge the norms that underpin American democracy. His threats against ABC and NBC are not just attacks on two media outlets but on the principle of a free and independent press.

As defenders of democracy, we must remain vigilant against such threats and stand in support of free speech and press, recognizing them as essential pillars of a free society. Let this episode be a call to action, not just to oppose Trump's authoritarian impulses, but to affirm our commitment to the democratic values that truly make America great.
