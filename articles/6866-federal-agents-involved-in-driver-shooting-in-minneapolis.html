Federal agents involved in driver shooting in Minneapolis

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosJanuary 8, 20261 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Border Patrol commander Greg Bovino and members of U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) stand guard after a driver of a vehicle was shot in Minneapolis, Minnesota on Jan. 7, 2026. REUTERS/Tim Evans

Federal agents were reportedly involved in a shooting in Minneapolis Wednesday morning where a driver was shot inside a vehicle, according to reports.

The incident happened in the area of South Minneapolis and a group of ICE agents were in the area, Reuters reported. Border Patrol commander Greg Bovino and ICE agents were at the scene of the shooting.

“We are aware of a shooting involving federal law enforcement near East 34th Street and Portland Avenue. Please avoid this area,” the city of Minneapolis posted on X.

The driver’s condition was not immediately known.
