"Undocumented Pharmacist" and the Cowardice of Conservative Meme Culture

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosMay 23, 20252 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

There’s a particular brand of reactionary rhetoric that pretends it’s clever while doing nothing more than puking out false equivalences and cruelty masquerading as insight. The image in question—featuring Breaking Bad's Walter White—is a masterclass in this genre of xenophobic pablum.

“Calling an illegal alien an undocumented immigrant is like calling a meth dealer an undocumented pharmacist.”

Let’s break that down, shall we?

1. The Language of Dehumanization

"Illegal alien" is a term that exists solely to strip individuals of their humanity and frame them as criminal threats. No one is illegal. Actions may violate statutes—crossing a border without papers, for instance—but people are not crimes. "Undocumented immigrant" is not a euphemism; it’s a recognition of status without moral judgment, the bare minimum of decency.

By that logic, calling a jaywalker an “illegal pedestrian” would sound equally absurd. But the point here isn’t accuracy—it’s moral grandstanding.

2. False Equivalence on Meth

A meth dealer peddles addiction, destruction, and death. Undocumented immigrants pick your strawberries, clean your hospitals, build your homes, and prop up entire sectors of the economy for wages no citizen would accept. One group is essential to your life. The other is a walking tragedy.

Comparing these two is not just ignorant—it’s malicious. It tells you everything about the speaker and nothing about the subject.

3. Weaponizing Walter White

The irony is rich: Walter White, a character who embodies privilege and moral collapse, is used here as a stand-in for the voice of reason. But let’s be clear—Walter White is not the hero. He is a cautionary tale, a man who thought he was above consequences, who justified monstrous acts with tortured logic. Just like the creators of this meme.

Using him as a mouthpiece for immigration policy is like quoting Voldemort in a debate about school funding. It's asinine and grotesquely on-the-nose.

4. The Cowardice of Simplified Outrage

This meme doesn’t seek truth. It seeks outrage. It’s a dog whistle in the shape of a punchline. It pretends to be edgy, but it’s just lazy propaganda designed to make small minds feel tall.

And the worst part? It hides behind "humor" when called out. “Can’t you take a joke?” they’ll say, as if degrading human dignity is a setup for a sitcom.

Let’s be absolutely clear:

  • Seeking a better life is not a crime.

  • Contributing to society despite systemic barriers is not villainy.

  • And no matter how many edgy graphics they slap on it, cruelty is not cleverness.

This isn’t a meme. It’s a mask for hate. And it deserves to be seen exactly as it is: pathetic, punchless, and profoundly wrong.
