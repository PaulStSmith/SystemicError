'Embarrassing': Trump's 'atrocious' plan to profit from Ukraine war stuns former diplomat

Michael McFaul, who served as the U.S. ambassador to Russia from 2012 to 2014, discussed President Donald Trump's handling of the war in Ukraine on MSNBC's "Alex Witt Reports" on Sunday. McFaul said Trump's Russian counterpart has effectively been allowed to dictate how the war continues because Trump has been focused on the wrong objectives.

One aspect of the war that McFaul said Trump seems to be focused on is who is at fault for the peace negotiations breaking apart, instead of providing new weapons to Ukraine so they can continue fighting Russia. This has allowed Russian President Vladimir Putin to expand the war in Ukraine with almost no consequences, McFaul added.

"He needs to announce his new military assistance package for Ukraine," McFaul said. "It's great that he's allowing the Europeans to buy them from us, but it is atrocious to me and embarrassing to me that we are now profiting from the war in Ukraine."

Trump has also allowed Putin to cross his red lines with seeming impunity, McFaul said. That seems likely to continue unless Trump stands up to Putin, he added.

"The president has to put in place some consequences," McFaul said. "He has to implement new sanctions. And not just on the Indians. Directly on the Russian banks that are still not sanctioned. Directly on the so-called shadow fleet that runs Russian oil around the world. He can seize their assets. We still have $5 billion to $10 billion of Russian central bank assets in the United States."

Our Analysis:

Analyzing the Atrocious: Trump's Profit from the Ukraine War

In a landscape marred by conflict and political maneuvering, the recent comments by Michael McFaul, a former U.S. ambassador to Russia, cast a stark, damning light on the Trump administration's handling of the war in Ukraine. McFaul's insights, delivered with the precision of someone who has navigated the intricate web of international diplomacy, expose a strategy—or lack thereof—that prioritizes profit over peace, and personal gain over global stability.

The Profit Motive

McFaul's assertion that President Donald Trump's plan to profit from the ongoing war in Ukraine is "atrocious" is not just a critique; it's a clarion call to examine the moral vacuity at the heart of such intentions. The very idea that the U.S. is profiting from a conflict that has displaced millions and taken countless lives is not only embarrassing but also ethically reprehensible. The focus on who is to blame for the peace negotiations falling apart, rather than arming Ukraine to defend itself, is a diversion—a smokescreen masking the true intentions of profiteering.

The Puppeteer: Putin's Influence

Trump's apparent subservience to Vladimir Putin is another critical point McFaul brings to light. By allowing Putin to dictate the progression of the war with minimal pushback, Trump effectively gives free rein to the expansion of conflict, undermining not just Ukrainian sovereignty but also global peace and security. The lack of consequences for crossing "red lines" is not just a failure; it's a tacit endorsement of Putin's aggression.

The Call to Action

McFaul's recommendations are clear and grounded in a strategic approach to counteract the current administration's failures. The urgent need for a new military assistance package for Ukraine underscores the necessity of support over profit. Trump's inaction and focus on the wrong objectives have only emboldened Putin and exacerbated the crisis.

Furthermore, McFaul's call for implementing new sanctions directly on Russian banks and the shadow fleet involved in circumnavigating oil sanctions is a step towards accountability. The suggestion to seize Russian central bank assets in the United States is not just a punitive measure; it's a means to exert pressure and signal a firm stance against the violation of international norms.

Conclusion: A Reflection on Leadership and Morality

The analysis of Trump's handling of the Ukraine war, as per McFaul's observations, paints a picture of a presidency marred by moral and ethical bankruptcy. The prioritization of profit over peace, the undue influence of foreign dictators, and the glaring absence of strategic, principled leadership are not just policy failures; they are a reflection of a deeper systemic rot.

As observers, critics, and participants in the global order, the revelations and recommendations put forth by McFaul should serve as a wake-up call. The need for leadership that is both morally grounded and strategically astute has never been more critical. The war in Ukraine is not just a geopolitical chess game; it's a humanitarian crisis that demands a response rooted in integrity, compassion, and decisive action. Anything less is, indeed, atrocious.
