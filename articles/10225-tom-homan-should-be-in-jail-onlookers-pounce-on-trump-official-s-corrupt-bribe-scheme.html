'Tom Homan should be in jail': Onlookers pounce on Trump official's 'corrupt' bribe scheme

Tom Homan, U.S. President Donald Trump’s border czar, speaks to reporters at the White House in Washington, U.S., February 6, 2025. REUTERS/Kevin Lamarque

"The most corrupt administration we have ever seen" is how Rep. Pramila Jayapal (D) reacted to a report that Donald Trump's border czar had accepted a $50,000 bribe from undercover agents.

Raw Story reported on Saturday about a MSNBC exclusive in which the outlet claimed that Homan had previously been investigated for accepting $50,000 from undercover FBI agents. That caused U.S. Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez (D-NY) to issue a stinging four-word response to Homan.

It also caused a senator to declare that Attorney General Pam Bondi "knew" about the reported bribery operation.

Reactions came in fast online.

Journalist Adam Klasfeld called the report a "must-read scoop."

Conor James Lamb, an attorney and politician who served as the U.S. representative from 2018 to 2023, said, "Dems must immediately demand the entire investigative file and not give DOJ another dollar till they get it. *Our* money is paying for this corruption."

Dem strategist Mike Nellis said, "Congress should haul in Pam Bondi and Kash Patel this week to explain why the FBI caught Tom Homan taking bribes—and why Trump’s DOJ killed the case."

"That’s corruption, plain and simple," Nellis then added.

Cato Institute's The Alex Nowrasteh also chimed in:

"The FBI recorded Trump’s border czar accepting a $50,000 bribe. Then the Trump administration buried it to protect their guy. If true, Tom Homan should be in prison with those who covered it up. Pretty minor corruption for Trump’s 2nd term though."

Maya Wiley wrote, "When the collar is white & the work is to racially profile & abuse people seems old fashioned bribery is protected."

"We shouldn’t be surprised but we must be outraged," the analyst wrote Saturday.

Former MSNBC personality Keith Olbermann said, "BREAKING: Simply put this corrupt bully [Homan] now needs to be cuffed, hands behind his back, and led across the border."

Our Analysis:

A Deep Dive into Corruption and Outrage: The Case of Trump's Border Czar

In what can only be described as a shocking revelation, a report has come to light involving Donald Trump's border czar, Tom Homan, and a $50,000 bribe from undercover agents. This incident, reported by Raw Story and stemming from an MSNBC exclusive, has ignited a firestorm of reactions from political figures and commentators alike, all pointing to a broader narrative of corruption within the Trump administration.

The Allegations

The core of this scandal revolves around the alleged acceptance of a $50,000 bribe by Tom Homan from undercover FBI agents. This act, if proven true, is not just a mere lapse in judgment but a grave violation of public trust and legal integrity. The implications are far-reaching, touching on issues of corruption, accountability, and the erosion of democratic institutions under the Trump administration.

Political Reactions

Rep. Pramila Jayapal (D) didn't mince words, labeling the Trump administration as "the most corrupt administration we have ever seen." This sentiment was echoed by U.S. Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez (D-NY), who delivered a succinct yet powerful four-word response to Homan. Meanwhile, a senator has outright stated that Attorney General Pam Bondi "knew" about the bribery operation, implicating her in the scandal.

Conor James Lamb, highlighting the urgency of the situation, demanded that Democrats "immediately demand the entire investigative file" from the DOJ, emphasizing that public funds should not support corruption. Mike Nellis called for immediate congressional action to question both Pam Bondi and Kash Patel about their knowledge and involvement in the matter.

Commentary and Analysis

Journalists and analysts have been quick to weigh in, with Adam Klasfeld and Maya Wiley pointing out the systemic and racial undertones of the corruption. The Cato Institute's Alex Nowrasteh even remarked that, in the grand scheme of Trump's second term, this bribery case might be considered "pretty minor," a statement that speaks volumes about the perceived depth of corruption within the administration.

Keith Olbermann's dramatic call for Homan to be "cuffed" and "led across the border" epitomizes the outrage felt by many. It's a symbolic gesture, suggesting that those who engage in corruption, especially when tied to policies of racial profiling and abuse, deserve not only legal retribution but public condemnation.

Conclusion: The Bigger Picture

This scandal is not just about a $50,000 bribe. It's about what that bribe represents: a systemic rot within an administration that has, time and again, shown a disregard for the rule of law and ethical governance. The call for immediate action by political figures and commentators is not just about seeking justice in this particular case but about sending a clear message that corruption at the highest levels of government is intolerable.

As this story unfolds, one thing is clear: the outrage is justified, and the demand for accountability is non-negotiable. The people deserve answers, and those implicated in this scandal must be held to account. The integrity of our democratic institutions depends on it.
