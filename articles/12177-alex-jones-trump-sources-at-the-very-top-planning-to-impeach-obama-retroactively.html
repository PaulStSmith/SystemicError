Alex Jones: Trump 'sources at the very top' planning to impeach Obama 'retroactively'

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosAugust 1, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Our Analysis:Navigating the Quagmire of Absurdity: Alex Jones and the Retroactive Impeachment Fantasy

The Alex Jones Show/screen grab

Conspiracy theorist Alex Jones claimed to have "sources" within President Donald Trump's administration that were orchestrating a retroactive impeachment of former President Barack Obama.

During his Thursday program, Jones encouraged his audience to urge the Trump administration to move forward with prosecutions as retaliation for the probe into Russia's election interference in 2016.

"They're the real predators, and it's time politically, non-violently, to cut their heads off and to just do it," Jones said of Democrats. "And I mean that, again, metaphysically, is an archetype. They started the fight. Now we're going to finish it."

"I'm not saying 100%, but from my Justice Department sources at the very top, right below the very top, and from everything else and how scared the Democrats are acting, they have all the proof, they're releasing the proof, and they have every intention," he continued. "They're going to indict Brennan, Clapper, Comey, his daughter, Hillary, all of them."

"And they're getting ready to try impeachment on Obama retroactively to strip him of presidential immunity. And that's going on."

The U.S. Constitution states that "[t]he President, Vice President and all Civil Officers of the United States, shall be removed from Office on Impeachment ... and Conviction."

However, it's not clear that Congress has the authority to impeach former presidents.

Our Analysis:

Navigating the Quagmire of Absurdity: Alex Jones and the Retroactive Impeachment Fantasy

In a recent episode of The Alex Jones Show, we were treated to yet another fantastical voyage into the realm of conspiracy theories—an area where Jones has long established himself as both captain and navigator. His latest claim? That there is an imminent plan, supposedly hatched within the ranks of the Trump administration, to retroactively impeach former President Barack Obama. This assertion is not just a leap into the absurd; it's a cannonball into the ocean of lunacy.

The Claim: According to Jones, his "sources" within the Trump administration have revealed a plan to move forward with prosecutions against various Democrats, including Obama, as a form of retaliation for the investigation into Russia's interference in the 2016 election. Jones urges his audience to pressure the Trump administration to act upon these alleged plans, declaring, "They're the real predators, and it's time politically, non-violently, to cut their heads off and to just do it." He quickly clarifies his violent rhetoric as metaphorical, yet the underlying message is unmistakable—a call to political warfare against Democrats.

The Reality Check: Before diving into the murky waters of Jones' claim, let's establish a fundamental understanding of impeachment. The U.S. Constitution outlines the impeachment process as a mechanism to remove current officials from office, not as a post-tenure punitive measure. The suggestion that Congress might retroactively impeach a former president is, therefore, not grounded in any legal reality; it's a misinterpretation—or perhaps deliberate distortion—of constitutional law. Furthermore, Jones' assertion that such a plan is "going on" lacks any credible evidence, relying solely on unnamed "Justice Department sources" and speculative interpretations of Democrats' behavior.

The Players: Jones' narrative implicates a who's who of Democratic figures, including John Brennan, James Clapper, James Comey, "his daughter," and Hillary Clinton. By casting a wide net over these individuals, Jones attempts to reinforce the notion of a deep-state conspiracy against Trump and his allies. The inclusion of Obama in this alleged plot to "strip him of presidential immunity" via retroactive impeachment is both a new height of fantasy and a deep dive into the playbook of political vendetta.

The Motivation: What drives Jones to make such outlandish claims? It's a concoction of political partisanship, a penchant for conspiracy mongering, and a strategy to engage and inflame his audience. By presenting himself as privy to insider information, Jones elevates his status among his followers, feeding into the narrative of an ongoing battle between the righteous and the corrupt, with himself as a key figure in the fight.

Conclusion: Alex Jones' claim about a plan to retroactively impeach Barack Obama is a masterclass in conspiracy theory fabrication. It disregards constitutional law, relies on nonexistent evidence, and seeks to further polarize the political landscape. Such rhetoric, especially when laced with metaphors of violence, is not just irresponsible—it's dangerous. As consumers of media and participants in the political discourse, we must remain vigilant against these baseless narratives and committed to dissecting fact from fiction. In the theater of the absurd that Jones orchestrates, it's critical to remember that the stage is built on a foundation of misinformation and sensationalism.
