Not even the truth is safe.

If the Trump regime can revoke student visas because students exercise their freedom of speech on a university campus, freedom of speech is not secure for any of us.

If the Trump regime can abduct a permanent resident of the United States and send him to a torture prison in El Salvador, without any criminal charges, no American is safe.

What do we do about this?

We stand up to it. We resist it. We denounce it. We boldly and fearlessly reject it —regardless of the cost, regardless of the threats.

As columnist David Brooks writes in his column yesterday (I’m hardly in the habit of quoting David Brooks):

It’s time for a comprehensive national civic uprising. It’s time for Americans in universities, law, business, nonprofits and the scientific community, and civil servants and beyond to form one coordinated mass movement. Trump is about power. The only way he’s going to be stopped is if he’s confronted by some movement that possesses rival power.

But what does a national civic uprising look like?

It may look like a general strike — a strike in which tens of millions of Americans refuse to work, refuse to buy, refuse to engage in anything other than a mass demonstration against the regime.

And not just one general strike, but a repeating general strike — a strike whose numbers continue to grow and whose outrage, resistance, and solidarity continue to spread across the land.

I urge all of you to start preparing now for such a series of general strikes. I will inform you of what I learn about who is doing what. (One possible place to begin is here.)

In the meantime: This evening, Friday, April 18, bells will be sounded in Boston’s Old North Church (the one-if-by-land church where lanterns signaled Paul Revere to warn the Minutemen of the approaching troops) and in churches across the country, to celebrate the 250th anniversary of the Battles of Lexington and Concord, which began the American Revolution. I urge you to have your place of worship join in the ringing. (More information can be found here.)

Tomorrow, Saturday, April 19, protests are being organized around the country by 50501. See here.

My friends, what the Trump regime has unleashed on America is intolerable. It is time — beyond time — for a national civic uprising. We must take action.

Our Analysis:

The Trump Regime: An Assault on Academic Freedom and Civil Liberties

In an era where the truth is often battered and bruised under the guise of political maneuvering, the recent actions attributed to the Trump regime paint a chilling scenario for the pillars of democracy: freedom of speech, academic liberty, and personal security. The allegations, as put forth in the article, signal not just an overreach but a potential despotism that should alarm every sentient being who values the principles upon which the United States was founded.

The Autocratic Grip on Education: The assertion that the Trump administration seeks to control what universities can teach, research, or publish is a direct assault on academic freedom. This is not merely an overstep; it is an affront to the autonomy of educational institutions, a cornerstone of democratic societies. The idea that a government could dictate the flow of knowledge and the exchange of ideas is reminiscent of authoritarian regimes, not a country that prides itself on freedom and innovation.

Suppressing Student Voices: The purported move to revoke student visas as a penalty for exercising free speech is a stark reminder of how fragile these rights have become. The freedom to speak one's mind, to challenge and to inquire, lies at the heart of the academic endeavor. To threaten students with deportation for engaging in discourse is not only morally reprehensible but undermines the United States' standing as a bastion of higher learning and open dialogue.

Abduction and Torture Claims: Perhaps the most harrowing accusation is the alleged abduction of a permanent resident to be sent to a torture prison in El Salvador. This claim, if true, represents a terrifying breach of human rights and due process. It suggests a regime that operates with impunity, beyond the bounds of law and morality, where anyone deemed an adversary can be whisked away without a trace. This is not the mark of a democratic government but the hallmark of tyrannical rule.

What is to be Done?

The call to action, as outlined in the article, is a powerful one. It speaks to the need for a comprehensive national civic uprising. The concept of a general strike, of mass demonstrations, and coordinated resistance is a testament to the gravity of the threat posed. It recognizes that in the face of such authoritarian tactics, silence or passivity equates to complicity. The invocation of historical acts of resistance, such as the reference to the Battles of Lexington and Concord, serves not just as a call to arms, but a reminder of the revolutionary spirit that birthed the nation.

However, the proposal of a national civic uprising, while noble in intent, demands careful consideration. The power of collective action cannot be underestimated, but the path towards it must be paved with strategic planning, inclusivity, and a clear set of objectives. It is not enough to resist; one must know what they are fighting for and ensure that the methods employed do not mirror the oppressiveness of the adversary.

Final Thoughts

The allegations against the Trump regime, as discussed in the article, are grave and, if true, demand immediate and decisive action. The envisioned uprising is a clarion call to all who hold dear the values of liberty, justice, and democratic governance. Yet, as we stand at this crossroads, it is imperative that the resistance remains grounded in the principles it seeks to defend. To combat authoritarianism, one must not only expose and denounce its actions but embody the change they wish to see in the world. Only then can the ideals of freedom and democracy be truly safeguarded.
