Very early Sunday morning, at about 1AM, Donald Trump continued his attack on Harvard University by raising suspicions about the foreign students attending the venerable educational institution.

Doubling down on an administration decision to cancel Harvard's student visa program –– which has been put on hold by a federal judge –– the president took to Truth Social and demanded personal information on the students.

Taking exception to Harvard's legal filing that made the claim that his move was a "blatant violation of the First Amendment, the Due Process Clause, and the Administrative Procedure Act," the president fired back.

He then demanded, "We want to know who those foreign students are, a reasonable request since we give Harvard BILLIONS OF DOLLARS, but Harvard isn’t exactly forthcoming. Our Analysis:

Trump's Latest Tirade: Harvard and Its Foreign Students

In what can only be described as a continuation of his hallmark blend of xenophobia and ignorance, Donald Trump launched a very early Sunday morning broadside against Harvard University. This time, his ire is directed at the foreign students who grace the halls of this "venerable educational institution." Not content with the havoc wreaked by his administration's policies, he's doubling down in a manner that's as baffling as it is alarming.

The Demands: A Violation or Just Plain Vindictiveness?

Trump's demand for personal information on Harvard's foreign students is not just a gross overreach; it's a glaring testament to his disregard for privacy, academic freedom, and the basic principles of decency. This move, supposedly in retaliation against Harvard's legal challenge, is framed as a response to what he perceives as an affront. However, it reeks of vindictiveness rather than any legitimate concern for American education or its financial implications.

"Why isn’t Harvard saying that almost 31% of their students are from FOREIGN LANDS," Trump kvetched on Truth Social, evidently mistaking diversity for a plot against the nation. The suggestion that these countries are freeloading on the American education system is both ludicrous and misleading. It ignores the substantial fees these students pay, often at higher rates than domestic students, which contribute significantly to the economy and the funding of institutions like Harvard.

The Financial Fallacy

Trump's tirade about Harvard's wealth and federal funding is a red herring. Yes, Harvard has a massive endowment—billions, in fact. However, the simplistic equation of "We give Harvard BILLIONS OF DOLLARS" is misleading at best. Federal funding for universities is tied to research, financial aid for students (including Americans), and other programs that benefit the nation as a whole. It's not a handout to the university but an investment in the country's future.

Furthermore, the former president's demand for names and nationalities of foreign students under the guise of this financial argument is not just a breach of privacy; it's potentially a violation of their rights. It's a dangerous precedent, suggesting that financial contribution to an institution gives the government carte blanche to invade personal privacy and academic freedom.

The Real Issue at Hand

This isn't about Harvard's endowment or the financial contributions of foreign nations to their students' education. It's about Trump's continuous assault on the values of diversity, academic freedom, and international collaboration. It's a smokescreen, a distraction from the real issues—like systemic inequality, the need for comprehensive immigration reform, and the fostering of an educational environment that welcomes students from all backgrounds as a means of enriching both the academic and social fabric of the United States.

The Bottom Line

Trump's early morning outburst is more than just a rant; it's a manifestation of his administration's broader policies that sought to undermine the educational and cultural exchanges that have long been a cornerstone of American higher education. It's a stark reminder of the need for vigilance and advocacy against such narrow-minded and divisive tactics. Let's not be distracted by the spectacle. Instead, let's focus on the critical importance of maintaining an open, diverse, and inclusive academic community in the face of such assaults.
