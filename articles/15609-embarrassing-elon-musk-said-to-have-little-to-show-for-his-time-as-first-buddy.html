'Embarrassing': Elon Musk said to have 'little to show' for his time as 'first buddy'

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosApril 27, 20254 min read

FILE PHOTO: U.S. President-elect Donald Trump greets Tesla CEO and X owner Elon Musk during a rally the day before Trump is scheduled to be inaugurated for a second term, in Washington, U.S., January 19, 2025. REUTERS/Brian Snyder/File Photo

Elon Musk will be leaving Washington with little to show for his controversial time in government, according to a new analysis.

The tech billionaire spent more than $288 million of his own money to get Donald Trump re-elected and was rewarded with a perch overseeing the so-called Department of Government Efficiency, but Axios reported that the most powerful political outsider ever is walking away with "a legacy of self-destruction."

"He's leaving with his reputation wounded, relationships severed, companies in crisis, fortune diminished — and little to show for DOGE but chaos and contested savings," Axios reported. "Musk has publicly acknowledged the enormous toll that DOGE — which he's characterized as a patriotic, existential project — has taken on his private life."

The president still supports Musk, who called himself the "first buddy," but the tech CEO's favorability ratings have crashed as his team slashed federal programs, cut thousands of government jobs and hacked into the private data of millions of Americans, and his companies have lost billions of dollars in value as his personal brand became politically toxic.

"Tesla, battered by boycotts, protests and even firebombings, saw its net income plunge 71 percent in the first quarter — triggering Musk's decision this week to scale back his involvement in DOGE," Axios reported.

Musk's net worth has dropped by a staggering $122 billion this year, almost as much as the $160 billion DOGE claims to have saved the government, but experts say that figure is wildly exaggerated.

"DOGE's verified savings have been less than 1/10 of 1 percent of federal spending," said Jessica Riedl, a senior fellow and budget expert at the conservative Manhattan Institute. "There have been embarrassing accounting errors, lots of public statements that turned out to be false or misleading, or actions slapped back by the courts."

Republicans have hailed DOGE for its cuts to government spending, but experts say those savings are offset by cuts to the Internal Revenue Service.

"The spending savings are so small that they will be undoubtedly overwhelmed by the significant tax revenue losses which result from gutting IRS tax enforcement," Riedl said. "It makes a mockery of claims that DOGE is really just about cutting deficits."

Our Analysis:

The Illusion of Efficiency: Musk's DOGE Debacle

In what reads like a tragicomic saga of ambition clashing with reality, Elon Musk's tenure at the helm of the so-called Department of Government Efficiency (DOGE) has culminated in a spectacle of self-destruction and chaotic mismanagement. With a staggering $288 million of his own wealth squandered in a futile attempt to re-elect Donald Trump, Musk's political adventure has ended not with the bang of transformative success but with the whimper of a tarnished legacy.

The High Cost of Political Ambition

Musk, a figure once synonymous with futuristic innovation and unbridled entrepreneurial spirit, has seen his reputation crumble under the weight of political toxicity. Axios's report paints a picture of a man who, despite his vast resources and influence, has managed to diminish his fortune by a jaw-dropping $122 billion in a single year. This financial hemorrhage is nearly as astonishing as the claimed $160 billion saved by DOGE—a figure that experts like Jessica Riedl of the Manhattan Institute have called out as wildly exaggerated.

A Legacy of Chaos

Under Musk's stewardship, DOGE's legacy is one of "chaos and contested savings" rather than the promised efficiency and streamlined governance. His tenure has been marred by public relations disasters, including the severe backlash against his drastic federal program cuts, the elimination of thousands of government jobs, and the egregious hacking into the private data of millions of Americans. These actions have not only eroded Musk's personal brand but have also precipitated a crisis for his companies, with Tesla experiencing a 71% plunge in net income.

Misguided Priorities

The report underscores a glaring irony in Musk's approach: the purported savings from slashing government spending are eclipsed by the revenue losses stemming from cuts to the Internal Revenue Service's tax enforcement. This not only questions the efficacy of DOGE's strategies but also highlights the myopic vision of prioritizing spending cuts over ensuring the government's ability to generate revenue. It's a classic case of being penny-wise but pound-foolish, making a mockery of any claims that DOGE was about reducing deficits.

Conclusion: A Cautionary Tale

Elon Musk's foray into government, characterized by a blend of hubris and mismanagement, serves as a cautionary tale about the perils of entrusting political neophytes with the complex machinery of governance. The DOGE saga is a testament to the fact that business acumen does not necessarily translate to political savvy or governance expertise. As Musk walks away from this debacle, one can only hope that this episode serves as a sobering reminder of the need for experienced, knowledgeable, and principled leadership in government.

In the end, Musk's tenure at DOGE will be remembered not for the efficiency it promised but for the inefficiency it epitomized. The only efficiency demonstrated was in the rapid dismantling of his own reputation and the squandering of his fortune. A legacy of self-destruction, indeed.
