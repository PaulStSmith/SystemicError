Kristi Noem shocks with out-of-control 'screaming' at DHS staff meeting: insiders

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosSeptember 23, 20254 min read

Homeland Security Secretary Kristi Noem speaks to employees at the Department of Homeland Security, Tuesday, Jan. 28, 2025, in Washington, U.S. Manuel Balce Ceneta/Pool via REUTERS/File Photo

Homeland Security Secretary Kristi Noem stunned department officials with profane outbursts reacting to a series of critical news reports examining her chaotic leadership.

The former South Dakota governor and her de facto chief of staff Corey Lewandowski gathered DHS officials to complain about June reports showing that she demanded to personally sign off on payments over $100,000, which caused delays and bottlenecks throughout the department. Employees told New York Magazine the pair – rumored to be a couple – harangued them in harsh terms.

“They were screaming!" a DHS employee told the magazine. “The level of disrespect and screaming at everybody in that room — I think people were really shocked and taken aback.”

Another former DHS staffer said Noem and Lewandowski were clearly embarrassed by the reports, and they said the duo fed off one another's negative energy as Noem dropped “multiple F-bombs" on subordinates. Sources said they were baffled when the pair accused others in the room of “lining their pockets” from government contracts – which struck them as projection.

“People are scared s---less of Corey,” said an administration official.

Lewandowski, a former Donald Trump campaign adviser, has been serving on a temporary basis as Special Government Employee after the White House ended his campaign to be DHS chief of staff over his alleged payments from foreign governments and rumors of an affair – the "worst-kept secret in DC," according to one official – between the two, who are each married to other people.

“Everything has to go through Corey," said a lobbyist who has done work with DHS. “It’s all based on ‘You’re my buddy, or you’re not my buddy. You hired my friend, or you didn’t hire my friend.’ That place just runs that way."

Our Analysis:

Analysis: The Chaotic Leadership of Homeland Security Secretary Kristi Noem

In what can only be described as a spectacle of unprofessionalism and mismanagement, Homeland Security Secretary Kristi Noem has reportedly stunned department officials with her conduct. According to a detailed piece by New York Magazine, Noem, along with her de facto chief of staff Corey Lewandowski, has turned the Department of Homeland Security (DHS) into a veritable theater of the absurd.

Profanity-Laden Leadership

The reports of Noem's profane outbursts in response to critical news coverage of her leadership style are alarming. It’s one thing for leaders to challenge criticism or seek to correct public narratives, but it’s entirely another to resort to screaming and hurling insults at the very officials tasked with managing national security. Such behavior doesn't just undermine professional decorum; it erodes the foundational trust and respect necessary for any high-functioning team.

Bottlenecks and Delays

Noem’s demand to personally sign off on payments exceeding $100,000 is a telling example of micromanagement gone awry. This bottleneck, ostensibly created in the name of oversight, likely did more to hamper the department's efficiency than to safeguard its coffers. It’s a classic case of leadership failing to see the forest for the trees, prioritizing control over effectiveness.

A Questionable Partnership

The dynamic between Noem and Lewandowski raises more than a few eyebrows. Lewandowski's role as a Special Government Employee, following a thwarted bid to become DHS chief of staff amid allegations of receiving payments from foreign governments and rumors of an affair with Noem, is problematic. This arrangement reeks of cronyism and conflict of interest, turning the DHS into a playground for personal vendettas and alliances rather than a stalwart of national security.

Accusations of Projection

The irony of Noem and Lewandowski accusing others of "lining their pockets" from government contracts cannot be overstated. This accusation, coming from individuals embroiled in their own controversies, is a textbook example of projection. It’s an attempt to deflect from their shortcomings by casting aspersions on others, further undermining the integrity of the department.

The Culture of Fear

The climate of fear instigated by Lewandowski, as reported by administration officials and lobbyists, is antithetical to the principles of good governance. Leadership that relies on fear, favoritism, and intimidation is not leadership at all. It's a regime of insecurity and instability, where decisions are made not based on merit but on personal loyalty.

Conclusion

The situation within the DHS, as outlined in these reports, is untenable. Leadership that resorts to profanity, micromanagement, cronyism, and intimidation not only fails its immediate constituents but also compromises national security. The behavior of Noem and Lewandowski should not just be a cause for concern; it should be a clarion call for comprehensive reform. For the sake of the department and the nation, let's hope that call is heeded.
