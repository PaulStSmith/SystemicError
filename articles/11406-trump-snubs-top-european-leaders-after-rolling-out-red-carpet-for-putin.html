President Donald Trump and his advance team were highly criticized as images of U.S. soldiers on their hands and knees rolling out a red carpet in front of Russian President Vladimir Putin’s plane on the tarmac in Alaska made the rounds on the internet on Friday.

But the President of the United States was not on hand to greet the seven European leaders who jetted to Washington, D.C. and headed to the White House on Monday.

Critics are angered that Trump instead had U.S. Chief of Protocol Monica Crowley at the door to welcome them.

“A gathering of so many European leaders at the White House at one time with such little notice is unprecedented in recent times,” according to The Daily Beast. The leaders include: British Prime Minister Keir Starmer, European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen, Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni, French President Emmanuel Macron, German Chancellor Friedrich Merz, Finnish President Alexander Stubb, and Secretary General of NATO Mark Rutte.

Trump did take the time to boast that he was hosting the European leaders: “A big day at the White House. We have never had so many European Leaders here at one time. A great honor for America!!! Let’s see what the results will be??? President DJT.”

Trump greeted Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy at the White House, but no red carpet.

Our Analysis:

A Tale of Two Greetings: Unpacking the Dichotomy in Diplomatic Decorum

In a recent spectacle of diplomatic dissonance, President Donald Trump has, once again, stirred the pot of international politics with actions that can only be described as bewilderingly inconsistent. The internet was ablaze with images that seemed torn from a satirical sketch, showcasing U.S. soldiers on their hands and knees, painstakingly rolling out a red carpet for Russian President Vladimir Putin’s aircraft in Alaska. Meanwhile, a slew of European leaders received a markedly different welcome as they arrived in Washington, D.C., only to be greeted by the U.S. Chief of Protocol rather than the President himself.

The Red Carpet Rollout: A Visual Metaphor?

Let's dissect the imagery here: American soldiers, symbols of the nation's military prowess and sovereignty, are seen kneeling before the plane of Putin, a leader widely criticized for his authoritarian regime and alleged war crimes. This scene, now immortalized as Putin's phone wallpaper, is a disturbing visual metaphor for subservience. It's an image that reeks of irony and begs the question: Why would the administration choose such a submissive posture towards Russia?

A Snub of Historical Proportions

Contrast this with the reception of seven European leaders, including prominent figures like British Prime Minister Keir Starmer and French President Emmanuel Macron. These leaders, representing some of America's oldest and most steadfast allies, were met with what can only be described as a diplomatic cold shoulder. The absence of President Trump at such a significant gathering is not only a breach of protocol but a clear message of where his priorities lie. It’s as if the administration is more interested in rolling out the red carpet for adversaries rather than allies.

Trump's Priorities: A Question of Allegiance

Trump's boast about the gathering of European leaders at the White House only adds insult to injury. His tweet, proclaiming the event as a "great honor for America," rings hollow in the absence of any meaningful engagement. It's akin to inviting guests to a banquet only to leave them at the doorstep. Moreover, the Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy's reception devoid of any ceremonial pomp further underscores the administration's skewed priorities.

Conclusion: The Fallout of Flawed Diplomacy

This tale of two greetings is more than a mere faux pas; it's a glaring indictment of the Trump administration's approach to foreign policy. The preferential treatment afforded to Putin, juxtaposed with the lukewarm welcome extended to European allies, sends a dangerous message on the international stage. It speaks to a fundamental misunderstanding of the values and allegiances that underpin American foreign policy.

As we stand witness to these bewildering events, one can't help but ponder the long-term impacts of such diplomatic blunders. The administration's actions not only erode the trust and respect of our allies but embolden our adversaries. In the grand chessboard of international relations, Trump's moves are akin to sacrificing one's queen for a pawn. The question that remains is: Who will bear the brunt of these systemic errors?

In the end, the saga of the red carpet rollout is a stark reminder of the importance of consistent, principled diplomacy. As the saying goes, "Actions speak louder than words." Unfortunately, the actions of the Trump administration speak volumes, and the message they convey is one of disarray and misplaced priorities.
