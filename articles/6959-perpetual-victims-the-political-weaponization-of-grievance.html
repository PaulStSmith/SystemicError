Perpetual Victims: The Political Weaponization of Grievance

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosJanuary 5, 20264 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

The Closed Loop of Eternal ThreatPower Without ResponsibilityThe Apocalyptic TemplateWhy Facts Bounce OffManufactured FragilityThe License That FollowsThe Real Danger

There is a common assumption among liberals, technocrats, and institutionalists: that claims of persecution coming from the right are not real, but the result of brainwashing—of a rank-and-file audience marinated in right-wing media, fear narratives, and manufactured outrage.

That assumption is only half right.

Yes, the base has been conditioned. Yes, grievance is taught, reinforced, and monetized. But what has become impossible to ignore is this: the mentality does not stop at the bottom. It runs all the way to the top of the hierarchy, including those who have objectively “won” the capitalistic game.

That realization forces a recalibration.

What we are witnessing today—across authoritarian movements, reactionary politics, and apocalyptic belief systems—is not fear as a response to powerlessness. It is victimhood as a strategy. A deliberately cultivated posture that converts dominance into moral innocence and accountability into persecution.

This isn’t paranoia confined to the misinformed masses. It’s infrastructure—shared, reinforced, and actively maintained by people with immense wealth, institutional access, and political power.

The Closed Loop of Eternal Threat

The modern grievance narrative operates inside a sealed epistemic system. Within that system, evidence is not evaluated; it is sorted. Anything that confirms the sense of impending doom is amplified. Anything that contradicts it is reframed as proof of conspiracy.

The logic is brutally efficient:

  • If the feared crackdown never happens, it proves vigilance worked.

  • If it does happen, it proves the warning was justified.

There is no falsification condition. No failure state. The belief cannot be disproven because it is not designed to describe reality—it is designed to preserve identity.

This is why timelines are always vague. Why threats are always existential but never specific. Why the enemy is omnipresent yet strangely incompetent. Precision would make the claim testable. Vagueness makes it eternal.

Power Without Responsibility

One of the most destabilizing features of contemporary politics is watching movements that control courts, legislatures, police forces, media conglomerates, and vast financial networks continue to speak as though they are hunted dissidents.

This isn’t contradiction. It’s camouflage.

Victimhood grants three extraordinary advantages:

  1. Preemptive moral absolution – Any future action, no matter how extreme, becomes “self‑defense.”

  2. Immunity from accountability – Criticism is reframed as persecution, investigation as harassment, law as tyranny.

  3. Epistemic insulation – Contrary facts are dismissed as propaganda by the persecutor.

Once adopted, grievance turns power into innocence. It allows domination while preserving the self‑image of righteousness.

The Apocalyptic Template

This structure is not new. It is borrowed—almost verbatim—from apocalyptic religious movements.

In those systems, believers are taught to live in a constant state of imminent catastrophe. The end is always near, but never arrives. Each failed prophecy strengthens the faith rather than weakening it. Survival becomes proof of chosenness.

Politics has simply imported the template.

Replace divine judgment with demographic change. Replace heresy with dissent. Replace salvation with “taking the country back.” The mechanics are identical.

The point is not prediction. The point is permanent mobilization.

Why Facts Bounce Off

This is why data, charts, court rulings, and expert testimony so often fail to penetrate grievance‑based movements.

Facts are not competing inputs. They are treated as hostile acts.

When identity is anchored in persecution, disproving the threat feels like an attempt at erasure. To accept evidence would mean surrendering the moral framework that justifies anger, hierarchy, and exclusion.

You cannot “debunk” a belief that functions as a social glue.

Manufactured Fragility

Perhaps the most corrosive element of this system is how it redefines strength as vulnerability.

Any demand for equal treatment becomes an attack.
Any loss of unearned privilege becomes oppression.
Any refusal to defer becomes violence.

This inversion allows those at the top of social hierarchies to present themselves as the most endangered people in the room—while wielding the most power.

It is not accidental. It is taught.

The License That Follows

Once a group internalizes the belief that it is under existential threat, normal ethical constraints dissolve.

Cruelty becomes precaution.
Authoritarianism becomes protection.
Dehumanization becomes realism.

And because the threat is eternal, the emergency never ends.

History shows us where this leads. Not in a single dramatic collapse, but in a slow normalization of the unthinkable—each step justified as necessary, reluctant, defensive.

The Real Danger

The most dangerous thing about grievance politics is not that it is false.

It is that it is coherent.

Internally consistent. Emotionally reinforcing. Socially contagious.

It offers certainty in chaos, innocence in power, and meaning in resentment. And once it takes hold, it does not require lies so much as it requires participation.

That is why it spreads. That is why it persists. That is why it survives exposure.

This is not a misunderstanding waiting to be clarified.
It is a system that must be named, confronted, and dismantled.

Because a society that allows its most powerful actors to permanently cosplay as victims is a society preparing itself for cruelty—with a clean conscience.
