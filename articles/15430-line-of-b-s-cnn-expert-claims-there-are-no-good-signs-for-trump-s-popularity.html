'Line of B.S.': CNN expert claims 'there are no good signs' for Trump's popularity

As President Trump celebrates his first 100 days in office, national polls are showing Americans might not be happy about it because Trump's approval ratings are on the decline.

CNN's chief data analyst Harry Enten broke down the numbers, claiming those who argue the polls are off, “quote, unquote, ‘you can't trust the polls.’ Now, that is a line of B.S. That is a scientific term for it is B.S. What am I talking about here? Well, let's just talk about Trump's average net approval rating. I average across five recent national polls among registered voters. So we're talking voters here, not adults. Currently he's averaging, get this, -12 points way underwater,” Enten said.

“But okay, let's say the polls are off like they were in 2024. What would his net approval rating be? Well, it looks pretty gosh darn similar. It's -10 points. He's still way underwater. Historically low for a president only basically matching himself last time around.”

Enten noted this is way below Presidents Joe Biden, Barack Obama, and George W. Bush.

He then broke down what the rating would look like if the polls were off like they were in 2018. “Get this, his net approval rating, we apply that same error, would be -13 points, which is actually a point worse than it currently is, according to the average of the national polls, at -12 points. That is because in 2018, the national polls actually underestimated democrats when it came to the national house vote,” Enten recalled.

“So if the polls are off like they were in 2018, that does Donald Trump no favors in terms of his popularity, it's actually slightly worse, and one last thing I will point out, it is Trump's net approval rating, we take a look at all the five national polls underwater, underwater, underwater, underwater, underwater, all by double digits. At this particular point, there are just no good signs for Donald Trump when it comes to his popularity, at least when it comes to his net approval rating.”

Our Analysis:

Trump's Approval Rating: A Dive into the Depths

In the endless circus that is American politics, the spotlight once again falls on President Donald Trump as he marks his first 100 days in office. A period traditionally seen as a honeymoon phase for new commanders-in-chief, Trump's tenure thus far has been anything but. According to CNN's chief data analyst, Harry Enten, Trump's approval ratings are not merely in a slump; they're submerged. Enten's analysis cuts through the fog of political spin with the precision of a scalpel, revealing a president whose popularity is not just low but historically so.

The Myth of Misleading Polls

Enten addresses a common refrain among Trump's supporters and skeptics of polling in general: the idea that polls are inherently unreliable or biased. This argument is often trotted out as a convenient scapegoat when numbers don't align with one's preferred narrative. However, Enten dismisses this notion as, in his words, "B.S." - a blunt yet fitting description for a baseless claim. His analysis is grounded in data from five recent national polls among registered voters, not just any adults with an opinion. This distinction is crucial, emphasizing that these numbers reflect the sentiments of those with the power to vote, not just voice their opinions.

A Historical Low

Trump's current average net approval rating sits at a dismal -12 points. Even entertaining the possibility of polling inaccuracies does little to improve this picture; adjusting for potential errors still leaves Trump "way underwater." Compared to predecessors like Joe Biden, Barack Obama, and George W. Bush, Trump's ratings are abysmally low, highlighting a stark departure from historical norms for presidential popularity.

The 2018 Precedent

Enten further dismantles the polling skepticism by referencing the 2018 national polls, which, if anything, underestimated Democrats' performance. Applying the same error margin from 2018 to today's numbers would actually worsen Trump's approval rating to -13 points. This point is particularly damning, illustrating that even in a scenario where polls undersell Democratic support, Trump's popularity doesn't just suffer; it sinks even lower.

No Silver Lining

Throughout Enten's analysis, the theme is clear: Trump is facing an uphill battle in terms of public perception. With each of the five national polls indicating double-digit disapproval, the message from the American electorate is unmistakable. Despite what Trump or his ardent supporters might claim, the data reveals a presidency in peril, at least in the court of public opinion.

In sum, Harry Enten's breakdown is a sobering reminder of the chasm between Trump's presidential performance and the expectations of the American public. It's a narrative that goes beyond partisan politics, touching on the fundamental principles of leadership, accountability, and the democratic process. As Trump's first 100 days come to a close, one can't help but wonder: is this a temporary dip or a harbinger of things to come? Only time will tell, but for now, the numbers speak volumes, and they do not bode well for the 45th president.
