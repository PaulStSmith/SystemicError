Analyst rocked as voters ‘shift heavily’ against Trump on key issue: ‘Really telling’

Once one his strongest performing issues, President Donald Trump’s immigration policy has continued to tank among voters amid his administration’s chaotic immigration enforcement operations in Minnesota, and, according to a new poll conducted after the killing of Alex Pretti, has reached stunning new lows among voters that left one election analyst rocked.

“In these elections that are won on the margins, particularly in the swing states, independents are a really critical group,” said Marc Trussler, senior elections analyst for NBC News’ Decision Desk in the outlet’s report Wednesday. “To see them shift so heavily on one of the president’s key priorities is really telling.”

Kicking off last December, the Trump administration’s immigration enforcement operation in Minnesota – dubbed Operation Metro Surge – has been chaotic, violent and deadly, with videos of altercations between federal immigration enforcement officials and protesters spreading wildly on social media.

The chaos has dragged Trump’s favorability down significantly, with a private GOP poll from January revealing that a staggering 60% of independent voters rejected Trump’s immigration policy outright, a significant increase from when the president took office. And now, in the wake of two high-profile killings by federal immigration officials, those numbers have only worsened for Trump.

According to the poll, conducted by NBC News between Jan. 27 and Feb. 6, a total of 60% of Americans either somewhat or strongly disapprove of Trump’s handling of immigration, a staggering increase from the 49% who disapproved recorded last June. Furthermore, 65% of voters said the United States was “on the wrong track,” a jump of five percentage points since last April.

Trump has appeared to recognize the political liability his immigration enforcement operations in Minnesota have become, sidelining his own Homeland Security secretary in dispatching his border czar, Tom Homan, to take over the operation in Kristi Noem’s stead, though according to Trussler, such moves are too little, too late.

“Those things hadn’t moved the needle that much,” Trussler said. “It is really the chaos of Minnesota that has done it.”

Our Analysis:

The Downfall of Trump’s Immigration Policy: A Critical Analysis

In an era where political turbulence is the norm, the recent nosedive of President Donald Trump’s immigration policy among voters, especially in the wake of the chaotic enforcement operations in Minnesota and the tragic killing of Alex Pretti, presents a striking case of political miscalculation and its repercussions. The details, as reported by NBC News, reveal a stark reality: a policy once touted as a cornerstone of Trump’s administration is now a significant liability, particularly among independent voters—a demographic crucial in swing states.

The Crux of the Crisis

Operation Metro Surge, initiated in December under Trump’s directive, has been embroiled in controversy, marked by violence and chaos. This operation, intended to enforce immigration laws more stringently, has instead led to widespread criticism and condemnation, as evidenced by the spread of altercation videos on social media. These incidents have not only marred the administration’s public image but have also significantly impacted Trump’s favorability ratings, plunging them to new lows.

A private GOP poll from January starkly reveals the changing tide, with a staggering 60% of independent voters outright rejecting Trump’s immigration policy. This figure is not just a number; it’s a glaring red flag for an administration that once prided itself on its tough stance on immigration.

The Polls Speak Louder Than Words

The numbers are indeed grim. According to an NBC News poll conducted between January 27 and February 6, 60% of Americans either somewhat or strongly disapprove of Trump’s handling of immigration. This is a significant jump from the 49% who disapproved last June. Furthermore, 65% of voters believe the United States is “on the wrong track,” a sentiment that has seen a five percentage point increase since last April.

These figures are not just statistics; they are a testament to a growing disenchantment with Trump’s policies. The decision to sideline the Homeland Security secretary in favor of Tom Homan, the border czar, in managing the Minnesota operations is a move that reeks of desperation. As Marc Trussler, senior elections analyst for NBC News’ Decision Desk, aptly puts it, these are efforts that are “too little, too late.”

The Significance of Independent Voters

The shift in sentiment among independent voters is particularly telling. These individuals, often seen as the balance in tightly contested elections, have swung heavily against one of Trump’s key priorities. This shift is not merely a reflection of disapproval but a stark warning that foundational issues can become significant liabilities if mishandled.

Conclusion: A Political Quagmire

The chaos in Minnesota has unmistakably been a turning point in the perception of Trump’s immigration policy. The administration’s attempts to recalibrate and mitigate the fallout have so far been ineffective, highlighting a fundamental disconnect between policy objectives and their execution on the ground.

As we inch closer to elections, the question remains: Can Trump and his administration recover from this self-inflicted wound, or will this mark the beginning of a larger political fallout? The numbers, as they stand, paint a bleak picture for an administration grappling with the consequences of its actions—a scenario that serves as a cautionary tale for the potent mix of politics and policy gone awry.
