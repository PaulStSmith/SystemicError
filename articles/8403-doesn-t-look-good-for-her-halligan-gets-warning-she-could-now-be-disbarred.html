'Doesn't look good for her': Halligan gets warning she could now be disbarred

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosNovember 25, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Our Analysis:The Perilous Path of Lindsey Halligan: A Tale of Legal Reckoning

Lindsey Halligan could face potential disbarment over ethics violations during the indictments of former FBI director James Comey and New York Attorney General Letitia James, which were dismissed on Monday. (Screenshot/CNN)

Lindsey Halligan could face potential disbarment over her actions in the cases involving President Donald Trump‘s enemies former FBI director James Comey and New York Attorney General Letitia James, an analyst said Monday.

Bradley Moss, a national security attorney, told CNN that Halligan’s career future is now in question.

“It doesn’t look good for her in terms of any real idea that she’s going to get to remain there, unless somehow they succeed on appeal,” Moss said. “But a bigger concern I would have about Lindsey Halligan is a lot of what came out, particularly in the Comey case, about how she presented things to the grand jury, that speaks to potential ethics violation, that speaks to potential disbarment at some point by which ever state bar she’s licensed in.

“That’s no small thing... that’s in real jeopardy from what we learned from this whole case.”

United States District Judge Cameron McGowan Currie Monday dismissed the federal government’s indictments, citing that Halligan, who was Trump’s former personal attorney and appointed to her role as Interim U.S. Attorney for the Eastern District of Virginia, was invalid because it was never approved by the Senate.

“This is a mess of their own making and it’s the president’s personal vendetta and it’s blown up in their faces on a Monday morning as we’re getting ready for Thanksgiving,” Moss added.

Our Analysis:

The Perilous Path of Lindsey Halligan: A Tale of Legal Reckoning

In a dramatic twist that reads like a cautionary tale for legal professionals navigating the treacherous waters of high-profile political litigation, Lindsey Halligan, a former personal attorney for President Donald Trump, finds herself at the precipice of potential disbarment. This precarious situation stems from her involvement in cases targeting Trump’s adversaries, notably former FBI Director James Comey and New York Attorney General Letitia James. The gravity of her predicament cannot be overstated, with national security attorney Bradley Moss shedding light on the looming dark clouds over Halligan’s career.

The Core of the Controversy

At the heart of this controversy is Halligan’s conduct in the Comey case, which has raised serious concerns about ethical violations. According to Moss’s analysis on CNN, the manner in which Halligan presented information to the grand jury not only jeopardizes her standing within the legal profession but also signals a profound ethical lapse that could lead to disbarment. This is no mere slap on the wrist; it’s a potential career-ending calamity.

A Judicial Reprimand

Adding insult to injury, United States District Judge Cameron McGowan Currie delivered a decisive blow by dismissing the federal government’s indictments against Comey and James. Judge Currie’s ruling underscored a fundamental flaw in Halligan’s appointment as Interim U.S. Attorney for the Eastern District of Virginia—a role that, critically, was never sanctioned by the Senate. This procedural misstep is emblematic of a broader disregard for legal norms and underscores the chaotic underpinnings of what Moss aptly describes as a “mess of their own making.”

The Vendetta Unravels

What emerges from this debacle is a vivid illustration of the perils of intertwining legal pursuits with personal vendettas. Moss’s commentary lays bare the consequences of such entanglements, portraying a scenario where professional integrity and ethical standards are sacrificed at the altar of political warfare. The fallout from this approach is not only a “Monday morning” headache but a stark warning of the long-term repercussions for those who navigate these murky waters.

A Moment of Reflection

Lindsey Halligan’s predicament serves as a potent reminder of the ethical and professional standards that anchor the legal profession. The potential disbarment she faces is a testament to the severity with which the legal community views deviations from these standards. It’s a moment for reflection for all legal practitioners about the paramount importance of maintaining ethical integrity, even—perhaps especially—when the stakes are high and the political pressures intense.

In sum, the unraveling of Halligan’s career trajectory offers a cautionary tale of the consequences when legal practice is compromised by ethical breaches and procedural shortcuts. The saga underscores the indispensable value of ethical vigilance in the legal profession, serving as a stark reminder that the path of justice demands unwavering adherence to the highest standards of conduct.
