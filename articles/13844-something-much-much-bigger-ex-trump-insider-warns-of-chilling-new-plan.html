Ex-Trump insider Lev Parnas over the weekend noted the situation surrounding the Los Angeles protests, saying "the clock is ticking" as Trump has "troops in the streets, tanks on standby, and plans to crush the resistance."

"I know a lot of you woke up this morning in shock—staring at the footage out of Los Angeles, asking yourselves: How could this happen? Is this real? Is this really happening in the United States of America? But let me be clear, folks—this is real. It is happening. And I need you to know that my sources deep inside Trump World have been warning me that what we witnessed overnight was no isolated event. It’s part of something much bigger. And what I have to tell you today is chilling," he wrote.

Parnas continued:

"Yesterday, when Donald Trump deployed over 2,000 federal troops into Los Angeles under the justification of 'restoring order,' most headlines ran with it like it was some isolated act of retaliation—just another example of Trump punishing blue states. And yes, part of this is personal. Make no mistake: this is revenge against California, against Governor Gavin Newsom, and against Los Angeles Mayor Karen Bass. But it’s not just about settling scores."

According to Parnas, in reality, "This is about control."

"What happened yesterday in Los Angeles was a test run—a dry rehearsal for something much, much bigger. I’ve been hearing from multiple trusted insiders that Trump’s inner circle—particularly Pete Hegseth and some of the more radicalized loyalists embedded at the Pentagon—are actively drafting operational plans for the mass mobilizations expected on June 14th," he added. "This isn’t just about Trump’s birthday. This isn’t just about another vanity parade. This is about shutting down resistance."

Parnas added that Trump "fears the American people rising up on June 14th, in all 50 states, with signs that say No Kings—with voices that remind the world that we are still a democracy."

"That’s why he’s pushing for tanks. That’s why he wants the biggest military display America has seen since the Cold War. Because he wants the optics of power. He wants to smother the story before it even begins. He wants his parade to dominate the headlines, not the marchers in the streets fighting for democracy," he said. "But I need you to understand this: According to my sources, there are discussions happening right now—within Trump’s most trusted circle—about invoking martial law if the protests 'get out of hand.' They’re looking for any excuse. Any video. Any act of violence. Any disruption. That’s all they need to justify a crackdown."

He then warned people not to fall for a trap being set by Trump associates.

"And it gets worse. What I’m being told is that Trump allies—including elements connected to Proud Boys, III Percenters, and other far-right militia networks—are planning to infiltrate the June 14th protests. Not to support them. To sabotage them. Their goal? Create chaos. Spark confrontation. Trigger a response from law enforcement. And then hand Trump the justification he needs to clamp down," Parnas wrote. "Let me say this loud and clear: We cannot give them that excuse."

Parnas went on to encourage peace, and told protesters to "stay vigilant."

Our Analysis:

The Chilling Warning from a Former Trump Insider: A Deep Dive

In a recent revelation that seems ripped straight from a dystopian novel, Lev Parnas, a former associate of Donald Trump, has issued a dire warning about the unfolding situation in Los Angeles—and potentially across the United States. According to Parnas, what we're witnessing is not an isolated incident of authoritarian overreach but a prelude to a much darker design. Let's unpack the layers of this alarming disclosure with the precision of a scalpel, shall we?

Troops, Tanks, and a Terrifying Plan

Parnas begins his ominous message by painting a vivid picture of the military might that Trump has allegedly deployed in Los Angeles: over 2,000 federal troops, with tanks on standby, ready to "crush the resistance." This isn't just a response to protests; this is a show of force that screams of a deeper agenda.

Why California? According to Parnas, this aggressive mobilization isn't just about restoring order. It's personal. Trump, with his penchant for vendettas, is apparently targeting California, Governor Gavin Newsom, and Los Angeles Mayor Karen Bass. Yet, Parnas insists this vendetta masks a more sinister goal: control.

A Test Run for a Darker Scheme

Parnas alleges that the deployment in Los Angeles is merely a "test run" for something far more expansive. Citing sources "deep inside Trump World," he points to plans for mass mobilizations on June 14th, ominously hinting at a nationwide crackdown intended to stifle any form of resistance against the Trump administration.

The Real Fear: Democracy. The crux of Parnas's warning lies in Trump's purported fear of a democratic uprising. With plans for a grandiose military display, Trump aims not only to intimidate but to overshadow and suppress the voices clamoring for democracy.

The Martial Law Threat

Perhaps the most chilling aspect of Parnas's revelation is the discussion of invoking martial law. This isn't about maintaining peace; it's about seizing any pretext for a crackdown on civil liberties, with the sights set on quashing dissent under the guise of national security.

The Sabotage Strategy

Adding another layer to this complex plot, Parnas warns of efforts by far-right militia networks, including the Proud Boys and III Percenters, to infiltrate and sabotage the June 14th protests. This tactic is designed to create the chaos needed to justify a heavy-handed government response.

A Call to Vigilance

Parnas ends his message with a plea for peace and vigilance. It's a call to not play into the hands of those seeking to exploit violence for authoritarian ends.

Analysis and Commentary

Let's be clear: if even a fraction of Parnas's claims are accurate, we're staring down the barrel of a constitutional crisis the likes of which the United States has never seen. The use of military force against civilians, the personal vendettas guiding policy, the potential for martial law, and the sabotage of democratic protests are hallmarks of a tyrannical regime, not a democracy.

Questions Abound. How credible are Parnas's sources? Given his history and connections, it's plausible he has insight into "Trump World." However, skepticism is healthy, and we must ask whether these revelations are part of a larger disinformation campaign or if they hold water.

The Bottom Line. If there's truth to Parnas's warning, then the United States is on the precipice of an authoritarian turn. The implications for democracy, civil liberties, and the very fabric of the nation are profound. As citizens and as a collective, staying informed, peaceful, and vigilant is not just advisable—it's imperative.

In democracy's defense, we must not blink.
