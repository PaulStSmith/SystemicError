'What an embarrassment': Famed rocker lets loose on Trump after White House makeover

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosAugust 20, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

U.S. President Donald Trump and Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy meet at the Oval Office of the White House, amid negotiations to end the Russian war in Ukraine, in Washington, D.C., U.S., August 18, 2025. REUTERS/Kevin Lamarque

Grammy-winning rocker Jack White unleashed a blistering attack on Donald Trump's opulent redecoration of the White House, calling it a transformation into something resembling "a vulgar, gold leafed and gaudy, professional wrestler's dressing room."

The White Stripes frontman's fury was triggered by a photo showing Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy meeting with Trump in an Oval Office now dripping with gold-framed paintings, gold trinkets, and trophies. White didn't hold back in his Instagram post Monday night.

"Look at his disgusting taste, would you even buy a used car from this conman, let alone give him the nuclear codes?" White wrote. "A gold-plated Trump bible would look perfect up on that mantle with a pair of Trump shoes on either side, wouldn't it? What an embarrassment to American history."

Trump's gaudy makeover includes gold cherubs imported from Mar-a-Lago, gold vases, gold urns, gold picture frames, and a gold "Trump" crest over the historic building's entrance. Even the presidential seal on the Oval Office ceiling has been painted gold. The Rose Garden lawn has been paved over and decorated with metal furniture and yellow umbrellas.

White, who previously called Trump an "obvious fascist" and "wannabe dictator," made sure to praise Zelenskyy in contrast: "Also pictured in this photograph, a REAL leader of a nation in a black suit."

The musician has clashed with Trump before, dropping a copyright lawsuit last November after Trump used "Seven Nation Army" without permission. "Don't even think about using my music you fascists," White had warned.

White concluded his latest broadside by predicting Trump supporters will eventually distance themselves: "The people who have supported this professional liar, egomaniac conman all these years will all pretend they never did once he's gone."

Our Analysis:

Jack White's Colorful Critique of Trump's White House Decor

In a recent tirade that's as vivid as the decor it lambasts, Grammy-winning rocker Jack White has taken to Instagram to unleash a veritable storm of disdain towards Donald Trump's redecoration of the White House. Dubbing it a transformation into what could be mistaken for a "vulgar, gold leafed and gaudy, professional wrestler's dressing room," White's commentary wasn't just a critique of aesthetic choices; it was a scathing indictment of what he perceives as the former president's taste, ethics, and leadership.

The Gaudy Gold Makeover

Trump's penchant for gold is well-documented, but according to White, this obsession has reached new heights, or perhaps more aptly, new lows. The Oval Office, once a symbol of American leadership and restraint, has been transformed into a shrine to opulence and excess. Gold-framed paintings, gold trinkets, and trophies now apparently dominate the space, culminating in a gold "Trump" crest over the historic building's entrance and even the presidential seal painted gold. The Rose Garden, once known for its greenery, has been paved over, its natural beauty replaced with metal furniture and yellow umbrellas.

Jack White's disdain for this transformation is palpable. His rhetorical question, "would you even buy a used car from this conman, let alone give him the nuclear codes?" is a cutting jab at Trump's credibility, framed by a critique of his decorating decisions. The mention of a "gold-plated Trump bible" alongside "Trump shoes" is a masterstroke in satire, highlighting the perceived narcissism that fueled such decisions.

A Contrast in Leadership

Amid his critique of Trump's aesthetic and ethical choices, White makes it a point to praise Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy, who was pictured with Trump. Describing Zelenskyy as a "REAL leader of a nation in a black suit," White draws a stark contrast between the two figures, underscoring his view of Trump as more showman than statesman.

The Clash with Trump's Camp

White's feud with Trump isn't new. The musician previously dropped a copyright lawsuit against Trump for using "Seven Nation Army" without permission, warning, "Don't even think about using my music you fascists." This historical context adds depth to his current criticisms, painting a picture of long-standing opposition to Trump's politics and personality.

Predictions of Regret

In a poignant conclusion to his broadside, White predicts that Trump supporters will eventually disavow their allegiance, saying, "The people who have supported this professional liar, egomaniac conman all these years will all pretend they never did once he's gone." This statement not only criticizes Trump but also indicts those who've supported him, suggesting a future of collective amnesia and regret.

Conclusion

Jack White's critique is more than just a commentary on Trump's taste—it's a reflection on the intersection of aesthetics, ethics, and leadership. Through sharp wit and scathing analysis, White not only decries the physical transformation of the White House but also challenges the moral and ethical transformations he perceives under Trump's tenure. Whether one agrees with White's assessment or not, it's clear that the rocker's words are a colorful addition to the ongoing discourse surrounding one of the most polarizing figures in recent American history.
