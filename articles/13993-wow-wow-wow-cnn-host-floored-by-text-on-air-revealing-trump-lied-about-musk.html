'Wow wow wow:' CNN host floored by text on air revealing Trump lied about Musk

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosJune 6, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

CNN

President Donald Trump finally responded to Elon Musk's criticism of his "big beautiful bill" and commented on the black eye he sported in his White House farewell, and CNN's Dana Bash received a text while on air pointing out the significance of those remarks.

The president's billionaire benefactor left his role as a special government employee last week, and days later he harshly criticized the Republican budget bill, but Trump did not comment on Musk's remarks until Thursday afternoon in an Oval Office meeting with German chancellor Friedrich Merz.

"You know, I can understand why he's upset," Trump said. "Remember he was here for a long time. You saw a man who was very happy when he stood behind the Oval desk and, even with the black eye, I said, 'Do you want a little makeup? We'll get you a little makeup.' But he said, 'No, I don't think so,' which is interesting and very nice. He wants to be who he is, so you could make that statement, too, I guess."

"Look, Elon and I had a great relationship," Trump added. "I don't know if, well, anymore. I was surprised because you were here. Everybody in this room practically was here as we had a wonderful sendoff. He said wonderful things about me. You couldn't have nicer, said the best things."

While the president's response to Musk's criticism, and the apparent distance he placed between himself and the tech mogul, were more newsworthy, Bash noted that Trump's comments about the black eye were also notable in their own right.

"As we go to break, I don't want to harp on the black eye too much, but I just want to say that a colleague just texted me to remind me that he, at the time, he said he didn't notice Musk's black eye," Bash said, "and now he said, there it is, and now he said, 'Oh, I offered to give him makeup.' So he very much noticed the black eye. He wants that to be in the zeitgeist, along with other things."

"Just wow," Bash added. "Wow wow wow."

Our Analysis:

The Art of Political Distraction and Ego

In the latest episode of political theater, President Donald Trump's response to Elon Musk's criticism of the so-called "big beautiful bill" offers a masterclass in the subtle art of distraction and the not-so-subtle art of ego preservation. The scene is set in the Oval Office, with German Chancellor Friedrich Merz as an unwitting backdrop to this peculiar American drama.

The Musk Critique

First, let's dissect the core issue: Elon Musk, the billionaire with a penchant for space and electric automobiles, suddenly finds himself at odds with the Republican budget bill. Musk's departure from his role as a special government employee and his subsequent criticism could have been a moment for introspection or policy defense from Trump. Instead, the president's delayed response focuses less on the substance of Musk's critique and more on a bizarre anecdote involving a black eye and a refusal of makeup.

The Black Eye Distraction

Trump's anecdote about Musk's black eye at the White House farewell is a fascinating diversion. It's as if Trump is attempting to shift focus from a significant policy critique to a minor physical imperfection and Musk's choice to embrace authenticity over cosmetic cover-up. This maneuver is classic Trump, using personal anecdotes to cloud the issue at hand. However, the president's subsequent comments, "He wants to be who he is, so you could make that statement, too, I guess," reveal a deeper intent to humanize Musk while simultaneously distancing himself from the billionaire's criticism.

The Ego Preservation

Trump's statement, "Look, Elon and I had a great relationship. I don't know if, well, anymore," serves as a stark reminder of the transactional nature of Trump's world. Loyalty and praise are paramount; criticism, even from a former ally, is met with public rebuke. Yet, in his rebuke, Trump cannot help but remind us of the "wonderful sendoff" and the "best things" Musk said about him. This is Trump's ego at work, preserving his image as a beloved leader even as he acknowledges a rift.

The Media's Role

CNN's Dana Bash's reaction, "Just wow. Wow wow wow," encapsulates the media's fascination with Trump's antics. Her focus on the black eye anecdote, while acknowledging its newsworthiness, also plays into the distraction Trump so often seeks to create. The media's challenge remains to navigate these distractions without losing sight of the substantive issues at hand.

Conclusion

In this latest act, Trump demonstrates his adeptness at controlling narratives, even in the face of criticism from powerful figures like Elon Musk. The black eye story, while seemingly trivial, serves multiple purposes: it distracts from Musk's critique, preserves Trump's ego by highlighting past praise, and forces the media to engage with the spectacle rather than the substance. As the political theater continues, one can't help but wonder: what crucial issues are being obscured by these dramatic diversions?
