At the conclusion of Donald Trump's speech to graduating West Point cadets on Saturday, CNN host Victor Blackwell called portions of Trump's comments a "political rally" and CNN reporter Kevin Liptak pointed out that was when the speech fell flat with attendees.

During the address that included his opinion on marrying "trophy wives" –– where he advised the soon-to-be second lieutenants, "A lot of trophy wives doesn't (sic) work out" –– he also brought up his election victory last year while complaining about being investigated

According to Liptack, Trump did not get close to the type of response he normally gets from his MAGA rally crowds.

"I think this was a speech that really kind of combined the traditional commencement speech; he talked to these 1002 cadets who will soon become second lieutenants in the U.S. Army, about his advice, about how he sort of sees them carrying out their mission going forward," Liptak told the host.

"It was also, as you pointed out, something of a political speech in some moments when the president clearly had gone off the teleprompter," he reported before admitting, "It's always interesting to see how those land outside of a political setting."

Our Analysis:

Trump's West Point Speech: A Misguided Attempt at Rallying Troops?

In a display that can only be described as bizarrely off-brand, former President Donald Trump transformed a West Point graduation ceremony into a platform for his political grievances and self-aggrandizement. CNN's coverage of the event, with insights from host Victor Blackwell and reporter Kevin Liptak, paints a picture of a moment that was as cringeworthy as it was inappropriate.

A Political Rally in Disguise?

At a ceremony meant to celebrate the achievements and future service of graduating cadets, Trump seemingly couldn't resist the urge to veer into the political lane, treating attendees to a speech that, as Blackwell aptly described, resembled a "political rally." This deviation was not only out of place but also highlighted a fundamental misunderstanding of the audience and the occasion. The fact that Liptak noted the speech "fell flat" with attendees is hardly surprising. Military graduations are steeped in tradition, honor, and a forward-looking vision towards service, not a backdrop for rehashing election victories or airing personal grievances.

Trophy Wives and Political Grievances

Trump's advice on avoiding "trophy wives" is as laughable as it is lamentable, especially in a setting that demands gravitas and wisdom from its speakers. This, coupled with his inability to steer clear of discussing his electoral victory and subsequent investigations, underscores a startling lack of self-awareness or, perhaps, a deep-seated narcissism that prevents him from recognizing the solemnity of the occasion. Liptak's observation that these moments did not resonate with the audience is a testament to the cadets' understanding that such topics have no place at a military graduation.

The Response: A Telling Silence

While Trump is known for his ability to rile up a crowd, his typical rally rhetoric was met with what can best be described as polite indifference by the West Point audience. This reaction—or lack thereof—speaks volumes. It indicates a clear disconnect between Trump's perception of his audience and the reality of their expectations and values. The fact that these moments were "not sort of an overwhelming crowd like you might hear at a rally," as Liptak points out, reveals a poignant truth: Trump's brand of politics and personal vendettas have little currency in a setting dedicated to honor, duty, and country.

Conclusion: Missed Opportunities and Misplaced Priorities

Trump's speech at West Point was a missed opportunity to inspire, unite, and commend the next generation of military leaders. Instead, it served as yet another reminder of his penchant for making every moment about himself, regardless of the appropriateness or the setting. The cadets, poised to embark on careers of service and sacrifice, deserved better than to be audience to a tired rerun of Trump's political rallies. They deserved a speech that acknowledged their achievements, the challenges ahead, and the vital role they play in safeguarding democracy—themes that were conspicuously absent in Trump's self-centered diatribe.

In the end, this event should serve as a cautionary tale about the importance of understanding your audience and the occasion. Trump's failure to do so not only diminished the significance of the ceremony but also highlighted his ongoing inability to transcend his own ego for the greater good.
