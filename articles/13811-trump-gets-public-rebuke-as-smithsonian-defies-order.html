Trump gets public rebuke as Smithsonian defies order

By Paulo Santos

WASHINGTON DC, US - Feb 15, 2022: The Smithsonian National Museum of American History, Behring Center (via Shutterstock)

The Smithsonian hit back at the Trump administration late Monday, committing to be “free from political or partisan influence" as it refused to kowtow to the president's orders.

“Since its inception, the Smithsonian has set out to be a nonpartisan institution,” the museum's Board of Regents wrote. ‘As the nation’s museum, the Smithsonian must be a welcoming place of knowledge and discovery for all Americans.”

The museum's hit back followed an executive order that President Donald Trump issued to remove "improper ideology" from the Smithsonian museums and the National Zoo, accusing them of trying to rewrite American history on race and gender.

“To reinforce our nonpartisan stature, the Board of Regents has directed the Secretary to articulate specific expectations to museum directors and staff regarding content in Smithsonian museums," the board wrote.

They “give directors reasonable time to make any needed changes to ensure unbiased content, and to report back to the Board on progress and any needed personnel changes based on success or lack thereof in making the needed changes.”

“The Board of Regents will continue its vigilant, independent oversight of the Smithsonian and its museums to protect their rigorous scholarship and expertise, nonpartisanship, and accuracy, and ensure that the Smithsonian is welcoming to all Americans,” the Smithsonian said.

“The Board of Regents is committed to ensuring that the Smithsonian is a beacon of scholarship free from political or partisan influence, and we recognize that our institution can and must do more to further these foundational values,” the board added.

The Smithsonian noted, “All personnel decisions are made by and subject to the direction of the Secretary, with oversight by the Board.”

Our Analysis:

The Smithsonian's Defiant Stand: A Closer Look

The Smithsonian Institution has taken a bold stance that could serve as a beacon of resistance in an era where political interference in educational and cultural institutions seems increasingly brazen. The late Monday announcement, where the Smithsonian vowed to remain "free from political or partisan influence," is not just a rebuttal but a declaration of independence from the encroaching tendrils of the Trump administration's attempts to reshape the narrative on race and gender in America.

The Unyielding Foundation of Nonpartisanship

The Smithsonian's response to President Donald Trump's executive order—which audaciously demanded the removal of what he termed "improper ideology" from the institution—was both swift and unambiguous. The Board of Regents' statement, asserting the Smithsonian's commitment to being a "nonpartisan institution," is a testament to the values upon which the institution was founded. The assertion that the Smithsonian must be a "welcoming place of knowledge and discovery for all Americans" is not just a noble ideal; it's a necessary stance in the face of attempts to politicize educational content.

The Executive Order: A Misguided Assault on Education

Trump's executive order, aimed at sanitizing the Smithsonian's content, is a stark illustration of a government overreach into areas of scholarly research and historical narrative. The audacity to label the institution's exploration of America's fraught history with race and gender as an attempt to "rewrite American history" is not just misguided; it's dangerous. It represents an attempt to erase or dilute uncomfortable truths, a tactic all too familiar in authoritarian regimes—not democratic societies.

The Smithsonian's Strategic Response

The Smithsonian's strategy, as outlined by the Board of Regents, to give directors "reasonable time to make any needed changes to ensure unbiased content," is a clever maneuver. It signals a willingness to engage in self-scrutiny while simultaneously reinforcing the institution's autonomy and scholarly integrity. Moreover, the commitment to report back on progress and any needed personnel changes based on the success or lack thereof in implementing these changes is an indication of the Smithsonian's commitment to transparency and accountability.

The Role of the Board of Regents

The Board of Regents' pledge to continue its "vigilant, independent oversight" is a crucial aspect of this saga. Their role in protecting the Smithsonian's "rigorous scholarship and expertise, nonpartisanship, and accuracy" cannot be overstated. Their commitment ensures that the Smithsonian not only remains a "beacon of scholarship free from political or partisan influence" but also actively works to "further these foundational values."

Conclusion: A Beacon in Dark Times

The Smithsonian's firm stance in the face of political pressure is a reminder of the importance of protecting educational and cultural institutions from the whims of transient political agendas. The assertion that "all personnel decisions are made by and subject to the direction of the Secretary, with oversight by the Board" is a clear message: the Smithsonian will not kowtow to external pressures. It will remain a place where all Americans can learn from a history that is not sanitized for comfort but presented in its full, often uncomfortable, truth. This is the kind of resistance that not only preserves but also advances democracy.
