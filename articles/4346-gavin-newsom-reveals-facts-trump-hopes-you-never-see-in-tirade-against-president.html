Gavin Newsom reveals 'facts Trump hopes you never see' in tirade against president

California Governor Gavin Newsom has ripped into four so-called myths surrounding the rise in oil and gas prices in the United States.

A decision by Donald Trump to join Israel in striking Iran has seen oil prices skyrocket. The war with Iran has worried experts and economists as they remain unsure when prices will drop, though the president has said the costs will stabilize shortly. Newsom’s Press Office has since taken to X and criticized four aspects of the price hike, debunking several myths with a post to the social media platform.

Newsom’s Press Office wrote, “Trump launched military strikes on Iran with no plan for the fallout, and Americans are paying on average $0.56 per gallon more at the pump. Here are the facts Trump hopes you never see.”

The post includes four images, one of which debunks the myth that repealing gas taxes lowers prices. Newsom’s team explained, “Gas tax holidays often become windfalls for oil companies, not savings for drivers. As seen in Florida, where companies pocketed much of the benefits.”

Newsom’s team also pointed out their protections for drivers, which were put in place by the current Governor of California. The team cited both the SBX1-2 and the ABX1-2 as agreements that brought in “transparency and oversight rules for oil companies.”

It comes after a brag from Trump’s administration received further criticism from Newsom and his team.

The Rapid Response X account published a photo of a missile with the words “no mercy” written on top. A brief statement was also issued by the official government account, reading, “We have Only Just Begun to Fight.”

Newsom’s team wrote, “FYI this is costing the American people an extra $1.5billion at the pump this week.” Newsom’s team had previously criticized the government’s rhetoric around the Iran war, with oil price hikes deemed an unforgivable consequence of Trump’s war with Iran.

Further posts from Newsom’s Press Team on Tuesday and earlier this week have suggested the president launched a strike on Iran to distract from controversies within his administration.

A picture of a graph showing the search history for the Jeffrey Epstein files has also been shared by Newsom’s Press Office. The team added, “Trump’s motives for his decisions over the last 2 weeks in one chart.”

Our Analysis:

Unpacking the Political Rhetoric: Newsom vs. Trump on Oil Prices

In an era where political discourse often devolves into a cacophony of blame games and misinformation, California Governor Gavin Newsom’s recent foray onto social media to debunk myths surrounding the spike in oil and gas prices stands out as a beacon of attempted clarity. The causality? A decision by former President Donald Trump to engage militarily with Iran, ostensibly catapulting oil prices to distressing heights. But as with any political narrative, the devil is in the details—or in this case, the myths.

Myth-Busting in the Age of Social Media

Newsom’s Press Office took to X, armed with a quartet of posts aimed at dispelling what they term the “so-called myths” about the oil price surge. The claim? That Trump’s military adventurism in Iran, devoid of a coherent follow-up plan, directly burdened Americans with an additional $0.56 per gallon at the pump. The assertion is bold, direct, and serves a dual purpose: highlighting the unforeseen economic repercussions of foreign policy while simultaneously critiquing the Trump administration’s strategic foresight.

The Gas Tax Holiday Mirage

One of the more incisive critiques involves the debunking of gas tax holidays as an effective means to lower consumer costs. Citing the example of Florida, where such measures resulted in windfall profits for oil companies rather than tangible savings for drivers, Newsom’s team underscores a critical flaw in populist policy proposals: the gap between intention and outcome. This critique not only dismantles the myth but also subtly indicts the corporate greed festering at the heart of the oil industry.

Legislative Safeguards and Political Bravado

In defending California’s approach, Newsom’s team references SBX1-2 and ABX1-2, legislative efforts aimed at injecting transparency and oversight into the opaque operational practices of oil companies. The juxtaposition between California’s proactive legislative stance and the federal government’s bellicose rhetoric is stark. It’s framed as a contrast between pragmatic governance and reckless political posturing, notably critiqued through the lens of a “Rapid Response X” account post featuring a missile emblazoned with “no mercy”. This imagery, coupled with a declaration of enduring combat, is lambasted by Newsom’s office as emblematic of a broader disregard for the domestic fallout of international conflicts.

The Cost of War and Political Distraction

The assertion that Trump’s aggressive maneuvers on the international stage served as a smokescreen for domestic controversies is a serious allegation, bolstered visually by the inclusion of a graph depicting search trends for the Jeffrey Epstein files. This suggests a calculated attempt to divert public attention from scandal through the spectacle of war—a strategy as old as politics itself, yet no less ethically questionable for its age.

Conclusion: The War of Narratives

In dissecting the Newsom vs. Trump narrative on oil price hikes, what emerges is a multifaceted debate that extends beyond mere economics into the realms of foreign policy, environmental stewardship, and the ethical considerations of governance. Newsom’s sharp critique, grounded in a mix of fact and strategic framing, exemplifies the modern political battleground: a war of narratives fought not only through policy but also through the potent medium of social media.

However, as with any narrative constructed in the politically charged echo chambers of social media, readers would do well to approach these claims with a healthy dose of skepticism and a commitment to independent fact-checking. The truth, as they say, often lies somewhere in the middle, obscured by layers of political spin and strategic obfuscation.
