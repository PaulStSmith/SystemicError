Tariffs for Tycoons: How Trump’s Economic Bully Pulpit Became Musk’s Business Development Strategy

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosMay 24, 20253 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

The Con of the Century, Packaged as PatriotismFrom Tariff to Takeover: The Starlink PatternA Hidden Tax for Musk’s Market ShareThe Billionaire Buddy SystemThis Is Not Normal

When Donald Trump launched his tariff war, the story he sold to America was one of blue-collar resurgence: steel mills roaring back to life, coal dust in the air like patriotic confetti, and forgotten towns humming with the rebirth of American manufacturing. What he delivered instead was a masterclass in elite grift — a hidden tax on the working class, wielded like a crowbar to pry open global markets for his billionaire friends. Chief among them: Elon Musk.

The Con of the Century, Packaged as Patriotism

Trump’s tariffs were never about jobs. This much is now crystal clear. They were about leverage — economic warfare masquerading as populist policy. By slapping punitive tariffs on countries from China to Lesotho, Trump didn’t strengthen the American worker; he weakened them. The burden of these tariffs fell squarely on U.S. consumers and small businesses, who watched prices rise while the supposed benefits of “economic nationalism” were siphoned off to the usual suspects: the already-wealthy.

And that’s where Elon Musk glides in, cape billowing, checkbook open.

From Tariff to Takeover: The Starlink Pattern

Reports from The Washington Post, Financial Times, and Times of India have confirmed a disturbing pattern: countries hit with U.S. tariffs, or warned of impending economic penalties, miraculously become fertile ground for Musk’s ventures — especially Starlink.

Take Lesotho, for instance. Shortly after the Trump administration slapped the country with a 50% tariff, Lesotho fast-tracked a 10-year license to Starlink, signaling goodwill and a quiet surrender. In South Africa, Musk refused to comply with the country’s equity rules for telecom firms — but no problem. After a high-profile meeting between Trump and South African President Cyril Ramaphosa, those rules started to “mysteriously” soften.

India, Bangladesh, Cambodia — all show similar trajectories: U.S. pressure rises, regulatory red tape disappears, and Starlink satellites begin orbiting overhead like corporate vultures.

What we’re witnessing is not just capitalism. It’s colonialism via constellation.

A Hidden Tax for Musk’s Market Share

While Musk’s companies skyrocket into new markets, American consumers — the very people Trump claimed to fight for — are stuck footing the bill. Tariffs function as a regressive tax: you don’t see them, but they drain your wallet just the same. From groceries to electronics, the cost of living crept up thanks to Trump’s trade policies. Musk’s global dominance was subsidized by your grocery receipt.

This isn’t just unethical — it’s grotesque.

The Billionaire Buddy System

The Trump-Musk bromance is no secret. Public praise. Private meetings. Overlapping ideologies of deregulation, libertarian cosplay, and social media chaos. But what’s more alarming than the friendship is the infrastructure of mutual benefit built beneath it.

Musk gains access. Trump gains clout. The American public gets screwed.

And if you think this is just business-as-usual, remember: multiple U.S. Senators have now called for investigations into potential ethics violations tied to these deals. Diplomatic cables suggest U.S. envoys were told — explicitly — to advocate for Musk’s business interests under the guise of trade diplomacy.

That’s not just corruption. That’s state capture with a Tesla logo.

This Is Not Normal

Let’s be clear: every administration throws bones to donors. But this is not just a bone — it’s the entire skeleton of U.S. trade policy being bent into a throne for Elon Musk.

Trump weaponized America’s economy not to uplift its people, but to boost his brand and bless his benefactors. Tariffs were the loaded gun. Musk was the golden child. And the rest of us? We were just collateral damage in a con so vast it wrapped itself in the flag and called it freedom.

In the end, Trump's “America First” turned out to be nothing more than “Billionaires First, America Pays.”

The working class got the slogans. Elon got the satellites.

And if we don't start calling this what it is — institutionalized kleptocracy — then we deserve every penny we lose to their next con.

This isn’t a glitch.
It’s the design.
