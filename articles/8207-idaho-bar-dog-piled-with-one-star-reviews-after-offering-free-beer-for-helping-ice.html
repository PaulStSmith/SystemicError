Idaho bar dog-piled with one-star reviews after offering free beer for helping ICE

A bar in Idaho is getting pummeled with one-star reviews online after offering customers “free beer for one month” if they provide proof of having assisted Immigration and Customs Enforcement in deporting an undocumented migrant.

“ALERT: Anyone who helps ICE identify and ultimately deport an illegal from Idaho gets FREE BEER FOR ONE MONTH at Old State Saloon!” reads an online post Saturday from Old State Saloon, a bar and grill in Eagle, Idaho.

The bar’s Google page was immediately flooded with negative reviews, many of which staff at Old State Saloon responded to, calling reviewers “angry progressive liberals” and “liars.”

“And the 1 star reviews roll in from the loser LEFT,” reads another post from the Old State Saloon X account.

One such one-star review reads: “Don’t bother. Place is trashy and overly political.” Old State Saloon staff, in turn, responded to the review.

“Another liar. Politics is not our entire personality. We have locally focussed food and entertainment along with organic coffee that is FREE for veterans!” reads the response from Old State Saloon.

“We host home school meetups, line dancing lessons, karaoke nights, etc. [You] must be an angry progressive liberal to be so sensitive and such a liar.”

Old State Saloon even resorted to attacking one-star reviews that made no mention of the bar’s political activism, such as from one reviewer who called the food “super greasy.”

“Liar,” Old State Saloon responded. “You never came to Old State Saloon. Let me guess, another lib? All you people do is lie, lie, lie.”

The post gained enough traction that the official X account for the Department of Homeland Security reported Old State Saloon’s initial post, drawing even more attention to Old State Saloon from both supporters and critics alike.

“Oh so this is where the Nazis hang out in Idaho?”wroteX user “Wu Tang is for the Children,” a popular political and cultural commentator who’s amassed more than 281,000 followers

Our Analysis:

A Dissection of Old State Saloon’s Controversial Campaign

Old State Saloon, a bar in Eagle, Idaho, recently ignited a firestorm with a promotion that can only be described as deeply troubling and ethically bankrupt. In a clear attempt to align with anti-immigrant sentiments, the establishment offered “free beer for one month” to customers who could prove they assisted Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) in the deportation of undocumented migrants. This move was not just a marketing strategy but a blatant endorsement of vigilantism and a gross violation of human rights principles.

The Backlash: A Digital Uprising

As expected, the bar’s Google page was inundated with one-star reviews, a digital form of protest against such a heinous offer. The response from Old State Saloon was nothing short of vitriolic, labeling critics as “angry progressive liberals” and “liars.” This reaction exposes a deep-seated animosity towards any form of dissent, painting a picture of a business that prioritizes political controversy over customer service and community respect.

A Distortion of Reality

In an attempt to counter the backlash, Old State Saloon claimed that their identity wasn’t solely political, citing their involvement in local events and offerings. However, this defense falls flat when juxtaposed with their aggressive and exclusionary promotional campaign. Their assertion that they are not defined by politics is contradicted by their decision to engage in a politically charged action that directly impacts the lives of individuals and families within the immigrant community.

Attacking the Critics

The bar’s strategy to dismiss all criticism as lies, including complaints about the quality of their food, reveals a troubling tendency to discredit any negative feedback, regardless of its nature. This approach not only undermines the credibility of genuine customer grievances but also highlights a worrying disregard for accountability.

The Bigger Picture: A Reflection of Societal Divides

The incident at Old State Saloon is symptomatic of a broader societal issue: the deepening divide and increasing polarization within communities. By offering incentives for actions that contribute to the suffering of others, Old State Saloon has positioned itself as a catalyst for division, promoting a culture of suspicion and animosity.

The Role of Governmental Intervention

The involvement of the Department of Homeland Security, via its official X account, in reporting the bar’s post, underscores the gravity of the situation. It is a clear indication that governmental bodies are on alert and willing to step in when businesses cross the line from free speech into promoting actions that could lead to harm and discrimination.

Conclusion: A Call for Ethical Business Practices

The Old State Saloon controversy is a stark reminder of the responsibility businesses have to their communities. Engaging in or promoting practices that infringe on the rights and dignity of any group not only damages a business’s reputation but also contributes to societal divisions. It is imperative that businesses operate within a framework of ethical conduct, prioritizing respect, inclusivity, and community well-being over divisive and harmful promotional tactics.
