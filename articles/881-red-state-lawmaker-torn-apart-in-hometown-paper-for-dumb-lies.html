Parasites in the Politics, Not Just the Cattle

The Useful Lie

A Kansas senator took a public-health problem in the beef supply and did what the modern right does best: turned it into an immigrant panic. The immediate target was Joe Biden and migrants from Central America, but the real story in the source material is simpler and uglier. This is not a mystery unfolding by accident. It is a familiar exercise in blame laundering.

Who Actually Had Power

The people with actual institutional power are not the people being blamed. According to the source, the Trump orbit and Elon Musk’s DOGE apparatus helped defund an animal-health program that had been containing screwworm. That is the decision-making center. Roger Marshall is not solving the problem; he is providing a loud, obedient cover story on Newsmax.

That distinction matters. Senators do not just “comment” when they have a platform and a political incentive to protect the administration. They participate in the insulation of power from consequence.

Scapegoating as Administration Style

Marshall’s line about migrants carrying screwworm north is not just ugly. It is politically convenient nonsense. The source points out that scientists attribute the reintroduction primarily to organized crime and the smuggling of illicit cattle from Central America. That is a materially different explanation from Marshall’s xenophobic theater, and he knows the difference well enough to ignore it.

This is the governing style now: turn a policy failure into a racialized morality play, then pretend the lie is public service. Migrants become a dumping ground for elite incompetence. Hispanics become a rhetorical shield for Republican negligence.

The Real Damage Was Enabled Upstream

The source also points to a second layer of institutional failure: DOGE cut the screwworm prevention program, and ranchers were left without meaningful communication. That is not a clerical error. It is a deliberate stripping of capacity, followed by surprise when the damage appears.

This is how sabotage gets normalized. The cuts happen quietly, the harm arrives later, and then politicians perform indignation as if they were bystanders. They are not bystanders. They are the cause, or at minimum the enablers.

When Records Disappear, So Does Accountability

The blank doge.gov page is the most revealing detail in the source. Once the administration boasted about supposed savings; now the record is gone. That is not transparency failing by accident. It is the bureaucracy of evasion. First you cut the program. Then you erase the paper trail. Then you let proxies like Marshall blame foreigners for the wreckage.

That sequence is not confusion. It is institutional cowardice with a communications strategy.

The Pattern Is the Point

The screwworm story is not really about livestock disease. It is about how power protects itself after creating a preventable problem. Defund the public function, manufacture a scapegoat, bury the documentation, and let allied politicians shout at the wrong people on cable television.

That is the governing logic on display here: cruelty upstream, propaganda downstream, accountability nowhere in sight.
