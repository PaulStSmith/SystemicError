Republican who joked about Dem's shooting now outraged about Trump piñata: 'Condemn this!'

A demonstrator hits a pinata, depicting U.S. President Donald Trump during a “No Kings” protest against Trump’s policies, in Chicago, Illinois, U.S., October 18, 2025. (REUTERS/Jim Vondruska)

Sen. Mike Lee (R-UT) demanded that Democrats condemn a video that depicts demonstrators striking a piñata bearing the likeness of President Donald Trump, and went so far as to suggest that such behavior exhibited in the video encourages assassinations.

“This isn’t funny,” Lee wrote in a social media post on X Sunday night, sharing alongside it the video of a child striking a piñata made to look like the president. “This is how assassination culture takes root. Democrats: please condemn this!”

The video was initially shared by the far-right and anti-LGBTQ social media account “Libs of TikTok.”

“They want us all dead,” reads the original post from “Libs of TikTok” on X that was shared by Lee. “They’re teaching their kids to kill us.”

Lee received renewed scrutiny this week for having voiced outrage over those mocking the recent fall of Sen. Mitch McConnell (R-KY), despite himself having mocked former President Joe Biden’s fall boarding Air Force One just months prior, and also making a joke in reference to the fatal shooting of a Minnesota Democratic lawmaker and her husband in June.

Lee has undergone something of a transformation of sorts during his time in office, according to The Atlantic staff writer Tim Alberta, who noted the lawmaker’s former anti-Trump stance had fully reversed by 2020 where he supported the president’s efforts to overturn the results of the presidential election.

For Spencer Stokes, Lee’s first chief of staff in the Senate, the lawmaker’s shift in ideology came down to one matter: respect.

“Had he not gotten caught up in the Tea Party movement when he first got elected, he might have had a very different career,” Stokestold Alberta. “He might have been much more of a mainstream Republican. But Mike craves respect. Those groups on the right gave it to him. And because there were no accolades from the mainstream, he stayed where the accolades were.”

Our Analysis:

The Hypocrisy of Mike Lee: A Deep Dive into Political Opportunism

In a recent spectacle of what can only be described as political theater at its most hypocritical, Sen. Mike Lee (R-UT) has taken to social media to express his outraged sentiments towards a video showing demonstrators, including a child, hitting a piñata resembling President Donald Trump. Lee’s call for Democrats to condemn this act, citing it as a seedling for “assassination culture,” is not just an exaggeration but a glaring example of selective outrage and hypocrisy that seems to pervade his political career.

The Video That Sparked Outrage

The video in question, shared by the controversial and far-right anti-LGBTQ social media account “Libs of TikTok,” depicts a scene that is hardly unfamiliar in the realm of political demonstrations. The act of striking a piñata bearing the likeness of a political figure, while distasteful to some, is a far cry from the incitement of violence or assassination culture that Lee suggests. His dramatic interpretation is not only a gross exaggeration but also a manipulation intended to stir his base under the guise of moral concern.

A Pattern of Selective Outrage

Lee’s selective outrage becomes even more pronounced when considering his own history of mocking or minimizing real instances of harm against political figures. His criticism of those who mocked Sen. Mitch McConnell’s (R-KY) recent fall, contrasted with his own mockery of President Joe Biden’s stumble and a distasteful joke about the fatal shooting of a Minnesota Democratic lawmaker, reveals a disturbing double standard. Lee appears to weaponize empathy and outrage only when it serves his political narrative, undermining the sincerity of his convictions.

The Transformation of Mike Lee

The article also sheds light on Lee’s political transformation, which Tim Alberta of The Atlantic attributes to a desperate quest for respect. Initially an anti-Trump figure, Lee’s complete reversal by 2020, supporting Trump’s unfounded efforts to overturn the election results, underscores a concerning willingness to abandon principle for popularity within the far-right factions of his party. His ideological shift, seemingly rooted in a desire for validation from the Tea Party and similar groups, paints a picture of a politician more concerned with accolades than integrity.

The Real Issue at Hand

What is truly alarming about Lee’s recent outburst is not the video itself but what it represents in the broader context of American politics. Lee’s call for condemnation from Democrats over a piñata incident, while remaining silent or even participatory in far more egregious acts of disrespect and incitement, highlights a pervasive issue of political opportunism and hypocrisy. It’s an exploitation of outrage, where condemnation is reserved not for acts of genuine harm or incitement but for moments that can be politically leveraged.

In Conclusion

Sen. Mike Lee’s reaction to the piñata video is a microcosm of a larger, systemic problem within political discourse. It’s a problem where the lines between genuine concern and political posturing are increasingly blurred, and where the outrage of the day is dictated not by the severity of the act but by its utility in the political arena. This incident should prompt not just condemnation of a distasteful act but a deeper reflection on the state of political integrity and the true meaning of leadership.
