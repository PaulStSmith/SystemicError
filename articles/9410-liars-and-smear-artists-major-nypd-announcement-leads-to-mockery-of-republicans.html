'Liars and smear artists': Major NYPD announcement leads to mockery of Republicans

Donald Trump, Mike Johnson, and other top Republicans were told they should eat crow on Saturday after the New York Police Department contradicted a popular right-wing narrative.

More than 100,000 people in New York gathered for the “No Kings” pro-democracy protests around the U.S. over the weekend, and the GOP had hyped up the notion that these would be violent protesters or even “terrorists.”

But in New York that wasn’t a problem, according to NYPD News.

“The majority of the No Kings protests have dispersed at this time and all traffic closures have been lifted,” the account wrote. “We had more than 100,000 people across all five boroughs peacefully exercising their first amendment rights and the NYPD made zero protest-related arrests.”

That led to mockery online.

Ex-prosecutor Ron Filipkowski sarcastically asked, “So no terrorism then?”

“Every single No Kings protest, all 2,500+ of them across the country, made Mike Johnson and House Republican leadership look like lying sacks of s--- for all the bulls--- that spewed out of their mouths about it this week. Again. Not very Christian,” he added.

Conservative commentator Bill Kristol said, “You know who should just f___ off? Mike Johnson and all those other Trumpist liars and smear artists.”

PatriotTakes, which purports to be “dedicated research monitoring and exposing right-wing extremism and other threats to democracy,” chimed in, “Wait, Republicans said these were all violent terrorists.”

No Lie with Brian Tyler Cohen chimed in, “NYPD announces that zero arrests were made at the massive No Kings protests today after Republicans spent days claiming the protestors are violent terrorists.”

Jon Favreau joked, “Wow NYPD covering up for Antifa smdh.”

Professor Adam Cochran added, “Wow NYPD (not known for being easy going) made ZERO arrests related to protesting in one of the largest NY protests ever.”

“Turns out being against fascism has NOTHING to do with violence!” he further noted.

Our Analysis:

Analysis: The Clash Between Reality and Right-Wing Narratives

The recent “No Kings” pro-democracy protests in New York, and indeed, across the United States, provided a stark contrast to the dire predictions peddled by leading figures in the Republican party, including Donald Trump and Mike Johnson. These predictions were not just erroneous; they were a glaring misrepresentation aimed at discrediting a legitimate exercise of democratic rights. The NYPD’s statement, confirming the peaceful nature of the protests, is not just a slap in the face to these fearmongers but a testament to the resilience of democratic values in the face of baseless vilification.

The Fearmongering Tactic

It’s a classic move in the political playbook: create a monster where there is none to stoke fear and justify draconian measures or discredit movements. The GOP’s narrative that the “No Kings” protests would devolve into violent, terrorist acts is a textbook example. Such a tactic is not only deceptive but dangerous, as it seeks to undermine the very essence of democracy - the right to peaceful assembly and protest.

NYPD’s Clapback

The NYPD, not known for mincing words or actions, delivered a reality check that was as clear as daylight. Over 100,000 people participated in the protests across all five boroughs of New York, exercising their First Amendment rights without a single arrest related to the protests being made. This outcome flies in the face of the GOP’s narrative and underscores a fundamental truth: being anti-fascist or pro-democracy does not equate to violence. This statement from the NYPD should, in a just world, compel figures like Trump and Johnson to eat crow. But will it? Unlikely, as accountability seems to be an alien concept to them.

The Public’s Verdict

The public’s response to the peaceful nature of the protests and the GOP’s failed fearmongering was swift and scathing. Figures like Ron Filipkowski and Bill Kristol did not hold back in their criticism, rightfully calling out the baseless and harmful rhetoric spewed by the GOP leadership. The mockery and condemnation from various quarters, including academics and conservative commentators, highlight a growing impatience with political strategies rooted in lies and fear.

The Bigger Picture

This episode is a microcosm of a larger issue plaguing American politics: the deliberate distortion of facts to serve political ends. The GOP’s narrative around the “No Kings” protests is not an isolated incident but part of a worrying trend where truth is the first casualty in the pursuit of power. Such tactics not only undermine democratic institutions but also sow division and mistrust among the populace.

Conclusion: A Call for Accountability

The peaceful outcome of the “No Kings” protests is a victory for democracy and a clear repudiation of the GOP’s fearmongering. It is a reminder that the right to protest, a cornerstone of democratic societies, can and should be exercised peacefully. However, this incident also underscores the urgent need for political accountability. Figures like Trump and Johnson, who peddle falsehoods with impunity, must be held to account. Democracy is resilient, but it is also fragile, and allowing lies to go unchallenged only serves to weaken its foundations.
