'Crime, savagery, filth, and scum': Trump flips out ahead of National Guard DC deployment

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosAugust 12, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Our Analysis:Trump's D.C. Drama: A Reality CheckTruth vs. Trump's TakeThe Irony of LiberationMisdirection and ManipulationA Call for Rational Discourse

Hours before Donald Trump is expected to hold a press conference to address what he believes is a crime wave occurring in the nation's capital, he took to Truth Social to paint a portrait of a city under siege that does not match crime reports.

The president has been promoting Monday's press availability where he will reportedly state he is deploying at least 1,000 National Guard troops to militarize the streets of Washington, D.C. which led one Fox News guest to claim on Sunday, "I found it troubling, because I also practiced law in the District of Columbia, that the president of the United States would say that crime is out of control. I take exception to that. Crime is not out of control in the District of Columbia."

Undaunted, Trump wrote on Friday morning, "Washington, D.C. will be LIBERATED today! Crime, Savagery, Filth, and Scum will DISAPPEAR. I will, MAKE OUR CAPITAL GREAT AGAIN! The days of ruthlessly killing, or hurting, innocent people, are OVER! I quickly fixed the Border (ZERO ILLEGALS in last 3 months!), D.C. is next!!! Thank you for your attention to this matter. President DJT"

Trump's latest post follows a Truth Social flurry on Monday morning where, in one, he raged at the New York Times, and in another attacked the intelligence of female Democrats while boasting about the cognitive tests he has "aced" — both at a time when he is being scrutinized over his friendship with convicted pedophile Jeffrey Epstein.

Our Analysis:

Trump's D.C. Drama: A Reality Check

Donald Trump's latest crusade, a vow to "liberate" Washington, D.C. from a purported crime wave, is a masterpiece of misinformation, fearmongering, and distortion. Before we dive into the specifics of Trump's declarations, it's crucial to address the glaring discrepancy between his portrayal of a city "under siege" and the actual statistics on crime in the nation's capital.

Truth vs. Trump's Take

Trump's narrative on social media platform Truth Social, where he claims Washington, D.C. will be "liberated" from "Crime, Savagery, Filth, and Scum," is a stark departure from reality. The bold assertion that D.C. is in the throes of a crime epidemic severe enough to warrant the deployment of "at least 1,000 National Guard troops" is misleading at best.

A Fox News guest rightly pointed out the dissonance in Trump's alarmist rhetoric, stating that crime is not "out of control" in D.C. This divergence from factual crime reports is troubling, to say the least. It suggests a deliberate attempt by Trump to manipulate public perception for reasons that may range from diverting attention from personal controversies to attempting to reassert control through a show of force.

The Irony of Liberation

Trump's proclamation that "Washington, D.C. will be LIBERATED today!" is ironic, given that the liberation he proposes comes in the form of militarizing the streets. The very notion that deploying National Guard troops is a means to make the capital "GREAT AGAIN" is fraught with authoritarian undertones. It implies that freedom and safety can only be achieved through force and intimidation, a perspective that undermines the principles of democracy and civil liberties.

Misdirection and Manipulation

It's impossible to ignore the timing and content of Trump's outbursts on Truth Social. Amidst scrutiny over his relationship with convicted pedophile Jeffrey Epstein, Trump has chosen to attack the intelligence of female Democrats and boast about acing cognitive tests. This misdirection tactic is transparent and reflects a desperate attempt to shift focus away from personal scandals.

Moreover, his unfounded claims of fixing the border with "ZERO ILLEGALS in last 3 months" are not only factually incorrect but also contribute to a narrative that seeks to demonize immigrants and glorify his administration's hardline policies.

A Call for Rational Discourse

In an era where truth is often drowned out by sensationalism and fearmongering, it's essential to critically analyze statements made by those in power. Trump's portrayal of Washington, D.C. as a city in dire need of "liberation" from rampant crime does not hold up under scrutiny. It's a narrative constructed for political gain, devoid of substantial evidence and detached from the realities on the ground.

As citizens and consumers of media, we must demand more from our leaders and hold them accountable for spreading misinformation. It's time to reject the politics of fear and division and embrace a discourse grounded in facts, reason, and the genuine pursuit of a safer, more united society.
