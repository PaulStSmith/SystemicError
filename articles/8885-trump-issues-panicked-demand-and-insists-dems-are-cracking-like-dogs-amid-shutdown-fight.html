Trump has increasingly demanded that Senate Republicans eliminate the filibuster – a procedural Senate rule that allows a bill to be blocked if it receives less than 60 votes – as a way to advance his agenda, which includes passing legislation to eliminate “transgender for everybody” and institute more tax cuts.

“The Democrats are cracking like dogs on the Shutdown because they are deathly afraid that I am making progress with the Republicans on TERMINATING THE FILIBUSTER!” Trump wrote on his social media platform Truth Social.

“Whether we make a Deal or not, THE REPUBLICANS MUST ‘BLOW UP’ THE FILIBUSTER, AND APPROVE HUNDREDS OF LONG SOUGHT, BUT NEVER GOTTEN, POLICY WINS LIKE, AS JUST A SMALL EXAMPLE, VOTER ID (IDENTIFICATION). Only a LOSER would not agree to doing this!”

Republicans, however, have remained skeptical at the idea, with some voicing fears that doing so could open the door for Democrats to advance their own policy agendas should they regain the majority in Congress.

Trump has managed to sway some Republican senators, including Sen. Ron Johnson (R-WI), who recently told Raw Story that he was in support of anything Trump wanted. Sen. Tommy Tuberville (R-AL) has also backed Trump’s efforts, writing on social media this week that senators must “blow up the filibuster.”

Others, however, remain skeptical, including House SpeakerMike Johnson(R-LA), who, despite not having the authority to eliminate the filibuster as a member of the House,openly defiedTrump on his request earlier this week.

Our Analysis:

Trump’s Call for Filibuster Destruction: A Dangerous Power Play

President Donald Trump’s latest outburst on his platform, Truth Social, demanding Senate Republicans to “blow up the filibuster,” is not just a typical Trumpian hyperbole but a clear indication of his disregard for democratic principles and the balance of power. His rhetoric, claiming that Democrats are “cracking like dogs” amid the government shutdown, is not only unbecoming of a former president but also emblematic of his divisive politics.

The Filibuster: A Thorn in Trump’s Side

The filibuster, a procedural rule in the Senate requiring a supermajority of 60 votes to pass most legislation, has long been a frustration for presidents seeking to push through their agendas. However, Trump’s call to eliminate it altogether under the guise of achieving “HUNDREDS OF LONG SOUGHT, BUT NEVER GOTTEN, POLICY WINS” is a dangerous proposition. It’s an attempt to bulldoze through controversial policies, such as “transgender for everybody” legislation and further tax cuts, without the need for bipartisan support.

Trump’s Misguided Assault on Democracy

By framing the filibuster as an obstacle to his agenda, Trump is essentially arguing for a majoritarian rule where the minority has no say. His assertion that only a “LOSER” would oppose such a move is not only a gross simplification but a direct threat to the democratic process. The filibuster exists as a mechanism to encourage debate and compromise, essential elements of a healthy democracy.

Republican Skepticism: A Rare Show of Prudence

It’s somewhat reassuring to see that not all Republicans are blindly following Trump’s demands. Figures like House Speaker Mike Johnson (R-LA), though lacking the authority to affect the filibuster, have shown a level of defiance against Trump’s undemocratic urges. This skepticism within the GOP reveals a concern for the potential long-term consequences of such a radical move. After all, eliminating the filibuster would not only serve Trump’s immediate interests but could also backfire on Republicans, opening the door for Democrats to pass their own agendas unchecked should they regain a majority.

A Dangerous Echo Chamber

What’s particularly alarming is the support Trump has garnered from senators like Ron Johnson (R-WI) and Tommy Tuberville (R-AL), who have expressed willingness to “blow up the filibuster.” Such endorsements within Trump’s echo chamber risk pushing the Republican party further into the realms of authoritarianism, where the whims of a single leader outweigh the principles of democratic governance.

Conclusion: A Call for Reason

Trump’s demand to dismantle the filibuster is a testament to his ongoing threat to democratic norms. It’s a power play masked as a fight for policy wins, driven by a desire for unchecked authority. The resistance from within his own party offers a glimmer of hope, but it’s imperative that more Republicans and the broader public recognize the dangerous implications of Trump’s proposal. Democracy thrives on debate, compromise, and the protection of minority voices, not on the eradication of checks and balances for expedient political gains.
