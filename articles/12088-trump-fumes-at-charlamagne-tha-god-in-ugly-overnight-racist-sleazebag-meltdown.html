Trump fumes at Charlamagne tha God in ugly overnight 'racist sleazebag' meltdown

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosAugust 4, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Hours after Charlamagne tha God was interviewed by his daughter-in-law Lara Trump on Fox News, Donald Trump launched a vicious attack on the popular Black podcaster as a "racist sleazebag."

Taking to Truth Social hours after "The Breakfast Club" co-host told Eric Trump's wife that he believes the MAGA movement will falter and that "I think this Epstein thing is going to be a way for traditional conservatives to take their party back, I really do," Trump went on a furious rant that was missing words as he complained about the comments.

"The very wonderful and talented Lara Trump, whose show is a big ratings success, put racist sleazebag Charlamagne 'The God'," he began before not finishing the sentence and switching to, "(Why is he allowed to use the word “GOD” when describing himself? Can anyone imagine the uproar there would be if I used that nickname?)."

He then rambled off a litany of complaints, "He’s a Low IQ individual, has no idea what words are coming out of his mouth, and knows nothing about me or what I have done - like just ending 5 Wars, including a 31 year bloodbath between Republic of the Congo and Rwanda, where Seven Million people have died, and there was no end in sight. He didn’t know that, or India and Pakistan or, wiping out Iran’s nuclear capabilities, or closing the horrendous open Border, or creating the greatest economy, where prices and Inflation have come way down, and where STUPID and CORRUPT JOE BIDEN set the record for doing the Worst Job as President, EVER. "

"But this dope, Charlamagne, would vote for Sleepy Joe or Kamala? Remember, one year ago our Country was DEAD, now it’s the 'HOTTEST' Country anywhere in the World. MAGA!!!" he added.

Our Analysis:

The Art of Deflection: Trump's Rant Against Charlamagne tha God

In a spectacle that is as predictable as it is disappointing, Donald Trump has once again taken to his platform of choice, Truth Social, to unleash a tirade against Charlamagne tha God. The catalyst for Trump's latest outburst? A conversation Charlamagne had with Lara Trump on Fox News, where he expressed his belief that the MAGA movement is on shaky ground, particularly due to the Jeffrey Epstein scandal. Within hours, Trump labeled Charlamagne a "racist sleazebag," an accusation both ironic and telling in its desperation.

The Projection of Insecurity

Trump's rant is a masterclass in projection and insecurity. Beginning his diatribe by attacking Charlamagne's moniker, questioning, "(Why is he allowed to use the word “GOD” when describing himself?)" reveals more about Trump's own ego than any legitimate criticism. The insinuation that Charlamagne's self-chosen nickname is somehow blasphemous, yet ignoring the myriad of self-aggrandizing titles Trump has embraced, underscores a fundamental hypocrisy.

Fact vs. Fiction: Trump's Claims

Trump's response spirals further into the realm of the absurd as he lists his supposed accomplishments, including the fantastical claim of "ending 5 Wars," one of which supposedly resolved a "31 year bloodbath between Republic of the Congo and Rwanda." This statement not only betrays a gross misunderstanding of geopolitical events but also an alarming disconnection from reality. The conflicts between the Congo and Rwanda, while devastating, were not ongoing for 31 years, nor were they singularly resolved by Trump's administration.

Moreover, Trump's assertion of "wiping out Iran’s nuclear capabilities" and "creating the greatest economy" are at best overstated and at worst, fabrications. His administration's approach to Iran's nuclear program was more about dismantling the JCPOA (Iran nuclear deal) than achieving any tangible disarmament, and the economic growth experienced during his tenure continued trends established by his predecessor, only to be overshadowed by the economic catastrophe brought on by the COVID-19 pandemic—a crisis exacerbated by his administration's mishandling.

The Misuse of MAGA

Trump concludes his rant with a rallying cry for MAGA, claiming the United States was "DEAD" a year ago but is now the "'HOTTEST' Country anywhere in the World." This hyperbolic statement not only ignores the systemic issues plaguing the country but also the divisive nature of the MAGA movement itself. Trump's vision of America's greatness is intrinsically linked to a regressive agenda, one that seeks to marginalize and silence voices of dissent—voices like Charlamagne tha God's.

Conclusion: The Irony of a "Racist Sleazebag" Accusation

The irony of Trump calling Charlamagne a "racist sleazebag" cannot be overstated. This is, after all, the same Donald Trump who has a long history of making racially insensitive remarks and promoting policies that disproportionately harm minority communities. His use of such an accusation is a transparent attempt to deflect criticism and project his own faults onto others.

In this latest episode of Trump's social media antics, we're reminded of the dangers of his rhetoric. It's not just the falsehoods and the inflammatory language; it's the clear intention to divide and distract from the real issues at hand. Charlamagne's critique of the MAGA movement and the Epstein scandal's potential ramifications struck a nerve because it hit close to home. Trump's response was to attack, deflect, and distort—a tactic we've seen all too often and one that we must continue to challenge with facts, analysis, and unwavering scrutiny.
