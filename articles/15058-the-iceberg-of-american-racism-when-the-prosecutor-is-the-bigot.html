The ICEberg of American Racism: When the Prosecutor is the Bigot

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosMay 10, 20252 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

When you peel back the bland, bureaucratic facade of ICE, don’t be surprised to find a white hood lurking underneath.

So the mask slips—again. This time, it’s James “Jim” Joseph Rodden, an ICE prosecutor out of Dallas, Texas, who has been exposed as the operator of a white supremacist X account. The same man entrusted with prosecuting immigration cases in the name of the U.S. government was spewing racist bile online, hiding behind a pseudonym but unable to mask the stench of fascism.

Rodden’s now-exposed alter ego, @GlomarResponder, wasn’t just flirting with dog whistles. He was using a bullhorn. His posts celebrated white nationalism, denigrated Black and Brown people, and trafficked in Nazi iconography like it was just another Tuesday. Among the greatest hits of this digital excrement:

  • “America is a White nation, founded by Whites. Our country should favor us.”

  • “Migrants are all criminals.”

  • “All blacks are foreign to my people.”

  • “It is our holy duty to guard against the foreign hordes.”

This isn't a fringe lunatic shouting into the void—this is a federal attorney with prosecutorial power over the lives of immigrants. People who came here seeking safety and a shot at a better life were instead facing a man who saw them as subhuman by default. A man with the power to destroy families and futures, all while marinating in white supremacy behind his desk and his badge.

ICE, of course, is "aware of the allegations." Which is establishment-speak for: “we’re not going to do a damn thing unless forced.” The agency, notorious for its own systemic cruelty, is hardly in a moral position to act shocked. Rodden wasn’t a rogue actor; he was a symptom of the rot.

Let’s be crystal clear: if this man hadn’t been unmasked by the press—not internal oversight, mind you, but reporters doing the job the government refuses to—he would still be happily litigating cases against the very people he publicly dehumanized. That’s not just a conflict of interest. That’s state-sanctioned racial terror in a suit and tie.

And while Representative Marc Veasey has called for an investigation, we must ask: how many more “Roddens” are out there? How many racists are cloaked in federal authority, hiding behind procedure while practicing persecution?

ICE was born from fear, fed by xenophobia, and has now matured into a tool of white nationalist enforcement. This is not a bug—it’s the blueprint.

We cannot fix this with wrist slaps and administrative leave. We need a systemic purge, starting with full transparency, external audits, and criminal accountability. Every prosecution touched by this man must now be reviewed. Every life he derailed deserves justice. And ICE itself? At this point, abolition isn’t radical—it’s overdue.

The hood is off. Don’t look away.
