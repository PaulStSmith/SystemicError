Trump's reckoning could come via a new 'trap of his own making': analysis

Donald Trump might like to hope that he and his allies are now in the clear, but according to a new analysis from The i Paper, he remains in “maximum danger” amid the Epstein files fallout, and his fate could be sealed by testimonies given in the wake of the midterms.

Writing for the outlet on Tuesday, political editor James Ball argued that Trump and his attorney general, Pam Bondi, “will almost certainly find that moving the world’s attention on is easier said than done” when it comes to the Epstein files.” Despite Bondi claiming that all of the Department of Justice’s files on Jeffrey Epstein have been released, the continuing testimonies from major figures are set to keep the story in the public eye for the foreseeable future.

So far, with Republicans in control of Congress, “Trump’s close associates are probably safe,” Ball argued. Billionaire and close Epstein confidant Les Wexner is set to testify next week. GOP leaders are considering a subpoena against Microsoft co-founder Bill Gates if he refuses to testify publicly. Conversely, Bill and Hillary Clinton are fighting to give public testimony as Republicans have insisted on a closed-door deposition instead.

“Hillary in particular is punching back hard on the story, claiming she has nothing to hide and accusing the White House of engaging in a ‘cover-up’ over the Epstein scandal,” Ball wrote.

Despite the efforts of Republican lawmakers to keep a lid on testimonies that might embarrass Trump, Ball argued that things are liable to get much worse for him on that front in the likely event that Democrats take the House majority in November’s midterms. With that kind of power once again, the party will be able to compel testimony that could be much more damaging for Trump, and keep the Epstein story in the spotlight for the rest of his second term.

“At the moment, Republicans control the House, and so they have control over the Epstein witness list,” Ball explained. No one can be made to testify without Republican say-so, meaning that in the short term, Trump’s close associates are probably safe. But the midterms are just months away, and Democrats are heavily expected to take control of the House – which would give them the power to demand evidence from anyone the Republicans leave out.”

He added later: “The White House is caught in a trap of its own making. During the election, it thought it could use the Epstein story to embarrass Democrats, and then drop it without anyone noticing once in power. That hasn’t worked, and their obvious desperation to move the story on only makes it harder for them to do so. That means the story is likely to drag on, not just for weeks, but for months or years. Some of Epstein’s victims have waited decades already for justice. They are not about to let it go.”

The Epstein files scandal, Ball noted, is one of the few issues that unites both Democrats and Republicans against Trump. Keeping the story alive, therefore, could continue to erode Trump and the GOP’s overall support with voters, helping Democrats gain an edge as they fight to retake the White House in 2028.

Our Analysis:

The Epstein Files Fallout: A Ticking Time Bomb for Trump?

In an era where political scandals often seem to come and go with the wind, James Ball of The i Paper shines a spotlight on what could be a relentless thunderstorm over Donald Trump’s head: the fallout from the Jeffrey Epstein files. Ball’s analysis, while speculative, draws a stark picture of a political landscape where Trump and his allies, far from being out of the woods, are in what could be described as “maximum danger.”

The Unyielding Spotlight

Firstly, let’s dissect the claim that despite efforts to move past the Epstein scandal, Trump and his former Attorney General Pam Bondi will find it “easier said than done” to shift public attention away. The assertion here hinges on the ongoing testimonies from major figures, a narrative thread that promises to keep unraveling, keeping the story alive and kicking. It’s a poignant reminder of how scandal, especially one as deeply unsettling as Epstein’s, can cling to public figures with a tenacity that’s hard to shake. The idea that all of the Department of Justice’s files on Epstein have been released, as claimed by Bondi, is met with skepticism. This skepticism is not without merit, considering the layers upon layers of intrigue and horror that have so far been peeled back in this case.

The Political Chess Game

Ball’s article paints a vivid picture of a political chess game, with Republican lawmakers seemingly maneuvering to protect Trump and his close associates, while Democrats, smelling blood in the water, prepare to strike should they take control of the House in the midterms. The mention of figures such as Les Wexner and Bill Gates, alongside the Clintons, in relation to the Epstein case, underscores the high stakes and high-profile nature of this scandal. It’s a saga with the potential to ensnare a wide net of influential individuals across the political and social spectrum.

The Battle for Public Testimony

The resistance against public testimony from the Clintons, as opposed to the GOP’s insistence on a closed-door deposition, is a classic example of political maneuvering. Hillary Clinton’s assertion of having “nothing to hide” and her accusations of a White House “cover-up” add layers of intrigue and defiance to the narrative. This tug-of-war over transparency and the quest for truth is emblematic of the broader struggle within American politics: a fight between maintaining power and holding power to account.

A Future Fraught with Danger

Ball’s argument that the Democrats’ potential control of the House post-midterms could spell disaster for Trump is grounded in a stark reality. The power to compel testimony and demand evidence could indeed unearth revelations that are damaging not just to Trump but to the broader Republican establishment. The White House’s alleged attempt to weaponize the Epstein story against Democrats, only to find itself ensnared in its own trap, is a testament to the unpredictable nature of political scandals.

The Uniting Force of Scandal

Perhaps most strikingly, Ball notes that the Epstein scandal is a rare issue that unites both Democrats and Republicans against Trump. This unity against a common adversary is significant, highlighting the universal revulsion at Epstein’s crimes and the potential complicity of those in high places. The erosion of support for Trump and the GOP, as Ball suggests, could indeed tilt the scales in favor of Democrats as they aim for the White House in 2028.

Conclusion

In summation, Ball’s analysis in The i Paper offers a compelling, if speculative, view of the potential ramifications of the Epstein files scandal for Donald Trump and his allies. The narrative is far from over, and the political and legal ramifications could indeed be profound. As the testimonies continue and the midterms approach, all eyes will be on how this scandal unfolds and who it may ultimately ensnare. The Epstein saga is not just a story of individual moral failings but a litmus test for the integrity of the American political system itself.
