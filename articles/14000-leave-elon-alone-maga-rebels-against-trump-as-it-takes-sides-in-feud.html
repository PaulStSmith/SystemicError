Truth Social users replied in droves with support for Elon Musk after Donald Trump posted a threat to take away the Tesla founder's government subsidies.

According to the Washington Post, Musk's companies have received $38 billion from the federal government over the past 20 years.

In their ongoing war of words Thursday, Trump posted, "The easiest way to save money in our Budget, Billions and Billions of Dollars, is to terminate Elon’s Governmental Subsidies and Contracts. I was always surprised that Biden didn’t do it!"

Replies to Trump's post ranged from "Elon is a great man!" to "Not a good look Trump."

The account of @realohiogirl, who describes themselves as #ULTRAMAGA, posted, "What is wrong with you??? Please stop."

"I’m not sure you’re gonna see this. I really hope and pray that you do! The country needs you and Elon so if you two could just work out your differences that would be fantastic," wrote @kitkat1963.

I am disappointed in the blowup between you and Elon. Together you were an unbeatable force. Apart, you are both weakened -something that makes our enemies very happy," wrote @sfs.

The account of WeLoveTrumpWLT told their 18,000 followers, "Sorry but bad take."

@tommykids11 posted, "ITS STARTING TO SOUND LIKE A CAT FIGHT...LEAVE ELON ALONE."

@AmandaHardin1776 wrote, "High school didn’t even have this much drama. Bread and circuses are lit in 2025."

"Need to leave the ego at the doors the well being of the nation is bigger and more important than any one or two man!" wrote @ckeathley1. "THERE ARE NO WINNERS IN WAR OF WORDS BETWEEN THE TWO JUST TWO LOSERS! THE SUBJECT OF EACH OTHER SHOULD BE A NO COMMENT BY BOTH AND A PRIVATE PHONE CALL BETWEEN THE TWO SHOULD BE DONE IF ANYTHING NEEDS SAID BETWEEN THEM!!"

Earlier Thursday, Musk live tweeted his responses to Trump's press conference comments about how "disappointed" he was that Musk spoke out against the "excess pork" in the "big beautiful bill" that passed the House.

Our Analysis:

The Billion-Dollar Playground: Musk vs. Trump on Truth Social

In an episode more befitting a reality TV script than the annals of serious political discourse, Donald Trump and Elon Musk have turned the digital corridors of Truth Social into their personal battleground. A recent flurry of posts and replies has laid bare not just a clash of titans but a spectacle that underscores the absurdity of billionaire politics in the digital age.

The Subsidy Squabble

Trump's suggestion to strip Musk of his $38 billion in federal subsidies over the past two decades is a provocative one. Yet, it's clothed in a glaring omission that these subsidies have been crucial in propelling Musk's ventures, from electric cars to space exploration, into the innovative behemoths they are today. Trump's astonishment that Biden hadn't already axed these subsidies speaks volumes, hinting at a political maneuver rather than a principled stance on government spending or environmental policy.

The Truth Social Theater

The responses to Trump's post on Truth Social ranged from the supportive to the critical, painting a vivid picture of a divided base. Users hailed Musk as a "great man," criticized Trump's "bad take," and lamented the drama reminiscent of "high school." This digital coliseum, with its 280-character maxims and hashtags, has become the stage for a public plea for reconciliation between two of the most controversial figures in American public life.

The Irony of MAGA Discord

What stands out is the irony of the #ULTRAMAGA crowd, traditionally united in their fervor, now airing their grievances and calling for peace between their champions. The comments reflect a broader existential crisis within this faction, torn between allegiance to Trump and admiration for Musk's entrepreneurial spirit and anti-establishment posturing. The call for Musk and Trump to "work out their differences" for the "well-being of the nation" underscores a yearning for unity in the face of perceived external threats, yet seems blissfully unaware of the deeper systemic issues that allow such ego-driven feuds to capture national attention.

Bread, Circuses, and Billionaire Feuds

AmandaHardin1776's quip that "Bread and circuses are lit in 2025" hits the nail on the head. This entire episode is symptomatic of a society obsessed with the spectacle over substance, where billionaire spats are consumed with the same fervor as reality TV dramas. It's a distraction from the pressing issues facing everyday Americans, wrapped in the guise of political discourse.

The Bottom Line

The Musk-Trump saga is more than a war of words; it's a stark illustration of how the ultra-wealthy can dominate the conversation, drawing attention away from systemic change and towards their personal vendettas. As entertaining as some may find this billionaire bickering, it's a poor substitute for meaningful political engagement and reform. The real losers in this war of words aren't Musk or Trump, but the public, whose interests are sidelined by the spectacle.

In the end, the call for a private phone call to resolve their differences might be the most sensible suggestion to emerge from this debacle. Perhaps then, the rest of us can refocus on the systemic issues that "Systemic Error" continues to highlight, rather than being distracted by the latest episode of the billionaire soap opera that American politics has become.
