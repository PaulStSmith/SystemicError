Disgruntled ex-Nancy Mace staffer warns Trump she's bad for the GOP: 'Scratch her name'

By Paulo Santos

Rep. Nancy Mace (R., S.C.) looks on from the O’Neill House Office Building in Washington, D.C., February 28, 2024. (Evelyn Hockstein/Reuters)

A former congressional staffer for Rep. Nancy Mace (R-SC) posted a scathing manifesto to X on Monday, slamming her as leading the Republican Party down a dangerous path for her own self-interest — and urging President Donald Trump to cut her loose.

Mace, who bucked the GOP on a number of recent issues, including being an early supporter of releasing the Jeffrey Epstein sex trafficking case files, is currently running for governor of South Carolina, where she has picked ugly public fights with rival candidates.

“Nancy Mace has yet again decided to turn her back on MAGA to hug the political cactus that is the Rand Paul + Thomas Massie wing of the Party,” wrote GOP consultant J. Austin McCubbin, saying that he has resigned from her employment.

“My name has been used publicly, while going back on her word to pay me, to trade on my Team Trump status and to work on her behalf with the White House, and I am 100% breaking with her campaign out of loyalty to the President,” he continued, saying that he has spent months frustrated with her public breaks from Trump, but her recent financial support for Paul’s PAC was “the straw that broke the camel’s back.”

“Nancy Mace is wittingly or unwittingly a proxy for Rand Paul’s 2028 presidential campaign,” McCubbin wrote. “She wouldn’t hear it when I warned her about the political risks and offered alternative solutions. She accused me of mansplaining, a woke term used by people who hate men, who ironically are her best demographic. That dog don’t hunt. Anyone who knows me knows that I have nothing personally against Rand or Nancy. This is about loyalty. My loyalty lies with President Trump, my colleagues I worked with in the campaign trenches and the MAGA base that I had the privilege to work with as the Team Trump SC State Director.”

“My advice to the President, my friends in the White House, and South Carolina Trump voters: scratch her name from the list,” he concluded. “As we say in South Carolina, there’s no lesson in the second kick of a mule.”

This comes after years of reporting that, behind the scenes, Mace has alienated those in her orbit with erratic behavior. Last year, a political consultant CEO who worked with her said he had cut ties “because I’m a political consultant and not a babysitter, a sex therapist or a doctor who can prescribe fixes for chemical imbalances. I don’t have time for her constant egotistical b------ and drama in my life.”

The Mace Manifesto: A Tangled Web of GOP Intrigue

In a political landscape that increasingly resembles a high-stakes drama more than a functioning democracy, the latest episode comes from the camp of Rep. Nancy Mace (R-SC), who finds herself at the center of a scathing critique from a former congressional staffer. This staffer, J. Austin McCubbin, has taken to X (formerly Twitter) to air grievances in a manner that not only shakes the foundations of Mace’s political aspirations but also lays bare the simmering tensions within the Republican Party. Here, we dissect the layers of this unfolding saga, which is rich in accusations, personal vendettas, and the ever-present shadow of former President Donald Trump.

The Accusation: McCubbin’s manifesto is not just a resignation letter; it’s a declaration of war against what he perceives as Mace’s betrayal of MAGA principles. Describing her alignment with the Rand Paul + Thomas Massie wing of the GOP as a “hug to the political cactus,” McCubbin positions Mace as a figure leading the Republican Party down a perilous path—a path allegedly paved with self-interest and political miscalculations. The charge here isn’t just about policy disagreements; it’s about perceived loyalty, or the lack thereof, to Trump’s vision for America.

The Financial Fiasco: A significant bone of contention, according to McCubbin, revolves around financial improprieties. He claims Mace went back on her word regarding payment for his services, exploiting his “Team Trump status” for her personal gain. This accusation, if true, not only casts a shadow on Mace’s integrity but also highlights a troubling trend of financial mismanagement within political campaigns.

The Loyalty Litmus Test: At the heart of McCubbin’s manifesto is an age-old political conundrum: loyalty. His allegiance to Trump is unequivocal, positioning anyone who strays from the MAGA line as an adversary. This litmus test of loyalty to an individual over party or principle is a dangerous precedent, undermining the foundational values of democratic discourse and governance.

The Gendered Jab: McCubbin’s critique takes a particularly distasteful turn when he accuses Mace of “mansplaining,” leveraging a term often used to highlight sexist dynamics, to dismiss his concerns. This move not only undermines the validity of gendered critiques of power but also ironically highlights the toxic masculinity that pervades political consultancy.

The Call to Action: The manifesto concludes with a clarion call to Trump, his administration, and South Carolina voters to ostracize Mace, metaphorically likening her political trajectory to an unlearned lesson from a mule’s kick. This divisive rhetoric serves not to unify or heal but to further entrench the factionalism that has come to define the Republican Party in the post-Trump era.

Conclusion: McCubbin’s public denouncement of Nancy Mace is a microcosm of the broader identity crisis facing the Republican Party. It’s a saga that underscores the volatile mix of personal vendettas, financial disputes, and loyalty tests that shape the current political discourse. While the truth of McCubbin’s claims remains to be fully uncovered, one thing is clear: the GOP is at a crossroads, with its soul caught in the balance. As the party grapples with its future direction, the question remains—will it choose the path of principled debate and democracy, or will it continue down the road of personal loyalty tests and bitter infighting? Only time will tell, but if this episode is any indication, the journey promises to be tumultuous.
