RFK Jr.'s nephew demands his resignation: 'Threat to every American'

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosSeptember 6, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Robert F. Kennedy Jr. testifies before a Senate Finance Committee hearing. REUTERS/Jonathan Ernst

Former Rep. Joe Kennedy III (D-MA) is demanding his uncle Robert F. Kennedy Jr. resign, saying he's endangering American lives.

In a statement he posted on X, the younger Kennedy said, "Robert Kennedy Jr. is a threat to the health and well-being of every American. A United States Secretary of Health and Human Services is tasked with protecting the public health of our country and its people. At yesterday's hearing, he chose to do the opposite: to dismiss science, mislead the public, sideline experts sow confusion."

He warned that no one will be "spared the pain" that Kennedy will cause, as any person can expose the vulnerable to diseases.

"It doesn't matter how rich or powerful you are or what state you live in—the heartbreak of watching a loved one fall ill knows no borders," the former congressman said.

"The challenges before us—from disease outbreaks to mental health crises—demand moral clarity, scientific expertise, and leadership rooted in fact. Those values are not present in the Secretary's office. He must resign," former Rep. Kennedy closed.

Jack Schlossberg, the grandson of John F. Kennedy and son of Caroline Kennedy, added his voice as well.

Our Analysis:

The Kennedy Conundrum: A Stark Call for Resignation

In the latest episode of the Kennedy family drama, former Representative Joe Kennedy III has taken an unprecedented step by publicly calling for the resignation of his uncle, Robert F. Kennedy Jr., from the position of United States Secretary of Health and Human Services. This demand, articulated through a scathing statement on X, not only underlines deep familial rifts but also exposes a concerning narrative about leadership and public health at the highest levels of government.

Joe Kennedy III's Accusations: Bold and Unambiguous

Joe Kennedy III's statement is nothing short of a blistering attack on his uncle's competence and integrity in office. Describing Robert F. Kennedy Jr. as a "threat to the health and well-being of every American" is a powerful condemnation coming from within the same bloodline, known for its political legacy. The accusation that the Secretary chose to "dismiss science, mislead the public, sideline experts sow confusion" during a recent hearing is a serious charge. It implies not just incompetence, but a willful endangerment of public health.

The Danger of Dismissing Science

At the heart of Joe Kennedy III's criticism is the allegation of dismissing scientific evidence and expertise. In an era where misinformation can spread faster than factual information, the role of the Secretary of Health and Human Services is crucial in safeguarding public health through evidence-based policies and communications. By accusing Robert F. Kennedy Jr. of doing the opposite, Joe Kennedy III highlights a fundamental and dangerous flaw in the current leadership: a disregard for the scientific method and expert advice. This, he argues, is not just a failure of policy but a moral failing that puts every American at risk.

The Universal Impact of Poor Leadership

Joe Kennedy III's warning that "no one will be spared the pain" Kennedy Jr. could cause is a grim reminder of the universality of health crises. Diseases do not discriminate by wealth, power, or state lines. The emotional resonance of "the heartbreak of watching a loved one fall ill" speaks to the shared vulnerability of all Americans. This is a powerful rhetorical move, framing the issue as one that transcends political or familial loyalties and strikes at the core of human experience.

A Demand for Resignation Rooted in Moral Clarity

The call for resignation is predicated on a perceived absence of "moral clarity, scientific expertise, and leadership rooted in fact" in the Secretary's office. These are damning allegations that, if true, justify the demand for immediate resignation. Leadership, especially in matters of public health, requires a commitment to truth, science, and the wellbeing of the populace—a commitment Joe Kennedy III argues his uncle lacks.

Adding Insult to Injury: "RFK LOSER"

Jack Schlossberg's contribution to the discourse, albeit less formal, underscores the severity of the situation. His blunt assessment of his uncle's performance as "choking so badly LIVE" adds a layer of public humiliation to the call for resignation. It's a stark, albeit less dignified, reinforcement of the message that Robert F. Kennedy Jr.'s tenure as Secretary of Health and Human Services is seen as not just ineffective, but embarrassingly so, within his own family.

Conclusion: A Family Divided, A Nation Concerned

The public denouncement of Robert F. Kennedy Jr. by members of his own family is more than a private dispute aired in public; it's a serious indictment of his capacity to serve in a role critical to the health and safety of the American people. Joe Kennedy III and Jack Schlossberg's statements, though different in tone, both serve to highlight what they see as a dangerous incompetence at the heart of the U.S. health department.

Whether this call will lead to any real change remains to be seen, but one thing is clear: in the eyes of his own kin, Robert F. Kennedy Jr.'s leadership is not just failing—it's a direct threat to the nation's wellbeing. In the end, the issue at stake transcends personal or political allegiances—it's about the fundamental responsibility of government officials to protect the public. And on this count, according to his relatives, Kennedy Jr. has been woefully inadequate.
