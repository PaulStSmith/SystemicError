'Corrupt and unlawful!' Trump comes after megastars Beyoncé and Oprah

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosMay 20, 20255 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

President Donald Trump called for investigations of big-name performers who backed Kamala Harris in the 2024 election.

The president unspooled a late-night rant against Bruce Springsteen, Oprah Winfrey, Beyoncé and Bono for their support of the vice president he ultimately defeated in November's election, posting on Truth Social at 1:34 a.m. EST that all four megastars should be investigated for allegedly illegal campaign contributions.

"How much did Kamala Harris pay Bruce Springsteen for his poor performance during her campaign for president?" Trump posted in all capital letters. "Why did he accept that money if he is such a fan of hers? Isn't that a major and illegal campaign contribution? What about Beyoncé? ... And how much went to Oprah, and Bono?"

The Harris campaign paid $1 million to Winfrey's production company for a “Unite for America” livestream rally in September, but it's not clear whether Springsteen or Beyoncé received any payments for their rally performances. Beyoncé's mother insisted "she did not receive a penny," and it's not clear that Bono even campaigned with Harris.

"I am going to call for a major investigation into this matter," Trump posted. "Candidates aren’t allowed to pay for ENDORSEMENTS, which is what Kamala did, under the guise of paying for entertainment. In addition, this was a very expensive and desperate effort to artificially build up her sparse crowds. IT’S NOT LEGAL! For these unpatriotic 'entertainers,' this was just a CORRUPT & UNLAWFUL way to capitalize on a broken system. Thank you for your attention to this matter!!!"

Trump lashed out at Springsteen last week over his harsh criticism from a stage in the United Kingdom, suggesting he would face consequences when he returned to the U.S. The rocker followed up with additional remarks Saturday in Manchester.

“In my home America, the America I've written about that has been a beacon of hope and liberty for 250 years, it is currently in the hands of a corrupt, incompetent and treasonous administration,” Springsteen said over the weekend. “Tonight, we ask all who believe in democracy and the best of our American spirit to rise with us, raise your voices and stand with us against authoritarianism and let freedom ring. This is 'Land of Hope and Dreams.'

”

Our Analysis:

The Unraveling of a Presidential Tirade: Trump vs. The Icons

In a move that reeks of desperation and a profound misunderstanding of both legalities and basic decency, President Donald Trump has once again taken to his digital soapbox, Truth Social, to unleash a tirade against some of the most beloved figures in entertainment. The targets? Bruce Springsteen, Oprah Winfrey, Beyoncé, and Bono—all for their support of his political adversary, Kamala Harris, in the 2024 election.

At the ungodly hour of 1:34 a.m. EST, Trump’s fingers danced across his phone to accuse these megastars of engaging in allegedly illegal campaign contributions. His evidence? Nothing more than a paranoid concoction of his imagination, sprinkled with a healthy dose of capital letters for emphasis. Trump’s allegations hinge on the idea that performances or endorsements from such high-profile figures must surely involve clandestine financial arrangements, all in favor of Harris.

Unpacking the Accusations

Trump's post questioned, in all caps, the integrity of these artists, implying that their support for Harris could only be due to under-the-table payments. Specifically, he highlighted a $1 million payment to Winfrey’s production company for a “Unite for America” livestream rally, while casting aspersions on the involvement of Springsteen, Beyoncé, and, somewhat bewilderingly, Bono—who it's not even clear campaigned with Harris.

Let’s address the elephant in the room: the payment to Winfrey’s production company, which is a legitimate expense for event production, not a nefarious scheme to buy endorsements. As for the other artists, accusations fly without evidence, particularly against Beyoncé, whose mother confirmed “she did not receive a penny.”

The Legal and the Ludicrous

Trump's understanding of campaign finance law appears to be as tenuous as his grasp on reality. The crux of his argument—that candidates aren’t allowed to pay for endorsements—is a gross simplification and misunderstanding of what constitutes a legal campaign contribution and what constitutes an endorsement.

Artists and celebrities have long participated in political campaigns, often performing or speaking at rallies to show their support. This is not a new phenomenon, nor is it illegal. Their participation is often a donation of their time and influence, not a quid pro quo transaction.

The Desperation in Deflection

Trump’s call for investigation into these matters speaks volumes about his desperation to deflect from his own controversies. Labeling these efforts as “unpatriotic” and “a CORRUPT & UNLAWFUL way to capitalize on a broken system” is not just ironic but hypocritical, considering Trump’s own history with the legal and ethical boundaries of campaign finance.

The Bigger Picture

Beyond the legal inaccuracies and the baseless allegations, Trump’s rant is an attack on the very essence of democratic expression and the role of art and culture in political discourse. Springsteen’s words, spoken from a stage in the United Kingdom, resonate with a poignant truth about the current state of American democracy and its leadership. To stand against authoritarianism and for freedom is not an act of corruption but of patriotism.

In his relentless pursuit of enemies, Trump has once again shown that his greatest adversary is the truth itself. His late-night rant is less a legitimate call for investigation and more a testament to his inability to tolerate criticism or dissent.

In a democracy, artists, entertainers, and indeed all citizens, have the right to express their political beliefs and to support the candidates of their choice. To suggest otherwise is not only legally baseless but an affront to the principles of freedom and democracy. Trump’s latest tirade is a sad commentary on his presidency and a stark reminder of the importance of standing against such unfounded attacks on democratic expression.
