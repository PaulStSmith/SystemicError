Aide reveals Trump considering scrapping cornerstone of US Constitution: ‘It’s an option’

Donald Trump senior advisor Stephen Miller said Friday that the White House is “actively looking at” suspending the ability of migrants to challenge their detention in court, according to Bloomberg.

“The Constitution is clear,” Miller said, citing the clause allowing for suspension of habeas corpus during times of invasion. “So it’s an option we’re actually looking at, but a lot of it depends on whether the court will do the right thing or not.”

The eyebrow-raising comments came Friday as Miller addressed reporters, where he floated the possibility of the move, which would essentially halt habeas corpus rights for government detainees. It is intended to speed up the administration’s deportation efforts, Bloomberg reported.

“Habeas corpus allows for those detained by the government to challenge the detention and be released from imprisonment after wrongful arrest,” Bloomberg said. “In immigration proceedings, habeas reviews can consider how long migrants are being held before deportations, and the conditions of their imprisonment.”

Miller’s remarks came hours after a federal judge in Vermont ordered the release of a Tufts University doctoral student detained in March as part of Trump’s sweeping crackdown on foreign students involved in pro-Palestine campus protests.

The White House adviser concluded that the ruling was part of a “judicial coup” and insisted that the executive branch had “absolute authority” to revoke visas without court interference, Bloomberg noted.

“This judicial coup by a handful of Marxist judges to frustrate that effort can only be understood as an attack on democracy,” Miller said.

Our Analysis:

A Dangerous Precedent: Stephen Miller's Habeas Corpus Suspension Proposal

In an audacious display of authoritarianism, Stephen Miller, a senior advisor to Donald Trump, has floated the idea of suspending habeas corpus for migrants—a fundamental legal safeguard against arbitrary detention. According to Bloomberg, this proposal is under active consideration as a means to expedite the Trump administration's deportation efforts. The gravity of this suggestion cannot be overstated; it represents a direct assault on the bedrock principles of justice and liberty.

The Misuse of the Constitution

Miller invokes the Constitution, citing the clause that permits the suspension of habeas corpus during times of invasion. This interpretation is not only grossly misleading but also a dangerous manipulation of legal texts to serve a blatantly xenophobic agenda. To equate the situation at the borders with an 'invasion' is to stretch the imagination to its breaking point. It is a clear attempt to stoke fear and justify draconian measures against vulnerable populations.

The Real Impact on Human Lives

The implications of such a policy are horrifying. Habeas corpus is a critical protection that allows those detained by the government to challenge their detention. Removing this right would leave migrants at the mercy of indefinite detention without recourse—a flagrant violation of human rights. Moreover, the conditions of migrant detention facilities, often described as squalid and inhumane, underscore the cruelty of prolonging detention without legal oversight.

Judicial Autonomy Under Attack

Miller's comments following a federal judge's decision to release a detained Tufts University doctoral student reveal a disdain for judicial independence. Branding the ruling as part of a "judicial coup" and accusing judges of being "Marxist" for upholding the rights of individuals is not only baseless but a deeply troubling attack on the judiciary's role in checking executive power. His assertion that the executive branch has "absolute authority" to revoke visas without court interference is a stark illustration of the administration's authoritarian impulses.

Conclusion: A Threat to Democracy

Stephen Miller's proposal to suspend habeas corpus rights for migrants is a stark reminder of the Trump administration's willingness to undermine fundamental legal protections in pursuit of its immigration policies. This move, couched in the language of constitutional necessity, is nothing short of an authoritarian overreach that threatens the very foundations of democratic governance.

It is incumbent upon all who cherish civil liberties and the rule of law to vigorously oppose such measures. The suspension of habeas corpus—especially under such dubious pretenses—sets a perilous precedent that endangers not only migrants but the integrity of the American legal and political system itself.
