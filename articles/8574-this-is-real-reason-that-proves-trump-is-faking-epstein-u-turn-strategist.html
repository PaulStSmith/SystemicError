President Donald Trump abruptly reversed his opposition to releasing the Jeffrey Epstein files, and CNN’s Maria Cardona believes he’s only “faking” support for a congressional vote on the matter.

Trump has been unable to turn the page on the long-simmering scandal involving the late sex offender — a longtime friend of his — and House Republicans have refused to bow to his pressure campaign to vote against a procedural motion compelling the Department of Justice to release federal records on his trafficking network.

“The bottom line is that this should be about the survivors,” Cardona told “CNN This Morning.” “This shouldn’t be political. I completely agree with [fellow panelist Garrett Graff] and everyone who’s been talking about how we can’t lose sight of that. But the problem is, it has become political, not because of Democrats.”

“This is not a Democrat hoax,” she added. “This has become political because Republicans made it an issue during their campaign. During the 2024 campaign, they ran on it and they said, let’s remember, [Attorney General] Pam Bondi said, ‘It’s on my desk, I’m about to release it right now.’”

Cardona pointed out that Trump, as chief executive, could authorize Bondi to release DOJ files on his disgraced former friend without Congress’s intervention, but he hasn’t done that and fought against congressional efforts to shake them loose until it became clear the vote would pass.

“The reason why I think that Donald Trump’s kind of, you know, faking the fact that he wants this out is because he could put it out now if he wanted to,” Cardona said. “Why wait for this vote? That will be incredibly embarrassing to him when massive numbers of Republicans are going to vote for it, when we know that this is not something that he wants out there.

“If he really wanted it out there, he could have done it four months ago, and he could do it right now.”

Our Analysis:

The Political Charade around the Epstein Files

In a recent development that could easily be mistaken for a plot twist in a convoluted political thriller, President Donald Trump has made an abrupt about-face on his stance regarding the release of the Jeffrey Epstein files. CNN’s Maria Cardona, in a segment that could easily serve as a primer on political opportunism, has astutely observed that Trump’s newfound advocacy for transparency is nothing but a facade, a mere performance aimed at navigating the treacherous waters of political expediency.

The Facade of Advocacy

Trump’s volte-face is not born out of a genuine concern for justice or the well-being of Epstein’s victims. It is a calculated move, designed to mitigate the political fallout that comes with being associated, even tangentially, with the late sex offender Jeffrey Epstein. This maneuvering is particularly insidious given Trump’s historical connection with Epstein, adding a layer of personal intrigue to the political machinations.

Maria Cardona’s assertion that Trump could have authorized the release of these files at any moment, without waiting for a congressional mandate, cuts to the heart of the matter. This isn’t about transparency; it’s about political theatre. Trump’s reluctance to release the files until it became politically untenable not to do so reveals a disheartening truth about his priorities. It’s evident that the survivors of Epstein’s crimes are mere afterthoughts in this political calculus.

The Political Becomes Personal

The claim that this debacle has become political “not because of Democrats” but through the actions of Republicans is a critical observation that deserves attention. It highlights how the exploitation of sensitive issues for political gain is not a partisan problem but a systemic one. The mention of Attorney General Pam Bondi and her cryptic remarks about the files being “on my desk” only adds to the aura of manufactured drama surrounding the whole affair.

This situation is a stark reminder of how political figures often weaponize sensitive issues. The Republicans’ decision to campaign on the promise of releasing the Epstein files is a cynical ploy, leveraging the public’s hunger for justice and closure for political leverage. This maneuver is emblematic of the broader problem of political exploitation of judicial matters.

Lost in the Political Shuffle: The Survivors

At the heart of Cardona’s commentary, and indeed the entire Epstein saga, are the survivors. Their quest for justice has been overshadowed by the political circus that has unfolded around the Epstein files. This should be a moment of reflection, an opportunity to center the voices and needs of those who suffered at Epstein’s hands. Instead, it has become another episode in the seemingly endless series of political gambits.

The fact that this issue has garnered attention primarily as a political football rather than a genuine concern for justice is a damning indictment of our current political landscape. It underscores the need for a reevaluation of our priorities, both as a society and within the political establishment.

Conclusion

Trump’s sudden advocacy for the release of the Epstein files is a stark reminder of the cynicism at the heart of modern politics. It’s a maneuver that seeks to sanitize his image, distract from the substantive issues at hand, and exploit the survivors’ quest for justice for political gain. As observers and participants in this democratic experiment, we must demand better, holding our leaders to account, not just for their actions, but for the intent behind them. The survivors deserve nothing less.
