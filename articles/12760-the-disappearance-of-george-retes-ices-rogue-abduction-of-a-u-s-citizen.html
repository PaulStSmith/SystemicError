The Disappearance of George Retes: ICE’s Rogue Abduction of a U.S. Citizen

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosJuly 14, 20253 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

On July 10, 2025, in the quiet town of Camarillo, California, the United States government committed a crime against one of its own. George Retes, a 25-year-old disabled U.S. Army veteran and American citizen, was violently seized by ICE agents in a display of force better suited to a war zone than a farm field. They smashed the window of his car, pepper-sprayed him, tackled him to the ground, and vanished him without a word. No arrest record. No charges. No location. No answers.

Let’s be crystal clear: this was not an arrest. It was an abduction. A state-sponsored kidnapping executed by a rogue federal agency wielding the full impunity of a system that no longer feels constrained by the rule of law, human rights, or common decency.

George Retes wasn’t protesting. He wasn’t evading law enforcement. He was doing his job—working security at Glass House Farms. His only “crime” was having brown skin and being in the wrong place at the wrong time when ICE, emboldened by its post-constitutional swagger, raided the area under the pretense of immigration enforcement. In reality, they were hunting bodies to disappear—any body would do.

According to verified reports from ABC7 and multiple legal representatives, George Retes was a citizen. Not undocumented. Not foreign-born. A U.S. citizen with military service under his belt. That didn’t stop ICE. Citizenship has become a clerical error to them—one they correct with handcuffs and pepper spray.

His family hasn’t heard from him since. Local police claim their hands are tied. The sheriff’s office shrugs. The Carrillo Law Firm, now representing his family, confirms they’ve received no response from ICE about his whereabouts. All anyone has are guesses: Ventura County Jail, Adelanto ICE Processing Center, Otay Mesa in San Diego. Sites notorious for abuse, neglect, and the bureaucratic magic trick of turning people into ghosts.

ICE isn’t talking—because they don’t have to. They operate like a rogue paramilitary, fortified by layers of red tape and backed by a government that has decided some citizens are worth less than others. They shuffle detainees between jails and black sites, bypassing due process and habeas corpus, as if the Constitution were a bedtime story no longer relevant to the state’s interests.

And let’s not whitewash this as some bureaucratic “mistake.” This is part of a pattern. The Carrillo firm is also representing Andrea Velez, a 32-year-old U.S. citizen who ICE similarly kidnapped during a prior raid. It took her family over 24 hours just to find her. ICE didn’t notify anyone. They never do. The cruelty is the point.

Under 42 U.S. Code § 1983, this conduct is not only immoral—it is illegal. Every federal agent who strips a citizen of their constitutional rights can be held personally liable. That includes the ICE thugs who brutalized George Retes, the supervisors who signed off on the raid, and the bureaucrats who processed him like cattle. This is a criminal enterprise in federal uniform.

This is terrorism, funded by your tax dollars. This is what it looks like when your government decides your rights are conditional, your citizenship revocable, and your service disposable. George Retes fought for this country, and in return, it made him vanish.

We are not letting this go. We are not going to shut up. If they can disappear George Retes, they can disappear any of us. They aren’t deporting anymore—they’re disappearing. And unless we fight back, unless we call this what it is, they will keep doing it until no one is left to protest.

Abolish ICE. Burn the whole playbook. Demand the return of George Retes.

And hold every single one of them accountable.

This isn’t a glitch.
It’s the design.
