Trump's rats just realized their golden pardons are poison

Based on an article by Jason Miciak

Paulo SantosAugust 26, 20262 min read

Trump Is Not Being Defeated by Gravity. He Is Being Repriced.

The Leak Is the Story

The source material centers on a senior Trump-world leak about collapsing approval numbers and a White House that may be starting to wobble. That is the useful fact. Everything else is garnish. The real signal is not that Trump is suddenly misunderstood. It is that people close enough to him to hear the floor crack are already looking for exits.

Loyalty Ends When the Cost Rises

Staff leaks are not civic virtue. They are self-protection. The people around Trump are not suddenly discovering principle; they are discovering risk. When a political operation starts to look expensive, unstable, and legally radioactive, the same people who sold themselves as loyalists begin shopping for distance. That is how authoritarian politics works in practice: not through sudden conscience, but through elite abandonment once the price of proximity gets too high.

The Economy Matters Because Power Is Material

The article is right to point to the economy, war, and the sense that Trump is “in it for the money.” Those are not separate embarrassments. They are the core of the arrangement. Trump’s political durability depends on convincing enough people that he can deliver power, immunity, and upward redistribution. When inflation, war, and obvious grift collide, the spell breaks. Voters do not need a seminar on authoritarianism to notice they are being used.

Republicans Are Not Confused

The source gestures toward Republicans suddenly worrying about Congress, investigations, and the next administration. That is not a moral awakening. It is exposure. They know exactly what they helped build. They understand that a weakened Trump is not just less useful; he is more dangerous to them because he becomes less able to protect the network that fed him, covered for him, and took the winnings while pretending not to see the theft.

Polling Is Not Salvation

The piece leans hard on approval numbers, as if the main drama is whether Trump can sink into the high 20s. Numbers matter, but they are not magic. Authoritarian figures survive by capturing institutions, not by being broadly admired. The more important question is whether the institutions around him still treat him as an asset. Staff leaks, congressional hesitation, and donor anxiety matter because they reveal whether the machinery is still willing to run interference.

The Pattern Is Elite Exit Before Accountability

This story is less about Trump’s character than about the standard operating procedure of American power: enable the damage while it is profitable, then reposition when the damage becomes a liability. That is the pattern. The danger is not only the man at the center. It is the class of officials, operatives, and lawmakers who keep telling themselves they are managing him right up until the moment they start leaking, blaming, and fleeing. Accountability does not arrive because they become ashamed. It arrives only when staying loyal stops paying.
