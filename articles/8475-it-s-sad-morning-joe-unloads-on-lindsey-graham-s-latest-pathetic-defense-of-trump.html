'It's sad!' Morning Joe unloads on Lindsey Graham's latest 'pathetic' defense of Trump

Sen. Lindsey Graham (R-SC) speaks during a Senate Judiciary Committee oversight hearing with U.S. Attorney General Pam Bondi on Capitol Hill in Washington, D.C. on Oct. 7, 2025. REUTERS/Jonathan Ernst

Joe Scarborough blasted Sen. Lindsey Graham (R-SC) on Friday for supporting President Donald Trump’s death threats against a handful of Democratic lawmakers who had reminded active duty service members that they are obligated to refuse illegal orders.

“Lindsey knows better, that’s what’s so pathetic about it,” Joe Scarborough said on MS NOW’s “Morning Joe” Friday morning. “It’s sad!”

On Tuesday, a video featuring several Democratic lawmakers was published on social media, in which they urged active duty service members to “refuse illegal orders.” On Thursday, Trump fired off at the Democratic lawmakers over the video, first suggesting they be imprisoned, and later that they be sentenced to death.

Graham, who served in the military for more than three decades, backed Trump in his calls for the Democrats who participated in the video to be investigated, and potentially be sentenced to death. Scarborough, who said he considered Graham to have “been a friend” for “a long time,” didn’t mince words in his condemnation of the South Carolina senator.

“Come on, Lindsey! You of all people know that troops cannot commit illegal actions!” Scarborough said. “You should be saluting your colleagues for telling them ‘don’t commit illegal actions!’”

Scarborough also ripped into Trump, arguing that his threats to have Democratic lawmakers sentenced to death were a mere ploy to distract from the several scandals currently hammering his administration.

“Donald Trump, he’s trying to distract from the Epstein files! He’s trying to distract from the fact that he has the lowest approval rating that he’s had in the second term, he’s trying to distract from the fact that Democrats have the largest generic ballot lead that I can remember in my lifetime, 54 to 41,” Scarborough said.

“He’s trying to distract from the fact that only one in three Americans say that they think Donald Trump is handling the government the right way, you could go on and on! So what do you do? You say these senators should be executed!”

Our Analysis:

The Disturbing Dance of Death Threats and Political Deflection

In an era where political discourse has plummeted into the abyss of the absurd, Joe Scarborough’s recent condemnation of Senator Lindsey Graham (R-SC) serves as a chilling reminder of how far we’ve strayed from rational, ethical governance. Graham’s endorsement of President Donald Trump’s death threats against Democratic lawmakers is not only deplorable but a glaring testament to the moral decay festering within the halls of power.

Lindsey Graham: A Tale of Tragic Complicity

Lindsey Graham, a figure who has served in the military for over three decades, should ostensibly stand as a bastion of lawful conduct and military ethics. His backing of Trump’s call for the death penalty against Democrats who reminded service members of their duty to refuse illegal orders is not just ironic; it’s pathetically tragic. How does a man, deeply ingrained in the ethos of military service, come to support such a flagrant disregard for legal boundaries and moral decency?

Scarborough’s rebuke, “Come on, Lindsey! You of all people know that troops cannot commit illegal actions!” underscores the absurdity and danger of Graham’s stance. It’s a call for sanity in a political landscape that increasingly resembles a lunatic’s playground. By advocating for the investigation and potential execution of lawmakers upholding the principle of lawful military conduct, Graham has effectively abandoned his allegiance to the Constitution in favor of political sycophancy.

Trump’s Distraction Tactic: As Transparent as it is Treacherous

Donald Trump’s penchant for creating chaos to divert attention from his administration’s failures is well-documented. His threats against Democratic lawmakers are a desperate attempt to shift the narrative from the “Epstein files,” his plummeting approval ratings, and the growing dissatisfaction among the American populace. Scarborough’s analysis paints a picture of a president flailing to maintain a grip on power by any means necessary, including the vilification and potential eradication of political opposition.

These threats, as Scarborough rightly points out, are not just the ramblings of a man cornered by his inadequacies but a calculated move to sow discord and distract. It’s a tactic pulled straight from the authoritarian playbook: create an enemy, then destroy them, thereby diverting attention from one’s own failings.

The Unforgivable Normalization of Extremism

What’s most alarming in this debacle is not just the callous disregard for human life and legal norms but the normalization of such extremism. When figures like Lindsey Graham, who once held the respect of their peers across the aisle, capitulate to the whims of a leader bent on preserving power at any cost, it signals a dangerous shift in our political culture.

The silence and complicity of the Republican Party in the face of Trump’s increasingly authoritarian leanings serve to normalize a political environment where death threats become a tool for political maneuvering. This normalization of extremism threatens the very foundations of our democracy and the rule of law.

In Conclusion: A Call to Reason

The events dissected by Scarborough on “Morning Joe” are a somber reminder of the precarious state of American democracy. When death threats against lawmakers are entertained and even supported by individuals in the highest echelons of power, we must question the integrity and viability of our political system.

Lindsey Graham’s support for Trump’s reprehensible threats is not just a betrayal of his military background and the legal obligations it upholds but a stark illustration of the moral bankruptcy pervading our political landscape. This is not merely politics as usual; it’s an affront to the principles upon which the United States was founded.

As we navigate these turbulent waters, let us not become desensitized to the gravity of these developments. Instead, let us hold steadfast to the values of democracy, legality, and moral decency. The future of our republic depends on it.
