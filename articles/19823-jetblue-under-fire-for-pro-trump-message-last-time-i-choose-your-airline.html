JetBlue under fire for pro-Trump message: 'Last time I choose your airline'

By Paulo Santos

A passenger flying from Los Angeles to Palm Beach, Florida, on Monday was shocked to hear a JetBlue employee thanking President Donald Trump during a special President’s Day in-flight message on the plane.

Photographer James Weir posted his reaction to the announcement on JetBlue Flight 2010 on Threads, describing the moment an airline flight attendant tried to show gratitude for the Trump administration — and why he found it so outrageous.

“@jetblue The fact that the pilot or flight attendant chose to begin the flight by specifically thanking President Trump and his cabinet for all that they do means this will be the last time I choose your airline,” Weir wrote.

“My flight 2010 LA to Palm Beach just landed. The comment about Trump was made as part of a ‘It’s Presidents’ Day…’ announcement,” Weir added. “I spoke to the flight attendant in the back who said it was the number 1, as in head flight attendant, that decided to do that and that they also disagreed with the decision. The person I spoke to then spoke to said person about not making a similar announcement on the return flight.”

Other users and JetBlue customers fired off scathing responses.

“@jetblue I’m Mosaic with 350k miles logged in the past 3 years. I fly into the Palm Beach airport regularly. If I had been on this flight, and we were still on the ground, I would’ve asked to get off. The flight attendant making this announcement is not safe for me to be around. Your manuals prohibit political speech from staff while on duty. I want to hear that this person’s employment was terminated. You owe every passenger on that flight an apology,” attorney Karen Myatt wrote on Threads.

“@jetblue, forcing political views on paying customers isn’t a good look. I’m going on vacation to escape the f---ery going on in this country. I don’t want it shoved down my throat on a flight,” user kmr71170 wrote on Threads.

Raw Story reached out to JetBlue for comment.

Our Analysis:

A Turbulent Flight of Political Proclivities

In an incident that soars far beyond the expected airline announcement protocols, a JetBlue employee decided that President’s Day was the opportune moment to express gratitude towards President Donald Trump and his administration. This act, performed aboard JetBlue Flight 2010 from Los Angeles to Palm Beach, has ignited a firestorm of controversy and backlash from passengers and onlookers alike.

The Incident Unfolds

Photographer James Weir’s experience, as shared on Threads, encapsulates the shock and dismay that many felt upon hearing the announcement. Weir’s declaration that this would be his last flight with JetBlue over the politicized message underscores a broader sentiment of betrayal and discomfort among the airline’s clientele. The decision by the head flight attendant to specifically thank Trump, bypassing a neutral celebration of President’s Day, not only polarized passengers but also violated JetBlue’s own policies against political speech by staff while on duty.

Customer Backlash

The reactions from JetBlue customers, including attorney Karen Myatt and user kmr71170, reveal a deep-seated opposition to the imposition of political views in what should be a neutral space. Myatt’s readiness to disembark if given the chance, and her demand for the termination of the flight attendant’s employment, speak volumes to the severity with which these passengers view the breach of protocol. Similarly, kmr71170’s desire for a politics-free journey reflects a broader desire for escapism amidst the charged political climate.

Analysis: The Crossing of an Unspoken Line

JetBlue’s silence in the face of inquiries, as indicated by Raw Story’s outreach for comment, is deafening. It suggests an underestimation of the grave implications of allowing employees to use the airline’s platform for political endorsements. This incident not only disrupts the expected neutrality of the travel experience but also raises questions about the enforcement of company policies designed to prevent such occurrences.

The implications are clear: when employees of a service industry, especially one as intimate and enclosed as an airline, decide to voice personal political beliefs, it places the company at risk of alienating a significant portion of its clientele. The fact that this was done so blatantly, with apparent disregard for company policy or passenger comfort, is both astounding and unacceptable.

Moreover, it reflects a misunderstanding of the role that service providers play in the lives of their customers. People choose airlines for convenience, comfort, and reliability — not for political discourse. When they are subjected to unsolicited political opinions, especially in a space where they cannot easily remove themselves from the situation, it constitutes a breach of the implicit contract between service provider and consumer.

Conclusion: A Lesson in Customer Respect

This incident should serve as a stern warning to JetBlue and other companies about the importance of maintaining neutrality in customer interactions. The backlash is not merely about differing political views but about the right of consumers to receive services free from unsolicited and divisive political endorsements.

As this story unfolds, JetBlue has an opportunity to reaffirm its commitment to customer comfort and neutrality, ensuring such incidents are relegated to the past. Failure to do so not only jeopardizes its reputation but also signals a dangerous precedent for the intrusion of politics into spaces traditionally designed for escapism and neutrality.
