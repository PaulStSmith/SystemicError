Snarling GOP rains hell on the wrong man — as a ghastly calamity lurks

Based on an article by John Casey

Rand Paul’s Theater, America’s Decline

The Hearing Was the Point

Dr. Anthony Fauci sat through a congressional spectacle and declined to play along, invoking the Fifth Amendment more than 100 times. Rand Paul turned a hearing supposedly about COVID origins into a humiliation ritual, with a lawyer removed and Republican members lobbing personal attacks instead of serious questions. That is the shape of the performance: not inquiry, not oversight, but a camera-ready grievance session designed to keep an old feud alive.

Who Actually Holds Power

The people with power here are not Fauci and certainly not the retired scientist Paul is trying to prosecute by television. The real power sits with Republican officials who control the agenda, the committee stage, and the machinery of state. Paul can summon a hearing. Trump can keep the party’s attention fixed on a dead-end narrative. And the administration, according to the source, can gut pandemic preparedness while pretending the real crisis is a document trail and a set of personal diaries.

The Misdirection Is the Strategy

Paul says he wants answers about COVID origins. The article makes clear that was the cover story, not the purpose. The actual project is to recycle a conspiracy with political mileage: Fauci as a villain who can stand in for masks, lockdowns, public health restrictions, and the broader bitterness of the pandemic era. That is not oversight. It is blame management for a movement that refuses to confront its own failures and instead needs a familiar target to animate resentment.

The Real Damage Is Being Done Now

The source buries the most important fact in plain sight: the institutions meant to handle the next emergency are being weakened. Federal pandemic preparedness has been scaled back, international coordination is being rejected, and HHS has been gutted through mass layoffs, with cybersecurity, public health, and biodefense capacity pushed out. The administration is also hollowing out election integrity enforcement. Those are not side effects. They are the consequences of a governing class that would rather perform anger than maintain the systems that keep people alive and elections functional.

Fauci as Substitute

Fauci is being made to carry the weight of a century-scale crisis that no single person could control. The article is right to note that he was not infallible, because nobody was in a once-in-a-century pandemic. But that basic fact is precisely why the scapegoating is so dishonest. When leaders turn a complex public failure into a morality play about one man, they are not clarifying history. They are evading responsibility for the decisions, delays, lies, and institutional decay that actually shaped the outcome.

The Pattern

This is the Republican method in miniature: manufacture a villain, replay the grievance, and use the noise to cover the work of dismantling public capacity. Paul’s crusade against Fauci is not about accountability. It is a political shelter for negligence. While the party keeps feeding its base a stale revenge fantasy, the country becomes less prepared for the next pandemic, less protected against election sabotage, and more dependent on leaders who treat state capacity as expendable so long as the cameras keep rolling.
