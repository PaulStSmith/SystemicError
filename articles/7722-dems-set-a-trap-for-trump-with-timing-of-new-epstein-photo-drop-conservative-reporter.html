Dems set a trap for Trump with timing of new Epstein photo drop: conservative reporter

A decision by Democrats to release yet another batch of Jeffrey Epstein photos, this time including one of Donald Trump with his arms around six women who had their faces blacked out, was a calculated move to bait him, according to one conservative journalist.

Appearing on MS NOW on Friday afternoon, David Drucker of The Dispatch suggested Democrats set a trap for the president, hoping he will rage about the Epstein scandal that continues to cast a cloud over his presidency and divide the MAGA base.

Coming at the end of a week that included Trump holding a rally in a small Pennsylvania resort town in an effort to tamp down on complaints about the economy, which have sent his approval numbers into a death spiral, Drucker claimed Democrats are trying to get in Trump’s head.

“David, politically speaking, what do you think this accomplishes?” asked host Chris Jansing.

“Look, I want to reiterate that we’re talking about some horrible crimes with victims that continue to be victimized because of the longevity in the public sphere of this case,” he began. “But I could imagine, despite the bipartisan pressure to release all of the information that we usually refer to as the Epstein files, if you’re Democrats in the House of Representatives, some of the political benefits are that it continues to raise the specter of Donald Trump’s relationship with Jeffrey Epstein.”

“Just doing that is likely to get Trump to lash out, distract him from discussing things that voters are in many ways more interested in, such as the economy,” he elaborated. “It also puts more pressure on Republicans in Congress to sort of reiterate the fact that they have broken with the president and also want more information or not, or defend him.”

“And so it can have the benefit, from a political standpoint, of continuing to sow some divisions among Republicans on Capitol Hill, even though they, at the end of the day, they all collectively supported the release of this information.”

Our Analysis:

The Calculated Release: Unpacking the Strategy Behind the Epstein-Trump Photos

In a move that reeks of political maneuvering and media manipulation, Democrats have decided to release yet another batch of Jeffrey Epstein photos, this time featuring Donald Trump in a compromising frame—wrapped in the company of six women, identities obscured. This calculated release, as dissected by conservative journalist David Drucker on MS NOW, is not merely a throwaway gesture but a meticulously laid trap for the former president.

The Bait: The strategy is transparent—bait Trump into a frenzy, dragging him back into the murky waters of the Epstein scandal that looms like a specter over his presidency. It’s a ploy designed to exacerbate the existing divisions within the MAGA base, at a time when Trump’s approval ratings are plummeting, courtesy of the economic discontent gripping the nation.

The Timing: It’s no coincidence that this photo dump comes on the heels of Trump’s rally in a small Pennsylvania resort town, an apparent attempt to quell the economic grievances denting his popularity. This move by the Democrats is strategically aimed to divert the conversation from economic issues to the scandalous, thereby potentially distracting Trump from addressing matters of actual concern to the electorate.

Political Chess: Drucker’s analysis sheds light on a broader political strategy at play. By reigniting the Epstein-Trump connection, Democrats are not merely targeting Trump but are also putting Republicans in Congress in a tight spot. Will they distance themselves from Trump, demanding more transparency and information, or will they leap to his defense, potentially alienating a segment of the electorate that is tired of the scandal and seeks focus on more pressing issues, like the economy?

The Divisive Goal: It’s clear that the Democrats’ objective is twofold: keep Trump tangled in controversy and deepen the fissures within the Republican ranks. This strategy could, theoretically, dilute Republican efforts to present a united front on policy issues, especially those directly impacting the American populace, such as economic recovery and stability.

The Ethical Quandary: Amid these political machinations, the victims of Epstein’s heinous crimes become pawns in a larger game of political chess. The exploitation of their suffering for political gain is a disturbing reminder of the depths to which political entities will sink to score points against their adversaries. It’s essential to question the moral implications of such tactics, regardless of the political benefits they may yield.

In summary, the release of the Epstein-Trump photos is a calculated move aimed at destabilizing Trump and exploiting internal Republican divisions. However, it’s crucial to recognize the potential ethical violations involved in leveraging criminal activity for political gain. As this strategy unfolds, one must wonder about the long-term implications for political discourse and the integrity of democratic institutions. Will the pursuit of political advantage continue to overshadow the pressing needs and concerns of the electorate? Only time will tell, but for now, the chess game continues, with the American public keenly watching.
