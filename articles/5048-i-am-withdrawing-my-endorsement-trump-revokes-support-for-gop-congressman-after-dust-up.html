'I am withdrawing my endorsement': Trump revokes support for GOP congressman after dust up

Donald Trump on Saturday revoked his endorsement for a Republican congressman, instead backing a challenger.

Trump over the weekend continued to lash out after the Supreme Court struck down his key tariff policy goals. The president first struck out at the Supreme Court Justices who ruled against him, and then took out his frustration on a GOP lawmaker.

Taking to Truth Social, Trump wrote, “Based on a lack of support, in particular for the unbelievably successful TARIFFS imposed on Foreign Countries and Companies which has made America Richer, Stronger, Bigger, and Better than ever before, I am hereby WITHDRAWING my Endorsement of RINO Congressman Jeff Hurd, of Colorado’s 3rd District, and fully Endorsing Highly Respected Patriot, Hope Scheppelman, to take his place in Congress.”

He added, “Congressman Hurd is one of a small number of Legislators who have let me and our Country down. He is more interested in protecting Foreign Countries that have been ripping us off for decades than he is the United States of America. Taking back an Endorsement is a difficult decision for me. I have only done it once before, with a former Congressman named Mo Brooks, from Alabama, who was leading by 54 points after my Endorsement for U.S. Senate, and then, he unexpectedly, for strictly political reasons, changed his views on the Rigged Election of 2020, and lost his Race in a Landslide to Katie Britt, who I Endorsed. These are the decisions that must be made, however, to MAKE AMERICA GREAT AGAIN!”

Trump went on to say:

“On the other hand, Hope Scheppelman is a distinguished Critical Care Nurse Practitioner, and a brave U.S. Navy Veteran, who knows the America First Policies required, and will do everything necessary to Defend our Country, Support our Military/Veterans, and Ensure PEACE THROUGH STRENGTH. In Congress, Hope will work tirelessly to Grow our Economy, Cut Taxes and Regulations, Advocate for our Amazing Farmers and Ranchers, Promote MADE IN THE U.S.A., Champion American Energy DOMINANCE, Safeguard our Elections, Keep our Border SECURE, Stop Migrant Crime, and Defend our always under siege Second Amendment.”

He then concluded, “Hope Scheppelman has my Complete and Total Endorsement to be the next Representative from Colorado’s 3rd Congressional District and, unlike RINO Jeff Hurd, HOPE WILL NEVER LET YOU DOWN!”

Our Analysis:

Analyzing Trump’s Endorsement Reversal Drama

Donald Trump’s political maneuverings are never dull, and his latest endorsement flip-flop is a testament to his unpredictable nature and strategic - if not erratic - decision-making process. In a move that has ruffled feathers within the Republican party, Trump has withdrawn his endorsement from Congressman Jeff Hurd of Colorado’s 3rd District, throwing his weight behind Hope Scheppelman, a challenger deemed more aligned with his vision of America.

The Tariff Tantrum

Trump’s dissatisfaction stems from the Supreme Court’s rejection of what he considered key tariff policy goals, a decision that has clearly left the former president seething. His response? To lash out at those he perceives as traitors to his cause, including members of his own party. Trump’s tirade on Truth Social, where he lambasted Hurd for a perceived lack of support for his “unbelievably successful TARIFFS,” is classic Trump – a blend of hyperbole and aggression, with a dash of victimhood.

The claim that these tariffs have made America “Richer, Stronger, Bigger, and Better than ever before” is a contentious one. Economists and trade experts have long debated the tangible benefits of Trump’s trade policies, with many arguing that the tariffs have, in fact, harmed American businesses and consumers more than they’ve helped.

The Art of the Endorsement

Trump’s endorsement is a powerful tool, one that he wields with the finesse of a blunt instrument. By withdrawing his endorsement from Hurd and placing it on Scheppelman, Trump sends a clear message: loyalty to his agenda is paramount, far above party unity or individual merit. This is not about who can best serve Colorado’s 3rd District, but who can best serve Donald Trump’s interests.

His scathing critique of Hurd as a “RINO” (Republican In Name Only) who prefers protecting “Foreign Countries that have been ripping us off” over America is a stark reminder of Trump’s binary worldview. You’re either with him or against him, a patriot or a traitor, a friend or an enemy.

Hope Scheppelman: The Chosen One

Scheppelman, with her distinguished background as a Critical Care Nurse Practitioner and a U.S. Navy Veteran, certainly has a resume that resonates with American values and patriotism. Trump’s endorsement paints her as a savior figure, ready to “Defend our Country” and “Ensure PEACE THROUGH STRENGTH.” It’s a narrative that plays well with Trump’s base, emphasizing military service, economic growth, and national security.

However, the endorsement also reads like a checklist of Trump’s favorite talking points, from tax cuts and deregulation to “American Energy DOMINANCE” and the ever-vague promise to “Safeguard our Elections.” It’s a reminder that for Trump, policy specifics are often less important than broad, emotionally charged statements.

Conclusion: The Unpredictable Chess Game

Trump’s endorsement reversal is more than just a political jab; it’s a calculated move in the unpredictable chess game that is Republican politics. It signals to other Republicans that disloyalty - or perceived disloyalty - will not be tolerated, no matter the cost. It’s a reminder of Trump’s enduring influence within the party, his willingness to shake up the status quo, and his relentless pursuit of those who dare to question his policies.

As for Hope Scheppelman, receiving Trump’s “Complete and Total Endorsement” is both a blessing and a curse. It endows her campaign with significant momentum and visibility but also ties her fate to the whims of a leader known for his capriciousness.

In Trump’s political universe, endorsements can be powerful weapons, but they’re also double-edged swords. The fallout from this latest episode will be telling, not just for Hurd and Scheppelman, but for the Republican Party at large.
