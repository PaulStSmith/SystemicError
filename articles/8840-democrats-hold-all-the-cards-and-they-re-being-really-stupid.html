Democrats hold all the cards — and they're being really stupid

At this hour, enough Senate Democrats seem willing to cave to give Republicans the 60 votes they need to end the shutdown without agreeing to Democratic demands to restore Obamacare subsidies starting next January.

IMHO, this is really stupid.

Democrats hold all the cards. As even Trump admitted after Tuesday’s blowout, voters chose Democrats across the board because of the shutdown — which voters are blaming on Republicans.

Given this, why in hell should Democrats cave?

Senate Democrats never voted for Trump’s Big Ugly bill that removed the Obamacare subsidies (among many other travesties) because Republicans used a process called “reconciliation” which allowed them to pass the Big Ugly with a bare Senate majority and no Democratic votes.

So now that Democrats finally have some bargaining leverage, why would they give it up?

Yes, some Americans are hurting right now because of the shutdown. But do Senate Democrats really believe that Americans will hurt any less when government is reopened and Trump and his sycophants and lapdogs can hack away at whatever programs they dislike?

I admire Senate Democrats’ soft hearts but not their soft heads. I hope there’s still time for them to regain their mettle.

Our Analysis:

Senate Democrats: A Case of Misplaced Priorities and Miscalculated Moves

In a recent development that can only be described as bewildering, Senate Democrats appear poised to relinquish their leverage in the government shutdown debacle. This move, inexplicably favoring Republican demands without securing the restoration of Obamacare subsidies, is a strategic blunder of the highest order.

Why This Is a Colossal Mistake

First and foremost, the political landscape is overwhelmingly in the Democrats’ favor. The recent electoral outcomes made it abundantly clear: voters have voiced their discontent with the Republican handling of the shutdown, placing the blame squarely on their shoulders. This public sentiment handed Democrats a golden opportunity to stand firm on their demands, particularly the restoration of Obamacare subsidies—a cornerstone of affordable healthcare for millions.

The decision by Senate Democrats to potentially cave in without securing these vital concessions is nothing short of political malpractice. It’s akin to a chess player in a winning position suddenly tipping over their king. The reasoning—or lack thereof—behind this move is baffling, especially considering the stakes involved.

The Reconciliation Red Herring

Senate Democrats never lent their support to Trump’s “Big Ugly” bill, a legislative monstrosity that stripped away Obamacare subsidies, among other regressive measures. This bill was passed through reconciliation, a process requiring only a simple majority, thus bypassing any need for Democratic support. The fact that Democrats are now considering capitulation without extracting significant concessions is a testament to a staggering failure to grasp the essence of political leverage.

The Misguided Sacrifice

Yes, the shutdown has inflicted pain on many Americans, and the urgency to alleviate this suffering is understandable. However, the notion that reopening the government under Republican terms—without safeguarding key healthcare subsidies—will somehow mitigate this pain is deeply flawed. If anything, it sets a dangerous precedent, signaling to Republicans that Democratic resolve can be easily undermined with enough pressure.

The immediate repercussions of such a capitulation are clear: it would embolden Trump and his “sycophants and lapdogs” to further dismantle programs critical to the well-being of ordinary Americans. The long-term consequences, however, are even more dire. It would erode Democratic credibility, undermine their negotiating position in future standoffs, and, most importantly, betray the trust of the very voters who propelled them to victory on the promise of staunch resistance against Republican excesses.

A Plea for Political Courage

The soft-heartedness of Senate Democrats, as Robert Reich aptly notes, is not under scrutiny here—it’s their “soft heads” that are cause for concern. Compassion without strategic acumen is a recipe for political disaster. As the clock ticks, one can only hope that Senate Democrats will recalibrate their approach, recognizing that the path to meaningful change is paved with both empathy and resolve.

In politics, as in life, there are moments that demand not just good intentions, but also the courage to stand firm in the face of opposition. This is one such moment for the Senate Democrats. The hope remains that they will seize it, not just for the sake of their political future, but for the countless Americans whose lives depend on the policies at stake.
