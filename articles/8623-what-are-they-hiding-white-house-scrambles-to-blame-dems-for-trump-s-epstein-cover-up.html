'What are they hiding?' White House scrambles to blame Dems for Trump's Epstein cover-up

The White House tried to blame Democrats for a lack of “transparency” on Jeffrey Epstein after President Donald Trump reversed course and encouraged Republicans to vote for a House petition calling for the Department of Justice to release files related to the notorious sex offender.

After it became clear that Republicans were going to vote for Rep. Thomas Massie’s (R-KY) discharge petition, Trump announced on Sunday that he had changed his mind on the release of the Epstein files.

“House Republicans should vote to release the Epstein files, because we have nothing to hide, and it’s time to move on from this Democrat Hoax perpetrated by Radical Left Lunatics in order to deflect from the Great Success of the Republican Party,” the president wrote on Truth Social.

Trump had previously called Republicans “stupid” and “foolish” for supporting Massie’s discharge petition.

On Monday, the White House communications office quickly tried to paint Democrats as the obstacle to “transparency” on Epstein.

“Where Is Democrats’ Transparency on Epstein?” an article on the White House website asked. “Why haven’t Democrats shown the same transparency — and what else are they hiding?”

The article noted that Epstein contributed to Democratic Del. Stacey Plaskett of the U.S. Virgin Islands.

“Why aren’t Democrats talking about the fact that former President Bill Clinton traveled on Epstein’s aircraft 26 times?” the article asked. “Why aren’t Democrats talking about the fact that Democrat so-called ‘journalist’ Katie Couric had dinner at Epstein’s residence after he was already a convicted sex offender who had served jail time?”

“Why aren’t Democrats talking about the fact that the Democrat National Committee refused to return $32,000 in donations from Epstein?” the White House continued. “It’s time for Democrats to answer for their longstanding ties to Epstein. Then, we can get back to what matters to the American people.”

Last week, White House Press Secretary Karoline Leavitt declined to deny that Trump had spent “hours” at Epstein’s home with a sex-trafficking victim.

“This is another distraction campaign by the Democrats and the liberal media, and it’s why I’m being asked questions about Epstein instead of the government reopening because of Republicans and President Trump,” the press secretary complained.

Our Analysis:

The White House’s Transparent Deflection: A Closer Look at the Epstein Controversy

The latest maneuver from the White House to shift the narrative surrounding the Jeffrey Epstein scandal onto Democrats is nothing short of a masterclass in political deflection. After President Donald Trump reversed his stance and encouraged Republicans to support the release of files related to Epstein, the White House has embarked on a campaign to paint Democrats as the true adversaries of transparency. However, a critical examination of the facts reveals a different story.

Trump’s Sudden Pivot and the Accusation of a “Democrat Hoax”

Initially, President Trump derided Republicans for their support of Rep. Thomas Massie’s discharge petition to release the Epstein files, labeling them as “stupid” and “foolish.” Yet, in a swift reversal, Trump took to Truth Social, proclaiming, “House Republicans should vote to release the Epstein files, because we have nothing to hide.” This statement, paired with the accusation of a “Democrat Hoax perpetrated by Radical Left Lunatics,” exemplifies Trump’s tendency to shift blame and create diversions.

The White House’s Blame Game

The White House’s attempt to redirect scrutiny toward Democrats is evident in their recent online article, which poses rhetorical questions about Democrats’ lack of “transparency” on Epstein. Highlighting Epstein’s connections to Democratic figures and the DNC’s refusal to return donations from Epstein, the White House’s strategy is clear: muddle the waters and distract from the core issue at hand.

What’s Being Ignored?

The article conveniently omits any acknowledgment of Trump’s own connections to Epstein, including the White House Press Secretary’s evasion when asked about Trump spending “hours” at Epstein’s home with a sex-trafficking victim. This selective amnesia is a tactic to divert attention from the bipartisan nature of Epstein’s relationships and to frame the controversy as a partisan issue.

The Real Questions

While the White House raises questions about Democrats’ associations with Epstein, it fails to address the larger systemic issues that the Epstein scandal unveils. The focus should not solely be on partisan affiliations but rather on the mechanisms of power and influence that allowed Epstein’s actions to go unchecked for so long. The attempt to politicize the scandal only serves to obscure the need for a thorough investigation into Epstein’s network and the systemic failures that facilitated his crimes.

Conclusion: A Distraction from Substance

The White House’s campaign to blame Democrats for a lack of transparency on Epstein is a calculated distraction from the substantive issues at play. By framing the discourse as a partisan battle, the administration seeks to deflect from Trump’s own entanglements and the bipartisan nature of Epstein’s connections. True transparency requires a focus on the systemic flaws exposed by the Epstein scandal, not a diversionary blame game. It’s time for all involved to move beyond partisan finger-pointing and commit to uncovering the full extent of Epstein’s network and holding those accountable, regardless of their political affiliations.
