Nancy Mace careens from ‘death penalty’ call to prayers for ‘lost’ Charlie Kirk suspect

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosSeptember 16, 20255 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Our Analysis:The Blatant Hypocrisy and Political Opportunism of Rep. Nancy Mace

FILE PHOTO: U.S. Representative Nancy Mace (R-SC) walks offstage to speak to reporters after a press conference put on by House Oversight Committee Republicans at the U.S. Capitol in Washington, U.S., May 10, 2023. REUTERS/Craig Hudson/File Photo

Over the course of two days, U.S. Rep. Nancy Mace (R-SC) has taken starkly shifting positions on the alleged assassin of right-wing commentator Charlie Kirk—repeatedly blaming Democrats, calling for the death penalty, then urging prayer for the suspect to find Jesus Christ.

When news broke on Wednesday that Kirk—who had a large following on the right, and was close with President Donald Trump and his family—had been shot and had died, Mace declared, “They killed Charlie Kirk.” She did not specify who she thought “they” were.

Two hours later, Mace wrote, “It’s time to bring back the death penalty.” By late Thursday morning, she charged, “The Left owns what happened yesterday.”

She also posted a video of herself from Wednesday, before Kirk had been pronounced dead, saying she was “devastated.”

Using a slur for transgender people, she said that Kirk, when he was shot, was talking about mass violence.

“I hope that every single Democrat across the country will stand up and acknowledge that they have a problem within their party,” she urged. “So when are my colleagues on the left side of the aisle, the Democrats, going to own the violence that’s happening in this country against Republicans and against conservatives?”

“It’s only going to get better if Democrats step up to the plate and take responsibility for what’s happening in this country—the threats that are happening—and come together.”

“Democrats own this. Democrats own this,” she said. “Democrats own this 100%.”

Less than two hours after that post, echoing her earlier claim, she added: “Let me say this as nice as I can: DEMOCRATS OWN THIS.”

On Thursday, responding to now-retracted reports that the alleged shooter might have been transgender or pro-transgender, Mace posted video of her telling reporters, “Enough is enough.”

Again using a slur for transgender people, she said it “sounds like” the shooter was transgender or pro-transgender.

“The left, the Democrat Party, has allowed this stuff to happen,” she also charged.

Late Friday morning, Mace posted an image of the alleged assassin, about whom little was known, and wrote: “Bring back the death penalty.”

Also on Friday, amid reports that the alleged shooter, identified as Tyler Robinson, had turned himself in to law enforcement, Congresswoman Mace wrote: “We know Charlie Kirk would want us to pray for such an evil, and lost individual like Tyler Robinson to find Jesus Christ. We will try to do the same.”

Less than one hour later, she added, “We truly believe if Tyler Robinson had ever sat down across from Charlie, the great debater, the man of faith and grace he was, Tyler wouldn’t have pulled the trigger.”

Friday afternoon, she concluded, “Some crimes are so evil, the only just punishment is the death penalty.”

Our Analysis:

The Blatant Hypocrisy and Political Opportunism of Rep. Nancy Mace

In an astonishing display of political opportunism, U.S. Rep. Nancy Mace (R-SC) has taken us on a bewildering roller-coaster of reactions to the tragic assassination of right-wing commentator Charlie Kirk. Her statements, which swung wildly from blaming Democrats outright to advocating for the death penalty, and then, in a sudden twist, calling for prayers for the suspect, lay bare a troubling trend in political discourse: the manipulation of tragedy for partisan gain.

Mace’s Initial Reaction: A Dangerous Blame Game

Initially, Mace's choice to declare, “They killed Charlie Kirk,” without specifying who "they" were, was a reckless incitement of blame. It's a tactic designed to stoke the fires of division, suggesting a collective guilt among Democrats for the actions of a lone individual. Her subsequent claim, “The Left owns what happened yesterday,” further entrenches this dangerous narrative, suggesting an entire political ideology is responsible for the heinous act of one person.

The Call for the Death Penalty: A Disturbing Pivot

Not content with merely casting blame, Mace escalated her rhetoric by advocating for the return of the death penalty. This pivot is particularly jarring, not only for its extremity but also for its timing, coming hot on the heels of her generalizations about the left's responsibility. It's a stark reminder of the lengths some politicians will go to, leveraging tragedy to push for harsher legal measures.

A Sudden Shift to Calls for Prayer

In a bewildering turn, Mace then shifted her stance entirely, calling for prayers for the alleged assassin to find Jesus Christ. This sudden appeal to forgiveness and redemption contrasts sharply with her earlier calls for vengeance and punishment. It's hard not to see this as anything but a calculated attempt to humanize her position and appeal to the religious and moral high ground after days of divisive rhetoric.

The Use of Slurs and Misinformation

Compounding the irresponsibility of her shifting stances, Mace's use of slurs against transgender people and her baseless speculation about the shooter's identity before facts were established is reprehensible. It smacks of an attempt to leverage existing prejudices and stereotypes for political gain, further endangering already vulnerable communities.

Conclusion: Political Opportunism at Its Worst

Rep. Nancy Mace's response to the assassination of Charlie Kirk is a case study in political opportunism and hypocrisy. By shifting her stance so dramatically, from blaming an entire political ideology, to calling for the ultimate punishment, to urging forgiveness, she has demonstrated a willingness to exploit tragedy for political points. Her actions are a disservice to the gravity of the situation and to the constituents who deserve thoughtful, consistent leadership in times of crisis.

It's essential to call out this behavior for what it is: a cynical manipulation of a tragedy for political gain. We must demand better from our leaders, insisting on responsibility, consistency, and a commitment to facts and decency over opportunism and division.
