'Incompetent': Trump hurls new insults at Gavin Newsom in early-morning tirade

Governor of California Gavin Newsom talks to U.S. President Donald Trump upon Trump’s arrival to tour areas impacted or destroyed by the southern California wildfires, at Los Angeles International Airport in Los Angeles, California, U.S., January 24, 2025. REUTERS/Leah Millis

President Donald Trump started Wednesday Morning by railing against “incompetent” California Governor Gavin Newsom.

“The INCOMPETENT Governor of California was unable to provide protection in a timely manner when our Ice Officers, GREAT Patriots they are, were attacked by an out of control mob of agitators, troublemakers, and/or insurrectionists. MAKE AMERICA GREAT AGAIN!” Trump said on his Truth Social platform.

Trump has been hurling insults at Newsom since the weekend after immigration protests took Los Angeles by storm.

The President has sent in 4,000 National Guard officers and 700 Marines to quell the protests, which have turned into looting and rioting in the overnight hours.

On Monday, Trump floated the idea of arresting Newsom. Border Czar Tom Homan has publicly said he will not arrest the governor.

Newsom, for his part, has traded blows with Trump. On Monday, the governor slammed Trump in a social media post, saying that the troops he sent to Los Angeles had no food or a place to stay.

Newsom has also said “democracy is under assault” because of Trump’s move to send in troops.

Our Analysis:

Analyzing the Escalation in California: Trump vs. Newsom

In a recent eruption of political vitriol, President Donald Trump has launched a scathing attack on California Governor Gavin Newsom, labeling him as "incompetent" on his Truth Social platform. Trump's tirade comes amidst the chaos unfolding in Los Angeles following immigration protests, which, according to him, were mishandled by Newsom, leading to assaults on ICE officers by what Trump describes as an "out of control mob of agitators, troublemakers, and/or insurrectionists."

The Deployment of Force

Trump's decision to deploy a staggering 4,000 National Guard officers and 700 Marines to Los Angeles is a testament to his administration's heavy-handed approach to quelling civil unrest. This move, ostensibly to control looting and rioting during the overnight hours, raises significant concerns about the militarization of our cities and the potential erosion of civil liberties under the guise of maintaining public order.

The Threat Against Democracy

Governor Newsom's countercharge that "democracy is under assault" is not a statement to be taken lightly. The implication that Trump's actions—sending in troops without adequate provisions for them, no less—are a direct threat to the democratic fabric of the nation is a grave accusation. Newsom's remarks highlight the tension between federal and state powers and underscore the fear of overreach by the executive branch.

The Rhetoric of Insults

The exchange of insults between Trump and Newsom, while perhaps politically expedient for their respective bases, does little to address the underlying issues at hand. Trump's labeling of ICE officers as "GREAT Patriots" and his derogatory characterization of protesters as "agitators, troublemakers, and/or insurrectionists" simplifies a complex situation into a binary of good vs. evil. This black-and-white framing neglects the nuanced reasons behind the protests and the legitimate concerns of those who feel their voices are unheard.

A Missed Opportunity for Leadership

In the midst of this turmoil, what stands out is a missed opportunity for genuine leadership. Rather than fostering dialogue or seeking collaborative solutions to the tensions in Los Angeles, both Trump and Newsom have chosen the path of confrontation and escalation. Trump's suggestion to arrest Newsom, although rebuffed by Border Czar Tom Homan, is a startling reflection of the breakdown in civil discourse at the highest levels of government.

The Real Losers

As this political drama unfolds, the real losers are the citizens of California, particularly those in Los Angeles caught between the protests and the military response. The lack of adequate provisions for the deployed troops, as pointed out by Newsom, is a glaring oversight that speaks to a broader disregard for the human costs of such interventions.

Conclusion

The situation in California is a microcosm of the larger political and social divisions plaguing the United States. The escalation between President Trump and Governor Newsom serves as a stark reminder of the need for leadership that transcends petty insults and seeks to heal and unite rather than divide and conquer. As the nation watches, one can only hope that cooler heads will prevail, and a path forward can be found that respects the rights and dignity of all involved.
