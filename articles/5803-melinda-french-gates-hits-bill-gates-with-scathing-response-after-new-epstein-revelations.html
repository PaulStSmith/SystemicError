Melinda French Gates hits Bill Gates with scathing response after new Epstein revelations

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosFebruary 4, 20265 min read

Melinda French Gates responded to the allegations involving her ex-husband Bill Gates in the Epstein files during a conversation on NPR’s Wild Card podcast set to release in full on Thursday. (NPR/Wild Card/Screenshot)

Philanthropist Melinda French Gates had a sharp response to her ex-husband Bill Gates after allegations surfaced in the most recent trove of Epstein files, showing private emails between the late financier and convicted sex offender Jeffrey Epstein and the former Microsoft CEO.

In the emails, which were part of the more than 3 million documents released by the Department of Justice last week, it was suggested that Bill Gates had additional affairs and had come to Epstein so he could “facilitate trysts with married women and to get medication to treat an STI from ‘sex with Russian girls,’” NPR reported Tuesday.

In her first time speaking publicly about the allegations, French Gates responded to the allegations in the Epstein files during a conversation on NPR’s “Wild Card” podcast.

“I think we’re having a reckoning as a society, right,” French Gates said. “No girl, no girl, should ever be put in the situation that they were put in by Epstein and whatever was going on with all of the various people around him. No girl, I mean, it’s beyond heartbreaking, right? I remember being those ages those girls were. I remember my daughters being those ages, right? So for me, it’s personally hard whenever those details come up because it brings back some very, very painful times in my marriage. But I have moved on from that. I purposely pushed it away and I moved on. I’m in a really unexpected, beautiful place in my life.”

She pointed to her former husband and others among Epstein’s circle — putting the onus on them to respond.

“Whatever questions remain there of what — I can’t even begin to know all of it — those questions are for those people and for even my ex-husband,” she said. “They need to answer to those things, not me.”

Bill Gates co-founded Microsoft, one of the world’s largest software companies, and amassed one of the world’s largest personal fortunes before transitioning to philanthropic work through the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation. Melinda French Gates is an American businesswoman and philanthropist who was married to Bill Gates for 27 years and served as co-chair of the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation until their divorce in 2021, with the couple having focused their foundation’s work on global health, poverty reduction and education initiatives before separating and establishing separate philanthropic endeavors.

The full NPR interview with French Gates was slated to be released on Thursday.

Our Analysis:

The Gates-Epstein Scandal: A Mirror to Societal Rot

In a recent development that could easily be mistaken for a plotline in a lurid thriller, allegations have resurfaced, entangling two of the most recognized names in technology and philanthropy: Bill Gates and Jeffrey Epstein. The latest cache of the Epstein files, courtesy of the Department of Justice, reveals a sordid tapestry of private emails between the late financier and convicted sex offender Jeffrey Epstein and former Microsoft CEO Bill Gates. Within these digital pages, allegations suggest Gates sought Epstein’s help for “facilitating trysts with married women” and obtaining medication for an STI “from ‘sex with Russian girls,’” as reported by NPR. This is not just a scandal; it’s a damning indictment of the moral bankruptcy at the highest echelons of power.

Melinda French Gates: A Stand of Dignified Outrage

Melinda French Gates’ response to these lurid revelations is nothing short of sharp and scornful. Speaking publicly for the first time on NPR’s “Wild Card” podcast about the allegations, her words slice through the air with the precision of a scalpel: “No girl, no girl, should ever be put in the situation that they were put in by Epstein and whatever was going on with all of the various people around him.” This statement is not just a condemnation of Epstein’s vile machinations but a pointed critique of the systemic enabling by powerful individuals, including her ex-husband.

French Gates’ commentary transcends mere personal grievance; it’s a societal wake-up call. She articulates a universal horror, referencing not just the young victims but her own daughters, evoking a powerful, empathetic response. Yet, in her pointed redirection of inquiries back to her former husband and Epstein’s circle, she embodies a refusal to be the answerer for sins not her own. Her stance is clear: the men entangled in this web of depravity must be the ones to face the music.

The Gates Foundation: A Legacy Under Question

This scandal casts a long, dark shadow over the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation’s legacy. Once hailed as a beacon of global philanthropy, with initiatives aimed at health, poverty reduction, and education, the foundation now finds itself in a precarious position. Can its commendable work be untangled from the personal failings of its founders? This question is not just academic but goes to the heart of a larger issue in philanthropy: the disconnect between public benevolence and private morality.

Bill Gates, a titan of industry turned philanthropist, has seen his personal narrative shift from that of a visionary leader to one embroiled in controversy. The implications of his alleged actions, facilitated by a convicted sex offender, are not merely personal peccadilloes but speak to a disturbing trend of impunity among the elite. The power dynamics at play, the exploitation of vulnerable individuals, and the sheer audacity of seeking medical treatment for an STI contracted under dubious circumstances—all these elements paint a picture of a systemic rot that philanthropy alone cannot cleanse.

In Conclusion: A Call for Accountability

Melinda French Gates’ poised yet fierce response to the allegations against her ex-husband is more than just a personal vindication; it’s a clarion call for societal accountability. In her refusal to shoulder the burden of her ex-husband’s alleged misdeeds, she challenges us to look beyond the individual to the societal structures that enable such behavior.

The Gates-Epstein scandal is not an isolated incident but a symptom of a deeper malaise. It demands a reckoning—not just with the individuals involved but with the systems of power that allow the rich and powerful to exploit and discard the most vulnerable with impunity. In this moment of moral crisis, silence is complicity. It’s high time for those entangled in Epstein’s web—and indeed, society at large—to confront the uncomfortable truths laid bare before us.
