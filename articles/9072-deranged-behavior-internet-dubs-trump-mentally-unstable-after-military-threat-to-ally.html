'Deranged behavior': Internet dubs Trump 'mentally unstable' after military threat to ally

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosNovember 3, 20254 min read

Donald Trump’s threat to send troops to attack people on allied land raised some red flags this weekend.

Trump on Saturday took to Truth Social to threaten Nigeria, which is known as a strategic partner for the U.S.

“If the Nigerian Government continues to allow the killing of Christians, the U.S.A. will immediately stop all aid and assistance to Nigeria, and may very well go into that now disgraced country, ‘guns-a-blazing,’ to completely wipe out the Islamic Terrorists who are committing these horrible atrocities,” Trump said on Truth Social Saturday. “I am hereby instructing our Department of War to prepare for possible action. If we attack, it will be fast, vicious, and sweet, just like the terrorist thugs attack our CHERISHED Christians! WARNING: THE NIGERIAN GOVERNMENT BETTER MOVE FAST!”

The internet was quick to respond to the president’s claim about the potential Nigerian military strike.

Ex-prosecutor Ron Filipkowski said, “At some point we might have to consider the fact that he might be mentally unstable.”

Independent journalist Aaron Rupar said, “Trump posts crazy stuff like this at times where he feels like he’s not getting enough attention and wants people to start talking about him again.”

Rupar then added, “It’s deranged, narcissistic behavior.”

Jeff Timmer, a conservative analyst who opposes Trump, also chimed in, “In the old days, when in moods like this, he’d just lurk in dressing rooms at Bergdorf’s or head over to Epstein’s place.”

“U.S. Capitol, Greenland, Los Angeles, Iran, Canada, democracy, Washington DC, Portland, narco-boats, Chicago, pleasure fishing boats, the Constitution, Venezuela, the rule of law, Nigeria. All the same to the lawless, corrupt, chickens---, moron in the WH,” Timmer wrote on social media.

Professor Timothy Snyder added, “Is it just me or do these proclamations sound more and more like the work of Dement AI?”

Our Analysis:

Trump’s Threat to Nigeria: A Deep Dive into Absurdity and Impulse

Donald Trump’s latest foray into the realm of international diplomacy—or, more accurately, the lack thereof—has once again illuminated the dangerous tightrope walk between rational governance and erratic personal vendettas. On a platform notorious for unfiltered rants, Trump’s threat to send U.S. troops “guns-a-blazing” into Nigeria, a strategic ally, over the treatment of Christians, isn’t just reckless; it’s a vivid illustration of a diplomacy strategy that’s been thrown out of the window, favoring instead the kind of bravado one might expect in a poorly scripted action movie.

The Statement: A Study in Impulsiveness** Trump’s declaration on Truth Social to potentially attack an allied nation under the guise of protecting Christians is not just a flagrant disregard for international law but also a stark reminder of the former president’s penchant for making foreign policy pronouncements as if he’s still on the campaign trail. The use of phrases like “fast, vicious, and sweet” to describe a potential military operation is not only disturbing but betrays a fundamental misunderstanding of the complexities and gravitas of military action.

The Reactions: From Concern to Ridicule The reactions to Trump’s post ranged from concern over his mental stability to outright ridicule of his approach to governance. Notably, Ron Filipkowski, an ex-prosecutor, raised alarms about Trump’s mental state. This isn’t just a throwaway comment; it’s a serious concern coming from a legal professional about the mindset of a man who once held, and evidently seeks to reclaim, the highest office in the land.

Aaron Rupar and Jeff Timmer’s comments further underscore the perception that Trump’s erratic posts are more about seeking attention and sating his ego than any coherent policy stance. Timmer’s scathing roundup of Trump’s targets of ire, likening Nigeria to everything from the U.S. Capitol to pleasure fishing boats, paints a picture of a man whose grasp on reality—and legality—is tenuous at best.

The Broader Implications: Democracy at Risk Perhaps the most chilling observation comes from Professor Timothy Snyder, who equates Trump’s proclamations to the outputs of “Dement AI.” This isn’t just a witty jab; it’s a dire warning about the potential for such erratic leadership to undermine the very fabric of American democracy and its standing on the world stage. Trump’s statement is emblematic of a broader disregard for the rule of law, international relations, and the principles of democracy.

Conclusion: A Call for Rationality

Donald Trump’s threat against Nigeria is not an isolated incident but rather a symptom of a much larger issue: the erosion of rational, principled leadership in favor of impulsive, attention-seeking behavior. Such actions not only endanger international relations but also threaten the principles of democracy and rule of law. It’s a stark reminder of the need for vigilant, informed, and rational discourse in the face of such challenges to the global order and democratic norms.
