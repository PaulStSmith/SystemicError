'Stay home': MAGA world 'heartbroken' as US athletes turn on Trump

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosFebruary 10, 20265 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Conservative website Townhall and the MAGA community are exploding over American athletes “going viral” for “choosing to diss the country that they were sent to represent at the 2026 Winter Olympic Games.”

The MAGA community and its entertainers take as fact that President Donald Trump and America are one and the same, despite one being a 250-year-old nation and the other being a 79-year-old man in declining health.

Townhall notes American freestyle skier Hunter Hess posted on social media that representing his home country conjured “mixed emotions” because there’s “a lot going on” that he’s “not the biggest fan of.”

“If it aligns with my moral values, I feel like I’m representing it,” said Hess. “Just because I wear the flag doesn’t mean I represent everything that’s going on in the U.S.”

Another American skier, Chris Liller, admitted he was “heartbroken” over “what’s going on with ICE and the protests.”

“I think that, as a country, we need to focus on respecting everybody’s rights and treating our citizens, as well as everybody, with love and respect,” Liller said. “I hope that, when people look at athletes competing in the Olympics, they realize that’s the America we’re trying to represent.”

Both of the posts Townhall cited were reposted along with harsh comment from critics, with MAGA entertainment site RedWave Press calling Liller’s statement “disgusting,” and MAGA commenter Jon Root describing Hess as “insufferable.”

“If you’re not proud to represent the red, white, and blue, stay home,” Root said on X.

Much of the MAGA community was stung with similar fury as they witnessed Trump’s long-distance popularity plummet in real time. A mere handful of months ago, the president drew cheers and adoration from athletes and entertainers. But now his second-in-command gets booed at the Olympic opening ceremony and Trump refuses to attend popular sports events for fear of getting slammed by crowds of critics.

“YOU chose to wear our flag. YOU chose to represent our country. YOU chose to compete at the Olympics. If that’s too hard for you, then GO HOME,” lamented Trump-endorsed gubernatorial candidate Byron Donalds on X.

“These athletes make me physically sick,” posted MAGA commenter Mark Walker on X. “… If the US Olympic Committee had any integrity, which of course it doesn’t, they would send these entitled brats home where they could sort out their ‘mixed emotions.’”

“Send him home now and give his spot to a more appreciative athlete who will respect his country, God and the president of the United States,” posted another MAGA account.

Our Analysis:

The MAGA Meltdown: A Study in Misplaced Outrage

In a display that can only be described as a tempest in a teapot, the MAGA community and its affiliates at Townhall have unleashed a torrent of indignation toward American athletes expressing nuanced feelings about representing their country at the 2026 Winter Olympic Games. This outrage, predictably, misses the forest for the trees, focusing on loyalty tests rather than the substance of the athletes’ statements.

The Conflation of Critique with Disloyalty

The heart of this controversy lies in the MAGA camp’s inability—or unwillingness—to distinguish between dissent and disloyalty. Athletes Hunter Hess and Chris Liller have articulated feelings that do not align with the unbridled nationalism expected by their critics. Hess speaks of “mixed emotions” and a desire to represent his country in a way that aligns with his moral values. Liller expresses heartbreak over domestic issues and aspires for an America that prioritizes respect and love for all its inhabitants. These are not radical positions. They are measured, thoughtful reflections on the complexities of patriotism in a polarized era.

Yet, to the MAGA crowd, such nuance is heretical. The demand for blind allegiance—wherein wearing the flag is equated with uncritical support for all actions taken by the country—demonstrates a fundamental misunderstanding of what patriotism can and should be. Patriotism, in its most constructive form, involves a love for one’s country that is not blind to its flaws but engaged in a continual process of critique and improvement.

The Irony of the MAGA Position

The article highlights the MAGA community’s conflation of President Donald Trump with the United States itself, revealing an unsettling trend towards personality cults rather than democratic patriotism. The irony here is palpable: a movement that so often champions “freedom” and “liberty” seeks to quash the very freedoms of expression and thought when it comes to representing the U.S. on the global stage.

The Misguided Attack on Athlete Integrity

The vitriol aimed at these athletes by figures like MAGA commenter Jon Root and Trump-endorsed gubernatorial candidate Byron Donalds is not just misplaced; it’s fundamentally counterproductive. Root’s suggestion that athletes who are not unabashedly proud to represent the red, white, and blue should “stay home” and Donalds’s lamentation that critical athletes make him “physically sick” are emblematic of a broader intolerance for dissent.

This stance is particularly ironic considering the rich history of athletes using their platform to draw attention to social issues and injustices within their countries—activities that have often led to significant social progress. To argue that athletes should simply “shut up and play” is to ignore the potent role sports have played in societal change.

The Reality of Athlete Representation

What the MAGA community fails to grasp is that America is not a monolith. It is a tapestry of diverse thoughts, beliefs, and values. Athletes, like all citizens, have the right to voice their concerns and aspirations for their country. The belief that representing one’s country at the Olympics is an endorsement of all its policies and actions is not only misguided but dangerous. It stifers the very freedoms that make democratic societies thrive.

The outrage over Hess and Liller’s statements is a classic example of misplaced priorities. Instead of engaging with the substance of their concerns, critics have chosen to attack their patriotism. This is not just an affront to the principles of free expression but a missed opportunity to reflect on how America presents itself on the world stage and the values it seeks to embody.

In the end, the MAGA community’s response to these athletes is a revealing study in the dangers of conflational thinking and the importance of embracing a more nuanced, inclusive understanding of patriotism. True patriotism involves grappling with the complexities of national identity, not denying them.
