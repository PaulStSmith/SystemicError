Trump's made his pick for successor as president — and it's all about money: analyst

Based on an article by Nicole Charky-Chami

Paulo SantosAugust 18, 20262 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

A Political Party Turned Family Asset

The Real Story

This is not about a clever media product. It is about a sitting president building a private revenue machine around the Republican Party, his family name, and the possibility of future power. The source’s core fact pattern is simple: Trump Media is moving to sell early access to Truth Social posts, and the financial value of that access rises sharply if a Trump stays in the presidential pipeline.

Who Holds Power

The only institutional power that matters here is Trump’s. He sits at the center of the party’s brand, its donor logic, and its future ambitions. JD Vance and Marco Rubio are mentioned only as the people whose ascent would collapse the value of the scheme. That is the tell. This is not a neutral business decision. It is a succession structure built to reward family control and punish independence.

The Product Is the Point

Jonathan V. Last calls the move potentially “the greatest trick in the history of insider trading,” and the phrase matters because it captures the shape of the operation even if the legal mechanics remain complex. The point is not merely that Trump Media is monetizing information. It is that the family may be able to convert privileged access into market advantage while laundering the whole thing through a social platform. That is what political corruption looks like when it grows a fintech skin.

The Misdirection

The safest mistake in stories like this is to treat them as personality quirks, entrepreneurial hustle, or Trumpian chaos. That is a dodge. The source describes deliberate infrastructure: the ballroom, the underground bunker, the Trump 2028 hats, the effort to monkey with the midterm elections, and now Truth API. These are not random stunts. They are assets, signals, and leverage points in a long campaign to keep the party inside the family fence.

Why Don Jr. Matters

Last’s claim that this hints Don Jr. may run for president is less gossip than consequence. If the family can make a future candidacy worth hundreds of millions of dollars, then the candidacy itself becomes part of the business model. The presidency stops being a public office and becomes a downstream monetization event. That is the political meaning of the story: the office is being treated as an appreciating family instrument, not a democratic trust.

The Pattern

The larger pattern is oligarchic capture dressed up as politics. A major party is being organized around one family’s media, money, and succession plans, while the system around it keeps calling the arrangement “fascinating” instead of naming it what it is: concentrated power using public office as private property. The scandal is not that Trump is subtle. The scandal is that he is doing this in plain view, and the system still keeps mistaking open predation for strategy.
