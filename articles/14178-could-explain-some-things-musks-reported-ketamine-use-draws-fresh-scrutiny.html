‘Could explain some things’: Musk’s reported ketamine use draws fresh scrutiny

MSNBC’s Nicolle Wallace didn’t mince words Friday as she unpacked explosive new reporting surrounding Elon Musk’s drug use during his time on the campaign trail as one of President Donald Trump’s most visible supporters.

“This next story could explain some things,” Wallace told viewers during her show “Deadline: White House,” as she reacted to a bombshell New York Times report that revealed Musk used ketamine “near daily” while campaigning with Trump in 2024, allegedly mixing it with ecstasy, psychedelic mushrooms and other drugs.

“It wasn’t just the volume,” Times reporter Megan Twohey told Wallace on Friday. “It was that these drugs appeared to be mixed.”

Musk had acknowledged previously using legally prescribed ketamine “every couple of weeks” to treat depression, according to Twohey. But, the journalist told Wallace that sources close to Musk described more frequent – and concerning – use.

“At times, the ketamine use was near daily and got to the point where it was causing bladder issues,” she said.

Twohey described Musk as traveling with a “pill box” that contained as many as 20 pills, including one resembling Adderall.

“There have been people close to him who have had concerns about his drug use,” she added.

Ketamine, a powerful anesthetic, has FDA-approved uses for depression and pain – but only under strict medical supervision.

“The FDA has warned that the use of this drug can come with these serious side effects and risks,” Twohey said. Among them, she added, include “addiction and bladder issues.”

Both Musk and the White House declined to comment on the report.

Our Analysis:

The Musk Maelstrom: A Deep Dive into Allegations and Implications

In a recent segment that could only be described as revelatory, MSNBC’s Nicolle Wallace brought to light a story that reads like a script from a political thriller, yet this tale is uncomfortably woven into the fabric of real-life American politics. The centerpiece? Elon Musk, a figure already shrouded in controversy, now embroiled in explosive allegations regarding his drug use while actively supporting President Donald Trump on the campaign trail for the 2024 elections.

The Allegations: A Cocktail of Concerns

According to a "bombshell" report by The New York Times, Musk’s drug use wasn't just occasional; it was allegedly a near-daily ritual that included a concoction of ketamine, ecstasy, psychedelic mushrooms, and other drugs. This isn't your average Friday night unwind. This is a concerning pattern of behavior, especially for someone in such close proximity to the epicenter of American political power.

Musk's predilection for ketamine, a substance he admitted to using every couple of weeks to treat depression, has now been cast in a much more alarming light. The report claims his use was far more frequent and mixed with other substances, raising red flags about addiction and more immediate health issues, such as bladder problems. The imagery of Musk traveling with a “pill box” containing as many as 20 pills, as described by Megan Twohey of The New York Times, paints a picture of dependency that's difficult to ignore.

FDA Warnings: A Red Flag Ignored?

Amid these startling revelations, it's crucial to highlight the serious nature of ketamine use without strict medical supervision. Approved by the FDA for treating depression and pain, ketamine is not a drug to be trifled with. The side effects and risks, including addiction and severe bladder issues, are well-documented. Yet, the cavalier manner in which Musk is alleged to have used these substances begs the question: were these warnings ever considered?

Silence Speaks Volumes

Both Musk and the White House's refusal to comment on the report is as telling as it is disappointing. In the absence of denial or explanation, the public is left to navigate a murky sea of speculation and concern. The implications of such behavior, if true, are not confined to Musk's personal life; they bleed into the political arena, raising questions about judgment, influence, and the potential risks posed to public welfare and national decision-making.

Conclusion: A Sobering Reflection

This story is more than just a sensational headline. It's a stark reminder of the complexities and potential pitfalls at the intersection of power, politics, and personal behavior. The allegations against Musk, if substantiated, could have profound implications not just for him but for the political figures and movements he supports. It’s a cautionary tale that underscores the necessity of transparency, accountability, and the adherence to ethical standards by those in positions of influence and power.

In the end, what’s most disconcerting is not just the alleged drug use, but the possibility that such behavior is considered acceptable or, even worse, inconsequential, within certain echelons of power. It’s a systemic error that needs urgent correction.
