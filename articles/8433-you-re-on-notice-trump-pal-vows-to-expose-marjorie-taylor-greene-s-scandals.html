'You're on notice': Trump pal vows to expose Marjorie Taylor Greene's 'scandals'

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosNovember 24, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Our Analysis:The Latest Episode in the MAGA Saga: Laura Loomer vs. Marjorie Taylor Greene

A Donald Trump ally and confidante on Sunday threatened to reveal the “scandals” of Marjorie Taylor Greene, a MAGA ally who recently feuded with Trump.

Laura Loomer, who has been called the “Trump Whisperer” for her close association with the president, has long feuded with Greene over several different issues. Loomer has sided with Trump as the president has cast Greene out of his orbit, and she further escalated her attacks on the lawmaker over the weekend.

First, Loomer said, “Some people got very angry with me for saying I wish Marjorie Traitor Greene the worst. They told me it was unbecoming. I thought long and hard about this today, as some people I know told me to be better. I’d like to take this moment to let everyone know that in retrospect, I agree. That being said, after consulting with Allah today, I decided I want to wish [Greene] the best in her future career as a CNN contributor and runner up for cohost of the View.”

But that was not all.

“A lot of MTGs[sic] enemies are talking to me. Her scandals are not over,” Loomer added in a separate X post Sunday before tagging Greene in a direct message. “You’re on notice.”

Our Analysis:

The Latest Episode in the MAGA Saga: Laura Loomer vs. Marjorie Taylor Greene

In the swirling maelstrom that is MAGA politics, the latest spat between Laura Loomer and Marjorie Taylor Greene offers a revealing glimpse into the fractious and tumultuous nature of alliances within the Trump orbit. Loomer, dubbed the “Trump Whisperer” for her closeness to the former president, has taken a public swipe at Greene, a fellow MAGA ally, in a manner that underscores the volatile and often personal dynamics at play among these figures. Let’s dissect this sordid affair with the precision of a surgeon and the skepticism of a seasoned analyst.

First off, the very notion of Laura Loomer, a figure who’s no stranger to controversy herself, threatening to expose the “scandals” of Marjorie Taylor Greene, is rich with irony. Both individuals have built their political personas on the bedrock of right-wing extremism and conspiracy theories, making this conflict akin to a pot calling the kettle black. Loomer’s initial comment wishing Greene the worst, followed by a sarcastic retraction, reeks of the kind of pettiness and backstabbing that seems to be endemic in these circles.

Loomer’s statement that she consulted with “Allah” before deciding to wish Greene a sarcastic “best” in her “future career” as a CNN contributor and runner-up for cohost of The View is dripping with disdain and mockery. This not only highlights the personal animosity between Loomer and Greene but also the performative aspect of their public personas. The use of religion and the invocation of Allah in this context is particularly distasteful, showcasing a disregard for genuine faith and belief.

Furthermore, Loomer’s ominous warning that Greene’s “scandals are not over” and putting her “on notice” via an X post is emblematic of the performative drama that often accompanies political discourse in the age of social media. It’s a spectacle designed more for public consumption and self-aggrandizement than any substantive political disagreement or ideological schism.

The irony of this entire scenario cannot be overstated. Here we have two individuals, both of whom have been embroiled in their fair share of controversies, engaging in a public spat that seems more befitting of a reality TV storyline than serious political discourse. It’s a stark reminder of the depths to which political dialogue has sunk, particularly within certain factions of the right-wing ecosystem.

Implications for the MAGA Movement

This public feud between Loomer and Greene, with Donald Trump as a spectral figure in the background, underscores the inherent instability and infighting that plagues the MAGA movement. It’s a vivid illustration of how personal ambitions and vendettas often overshadow collective political goals, leading to a fracturing from within.

Moreover, this incident raises questions about the sustainability of a political movement that often seems more focused on personal loyalty to Trump than on any coherent set of policy goals or principles. The ease with which alliances are formed and then shattered in this sphere speaks to the fragile foundation upon which the MAGA movement is built.

In conclusion, the Loomer-Greene spat is more than just a petty quarrel; it’s a microcosm of the larger issues facing the MAGA movement. The prioritization of personal feuds and performative outrage over substantive political engagement is a systemic flaw that threatens to undermine the movement from within. As observers, we must ask ourselves whether this represents the inevitable implosion of a political faction too consumed by its own internal contradictions to sustain itself in the long term.
