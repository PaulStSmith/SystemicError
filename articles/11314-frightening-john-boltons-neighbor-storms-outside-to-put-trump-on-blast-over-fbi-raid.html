'Frightening': John Bolton’s neighbor storms outside to put Trump ‘on blast’ over FBI raid

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosAugust 23, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Our Analysis:The Politicization of Justice: Trump's Dangerous GameIn Conclusion

REUTERS/Tasos Katopodis

Following the early Friday raid of the home of John Bolton, former White House security advisor, a neighbor of his stormed outside amid the media frenzy to put President Donald Trump “on blast” for targeting his political enemies.

“All of this retribution, vengeance, and using the DOJ, the FBI and the power of government to go after anybody that disagrees with him is frightening,” said the man identified as Bolton’s neighbor, wearing a shirt bearing the phrase “Trump is a national disgrace,” reported Florida’s Voice News Director Eric Daugherty on Friday.

“And it’s only going to get worse if we don’t do something to make it stop. It scares the crap out of me.”

Trump has dubbed Bolton a “low life,” attacking him as recently as Friday morning after the raid for criticizing him in media appearances. The Trump administration has also demonstrated a willingness to wield the Justice Department as a tool to target his political enemies.

Edward Martin, Trump’s pick for DOJ pardon attorney, drew scrutiny recently for sharing a threatening social media post targeting New York Attorney General Tish James, who successfully sued Trump for fraud and slapped him with a fine of more than $355 million.

Trump has also instructed his DOJ to investigate the Democratic fundraising platform ActBlue, and has issued executive orders to restrict federal contracts with law firms that had handled cases adverse to Trump. Trump’s DOJ has also launched a criminal probe into former President Barack Obama over allegations that he and others manufactured a narrative that Trump colluded with Russia during the 2016 election.

The raid on Bolton’s home is linked to an investigation over the mishandling of classified documents, with Newsmax reporting earlier on Friday that the raid was sparked after CIA Director John Ratcliffe shared intelligence with FBI Director Kash Patel.

Our Analysis:

The Politicization of Justice: Trump's Dangerous Game

In what can only be described as a disturbing use of power, the recent raid on John Bolton's home has thrown a glaring spotlight on President Donald Trump's propensity to leverage governmental agencies against his perceived adversaries. The incident, reported by Florida’s Voice News Director Eric Daugherty, has ignited a firestorm of criticism and fear, notably from a source close to the chaos—Bolton's own neighbor.

The neighbor's impromptu press conference, complete with a "Trump is a national disgrace" tee, was not just a fashion statement but a bold declaration of resistance. His words, "All of this retribution, vengeance, and using the DOJ, the FBI and the power of government to go after anybody that disagrees with him is frightening," encapsulate a sentiment felt by many Americans. This isn't just about Bolton; it's about the very fabric of American democracy being under siege.

Trump's Vendetta Politics

Trump's attack on Bolton, whom he disparagingly called a “low life,” is part of a broader, more insidious pattern. The former President has not shied away from utilizing the Department of Justice (DOJ) as a cudgel against those he deems threats or traitors. The raid, ostensibly over the mishandling of classified documents, reeks of personal vendetta rather than justice served.

Edward Martin, Trump’s pick for DOJ pardon attorney, has already drawn ire for his threatening social media antics against New York Attorney General Tish James—a legal professional who dared to hold Trump accountable for fraud. This is not the behavior of a disinterested legal appointee but that of a loyalist enforcer.

Furthermore, Trump's directive to the DOJ to investigate ActBlue, the Democratic fundraising juggernaut, along with his executive orders aimed at law firms opposing him, highlights a brazen misuse of executive power for partisan warfare. And let's not overlook the criminal probe into former President Barack Obama, a move so blatantly politically motivated it would be laughable if it weren't so terrifyingly authoritarian.

A Call to Action

The neighbor's stark warning, "It's only going to get worse if we don’t do something to make it stop," should resonate as a call to arms. This isn't merely political theater; it's a systematic assault on the checks and balances that preserve our nation's democratic integrity. The fear that this instills, as aptly put by the neighbor, "scares the crap out of me," should be a universal reaction.

Trump's actions are emblematic of a leader who sees himself above the law, wielding power as both shield and sword to protect his interests and punish his foes. The raid on Bolton's home, under the guise of safeguarding national security, instead undermines it by showcasing to the world that in Trump's America, loyalty to the leader supersedes loyalty to the Constitution.

In Conclusion

What this episode reveals is a chilling blueprint for governance by retribution, where dissent is met with the full force of the state's power. The implications for American democracy, the rule of law, and the fundamental principles of freedom and justice are profound and deeply concerning.

The neighbor's outcry, while compelling, is but a single voice in the cacophony of dissent. It is incumbent upon all who value democracy, regardless of political affiliation, to recognize the dangerous path we tread when the instruments of justice are manipulated by those in power for personal vendettas. This is not just a wake-up call; it's a five-alarm fire threatening the very pillars of our society.
