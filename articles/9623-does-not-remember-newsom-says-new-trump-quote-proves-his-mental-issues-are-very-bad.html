Governor of California Gavin Newsom says U.S. President Donald Trump just revealed the extent of his mental health issues.

Newsom was referring to the president’s decision to make a midnight post in which he bizarrely claimed that it was the Biden administration who placed FBI agents in the crowd on Jan. 6, despite the fact that Trump himself was the president at that time.

The president took his social media site in the dead of night to write, “THE BIDEN FBI PLACED 274 AGENTS INTO THE CROWD ON JANUARY 6,” at a time when it would have been Trump’s FBI as the then-president was contesting valid election results.

He added, “If this is so, which it is, a lot of very good people will be owed big apologies.”

That led Newsom’s press office to weigh in just hours later. He wrote, “IN A LATE NIGHT POST, TRUMP DOES NOT REMEMBER WHO WAS PRESIDENT ON JAN 6, 2021 (HE WAS), WEIRDLY SHOUTS ‘DO SOMETHING’ (LIKELY AT CLOUDS). HIS MENTAL ISSUES ARE VERY BAD!”

Our Analysis:

Analyzing the Midnight Musings of a Former President

In an absurd twist that seems ripped straight from the pages of a poorly scripted political drama, Governor Gavin Newsom of California has pointed out the glaringly obvious: Former President Donald Trump’s recent social media tirade exposes not just a troubling disconnect from reality, but a blatant disregard for the factual timeline of his own administration. Trump’s midnight outburst, where he claimed that “THE BIDEN FBI PLACED 274 AGENTS INTO THE CROWD ON JANUARY 6,” not only defies logic but also basic calendar comprehension. It’s a statement so bewilderingly inaccurate that it demands a double-take.

The Facts: A Reality Check

Let’s lay out the cold, hard facts: 1. Donald Trump was, undeniably, the President on January 6, 2021. This is not up for debate; it’s a historical fact, as easily verifiable as the rising and setting of the sun. 2. The FBI, during this period, was under the jurisdiction of the Trump administration. To suggest that the Biden administration, which hadn’t yet taken office, somehow orchestrated an elaborate scheme involving FBI agents in the crowd during the Capitol riot is to argue against the basic principles of time, logic, and governance.

The Claims: A Bizarre Twist

Trump’s claims, delivered in the “dead of night,” as Newsom aptly describes, are not just a misstep or a minor factual inaccuracy. They represent a profoundly troubling narrative distortion that seeks to rewrite the events of a day marked by violence and chaos, all while attempting to shift the blame onto the incoming administration. It’s a maneuver as transparent as it is desperate, highlighting a willingness to manipulate public perception at all costs.

The Reaction: Pointing Out the Obvious

Governor Newsom’s response, characterized by a mix of incredulity and sarcasm, underscores the absurdity of the situation. By mimicking Trump’s all-caps approach, Newsom isn’t just mocking the former president’s style; he’s drawing attention to the ridiculousness of the claim itself. The suggestion that Trump might be “WEIRDLY SHOUTS ‘DO SOMETHING’ (LIKELY AT CLOUDS)” is more than a witty retort—it’s a stark illustration of how far removed from reality these allegations are.

The Implications: Beyond the Midnight Ramblings

This episode is more than just a footnote in the ongoing saga of post-presidential Trump. It’s a glaring example of how misinformation can be weaponized, how narratives can be twisted, and how the lines between fact and fiction can be blurred by those in positions of power. The real danger lies not in the ramblings themselves, but in the potential they have to influence public opinion and undermine faith in democratic processes.

In Conclusion:

Governor Newsom’s reaction to Trump’s bewildering claim is more than just political banter; it’s a necessary defense of truth in the face of blatant falsehoods. As observers, it’s crucial to critically evaluate these statements, understand the motivations behind them, and recognize the impact they can have on our perception of reality. The midnight musings of a former president might make for sensational headlines, but the underlying issues they reveal—about truth, trust, and the integrity of our democratic institutions—are deeply serious matters.
