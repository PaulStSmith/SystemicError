MSNBC's Maddow shreds Trump White House for labeling 13-year-old immigrant a 'terrorist'

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosApril 17, 20255 min read

MSNBC's Rachel Maddow expressed skepticism on her show late Monday at a new report that Trump administration added thousands of migrants to the Social Security Administration's death file — even though they are alive — alleging they all have links to either terrorist activity or criminal records.

The Trump administration directed the agency to add more than 6,000 living immigrants to its "death master file," a database of people who have died and whose Social Security numbers should no longer be used. The move was meant to pressure the immigrants, many of whom were legally allowed into the country under temporary programs established by the Biden administration, into self-deporting from the United States.

By adding their names to the database, their Social Security numbers were invalidated, effectively cutting them off from various financial services, legal employment, and government benefits.

"You can lose basically everything, almost instantly," noted Maddow. "Your health insurance, your home, your bank account, your job, your credit card, even your phone. Everything."

Maddow emphasized, "they're imposing on them a civil and financial death."

Maddow read from a Washington Post report from over the weekend in which the White House insisted, providing no evidence, the migrants affected were identified by the Department of Homeland Security as having criminal records or being on the terrorist watch list.

"Do they?" she retorted. "The records the Post reviewed showed those more than 6,000 people included a 13-year-old! A 14-year-old, and two 16-year-olds, as well as one person in their 80s and a handful in their 70s. They're all terrorists? Even the 13-year-old? All those people now legally dead, according to Social Security."

Maddow noted that one Washington Post source warned the Trump administration could "do this to anyone."

"'Anybody granted the appropriate permissions within Social Security could mark someone as dead without having to prove their demise in any way,'" she said, reading from the report. "'Employees' fear was partly that a bad actor who gained access to government credentials could label groups of living individuals as dead to target them for punishment.'"

Employees raised the alarm that the Trump administration "might try to use the database to go after people the president dislikes."

To boot, one source told the Post, that concern is the concern "about this misuse of the Death Master File is so high that the General Counsel’s office inside Social Security is, quote: 'Preparing an opinion that will find the Trump administration’s unprecedented use of the death database to be a violation of privacy law.'"

"In other words: to be illegal," she emphasized, adding: "Obviously, if the General Counsel of the administration did that, that might be a big deal."

Sparky's Analysis:

Analyzing the Maddow Report on the Trump Administration's Misuse of the Social Security Death File

In a striking revelation that sounds almost dystopian, MSNBC's Rachel Maddow has shed light on a rather alarming maneuver by the Trump administration: the addition of thousands of living migrants to the Social Security Administration's death master file. This action, ostensibly aimed at pressuring these individuals into self-deportation, reeks of a brazen misuse of power and a flagrant disregard for legal and ethical standards.

The Alleged Rationale

The Trump administration's justification, as per Maddow's analysis, hinges on the claim that these migrants, numbering over 6,000, were all linked to either terrorist activity or had criminal records. This rationale is not only unsubstantiated but also implausible. Maddow rightly questions the veracity of these claims, highlighting the inclusion of minors and elderly individuals among the so-called "terrorists." This point alone should raise serious doubts about the administration's narrative. Are we to believe that a 13-year-old, two 16-year-olds, and seniors in their 70s and 80s pose a security threat so grave that it warrants their civil and financial annihilation?

The Consequences

The implications of being falsely added to the death master file are catastrophic. Maddow's enumeration of the consequences — loss of health insurance, housing, banking services, employment, and more — underscores a deliberate attempt to impose a "civil and financial death" on these individuals. Such an action goes beyond immigration policy; it's a cruel and unusual punishment that strips people of their dignity and basic rights.

The Broader Implications

Maddow's report touches on a chilling possibility: the potential for misuse of the death master file against political adversaries or any group deemed undesirable by those in power. The fear among Social Security employees that this database could be weaponized is a stark warning of the dangers of unchecked executive power. When government officials can, without proof, declare individuals legally dead, we're venturing into the territory of authoritarianism.

Legal and Ethical Concerns

The piece finally hints at some pushback within the system, with the General Counsel’s office of the Social Security Administration reportedly preparing an opinion on the legality of this unprecedented use of the death database. Maddow's anticipation of this being a big deal is an understatement. It's potentially a watershed moment for accountability and the rule of law in U.S. governance.

Conclusion

Maddow's report is a harrowing exposé that should concern anyone who values civil liberties and the rule of law. The Trump administration's alleged actions represent not just a systemic failure but a moral one. The misuse of the Social Security death file to target migrants is a stark reminder of the lengths to which certain factions will go to enforce their immigration agenda, irrespective of the human cost.

This episode should serve as a wake-up call for the need for stringent oversight and checks on executive power. Without them, the slide into practices that erode the very foundation of democracy and human rights is all too easy.
