‘The bully is becoming a lame duck’: Ex-Trump loyalist goes scorched earth on president

Outgoing Rep. Marjorie Taylor Greene (R-GA) painted a bleak picture for President Donald Trump Saturday, labeling him as a “lame duck” who’s lost his grip on power.

Once among Trump’s most loyal supporters, Greene had a public spat with Trump amid her scrutiny of the U.S.-Israel relationship, and during her push for the Trump administration to release its files on Jeffrey Epstein. Trump would ultimately pull his endorsement for Greene and pledge to back a GOP opponent before Greene announced her retirement from Congress.

Having severed all ties with Trump, Greene took to social media Saturday to celebrate what she described as a “freeing” feeling after having “[broken] the chains from the bully.”

“The rising grassroots movement, totally outside political control, is the unstoppable collision of the right and the left who will fight with fury for America and OUR future and children’s future and our grandchildren’s future,” Greene wrote in a social media post on X.

“They’ve divided us for far too long and it’s destroyed too much. I refuse to be divided any longer. I refuse to hate my fellow Americans. I love my fellow Americans and I love my country. The old guard is dying, the bully is becoming a lame duck, and real America is rising.”

Trump first attacked Greene amid her calls for the Trump administration to release its files on Epstein, calling her a “ranting lunatic” and encouraging a Georgian to run against her in the upcoming Republican primary election. He also said that Greene had called him repeatedly, and suggested she was upset that he didn’t answer all of her calls.

Greene dispute Trump’s assertion that she called him repeatedly, and argued that instead, it was her support of the Epstein Files Transparency Act that “sent [Trump] over the edge.”

“It’s astonishing really how hard he’s fighting to stop the Epstein files from coming out that he actually goes to this level,” Greene wrote on social media last month

Our Analysis:

The Greene-Trump Fallout: A Political Schism Unveiled

The recent public spat between outgoing Rep. Marjorie Taylor Greene (R-GA) and former President Donald Trump unveils more than just a personal falling out; it highlights a seismic shift within the Republican Party and potentially forecasts a new era in American politics. Greene, once a steadfast ally of Trump, has now labeled him a “lame duck” who’s lost his grip on power, painting a picture starkly contrasting the unity often showcased by the party’s far-right faction.

The Root of the Rift

The core of Greene’s disillusionment appears to stem from two critical areas: the U.S.-Israel relationship and the Jeffrey Epstein scandal. These are not mere tabloid fodder but touch on deep political and moral questions that resonate with a substantial portion of the American electorate. Greene’s advocacy for the Trump administration to release its files on Epstein, coupled with her critique of U.S.-Israel policy, positions her at a unique intersection of political concerns that transcend the typical partisan divides.

Trump’s Retaliation: A Strategic Miscalculation?

Trump’s response to Greene’s criticisms and actions—ranging from withdrawing his endorsement to attacking her on social media as a “ranting lunatic”—reveals a potential miscalculation in managing his political alliances. By opting to publicly chastise Greene, Trump risks alienating the very base that champions transparency and challenges to the status quo, values that Greene’s stance on the Epstein Files Transparency Act epitomizes.

The Phenomenon of Political Reawakening

Greene’s declaration of feeling “freeing” after breaking ties with Trump taps into a broader sentiment of disenchantment and desire for a new political path. Her proclamation of a rising “grassroots movement”—one that fiercely advocates for the future of America beyond traditional party lines—signals a growing appetite for transformative change. This isn’t merely a personal revelation but a clarion call to those feeling marginalized by the current political system’s divisiveness.

Implications for the Republican Party and Beyond

The Greene-Trump fallout should not be dismissed as a mere political squabble. It underscores a pivotal moment for the Republican Party and American politics at large. The fallout signifies a potential fracturing within the GOP, challenging its unity and possibly recalibrating the political landscape.

Greene’s narrative of liberation from Trump’s influence and her vision of a united front against divisiveness could resonate with a broad spectrum of Americans. However, the question remains: Is this the dawn of a new political era, or merely a fleeting moment of rebellion?

In conclusion, the Greene-Trump spat is emblematic of deeper political undercurrents stirring within the Republican Party and the American electorate. It poses fundamental questions about leadership, loyalty, and the future direction of political discourse in the United States. The implications of this fallout will likely reverberate through the upcoming election cycles, potentially realigning political alliances and ideologies. As Greene steps away from Congress and Trump grapples with his waning influence, the stage is set for a dramatic reimagining of American politics.
