Trump fires off crazed rant threatening to deport Hollywood legend: 'Seriously criminal'

President Donald Trump raged at a pair of Democratic lawmakers and a Hollywood legend – and threatened to deport them all.

Reps. Ilhan Omar (D-MN) and Rashida Tlaib (D-MI) each shouted at the 79-year-old president Tuesday night during his record-long State of the Union address before walking out before he finished, and Trump castigated them the following day on his Truth Social website.

“When you watch Low IQ Ilhan Omar and Rashida Tlaib, as they screamed uncontrollably last night at the very elegant State of the Union, such an important and beautiful event, they had the bulging, bloodshot eyes of crazy people, LUNATICS, mentally deranged and sick who, frankly, look like they should be institutionalized,” Trump posted.

Omar called out that Trump should be “ashamed” and Tlaib called him a liar, and both of them shouted that he had “killed Americans” as part of his immigration crackdown, and the president threatened to send Omar, who was born in Somalia, and Tlaib, who was born in Detroit, back to their places of origin.

“When people can behave like that, and knowing that they are Crooked and Corrupt Politicians, so bad for our Country, we should send them back from where they came — as fast as possible,” Trump posted. “They can only damage the United States of America, they can do nothing to help it.”

Trump then sneered at actor Robert DeNiro, whose criticism he claimed was worthy of criminal prosecution.

“They should actually get on a boat with Trump Deranged Robert De Niro, another sick and demented person with, I believe, an extremely Low IQ, who has absolutely no idea what he is doing or saying — some of which is seriously CRIMINAL!” Trump posted. “When I watched him break down in tears last night, much like a child would do, I realized that he may be even sicker than Crazy Rosie O’Donnell, who is right now in Ireland trying to figure out how to come back into our beautiful United States.”

“The only difference between De Niro and Rosie is that she is probably somewhat smarter than him, which isn’t saying much,” he added. “The good news is that America is now Bigger, Better, Richer, and Stronger than ever before, and it’s driving them absolutely crazy! PRESIDENT DONALD J. TRUMP.”

Our Analysis:

The Art of Unpresidential Behavior: A Deep Dive into Trump’s Latest Tirade

President Donald Trump’s recent outburst on his Truth Social website serves as a vivid exhibition of his enduring commitment to divisiveness and unfounded personal attacks. In a shocking display that could only be characterized as unbecoming of a leader of the free world, Trump targeted Democratic Representatives Ilhan Omar and Rashida Tlaib, alongside Hollywood icon Robert DeNiro, with a barrage of insults and threats that seem ripped straight from a schoolyard squabble rather than the musings of a former President.

Trump’s Attack on Democratic Lawmakers

In his tirade, Trump lambasted Reps. Ilhan Omar (D-MN) and Rashida Tlaib (D-MI) with a venom that is both disturbing and sadly predictable. Omar and Tlaib, known for their vocal criticism of Trump’s policies and behavior, dared to express their dissent during his State of the Union address. Their audacity to challenge Trump was met with a vitriolic response, one that not only questioned their intelligence but also their mental health. Trump’s choice of words—”Low IQ,” “LUNATICS,” “mentally deranged and sick”—not only undermines the seriousness of mental health issues but also continues his pattern of using dehumanizing language against his opponents.

Furthermore, Trump’s suggestion to deport Omar and Tlaib “back from where they came” is not only factually inane (Tlaib was born in Detroit, making her as American as Trump himself) but also echoes the xenophobic and racist undertones that have characterized his political rhetoric. It’s a dangerous flirtation with authoritarian ideals, suggesting dissent against his administration warrants exile.

DeNiro in the Crosshairs

Moving on to his criticisms of Robert DeNiro, Trump’s comments are equally baseless and juvenile. Labeling DeNiro as “sick and demented” and suggesting his criticisms are “seriously CRIMINAL” is a bewildering leap into the realm of fantasy. The idea that dissent or criticism could be deemed criminal is antithetical to the very principles of freedom of speech and expression that form the bedrock of American democracy.

The Underlying Irony

The irony of Trump’s final claim, that America is “Bigger, Better, Richer, and Stronger than ever before,” being juxtaposed against his own divisive and unfounded attacks cannot be overstated. Such claims ring hollow when the messenger himself seems committed to sowing division and discord at every opportunity.

Conclusion: The Real Damage to Democracy

In essence, Trump’s recent social media rampage is not just an attack on specific individuals but an assault on the very norms and values that underpin democratic discourse. His willingness to engage in baseless personal attacks, his flirtation with authoritarian responses to dissent, and his disregard for the principles of free speech are indicative of a leader who has strayed far from the responsibilities of his office.

It’s a stark reminder that the real damage to the United States comes not from its critics but from those who, in positions of power, choose to undermine the country’s foundational values through their words and actions. In the end, it’s not about political ideology but about maintaining the dignity and decorum expected of the highest office in the land. Trump’s latest outburst is a systemic error that Americans cannot afford to normalize.
