'The president is a lunatic': Trump reposts wild conspiracy theory on Joe Biden's autopen

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosDecember 3, 20255 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Our Analysis:Autopen Madness: Trump’s Latest Conspiracy

U.S. President Donald Trump holds up a letter from former President Joe Biden as he issues executive orders and pardons for January 6 defendants in the Oval Office at the White House on inauguration day in Washington, U.S., January 20, 2025. REUTERS/Carlos Barria

Donald Trump has added further fuel to a conspiracy surrounding the use of an autopen by presidential predecessor, Joe Biden.

The president has previously claimed he will cancel all executive orders Biden passed when using an autopen. He took to Truth Social and wrote, “Any document signed by Sleepy Joe Biden with the Autopen, which was approximately 92% of them, is hereby terminated, and of no further force or effect.”

It seems Trump is still thinking on the autopen days after this post, and a week after showing Saudi Prince Mohammed bin Salman a framed photograph of Biden’s autopen, which has replaced the presidential portrait of Biden.

Trump has frequently alleged that the Biden administration forged the Democrat leader’s signature using an autopen to sign off on broader actions he was not aware of. While there is no evidence to support this, it’s a claim Trump has shared several times. His most recent claim of this occurring had him dubbed a “lunatic” by concerned members of the public.

Taking to Truth Social, the president shared a screenshot from far-right conspiracy site InfoWars. Alex Jones made an unfounded claim that Michelle Obama “may have used” Biden’s autopen during his time in office.

The full claim reads, “Michelle Obama may have used Biden’s autopen in the final days of his disastrous administration to pardon key individuals.” While the claim from Patrick Byrne is absurd, it falls in line with the president’s thoughts on Biden’s autopen.

Trump sharing the screenshot has caused some concern with members of the public. One viral post to X had one person write, “The president is a lunatic.”

Political commentator Brian Krassenstein went on to say the move to cancel all executive orders from Biden signed with an autopen is “illegal”. They wrote, “THIS IS ILLEGAL and Trump used an autopen hundreds of times.”

Trump would go on to call Joe Biden “crooked” and says the executive orders he signed into law would be revoked as they had been signed “illegally”.

Trump wrote, “I am hereby cancelling all Executive Orders, and anything else that was not directly signed by Crooked Joe Biden, because the people who operated the Autopen did so illegally.”

Our Analysis:

Autopen Madness: Trump’s Latest Conspiracy

In a theatrical flourish that would be comical if it weren’t so disturbingly earnest, Donald Trump has once again catapulted himself into the spotlight with claims that border on the absurd. This time, his target is the use of an autopen by Joe Biden, an issue he has inflated into a full-blown conspiracy without a shred of evidence to support his claims. Trump’s obsession with Biden’s autopen is not just a fleeting rant; it’s a calculated maneuver to delegitimize the current administration’s actions and to stir the pot of political discord yet again.

Autopen Apocalypse: Trump’s Eccentric Claims

Trump’s assertion on Truth Social that approximately “92%” of Biden’s executive orders were signed with an autopen and are therefore “terminated, and of no further force or effect,” is a bold statement. It’s a declaration that not only lacks legal standing but also demonstrates a profound misunderstanding (or perhaps a willful misrepresentation) of presidential powers and the legal framework surrounding executive orders. The autopen, a device used for the automatic signing of documents, has been used by presidents for decades, including, ironically, by Trump himself.

The Theater of the Absurd: Michelle Obama and the Autopen

Adding another layer of surrealism to this narrative, Trump highlighted an unfounded claim from the far-right conspiracy site InfoWars, suggesting that Michelle Obama might have used Biden’s autopen to pardon key individuals. This allegation, plucked from the depths of conspiracy theory lore, is so baseless and bizarre that it would be laughable if it weren’t being taken seriously by some segments of the public. It’s a testament to the strange times we find ourselves in when the former president of the United States amplifies such nonsense.

Legal Reality Check

Political commentator Brian Krassenstein hit the nail on the head by labeling Trump’s move to cancel all autopen-signed executive orders as “illegal.” The legality of executive orders signed via autopen is not in question; it’s a practice that’s been utilized by presidents for years and is a recognized tool in the administration of government. Trump’s declaration of canceling these orders is not only legally unfounded but highlights a dangerous propensity to disregard the norms and laws governing the executive branch.

The Bigger Picture: Undermining Democracy

What’s truly at stake here is not the legality of the autopen or the specifics of any executive order signed by it. The real issue is the continued effort by Trump to undermine the legitimacy of the Biden administration and, by extension, the democratic processes that underpin American governance. By promoting unfounded conspiracies and claiming unilateral power to cancel legally issued executive orders, Trump is not just attacking Biden; he’s attacking the very foundations of the U.S. political system.

In conclusion, Trump’s latest tirade against Biden’s use of an autopen is more than just another baseless claim; it’s a symptom of a larger, more concerning trend of undermining democratic norms and institutions. It’s imperative that these claims are seen for what they are: an attempt to sow discord and delegitimize democratic governance. As always, the truth is the first casualty in Trump’s war on reality, but it’s a casualty we can ill afford.
