'I feel used!' Watch MAGA influencer's full-blown meltdown over Epstein files

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosJuly 9, 20252 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Real America’s Voice/screen grab

MAGA influencer Jack Posobiec vented rage at the Trump administration after Attorney General Pam Bondi seemed to wash her hands of files on accused sex trafficker Jeffrey Epstein.

At a cabinet meeting on Tuesday, a reporter asked Bondi if Epstein worked for an intelligence agency.

"Are you still talking about Jeffrey Epstein?" an angry President Donald Trump exclaimed, but Bondi agreed to take the question.

"First to back up on that, in February I did an interview on Fox and it's been getting a lot of attention because I said, I was asked a question about the client list and my response was, it's sitting on my desk to be reviewed, meaning the file along with the JFK, MLK files as well. That's what I meant by that," the attorney general said.

"To him being an agent, I have no knowledge about that," she added. "And that's it on Epstein."

Soon after her remarks, Posobiec responded with a tirade on his Real America's Voice show.

"And once again, we are told now all the way from the heights of the United States Attorney General that the Epstein case is closed," he complained. "There's no more information on Jeffrey Epstein. Why do you guys keep asking about Jeffrey Epstein? She says that we don't need to hear about Jeffrey Epstein ever again."

"You know what this sounds like?" he asked. "Pam Bondi sounds like Hillary Clinton right now... This is not how you treat the American people! This is not how you treat basic communications! This is not how you treat your voters and your supporters!"

Posobiec recalled that Bondi had called a group of MAGA influencers to the White House earlier this year with a promise of providing them the Epstein files.

"I feel very angry, upset, used, every word you want to know from having gone to the White House and receiving this binder full of baloney that was completely publicly available information already that we were told was new information on Epstein," he remarked. "We were told that more information was coming. There wasn't. You claim that you had the list on your desk. You didn't."

"And now, when people have the obvious response," he added, "of asking a question about it, you get in their faces and you tell them to stop asking?"

"You sound like Hillary Clinton right now, and this isn't what you promised the American people."
