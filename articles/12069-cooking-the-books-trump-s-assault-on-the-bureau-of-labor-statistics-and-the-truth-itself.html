Cooking the Books: Trump's Assault on the Bureau of Labor Statistics and the Truth Itself

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosAugust 5, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

In yet another authoritarian turn that would make Orwell roll in his grave, Donald Trump has fired the head of the Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) after receiving a jobs report that didn’t paint him in a flattering light. This isn’t just a petty tantrum from a thin-skinned egomaniac who can't handle bad press. It's part of a broader and deeply dangerous pattern: the systematic dismantling of institutions that deal in facts.

Let’s be clear: The BLS is an independent agency. It doesn’t serve the President, the Congress, or any party. It serves the American people by providing objective, data-driven insights about the U.S. economy. When you fire the head of such an institution because the numbers it publishes make you look bad, what you're doing is sending a chilling message: Only the data that flatters the regime is acceptable. Everything else will be punished.

This is how totalitarian systems take root. Not through overt military coups or tanks in the street, but through the slow, deliberate erosion of truth itself.

Trump’s apologists will, of course, say this is just about “bias” or “fake numbers.” But the numbers in question? Public. Transparent. Verifiable. And here’s where it gets even more absurd: Trump is accusing the BLS of giving Biden inflated job numbers during the 2024 election season while simultaneously releasing bad ones for his administration now.

That sounds damning — until you actually check the data.

Guess what? Biden's final pre-election jobs numbers were actually weaker than the recent figures released under Trump. The idea that the BLS manipulated the data to make Biden look good is not just a lie. It’s a lie that dissolves under the most basic scrutiny. Anyone can look at the historical tables and see for themselves. This isn’t classified intel. It’s right there, in black and white.

So what’s the game here? It’s simple: Trump doesn’t care about jobs numbers. He cares about narrative control.

If the numbers hurt him, they're fake. If they help him, they're real. If they helped someone else, they were rigged. If they hurt someone else, they were divine truth. It’s not economics. It’s cult psychology, weaponized and broadcast from a gold-plated megaphone.

And it’s not even new. We’ve seen this pattern for years:

  • When COVID numbers were bad, he blamed the testing.

  • When polls were bad, he called them rigged.

  • When the 2020 election results didn’t go his way, he tried to overthrow democracy itself.

Now he’s coming for the BLS. And not just with rhetoric — with personnel changes. This is how you poison the well. This is how you turn an independent, trusted statistical agency into a mouthpiece for propaganda.

And it gets worse.

In the wake of this firing, Trump and his allies have made it clear that the new BLS leadership will be expected to toe the line. To polish the numbers. To cook the books. In other words: to lie.

That’s not speculation. That’s the playbook. Trump has already accused the agency of fraud. Not based on evidence, but on vibes. On his “feeling.” On his gut, which apparently outranks trained economists and professional statisticians.

And so we arrive at the final stage of Trumpism: epistemic annihilation.

No truth. No objectivity. Just whatever The Leader says.

The terrifying part is not that Trump says these things. It’s that tens of millions believe him. That he can say, in the same breath, that the BLS gave Biden good numbers and him bad ones — even when the reverse is true — and his base will nod along, hypnotized by the rhythm of grievance.

We’re not in a debate over policy anymore. We’re in a battle over whether reality itself will survive.

Institutions like the BLS are essential not just for economists or policymakers, but for democracy itself. If we can’t agree on basic facts, if every number is partisan, if every report is fake unless it flatters the ruling party, then the machinery of governance breaks down. We might as well go full Soviet and just announce record grain harvests while people starve.

And here’s the bitter irony: Trump himself has long claimed that Biden manipulated job statistics to look good. Yet now, he's fired the commissioner because the same agency made him look bad.

That’s not concern for integrity. That’s pure, unfiltered projection.

He accuses his enemies of the very crimes he plans to commit. And then he commits them in broad daylight.

We should be terrified. Not because Trump lies—that’s a given—but because he’s actively trying to build a world where truth is whatever he says it is. Where the scoreboard changes when he’s losing. Where statistics become state propaganda. Where dissent is treason.

If he gets away with this, nothing will be safe. Not jobs data. Not inflation reports. Not GDP. Not elections.

We will be ruled not by laws, not by facts, but by ego. And that’s not a presidency. That’s a personality cult with nuclear weapons.

Call it out. Every time. Loudly. Mercilessly. Before there’s no truth left to defend.

This isn’t a glitch.
It’s the design.
