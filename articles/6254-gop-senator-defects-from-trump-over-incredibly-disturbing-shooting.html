GOP senator 'defects' from Trump over 'incredibly disturbing' shooting

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosJanuary 26, 20261 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Sen. Bill Cassidy (R-LA) speaks on Capitol Hill. REUTERS/Kent Nishimura

At least one Republican senator is calling for a full investigation into the incident that led to the death of an ICU nurse by DHS immigration agents.

After the death of Alex Pretti, who appears to have been shot by agents after being disarmed while carrying a concealed weapon, one GOP lawmaker had some strong words for the “credibility” of the GOP-priority agencies.

U.S. Senator Bill Cassidy, M.D. chimed in, “The events in Minneapolis are incredibly disturbing.”

“The credibility of ICE and DHS are at stake,” he added Sunday. “There must be a full joint federal and state investigation. We can trust the American people with the truth.”

The centrists over at Maverick replied with, “Republicans are defecting.”

Republicans against Trump added, “First Republican senator to call for a ‘Full joint federal and state investigation.’ (The Trump regime blocked local investigators from investigating the shooting.)”
