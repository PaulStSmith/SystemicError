'Clown show!' White House melts down over CNN town hall

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosOctober 17, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Our Analysis:The Art of Deflection: A White House Meltdown

White House Press Secretary Karoline Leavitt holds a press briefing at the White House in Washington, D.C., U.S., October 6, 2025. REUTERS/Kent Nishimura

The White House melted down Thursday afternoon over a CNN town hall in which Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez (D-NY) and Sen. Bernie Sanders (I-VT) discussed the ongoing government shutdown.

The two lawmakers insisted that Congress needed concrete action to secure long-term Affordable Care Act subsidies and blasted GOP leadership for delays and lack of negotiations.

Karoline Leavitt, the White House press secretary, shared a news release in which the Trump administration blasted Ocasio-Cortez and Sanders.

“Last night’s CNN clown show starring Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez and Crazy Bernie Sanders was nothing more than a desperate smokescreen to hide the brutal reality of the Democrat Shutdown,” she said. “Make no mistake: Chuck Schumer and his band of obstructionists — on orders from the Radical Left lunatics in control of the party — deliberately shuttered the government to extort their socialist agenda at the expense of hardworking Americans.”

The White House said Trump took action to ensure service members get paid and that critical nutrition assistance programs remain funded, which help low-income women and children.

“Meanwhile, unhinged Democrats are threatening litigation rather than doing what they were elected to do,” she said, adding: “Their hypocrisy knows no bounds, even as workers go unpaid and essential services grind to a halt — and last night’s spectacle only reinforced how hypocritical Democrats really are.”`

The White House then tore into Sanders over his responses.

“Not only did Crazy Bernie have no answer for why he previously called a government shutdown ‘a serious and dangerous action that we must do everything possible to prevent,’ he was left stuttering after a young man pointed out Chuck Schumer’s blistering hypocrisy,” the release said.

Democrats have so far refused to vote to pass a stopgap funding bill that woud reopen the government, digging in their heels over extending health insurance subsidies under the ACA. They have said millions who buy their own insurance will see their premiums double overnight if the subsidies expire.

Our Analysis:

The Art of Deflection: A White House Meltdown

In a theatrical display of deflection that would make even the most seasoned illusionists envious, the White House launched a scathing attack on Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez (D-NY) and Sen. Bernie Sanders (I-VT) following a CNN town hall focused on the ongoing government shutdown. The administration’s response, laden with ad hominem attacks and hyperbolic rhetoric, serves as a textbook example of political distraction tactics at their most transparent.

The Trump administration’s response to the reasoned critique by Ocasio-Cortez and Sanders was nothing short of a meltdown, as described by the original article. Karoline Leavitt, the White House press secretary, resorted to name-calling and unfounded accusations in an attempt to shift the blame for the government shutdown away from Republican leadership. By labeling the town hall a “CNN clown show” and dubbing Sanders “Crazy Bernie,” the administration not only undermines the seriousness of the dialogue but also attempts to discredit the messengers rather than address the message.

The White House’s statement accuses Democrats of “deliberately” shuttering the government to “extort their socialist agenda at the expense of hardworking Americans.” This claim, however, conveniently overlooks the core issue at hand: the need for concrete action to secure long-term Affordable Care Act (ACA) subsidies. It’s essential to dissect this narrative and question why ensuring healthcare for millions is framed as an “extortion” rather than a fundamental duty of governance.

Furthermore, the administration touted its efforts to ensure service members get paid and that critical nutrition assistance programs remain funded, seemingly as a way to position themselves as the protectors of the American people. However, this narrative collapses under the weight of its hypocrisy when considering the administration’s role in exacerbating the shutdown’s impact.

The press release’s attack on Sanders for his supposed hypocrisy and inability to address a government shutdown’s seriousness is particularly ironic. It’s a classic example of projection, as the current situation starkly illustrates the administration’s failure to “do everything possible to prevent” a shutdown. The White House’s strategy here is clear: divert attention from its inadequacies by attacking the opposition’s integrity.

Democrats’ refusal to pass a stopgap funding bill without addressing the impending doom of ACA subsidy expirations is portrayed as stubbornness rather than a stand for necessary healthcare protections. The administration’s framing ignores the dire consequences for millions of Americans should these subsidies lapse, revealing a troubling disconnect from the realities of everyday citizens.

In Conclusion:

The White House’s response to the CNN town hall is a masterclass in deflection and denial. By attacking Ocasio-Cortez and Sanders with ad hominem jabs and painting Democrats as hypocritical obstructionists, the administration attempts to distract from its failure to navigate a critical governmental impasse. This scenario underscores a dangerous penchant for political theatrics over substantive policy discussions, especially on issues as crucial as healthcare. As the shutdown’s impacts intensify, the administration’s unwillingness to engage in meaningful negotiations—not the specter of socialism or alleged Democratic hypocrisy—stands as the real “desperate smokescreen” obscuring the brutal reality of American politics.
