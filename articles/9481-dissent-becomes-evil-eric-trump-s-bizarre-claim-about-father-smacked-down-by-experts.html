'Dissent becomes evil': Eric Trump's bizarre claim about father smacked down by experts

Eric Trump, Executive Vice President of the Trump Organization, and son of U.S. President Donald Trump, attends an interview with Reuters in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, April 29, 2025. REUTERS/Amr Alfiky

Eric Trump’s controversial remarks — declaring that his father is guided by God and praising what he describes as a divinely orchestrated series of events leading America to this moment — are being blasted by a group of hundreds of national security experts.

“I can’t tell you how many things are lining up,” the President’s son told right-wing podcaster Benny Johnson.

“I mean, think about the fact that this book came out on Charlie Kirk’s birthday, on the same day as we have peace in the Middle East,” he said, of his new book. “You know, I mean, so many different factors are all coming together at once in the most unthinkable, unbelievable journey.”

“Look how much better humanity and our world is,” under Trump, he claimed.

“You know, we’re saving Christianity. We’re saving God, we’re saving the family unit. We’re saving this nation. I mean, you know, DEI is out the window, Benny. You know, I mean, you no longer have Colin Kaepernick kneeling for the national anthem,” Trump continued. “You no longer have Budweiser going, woke as h–, all of this is dead.”

Trump claimed a resurgence in church attendance, and “a return to people, you know, valuing their children, and valuing society, and believing in the white picket fence, and what the American dream represents, and what the American dream stands for, and American exceptionalism, and peace around the world, and that people can coexist with one another without having to, you know, pick up arms and destroy each other for no reason whatsoever, other than, you know, incompetent, and, you know, and egotistical governments.”

“It’s a beautiful time, and he will go to heaven for all of that,’ he said of his father. “God absolutely guided this journey.”

The Steady State, a group of over 330 former national security officials, slammed the Trump scion’s remarks.

“Eric Trump is not talking politics,” they wrote. “Instead, he’s declaring a new moral order.”

“Framing Trump as God-guided and destined for heaven, he claims America has been ‘saved’ from DEI, protest, and pluralism. This is prophetic, authoritarian language,” they warned.

“Dissent becomes evil, and their victory becomes divine destiny.”

Former Obama head speechwriter turned political commentator Jon Favreau remarked, “We’ve moved rather quickly from ‘God saved Trump’ to ‘Trump is saving God,’ which I guess is the foundation of the new MAGA religion?”

Author Jennifer Erin Valent, winner of a Christian writers’ award commented, “No one ‘saves’ God. Every Christian should know that and be repulsed by the very assertion.”

Our Analysis:

The Divine Delusion: Eric Trump’s Prophetic Pronouncements

In a recent display of messianic fervor, Eric Trump has taken to the airwaves, not merely to promote a book, but to propagate a divinely inspired narrative that positions his father, Donald Trump, as nothing short of a modern-day savior. This isn’t just political hyperbole; it’s a dangerous detour into authoritarian theology.

Eric’s Claims: A Breakdown - Divine Guidance: Eric asserts that his father’s journey to and in office was guided by God, intertwining political leadership with divine endorsement in a manner that is both audacious and unprecedented. - Saving Christianity and God: The claim that the Trump administration is saving Christianity and even God Himself is not just theologically absurd but is outright blasphemous to any serious practitioner of the faith. - The Destruction of DEI: The dismissal of Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI) initiatives as somehow contrary to American values speaks volumes about the worldview being peddled here. It suggests a return to a monocultural and unchallenged hegemony that ignores the rich tapestry of American society. - Peace and Prosperity Under Trump: Eric’s rosy depiction of the Trump era as one of peace and prosperity flies in the face of the divisiveness, unrest, and economic challenges that marked the period.

The Reaction: From Concern to Condemnation The response from over 330 former national security officials—grouped under The Steady State—is telling. These are not individuals prone to hyperbole, yet they’ve called out Eric’s statements for what they are: a declaration of a new moral order that veers dangerously close to authoritarianism. This isn’t about politics; it’s about a fundamental reshaping of American values under a guise of divine mandate.

Jon Favreau and Jennifer Erin Valent offer critical external perspectives, highlighting the absurdity and theological danger in claiming to save God. It’s a reminder that what we’re witnessing isn’t just political rhetoric; it’s the crafting of a new MAGA religion that places Donald Trump at the center of not just American politics but American faith.

The Bigger Picture: The Threat to Democracy

Eric Trump’s remarks are not merely the excited ramblings of a proud son. They’re a calculated move to equate dissent with heresy, positioning any opposition to the Trump agenda as not just un-American but anti-divine. This is how democracies die—not with a bang, but with the slow acceptance of authoritarian dogma.

  • The Danger of Divine Politics: When politicians claim divine endorsement, they place their actions beyond the realm of human critique. In Eric Trump’s vision, to challenge Trump is to challenge God.

  • The Erosion of Pluralism: The outright dismissal of DEI and the mocking of peaceful protests like Colin Kaepernick’s kneel are indicative of a broader attempt to silence dissenting voices and perspectives.

Conclusion: A Call to Vigilance

Eric Trump’s comments should not be dismissed as mere political theater. They represent a clear and present danger to the pluralistic and democratic values that underpin American society. The reaction from national security experts underscores the gravity of the situation. This is not about right vs. left; it’s about right vs. wrong.

As citizens, our response must be one of vigilance and resistance. We must recognize these dangerous narratives for what they are—a threat to the very fabric of our democracy—and work tirelessly to counteract them. The future of our republic depends on it.
