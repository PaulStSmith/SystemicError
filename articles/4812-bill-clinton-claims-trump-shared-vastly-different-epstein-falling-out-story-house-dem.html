Bill Clinton claims Trump shared vastly different Epstein 'falling out' story: House Dem

Former President Bill Clinton revealed to lawmakers Friday that he had a previous conversation with President Donald Trump about what really caused the rift between him and Jeffrey Epstein years ago.

Rep. Maxwell Alejandro Frost (D-FL) described what Clinton said during a closed-door House Oversight Committee deposition in Chappaqua, New York, over his relationship with Epstein.

“I’m happy to clarify. President Clinton brought up a conversation he had with Trump in NYC re: Epstein,” Frost wrote on X. “President Clinton said that Trump told him that he had a falling out with Epstein due to a land dispute. This directly refutes Trump’s claims about why he fell out with Epstein.”

Rep. Suhas Subramanyam (D-VA) told CNN additional details about what Clinton said in the testimony.

“Since Chairman [James] Comer (R-KY) did bring that up I can specifically address that, which is basically President Clinton was asked about well, he brought up the fact that he spoke with President Trump at a golf event, and President Trump had told him that they, he and Epstein had had a falling out, and it was because of a land dispute,” Subramanyam said.

“It wasn’t because of what Epstein was doing to girls who were working at Mar-a-Lago, so it absolutely does dispute something that President Trump has said,” Subramanyam added. “I think Congressman Comer, Chairman Comer had mentioned that President Clinton cleared or absolved President Trump of of anything. But that’s not true. The reality is all President Clinton said was that any of all the things he heard from President Trump himself, nothing he heard would indicate that President Trump was involved in wrongdoing. Again, the transcripts will come out but I can’t confirm that that’s what was said.”

The president has previously said that his falling out with Epstein was related to the late financier and convicted child sex offender hiring away Trump’s workers at his Mar-a-Lago spa.

Trump has maintained that he did not have any ties to Epstein, despite his name appearing throughout the Department of Justice’s 3 million documents, with him mentioned about 38,000 times.

“I don’t know anything about the Epstein files. I’ve been fully exonerated,” Trump said.

Former President Clinton delivered his opening statements Friday under oath and shared a statement on his social media.

“I saw nothing, and I did nothing wrong,” Clinton said.

Neither the Clintons nor Trump has been accused of any wrongdoing.

Lawmakers have indicated that a transcript would be released and include testimony from the Clintons. No date or deadline has been announced.

Our Analysis:

Dissecting the Clinton-Trump-Epstein Saga: A Cloak and Dagger in American Politics

In a recent revelation that seems to have been plucked straight from a political thriller, former President Bill Clinton has shed light on a conversation with President Donald Trump regarding the infamous Jeffrey Epstein. The plot thickens as Clinton’s testimony to the House Oversight Committee unveils a narrative contrary to Trump’s public claims about the cause of his rift with Epstein. This development not only adds layers to the already convoluted relationship between high-profile figures and Epstein but also challenges the credibility of statements made by Trump in the past.

The Land Dispute vs. The Moral High Ground

Rep. Maxwell Alejandro Frost’s disclosure of Clinton’s testimony reveals that Trump attributed his falling out with Epstein to a land dispute, a stark divergence from the more morally charged reasons one might expect given Epstein’s criminal activities. This revelation starkly contrasts with Trump’s previous insinuations that his distancing from Epstein was related to unethical behavior, particularly concerning the hiring practices at Mar-a-Lago.

The narrative of a land dispute as the root cause of the fallout is, at best, a mundane explanation for distancing oneself from a convicted child sex offender. It’s a convenient storyline that sidesteps the ethical and moral implications of associating with Epstein. The specificity of this explanation, as opposed to a more generic disavowal based on Epstein’s criminal behavior, raises questions about the values and priorities that informed Trump’s decisions.

The Question of Involvement and Wrongdoing

Another critical aspect of Clinton’s testimony, as highlighted by Rep. Suhas Subramanyam, is the clear refutation of any claims by Trump that his fallout with Epstein was due to the latter’s illegal activities. This clarification is crucial, especially considering the backdrop of Trump’s assertion that he “saw nothing” and “did nothing wrong.” The insistence on a lack of involvement with Epstein’s wrongdoings, juxtaposed with Trump’s mention in the Department of Justice documents about 38,000 times, casts a long shadow of doubt over his statements.

Trump’s claim of being “fully exonerated” seems to stand on shaky ground, given the lack of any substantial evidence to support this assertion. The reliance on a verbal exchange to absolve oneself of association with Epstein’s activities, without addressing the voluminous mentions in official documents, is a thin defense at best.

The Transparency Conundrum

The promise of the transcript release from the Clintons’ testimony could potentially offer clarity or further muddle the waters. The absence of a set date or deadline for this release only adds to the anticipation and speculation surrounding the case. The lack of accusations against the Clintons or Trump does little to quell public curiosity, as the intricate connections and alleged conversations between these figures and Epstein continue to intrigue and horrify the public.

Conclusion: A Web of Uncertainties

The Clinton-Trump-Epstein saga is a convoluted narrative that encapsulates the complexities and controversies of American political elites. The revelation of a land dispute as the reason for Trump’s fallout with Epstein, as opposed to a moral or ethical stance against Epstein’s criminal activities, is a telling reflection of the priorities and considerations within these circles.

As the public awaits the release of the full transcript, the discourse around this case exemplifies the broader challenges of navigating truth and accountability in the murky waters of high-profile associations with criminal figures. The saga, with its twists and turns, underscores the urgent need for transparency, integrity, and ethical leadership in the political realm. Without these, the American public is left navigating a labyrinth of half-truths and speculations, yearning for clarity and honesty that remains just out of reach.
