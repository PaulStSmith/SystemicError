How one Trump lackey embodies his utter contempt for the law

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosJuly 27, 20255 min read

The Trump administration’s contempt for the law and for the people of New Jersey is on full display yet again in the new fight over control of the U.S. Attorney’s Office.

With its push to keep a Trump loyalist as our state’s chief federal prosecutor, the White House is not only ignoring the law, but also tarnishing the reputation of judges appointed by both Republican and Democratic presidents and sowing chaos in a state that’s already seeing enough chaos these days, thank you very much.

Here are the details: Alina Habba, a personal attorney for President Donald Trump, was appointed interim U.S. attorney back in March, but her 120-day appointment expires this week and Trump only just nominated her for confirmation by the U.S. Senate, so her nomination has yet to move. Federal law says the state’s federal judges can name her replacement until the Senate votes to confirm her, so on Tuesday they announced they chose Habba’s deputy, Desiree Leigh Grace.

The judges did not say why they rejected the Trump administration’s push to keep Habba in the post. I have some ideas:

  • Her public slobbering over alleged sex trafficker Andrew Tate in January.

  • Her stated pledge to use her job as prosecutor to help get Republicans elected in New Jersey.

  • Her prosecution of Newark Mayor Ras Baraka, a case that was so nakedly partisan she had to drop it mere days later.

  • Her announcement of an investigation into our governor and attorney general on Fox News, a probe that she appears to have created just so she could go viral.

  • Her subpoena of Gov. Phil Murphy over some dumb but obviously not criminal comments he made in February suggesting he might hide an undocumented immigrant at his home.

Whatever the reason, federal law gives our district judges the power to choose the U.S. attorney when the president’s nominee is not confirmed. So they did, and now the Trump administration is accusing them of colluding with Democrats to punish Dear Leader.

“This Department of Justice does not tolerate rogue judges — especially when they threaten the President’s core Article II powers,” Attorney General Pamela Bondi said on social media.

“The district judges in NJ just proved this was never about law — it was about politics,” said Bondi deputy Todd Blanche.

This is dangerous nonsense. Bondi and Blanche know that federal law is on the judges’ side here, but they’re taking advantage of the public’s ignorance of the law to cast the judges as enemies of the state.

I worry about how this will end. The Trump administration claimed they removed Grace, whose sole crime is that the federal judges who oversee cases in New Jersey think she’s better qualified than Habba (considering Grace has worked in the U.S. Attorney’s Office for nearly a decade, that’s unquestionably true). But Grace told the New Jersey Globe she intends to take over as U.S. attorney on Saturday. I can’t imagine that will sit well with all the president’s men.

If what’s happening in New York is any indication, we’re stuck with Habba. Trump’s pick for U.S. attorney in New York’s northern district, John Sarcone, was not chosen to remain in the position by that district’s judges, so the president gave him a different title, and now Sarcone is still the district’s acting U.S. attorney.

Good news for Trump. Bad news for anyone interested in a government that works for anyone but him.

Our Analysis:

The Trump Administration's Legal Contortions: A Case Study

The latest debacle surrounding the Trump administration's manipulation of the U.S. Attorney’s Office in New Jersey is not just a mere political skirmish; it's an outright exhibition of contempt for legal norms and democratic principles. The administration's efforts to install Alina Habba, a Trump loyalist, as the state’s chief federal prosecutor, blatantly disregards federal law and undermines the integrity of the judiciary, appointed by presidents from both parties. Let's dissect the layers of this legal farce and its implications.

Legal Loopholes and Partisan Ploys

First and foremost, the administration's insistence on keeping Habba in power, despite her 120-day interim appointment nearing its end and her nomination hanging in limbo, smacks of desperation and legal manipulation. Federal law explicitly allows the state’s federal judges to appoint a replacement until the Senate confirms a new U.S. Attorney. Ignoring this, the Trump administration has not only shown a flagrant disregard for the law but has also painted a target on the back of the judiciary, accusing them of collusion with Democrats. This is a dangerous precedent.

The Qualifications Question

The judges' choice of Desiree Leigh Grace over Habba speaks volumes. With nearly a decade of experience in the U.S. Attorney's Office, Grace's qualifications are not up for debate. Habba's tenure, however, is marred by controversy: from her alleged sycophantic behavior towards individuals of dubious repute to her blatantly partisan legal pursuits. The judges' decision was not only legal; it was logical. Yet, the administration's reaction was to cry foul, accusing the judiciary of playing politics. The irony is as thick as it is unsettling.

The Dangerous Rhetoric of "Rogue Judges"

Attorney General Pamela Bondi's statement accusing the judges of threatening the President's core Article II powers is as dangerous as it is misleading. Bondi, along with her deputy, Todd Blanche, are leveraging the public's lack of legal knowledge to vilify the judiciary and sow discord. This tactic of painting the judiciary as enemies of the state for simply following the law is not just reprehensible; it's a threat to the very foundations of our legal system.

A Government That Works for One

The situation in New Jersey is not an isolated incident. The administration's maneuver to keep John Sarcone in a position of power in New York's northern district, despite the judges' decision, is indicative of a broader strategy: to ensure the government works solely for Trump's benefit, at the expense of legal integrity and democratic principles.

Conclusion

The Trump administration's actions in New Jersey are a blatant display of contempt for the law and a deliberate attempt to undermine the judiciary. This is not just about partisan politics; it's about the erosion of democratic norms and the rule of law. The administration's willingness to flout legal procedures and vilify judges for political gain is not only dangerous; it's a stark reminder of the lengths to which it will go to consolidate power. The public must remain vigilant, informed, and ready to defend the principles that underpin our democracy. Otherwise, we risk descending further into a state where the law serves the whims of those in power, rather than the people it's meant to protect.
