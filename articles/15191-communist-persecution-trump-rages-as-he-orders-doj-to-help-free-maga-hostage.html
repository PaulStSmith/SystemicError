‘Communist persecution!’ Trump rages as he orders DOJ to help free MAGA 'hostage'

By Paulo Santos

Republican presidential nominee and former U.S. President Donald Trump makes a campaign stop at manufacturer FALK Production in Walker, Michigan, U.S. September 27, 2024. REUTERS/Brian Snyder

President Donald Trump on Monday escalated his defense of former Mesa County, Colorado, Clerk Tina Peters, who was convicted last year in a scheme to undermine the 2020 election, calling her a “political prisoner” and demanding her immediate release.

In a post to his Truth Social media platform, Trump lashed out at Democratic Colorado Attorney General Phil Weiser, who he accused of ignoring “Illegals committing Violent Crimes like Rape and Murder in his State,” while targeting Peters, an election denier whom Trump described as “a 69-year-old Gold Star mother who worked to expose and document Democrat Election Fraud.”

“Tina is an innocent Political Prisoner being horribly and unjustly punished in the form of Cruel and Unusual Punishment,” Trump wrote Monday. “This is a Communist persecution by the Radical Left Democrats to cover up their Election crimes and misdeeds in 2020. The same Democrat Party that flies to El Salvador to try to free an MS-13 Terrorist, is cruelly imprisoning, perhaps for life, a grandmother whose brave and heroic son gave his life for America.”

Peters was convicted in August on seven criminal charges related to a security breach that occurred in the spring of 2021 in her own office, according to media reports. Prosecutors said she allowed unauthorized access to voting equipment to prove false claims of election fraud – part of a broader MAGAfied movement to challenge Trump’s 2020 election loss.

“Colorado must end this unjust incarceration of an innocent American,” Trump concluded. “I am hereby directing the Department of Justice to take all necessary action to help secure the release of this 'hostage' being held in a Colorado prison by the Democrats, for political reasons. FREE TINA PETERS, NOW!”

A judge last year sentenced Peters to nine years in prison.

Our Analysis:

The Theatre of the Absurd: Trump's Latest Crusade

In a move that can only be described as a cacophony of irony and distortion, former President Donald Trump has launched a blistering attack on the Colorado legal system, elevating Tina Peters—a convicted felon involved in undermining the 2020 election—to the status of a "political prisoner." Trump's narrative not only stretches the bounds of reality but snaps them, revealing a dangerous precedent in the politicization of legal accountability.

The Misdirection Play

Trump's defense of Peters is rife with the kind of misdirection that would make a stage magician envious. By labeling Peters a "69-year-old Gold Star mother" and a victim of "Communist persecution," Trump attempts to cloak the undeniable facts of her conviction in a veil of martyrdom. This isn't just a defense; it's a full-blown theatrical production aimed at rewriting the narrative around the 2020 election and its aftermath.

Let's Break Down the Facts:

  • Tina Peters was convicted on seven criminal charges related to a security breach aimed at proving unfounded claims of election fraud. This isn't a matter of political opinion but of legal judgment based on evidence and due process.

  • Trump's attack on Colorado Attorney General Phil Weiser is a classic diversion tactic, implying that the state's legal priorities are misplaced without providing a shred of evidence to support his claims of negligence towards "Illegals committing Violent Crimes."

  • The invocation of Peters as a "political prisoner" is not just misleading; it's a slap in the face to actual political prisoners worldwide who are jailed for their beliefs, not criminal actions.

The Dangerous Precedent

Trump's call for the Department of Justice to intervene in a state legal matter over what he deems "political reasons" is a chilling proposition. It smacks of authoritarianism and a profound misunderstanding (or deliberate misrepresentation) of the separation of powers and federalism principles that underpin the United States' legal system.

The Reality Check

Trump's statement, "This is a Communist persecution by the Radical Left Democrats to cover up their Election crimes and misdeeds in 2020," is not just a baseless accusation; it's an ironic twist of reality. It’s the kind of statement that, in its bold disconnection from facts, seeks to sow further division and undermine faith in the electoral process.

Conclusion: The Mask of Martyrdom

In elevating Tina Peters to the status of a martyr, Trump is not just defending one individual's criminal actions; he's attempting to sanctify a broader movement that seeks to undermine the very foundations of democracy under the guise of protecting it. This isn't about justice for Trump; it's about perpetuating a narrative that serves his political ends, regardless of the cost to the nation's legal and democratic institutions.

As readers, it's crucial to see through the smoke and mirrors of such political theatrics and ground our understanding in facts, legal principles, and the enduring values that uphold democratic governance. The case of Tina Peters is not one of political persecution but of legal accountability. To suggest otherwise is not just misleading; it's a dangerous game that threatens to erode the very fabric of American democracy.
