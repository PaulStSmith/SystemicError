'Beginning of the end': Internet erupts as Trump turns on Marjorie Taylor Greene

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosNovember 16, 20255 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

REUTERS/Evelyn Hockstein

Social media erupted Friday night after President Donald Trump and his one-time MAGA ally Rep. Marjorie Taylor Greene (R-GA) publicly turned on each other in a stunning and highly public meltdown.

The fallout began after Trump abruptly withdrew his endorsement of Greene and accused her of veering “far left” – a break few in the conservative movement ever expected. Greene quickly fired back with an extended statement less than an hour later, accusing Trump of lying, panicking over the looming release of the Jeffrey Epstein files, and trying to intimidate GOP lawmakers.

Over social media, journalists, political commentators and activists from across the political spectrum reacted in a wave of disbelief – and, for many, delight.

“Wow. Trump dumps MTG WHAT A NIGHT,” Sam Stein, managing editor of “The Bulwark” posted. “You love to see it!” commented right-wing influencer Laura Loomer.

“A reminder that Trump is loyal to no one. No one,” former MSNBC host Mehdi Hasan wrote on X.

Lincoln Project co-founder Rick Wilson appeared to invite Greene to the other side, writing: “Welcome to the resistance” above the Georgia lawmaker’s reply post to Trump.

The online uproar came amid a chaotic evening in which Trump – after weeks of Greene breaking with him on several key issues – unleashed a blistering attack against her, sparking a full-blown public feud.

“This fallout with Marjorie Taylor Greene marks the beginning of the end for MAGA,” according to the popular social media account @CalltoActivism. “It’s quickly approaching the time Trump’s endorsement will be worse than the plague for Republican lawmakers. Bookmark this tweet.”

Democratic influencer Harry Sisson framed the back-and-forth between the former MAGA allies as a sign that Trump “is completely spiraling right now. The MAGA civil war just got even worse.”

“After 10 years, the tweets (truths) don’t stun like they once did,” Wall Street Journal reporter Josh Dawsey told his X followers. “But his post tonight unendorsing MTG is really worth a read.”

Lincoln Project co-founder Mike Madrid quipped to his own social media followers: “She’s gonna be a great new hostess on The View.”

Meanwhile, Trump took to his Truth Social platform to post a series of screenshots from commentators slamming Greene.

Our Analysis:

The Meltdown Spectacle: Trump vs. Greene

In what can only be described as a political implosion of the highest order, the once unbreakable alliance between President Donald Trump and Representative Marjorie Taylor Greene has disintegrated in a public spectacle worthy of a prime-time drama. This feud, erupting on social media, serves as a stark reminder of the volatile nature of political alliances, particularly within the MAGA movement. Let’s dissect the layers of this meltdown, shall we?

Trump’s Accusations: Veering “Far Left”

The notion that Marjorie Taylor Greene, a figure synonymous with far-right rhetoric, has somehow veered “far left” is so absurd it borders on satirical. Trump’s abrupt withdrawal of his endorsement based on this premise is a bewildering strategic move. It’s a testament to Trump’s penchant for hyperbole and a stark illustration of his transactional view of loyalty. Greene’s aggressive rebuttal, accusing Trump of lying and panicking over the Jeffrey Epstein files, adds a layer of intrigue and conspiracy to the already explosive situation.

The Reaction: Schadenfreude and Speculation

The social media frenzy that followed this public spat was predictably mixed with disbelief, delight, and a hefty dose of schadenfreude. Figures from across the political spectrum, including Sam Stein and Laura Loomer, couldn’t hide their glee. Meanwhile, Mehdi Hasan’s remark that “Trump is loyal to no one” hits the nail on the head, reaffirming the former president’s reputation for discarding allies when they no longer serve his interests.

The Lincoln Project’s response, particularly Rick Wilson’s sarcastic “Welcome to the resistance,” encapsulates the opportunistic nature of political commentary, where today’s foe can become tomorrow’s ally, albeit facetiously in this context.

The Fallout: A MAGA Civil War?

The dramatic falling out between Trump and Greene is emblematic of deeper fractures within the MAGA movement. The suggestion by @CalltoActivism that this marks “the beginning of the end for MAGA” might be premature, but it certainly highlights the instability and infighting plaguing the movement. The notion that Trump’s endorsement could become “worse than the plague” for Republican lawmakers is perhaps an overstatement, yet it underscores the diminishing returns of aligning too closely with such a polarizing figure.

Harry Sisson’s commentary on the MAGA “civil war” and Trump’s supposed spiraling only adds to the chaotic narrative surrounding this feud. While Trump’s social media activity, particularly his sharing of commentators slamming Greene, paints a picture of a man lashing out, it’s essential to remember that in the realm of political theater, appearances can be deceiving.

Conclusion: The View from the Sidelines

As spectators, it’s tempting to view this feud as a mere popcorn moment, a fleeting instance of political entertainment. However, the implications of such public infighting are significant. They speak to the fragility of alliances within the MAGA movement, the unpredictable nature of Trump’s endorsement, and the broader instability within the Republican Party. The quip by Mike Madrid about Greene potentially becoming a hostess on “The View” might be humorous, but it also hints at the reality that in today’s political landscape, yesterday’s pariahs can become today’s pundits.

In the end, this meltdown between Trump and Greene is more than just a spectacle; it’s a symptom of a deeper malaise within American politics, where loyalty is fleeting, and alliances are as stable as the shifting sands. As we continue to watch this drama unfold, one thing is clear: in the MAGA universe, expect the unexpected.
