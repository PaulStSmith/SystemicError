Political scientist Larry Sabato is warning that President Donald Trump and the Republican Party may exploit the likely federal government shutdown, saying that the GOP, as the “anti-government party,” is “perfectly happy” when the government shuts down and “perfectly happy to eliminate quite a number of domestic policy programs.”

Republicans will “go after anything that Democrats normally favor, and they’ll probably try to do it in a way that will not be repairable once this shutdown ends,” warned Professor Sabato, founder and director of the Center for Politics at the University of Virginia. He noted that the Trump administration’s goals include shrinking or eliminating large parts of the federal government.

Calling it “a very real threat,” Sabato told CNN that the White House has already warned that a shutdown could provide “a pretext for laying off many more federal workers.”

“They’re going to use this as best they can. Never let a crisis go to waste — that’s their theory.”

Sabato said it “would take a pocket full of miracles to stop what’s likely to happen at midnight,” and warned that “Trump is not bluffing.”

“The reason the implications are serious is because so much of what the U.S. government does grinds to a halt,” Sabato also said.

“Probably the most serious part of it is that the troops and the TSA officers and lots of other people, including air traffic controllers, while they have to show up, are not paid. So if this goes on for 35 days, there are a lot of families who are without food.”

“But, look,” he added, “the only thing that could break this and keep it relatively short is if you had mass protests from, say, the TSA workers and the air traffic controllers.”

“Oh, and by the way, the military — none of them are paid during a government shutdown, yet they are mandated to attend work.”

“Well, you know, that tends to wear on people, especially if they can’t pay their weekly or monthly bills.”

America, Sabato said, is “headed for another disaster and another advertisement to the world that the American system no longer works.”

Our Analysis:

The Sabato Warning: A Harbinger of Political Opportunism

In a recent discourse that should send chills down the spine of any American paying attention, political scientist Larry Sabato has issued a stark warning about the likely consequences of the looming federal government shutdown. Sabato, a seasoned observer of the American political landscape, is not one to sound the alarm without cause. His analysis, grounded in a solid understanding of the dynamics at play, suggests that the shutdown is not merely a procedural hiccup but a strategic opportunity for President Donald Trump and the Republican Party.

GOP: The “Anti-Government Party”

Sabato’s assertion that the GOP, often branded as the “anti-government party,” might find perverse satisfaction in the shutdown is a bitter pill to swallow. The idea that a major political party could be “perfectly happy” to see the government grind to a halt, potentially jeopardizing countless domestic policy programs, is a damning indictment of the current state of American politics. According to Sabato, the Republicans’ glee is not just in the cessation of government operations but in the opportunity to dismantle programs favored by Democrats, aiming to inflict damage that could be beyond repair.

A Pretext for Downsizing

The White House’s alleged warning, as highlighted by Sabato, that the shutdown could serve as “a pretext for laying off many more federal workers,” should raise eyebrows. It’s a stark reminder of the Trump administration’s long-standing goal to shrink the federal government. This strategy, under the guise of a budgetary impasse, reveals a chilling level of cynicism and opportunism. Sabato’s dire warning, “Never let a crisis go to waste,” underscores the calculated use of the shutdown to advance political and ideological objectives.

The Human Cost

Perhaps the most harrowing aspect of Sabato’s analysis is the spotlight on the human cost of the shutdown. The immediate impact on military personnel, TSA officers, air traffic controllers, and other federal employees—who, despite being mandated to work, would not receive pay—is a grave concern. The potential for this situation to extend for an extended period, as suggested by the 35-day benchmark, could lead to severe financial distress for countless American families. The invocation of a scenario where essential workers are left without food is not just scaremongering but a realistic appraisal of what prolonged federal paralysis could entail.

A System in Disarray

Sabato’s concluding remarks, lamenting the shutdown as yet another “advertisement to the world that the American system no longer works,” should serve as a wake-up call. The notion that America is “headed for another disaster” is not mere hyperbole but a reflection of a political system in disarray, unable to meet the basic needs of its citizens and govern effectively.

In Conclusion

Larry Sabato’s warning is not just an academic exercise but a clarion call to all stakeholders in the American political system. The potential exploitation of the federal government shutdown by President Trump and the Republican Party, as outlined by Sabato, is a textbook example of political opportunism that could have lasting repercussions on the fabric of American governance and society. The onus is now on the American people, and their elected representatives, to heed Sabato’s warning and act decisively to avert the dire consequences he outlines. Otherwise, the shutdown will not just be a temporary inconvenience but a symptom of a deeper, more systemic malaise afflicting American democracy.
