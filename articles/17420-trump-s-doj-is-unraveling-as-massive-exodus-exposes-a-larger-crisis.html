Trump's DOJ is unraveling as massive exodus exposes a larger crisis

Weaponizing Justice: How Trump’s DOJ Manipulations Undermine the Rule of Law

A Hollowed-Out Institution

The recent revelations about the severe understaffing at the Department of Justice (DOJ) under President Donald Trump’s administration uncover not just an overburdened system, but a strategically weakened one. Reports indicate that the DOJ has lost over 40% of its appellate lawyers since early 2025, leading to a significant backlog in cases. This is not mere bureaucratic inefficiency; it is a calculated erosion of institutional capability. When a lawyer at the DOJ admits in a court filing that there is no one available to take on new cases, it signals a crisis deliberately fostered at the highest levels of government.

Misdirection and Misuse of Power

The Trump administration has not only reduced the DOJ’s capacity through staffing cuts but has actively redirected its remaining resources towards vindictive pursuits. The administration has been using the DOJ to target political adversaries, as evidenced by a slew of high-profile indictments against individuals such as New York Attorney General Letitia James and former FBI Director James Comey. This misuse of power for personal vendettas diverts attention and resources from its primary function—upholding the law impartially—and reveals a stark abuse of executive power.

The Consequences of Retaliatory Politics

The strategic gutting of the DOJ serves a dual purpose for Trump: it weakens a key institution that could hold him and his allies accountable, and it transforms it into an instrument of political retribution. The targeting of political opponents through legal means underlines a dangerous shift towards authoritarianism. This is not just about staffing shortages or bureaucratic backlog; it is about repurposing federal law enforcement to serve the whims of Trump’s political agenda, compromising the very foundation of democratic governance.

A Pattern of Institutional Decay

The situation at the DOJ is a microcosm of a larger trend under Trump’s governance: the deliberate weakening of institutions to consolidate power. Similar patterns are observable in other federal agencies, such as the FBI and DEA, both of which have also experienced significant staff reductions. This systemic hollowing out of critical institutions poses a profound threat not only to the efficacy of these organizations but to the principle of rule of law itself.

Conclusion: A Call for Restoring Integrity

The revelations about the DOJ’s dysfunction demand more than passive concern; they necessitate active resistance and reform. It is crucial for the integrity of the United States’ justice system that there be a restoration of independence and strength to the DOJ and other similarly affected institutions. The current trajectory under Trump’s administration is not merely an administrative failure—it is a deliberate assault on the bedrock of American democratic principles. As citizens and stewards of these principles, it is our responsibility to demand transparency, accountability, and the depoliticization of federal law enforcement. Without these actions, the rule of law will remain a pawn in the hands of those who wield power not for justice, but for personal and political gain.
