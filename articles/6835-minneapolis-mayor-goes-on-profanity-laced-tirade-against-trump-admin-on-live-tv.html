Minneapolis mayor goes on profanity-laced tirade against Trump admin on live TV

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosJanuary 8, 20261 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Minneapolis Mayor Jacob Frey press conference (Photo: Screen capture)

Minneapolis, Minnesota Mayor Jacob Frey (D) unleashed on the Department of Homeland Security (DHS) for a statement that a woman fatally shot by an ICE official was a “domestic terrorist.”

The description of the incident from DHS, which is out of Washington, D.C., describes a scene that flies in the face of eyewitnesses and what videos have captured on the scene. DHS spokeswoman Tricia McLaughlin said “violent rioters” in Minneapolis were “attempting to run over our law enforcement officers.“

Frey called the DHS statement “bulls——” and went on to tell federal agents, “get the f—— out of my city.”

“What they are doing is causing chaos and distrust. They’re ripping families apart,” he said.

“They’re sowing chaos on our streets. And in this case, quite literally killing people. So they are already trying to spin this as an action of self-defense. Having seen the video of myself, I want to tell everybody directly that this was an agent recklessly using power that resulted in somebody dying, getting killed,” Frey continued.
