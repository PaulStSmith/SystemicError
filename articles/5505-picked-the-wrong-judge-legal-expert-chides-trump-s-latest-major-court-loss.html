'Picked the wrong judge': Legal expert chides Trump's latest major court loss

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosFebruary 11, 20264 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Our Analysis:The Unrelenting Assault on Voter Rights by Trump’s DOJ

U.S. President Donald Trump arrives at the White House, in Washington, D.C., U.S., February 9, 2026. REUTERS/Annabelle Gordon

A legal expert chided President Donald Trump’s administration on Monday after it suffered its latest major court loss.

Last week, a federal judge in Oregon issued a blistering order against the Trump administration, declaring in no uncertain terms that the Department of Justice cannot be trusted to protect voter rights. The order was issued at a time when the Trump administration is attempting to secure voter registrations from mostly blue states, a move that has alarmed several election experts.

Marc Elias, a voting rights attorney, discussed the ruling in Oregon on a new episode of “Democracy Watch” with progressive YouTuber Brian Tyler Cohen.

“Boy, did Donald Trump’s DOJ pick the wrong federal judge to wind up in front of, because this judge didn’t just rule against the Department of Justice,” Elias said.

He added that the judge declared Trump’s DOJ “can no longer be taken at its word.”

“[The court] can no longer assume that in these cases, when [the DOJ] says it wants these data for legitimate reasons, that in fact it wants them for illegitimate reasons.”

The Trump administration also appears to be resorting to other measures to secure the voter data it wants. For instance, Trump’s FBI raided an election office in Fulton County, Georgia, and hauled away roughly 700 boxes of materials related to the 2020 general election, including the county’s voter rolls.

Our Analysis:

The Unrelenting Assault on Voter Rights by Trump’s DOJ

In a move that can only be described as a brazen attack on the integrity of the United States’ electoral process, the Trump administration has yet again found itself on the losing end of a legal battle. This time, the arena was a federal court in Oregon, where the administration’s thinly veiled attempts to undermine voter rights were laid bare for all to see. Marc Elias, a titan in the realm of voting rights law, shone a spotlight on this judicial rebuke during an episode of “Democracy Watch” with Brian Tyler Cohen, revealing the depths to which the Department of Justice (DOJ) under Trump has sunk.

The ruling in question was nothing short of a judicial condemnation. The federal judge’s order was unequivocal: the Trump administration’s DOJ cannot be trusted. At a time when democracy seems perpetually under siege by those in power, such a statement from the judiciary is both shocking and sadly unsurprising. The administration’s efforts to collect voter registrations, particularly from states that lean Democratic, is a transparent attempt to disenfranchise voters under the guise of protecting electoral integrity.

Elias’s commentary underscores a critical aspect of this saga: the Trump DOJ chose the wrong judge to try their antics with. This isn’t merely a case of a legal strategy backfiring; it’s a testament to the resilience of the judicial branch in the face of attempts to corrode the democratic process. The judge’s assertion that the DOJ’s words can no longer be taken at face value is a damning indictment of an agency that is supposed to uphold justice, not subvert it.

Moreover, the administration’s tactics don’t stop at legal battles. The FBI’s raid on an election office in Fulton County, Georgia, where approximately 700 boxes of materials related to the 2020 general election were seized, is a stark example of the lengths to which Trump’s administration will go to secure voter data. This action is not only alarming but also a clear signal of the administration’s willingness to use federal agencies to further political goals.

The implications of these actions are profound. When a government seeks to manipulate voter data and erode trust in the electoral process, it strikes at the very heart of democracy. The judge’s ruling in Oregon isn’t just a setback for the Trump administration; it’s a warning to all who value democratic principles. The DOJ’s behavior, as highlighted by Elias, is not an anomaly but a symptom of a broader, more insidious attempt to undermine voter rights.

As we navigate these turbulent times, the importance of vigilance cannot be overstated. The battle for the soul of America’s democracy is ongoing, and as the events in Oregon and Georgia illustrate, it is a battle that requires the steadfast attention and resistance of all who believe in the principles upon which the United States was founded. The Trump administration’s disregard for these principles, as evidenced by its latest court loss and the actions that led to it, must not go unchallenged.
