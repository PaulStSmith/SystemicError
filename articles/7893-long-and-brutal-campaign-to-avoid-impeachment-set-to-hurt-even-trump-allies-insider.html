'Long and brutal campaign' to avoid impeachment​ set to hurt even Trump allies: insider

Donald Trump’s attempt to ensure Republicans can retain control of the House of Representatives in the 2026 midterms is set to face a stress test on Monday as GOP lawmakers in Indiana prepare to vote on redistricting.

With the conservative majority of the Supreme Court handing the president an assist last week by allowing race-based changes in Texas districts, Indiana has become a major focus of the White House.

That has led one former Indiana GOP lawmaker to go public and bluntly admit the president is trying to save his own hide.

According to a report from Politico, Indiana state senators will meet this week to consider a map passed last Friday by the Indiana House that could result in a 9-0 GOP delegation in the House.

The report noted the White House has enlisted reinforcements to go after recalcitrant GOP lawmakers who have been balking at making the changes the president is demanding.

Politico reported, “On Friday, Turning Point Action announced that it was partnering with several Trump-aligned super PACs to target Indiana Republicans who are blocking the president’s redistricting effort, including threatening to spend millions of dollars to primary resistant members of the Indiana GOP.”

Former Indiana House Republican Mike Murphy confessed, “These guys and ladies are under intense, 24-hour-a-day pressure and I don’t know if they can withstand it, ultimately — we will see.“

“I feel badly for them and their families, primarily. They came to be public servants, and instead they are pawns in really what I consider to be Trump’s strategy to avoid a third impeachment and potentially set himself up for a third term,“ he said.

Politico reported that three lawmakers from the state have indicated that, as of now, the White House doesn’t have the votes it needs for the change.

One Indiana Republican warned, “Some people think Trumpworld is bluffing or doesn’t have any juice left and this will just go away if the state Senate rejects the maps. The reality is that will only be the start of a long and brutal campaign to purge the state of anyone who opposed Trump on this issue. And there will likely be collateral damage that hurts even those who supported Trump.”

Our Analysis:

The Unvarnished Truth About Trump’s Redistricting Gambit

In an audacious move that reeks of desperation and manipulative tactics, Donald Trump’s current ploy to manipulate the redistricting process in Indiana lays bare the lengths he will go to secure a Republican stranglehold on the House of Representatives in the 2026 midterms. The Politico article under scrutiny here paints a damning picture of a president willing to bulldoze over democratic norms and principles to “save his own hide.”

The Supreme Court’s Dubious Assist

First and foremost, let’s talk about the elephant in the room: the conservative majority of the Supreme Court providing what can only be described as an “assist” to Trump’s agenda by allowing race-based changes in Texas districts. This decision not only sets a dangerous precedent but also emboldens similar maneuvers in states like Indiana, turning redistricting into a weaponized tool for political gain rather than a process aimed at fair representation.

The Indiana Frontline

Indiana, now in the eye of the storm, is witnessing a stark display of Trump’s influence over the Republican Party. The state senators are considering a map that could potentially result in a 9-0 GOP delegation in the House, a move that screams of gerrymandering. The involvement of super PACs, such as Turning Point Action, in threatening to spend millions to primary resistant members of the Indiana GOP is nothing short of political bullying. This aggressive push underscores a chilling reality: Trump’s shadow looms large over the GOP, dictating terms and demanding loyalty at the expense of democratic integrity.

The Real Victims

Former Indiana House Republican Mike Murphy’s comments shine a light on the real victims of this power play: the lawmakers and their families. These public servants, elected to represent and serve their constituents, find themselves caught in the crosshairs of a ruthless campaign to consolidate power. Murphy’s candid admission that this is all part of Trump’s strategy to avoid a third impeachment and potentially secure a third term is a sobering reminder of the personal ambitions driving these undemocratic actions.

The Stark Warning

The warning from an unnamed Indiana Republican that rejecting the proposed maps would only be the “start of a long and brutal campaign to purge the state of anyone who opposed Trump on this issue” is particularly harrowing. It not only highlights the toxic environment of fear and retribution that pervades today’s GOP but also signals the potential for collateral damage to the party and the democratic process itself.

Conclusion

What we’re witnessing in Indiana is not an isolated incident but a symptom of a larger sickness afflicting American politics: the relentless pursuit of power at the expense of democratic norms and principles. Trump’s redistricting efforts, supported by a complicit Supreme Court and enforced through intimidation and threats, represent a clear and present danger to the fabric of our democracy.

The Politico article, while shedding light on these developments, perhaps doesn’t fully capture the gravity of the situation. This is not merely a political maneuver; it’s an assault on the democratic process, a blatant attempt to manipulate electoral outcomes, and a dire warning of what’s at stake if such tactics are left unchecked. The need for a vigilant and informed electorate, ready to defend democratic principles, has never been more critical.
