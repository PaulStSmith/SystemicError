J6 cop appears to cough profanity during Republican's questioning of Jack Smith

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosJanuary 29, 20261 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

(Screenshot)

Former Washington, D.C. Metro Police Officer Michael Fanone appeared to cough an attack on a Republican member of Congress during the Judiciary Committee hearing with former special counsel Jack Smith on Thursday.

Rep. Troy Nehls (R-Texas) paused his questioning of Smith to tell the MPD and Capitol Police officers who have been forced to step down from their service that it wasn’t the fault of President Donald Trump on Jan. 6, rather it was a failure by police leadership.

Chairman Jim Jordan (R-Ohio) told Fanone to be “in order.”

Nehls lost it.

“Last thing, your hand gestures, Mr. Fanone — you need medication,” he shamed.

Jordan continued to try and control the members.
