This horrific pattern shows who is truly in charge of the White House

Based on an article by Thom Hartmann

The Governing Style Is Theft, Blame, and Sabotage

Blame Down, Loyalty Up

The source material describes a government that keeps punching down while covering for the people actually doing the damage. Ukraine gets blamed for inflation after resisting Russia. Russia’s aid to Iran and attacks on American troops are waved away. Canada gets threatened. The message is consistent: the weak are scolded, the aggressors are excused, and the public is told this is all normal.

That is not confusion. It is a political method.

Power Sits With the People Making the Calls

The institutional power in this story is not abstract. It sits with Trump and the people he appointed: the Treasury Secretary, the Secretary of State, Pete Hegseth, and the rest of the loyalist circle. The source is explicit that Trump gave the orders and created the environment. That matters, because it strips away the lazy fiction that this is some unmanaged drift.

When the state acts this way, the question is not who is being “divisive.” It is who controls the levers, who signs off, and who benefits when the machine is turned toward destruction.

Misdirection Is the Point

The article’s strongest claim is also its most obvious one: this administration keeps manufacturing noise to bury responsibility. Military escalation becomes “defense.” Illegal strikes become policy. The hollowing out of institutions becomes “reform.” Media punishment becomes “accountability.” Election sabotage becomes concern over fraud.

That framing is designed to misdirect attention away from decisions and toward distractions. If troops are dying, if stockpiles are being drained, if agencies are being gutted, if scientists are leaving, if food aid is cut, if labor power is crushed, the press is supposed to chase the spectacle instead of naming the operators.

The Government as a Private Extraction Scheme

The source lays out a simple pattern: public power is being used to move money upward and risk outward. Tax cuts for the top, Medicaid cuts for everyone else. SNAP cuts that hit children. NIH grants canceled while Europe and Canada recruit the scientists. Antitrust enforcement ignored. Foreign Corrupt Practices Act enforcement paused. Crypto deregulated. Defense and betting interests tied to the president’s family and allies. Pentagon money flowing where friends can capture it.

That is not incidental corruption around the edges. It is the operating logic. Public institutions are being converted into transfer mechanisms for the well-connected while everyone else absorbs the damage.

The Security State Is Being Rewired for Loyalists

The military and security examples in the source are not random. They form a pattern of internal capture. Promotions blocked. Officers pressured. civilian and uniformed leadership left vacant. Troops and cities subjected to armed deployments. Federal agents masked and unmarked in neighborhoods. Press suppression inside the military. Election security hollowed out.

A government that can no longer distinguish between public service and personal loyalty is already broken in the only way that matters. It still has uniforms, titles, and procedures, but those are being used to discipline dissent and protect the people in charge.

What This Story Actually Reveals

The larger pattern is not merely incompetence. It is authoritarian governance with a profit motive. The state is being used to weaken institutions, reward insiders, terrify opponents, and shift the costs onto people with the least leverage. That is why the blame keeps landing on Ukraine, scientists, children, workers, soldiers, and public agencies instead of on the people making the decisions.

The real scandal is not that the government is failing. It is that, for the people running it, the failure is the point.
