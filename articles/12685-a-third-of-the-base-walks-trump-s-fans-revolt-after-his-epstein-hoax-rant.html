'A third of the base walks': Trump's fans revolt after his Epstein 'hoax' rant

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosJuly 17, 20255 min read

Donald Trump (Photo by Brendan McDermid for Reuters)

Any hope that Donald Trump might have had that a Wednesday Truth Social post calling the Jeffrey Epstein debacle a "hoax" would satisfy his MAGA base was quickly dashed within minutes.

The president went off on a rant where he blamed Democrats for being behind what he wants to be referred to as a "scam" and the "Jeffrey Epstein Hoax."

He then turned on "my PAST supporters [who] have bought into this 'bulls--t,' hook, line, and sinker."

That did not sit well with some of his supporters who want questions about Epstein and his death answered.

In the comments after his post, Greeks For Trump wrote, "Why is the Epstein case so important to the MAGA movement? It’s WHY Trump won in 2016. He promised to rip the mask off the globalist elite and drain their filthy swamp. If the GOP betrays that mission again, at least a third of the base walks. And they will lose 2026. Guaranteed."

"So prove it was hoax instead of bashing your 'past supporters' because that isn’t helping your midterm chances," advised Publius Salon which received over 1K likes.

Greenwood TS complained, "You are pissed off that MAGA does not buy into your temper tantrum. This is your Waterloo Mr. President. Blaming people like me who have stood by you through thick and thin is disloyalty. Without us you are nothing but a bully running out of choices. Release the complete Epstein file’s unredacted. By the way we didn’t vote for escalating the Ukraine war into WW3. What happened to America First."

"Epstein was arrested and convicted by a child trafficking task force. That part is fact. So who were the recipients of his trafficking? You're running cover for something very nefarious and you want us to look the other way. Not going to happen. Your explanations are puerile at best and if you're willing to cave on this, what else and who else are you willing to sacrifice? If you're willing to let child rapists walk free, then you absolutely have no problem making policy that hurts my family and me. You've lost my trust," the president was admonished.

That led Harold Edwards to admit, "This post hurts my feelings, I have supported him for all three elections and stood by his decisions and policies. Yes he has done amazing things in the last few months and we should all be proud of that. But to say he no longer wants our support is heartbreaking to everyone that voted for him."

"President Trump, I support you 100%. Your characterization of the Epstein case as a hoax is confusing and concerning. If this was a 30 year hoax, then why is Maxwell in prison for sex trafficking? Why was Epstein arrested and held without bail? Why did the AG and FBI Director in 2008 whitewash the entire case? If Maxwell is guilty of Trafficking minors, who did she traffic them to? Just release the case files and force the NY Judge to remove the seal on the Maxwell case…. If it was/is a hoax that would prove it……." MRR suggested.

Deplorothal was brief, replying: "Dude… Not cool."

"You know when somebody's trying so hard to convince you it's pathetic... Come on Donald You're better than this," Pancake Drip lamented.

Our Analysis:

The Unraveling of Trump's MAGA Foundation: An Epstein Conundrum

In a move that can only be described as both baffling and self-destructive, former President Donald Trump has launched himself into the eye of his own storm, this time by dismissing the Jeffrey Epstein scandal as a "hoax" on Truth Social. This proclamation is not only a stark deviation from reality but also a gamble that has evidently backfired, highlighting a fissure in his once unassailable base.

The MAGA Base's Discontent

Trump's attempt to rewrite the narrative around the Epstein debacle as a Democratic "scam" has not only failed to pacify his supporters but has incited a wave of backlash from within his own ranks. Statements from user groups such as Greeks For Trump underscore a fundamental expectation among MAGA adherents: that Trump would dismantle the perceived corruption of the global elite—a promise now seen as betrayed.

The Irony of MAGA's Disillusionment

The irony here is palpable. Trump, who has styled himself as the ultimate outsider and truth-teller, is now being challenged by his followers for seemingly running interference for what they view as the epitome of elite corruption and depravity. The call from Greenwood TS for the unredacted Epstein files speaks to a profound sense of betrayal, underpinning a narrative that Trump is not only abandoning his promises but actively obstructing the "America First" agenda he once championed.

The Epstein Case: A Litmus Test for Loyalty

What stands out is the Epstein case emerging as an unexpected litmus test for Trump’s loyalty to his base. Accusations from the likes of Publius Salon and MRR demand transparency and truth, rather than deflection. The notion that Epstein's arrest and conviction for child trafficking are irrefutable facts makes Trump's hoax claims not just questionable but outright offensive to his supporters. This is not merely a political misstep; it is a moral failure in the eyes of his once-devoted followers.

The Fallout: A Warning for 2026

The backlash from Trump’s post is not a mere blip in his political trajectory but a potent warning of what might lie ahead. The sentiment expressed by Greeks For Trump, predicting a significant portion of the base’s departure should the GOP abandon its mission, is a dire forecast for 2026. This isn’t just about losing an election; it’s about fracturing the very foundation upon which Trump’s political identity was built.

Conclusion: A Self-Inflicted Wound

In his quest to maintain relevance and authority within the Republican Party, Trump has inadvertently exposed his Achilles' heel. By labeling the Epstein case a "hoax" and attacking his critics within the MAGA movement, he has alienated a core segment of his base that once viewed him as an unassailable hero. This episode reveals not just a crack in Trump's armor but a profound misjudgment of the values and priorities of his supporters. The question now is not whether Trump can recover from this self-inflicted wound, but whether his base is willing to overlook this betrayal in the name of political solidarity. The answer, however, seems to lean heavily towards a resounding "no," marking a potential turning point in Trump's iron grip on the MAGA movement.
