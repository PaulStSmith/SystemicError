The newly released warrant application behind the FBI raid of 2020 election ballots from Fulton County, Georgia is under new scrutiny. A new report reveals the FBI’s criminal investigation originated from a referral sent by a “stop the steal” campaign attorney known for “spreading baseless allegations of election fraud.”

The supporting affidavit for the warrant to search Fulton County election records was unsealed Tuesday, publicly revealing the mechanics behind the invasive search and seizure conducted by President Donald Trump’s politicized FBI.

“The FBI criminal investigation originated from a referral sent by Kurt Olsen, presidentially appointed Director of Election Security and Integrity,” stated an attachment to the application for the warrant approved by U.S. District Court magistrate judge Catherine M. Salinas.

Politico revealed that Olsen is also being furnished with “highly classified material“ with few limitations, despite Olsen having no intelligence background or experience. Some of the documents he has received from American spy agencies reportedly includes “sensitive compartmented intelligence programs,” which are among the most secretive materials held by intelligence agencies. He also reportedly has a direct line to Trump, and exploits those ties whenever agencies push back against his demands for material.

“Every time he hits a roadblock, he just calls POTUS,” An anonymous source told Politico.

Social media slammed the news that the same lawyer receiving highly sensitive voter data is also the source of the warrant that delivered the info.

“A beleaguered election denier — who had already been debunked many times — somehow convinced Trump and Bondi and Patel and Gabbard that there was cheating in Georgia when, in fact, it was disproven many times over,” posted one commenter on X. “Poorest Loser Ever.”

“So this is basically a rehash of every single conspiracy about the G.A. elections, filed as an affidavit by an FBI agent,” said another critic on X. “They’re even bringing back the ‘folded ballots’ thing. Incredible.”

“Kurt Olsen was one of Kari Lake’s attorneys when she kept suing to be the Governor of Az. He was charged with making FALSE STATEMENTS TO THE COURT,” posted yet another commenter.

“Basically, the person who issued the warrant has a big f—— red hat on their head right now,” said another.

Our Analysis:

The Politicization of Election Integrity: A Sharp Analysis

In what reads like a chapter ripped from a dystopian novel, the recent revelations surrounding the FBI’s raid of 2020 election ballots in Fulton County, Georgia, expose a troubling intersection of partisan politics and law enforcement actions. This scenario is not merely a story of procedural missteps; it is a glaring illustration of how the tendrils of politicization have wrapped themselves around the pillars of democracy, threatening the integrity of the electoral process.

The Unsettling Origins of the Investigation

At the heart of this controversy is Kurt Olsen, a figure whose credentials are as dubious as his motivations. Described as a “stop the steal” campaign attorney notorious for “spreading baseless allegations of election fraud,” Olsen’s influence over the FBI’s decision to raid Fulton County’s election records is both alarming and absurd. The fact that a warrant application for such a pivotal action originated from an individual deeply entrenched in debunked conspiracy theories is not just a red flag; it’s a blazing inferno signaling the breakdown of reasoned, impartial investigative criteria.

The Role of Olsen: Confounding and Dangerous

Olsen, now astonishingly serving as the Director of Election Security and Integrity, is depicted as wielding an alarming amount of power, with Politico reporting his access to “highly classified material” despite possessing no intelligence background. This scenario is not merely problematic; it’s akin to handing the keys to the kingdom to someone who believes the castle doesn’t exist. The notion that Olsen can directly influence the President to bypass resistance from intelligence agencies is indicative of a system where checks and balances have not just failed but have been deliberately dismantled.

The FBI’s Dubious Expedition

The mechanics behind the FBI’s invasive search and seizure in Fulton County, as unveiled through the unsealing of the warrant application, reveal a disturbing picture of law enforcement being weaponized for political ends. The underlying affidavit, driven by Olsen’s referral, serves as a testament to how easily the levers of power can be manipulated by those with access to the highest echelons of government.

Public Reaction: A Mirror to the Madness

The backlash on social media underscores a collective disbelief and disdain towards the entire ordeal. Critics and commentators alike have lambasted the episode as a reiteration of disproven conspiracies regarding the Georgia elections, with one commenter poignantly dubbing the situation “Poorest Loser Ever.” This public sentiment reflects a broader exasperation with the continuous recycling of baseless election fraud claims, further eroding public trust in electoral integrity.

A Red Hat of Bias

Perhaps the most damning critique comes from the observation that the warrant issuer might as well be wearing a “big f—— red hat,” symbolizing a blatant bias toward Trump’s narrative. This imagery encapsulates the crux of the issue: the investigation into Fulton County’s ballots is not rooted in a genuine pursuit of justice but is rather a politically motivated witch hunt, spearheaded by individuals who have shown a flagrant disregard for the truth.

In Conclusion: Democracy in Jeopardy

The events surrounding the FBI raid in Fulton County are not just a reflection of a flawed operation; they are indicative of a systemic erosion of democratic norms, where the lines between law enforcement and political partisanship have become dangerously blurred. As we navigate these tumultuous waters, it is imperative that we demand accountability, transparency, and a reinstatement of the principle that no one, regardless of their position or power, is above the law. The very foundation of our democracy depends on it.
