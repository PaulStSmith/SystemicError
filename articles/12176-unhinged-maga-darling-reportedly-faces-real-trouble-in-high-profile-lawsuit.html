'Unhinged': MAGA darling reportedly faces 'real trouble' in high-profile lawsuit

Candace Owens. (Shutterstock)

Right-wing podcaster Candace Owens may have bitten off more than she can chew in the legal fight with the first lady of France, according to a report in The Bulwark.

Since leaving Ben Shapiro's "The Daily Wire," Owens has pushed the outrageous theory that Brigitte Macron is a transgender woman who has committed a litany of crimes and has links to a centuries-old cult that's working to undermine Western civilization.

Owens "went so far as to beg the Macrons to sue her" if her claims weren't true, even "offering to pay their legal bills to do it," wrote senior reporter Will Sommer.

The Macrons called Owens's bluff, hiring prominent libel firm Clare Locke and filing a 219 page complaint in Delaware Court, prompting Sommer to declare, "Candace Owens is in some REAL trouble."

Far from cowering, however, Owens has doubled-down by re-airing four shows repeating her unfounded claims.

Owens's allegations are "a little hard to sum up," Sommer wrote, "in part because her story keeps shifting—as the Macrons’ lawsuit notes, Owens dropped some of her 'evidence' as it has been disproven—to describe them as unhinged doesn’t go anywhere near far enough."

Sommer noted plenty of evidence proving Owens's claims are false, "including photographs and Brigitte Macron’s birth announcement in a local newspaper."

The Macrons' lawsuit could lead to Owens paying hefty legal damages — that could prove financially devastating, according to Sommer. Possibly even more harmful is the revelation that MAGA is now split between Owens and many of her conspiratorial compatriots.

Also detrimental to MAGA are the potential headaches for Donald Trump, "who has had to tend to some awkwardly complicated diplomatic relations" in the past, and may now have to tread lightly around the Macrons.

Our Analysis:

Candace Owens: Biting More Than She Can Chew or a Calculated Move?

Candace Owens, a figure no stranger to the limelight for her often controversial and polarizing statements, seems to have stepped into a legal quagmire that would make even the most audacious provocateur pause. According to a report by The Bulwark, Owens's latest escapade involves none other than the first lady of France, Brigitte Macron, with Owens propagating an outrageously unfounded theory that Macron is a transgender woman embroiled in crimes and linked to a "centuries-old cult" aiming to topple Western civilization.

The Legal Labyrinth

It's not just the audacity of Owens's claims that's startling but her open invitation for legal scrutiny. She didn't just cross the line; she sprinted over it, "begging the Macrons to sue her" if her assertions were false, and even offered to foot the bill for their legal expenses. The Macrons, in response, didn't hesitate to call her bluff. By hiring Clare Locke, a prominent libel firm, and filing a comprehensive 219-page complaint in Delaware Court, they've set the stage for a legal showdown that, as Will Sommer aptly notes, puts Owens in "some REAL trouble."

The Shifting Sands of Owens's Narrative

Owens's strategy, if it can be called that, appears to be one of doubling down on her claims despite the legal storm brewing on the horizon. She re-aired four shows repeating her unfounded allegations against Macron, displaying a level of defiance that borders on recklessness. Sommer points out a crucial flaw in Owens's approach: her narrative is as stable as quicksand, shifting and dropping pieces of "evidence" as they are debunked. To describe her allegations as "unhinged" is, indeed, an understatement.

The Fallout

The implications of Owens's actions extend far beyond her personal legal woes. Financially, the lawsuit might leave Owens facing "hefty legal damages" that could devastate her financially. But the ripple effects touch on broader political and diplomatic fronts as well. The MAGA movement finds itself embroiled in internal strife, with factions split between Owens and her fellow conspiratorial compatriots. This infighting could be particularly damaging, not just to the cohesion of the movement but to its public image.

Furthermore, the situation presents potential diplomatic headaches for Donald Trump, complicating an already intricate web of international relations. The last thing Trump needs is another diplomatic minefield to navigate, especially one that could have been entirely avoidable.

In Conclusion

Candace Owens's legal battle with the Macrons is not just a personal vendetta gone awry; it's a cautionary tale of what happens when sensationalism and unfounded conspiracy theories are allowed to run rampant. The consequences of Owens's actions are far-reaching, affecting not just her but the MAGA movement and potentially straining U.S.-France relations. As this saga unfolds, one thing is clear: the cost of such baseless provocations is steep, and not just in legal fees. The true price might be the credibility and unity of an entire political movement, caught in the crossfire of one individual's reckless crusade.
