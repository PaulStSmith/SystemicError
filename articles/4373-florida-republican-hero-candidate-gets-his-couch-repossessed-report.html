Florida Republican 'hero' candidate gets his couch repossessed: report

The New Republic reports Florida’s newest, most rousing GOP candidate is churning the waters of ridicule.

“Florida’s gubernatorial hopeful James Fishback is a hero of the Republican Party’s growing sect of young white nationalists. He’s also a laughingstock among his own staffers,” said the New Republic.

Citing personal texts that Fishback’s former staff sent to the Bulwark, NR says the Sunshine State’s young Republican candidate is still apparently reeling from debt and an failure to get a grip on his finances.

“Fishback is $200,000 in debt due to a legal battle with his former employer Greenlight Capital, a hedge fund at which he was reportedly a low-achieving junior ‘research analyst,’” reports the New Republic. “When he started his own firm, that title was mysteriously bumped to ‘head of macro’—a promotion his former employer evidently did not appreciate. Fishback’s legal bills could balloon to nearly $2 million, and his staffers are concerned about their boss’s ability to pay.”

“Jesus Christ. I’ve created Frankenstein,” Fishback’s former county outreach chair Bryant Fulgham said in a texted conversation with campaign manager Emma Wright — after Wright claimed Fishback’s couch had been repossessed by debt collectors.

“Oh my god sh——. We’re gonna be sleeping on the floor soon up there,” Fulgham wrote, adding two weeping emojis.

Wright joked back that Fishback’s Tesla would likely be next: “Telsa (also being seized): STAY STRONG COUCH,” she wrote, adding a Black fist-up emoji.

Coincidentally, the New Republic reports that just last week, Greenlight Capital asked a judge to determine that the Tesla Fishback drives on the campaign belongs to him and not his father. This means his car could be repossessed, if the judge is onboard.

Other texts between Fulgham and Wright from February depict Wright lamenting that Fishback should simply hand over his gold watch to his former employers to keep them quiet.

“Like bro just give them the Rolex and call it a day,” Wright wrote.

“He claims he lost it or something idk,” Wright replied.

“Oh ffs,” Fulgham replied. “ … Yeah ‘I lost it in my safety deposit box,’” Fulgham joked.

Other texts reveal GOP staffers referring to Fishback’s supporters as “YNs,” which is a slang term that includes the n-word.

“At another point, Wright dismissed holding events for college students — who provide a lot of enthusiasm for Fishback’s campaign — because they’re too ‘broke.’” Reported the New Republic.

Our Analysis:

The Farce of Florida’s GOP Hopeful: James Fishback

The New Republic has unleashed a blistering exposé on James Fishback, a name that is swiftly becoming synonymous not with political prowess but with a chaotic blend of incompetence and mockery. Let’s dissect the tragicomedy that is Fishback’s gubernatorial campaign, a narrative so rich in irony it almost reads like fiction.

A Mockery Among Men

Fishback, heralded as a “hero of the Republican Party’s growing sect of young white nationalists,” finds himself the butt of the joke, not just in wider political circles but within his own campaign team. The irony is almost too delicious. Here we have a candidate, who by all accounts should be reveling in the adoration of his extremist base, yet he can’t even command respect from the people closest to him. The texts from his former staffers, as reported, paint a picture of a campaign that’s less a political juggernaut and more a sinking ship helmed by Captain Incompetent.

A Financial Quagmire

The financial revelations are perhaps the most damning. Fishback, $200,000 in debt due to a legal spat with his former employer, seems to be a walking, talking cautionary tale about living beyond one’s means. The promotion from a low-achieving junior “research analyst” to “head of macro” at his own firm comes off as a desperate attempt to inflate his credentials, a move that has clearly backfired spectacularly.

Legal Battles and Lost Luxuries

The prospect of his legal bills ballooning to nearly $2 million is a staggering figure that should give any rational voter pause. How can someone who cannot manage his own finances be trusted with the budget of an entire state? The texts between Fishback’s staffers, discussing the repossession of his couch and possibly his Tesla, are so farcical they could be mistaken for a bad sitcom plot.

The Rolex Debacle

The Rolex saga is a microcosm of Fishback’s entire campaign: a blend of farce, mismanagement, and outright denial. The casual suggestion to just hand over his Rolex to placate his former employers is met with a revelation that he’s either lost it or is hiding it. This isn’t just financial irresponsibility; it’s a toddler losing his toys in the sandbox and then lying about it.

Derogatory Terms and Dismissal of Supporters

The use of the term “YNs” by GOP staffers to refer to Fishback’s supporters, incorporating a racial slur, is reprehensible. It’s a glaring example of the toxic environment surrounding Fishback. Moreover, the dismissal of engaging with college students due to their financial status, despite their enthusiasm for his campaign, speaks volumes about the campaign’s values, or lack thereof.

Conclusion: A Campaign in Disarray

James Fishback’s gubernatorial run is not just a failing campaign; it’s a case study in how not to run for office. From financial mismanagement and legal woes to a staff that mocks him behind his back, Fishback’s political ambitions are crumbling under the weight of his own incompetence. The real tragedy here is not Fishback’s impending downfall but that his campaign, rife with mockery, mismanagement, and moral bankruptcy, was ever considered a viable venture by some. The voters of Florida deserve better than a candidate who is laughed at by his own team and is drowning in debt. As the saga unfolds, one can only hope that this farcical campaign serves as a cautionary tale for future candidates: political ambition cannot and should not be built on a foundation of incompetence and ridicule.
