'It got back to Trump': Ousted advisor angered president after meeting with world leader

U.S. National Security Advisor Mike Waltz looks on as U.S. President Donald Trump (not pictured) meets with Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu (not pictured) in the Oval Office at the White House in Washington, U.S., April 7, 2025. REUTERS/Kevin Mohatt

Add to the list of offenses that led to Donald Trump removing Mike Waltz as his national security adviser was a meeting where the now-ousted official discussed military options against Iran with Prime Minister Bibi Netanyahu ahead of the president sitting down with the Israeli leader.

According to a Saturday report from the Washington Post, a combination of the "Signalgate" scandal and Waltz jumping the gun by meeting with Netanyahu without Trump's knowledge helped lead to him being shunted aside and nominated to be the next United Nations ambassador instead.

According to the Post report "It [the meeting] got back to Trump and the president wasn’t happy with it."

The report notes that White House chief of staff Susie Wiles was already unhappy with Waltz, and "increasingly felt he was not a good fit for the president," according to insiders

"Waltz’s fate was sealed by his inclusion of a journalist on a sensitive Signal group chat in March. But he had been clashing with other top officials since early in the administration, including over whether to pursue military action against Iran, senior officials and Trump advisers said Friday," the report notes before adding that the meeting, was viewed as, "Waltz was trying to tip the scales in favor of military action and was operating hand in glove with the Israelis."

As one Trump adviser explained, “If Jim Baker was doing a side deal with the Saudis to subvert George H.W. Bush, you’d be fired. You can’t do that. You work for the president of your country, not a president of another country.”

Our Analysis:

Analyzing the Ouster of Mike Waltz: A Tale of Intrigue and Diplomatic Missteps

The saga of Mike Waltz's removal as Donald Trump's national security adviser is a vivid illustration of the chaotic underbelly of Trump's administration, marked by unilateral diplomatic maneuverings and a stark deviation from protocol that seems more akin to a political thriller than the operations of the world's leading power.

The Precipitating Factors

First, the "Signalgate" scandal. The inclusion of a journalist in a sensitive Signal group chat by Waltz is not just a faux pas; it's a blatant disregard for the sanctity of confidential information that could potentially jeopardize national security. This alone should raise alarms about the competency and judgment of those at the helm of security affairs.

Second, the unauthorized meeting with Netanyahu. Waltz's decision to meet with Prime Minister Bibi Netanyahu to discuss military options against Iran before Trump's scheduled meeting is not just jumping the gun; it's a reckless endeavor that undermines the president's authority and the United States' diplomatic posture. The meeting, as reported, wasn't just a casual prelude but a strategic alignment with a foreign leader on a matter as grave as military action, without the consent or knowledge of the President of the United States.

The Implications of Waltz's Actions

The actions of Waltz, as described, are not merely a breach of protocol; they are indicative of a deeper, more concerning trend of individuals acting in capacities beyond their remit, motivated by personal agendas or alignments. The comparison drawn to a hypothetical scenario involving Jim Baker and the Saudis is apt and underscores a fundamental principle: You serve at the pleasure of the president of your country, not that of another.

This principle seems to have been lost on Waltz, whose conduct as described by the Trump adviser, was tantamount to acting as an agent of a foreign power rather than an adviser to the President of the United States. This is a grave concern that transcends partisan politics and enters the realm of national security and sovereignty.

The Broader Context

The ousting of Waltz and his subsequent nomination to be the next United Nations ambassador is a peculiar turn of events. It could be seen as a demotion or a strategic move to place him in a role where he could potentially be less damaging or, conversely, more useful in advancing specific agendas on the international stage. This decision, however, does not erase the troubling behaviors and decisions that led to his removal as national security adviser.

Conclusion

The narrative surrounding Mike Waltz's removal is a microcosm of the broader issues plaguing Trump's administration: a lack of coherence, a disregard for established protocols, and a troubling inclination towards unilateral and reckless decision-making. It serves as a cautionary tale of the dangers posed by individuals who prioritize personal or external agendas over their duty to their country and its leader.

In the final analysis, the saga of Mike Waltz is not just about a national security adviser overstepping boundaries; it's a stark reminder of the need for accountability, transparency, and adherence to protocol at the highest levels of government. Anything less is a disservice to the office they hold and the country they serve.
