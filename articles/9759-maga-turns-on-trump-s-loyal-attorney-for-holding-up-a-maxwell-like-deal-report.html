MAGA appears to be turning on one of President Donald Trump’s most loyal attorneys, which could spell trouble for the embattled official, according to a new report.

Will Sommer of The Bulwark reported on Monday that MAGA appears to be turning on Deputy Attorney General Todd Blanche, who was Trump’s criminal defense attorney during his New York fraud trial. MAGA loyalists allege that Blanche is holding up a “Maxwell-like deal” for Tina Peters, a Trump ally who is serving a prison sentence for allowing a Trump-aligned conspiracy theorist to steal voter data from Colorado.

“Blanche has become the target of a right-wing media campaign pushing for his ouster,” Sommer reported. “His accusers blame him for, among other things, holding up a Maxwell-like arrangement for Tina Peters, a conspiracy theorist and former county clerk in Colorado sentenced last fall to nine years in prison for letting another conspiracy theorist copy hard drives of voter data and post the stolen materials to his website.”

“And in an administration that takes many of its cues from right-wing media, that might spell trouble for even entrenched Trump loyalists like Blanche,” he continued.

Sommer reported that Blanche’s position at the Department of Justice makes him vulnerable to attacks from the MAGA base because of Trump’s refusal to release the Epstein files.

“No one’s position inside the MAGA movement is ever fully secure. And that holds particularly true for those who serve in Trump’s Justice Department, where each month a new top official seems to be in the crosshairs of activists yearning for prosecutions, accountability or just good old-fashioned liberal tears,” Sommer wrote.

Our Analysis:

The MAGA Mob Turns on Its Own: The Case of Todd Blanche

In a recent report by Will Sommer of The Bulwark, a new chapter in the ongoing saga of MAGA loyalty tests unfolds, revealing the precarious position of Deputy Attorney General Todd Blanche. Once a stalwart defender of President Donald Trump as his criminal defense attorney during the New York fraud trial, Blanche now finds himself in the crosshairs of MAGA loyalists. The reason? Allegations that he is obstructing a “Maxwell-like deal” for Tina Peters, a Trump ally and convicted former county clerk. This episode is emblematic of the MAGA movement’s cannibalistic tendencies and its implications for the Trump administration.

The Allegations Against Todd Blanche

At the heart of the controversy is the claim that Blanche is responsible for delaying a favorable legal arrangement for Tina Peters, who was sentenced to nine years in prison for her role in a conspiracy to steal voter data in Colorado. The nature of the deal Peters allegedly seeks, likened to that of Ghislaine Maxwell, hints at the expectation within MAGA circles for preferential legal treatment for its members, irrespective of their legal infractions.

The MAGA Media Onslaught

According to Sommer, Blanche has become the latest target of a right-wing media campaign demanding his removal. This initiative underscores a critical aspect of the MAGA movement: its reliance on and the influence of right-wing media in shaping its agenda and targeting perceived adversaries. In this ecosystem, loyalty to Trump is paramount, and deviation from the party line can result in swift and merciless backlash.

The Perils of MAGA Loyalty

The predicament faced by Blanche serves as a stark reminder of the volatile nature of standing within the MAGA movement. Despite his previous loyalty and service to Trump, Blanche’s current standing is jeopardized by the whims of the base and the fervor of right-wing media. This scenario illustrates a broader trend within the Trump administration and its supporters, where loyalty is a one-way street, and the goalposts of allegiance are constantly shifting.

The Bigger Picture

This incident is not merely an internal dispute within the MAGA movement but a reflection of a more disturbing trend of politicizing the justice system to serve partisan ends. The expectation that Blanche should facilitate a “Maxwell-like deal” for Peters, regardless of the legal merits, underscores a dangerous inclination towards undermining the principles of justice and accountability.

Conclusion

The unfolding drama surrounding Todd Blanche is a microcosm of the broader challenges facing the MAGA movement and the Trump administration. It highlights the precariousness of loyalty, the influence of right-wing media, and the troubling push towards a justice system that bends to the will of political loyalty rather than the rule of law. As this saga continues, it serves as a cautionary tale of the perils of political fanaticism and the erosion of democratic norms.
