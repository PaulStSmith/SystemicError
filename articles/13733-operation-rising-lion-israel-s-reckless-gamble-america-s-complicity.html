Operation Rising Lion: Israel's Reckless Gamble & America's Complicity

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosJune 13, 20253 min read

On June 13, 2025, under the codename Operation Rising Lion, the Israel Defense Forces launched a preemptive strike on Iran, targeting nuclear facilities in Natanz and Fordow, missile production sites, and high-ranking military officials, including the reported deaths of IRGC chief Hossein Salami and General Mohammad Bagheri. Explosions were heard across Tehran, with residential buildings collapsing and flames consuming city blocks.

This is not just another air raid in a decades-long cold war. It is the most significant military escalation between Israel and Iran in modern history, and it has the potential to plunge the entire region—and perhaps the globe—into open warfare.

What Just Happened?

Israel's attack was sweeping and unrelenting. Using a combination of F-35 jets and drones, the IDF:

  • Destroyed major infrastructure at Natanz and Fordow.

  • Targeted and reportedly killed senior Iranian military leaders.

  • Hit residential high-rises in Tehran's Farhazad district.

  • Launched decapitation strikes on Iran's military and political leadership.

Israel immediately declared a national state of emergency and grounded all civil aviation. Tehran responded with missile launches toward Israeli territory and proxy attacks near U.S. installations in Iraq.

Iran has promised severe retaliation.

U.S.: Innocent Bystander or Strategic Enabler?

While Secretary of State Marco Rubio insisted that “the U.S. was not involved in the strikes,” the timeline raises eyebrows:

  • President Donald Trump convened an emergency National Security Council meeting after the strikes had begun.

  • Trump’s past rhetoric encouraged military posturing, boasting that under his watch, “World War III would never happen.”

  • Yet military gear, including howitzers, was deployed for a Trump-led “dictator parade” the same week.

Rubio’s weak disclaimers fall apart under scrutiny. The U.S. funds, arms, and shields Israel in every international forum. To claim Washington was unaware or uninvolved is to insult the intelligence of the global public.

A Region on the Brink

Iran immediately activated retaliatory mechanisms:

  • 181 ballistic missiles were reportedly launched toward Israeli airbases.

  • Explosions rocked Baghdad, home to multiple U.S. bases.

  • Iranian proxies in Lebanon and Syria have mobilized.

  • Iranian leaders have gone underground, expecting assassination attempts.

Meanwhile, global markets panicked:

  • Oil prices surged over 8% in under 24 hours.

  • Oman nuclear talks scheduled for Sunday now seem dead.

  • Airlines canceled all flights into the region.

This isn’t a skirmish. This is a flashpoint.

Strategic Logic or Ideological Suicide?

Why now? Why this scale?

The likely motive: Israel’s Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu no longer believes diplomacy will curtail Iran’s nuclear ambitions. The strike is both a military and political message—to Iran, to the U.S., and to Trump’s critics.

But this isn’t 1981. This isn’t Osirak. Iran’s capabilities are dispersed and hardened. This strike, even if initially successful, invites a prolonged war.

And the cost? Civilian lives. Diplomatic collapse. Global energy shocks.

Trump: Master of Disaster

The video released by Midas Touch paints Trump not as a commander-in-chief, but as a deranged braggart:

“I’ll keep you out of World War III. What would happen if we had a war? We won’t, with me.”

The world now sees the inverse. Trump’s foreign policy is a blend of paranoia, performative militarism, and passivity.

  • He delayed responses.

  • He offered no path to de-escalation.

  • He let Netanyahu seize initiative.

This is leadership by absence. And history will remember it.

This is the cost of failed diplomacy, enabled by fascistic theater and blood-soaked bravado.

Final Word: Death, Lies & Propaganda

In the smoldering haze of Tehran and the bunkers of Jerusalem, leaders play war like chess. But people are not pawns. They’re dying.

This war wasn’t inevitable. It was engineered—through provocation, propaganda, and political cowardice. And the world, once again, will bear the cost.

Let no one pretend this was peacekeeping.

Let no one say the United States wasn’t involved.

Let no one claim this was justice.
