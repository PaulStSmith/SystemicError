'He's very sleepy': Ex-Obama adviser says Trump is 'out of the loop' on his own admin

A former advisor to Barack Obama and Joe Biden has suggested Donald Trump is “out of the loop” on his own administration.

Jen Psaki, who appeared on The Late Show with Stephen Colbert, was asked whether Trump is “out of the loop” on Secretary of War Pete Hegseth’s actions. Psaki, who was an advisor to both Obama and Biden during their presidencies, has suggested Trump does not have a grasp on what is going on throughout his administration, let alone in the war room.

Colbert asked, “You worked for two Commanders-in-Chief and I’m just curious when you look at the various ways that these strikes on these boats, these alleged drug boats, that when these strikes have been originally reported and ordered.”

“When you hear about why there was a second strike, actually it was a third strike, actually there was a fourth strike, and Donald Trump says, ‘I don’t know what any of that was about’, have you ever seen another commander-in-chief so out of the loop when it comes to, specifically, acts of war against another nation?”

Psaki replied, “I mean, I’ve never seen another president out of the loop, period, so much. National security issues, it’s true, I hate to say that, it’s sad to say that and he’s also very sleepy, but national security issues...

“I worked for two presidents and it’s not just that they were up to speed on what was happening, they demanded to know what was happening. I would be sitting in the Oval Office with President Obama, with President Biden, and there might be any range of military actions, not illegal ones happening where people are being killed, war crimes, not those, but any type of military action.

“They might be in the middle of a meeting about something else and they would say, ‘I need an update from the Secretary of Defense. I need an update from the National Security Advisor.’”

“Because they felt the most serious thing and decision that came to their desk was decisions about life and death, putting our men and women in uniform out of harm’s way. That’s what I experienced.”

Our Analysis:

Analysis: The Unsettling Disconnection of Leadership

The recent commentary by Jen Psaki on The Late Show with Stephen Colbert unveils a troubling narrative about the functioning—or malfunctioning—of Donald Trump’s administration. Psaki’s insights, derived from her tenure under both Barack Obama and Joe Biden, paint a stark contrast between the operational norms of past administrations and the chaotic underpinnings of Trump’s tenure. The allegations made are severe: a President “out of the loop” on critical national security issues, a statement that, if true, underscores a dangerous departure from the expected vigilance of the highest office in the land.

Trump’s Alleged Ignorance: A National Security Hazard?

The conversation between Psaki and Colbert hinges on a critical accusation: that Donald Trump was unaware of multiple strikes against alleged drug boats, actions tantamount to acts of war. The gravity of a Commander-in-Chief being uninformed about military operations under their command cannot be overstated. It suggests a systemic failure, a breakdown in the flow of vital information—or worse, a disinterest or incapacity to engage with such essential details.

The Role of the President in Military Operations

Presidential oversight of military operations is not a mere formality; it’s a cornerstone of American democracy and a safeguard against unwarranted or illegal military actions. The President’s role as the Commander-in-Chief is to ensure that military operations are conducted within the bounds of the law, ethically, and in the best interests of the United States and its people. Psaki’s comparison of Trump’s alleged disengagement with the engaged and demanding nature of Obama and Biden’s leadership styles highlights a fundamental expectation of any president: to be the final checkpoint for decisions of life and death.

Sleepy Leadership or Systemic Sleepwalking?

Psaki’s remark on Trump being “very sleepy” is a pointed jab but also raises an alarming question: Was the Trump administration sleepwalking through critical decisions? If the leader of the free world is “out of the loop” on national security issues, it’s not just a personal failure; it’s indicative of a systemic error where checks and balances are bypassed or ignored entirely. This isn’t about a single leader’s preference for engagement; it’s about the structural integrity of the decision-making processes at the highest levels of government.

The Expectation of Presidential Diligence

The anecdote shared by Psaki, where Obama and Biden would interrupt meetings for updates on military actions, underscores the expectation of presidential diligence. The role demands an unwavering commitment to being informed and making informed decisions, especially when lives are at stake. The contrast drawn by Psaki between her experiences and what she perceives as Trump’s lack of engagement is not just a critique of a different leadership style; it’s a warning about the perils of detachment and disinterest in the Oval Office.

Conclusion: The Need for Engaged Leadership

The implications of Psaki’s observations extend beyond partisan politics or personal critiques. They touch on the core responsibilities of the Presidency and the essential need for an engaged, informed leader who understands the weight of their decisions. If a president is “out of the loop” on acts of war, what else are they missing? And at what cost?

The narrative presented by Psaki on The Late Show with Stephen Colbert is a chilling reminder of the importance of vigilance, engagement, and responsibility in leadership. It’s a call to demand better, to expect more, and to hold those in power accountable for not just their actions, but their awareness and engagement with the critical issues of our time.
