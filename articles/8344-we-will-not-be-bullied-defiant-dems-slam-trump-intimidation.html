'We will not be bullied': Defiant Dems slam Trump 'intimidation'

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosNovember 26, 20255 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Democratic lawmakers who participated in a video warning US military personnel against following unlawful orders issued by President Donald Trump remained defiant after being contacted by the FBI.

As reported by Reuters on Tuesday, the FBI has requested interviews with Sens. Mark Kelly (D-Ariz.) and Elissa Slotkin (D-Mich.), as well as Reps. Chris Deluzio (D-Penn.), Maggie Goodlander (D-NH), Chrissy Houlahan (D-Md.), and Jason Crow (D-Colo.), just days after Trump demanded their imprisonment or even death for supposed “sedition.”

One US Department of Justice official told Reuters that the FBI interviews are to determine if the Democratic lawmakers engaged in “any wrongdoing” when they spoke out against the president potentially giving unlawful orders that pit the US military against American civilians.

The Democrats, however, vowed that they would not be intimidated by any FBI investigation.

In a social media post, Slotkin said that Trump’s push to jail the Democrats for exercising their First Amendment rights demonstrated the reason why they decided to participate in the video in the first place. Slotkin accused Trump of “weaponizing the federal government against his perceived enemies,” while adding that he “does not believe laws apply to him or his Cabinet.”

“This is not the America I know,” added Slotkin, a former CIA analyst. “I’m not going to let this next step from the FBI stop me from speaking up for my country and our Constitution.”

Houlahan, Crow, Goodlander, and Deluzio issued a joint statement accusing Trump of “using the FBI as a tool to intimidate and harass members of Congress,” and vowed that “no amount of intimidation or harassment will ever stop us from doing our jobs and honoring our Constitution.”

“We swore an oath to support and defend the Constitution of the United States,” they emphasized. “That oath lasts a lifetime, and we intend to keep it. We will not be bullied. We will never give up the ship.”

The FBI interview requests came just a day after the US Department of Defense (DOD) said it had “received serious allegations of misconduct” against Kelly, who is a retired US Navy captain, and was launching an investigation that could result in him being recalled to active duty to face court-martial hearings for violating the Uniform Code of Military Justice (UCMJ).

In a separate social media post, Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth attacked all the Democrats who participated in the video as the “seditious six” and said that Kelly had been singled out for DOD investigation because he was the only member who was still subject to UCMJ given his status as a retired naval officer.

Our Analysis:

Unpacking the Assault on Democratic Lawmakers

In an audacious move that blurs the lines between justice and political vendetta, the FBI, following directives possibly influenced by former President Donald Trump, has targeted Democratic lawmakers for their participation in a video message to US military personnel. The essence of their message was a caution against obeying unlawful orders, a stance that is not only legal but necessary in a democracy that prides itself on the rule of law.

The Allegation and Its Implications

The lawmakers in question, including Sens. Mark Kelly and Elissa Slotkin, alongside Reps. Chris Deluzio, Maggie Goodlander, Chrissy Houlahan, and Jason Crow, find themselves in the crosshairs of an investigation spurred by Trump’s call for their imprisonment or death on dubious charges of “sedition”. This flagrant misuse of power to suppress dissent under the guise of an FBI investigation is alarming, to say the least. It raises profound concerns about the weaponization of federal agencies against political adversaries, a tactic reminiscent of authoritarian regimes, not a supposed beacon of democracy.

The Irony of ‘Sedition’

The irony here is palpable. These lawmakers are being accused of sedition for simply reminding military personnel of their oath to the Constitution, which includes resisting unlawful orders. If anything, this act reinforces their commitment to the nation’s foundational principles. Yet, Trump’s response—demanding extreme penal measures for this act—is a grotesque inversion of justice, painting patriotism as treason.

The Response from the Accused

The defiance from the accused lawmakers is both commendable and necessary. Elissa Slotkin’s remarks underscore the crux of this issue: the apparent attempt by Trump to jail his critics for exercising their First Amendment rights is an alarming signal of his disregard for the Constitution. The joint statement from Houlahan, Crow, Goodlander, and Deluzio, emphasizing their undeterred commitment to their oath, is a testament to their resilience against this political intimidation.

Weaponization of the Federal Government

The use of the FBI to intimidate and harass members of Congress is a stark illustration of the potential for abuse of power within the federal government. This isn’t just about a few Democrats coming under fire; it’s a warning shot to any who would dare speak against the administration’s actions. It’s a strategy designed to stifle dissent and consolidate power by instilling fear.

The DOD’s Dubious Role

The Department of Defense’s decision to investigate Mark Kelly, potentially recalling him to active duty for a court-martial, is a disturbing development. This move, especially when coupled with Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth’s derogatory labeling of the lawmakers as the “seditious six”, reeks of political retribution rather than genuine concern for military discipline or national security. It sets a dangerous precedent where military justice systems can be manipulated for political ends.

Conclusion: A Call for Vigilance

This situation transcends partisan politics; it’s a litmus test for the nation’s commitment to its democratic principles and the rule of law. The unabashed attack on lawmakers for upholding their constitutional duties is a dire warning of the lengths to which power can be abused. As this saga unfolds, it is imperative for all who cherish democracy to remain vigilant, vocal, and resolute in the face of attempts to undermine the very foundations of the republic.
