Trump accused of making 'desperate' move to 'please' right-wing ally Laura Loomer

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosSeptember 6, 20255 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Our Analysis:The Loomer Effect: A Dangerous Precedent in Political Oversight

FILE PHOTO: Laura Loomer arrives ahead of former U.S. President Donald Trump’s debate with Democratic presidential nominee and U.S. Vice President Kamala Harris, in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, U.S., September 10, 2024. REUTERS/Eduardo Munoz/File Photo

U.S. Sen. Mark Warner (D-VA), vice chair of the Senate Intelligence Committee, is accusing the Trump administration of a “nakedly political” decision to cancel his non-publicized classified meeting with nonpartisan career intelligence professionals after attacks from conspiracy theorist Laura Loomer, who has strong ties within the Trump administration.

“Over the weekend, conspiracy theorist Laura Loomer discovered the unpublicized, classified visit and launched a campaign of baseless attacks against both me and NGA Director Vice Admiral Trey Whitworth for hosting what has always been considered a routine oversight meeting,” Warner said in a statement. “In response to Loomer’s criticism, political appointees canceled the visit, just the latest example of an administration seemingly desperate to please Loomer, a figure with a long history of extreme and outlandish fringe views, including 9/11 denialism, anti-Muslim harassment campaigns, and associations with white supremacists.”

Warner warned that this decision “threatens the principle of civilian oversight that protects our national security,” and labeled it a “dangerous precedent, calling into question whether oversight is now allowed only when it pleases the far-right fringe.”

Loomer has been described as a “far-right conspiracy theorist” by BBC News and a “right-wing agitator” by Wired.

“Loomer has posted an unending stream of racist and Islamophobic content online, while pushing conspiracy theories about school shootings, election fraud, and the January 6 attack on the Capitol,” Wired reported one year ago. “She has ingratiated herself with senior figures in Trumpworld, such as Roger Stone and Donald Trump Jr. And throughout it all, Trump has complimented her, online and in person at his rallies and Mar-a-Lago home. He has shared her wild racist conspiracies, and even supported her to run for Congress.”

Last week, calling it a “scoop,” Loomer invoked the “Deep State” in announcing the classified meeting, which she described as a “fireside chat,” as she called Senator Warner a “rabid ANTI-TRUMP DEMOCRAT SENATOR.”

READ MORE: ‘Fantasyland’: Democrat Denounces Trump’s ‘Unhinged’ Plan for ‘Personal Greed and Power’

“How are Anti-Trump Democrats who participated in the Russia conspiracy hoax even allowed to be hosted as speakers inside our Intel agencies under the Trump admin?” Loomer wrote in a question directed to Director of National Intelligence Tulsi Gabbard.

Tuesday evening, Loomer celebrated, writing: “Whitworth’s scheduled September 5th fireside love fest with anti-Trump Democrat Senator @MarkWarner Mark Warner has been CANCELED!”

Just days before Loomer’s “scoop,” Senator Warner blasted DNI Gabbard.

Responding to a Wall Street Journal report that Gabbard named an undercover senior CIA officer as she stripped them of their security clearance, Warner asked, “Is Tulsi Gabbard still the DNI or is that Laura Loomer’s job now?”

“Gabbard’s purge of nonpartisan subject-matter experts is foolish, irresponsible, and short-sighted. Her actions continue to put American national security at risk,” he added. “It’s time for her to go.”

NBC News added that in April, “the director and the No. 2 official at the National Security Agency were ousted from their positions, and Loomer took credit for the dismissals. She had met with President Donald Trump that week and thanked him for ‘being receptive to the vetting materials provided to you.'”

The Loomer Effect: A Dangerous Precedent in Political Oversight

In what can only be described as a brazen subversion of democratic norms, U.S. Senator Mark Warner's classified meeting with nonpartisan intelligence officials was abruptly canceled. This decision, seemingly made in response to a campaign of baseless attacks by conspiracy theorist Laura Loomer, stands as a stark illustration of the current administration's willingness to bend to the whims of the far-right fringe.

Laura Loomer, a name synonymous with far-right conspiracy theories and Islamophobic harassment campaigns, has somehow found herself wielding an inordinate amount of influence within the Trump administration. Her attacks on Senator Warner, tied to a routine oversight meeting, prompted a move that Warner rightly condemns as “nakedly political.” This incident is not just about the cancellation of a meeting; it's a glaring red flag regarding the erosion of civilian oversight—a cornerstone of U.S. national security.

Loomer's influence is not a sudden occurrence. She has ingrained herself with key figures in Trump's circle, pushing racist, Islamophobic, and baseless conspiracy theories with apparent impunity. Her association with senior Trumpworld figures like Roger Stone and Donald Trump Jr., coupled with Trump's own endorsement of her views, paints a disturbing picture of the administration's tolerance and even support for extremist ideologies.

Warner's canceled meeting is symptomatic of a larger, more concerning trend: the politicization of intelligence and national security. When decisions about oversight and intelligence are made not on the basis of national interest but to appease extremist voices, the very fabric of American democracy is at risk. This incident raises serious questions about the integrity of oversight under this administration and whether it has been compromised to cater to the "far-right fringe."

Moreover, the dismissal of nonpartisan intelligence officials, as highlighted by Warner in response to actions taken by Director of National Intelligence Tulsi Gabbard, further emphasizes the administration's disregard for expertise and nonpartisanship in national security matters. The ousting of senior National Security Agency officials, credited to Loomer's influence, underscores a troubling purge of expertise in favor of political loyalty.

The cancellation of Senator Warner's meeting, therefore, is not an isolated incident but a symptom of a broader disease afflicting the current administration: a willingness to compromise national security and democratic norms to satisfy extremist allies. It sets a dangerous precedent, indicating that oversight and intelligence operations are now subject to the approval of fringe elements rather than governed by the principles of democracy and national interest.

In this context, Warner's confrontation with the situation is not just about a canceled meeting; it's a call to action against the politicization of intelligence, the undermining of nonpartisan oversight, and the dangerous influence of conspiracy theorists within the highest echelons of government. The administration's actions, as illuminated by this incident, demand scrutiny, criticism, and immediate correction to safeguard the principles that underpin American democracy and security.
