(And if you aren’t subscribing to Richardson’s work you are missing what I believe to be the definitive snapshots of these worrisome times.)

From Richardson’s piece:

Tonight, Trump’s social media account posted a deepfake video of (Chuck) Schumer and (Hakeem) Jeffries speaking to reporters. In the doctored video, Schumer talks with Mexican music playing in the background, while Jeffries stands beside him wearing what appears to be a colorful Mexican sombrero and sporting a mustache with the ends waxed and turned up.

In the video, Schumer’s image is made to say: “There’s no way to sugarcoat it. Nobody likes Democrats anymore. We have no voters left because of all of our woke, trans bullsh*t. Not even Black people want to vote for us anymore, even Latinos hate us. So we need new voters. And if we give all these illegal aliens free healthcare, we might be able to get them on our side so they can vote for us. They can’t even speak English, so they won’t realize we’re just a bunch of woke pieces of sh*t, you know? At least for a while, until they learn English and they realize they hate us too.”

Here is an image from Trump’s childish, racist, and repugnant post:

A post by Donald Trump.

THIS is how the sickly President of the United States is spending his time. Snotty, spoiled five-year-olds behave with more class …

It is as if it never happened.

Well, millions saw it, and we should all be outraged by both the madman who shared it, and our worthless press, which can’t see the danger of it.

Hegseth, an alleged stone-cold drunk and liar, finished with his foul speech, but not before predictably being upstaged by his repugnant, orange boss, who projectile-vomited all over room, and fully half the citizens of the country he hates.

It is also worth pointing out because they were too damn stupid to consider it, that gathering all our nation’s top generals, admirals and their commander in chief in one room might be the single greatest threat to national and operational security I have ever seen — even stupider than texting war plans to a reporter.

But who cares about little things like that when you have a president who is a pathetic showoff and malignant narcissist, and lives only to feed his insatiable, bloated ego. Acting the tough guy in a roomful of tough guys, was just too much for this low-class coward to pass up.

He lives for it.

He also called people who protest against him and disagree with his policies: “The enemy within.”

Again, he is talking about U.S. citizens. WE are the enemy.

So let me just duck this in before I forget, because I am furious: I served my country, you draft-dodging candy-ass, and you can kiss my ass, sport.

Thanks.

A Scathing Rebuke of Media Complacency and Trump’s Dangerous Antics

In an era where the boundaries of decency seem to be redrawn with each passing day, the latest debacle involving the repulsive social media activities of the former President of the United States, Donald Trump, serves as a stark reminder of the dystopian reality we find ourselves navigating. Yet, what’s arguably more alarming than Trump’s childish and racist antics is the utter failure of our so-called watchdogs—the media—to fulfill their most basic duty: holding power accountable.

The Media’s Silent Complicity

As the article poignantly highlights, the media’s refusal to adequately report on Trump’s deepfake video—a video so egregiously offensive that it caricatures political opponents in a blatantly racist manner—underscores a disturbing trend of normalization. This isn’t just about one repugnant video; it’s about the systemic failure of media institutions to confront the spread of misinformation and outright lies perpetuated by figures of authority. By choosing to ignore or underreport such incidents, these institutions are complicit in the erosion of democratic norms and the emboldening of authoritarian tendencies.

Trump’s Continued Assault on Decency

Trump’s behavior, as detailed in the article, is nothing short of repugnant. From using a platform of immense influence to spread divisive and false narratives, to making outrageous suggestions about using American cities as military training grounds, his actions are those of a man unhinged from reality. Yet, what should horrify us even more is his unabashed attack on the very citizens he once swore to protect. Labeling dissenters and critics as “the enemy within” is a tactic ripped straight from the authoritarian playbook, designed to undermine trust in democratic institutions and silence opposition.

The Military’s Unwilling Pawn

The gathering of military leadership under the pretense of loyalty to one man, rather than to the Constitution and the country, is a dangerous precedent. Trump’s apparent desire to transform the military into a personal tool of authoritarian control is not only a betrayal of American values but a clear threat to national security. The military’s role is to defend the nation and its democratic principles, not to serve the whims of a tyrannical leader.

The Unrelenting March Towards Fascism

The article ends on a chilling note, warning of the gradual but unyielding encroachment of fascism. With a government potentially at a standstill, the military deployed in American streets, and the foundational right to vote under attack, the signs are alarmingly clear. This isn’t mere speculation but a dire warning based on observable facts and trends. Yet, as the author laments, don’t expect the media to call it what it is. In their silence and inaction, the media not only fails the public but also plays a pivotal role in the erosion of democracy.

Final Thoughts

The fight for democracy and decency is far from over, and complacency is a luxury we can no longer afford. Let us be clear-eyed about the dangers we face and steadfast in our commitment to exposing and combating them.
