Manufactured Doubt: Trump’s Strategy of Electoral Deception

A Tactic, Not an Investigation

The recent activities by former President Donald Trump and his allies concerning the 2020 Wisconsin election aren’t a sudden flare-up of civic duty or an earnest quest for truth. Instead, they are a calculated maneuver, a part of a broader strategy to undermine democratic processes. Despite comprehensive audits and verifications which have consistently upheld the integrity of the 2020 elections, Trump’s persistence in attacking the legitimacy of the results reveals a dangerous game of power and perception manipulation.

Targeting the Weak

The focus on Wisconsin, a pivotal swing state, showcases a tactical choice. The use of debunked claims and conspiracy theories to harass and intimidate election workers is not random but a deliberate attempt to sow doubt and fear among those who administer elections. The decision to continue propagating these falsehoods, as noted by officials and election workers, serves to erode trust in electoral systems and puts personal and professional lives at risk.

Institutional Harassment

The involvement of the FBI, as reported, in querying election workers and local officials under the guise of following up on these baseless claims is particularly insidious. This isn’t just an overstep; it’s a misuse of federal authority, marshaled to lend credence to Trump’s unfounded allegations. This tactic not only diverts FBI resources from real threats but also potentially weaponizes federal power against perceived political enemies.

Preparing the Battlefield

The true purpose of these continued attacks on the election’s integrity appears to be future elections. As David Becker points out, this is less about rectifying past wrongs than about laying groundwork for future disputes. By keeping the myth of a stolen election alive, Trump and his allies pre-condition their base to distrust election outcomes, priming them for accepting more radical actions to contest or overturn results.

The Broader Pattern of Authoritarian Manipulation

This strategy reflects a larger pattern often seen in authoritarian regimes but increasingly manifesting in American politics. It involves the deliberate spread of disinformation, undermining of public trust in institutions, and the weaponization of government powers for personal and political gains. This pattern is not just about one election; it’s about destabilizing the democratic framework to maintain power.

Conclusion: The Long Game of Democracy Erosion

Trump’s ongoing assault on the 2020 election results must be seen for what it truly is: a cornerstone of a longer-term strategy aimed at weakening democratic norms and enhancing personal power. The real danger lies not in the absurdity of the debunked claims, but in the chilling effect they have on election officials and the corrosive impact on public confidence in electoral processes. As these tactics are likely to be replicated and refined in subsequent elections, recognizing and resisting this strategy is crucial for safeguarding democratic integrity.
