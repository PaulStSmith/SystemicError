Veterans take a shot at 'so-called patriots' as ICE arrests 70-year-old Air Force vet

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosOctober 3, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Our Analysis:The Harrowing Scene in Chicago: A Veteran’s Arrest and the Battle for Civil Rights

Air Force veteran Dana Briggs acosted by federal agents (Photos: screen captures)

Air Force veteran Dana Briggs was captured on video telling federal agents to go away. Suddenly, he was on the ground, surrounded by federal agents and U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement staff, led away and arrested.

WLS News reported that veterans and other allies met outside of the Broadview ICE detention center on Thursday, demanding that ICE stop “harassing peaceful protesters and immigrants.” The protest comes after President Donald Trump announced he was sending in troops to control the crime in Chicago.

“Veterans from the non-profit ‘Common Defense,’ alongside Illinois Vets for Change and the [American Civil Liberties Union], spoke out against the increasing presence of ICE and use of military force in communities,” said the report.

The groups call the arrest of the elderly man an escalation of state-sanctioned violence.

“So-called patriots are turning a blind eye when they see mass deportation efforts tearing apart our community, turning a blind eye when they see masked ICE agents and National Guard troops flooding our streets and numbing us to the reality that our civil rights, our democracy is being crushed day by day,” said Jesse Rojo, who serves as the chair of Veterans for Change.

Our Analysis:

The Harrowing Scene in Chicago: A Veteran’s Arrest and the Battle for Civil Rights

In a stark and unsettling illustration of the current state of American democracy, the arrest of Air Force veteran Dana Briggs by federal agents and U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) staff is a scenario that demands our undivided attention and unreserved condemnation. This incident, captured on video for the world to see, is not just a solitary event but a vivid representation of the egregious overreach of federal power into the lives of peaceful protesters and, more broadly, into communities across the nation.

Veterans and Allies Stand Against ICE

The response from veterans and other allies outside the Broadview ICE detention center is a powerful reminder of the deep-rooted dissatisfaction with the way ICE operates and how the Trump administration’s policies are perceived by many. The involvement of organizations such as Common Defense, Illinois Vets for Change, and the American Civil Liberties Union underscores the gravity of the situation. These groups, formed by individuals who have served their country, are now finding themselves in the throes of another kind of battle: the fight for the soul of our nation.

The Trump Administration’s Controversial Deployment

President Donald Trump’s decision to send troops to Chicago to “control the crime” is a move fraught with controversy and potential for abuse. This action, coupled with the increased presence of ICE and military force in communities, signals a troubling escalation in the use of state-sanctioned violence against civilians. It’s a tactic that not only undermines the principles of democracy and civil liberties but also sows division and fear among the populace.

A So-Called Patriotism?

Jesse Rojo’s comments strike at the heart of the issue. The notion that “so-called patriots” are willingly turning a blind eye to the dismantling of community cohesion and the erosion of civil rights is both profound and deeply disturbing. The mass deportation efforts, the ominous sight of masked ICE agents and National Guard troops patrolling the streets - these are not signs of a healthy democracy. Instead, they are harbingers of an authoritarian overreach that threatens the very foundations of our society.

The Bigger Picture

The arrest of Dana Briggs is not an isolated incident; it is a symptom of a larger, more systemic problem facing the United States. The increased militarization of the police, the expansion of ICE’s powers, and the willingness of the current administration to deploy federal troops within the country’s own borders are indicative of a disturbing shift towards authoritarianism.

In this moment of national reckoning, it is imperative that we, as a society, confront these challenges head-on. The voices of veterans and allies outside the Broadview ICE detention center are not just cries of protest against one man’s arrest. They are a call to action against the creeping encroachment of a government willing to use its most powerful tools not to protect, but to intimidate its own people.

In closing, Dana Briggs’ arrest should not be viewed in isolation but as a call to vigilance and resistance against the forces that seek to undermine our democratic institutions and civil liberties. The battle for the soul of America is very much alive, and it’s a battle we cannot afford to lose.
