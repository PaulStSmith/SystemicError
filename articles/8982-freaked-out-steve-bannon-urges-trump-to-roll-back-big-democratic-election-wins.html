Freaked-out Steve Bannon urges Trump to roll back big Democratic election wins

Steve Bannon is freaking out about Tuesday’s election results that handed Democrats big wins – especially in a pair of key races.

Democrats significantly outperformed their election margins from last year and racked up wins in gubernatorial races in Virginia and New Jersey, but Bannon told Politico that President Donald Trump must take immediate action to undo Zohran Mamdani’s win in the New York mayor’s race and the results of the Proposition 50 redistricting measure in California.

“Two things I would do,” Bannon told Politico. “Number one, get [Assistant Attorney General] Harmeet Dhillon and the Justice Department to go out and file suit against this scam of [California Gov. Gavin] Newsom on this redistricting plan that went against the constitution of California. I would get the Justice Department to file suit, go get a [temporary restraining order] immediately, and then hit this thing and drag it out for a year.”

“Number two, I would get DHS, the State Department and the Justice Department looking immediately at Mamdani, who I am convinced did not get citizenship in a fair and honest way, and get those two things rolling,” Bannon added.

He urged the president to listen to Mamdani’s post-election speech in which he directly challenged the president, who has threatened to withhold federal support for New York City if Andrew Cuomo did not win, and Trump responded with a cryptic post on Truth Social.

“There should be even more than alarm bells,” Bannon said. “There should be flashing red lights all over.”

Bannon expressed fear that Mamdani would inspire imitators in other cities and “squeeze” business interests with “Marxist” policies.

“You’re going to see a whole new group of Mamdanis in these major urban cities because they’re just flooded with immigrants, right?” Bannon said. “That’s where his vote came from, principally, and the progressive left, these kids have come up through the public school system. This is the flower of what the progressive left has delivered over the last 40 or 50 years. You saw it tonight and people, we’re going to have a fight on our hands.”

“His speech tonight could not have been nastier or more aggressive,” he added. “He mocked Cuomo. That is one of the first families of the Democratic Party, particularly in the state of New York.”

Bannon believes Republicans must embrace Trump and the MAGA movement even tighter to avoid even worse losses in next year’s midterms.

“You’re either with MAGA or you’re not,” he said. “The Republican Party has no following. It has a bunch of donors, and it has the Fox TV network, and it has Karl Rove and all these worn-out ideas you see in the House and the Senate. It’s just embarrassing. The Republican Party is just a husk. When Trump is engaged, when Trump’s on the ballot, when Trump’s team can get out there and get low-propensity voters — because that’s the difference now in modern politics — when they can do it, they win. When he doesn’t do it, they don’t.”

“You double and triple down with Trump,” Bannon added. “If you’re not prepared to do that, you’re going to get smoked, because you’re not going to see Trump voters come out, like in Virginia, just like in New Jersey.”

Our Analysis:

Bannon’s Alarm: A Desperate Cry Amidst Democratic Victories

Steve Bannon, a figure synonymous with the fringes of political strategy and right-wing extremism, appears to be in a state of near panic following a series of Democratic wins in key races. His reaction, as reported by Politico, is both a testament to the fear gripping certain political echelons and a showcase of the desperation seeping into the strategies of those aligned with Trump’s MAGA movement. Bannon’s call to action, involving legal maneuvers and insinuations of illegitimacy concerning election results and political opponents, is not just an attack on the electoral process but a clear signal of a strategy bankrupt of ethical considerations and democratic values.

A Scramble Against Democracy

Bannon’s first order of business involves a direct assault on the integrity of the electoral system itself. His proposal to challenge the Proposition 50 redistricting measure in California under the guise of it being a “scam” by Governor Gavin Newsom blatantly disregards the will of the voters. This maneuver, suggesting the use of the Justice Department as a political tool to undermine electoral outcomes, is a stark illustration of Bannon’s disregard for the foundational principles of democracy.

Moreover, Bannon’s attack on Zohran Mamdani’s victory in the New York mayor’s race crosses into the realm of xenophobia and baseless conspiracy. By questioning Mamdani’s citizenship and suggesting federal departments should investigate him, Bannon not only undermines the election’s integrity but also stokes unfounded fears about the role of immigrants in American politics. Such tactics are emblematic of a broader strategy to delegitimize and demonize opponents rather than engage in democratic competition.

Fearmongering and the MAGA Strategy

Bannon’s comments reflect a deeper anxiety within certain political circles about the growing influence of progressive policies and politicians. His labeling of Mamdani’s policies as “Marxist” and his fearmongering about immigrants and the progressive left underscore a defensive posture against the shifting political landscape. Bannon’s portrayal of a future populated by “a whole new group of Mamdanis” is not just an admission of the perceived effectiveness of progressive policies but also a rallying cry to resist these changes through any means necessary.

This resistance, according to Bannon, must come through an even tighter embrace of Donald Trump and the MAGA movement. His assertion that the Republican Party is a “husk” without Trump highlights the cult of personality that has enveloped the party. Bannon’s strategy, focused on mobilizing “low-propensity voters” through Trump’s engagement, is a tacit acknowledgment of the GOP’s reliance on Trump’s charisma rather than substantive policy proposals to win elections.

The Bottom Line: A Dangerous Game

Steve Bannon’s reaction to the Democratic wins is more than just a political strategy; it’s a dangerous game that threatens the very fabric of American democracy. By advocating for legal challenges against legitimate election results and casting aspersions on the citizenship of elected officials, Bannon and those who echo his sentiments are engaging in tactics that undermine public trust in the electoral process.

The insistence on doubling down with Trump and the MAGA movement, despite the ethical and democratic costs, reveals a myopic focus on power at the expense of governance. As the political landscape continues to evolve, the strategies outlined by Bannon not only risk further polarizing the nation but also eroding the principles upon which it was founded.

In the end, Bannon’s call to arms against the Democratic victories is not just a reflection of political desperation but a clarion call for those who value democracy to remain vigilant and engaged. The fight for the soul of America is far from over, and the tactics employed by Bannon and his ilk represent a clear and present danger to the democratic norms that have sustained the nation for centuries.
