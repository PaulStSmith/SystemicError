The internet reacted Monday after reports that President Donald Trump has set his eyes on Arizona’s largest county in hopes to — again — try and litigate the 2020 presidential election and increase the federal government’s influence over elections.

The Trump administration subpoenaed records from Maricopa County, Arizona, on Monday, Politico reported. In January, the Trump administration raided election offices in Fulton County, Georgia, seizing 2020 ballots.

The 79-year-old president has long maintained the conspiracy theory that the 2020 election was “rigged” against him despite no evidence of fraud. In 2023, he was indicted in Fulton County for his attempts at reversing his loss in Georgia.

Republican and Arizona Senate President Warren Peterson wrote on X that he agreed with Trump, confirming the state would follow the federal demands.

“President Trump is 100% correct,” Peterson wrote. “Late last week I received and complied with a federal grand jury subpoena for records relating to the Arizona State Senate’s 2020 audit of Maricopa County. The FBI has the records. Any other report is fake news.”

On social media, people responded to Trump’s latest attempt to revive his 2020 election claims.

“Then there were 2 (with Fulton County): Arizona Republican Just hands over keys to election. He posts that he ‘received and complied with a federal grand jury subpoena for records relating to the Arizona State Senate’s 2020 audit of Maricopa County. The FBI has the records.’ Uggh,” Harry Litman, professor and former Deputy Assistant Attorney General at the Department of Justice, wrote on Bluesky.

“Good Lord. You lost man, move on,” author and historian Cal Schoonover wrote on Bluesky.

“To be clear: In 2021, the AZ Senate hired a now-defunct company, Cyber Ninjas, to lead ‘recount/audit’ into Maricopa’s 2020 results -- the Cyber Ninjas found that, in fact, Joe Biden had won the county, per their hand count, by 360 more votes than originally believed,” Vaughn Hillyard, MS NOW senior White House reporter, wrote on X.

“The subpoena of the audit records comes at a time that the Trump administration is seeking to rehash the 2020 election. Biden’s narrow win in Arizona, which was reaffirmed by the GOP-led audit, was a major focus of Trump’s,” Eliza Collins, Wall Street Journal politics reporter, wrote on X.

Our Analysis:

Trump’s Unyielding Crusade: A Scathing Analysis

In an era where political theatre often descends into the absurd, the latest act from President Donald Trump and his administration descends further, plumbing the depths of desperation. The internet’s collective eyebrow raised this Monday, and for good reason, as Politico broke the news that Trump has targeted Maricopa County, Arizona, in his latest, quixotic bid to challenge the 2020 presidential election results. This move, involving a federal subpoena for records, screams of an attempt not only to litigate past grievances but also to disturbingly increase the federal government’s grip over the electoral process.

Let’s dissect the audacity and the desperation in equal measure.

The Facts: A Recap of Recent Events

In January, a raid on Fulton County, Georgia’s election offices saw the seizure of 2020 ballots, marking a bold move by the Trump administration. Fast forward, and the 79-year-old president, undeterred by an indictment in Fulton County for attempts to overturn his Georgia loss, sets his sights on Arizona’s largest county. The message is clear: Trump’s fixation on the 2020 election is as unshakeable as it is unfounded.

Arizona Senate President Warren Peterson’s response on X (formerly Twitter), where he parrots the Trump line, is equally troubling. “President Trump is 100% correct,” Peterson claims, aligning state compliance with federal demands as a matter of course. This eagerness to capitulate to a federal grand jury subpoena, as if it were a badge of honor rather than an alarming overreach, speaks volumes about the current political climate in Arizona — and perhaps, the Republican Party at large.

The Reality Check

Despite the dramatics, the facts remain stubbornly, inconveniently true. The 2020 election, specifically in Maricopa County, underwent a scrutiny that few elections have — culminating in an audit led by the now-defunct company Cyber Ninjas. The conclusion? Biden’s victory not only stood but was found to be by a margin 360 votes wider than originally certified. This isn’t just a nail in the coffin of the “rigged election” narrative; it’s the entire lumberyard.

Yet, here we are, witnessing a subpoena for records related to an audit that already confirmed Biden’s win. It’s a staggering testament to the lengths the Trump administration will go to rehash, relitigate, and rewrite the narrative of the 2020 election.

The Social Media Chorus

Voices on social media, from legal experts like Harry Litman to historians like Cal Schoonover, express a mix of exasperation and disbelief. Litman’s lament over Arizona’s compliance and Schoonover’s blunt “You lost man, move on,” encapsulate the broader societal fatigue with Trump’s relentless, groundless challenges to a settled election.

In Conclusion: The Desperation Dance

The subpoena of the audit records in Maricopa County isn’t just an isolated incident; it’s a symptom of a broader, more dangerous disease afflicting American politics. It’s the willful ignorance of facts, the disdain for democratic processes, and the desperate dance of a man and a movement unwilling to accept reality.

As Trump’s administration continues its attempts to undermine the bedrock of American democracy, one can’t help but wonder: where does it end? The 2020 election has been scrutinized, audited, and affirmed. The results are in. The people have spoken. It’s high time for Trump, and those who echo his baseless claims, to listen.
