Harry Styles and Olivia Rodrigo have become the latest mega-celebrities to join rocker Bruce Springsteen in standing up against President Donald Trump.

Just days after Springsteen called Trump "corrupt, incompetent and treasonous" from an English stage — and got hit by a veiled threat in return — the two singers joined 250 others in putting their name to opposition to the president’s policies.

In an advert posted in the New York Times, first reported by Rolling Stone, they stood up against Trump’s plan to crush Planned Parenthood by slashing its funding grants.

The ad reads, “I’m for Planned Parenthood because I’m for freedom… because I’m for healthcare… because I’m for you and me — not the government — deciding what care we need and where we can get it."

Other stars publicly endorsing it included Pedro Pascal, Addison Rae, Megan Thee Stallion, Billie Eilish, Meghan Trainor and Cyndi Lauper.

Trump has been furious over celebrity criticism of himself and his policies in recent days, launching a war of words on Springsteen and threatening investigations into Beyoncé and Oprah Winfrey for their endorsements of his election opponent Kamala Harris.

Last week, Springsteen told a crowd during his United Kingdom tour: “In my home America, the America I've written about that has been a beacon of hope and liberty for 250 years, it is currently in the hands of a corrupt, incompetent and treasonous administration.”

Trump clapped back: “This dried out ‘prune’ of a rocker (his skin is all atrophied!) ought to KEEP HIS MOUTH SHUT until he gets back into the Country, that’s just ‘standard fare.”

“Then we’ll all see how it goes for him!”

Springsteen’s stance has been backed by Pearl Jam’s Eddie Vedder and singer Neil Young.

Our Analysis:

The Celebrities Versus The President: A Closer Look

In a world where the line between entertainment and politics blurs more each day, the recent New York Times advert signed by Harry Styles, Olivia Rodrigo, and a cadre of other celebrities including Bruce Springsteen stands as a testament not just to celebrity activism, but to the ongoing spectacle that is the Trump presidency. This is not merely a clash of ideals; it is a vivid illustration of the cultural battleground that America has become.

The Power of Celebrity Voices in Political Activism

First, let's acknowledge the undeniable influence that celebrities hold in modern society. When figures like Harry Styles and Olivia Rodrigo speak, millions listen. Their decision to join Springsteen in opposition to President Donald Trump's policies, particularly the attack on Planned Parenthood, is significant. It's not just a matter of lending their voices; it's about amplifying the message to a global audience that might otherwise remain disengaged from political discourse.

However, the effectiveness of such celebrity endorsements can be both a blessing and a curse. On one hand, they can mobilize younger demographics and bring attention to critical issues. On the other, they risk reducing complex political debates to mere popularity contests, where the message is only as strong as the celebrity's current standing in the fickle court of public opinion.

Trump's Retaliation: A Distraction Tactic?

President Trump's response to Springsteen's criticism is a classic example of his combative style. Referring to Springsteen as a "dried out ‘prune’ of a rocker" is not just a petty insult; it's a strategic distraction. By focusing on the messenger rather than the message, Trump attempts to shift the narrative from his administration's policies to personal attacks against his critics.

This tactic is not new. Throughout his presidency, Trump has engaged in a war of words with celebrities, often threatening investigations or retaliating in some form. The mention of threats against Beyoncé and Oprah Winfrey for their endorsements of Kamala Harris further illustrates how Trump uses his platform to intimidate and silence opposition.

The Role of the Media in Amplifying Celebrity Activism

The media plays a crucial role in this saga. The publication of the advert in the New York Times and its coverage by Rolling Stone highlights the symbiotic relationship between celebrities and the press. This relationship ensures that the message reaches a wide audience, but it also raises questions about the media's priorities. Are these stories newsworthy because of their impact on policy and public opinion, or simply because they involve high-profile figures?

The Bigger Picture: Democracy Under the Spotlight

Beyond the immediate drama, this incident shines a light on the broader state of democracy in America. When celebrities feel compelled to speak out against the government, and the President retaliates with personal attacks, it signals a deeper division within the country. Springsteen's description of America as "a beacon of hope and liberty for 250 years" currently under the rule of a "corrupt, incompetent and treasonous administration" underscores the stakes involved.

In conclusion, while the involvement of Harry Styles, Olivia Rodrigo, and others in political activism against Trump's policies is laudable, it also serves as a reminder of the challenges facing American democracy. The real test is whether these voices can inspire not just outrage, but meaningful change. As the spectacle unfolds, one thing remains clear: the battle for America's soul is far from over, and the role of celebrities in this fight is more than just a footnote.
