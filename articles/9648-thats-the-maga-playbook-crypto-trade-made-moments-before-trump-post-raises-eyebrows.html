‘That’s the MAGA playbook’: Crypto trade made moments before Trump post raises eyebrows

An anonymous online cryptocurrency trader reportedly scored an $88 million profit after making a lofty bet that the value of Bitcoin would plummet, a bet that was made just before a major tariff announcement from President Donald Trump that saw that bet pay off in spades, leaving online critics suspicious of insider trading within the Trump administration.

As reported in the Economic Times, an anonymous trader opened a large short position on the value of Bitcoin. A short is an investment strategy in which a trader borrows shares and sells them immediately, agreeing to repurchase and return the same number of shares later. The trader profits if the stock’s price falls, buying the shares back at a lower price and pocketing the difference.

The trader opened the short position Friday, and, according to the financial data and analytics platform Unusual Whales, had opened their account “30 minutes before Trump’s announcement of 100% tariffs on China.”

“Trump has been using the presidency to directly manipulate markets to increase the wealth of himself and his friends,” wrote Melanie D’Arrigo, executive director for the Campaign for New York Health, in a social media post on X Saturday.

“This is corruption. There’s a reason Trump fired the independent ethics watchdog that oversaw corruption in his administration.”

Trump has faced scrutiny in the past for his dealings with cryptocurrency, particularly with the establishment of his own meme cryptocurrency called “$Trump,” a venture that reportedly enriched Trump to the tune of more than $20 billion.

“This happens every single time Trump has a tariff announcement,” wrote X user “Kadafi Purp,” who frequently shares content critical of the Trump administration.

“Insiders benefit, retail investors and consumers, and American businesses suffer. That’s the MAGA playbook. Privatize gains and socialize losses.”

Others, like political commentator Brian Allen, called the trade a potential case of “insider trading on steroids.”

“A brand new crypto account was opened yesterday morning; 30 minutes before Trump announced 100% China tariffs, it opened a massive, multi-million dollar leveraged Bitcoin short,” Allen wrote to his more than 205,000 followers on X. “You don’t ‘guess’ a move like that. You get tipped off.”

Our Analysis:

The Shadowy Dance of Cryptocurrency and Power: An Analysis

In the realm of financial maneuverings and political machinations, the recent revelation of an $88 million profit from a cryptocurrency bet, timed suspiciously close to a significant policy announcement by President Donald Trump, raises more than a few eyebrows. This isn’t just a story about a fortunate gamble; it’s a glaring spotlight on the potential for insider trading and corruption at the highest levels of power.

The Bet That Smelled of Foreknowledge

An anonymous cryptocurrency trader made a bet—a large short position on Bitcoin—that would only pay off if the value of Bitcoin plummeted. This bet was not based on market trends or expert analysis but, it seems, on privileged information. The timing is too precise, too perfect. Placed merely 30 minutes before a major tariff announcement from Trump, this bet reeks of insider knowledge. Are we truly to believe this is a coincidence? That a trader, as if touched by the divine hand of Fortuna, just happened to make a massive, lucrative bet moments before an announcement that would inevitably stir the markets?

Trump’s Tariffs and Market Manipulation

This incident cannot be viewed in isolation. It’s part of a disturbing pattern where Trump’s announcements have been shown to have direct and significant impacts on markets. Melanie D’Arrigo, executive director for the Campaign for New York Health, doesn’t mince words, accusing Trump of using the presidency to “directly manipulate markets to increase the wealth of himself and his friends.” This isn’t just a failure of ethics; it’s a systemic exploitation of power.

The Cryptocurrency Conundrum

The involvement of cryptocurrency in this scandal is particularly troubling. Crypto, by its nature, is difficult to trace and regulate. This makes it an ideal vehicle for questionable financial activities. The creation of Trump’s own meme cryptocurrency, “$Trump,” which allegedly enriched him by over $20 billion, is a testament to how cryptocurrency can be manipulated at the whim of the powerful.

The Pattern of Privatization and Loss Socialization

The critiques leveled by users like “Kadafi Purp” on X highlight a broader, systemic issue: the MAGA playbook of privatizing gains while socializing losses. This pattern, where insiders benefit at the expense of retail investors, consumers, and American businesses, is not new. However, the blatant nature of this incident, combined with the potential for unchecked cryptocurrency manipulation, sets a dangerous precedent.

Insider Trading on Steroids

Political commentator Brian Allen’s description of the trade as “insider trading on steroids” is apt. The creation of a brand new crypto account, followed by a massive, multi-million dollar leveraged Bitcoin short just 30 minutes before a significant policy announcement, cannot be brushed off as a lucky guess. This is a calculated move, one that likely required insider information.

Conclusion: A Call for Transparency and Accountability

This incident should serve as a wake-up call. The potential for market manipulation, especially using the relatively unregulated realm of cryptocurrency, poses a significant threat to the integrity of financial markets and the principle of fair play. It’s a clear reminder of the urgent need for transparency and accountability in both the financial and political spheres. The question now is, will this call be heeded, or will it be lost in the cacophony of the next news cycle?

In a system where the powerful can seemingly manipulate the market with impunity, it’s the average citizen, investor, and consumer who pays the price. It’s time to demand more from our leaders and the systems that govern our markets. The integrity of our financial and political institutions depends on it.
