President Donald Trump heaped praise onto Washington, D.C. Mayor Muriel Bowser Saturday in a lengthy social media post, while at the same time, issuing her a “don’t go woke” warning and upping threats to other Democrat-led cities.

“Wow! Mayor Muriel Bowser of D.C. has become very popular because she worked with me and my great people in bringing CRIME down to virtually NOTHING in D.C.,” Trump wrote Monday in an online post on his social media platform Truth Social.

“Her statements and actions were positive, instead of others like [Illinois Gov. JB] Pritzker, [Maryland Gov.] Wes Moore, [California Gov. Gavin] ‘Newscum,’ and the 5% approval rated Mayor of Chicago, who spend all of their time trying to justify violent Crime, instead of working with us to completely ELIMINATE it, which we have done in Washington, D.C.”

Trump went on to declare that the nation’s capital had become a “crime-free zone” following the federal takeover of the city that has seen thousands of federal officers and National Guard members patrolling the city’s streets. The president has amped up threats to do the same in other Democrat-led cities, particularly Chicago, Illinois.

Regarding D.C., Trump claimed Bowser’s approval has “gone up” since the federal takeover, despite no recent polling having been conducted on her favorability, and credited her supposed polling boost to her cooperation with the White House. Overall approval for the D.C. federal takeover has been abysmal, with just 38% of Americans approving of the operation.

Trump went on, however, to issue Bowser a backhanded congratulations that came with a warning.

“Congratulations to Mayor Muriel Bowser, but don’t go Woke on us,” Trump wrote. “D.C. is a GIANT VICTORY that never has to end!”

Our Analysis:

Trump's Praise and Warning: A Closer Look at the D.C. "Crime-Free Zone"

In a bewildering display of self-congratulation and veiled threats, President Donald Trump has taken to Truth Social to lavish praise on Washington, D.C. Mayor Muriel Bowser, while simultaneously cautioning her against adopting "woke" policies. Trump's post, rich in hyperbole and scant on evidence, proclaims D.C. a "crime-free zone" following his administration's federal takeover—a claim that not only stretches the bounds of credibility but also distorts the complex reality of crime and governance in the capital.

Trump's Dubious Claims

First, let's dissect Trump's assertion that Mayor Bowser's popularity has surged "because she worked with me and my great people in bringing CRIME down to virtually NOTHING in D.C." This statement is problematic for several reasons:

  1. No Recent Polling Data: Trump's claim about Bowser's increased approval ratings lacks any substantiation, as he himself admits no recent polls have been conducted. This raises the question: where is he getting his information?

  2. "Crime-Free Zone" Hyperbole: Declaring D.C. a "crime-free zone" is not only factually inaccurate but also minimizes the ongoing challenges the city faces. Crime rates fluctuate for a myriad of reasons, many of which are outside the control of either the Mayor or the President.

  3. Federal Takeover Controversy: The federal takeover, characterized by an influx of thousands of federal officers and National Guard members, is far from universally acclaimed. With only 38% of Americans approving of the operation, it's a leap to describe it as a "GIANT VICTORY."

A Veiled Threat Wrapped in Praise

Trump's "don't go woke" warning to Bowser is a thinly veiled threat, emblematic of his broader campaign against Democrat-led cities. By singling out Bowser amidst backhanded compliments, Trump is not so subtly suggesting that her continued cooperation (or lack thereof) with his administration will determine her political and the city's operational fate. This paternalistic stance undermines the autonomy and complexities of urban governance, reducing it to a simple binary of compliance with or resistance to Trump's policies.

The Real Issue at Hand

The real story here isn't about Trump's purported success in eradicating crime in D.C., nor is it about Mayor Bowser's political standing. It's about the dangerous precedent set by a President who believes that heavy-handed federal intervention is the panacea for urban crime, and who uses his platform to issue warnings to elected officials who dare to think differently.

Conclusion

In his social media post, Trump has once again demonstrated his penchant for distortion, his disregard for the complexities of urban crime management, and his willingness to use his influence to coerce and intimidate. While the façade of a "crime-free" D.C. might serve his narrative, the reality on the ground tells a different story—one of a city grappling with the challenges of governance, crime, and federal overreach. Mayor Bowser, like all urban leaders, deserves the space to govern without the specter of presidential warnings for ideological non-compliance.
