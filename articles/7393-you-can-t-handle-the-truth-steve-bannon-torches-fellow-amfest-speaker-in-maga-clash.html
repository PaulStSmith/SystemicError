'You can't handle the truth!' Steve Bannon torches fellow AmFest speaker in MAGA clash

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosDecember 23, 20254 min read

Steve Bannon (Reuters)

Longtime Trump ally Steve Bannon ignited fresh infighting inside MAGA world on Friday after unloading on fellow right-wing influencer Ben Shapiro during remarks at AmericaFest 2025, Turning Point USA’s annual conference.

Speaking from the main stage in Phoenix, Bannon mocked Shapiro’s speech from the night before and accused him of being fundamentally opposed to President Donald Trump and the MAGA movement.

“Now, Benji Shapiro sat up here last night, and he was all, you know, it’s all about the truth,” Bannon said. “Ben, I’ve known you a long time, brother. You can’t handle the truth!”

While some in the crowd appeared to be surprised, most erupted in cheers and applause. He went on to declare: “Let’s face it, Ben Shapiro is the farthest thing from MAGA. Let’s be blunt, he is a hardcore ‘Never Trumper.”’

Bannon then accused Shapiro of attempting to undermine Trump during the 2016 campaign, claiming Shapiro tried to shift support to Sen. Ted Cruz (R-TX) and “barely supported Donald Trump” in the general election. He also claimed Shapiro “tried to upend Breitbart,” the conservative publication, and alleged that the MAGA influencer “hated Donald Trump.”

But Bannon wasn’t done just yet. He added that once Trump left the Oval Office after losing the 2020 election, Shapiro quickly backed Florida Gov. Ron DeSantis.

“Those were the darkest days we had,” Bannon told the crowd, referring to the post-2020 election period. “He’s consistently been against Trump.”

Shapiro, for his part, called Bannon during his speech on Thursday a “PR flake for Jeffrey Epstein,” and labeled him and fellow conservatives, including Tucker Carlson, Candace Owens and Megyn Kelly as “frauds and grifters,” Salon reported

Our Analysis:

Bannon vs. Shapiro: A Clash of Titans in MAGA World

In the latest spectacle to grace the MAGA stage, Steve Bannon, a longtime ally of Donald Trump, has publicly thrown down the gauntlet against Ben Shapiro, a fellow right-wing influencer. This confrontation unfolded at AmericaFest 2025, hosted by Turning Point USA, and has sent shockwaves through the conservative ecosystem. Let’s dissect the fiery exchange and its implications with the precision of a surgeon and the ruthlessness of a street fighter.

Bannon’s Broadside

Steve Bannon, wielding his trademark blend of bravado and venom, did not mince words. He accused Ben Shapiro of being fundamentally opposed to Donald Trump and the MAGA movement, an accusation that, if true, positions Shapiro as an ideological outlier in an arena that prides itself on unwavering loyalty to Trump. Bannon’s mockery of Shapiro’s allegiance to “the truth” was not just a personal jab but a philosophical broadside against what he perceives as Shapiro’s betrayal of the MAGA cause.

Bannon’s assertion that Shapiro is a “hardcore ‘Never Trumper’” and his recounting of Shapiro’s alleged efforts to derail Trump’s campaign in favor of Ted Cruz paints a picture of ideological warfare within the conservative ranks. Bannon’s narrative is clear: Shapiro is not just a dissenter; he is a traitor to the cause.

Shapiro’s Counterattack

Shapiro, for his part, did not come to the battleground unarmed. His labeling of Bannon as a “PR flake for Jeffrey Epstein” and his broad categorization of prominent conservatives, including Tucker Carlson and Candace Owens, as “frauds and grifters,” is a scorching retort. This is not just a squabble over policy or personality; this is an existential battle over the soul and future direction of the MAGA movement.

The Underlying Tensions

This clash underscores a deeper, more systemic issue within the MAGA world and the broader conservative movement. It’s not merely a spat between two high-profile figures; it’s symptomatic of the ideological fragmentation and infighting that threatens to splinter the movement. Bannon and Shapiro, both influential voices with substantial followings, represent divergent paths: one adhering strictly to Trumpism, the other potentially seeking a broader conservative renaissance that might not always align with Trump’s vision or tactics.

The Fallout

The cheers and applause that greeted Bannon’s tirade against Shapiro signify a worrying trend: the glorification of conflict over consensus. This moment of division could have lasting repercussions, further polarizing an already divided base and potentially alienating moderate conservatives who are uncomfortable with the escalating rhetoric and internal conflicts.

Conclusion

In the grand theater of conservative politics, Bannon vs. Shapiro is more than a mere sideshow; it’s a symptom of a movement at a crossroads. The MAGA world finds itself wrestling with its identity, torn between allegiance to a singular figure and the broader principles of conservatism. As the dust settles on this latest skirmish, one thing is clear: the battle for the soul of MAGA is far from over, and its outcome will shape the future of the conservative movement in America.

The spectacle of Bannon mocking Shapiro on such a public stage is indicative of the deep fissures within the movement — a movement that must choose between puritanical loyalty to Trump and a more nuanced approach to conservative ideology. This public airing of internal grievances may prove cathartic for some but could spell disaster for the cohesion and future political viability of the MAGA movement.
