Former Rep. Marjorie Taylor Greene (R-GA) suggested Tuesday that Laura Loomer was President Donald Trump’s “side piece” because she had not been named an official adviser or been given a permanent White House press pass.

Earlier this week, Greene lashed out after Loomer said she expected “Marjorie Traitor Greene” to attack the president throughout the midterms.

“I have to admit I was wrong when I warned Trump and his senior staff to stay away from Laura Loomer,” Greene wrote. “Her divisiveness, loyalty tests temper tantrums, Israel First agenda, and her manufactured bulls--- she calls loomered have all been great for the party and the President!”

“PLEASE make her Chair of the RNC! Wait that is not enough. Make her White House Chief of Staff!! How dare President Trump not reward his most loyal worshipper! Literally the self appointed High Priestess of MAGA or MIGA or whatever this thing has mutated into,” she continued. “I can’t believe they haven’t even given Laura the most simple and basic of all things, White House press credentials. This is an outrage!!”

On Wednesday, Greene noted that Loomer still did not seem to have a White House press pass.

“Has Laura Loomer received her hard pass WH press credentials yet?” she asked. “Late night phone calls are for side pieces.”

“Laura deserves to be treated so much better,” Greene added. “She loves Trump the most! It’s not fair! There are Muslim foreign press with WH hard passes, but they keep Laura out!”

Our Analysis:

The Theater of the Absurd: Greene, Loomer, and the MAGA Melodrama

In the latest installment of what can only be described as political theater at its most bizarre, former Rep. Marjorie Taylor Greene (R-GA) has outdone herself with comments that blur the line between petty high school drama and serious political discourse—assuming, of course, one can argue such a line ever existed within her rhetoric. Greene’s suggestion that Laura Loomer was President Donald Trump’s “side piece” due to the absence of an official advisory role or a White House press pass is not just a new low in political commentary; it’s a dive into an abyss of absurdity.

Greene’s Grievances: A Symphony of Sarcasm

Greene’s sarcastic plea for Loomer to be named Chair of the RNC or White House Chief of Staff is dripping with the kind of venom that has become her hallmark. Her tirade against Loomer’s “divisiveness, loyalty tests, temper tantrums, Israel First agenda, and her manufactured bulls--- she calls loomered” is rich, coming from a figure who has herself been a lightning rod for controversy and division within the GOP. The irony of Greene’s mocking endorsement of Loomer as “the self-appointed High Priestess of MAGA” would be humorous if it weren’t so painfully indicative of the fractures within the party.

The Outrage Over Press Credentials

Greene’s lamentation over Loomer’s lack of White House press credentials ventures into the realm of the absurd. Her comparison of late-night phone calls to the status of “side pieces” and the assertion that Loomer “loves Trump the most” are statements one might expect from a rejected suitor in a soap opera, not a sitting lawmaker. The comment about “Muslim foreign press” being granted access over Loomer not only smacks of Islamophobia but also highlights a disturbing trend of valuing loyalty over journalistic integrity in the distribution of press credentials.

The Real Outrage

The real outrage here should not be over who does or doesn’t have a hard pass to the White House press room. It should be directed at the fact that figures like Greene and Loomer continue to command attention and influence within American politics, turning serious political discourse into a circus. Their brand of politics—rooted in division, conspiracy theories, and outright bigotry—serves only to distract from the pressing issues facing the United States and the world.

In Conclusion: The Mutated MAGA

Greene’s concluding jab at the MAGA movement, suggesting it has mutated into something unrecognizable, is perhaps the only point of agreement to be found in her rant. Indeed, the movement, under the influence of figures like Greene and Loomer, has morphed into a caricature of its former self, prioritizing loyalty and spectacle over policy and progress. The true outrage is the degradation of political discourse to the point where the lines between satire and reality are indistinguishably blurred.

As the MAGA saga continues to unfold with Greene and Loomer as key players, one can’t help but wonder: when will the curtain finally fall on this theater of the absurd? And at what cost to the American political landscape?
