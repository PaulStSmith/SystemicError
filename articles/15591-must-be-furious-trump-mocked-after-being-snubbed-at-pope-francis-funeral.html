'Must be furious': Trump mocked after being snubbed at Pope Francis’ funeral

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosApril 27, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Our Analysis:The Dichotomy of Respect: Analyzing the Funeral of Pope Francis

U.S President Donald Trump, first lady Melania Trump, Poland’s President Andrzej Duda and Portugal’s President Marcelo Rebelo de Sousa attend the funeral Mass of Pope Francis, at the Vatican, April 26, 2025. REUTERS/Kai Pfaffenbach

On Saturday morning the funeral for Pope Francis — who died on April 21 at the age of 88 — was held in Vatican City in Italy. Top officials from around the world attended, including U.S. President DonaldTrump and Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky.

According to reports, Zelensky received a much warmer welcome than Trump. And X is being inundated with reactions.

The conservative Republicans Against Trump, posting CNN's coverage of the funeral, tweeted, " The crowd at the Pope’s funeral erupted in applause when President Zelensky arrived. Donald Trump must be furious."

In a separate tweet, Republicans Against Trump noted, "The strict dress code for the Pope’s funeral required men to wear dark suits and long black ties. Naturally, Trump was the only one who showed up in a blue suit and tie."

X user Anna Komsa observed, "While Zelensky was greeted with a spontaneous round of applause, Trump, in his blue suit at Pope Francis's funeral, received nothing."

Another X user, Barry Kenny, commented, "He is so self-absorbed he won’t have registered it, his toadies so cowardly they won’t say it, but Trump roundly ignored by Pope Francis’ funeral crowd, while @ZelenskyyUa spontaneously applauded - the people know who the true leader of the free world is. #popefrancisfuneral."

Political strategist Chris D. Jackson posted, "Trump may see himself as the main event, but at Pope Francis’s funeral, he’s reportedly stuck in the third row. Not surprising — it's no secret the Pope and his people didn’t care for him. If Biden were still president, he'd be up front — where real leaders sit."

X user Mike Thomas tweeted, "Watching the spectacle of the funeral of Pope Francis is special. Catholic or not you have to respect his humility and core humanity. Why is Trump in a blue suit and blue tie. At least Melania is correctly attired with a veil. What a great ovation for Zelensky (Trump none!)

The Daily Telegraph, meanwhile,tweeted a photo of Trump and Zelenskymeeting in Vatican City before the funeral.

Our Analysis:

The Dichotomy of Respect: Analyzing the Funeral of Pope Francis

The funeral of Pope Francis, a man celebrated for his humility and humanity, became an unexpected stage for the world to witness the stark contrast in global leadership respectability. As leaders from across the globe converged on Vatican City to pay their respects, two figures stood out, not just for their political stature but for the reactions they elicited from those in attendance: U.S. President Donald Trump and Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky. The palpable difference in their receptions speaks volumes, transcending mere personal popularity to underscore deeper global political currents.

The Warm Welcome Versus The Cold Shoulder

Reports indicate that Zelensky received a much warmer welcome than Trump. This isn't just a trivial popularity contest; it's a revealing glimpse into the world's pulse. The spontaneous applause for Zelensky, juxtaposed against the tepid or non-existent reception for Trump, isn't merely about the men themselves but what they are perceived to represent. Zelensky, lauded for his courage and leadership in the face of adversity, evidently resonates with the global community in ways Trump simply does not.

A Fashion Faux Pas or a Symbolic Rebellion?

Trump's choice of attire — a blue suit and tie amidst a sea of dark suits and long black ties as mandated by the funeral's strict dress code — is emblematic of his tenure: a deliberate defiance of established norms. While some might argue this is a minor point, it's a visible manifestation of Trump's broader approach to diplomacy and decorum. It's not just about fashion; it's about a fundamental disrespect for the occasion and, by extension, the man being mourned.

The Significance of Seating Arrangements

The reported relegation of Trump to the third row is another detail that, while seemingly minor, tells a larger story. In the world of diplomacy, such nuances are heavy with meaning. The claim that if Biden were president, he'd be seated at the front, serves as a stark reminder of the erosion of U.S. prestige under Trump's leadership. This isn't just about physical proximity to the altar but about the global standing and the respect accorded to leadership.

The Reaction of the Masses: A Barometer for Leadership

The crowd's reaction at the Pope’s funeral — the applause for Zelensky versus the indifference towards Trump — is a potent indicator of the global community's values. It's not just about the individuals but what they symbolize: humility, respect for international norms, and genuine leadership in times of crisis. The crowd's response is a rebuke of the narcissism and bravado that have come to characterize Trump's persona both at home and abroad.

Conclusion: A Tale of Two Leaders

In the solemnity of Pope Francis's funeral, a moment meant to transcend political divisions, we've been given a clear-eyed look at the current state of global leadership. The contrast between Zelensky and Trump at this event isn't merely a footnote in the annals of history; it's a stark reminder of the qualities the world respects and responds to in its leaders. As we reflect on Pope Francis's legacy of humility and humanity, let's also consider the lesson presented by the world's reaction to these two men: in the end, genuine respect cannot be demanded or assumed; it must be earned.
