Donald Trump’s overall approval rating is continuing to see a rapid downward decline, according to MSNBC’s Rachel Maddow, who revealed that even the president’s signature issue is beginning to see cracks of disapproval.

Maddow on Thursday walked viewers through a new CNBC/Hart Research poll, which placed Trump underwater by seven points, with 44% of those surveyed approving of his overall performance as president – versus 51% who disapproved.

But Maddow said Trump’s poll numbers grew “much worse” when respondents were asked their thoughts on the MAGA leader’s handling of specific issues.

While Trump’s approval on issues including tariffs, foreign policy and inflation were all underwater by a majority of those surveyed, Maddow flagged the survey responses on immigration as a crucial sign for the president.

“Throughout Donald Trump's entire life as a political figure, the one issue where he is almost always enjoyed, to my mind inexplicable, but nevertheless, net positive support from the American public is on the issue of immigration, but even on that – not anymore!” she said. “Look at this, a Quinnipiac poll from this month now shows that Americans disapprove of Trump’s handling of his signature issue of immigration by a five-point margin.”

The MSNBC host added in her searing takedown on Thursday that immigration issues were “supposed to be his best thing, right?”

“He’s underwater on all of it,” Maddow said. “Trump has been back in office for less than 100 days. The American people are quite disapproving of his agenda. The American people are protesting what he's doing more and more, and he's losing every single day in court on almost everything that gets him into court.”

Our Analysis:

The Crumbling Tower of Trump's Approval Ratings

The latest analysis by MSNBC's Rachel Maddow paints a stark picture of a presidency in decline, with Donald Trump's approval ratings plummeting across the board. The narrative is not just a simple downturn in popularity; it's a comprehensive failure of policy, perception, and political strategy. Let's dissect the revelations from the CNBC/Hart Research poll and what this means for Trump's tumultuous tenure.

First and foremost, Trump's overall approval rating sinking underwater by seven points is an alarming indicator for any incumbent. With 44% approving versus 51% disapproving, the numbers speak volumes about a presidency struggling to maintain its footing amidst a sea of discontent. But the devil, as they say, is in the details.

Signature Issue? More Like Signature Crisis

Trump's handling of immigration, his so-called "signature issue," is perhaps the most telling sign of his faltering grasp on the American psyche. Once a bastion of net positive support, Trump's immigration policies are now viewed unfavorably by a margin of five points according to a Quinnipiac poll. Maddow's emphasis on this point is crucial; it highlights a significant shift in public opinion against Trump's hardline stance on immigration. This isn't just a crack; it's a veritable chasm opening up beneath the feet of his administration.

A Sinking Ship on All Fronts

It’s not just immigration. The CNBC/Hart Research poll reveals a president "underwater on all of it" - be it tariffs, foreign policy, or inflation. These issues are fundamental to the well-being and security of the American people, and the widespread disapproval is a damning indictment of Trump's inability to steer the country in a direction that satisfies the electorate. Maddow's analysis underscores a presidency in peril, grappling with widespread disapproval on a multitude of critical fronts.

The Court of Public Opinion and the Actual Courts

Maddow's remarks on Trump losing "every single day in court on almost everything that gets him into court" is a scathing indictment of a presidency often mired in legal challenges. This isn't just about policy unpopularity; it's about the legal untenability of many of Trump's actions. The courts have become a battleground where Trump's agenda faces rigorous scrutiny and, more often than not, outright rejection.

Maddow's "Searing Takedown"

Maddow's analysis, described as a "searing takedown," isn't merely editorializing; it's a reflection of the palpable frustration and opposition Trump faces. From the streets filled with protestors to the courtrooms bustling with legal challenges, the narrative is clear: Trump's presidency is under siege, both in the court of public opinion and in the actual judicial system.

Conclusion: A Presidency on Precarious Ground

The CNBC/Hart Research poll and Maddow's commentary offer more than just numbers and opinions; they provide a glimpse into a presidency struggling under the weight of its own controversy and unpopularity. Trump's approval ratings, particularly on immigration, reveal a leader disconnected from the national sentiment. As the American people voice their disapproval in polls and protests alike, one thing becomes clear: Trump's tenure is not just challenged; it's in crisis.

In the end, Maddow's analysis isn't about partisanship; it's about holding power to account. The plummeting approval ratings and widespread disapproval of Trump's policies are a clarion call for introspection and change at the highest levels of American governance. Whether this call will be heeded remains to be seen, but the message is unmistakable: the Trump administration is facing a systemic error that requires immediate correction.
