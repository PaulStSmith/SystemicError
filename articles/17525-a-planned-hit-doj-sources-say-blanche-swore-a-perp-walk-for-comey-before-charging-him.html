Manipulation in the Department of Justice: The Comey Spectacle

Auditioning for Power: The Blanche Doctrine

The story of Todd Blanche, the interim Attorney General and personal attorney to Donald Trump, scripting a public indictment spectacle against former FBI Director James Comey is not just a tale of personal vendettas; it’s an illuminating expose of how law enforcement is manipulated at the highest levels for political performance. Carol D. Leonnig’s reporting uncovers that Blanche, dissatisfied with prior leniency, vowed not to repeat the same “mistake”, positioning the indictment as a theatrical display to secure his role by pleasing Trump.

The Comey Perp Walk: A Political Tool

Blanche’s strategy to employ what he termed a perp walk for Comey reveals the weaponization of legal mechanisms for spectacle and intimidation. This act wasn’t just about legal proceedings but about sending a message—a demonstration of loyalty to Trump and a warning to others who might cross his path. The use of a perp walk, traditionally a moment to parade alleged criminals before the media, underscores its misuse as a tool for political retribution rather than justice.

Institutional Decay and the Scapegoat Strategy

Leonnig’s insights into the DOJ under Blanche’s interim leadership highlight a troubling pivot towards authoritarian practices within American institutions supposed to uphold justice and law independently of political whims. The targeting of Comey based on flimsy accusations related to an Instagram post further illustrates the departure from legal norms and the scapegoating of individuals who are seen as political threats. This tactic diverts public attention from substantive issues, focusing it on manufactured crises.

Patterns of Political Loyalty over Legal Integrity

The narrative of Blanche’s actions fits a broader pattern of behavior within Trump’s circle: the prioritization of loyalty over competence, and the use of power to protect and project personal and political interests. Previous Attorney General Pam Bondi’s failures, as noted by Leonnig, stemmed not from a lack of trying but from a failure to force unjust prosecutions when resisted by career prosecutors. In contrast, Blanche’s readiness to bypass legal standards to appease Trump signals an alarming readiness to subvert justice for appeasement.

Systemic Insights: Protecting Power at All Costs

The implications of this story extend beyond individual actors like Blanche and Comey, pointing to a systemic rot within parts of the American justice system, where the lines between law enforcement and political theatre are increasingly blurred. This scenario is a stark reminder of the dangers posed when the mechanisms of justice are hijacked to serve the fleeting interests of political power, rather than the enduring principles of law and order.

In conclusion, the Comey indictment orchestrated by Blanche is not just about the legal merits of the case but is emblematic of a deeper, more pernicious movement towards politicizing justice. It starkly illustrates how high-ranking officials can manipulate legal frameworks to serve personal and political agendas, undermining democratic institutions and eroding public trust in the process. As this pattern unfolds, it becomes crucial to recognize and resist such abuses of power, reinforcing the independence of judicial processes from political theatrics.
