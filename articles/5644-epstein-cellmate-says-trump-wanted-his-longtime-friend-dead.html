Epstein cellmate says Trump wanted his longtime friend dead

Convicted sex offender Jeffrey Epstein was left alone and unprotected in his cell “on purpose” in hopes that he wouldn’t survive until his trial date, according to his former cellmate.

The Daily Beast reported Friday that Nicholas Tartaglione — a former police officer-turned quadruple murderer — claimed in a pardon/commutation document filed last year that Epstein was intentionally put in harm’s way. In the 21-page document the Beast obtained, Tartaglione insisted Trump wanted his former friend “dead.”

Part of that alleged plan may have included selecting Tartaglione to be Epstein’s cellmate at New York City’s Metropolitan Corrections Center (MCC). The Beast reported that Tartaglione has “a self-confessed hatred of child sex offenders.” In a September 2019 letter to the New York Daily News, Tartaglione argued it was suspicious for MCC to put him in the same cell as Epstein.

“The staff here at MCC had hundreds of inmates to choose from yet I was their first choice,” he wrote. “... It is no coincidence that prior to trial I was transferred to the Metropolitan Correctional Center in Manhattan and deliberately placed in the same cell as Jeffrey Epstein.”

Tartaglione’s recently unearthed petition comes on the heels of Miami Herald reporter Julie K. Brown — who initially broke the news that led to Epstein’s first indictment — finding that Tartaglione had once extorted Epstein and threatened his life. According to Brown, Epstein was found on the floor of his cell “with a piece of fabric around his neck,” and the convicted sex trafficker initially claimed that Tartaglione attempted to kill him. But Tartaglione wrote in his 2019 letter that both he and Epstein were targeted for assassination, and claimed that several attempts were made on his own life while at MCC.

“I clearly was not protected on purpose, nor was Epstein,” he wrote. “I truly believe that the government wanted both Epstein and me dead.”

Epstein later changed his story to say that he couldn’t remember the details of the attack. Eventually, the incident was described as an act of “self-mutilation.” Brown reported that details of the attack were later “expunged.” Epstein’s brother, Mark, continues to insist that his brother didn’t take his own life, and that his death was the result of foul play.

Our Analysis:

The Sinister Shadows of the Epstein Saga: A Closer Look

The recent revelations brought to light by The Daily Beast surrounding convicted sex offender Jeffrey Epstein’s time in New York City’s Metropolitan Corrections Center (MCC) before his death add yet another layer of darkness to an already murky narrative. According to Nicholas Tartaglione, Epstein’s former cellmate and a convicted quadruple murderer, there was a deliberate attempt to leave Epstein “alone and unprotected” in hopes that he wouldn’t survive until his trial. This claim, if true, not only points to a gross miscarriage of justice but also hints at a more sinister, orchestrated effort to silence Epstein permanently.

A Calculated Decision?

The decision to place Tartaglione, who has a “self-confessed hatred of child sex offenders,” in the same cell as Epstein raises eyebrows to say the least. The convenience of such a pairing cannot be overlooked, especially when considering the hundreds of other inmates that could have been chosen. Tartaglione’s assertion that this was no mere coincidence but a calculated decision by MCC staff suggests a level of premeditation that is as troubling as it is damning.

Trump Wanted Epstein “Dead”?

Tartaglione’s claim that former President Donald Trump wanted Epstein “dead” introduces a political dimension to the saga that cannot be ignored. While the veracity of such a claim is subject to proof, the mere suggestion adds a layer of conspiracy to an already convoluted story. This claim demands rigorous investigation, as it implicates not just the MCC, but potentially extends to the highest levels of political power.

A Pattern of Negligence or a Plot?

The narrative of negligence at the MCC, especially in the context of Epstein’s death, has been well-documented. However, Tartaglione’s allegations suggest something far more nefarious than mere negligence. The idea that both he and Epstein were deliberately left unprotected, coupled with the alleged attempts on both their lives, paints a picture of an institution not just failing in its duty but actively participating in a plot to ensure Epstein’s silence.

The Mysterious “Self-Mutilation”

The incident where Epstein was found with a “piece of fabric around his neck,” initially claimed to be an attack by Tartaglione, later described as “self-mutilation,” and finally, the details of which were “expunged,” is emblematic of the opacity and inconsistency that has plagued the entire Epstein saga. This incident, shrouded in mystery and contradiction, underscores the lack of transparency and accountability that has frustrated efforts to uncover the truth.

The Unending Quest for Truth

Mark Epstein’s insistence that his brother did not take his own life but was the victim of foul play continues to echo the sentiments of many who have followed this case. The constant emergence of new details, each more disturbing than the last, underscores the complex web of power, privilege, and corruption that Jeffrey Epstein navigated in life and, seemingly, in death.

In conclusion, the allegations made by Nicholas Tartaglione shed new light on the circumstances surrounding Jeffrey Epstein’s death, suggesting a level of orchestration and intent that goes beyond mere negligence. This case, emblematic of systemic failures at multiple levels, demands not just our attention but our outrage. The quest for truth in the Epstein saga is not just about bringing those responsible to justice but about challenging the structures of power that allowed such egregious breaches of justice to occur in the first place.
