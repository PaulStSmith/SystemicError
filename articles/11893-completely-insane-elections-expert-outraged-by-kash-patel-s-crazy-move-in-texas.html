A ploy to allow the FBI to catch Texas Democrats and return them to Texas for the special legislative session had one lawyer up in arms on Wednesday.

Texas Democrats fled the state last week to break quorum in the state legislature. The move prevents Texas Republicans from voting to pass a new election map that would take away four Democrat-held seats in the state.

President Donald Trump and Attorney General Pam Bondi are reportedly pushing the new map in Texas and other Republican-held states as a way to maintain the GOP majority following the 2026 midterm elections.

Texas Gov. Greg Abbott and Attorney General Ken Paxton have threatened to arrest any Democrats and force them to return to the state capitol.

So far, the efforts have not worked. But MAGA Sen. John Cornyn (R-TX), who will face Paxton in the 2026 primary elections, waded into the fight by requesting that the FBI help locate the Texas Democrats, even though it's been widely reported that they are in Illinois.

Marc Elias, a constitutional attorney, joined progressive YouTuber Brian Tyler Cohen on his show "No Lies" to discuss the events, which he described as a "charade."

"The fact that Kash Patel is involved in this is [illustrative] of how absolutely bats--- crazy the Republican Party has become; how absolutely lazy the legacy media has become; and how completely insane Texas Republicans are," Elias said.

Elias pointed out that the FBI has no jurisdiction to conduct such an operation because the Texas Democrats have not committed a federal crime. Leaving the state in protest is not a state crime in Texas either, Elias added.

He added that the FBI's involvement appears to be a way to boost Cornyn's chances of defeating Paxton in the midterms.

"John Cornyn thinks that the way he can win his Senate primary against Ken Paxton is to say, 'Well, Ken Paxton just tried to strip these people of their seats by going to the courts. I was tougher than that. I was for kidnapping,'" Elias said.

Our Analysis:

The Texas Quorum Break: A Deep Dive into Desperation

The recent events unfolding in Texas read like a twisted political thriller, yet they're not the product of a screenwriter's imagination but the stark reality of American politics in 2023. The ploy by MAGA Sen. John Cornyn (R-TX) to involve the FBI in apprehending Texas Democrats—who have fled the state in a strategic move to block a controversial election map—reeks of desperation and a blatant disregard for democratic principles and legal boundaries.

The Republican Scheme

At the heart of this debacle is a new election map, aggressively pushed by President Donald Trump and Attorney General Pam Bondi, aiming to dismantle four Democrat-held seats in Texas. This move is not just a strategy but a clear manifestation of the GOP's fear of losing control in the upcoming 2026 midterm elections. Texas Gov. Greg Abbott and Attorney General Ken Paxton's threats to arrest Democrats are not only undemocratic but highlight a tyrannical approach to governance, where power is maintained at any cost.

The FBI's Involvement: A Legal and Ethical Quagmire

The request for FBI involvement by Sen. John Cornyn is not just misguided but legally baseless. Constitutional attorney Marc Elias's comments shed light on the absurdity and danger of this request. The FBI has no jurisdiction in this matter, as fleeing the state to break quorum is neither a federal nor a state crime in Texas. This move, as Elias suggests, is nothing but a political stunt to bolster Cornyn's image against Paxton in the upcoming primary elections. It's a dangerous precedent when law enforcement is used as a pawn in political gamesmanship.

The Bigger Picture: The GOP's Descent into Madness

Elias's conversation with progressive YouTuber Brian Tyler Cohen, where he describes the situation as a "charade," underscores a broader issue: the GOP's descent into extremism. The involvement of figures like Kash Patel only amplifies this narrative. It's a clear indication of how far the Republican Party is willing to veer from democratic norms and legal standards in pursuit of power.

The Media's Role: A Lackluster Performance

Elias also points out the "lazy" response of legacy media to this unfolding drama. It's a critical observation, highlighting how the media's failure to adequately report and analyze these events contributes to a misinformed public. In an era where misinformation can spread like wildfire, the media's role in providing clear, factual reporting is more important than ever.

Conclusion: A Call to Sanity

The events in Texas should alarm anyone who values democracy and the rule of law. The use of political power to suppress opposition, the manipulation of electoral maps, and the potential misuse of law enforcement are symptoms of a democracy in distress. This situation is a stark reminder of the importance of vigilance, informed public discourse, and the need for a political landscape that values democratic principles over partisan victories. The GOP's current trajectory is not just a threat to Texas Democrats but to the very fabric of American democracy. It's time for a return to sanity, where political battles are fought with ideas and integrity, not through coercion and legal loopholes.
