Portland police official worries Trump just made things worse: 'Will increase protesters'

Donald Trump has announced his intentions to send troops to Portland, Oregon, and one local senior police official is worried the president will just be making matters worse.

Politico on Saturday published an article called Why Donald Trump is obsessed with Portland, in which the outlet attempts to explain the president’s “fixation” on the city.

“Five years ago, rioters in Portland set fire to government buildings, epitomizing for many on the right the lawlessness and chaos that swirled around the George Floyd protests,” according to the report. “President Donald Trump never forgot.”

After elaborating on Trump’s motives, Politico shares pieces of an interview with a senior police official in Oregon. The official was not allowed to speak about the issue, so was left with anonymity.

“But a senior police official at the Portland Police Department, who was granted anonymity because he was not authorized to talk about police operations, said sending in additional federal forces would escalate the situation,” according to the report. “’It will increase the number of protesters,’ he said. When asked what the PPD would do if that happens, he added: ‘I truly don’t know.’”

The report goes on the shed some more light on the current ICE protests.

“The senior police official said the situation continues to escalate as federal officers are doxxed, threatened or physically hurt — and then in turn take a more adversarial approach to the late-night protesters,” the report states.

“It is a political nightmare,” he said, according to Politico.

Our Analysis:

Trump’s Portland Obsession: A Dangerous Game

Donald Trump has floated the idea of deploying troops to Portland, Oregon, a move that has sparked considerable controversy and concern. A recent article published by Politico, titled Why Donald Trump is obsessed with Portland, attempts to unravel the former president’s fixation with the city, which has been a hotspot for protests and civil unrest. However, what stands out is not just Trump’s unwavering focus on Portland but the potential consequences of his actions, as highlighted by a senior police official’s apprehensions.

The Roots of Obsession

First, let’s dissect the rationale behind Trump’s so-called “obsession.” The article points to events from five years ago, when rioters in Portland set fire to government buildings during the George Floyd protests, as a pivotal moment for Trump and his supporters. This incident, according to Politico, epitomized the lawlessness and chaos that Trump and many on the right believe characterize the entire movement against police brutality and systemic racism. Trump’s inability to move past these events reveals a fundamental misunderstanding of the protests’ nature and objectives. It’s not simply about law and order; it’s about demanding justice and accountability.

The Local Perspective: A Warning Ignored

The insights provided by the unnamed senior police official in Portland should serve as a dire warning. This official, speaking anonymously for fear of retribution or overstepping bounds, starkly outlines the potential for escalation should Trump follow through with his plan to send in federal forces. The prediction that such actions would only increase the number of protesters and potentially lead to more violence is not just speculation; it’s grounded in recent history. The 2020 federal intervention in Portland only served to inflame tensions, proving that increasing militarized presence does not quell protests but rather exacerbates them.

A Political Nightmare

The situation described by the senior police official, where federal officers and protesters are caught in an escalating cycle of doxxing, threats, and physical confrontations, is indeed a “political nightmare.” However, it’s crucial to recognize that this nightmare is not just a product of the protests but of the federal government’s adversarial approach to handling them. By choosing to confront protesters with more force rather than engaging with the underlying issues they are protesting against, the government is perpetuating a cycle of conflict and division.

Conclusion: A Misguided Strategy

Trump’s intention to send troops to Portland is a misguided strategy that fails to address the root causes of the protests and could lead to further violence and unrest. The warnings from the senior police official highlight the need for a more nuanced and understanding approach to dealing with civil unrest. Unfortunately, Trump’s fixation on Portland seems to be more about projecting strength and appeasing his base than genuinely resolving the issues at hand. This approach is not only dangerous but counterproductive, and it underscores a fundamental lack of understanding of the protests and what they represent. In the end, the real obsession should not be with quelling unrest through force but with addressing the systemic injustices that have led to this point in the first place.
