'It gets weirder': Small detail in DOJ statement on Epstein's death ignites online frenzy

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosFebruary 9, 20265 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Our Analysis:The Curious Case of the Pre-dated Epstein Death Announcement

Jeffrey Epstein in an undated photo released by the Department of Justice on Dec. 19, 2025. (DOJ)

A small detail in a Justice Department statement on the 2019 death of Jeffrey Epstein has ignited an online frenzy of speculation, with many pointing to the document as proof that the disgraced financier’s death was known in advance.

Released last month by the DOJ as part of its publication of around 3.5 million Epstein files, the document in question appears to be a draft statement on the death of Epstein, who was found dead in his Manhattan jail cell on the Friday morning of August 10, 2019. The date on the draft statement, however, reads August 9, a full day before the disgraced financier was found dead.

“Unless it was typo (they got the day right, Friday, but date wrong, 9th instead of 10th) this is super weird,” wrote journalist and former MSNBC host Mehdi Hasan in a social media post on X Saturday night.

“Why would the Trump administration draft a press release saying that Epstein died a day before he actually died?” asked another, X user “Patriot Takes,” a popular political commentary account with more than 460,000 followers. “Oh. Oh my.”

Questions surrounding Epstein’s death have circulated for years, with a number of oddities surrounding the incident that was ultimately – and controversially – ruled a suicide.

For instance, Epstein was placed on suicide watch in late July after being found semi-conscious in his cell with marks on his neck, but was taken off suicide watch just days later on July 29, five days before his death. And, while his death was ruled a suicide by hanging, his neck bone injuries were more consistant with a homicide, according to Dr. Michael Baden, who was present during Epstein’s autopsy.

Other oddities, like cameras in the Manhattan jail malfunctioning the night of Epstein’s death, footage of a supposed earlier suicide attempt accidently being erased, Epstein’s cell being littered with what appear to be dozens of articles of clothing, and the noose Epstein allegedly used never being “definitively identified” have also fueled speculation.

“Couldn’t believe this was real so I double checked the DOJ site. It’s real,” wrote Clint Russell, the host of the political talk show podcast “Liberty Lockdown.” “U.S. attorney [Geoffrey] Berman announced Epstein’s death with letterhead dated the day before he was found dead.”

Russell also noted that Berman – whose name appears on the draft statement – ended up getting fired by President Donald Trump at the request of his then-Attorney General Bill Barr in a messy dispute.

“It gets weirder. Bill Barr announced Berman’s resignation less than a year later,” Russell wrote in a social media post on X.

“Berman denied he was resigning and was then removed by Barr on June 20th, 2020. Trump denied any involvement but it was confirmed by later reporting that in fact it was Trump who had Berman removed. This rabbit hole is endless.

Our Analysis:

The Curious Case of the Pre-dated Epstein Death Announcement

In what can only be described as a tale ripped straight from a conspiracy theorist’s dream journal, a small detail in a Justice Department statement concerning the 2019 death of Jeffrey Epstein has thrown the online world into a speculative frenzy. The heart of the matter? A document, ostensibly a draft statement on Epstein’s death, bore the date August 9, 2019—a full day before Epstein was reportedly found dead in his cell. This isn’t just a smoking gun; it’s a blazing cannon in the eyes of many.

The Devil’s in the Details

Let’s dissect this, shall we? The document in question was part of a colossal release by the DOJ, dumping around 3.5 million files related to Epstein into the public domain. Among these, a draft statement seemingly prepared in advance of Epstein’s death, if we’re to take the date at face value. Journalist Mehdi Hasan and the popular X account “Patriot Takes” were among the first to raise the alarm, sparking widespread speculation and disbelief.

The possibilities here are intriguing, to say the least. Was it a simple typo, a clerical error of monumental proportions? Or does it suggest a premeditation, a foreknowledge of Epstein’s demise that stretches the imagination and credulity of even the most ardent skeptics?

A Confluence of Oddities

The context surrounding Epstein’s death is a veritable buffet of conspiracy fodder. Removed from suicide watch days before his death, injuries consistent more with homicide than suicide, malfunctioning cameras, erased footage, and a cell “decorated” with an implausible amount of clothing—each detail on its own raises eyebrows. Together, they paint a picture of, at best, gross incompetence, and at worst, a cover-up of nefarious proportions.

The Berman Connection

To thicken the plot, the document bore the name of U.S. attorney Geoffrey Berman, who found himself ousted by Trump and Barr in a saga filled with denials, confirmations, and confusion. Clint Russell of “Liberty Lockdown” pointed out this connection, drawing a line from the predated announcement to the political machinations that followed.

Analysis: A Typo or a Tell?

In the grand scheme of things, the predated draft could be dismissed as a clerical error—an incredibly unfortunate and suspiciously timed typo. However, given the circus that has surrounded the Epstein case from the outset, it’s hard to take anything at face value.

The implications of a document drafted in anticipation of Epstein’s death are as disturbing as they are fascinating. It suggests a level of premeditation and coordination that transcends mere negligence or incompetence. It hints at a narrative being carefully crafted before the fact, ready to be deployed to control the unfolding media storm.

In a story already rife with twists, turns, and incredulities, this latest revelation (if it can be substantiated beyond a reasonable doubt) could be the thread that unravels a much larger, darker tapestry. For now, it remains a tantalizing piece of the puzzle—a puzzle that seems to grow more complex and convoluted with each passing revelation.

The truth of the matter may never fully come to light, but this doesn’t detract from the significance of these findings. At the very least, they demand a more thorough investigation, one that scrutinizes not just the circumstances of Epstein’s death but the systemic failures (or possible malfeasance) that allowed such an event to occur—and be potentially foretold.
