JD Vance lashes out at ‘street girl’ Jasmine Crockett in fiery rant

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosDecember 23, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Vice President JD Vance delivers remarks at Uline Inc., in Alburtis, Pennsylvania, U.S., December 16, 2025. REUTERS/Eduardo Munoz

Vice President JD Vance fired off at Rep. Jasmine Crockett (D-TX) Sunday while speaking at a conservative event in Arizona, ridiculing her for what he said was her “street-girl persona” and fake nails.

“Jasmine Crockett!” Vance said, speaking at Turning Point USA’s annual America Fest conference in Phoenix. “Oh, Jasmine Crockett, the record speaks for itself. She wants to be a senator, though her street-girl persona is about as real as her nails!”

Vance’s remarks were met with thunderous applause at the event venue packed wall-to-wall with young conservatives, an event organized by the right-wing advocacy group launched by his late friend, Charlie Kirk, who was assassinated in September in Utah.

Crockett has become a rising star in the Democratic Party, largely due to her frequent and fierce attacks on President Donald Trump, which has prompted him to respond by calling the congresswoman a “low-IQ person.” She has since launched a bid for Senate, looking to unseat Sen. John Cornyn (R-TX) with a campaign launch ad featuring audio of Trump insulting her.

Among progressives, however, Crockett has drawn scrutiny largely over her voting record as it pertains to Israel, voting for an appropriations bill that included a temporary prohibition on U.S. funding for the United Nations Relief and Works Agency for Palestine Refugees (UNWRA), the largest aid provider in Gaza amid Israel’s military siege.

America Fest was first launched in 2021, and featured Trump, former Alaska Gov. Sarah Palin and Kyle Rittenhouse, the Illinois man who became a prominent figure among conservatives after fatally shooting two people during protests in Kenosha, Wisconsin, the previous year.

Our Analysis:

Vance’s Tirade Against Crockett: A Dive into Political Vitriol and Strategy

In a recent spectacle of political discourse, Vice President JD Vance unleashed a barrage of criticism towards Rep. Jasmine Crockett (D-TX) at a conservative event in Arizona, targeting her with remarks that illuminate not just the tenor of political debate in America but also the strategic underpinnings of such attacks.

Vance’s Choice of Words: A Deliberate Provocation? Vance’s choice to ridicule Crockett by attacking her “street-girl persona” and “fake nails” is not merely an off-the-cuff remark; it’s a calculated move designed to belittle and diminish Crockett’s political and personal identity. This language is steeped in gendered and potentially racial undertones, aiming to undermine Crockett’s credibility and seriousness as a political figure. It’s a classic move in the playbook of political disparagement: reduce your opponent to a caricature, thereby making it easier to dismiss their policies and positions.

The Applause: A Reflection of the Political Climate The reaction to Vance’s comments — “thunderous applause” from the young conservatives present — is a stark reminder of the current political climate within certain segments of the American right. It underscores a worrisome trend towards valuing inflammatory rhetoric over substantive debate. This isn’t just about a room full of people agreeing with Vance; it’s about the broader acceptance and even celebration of political discourse that prioritizes insults over insight.

Crockett’s Rising Star and the Trump Factor Crockett has emerged as a significant figure within the Democratic Party, not least because of her direct confrontations with former President Donald Trump, who has resorted to calling her a “low-IQ person.” Her decision to launch a Senate bid with a campaign ad featuring Trump’s insult is a strategic move to galvanize opposition to Trump’s continued influence in politics. However, this strategy also paints a target on her back, making her a prime candidate for attacks from the right.

America Fest: A Symbol of Conservative Culture The venue for Vance’s comments, Turning Point USA’s annual America Fest, is itself a microcosm of the current conservative movement in America. With past speakers including Donald Trump, Sarah Palin, and Kyle Rittenhouse, the event showcases the figures and ideologies that are being celebrated and promoted within certain conservative circles. It’s a reminder of the cultural and political ecosystem that fosters and amplifies the kind of rhetoric Vance employed against Crockett.

The Complicated Dynamics of Progressive Scrutiny Interestingly, Crockett has not been immune to criticism from her side of the political spectrum, particularly regarding her voting record on issues related to Israel. This highlights a critical and often challenging aspect of Democratic politics: the diversity of opinions and the internal scrutiny that can complicate party unity. It’s a double-edged sword that allows for a broad tent but also makes it difficult to navigate complex geopolitical issues without facing backlash from within.

Concluding Thoughts: The State of Political Discourse Vance’s comments and the broader context of his attack on Crockett serve as a grim reminder of the state of political discourse in the United States. It’s a landscape where personal attacks often overshadow policy discussions, where political figures are reduced to caricatures, and where the complexity of individual positions is lost in the shuffle of partisan warfare. As observers and participants in this political environment, it’s crucial to recognize these dynamics, question them, and strive for a more substantive and respectful discourse.
