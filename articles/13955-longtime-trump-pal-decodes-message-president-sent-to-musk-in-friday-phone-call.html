Longtime Trump pal decodes 'message' president sent to Musk in Friday phone call

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosJune 7, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Our Analysis:The Trump-Musk Feud: A Saga of Egos and Influence

U.S. President Donald Trump speaks during a swearing-in ceremony for the interim U.S. Attorney for the District of Columbia, Jeanine Pirro, at the White House in Washington, D.C., U.S., May 28, 2025. REUTERS/Leah Millis

CNN's Dana Bash spoke with Donald Trump by phone Friday morning, and she said one of his former allies explained why the president had taken her call.

The broadcaster said she called the president on his personal cell phone from her own, and Bash reported that he tried to turn the topic of their conversation away form his feud with Elon Musk to economic matters, although he did jab his former ally, saying "the poor guy's got problems" but wished him well.

"They both have a lot to lose here, a lot to lose," Bash said. "I was texting with a former longtime friend of Donald Trump who said, you know, don't kid yourself, that one of the reasons he wanted to get the message out in the conversation he had with me, and he talked to a couple of other reporters this morning, is to try to send a message to Musk. Yeah, he's still mad and he's very upset with him but, like, you know, 'I'm calming down here,' because the president has a lot to lose here."

"Among the many things that Elon Musk has is a huge, huge megaphone, huge megaphone, his 200-and-something million followers," Bash added. "But also has his hands on the algorithm of that profile, that platform on X."

CNN's Aaron Blake said the feud had the potential to divide the MAGA movement, but he said most Republicans would side with the president.

"There was a lot of talk yesterday about who would Republicans choose, Trump or Musk, and I think it's pretty clear, you know, the vast majority of Republicans are going to side with Trump," Blake said. "This is the guy they've been with for a long time. He has real sway over that movement, but that doesn't mean that Musk, you know, deserting Trump and causing problems for him isn't a real problem for these people. You know, we saw it yesterday in one of Musk's posts, which I thought was really interesting, which was like, 'Trump is going to be around for three and a half years as president, I'm going to be here for 40-plus years.'"

"That, to me was Musk pointing at not just his war chest, but also his influence on his social media platform," Blake added. "Basically, you know, you guys shouldn't leap to join Donald Trump. You may not choose me, but you shouldn't leap to join Donald Trump because you'll have to deal with me for the foreseeable future."

Our Analysis:

The Trump-Musk Feud: A Saga of Egos and Influence

In what reads like a script from a political drama, we find ourselves witnessing a feud between two titans of their respective domains: Donald Trump and Elon Musk. According to CNN's Dana Bash, the former President had a convenient change of heart in a recent phone conversation, attempting to shift the narrative from his well-documented spat with Musk to more favorable economic matters. However, Trump could not resist taking a jab at Musk, labeling him as a "poor guy" with problems but nonetheless wishing him well. This maneuver is as transparent as it is trite, revealing Trump's tactical attempt to appear magnanimous while subtly undermining his adversary.

The Underlying Strategy

This episode illuminates a strategic play by Trump, underscored by Bash's interaction with one of Trump's former allies. The intent to "send a message" to Musk is as clear as day; Trump is signaling that despite his anger, he is willing to de-escalate the situation. Why? Because there's a lot at stake. Not just for Trump, but for Musk as well. This isn't just a petty squabble; it's a high-stakes game of influence and control.

Musk's Megaphone vs. Trump's Movement

Bash astutely points out Musk's "huge, huge megaphone" — his 200-and-something million followers on X (formerly Twitter) and his control over the platform's algorithm. This isn't merely about social media vanity metrics; it's about the ability to shape narratives and influence public opinion on a massive scale. Musk's not-so-veiled threat, hinting at his longevity in the public sphere compared to Trump's political lifecycle, isn't just bravado; it's a calculated reminder of his enduring influence.

The MAGA Movement's Loyalty Test

CNN's Aaron Blake offers an interesting insight into the potential impact of this feud on the MAGA movement. While the majority of Republicans might still side with Trump, Musk's defection poses a significant challenge. The idea that Musk's departure could cause "real problems" for Trump's base is not to be dismissed lightly. This isn't merely about choosing sides; it's about the potential fracturing of a movement.

The Bottom Line

This feud between Trump and Musk is more than a clash of egos; it's a battle for the soul of the MAGA movement and, by extension, a significant segment of the American electorate. Trump's attempt to pivot the conversation and Musk's assertion of his long-term influence are strategic plays in a larger game. Both men understand the power of their platforms and the loyalty of their followings. However, the real question remains: Who will emerge as the ultimate influencer?

The saga continues, but one thing is clear: in the realm of politics and power, influence is the most valuable currency. And both Trump and Musk are keenly aware of its worth.
