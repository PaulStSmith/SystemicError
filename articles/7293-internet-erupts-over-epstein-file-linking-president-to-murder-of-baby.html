Raw Story on Christmas Eve reported on a file released by the DOJ which shows a purported victim linking Donald Trump to the currently still unverified murder of a newborn baby, and the internet erupted with people flagging the potential crime.

The day before Christmas, we reported that Trump’s name had appeared on a newly released Epstein file in which a self-identified victim made allegations about a newborn baby being murdered and dumped in Lake Michigan. The Justice Department released the file ahead of Christmas Eve in which an unnamed individual, on Aug. 3, 2020, was looking for an update on the status of their earlier complaint. The report listed the victim’s unnamed uncle as the perpetrator and Trump as a witness.

The newly released files do not show that federal law enforcement agents or prosecutors suspected Trump of wrongdoing. A statement released by the DOJ read, “Some of these documents contain untrue and sensationalist claims made against President Trump that were submitted to the FBI right before the 2020 election.”

“To be clear: the claims are unfounded and false, and if they had a shred of credibility, they certainly would have been weaponized against President Trump already. Nevertheless, out of our commitment to the law and transparency, the DOJ is releasing these documents with the legally required protections for Epstein’s victims.”

But the bizarre baby story proved to have some staying power online.

Journalist David Shuster flagged the document on Wednesday, writing, “DOJ document EFTA00025010 shows FBI received a tip in August 2020 about Trump-Epstein. Allegations include 1985 trafficking of a 13 year old girl. When girl gave birth, Trump allegedly helped girl’s uncle murder the infant and dispose of in Lake Michigan.”

The file actually alleges that Trump was present, but it doesn’t say he took part.

A popular political commentator known as Supertanskiii went even further, saying, “The most rich and powerful men in the world have done (and likely still do) the most heinous s---, yeah, including child rape and murder.”

Our Analysis:

Analyzing the Allegations: The Trump-Epstein File

In a recent revelation that has sent shockwaves through the internet, Raw Story reported on a Department of Justice (DOJ) file that contains allegations linking Donald Trump to a purported, yet unverified, heinous crime. The document, released just before Christmas Eve, details a complaint from an unnamed victim alleging the murder of a newborn baby, with Trump purportedly being a witness to the act. Here’s a breakdown of the crucial elements of this story and the broader implications.

The Allegations: A Closer Look

The core of the report revolves around a DOJ file, specifically marked EFTA00025010, which came to light following a request for an update on a complaint made by an unnamed individual on August 3, 2020. The complaint alleges that in 1985, a 13-year-old girl was trafficked, gave birth, and the infant was subsequently murdered and disposed of in Lake Michigan. The document implicates the victim’s uncle in the act, with Donald Trump allegedly being present.

It is paramount to note, the file does not directly accuse Trump of participating in the murder. This distinction is crucial in the sea of sensationalism that often muddies the waters of discourse.

DOJ’s Statement: A Grain of Salt

The DOJ’s response to the release of these files is a cocktail of dismissal and defensiveness. Their statement emphasizes that the claims against Trump are “unfounded and false,” asserting that if there was any credibility to these allegations, they would have been used against Trump previously. This raises an eyebrow, or two, for several reasons:

  • Timing and Transparency: The release of these documents right before the 2020 election and the subsequent dismissal of their credibility by the DOJ raises questions about the timing and the motivations behind both the allegations and the DOJ’s transparency.

  • The Specter of Weaponization: The DOJ’s assertion that the allegations would have been “weaponized” against Trump if credible, unwittingly acknowledges the politicization of legal and ethical accountability. The idea that the veracity of allegations is tethered to their utility in political warfare is a damning indictment of our current political climate.

Online Reaction: A Spectrum of Belief

The internet’s response oscillated between horror and skepticism. Journalist David Shuster’s highlighting of the document added a semblance of legitimacy to the allegations, prompting widespread discussion. Meanwhile, political commentator Supertanskiii’s comments encapsulate a broader, more cynical view of the elite’s capacity for criminal behavior, suggesting that such acts, if true, are not beyond the realm of possibility given historical precedents.

The Veritable Quagmire of Truth and Perception

This story, laden with allegations of the most vile nature, sits at the intersection of truth, conspiracy, and political warfare. The sensational nature of the claims, coupled with the lack of direct evidence implicating Trump in the alleged act, creates a quagmire that is all too familiar in the digital age. The rapid spread of information (and misinformation) online further complicates the public’s ability to discern fact from fabrication.

In conclusion, while the allegations contained within the DOJ file are shocking, they remain unverified. The release of the document and the subsequent reactions underscore the fraught nature of truth in an era where allegations can be both weaponized and dismissed based on political expediency rather than fact. The call for transparency and accountability remains paramount, but so does the need for critical analysis and skepticism in the face of sensational allegations. In navigating these murky waters, one must tread carefully, armed with discernment and an unwavering commitment to the truth, however uncomfortable it may be.
