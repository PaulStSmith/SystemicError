‘Pathetic’: Outrage erupts after Trump admin steps up retaliation against ex-GOP official

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosMay 2, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Our Analysis:The Krebs Retaliation: A Stark Emblem of Political Vendetta

U.S. President Donald Trump speaks to the media on board Air Force One on the way to West Palm Beach, Florida, U.S., April 13, 2025. REUTERS/Nathan Howard

Former Trump administration cybersecurity official Chris Krebs said his removal from the U.S. Customs and Border Protection’s Global Entry program is clearly “another act of retribution from the administration,” according to a new CNN report.

Krebs, who provoked President Donald Trump’s anger when he publicly contradicted the MAGA leader’s repeated assertions that the 2020 election was stolen, told CNN he was notified Wednesday afternoon that his Trusted Traveler Program status had been revoked.

The latest development in Trump’s campaign against Krebs came weeks after Trump took the unprecedented step of ordering his Justice Department to investigate Krebs and a second former administration official – and stripped them of their security clearances.

The new move on Wednesday sparked a wave of backlash from political observers who sounded off over social media Wednesday night.

“Pathetic wanna be thugs,” The Bulwark’s Tim Miller wrote on X.

“I’m sure that house Republicans will immediately be holding hearings on the weaponization of government, of course,” attorney Aaron Reichlin-Melnick of the American Immigration Council told his X followers. “After all, this might be the single most obvious example of that we’ve seen in generations. I bet they’re all drafting press releases right now, ready to go!”

Political observer Linda Watt wrote in an X post: “Well, they’re investigating Chris Krebs for the audacity of stating that the 2020 elections were safe and valid.”

"A key First Amendment battle could break out over whether Chris Krebs has to wait in a monstrously long line whenever he reenters the country," Washington Post media reporter Scott Nover told his followers on X.

Our Analysis:

The Krebs Retaliation: A Stark Emblem of Political Vendetta

Chris Krebs, former cybersecurity official under the Trump administration, recently found himself on the receiving end of what can only be described as a petty and vindictive maneuver orchestrated by the powers that be. His expulsion from the U.S. Customs and Border Protection’s Global Entry program is not just an inconvenience—it's a blatant act of retribution. This decision, as reported by CNN, is a direct fallout from Krebs daring to challenge the MAGA narrative that claimed the 2020 election was stolen. Krebs, in a demonstration of integrity, publicly contradicted President Donald Trump, standing by the security and validity of the election results.

The removal from the Trusted Traveler Program is merely the latest in a series of vindictive actions taken against Krebs. Weeks prior, Trump had already escalated his vendetta by ordering the Justice Department to investigate Krebs and another former administration official, stripping them of their security clearances. This move alone was deeply troubling, signaling a chilling abuse of power to punish dissent within the ranks of government officials.

The backlash against this decision was swift and fierce, with political observers and commentators taking to social media to express their outrage. Tim Miller of The Bulwark succinctly captured the sentiment, labeling those behind these actions as “Pathetic wanna be thugs.” Indeed, the pettiness of the act is underscored by its sheer vindictiveness.

Aaron Reichlin-Melnick, an attorney at the American Immigration Council, pointed out the glaring hypocrisy in the situation. He sarcastically noted that House Republicans, who often decry the "weaponization of government," are conspicuously silent on this matter, despite it being a prime example of such weaponization. This silence is both hypocritical and telling, revealing a selective outrage that aligns solely with political convenience rather than principle.

Linda Watt and Scott Nover’s comments highlight another critical angle—the implications for free speech and the First Amendment. Krebs' punishment for simply stating a fact—that the 2020 elections were secure—raises alarming questions about the state of free speech in America. Nover’s quip about a potential First Amendment battle over Krebs' right to express himself without facing government retaliation, though made in jest, underscores a serious concern.

The situation surrounding Chris Krebs is emblematic of a broader, more disturbing trend: the use of governmental power to silence and punish dissent. It's a strategy that not only undermines the principles of democracy and free expression but also erodes public trust in our institutions. When officials are targeted not for wrongdoing but for speaking the truth, it sends a message that loyalty to leadership supersedes the loyalty to truth.

In the face of such blatant acts of retribution, it's crucial for those who value democracy and free speech to stand firm and speak out. The case of Chris Krebs isn’t just about one man being unfairly targeted; it’s a warning sign of the dangerous path down which political vendettas can lead us. The integrity of our democratic institutions and the protection of free speech are at stake, and the response to Krebs’ situation will be a telling indicator of the direction in which we’re headed.
