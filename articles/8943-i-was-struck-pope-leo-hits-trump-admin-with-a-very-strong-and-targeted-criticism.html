'I was struck': Pope Leo hits Trump admin with a 'very strong' and targeted criticism

Pope Leo XIV holds a general audience at Saint Peter’s Square at the Vatican on Oct. 1, 2025. REUTERS/Remo Casilli

Pope Leo XIV has issued one of his harshest and most direct criticisms of the Trump administration’s immigration policy yet.

According to BBC News, the pope, speaking to reporters outside the papal retreat at Castel Gandolfo, “said there were people who have lived in the US ‘for years and years never causing problems, who have been deeply affected’ by Trump’s hardline policy on migration. Speaking in English, and reaching a US audience directly, he reiterated the Catholic belief that every Christian will ultimately be judged on how they welcomed ‘the foreigner’.”

Pope Leo also condemned Trump’s bombing strikes on ships they suspect of trafficking drugs in the Caribbean — which many experts fear are bordering on war crimes — saying that it is only fanning the flames of conflict.

The pope, who has long made his position in support of welcoming migrants clear and continues a long-standing position of the Church, hails from Chicago, which has become ground zero for Trump’s effort to crack down on migrants and to silence protests over his agenda.

Catholic historian Austen Ivereigh told BBC, “I was struck by how direct his reference was because he’s obviously talking about the ICE round-ups. It was very strong.”

Trump long had a tense relationship with Pope Leo’s predecessor, Pope Francis, who set the Catholic Church on a more socially progressive path. Pope Leo has continued this trend, triggering anger from the MAGA fanbase.

Our Analysis:

Pope Leo XIV’s Scathing Critique of Trump’s Immigration Policy

In a move that’s bound to stir up more than just political dust, Pope Leo XIV has launched a formidable assault on the Trump administration’s draconian stance on immigration. According to a report by BBC News, the Pope’s comments, made from the serene confines of Castel Gandolfo, were not just a reiteration of the Catholic Church’s long-standing ethos of welcoming the stranger but a direct jab at policies that have, in his words, “deeply affected” individuals who have contributed positively to American society for years.

The Pope’s Bold Stance

The Pope’s choice to address this issue in English is a strategic move designed to ensure his message echoes through the corridors of power in the United States, bypassing any potential language barriers that could dilute its impact. By emphasizing the Christian duty towards “the foreigner,” Pope Leo XIV isn’t just calling for empathy; he’s underscoring a fundamental spiritual accountability that he believes the current U.S. administration has grievously overlooked.

Condemnation of Military Aggression

Pope Leo’s critique doesn’t stop at immigration. He extends his censure to the Trump administration’s controversial military actions in the Caribbean, specifically the bombing strikes on vessels suspected of drug trafficking. Labeling these actions as near war crimes, the Pope articulates a growing fear among international observers that such aggressive tactics do more harm than good, exacerbating conflicts rather than resolving them.

A Personal Connection

The Pope’s remarks carry an added layer of significance given his roots in Chicago, a city that finds itself squarely in the crosshairs of Trump’s aggressive immigration crackdowns. This personal connection to the epicenter of the debate adds a palpable authenticity and urgency to his criticisms.

Historical Context and Continuity

Catholic historian Austen Ivereigh‘s observation about the Pope’s directness underscores a critical point: Pope Leo XIV is not deviating from the Church’s path but reinforcing a legacy of social progressiveness initiated by his predecessor, Pope Francis. The transition from Francis to Leo represents a seamless continuation of the Church’s advocacy for the marginalized, challenging political leaders to reflect on the moral implications of their policies.

Triggering the MAGA Fanbase

The Pope’s forthright stance is predictably triggering backlash from Trump’s MAGA base, a demographic not known for its tolerance of dissenting views, especially from figures of moral authority that challenge the core of their political identity. This clash of ideologies isn’t just a footnote in the larger narrative; it’s a vivid illustration of the polarized world we inhabit, where even the voice of religious leadership becomes a battleground.

Conclusion

Pope Leo XIV’s criticisms of Trump’s immigration policy are a clarion call for a more compassionate and humane approach to governance, one that aligns with foundational Christian values. This isn’t merely about politics; it’s about the essence of what it means to be a community, a society, and indeed, a civilization. As the world grapples with these divisive issues, the Pope’s words serve as a reminder of the deeper moral and ethical considerations at play, challenging both leaders and citizens to reflect on the kind of world they wish to create and inhabit.
