'Seen enough': Big-name conservative joins calls to impeach RFK after disastrous hearing

GOP discontent with Robert F. Kennedy grows following a fiery hearing Thursday. REUTERS/Kaylee Greenlee

Calls for the impeachment of Robert F. Kennedy and demands for his resignation escalated on Thursday following a contentious hearing with Senate lawmakers, with even conservative Bill Kristol stating, "I've seen enough."

Kennedy, the Health and Human Services Secretary, was criticized by senators over recent moves, including firing Centers for Disease Control and Prevention Director Susan Monarez, a Trump appointee, and firing the full panel of the vaccine advisory board. Democrats have accused him of putting the public in great danger over spreading misinformation and gutting evidence-based research over vaccines, Axios reports.

And now the number of GOP critics are growing as support for him wanes, with some indicating Kennedy could be a liability as Republicans head into the midterm elections.

Kristol, editor at-large of The Bulwark, who served as chief of staff for Vice President Dan Quayle during the George H.W. Bush administration, sounded off after the fiery exchange in Washington, D.C.

"I’ve seen enough. Impeach Robert F. Kennedy, Jr.," Kristol posted.

He isn't the only voice calling for action.

"Impeach that quack, @SenBillCassidy. He’s irredeemable. Every day you delay risks more dead Americans. How much blood do you want on your hands?" conservative commentator Sarah Rumpf posted on X.

"Impeach his deeply dishonest and unhealthy ass!" Nation writer Joan Walsh posted on X.

Others issued a similar directive and demanded impeachment also apply to U.S. Supreme Court judges following news Thursday that nearly a dozen currently serving federal judges criticized the U.S. Supreme Court from behind the cloak of anonymity.

In a report cited on background with NBC News, judges described a growing concern over the court's increasingly frequent habit of overturning lower court rulings involving President Donald Trump's policies with little or no explanation.

"Another reason that impeachment must be on the table for judges," conservative activist and commentator Tom Fitton of Judicial Watch posted on X.

Our Analysis:

The Escalating Call for Impeachment: A Scathing Review

In what can only be described as a political circus, the recent uproar surrounding Robert F. Kennedy and the clamor for his impeachment have reached fever pitch. The article under scrutiny presents a narrative so loaded with irony and hypocrisy, it’s almost theatrical. Let’s dissect the madness, shall we?

Kennedy: A Controversial Figure in the Hot Seat

Robert F. Kennedy, serving as the Health and Human Services Secretary, finds himself in the eye of a political hurricane. His decisions to fire the CDC Director and dissolve the vaccine advisory board have sparked outrage and concern, not just among Democrats, but increasingly among Republicans. The accusations thrown at him? Endangering public health and spreading misinformation about vaccines. The gravity of these accusations cannot be overstated, yet the manner in which the political machinery has shifted into high gear over Kennedy’s actions reeks of opportunism and political maneuvering.

The Political Opportunists Enter the Fray

Enter figures like Bill Kristol and Sarah Rumpf, along with others who have taken to social media to voice their outrage. Kristol's statement, "I’ve seen enough. Impeach Robert F. Kennedy, Jr.", is particularly telling. It's a soundbite designed for virality rather than a nuanced critique. Kristol, a conservative with a history of opposing anything remotely left of his ideology, suddenly finds common ground with Democrats? The irony is not lost here.

Sarah Rumpf’s dramatic plea, "Impeach that quack... How much blood do you want on your hands?" is an exercise in hyperbole. Such emotionally charged rhetoric serves little purpose beyond inflaming tensions and dividing opinion further.

The Hypocrisy of the Impeachment Bandwagon

The article highlights a growing trend of using impeachment as a political tool rather than the grave constitutional remedy it is meant to be. The calls for Kennedy’s impeachment, while rooted in legitimate concerns over public health policy, are amplified by a chorus of voices more interested in political point-scoring than in the substantive issues at hand.

Furthermore, the sudden concern for public health by some of Kennedy’s critics, who previously downplayed the COVID-19 pandemic or opposed public health measures, is hypocritical at best. Their selective outrage, now weaponized against Kennedy, lacks credibility and reeks of political opportunism.

A Broader Pattern of Judicial Overreach?

The article also touches upon a broader, more systemic issue: the criticism of the U.S. Supreme Court's habit of overturning lower court rulings on Trump's policies without much explanation. This criticism, coming from nearly a dozen federal judges, points to a disturbing trend of judicial overreach and lack of transparency. Yet, the focus on Kennedy’s impeachment overshadows this critical issue, demonstrating the media and political figures’ penchant for sensationalism over substantive judicial critique.

Conclusion: The Theater of the Absurd

What we witness here is nothing short of a political spectacle, where the stakes are high, and the performances are calculated for maximum impact. Kennedy's actions, whether misguided or not, have become fodder for a political machine that thrives on division and spectacle. The calls for his impeachment, while perhaps warranted, are overshadowed by the opportunism and hypocrisy of those leading the charge.

In the end, the real casualties of this political theater are the American people, whose interests are sidelined by the relentless pursuit of political power and vengeance. It’s high time we demand more from our political discourse and hold our leaders—and their critics—to a higher standard.
