Trump threatens new investigation after Truth Social meltdown over 'historic' Jan 6

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosSeptember 28, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

FILE PHOTO: U.S. President Donald Trump gestures after signing the sweeping spending and tax legislation, known as the “One Big Beautiful Bill Act,” at the White House in Washington, D.C., U.S., July 4, 2025. REUTERS/Leah Millis/File Photo

President Donald Trump pledged to target his own former FBI director on Saturday in a fiery social media post regarding what he dubbed the “January 6th hoax.”

“It was just revealed that the FBI had secretly placed, against all Rules, Regulations, Protocols, and Standards, 274 FBI Agents into the Crowd just prior to, and during, the January 6th Hoax,” Trump wrote on his social media platform Truth Social.

“This is different from what [FBI] Director Christopher Wray stated, over and over again! That’s right, as it now turns out, FBI Agents were at, and in, the January 6th Protest, probably acting as Agitators and Insurrectionists, but certainly not as ‘Law Enforcement Officials.’”

Trump was likely referring to new reporting that revealed hundreds of FBI agents were present during the Jan. 6 Capitol riot. However, contrary to Trump’s claims, subsequent reporting has dispelled the idea that the agents were embedded among rioters and operating undercover, but rather, only present as a response to the crowd breeching the Capitol.

Nevertheless, Trump insisted that the revelation proved Wray lied, and perhaps in a manner similar to former FBI Director James Comey, who this week was indicted after Trump launched a campaign to against him.

“I want to know who each and every one of these so-called ‘Agents’ are, and what they were up to on that now ‘Historic’ Day,” Trump wrote.

“Many Great American Patriots were made to pay a very big price only for the love their Country. I owe this investigation of ‘Dirty Cops and Crooked Politicians’ to them! Christopher Wray, the then Director of the FBI, has some major explaining to do. That’s two in a row, Comey and Wray, who got caught LYING, with our Great Country at stake. WE CAN NEVER LET THIS HAPPEN TO AMERICA AGAIN!”

Our Analysis:

Dissecting Trump’s Fiery Rhetoric and Misleading Claims

In a recent tempest of tweets, former President Donald Trump has once again set the digital ether ablaze with his incendiary remarks concerning the January 6th Capitol riot. Trump’s allegations, steeped in conspiracy and deflection, target FBI Director Christopher Wray, along with the broader law enforcement community, accusing them of deceit and malfeasance. But a closer examination of the facts reveals a narrative far removed from Trump’s portrayal of events.

“The January 6th Hoax” - Trump’s Misleading Terminology

Trump’s description of the January 6th Capitol riot as a “hoax” is not only misleading but dangerously revisionist. Labeling the violent insurrection, which resulted in multiple deaths and significant injuries, as a “hoax” serves to undermine the gravity of the event and the threat it posed to American democracy. This rhetoric is not just factually incorrect; it’s a deliberate attempt to rewrite the narrative of one of the darkest days in recent American history.

Misrepresentation of FBI Involvement

Trump claims that 274 FBI agents were secretly embedded within the crowd during the Capitol riot, insinuating a vast conspiracy involving the FBI acting as “Agitators and Insurrectionists.” This assertion is a gross mischaracterization of the facts. Subsequent reporting clarified that while FBI agents were present, their role was in response to the breach of the Capitol, not as undercover provocateurs embedded among the rioters. Trump’s narrative seeks to deflect blame from the rioters—his supporters—onto the FBI, without basis in fact.

The Allegations Against Christopher Wray

Trump’s assault on FBI Director Christopher Wray is framed as a demand for accountability, accusing Wray of lying about the FBI’s involvement in the Capitol riot. However, this accusation crumbles under scrutiny. The presence of law enforcement at a scene of unfolding anarchy is standard procedure, not evidence of a deep-state conspiracy. Trump’s leap from Wray’s oversight of the FBI to accusations of lying and misconduct is a dangerous conflation of law enforcement duties with alleged political persecution.

Trump’s Campaign Against Comey and Wray

Trump’s mention of former FBI Director James Comey’s recent indictment alongside his criticism of Wray is a transparent attempt to conflate unrelated issues to sow doubt about the integrity of the FBI. This tactic is part of a broader strategy to discredit law enforcement and intelligence agencies that challenge or investigate his actions or those of his supporters. By invoking Comey’s indictment, Trump aims to create a narrative of systemic corruption within the FBI that targets him and his allies unjustly.

Conclusion: The Danger of Trump’s Rhetoric

Trump’s statements represent more than just a disgruntled ex-president airing grievances; they are a calculated attempt to undermine public trust in law enforcement and rewrite the history of the Capitol riot. By painting the FBI as corrupt and the January 6th insurrection as a “hoax,” Trump is not just spreading misinformation; he’s attacking the very foundations of American democracy.

The real issue at hand is not the alleged lies of Christopher Wray or the presence of FBI agents at the Capitol; it’s the ongoing effort by Trump and his allies to distort reality to fit their narrative. As fact-checkers and defenders of truth, we must remain vigilant, dissecting these misleading claims with sharp, fact-based analysis. The integrity of our democracy depends on it.
