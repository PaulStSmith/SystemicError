A woman told the FBI that in the early 2000s, when she was 16 years old, she had given a massage to Jeffrey Epstein at his New York home while the disgraced financier “was on a speakerphone call with Donald Trump” — just moments before being sexually assaulted, according to a newly released FBI memo.

First reported by the Daily Mail, the revelation comes from a transcript of a 2020 FBI interview released by the Justice Department last week as part of its publication of 50,000 new files related to Epstein. In the interview, the woman, whose name is redacted, told an FBI agent and analyst that she had been recruited by Epstein when she was a junior in high school.

The alleged victim told the FBI that she had two inconsequential visits to Epstein’s home, but that on the third visit, she was brought to an upstairs massage room along with an “older woman.”

“[Redacted] recalled walking down a hallway and to the right was the massage room with a bathroom,” the FBI memo reads.

“[Redacted] remembered a bathroom with dark marble and bright lighting with the toilet in its own section. Epstein came into the room and was like ‘let’s go, let’s go.’ Epstein got on the massage table and was on a speakerphone call with Donald Trump. [Redacted] started getting undressed and they started massaging him.”

The FBI memo did not specify the length of the alleged call between Epstein and Trump, nor did it specify when the call ended. It did, however, include further details of the alleged victim’s account of the massage, which the woman told the FBI escalated into sexual assault.

“[Redacted] started massaging Epstein’s feet and pointed to his back,” the memo reads. “Epstein grabbed [redacted ]’s hand and it escalated to the point where Epstein pulled [redacted] on top of him. Afterwards, Epstein gave [redacted] $300 to give to [redacted].”

Trump and Epstein have had a well-documented friendship dating back to the 1980s before the two reportedly cut ties with each other around 2004, according to Trump. A 2011 email from Epstein, however, suggests that the disgraced financier may have had a planned call with Trump that year, raising questions as to the timeline of their falling out.

While the aforementioned allegation was documented by the FBI, many of the Epstein-related documents produced by the DOJ contain unverified, uncorroborated allegations that do not constitute evidence, and do not establish wrongdoing. Trump is not facing any criminal charges or investigations related to the allegation.

Our Analysis:

The Epstein-Trump Connection: A Closer Look at the Alarming Allegations

In a revelation that reads like a script from the darkest corners of political and financial elite conspiracies, a recently released FBI memo sheds light on a disturbing encounter involving Jeffrey Epstein, Donald Trump, and a then-16-year-old girl. According to the Daily Mail, this account was part of 50,000 new files related to Epstein, released by the Justice Department. The narrative, emerging from the shadows of the early 2000s, not only rekindles the flames of the Epstein scandal but also drags Donald Trump back into the vortex of controversy.

The Allegation: A Detailed Account

The unnamed woman’s narrative, as disclosed in the FBI memo, is harrowing. Recruited by Epstein in high school, her third visit to his New York home escalated horrifyingly. While Epstein was on a speakerphone call with Donald Trump, she was coerced into giving Epstein a massage that culminated in sexual assault. The memo, painstaking in its detail, outlines a scenario where power dynamics and abuse intertwine, leaving a young girl victimized and compensated with a mere $300 for her silence.

The Trump-Epstein Connection: A Timeline of Controversy

The relationship between Trump and Epstein has always been a subject of speculation, with ties reportedly stretching back to the 1980s. Despite Trump’s claims of severing connections with Epstein around 2004, an email from Epstein in 2011 suggests otherwise, blurring the timeline of their association. This discrepancy not only raises questions about the veracity of Trump’s account but also about the depth of their relationship post-2004.

Unverified Allegations and the Burden of Proof

While the documents released by the DOJ contain a mix of verified and unverified allegations, it’s crucial to navigate these revelations with a critical lens. The specific allegation involving Trump, despite its detailed account, remains uncorroborated and does not, in isolation, constitute evidence of wrongdoing. However, it’s imperative to question why, in a sea of unverified claims, this particular narrative has emerged with such clarity and detail.

The Larger Implication: A Web of Elite Abuses

This account, albeit unverified, underscores a systemic issue far beyond the individuals involved. It highlights a world where the rich and powerful operate with impunity, often at the expense of the vulnerable. The alleged incident is not just a story of individual misconduct but a symptom of a broader, systemic failure to protect and a culture of silence that surrounds the elite’s transgressions.

Conclusion: The Need for Accountability

While Donald Trump is not facing any criminal charges or investigations related to this allegation, the court of public opinion is often less forgiving. The narrative presents a moment of reflection for society on the mechanisms of power, the silence of complicity, and the long, arduous road toward justice for the survivors of such abuses.

In the end, the Epstein-Trump connection, whether through verified allegations or uncorroborated claims, serves as a chilling reminder of the shadowy interactions that fuel systemic abuses. It’s a wake-up call for accountability, transparency, and a reevaluation of the moral compass guiding those in positions of immense power. As we sift through the allegations, let us not lose sight of the systemic errors that allow such narratives to exist in the first place.
