Trump admin must learn from 'cautionary' tale of one first-year disaster: conservative

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosFebruary 19, 20264 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Donald Trump (Reuters)

Donald Trump and his administration must learn from the backlash they experienced after attempting to cancel Jimmy Kimmel last year, according to a conservative.

The president has been a frequent critic of the talk show host, firing out Truth Social posts against the 58-year-old comedian on the social media platform. But an attempt by the admin to pull Kimmel from the air and subsequently cancel his show was met with pushback from audiences and other talk show hosts.

Veteran broadcaster and political commentator Charlie Sykes believes the Trump admin will have to take stock of the public’s reaction to their attempt at pulling Kimmel off the air.

Kimmel was off the air from September 17 through September 22 for comments made after the death of Charlie Kirk, but the talk show host was reinstated shortly after.

Sykes, speaking to Molly Jong-Fast and Fast Politics, said, “I did think that the attempt by the Trump administration to cancel Jimmy Kimmel was kind of an interesting, cautionary note from their point of view. What happened was, they kicked Jimmy Kimmel off the air, and what happened?

“The consumers of the product from Disney and of ABC revolted and forced them to bring him back. I think Kimmel is more potent than ever.”

Sykes went on to suggest that those companies serving Trump and his admin are foolish to do so, given the frequent changes in the president’s mood. He added, “Simply serving the whims of Donald Trump, first of all, it’s a fool’s errand because you’re never going to be able to suck up sufficiently to Donald Trump.

“You’re going to, eventually, have to report something that he does not like, some fact, some truth, some reality. And so all of those cave-ins, all of those surrenders, you know, what do they add up to?

“Because Trump’s going to turn on you sooner or later, right? Unless you close down, shut down, and go out of business.”

Our Analysis:

The Attempt to Silence Jimmy Kimmel: A Stark Illustration of Trump’s Authoritarian Impulses

In what can only be described as a brazen act of censorship, the Trump administration’s attempt to cancel Jimmy Kimmel, following his comments after the death of Charlie Kirk, is a chilling reminder of the lengths to which Trump is willing to go to silence criticism. This endeavor not only backfired spectacularly but also served to amplify Kimmel’s voice, making him, as veteran broadcaster Charlie Sykes aptly put it, “more potent than ever.”

The Backlash: A Testament to Public Sentiment

The public’s reaction to the administration’s attempt to pull Kimmel off the air was swift and unequivocal. Consumers of Disney and ABC, the parent company of Kimmel’s show, revolted against this blatant suppression of free speech, forcing the network to reinstate the talk show host. This episode serves as a powerful reminder that, despite the administration’s authoritarian tendencies, the public still holds considerable power. The attempt to cancel Kimmel was not only a failure but a miscalculation that underestimated the resolve of the American people to protect free expression.

The Folly of Serving Trump’s Whims

Sykes’ commentary on the futility of appeasing Trump highlights a broader issue within his administration and among his supporters. The constant shifts in Trump’s mood and demands create an environment where loyalty is fleeting, and no amount of sycophancy can guarantee favor. This dynamic is not only toxic but also unsustainable, leading to a culture of fear and opportunism that undermines the very principles of democracy.

The Irony of “Cancel Culture” Accusations

The irony of the Trump administration’s attempt to cancel Kimmel cannot be overstated. Trump and his allies have long decried “cancel culture,” yet this incident reveals a stark hypocrisy. When criticism is aimed at them, they are quick to engage in the very practices they condemn. This double standard is emblematic of a broader pattern of behavior that seeks to undermine critical voices and stifle dissent.

Conclusion: A Cautionary Tale

The failed attempt to cancel Jimmy Kimmel is more than just a misstep by the Trump administration; it is a cautionary tale about the dangers of attempting to control the narrative through censorship. It underscores the importance of vigilance in protecting our fundamental freedoms against those who would seek to curtail them for their own benefit. Let this episode be a reminder that, in the face of authoritarian impulses, public pushback and solidarity can and do make a difference.
