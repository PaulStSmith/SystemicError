Gavin Newsom’s Press Office has called out Donald Trump’s administration for failing to pay out on a dividend they promised nearly a year ago.

The DOGE dividend featured in planning documents written up during Elon Musk’s time in government, with the Department of Government Efficiency hoping to cut so much in-house expenditure that they could go on to give some money to taxpayers. This never came to be, though the team behind the Governor of California has not forgotten.

Also missing, aside from the DOGE dividend, is an update on tariff rebates, which were promised by the president’s team when he introduced tariffs to the economic plan.

Karoline Leavitt was asked for an update on the dividend pay, but it seems there is no new update from the White House. She was asked, “Is there an update regarding the tariff rebate checks the White House promised?”

Leavitt replied, “I don’t have an update for you.” Newsom’s Press Office has since mocked the lack of update and called out the president’s team for not having anything for those who completed their tariff check.

A post to the Gavin Newsom Press Office X account reads, “Confirmed our tariff check three times in Trump’s fundraising emails. Where’s the check? Where’s the DOGE dividend?”

Attached to their post is a screenshot of the Trump tariff check, with three “confirm check here” buttons on a page with a picture of Trump holding a stack of bills.

Newsom’s constant trolling of Trump comes as the Governor of California seemingly makes a move to bring down the president’s regime.

A recent trip to speak about energy with UK officials seemingly caused frustrations for Trump. “The more Trump takes the bait, the more attention you get in return,” an adviser said, regarding the British energy trip.

They added, “But it’s much bigger than that. It’s about bringing attention to the fact that what Trump is doing is out of step and it’s not normal and it’s not the American view. It certainly does not reflect what the American people want.”

Our Analysis:

The Promised Dividends: A Tale of Unfulfilled Promises

In the latest episode of political theater, Gavin Newsom’s Press Office has thrown a spotlight on what can only be described as a classic case of political amnesia—courtesy of Donald Trump’s administration. The bone of contention? A dividend, promised nearly a year ago, that has apparently vanished into the ether, much like the credibility of the promises made.

The DOGE Dividend Debacle

Let’s dissect this with the precision of a surgeon: The DOGE dividend, a brainchild of the Department of Government Efficiency during Elon Musk’s tenure, was supposed to be a testament to governmental frugality. The plan? Cut in-house expenditure so drastically that the government could afford to distribute some of its savings back to the taxpayers. A noble endeavor, indeed—if it had ever materialized. Instead, it joins the long list of phantasmagorical promises that seem to evaporate upon close inspection.

Tariff Rebates: The Ghost That Never Materialized

Adding to the mirage is the case of the tariff rebates. Introduced as a sweetener alongside Trump’s economic plan, these rebates were supposed to mitigate the financial impact of tariffs on the American populace. And yet, when pressed for an update, Karoline Leavitt—a spokesperson for the administration—could offer nothing but a shrug encapsulated in seven words: “I don’t have an update for you.” This response is not just inadequate; it’s a stark illustration of the administration’s disregard for accountability.

Newsom’s Trolling or Political Strategizing?

Governor Newsom, not one to let such opportunities slide, has taken to social media to underscore this failure, mockingly inquiring about the whereabouts of both the tariff check and the DOGE dividend. This isn’t merely trolling; it’s a calculated move to highlight the discrepancy between promises made and promises kept. Newsom’s approach, leveraging both humor and social media reach, shines a light on the larger issue at hand: the administration’s failure to deliver on its economic promises.

The Bigger Picture

This saga transcends the bickering between Newsom and Trump; it’s a symptom of a larger disease plaguing our political landscape: the casual issuance of promises with no real intent or ability to fulfill them. Newsom’s recent trip to the UK, aimed at discussing energy issues, further riles Trump’s camp, not merely for the attention it garners but for the stark contrast it draws between rhetoric and action.

An adviser’s comment encapsulates the strategy: “The more Trump takes the bait, the more attention you get in return.” But let’s not miss the forest for the trees. This isn’t just about political one-upmanship; it’s about holding leaders to account, about ensuring that promises made to the electorate are promises kept. It’s about reminding those in power that their responsibilities extend beyond the campaign trail, into the very lives of those they’ve pledged to serve.

In the final analysis, the DOGE dividend and tariff rebates saga serves as a stark reminder of the gulf between political promises and political action. As these promised dividends remain nothing more than figments of political imagination, one can’t help but wonder: when will our political discourse rise above the realm of fantasy and anchor itself in the tangible, in the real, in the achievable? Until then, we remain spectators in a theater of the absurd, where promises are the currency, and accountability seems forever in deficit.
