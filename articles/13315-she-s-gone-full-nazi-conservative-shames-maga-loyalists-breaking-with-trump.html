'She's gone full Nazi!' Conservative shames MAGA loyalists breaking with Trump

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosJune 25, 20252 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Photo of Erick Erickson speaking at an event (Gage Skidmore/Flickr)

Right-wing strategist and commentator Erick Erickson slammed President Donald Trump's most loyal MAGA supporters on X Tuesday, along with Trump's left-wing opponents, for taking extreme isolationist perspectives on the conflict with Iran.

The president ordered a strike on Iran's nuclear facilities over the weekend, and is now trying to manifest a ceasefire between Iran and Israel by posting about it on Truth Social.

"Through it all, the fearmongers will sow fear, dread, and doubt because their revenue stream depends on you staying in existential dread, relying on them to keep you informed and fearful, and not noticing their sleights of hand like discrediting American institutional accuracy then relying on institutional claims to amplify their fear," wrote Erickson. "It’s a grift."

He called out some of the worst offenders by name, including Democrats and MAGA individuals.

"Candace Owens is declaring Prime Minister Netanyahu is as bad as her school teachers claimed Adolph Hitler was. She’s gone full Nazi," wrote Erickson. "Steve Bannon was used as a prop by the President to distract from the President’s intentions to drop a bomb. Some guy the internet cares passionately about named Dave Smith now wants Donald Trump impeached. Ben Rhodes is back to crying. Marjorie Taylor Greene is declaring herself the base and separating from the President on an 80/20 issue because of her fear of Jewish space lasers. Matt Gaetz attempted to stake out a position against intervention, but the botox made it hard to understand him."

What all of this ultimately means, he said, is "the tips of the horseshoe have gotten crowded with the grifters, charlatans, isolationists, America haters, and frauds."

Although a loyal GOP footsoldier, Erickson has increasingly railed against some of Trump's most unorthodox ideas and the fervor with which his supporters adopt them, particularly the idea that imposing massive new tariffs on America isn't a tax.
