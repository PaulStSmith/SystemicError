A Fact of Life? No. A National Disease

Florida State University now joins the expanding graveyard of American educational institutions shattered by gunfire. Five dead. Six wounded. A suspect in custody. And what do we get from the halls of power? A Vice President who dares to say that school shootings are a “fact of life.”

No. The “birds and the bees” are a fact of life.
Taxes are a fact of life.
Wondering whether your child will make it home from school should not be.

The American Way of Death

Let’s be clear: I am not anti-gun. I believe in the right to own a firearm for self-defense, for hunting, for protecting one’s home and family. But when the “hunting rifle” in question has a high-capacity magazine and can fire dozens of rounds in seconds — you’re not hunting deer. You’re hunting people.

The fetishization of assault rifles as everyday tools of freedom is nothing more than a death cult with a press secretary. The 2nd Amendment was never meant to be a suicide pact, and yet every shooting is followed by the same canned defense: “It’s our right.”

Well-regulated militia? What regulation? What militia? We have children drilled more rigorously in active shooter protocols than civics or algebra.

The Blood on Their Hands

We’ve passed the point of honest debate. We’re in the phase of deliberate negligence. Every politician who blocks sensible firearm restrictions — red flag laws, background checks, bans on weapons of war — is now complicit.

The National Rifle Association — with its endless lobbying, propaganda, and bought-and-paid-for politicians — is not a civic group. It is not a rights organization. It is a domestic extremist movement, and it should be classified as a terrorist organization.

You cannot look at rows of dead children, stacks of body bags, and weeping families and still cling to your AR-15 like it’s a holy relic.

There is no freedom in fear. There is no liberty in lockdown. There is no “right” that justifies this level of national carnage.

Stop Calling It Normal

We have allowed a slow-motion atrocity to become background noise. Mass shootings are now routine — white noise under the national anthem.

To the Vice President: if this is what you call a “fact of life,” then you are not fit to speak on life at all.

This is not life. This is decay.
This is not liberty. This is rot wrapped in red, white, and blue.
This is not a nation defending freedom. This is a nation cannibalizing its future.

The G7’s Bloody Outlier

Let the numbers speak:

  • Between 2009 and 2018, the United States experienced 288 school shootings.

  • During that same time, Canada had 2, France 2, Germany 1 — and the rest of the G20 had zero.

That’s not a statistic — that’s an indictment.
The U.S. had 57 times more school shootings than its G7 peers combined. It’s not just bad luck. It’s a national disease, enabled by spineless politicians, profit-driven gun lobbies, and a public too numbed to scream anymore.

Source: Statista

Until Something Changes

Unless we finally admit that the problem isn’t just the shooter, but the system that arms him, excuses him, glorifies him —

—then tomorrow’s headlines are already written.

Only the names will change.
