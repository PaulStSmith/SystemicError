Trump's White House is sitting on report warning of heightened threats in the US: report

The White House has blocked the release of a joint intelligence bulletin warning state and local authorities of elevated terror threats stemming from Trump’s military assault on Iran, according to the Daily Mail.

A five-page report compiled by the FBI, Department of Homeland Security, and the National Counterterrorism Center was scheduled for Friday release but has been indefinitely held up by Trump officials.

The classified bulletin detailed “elevated threats by the government of Iran to US military and government personnel and facilities, Jewish and Israeli institutions and their perceived supporters, and Iranian dissidents and other anti-regime activists in the United States.”

Titled “A Public Safety Awareness Report: Elevated threat in the United States during US-Iran conflict,” the document warned: “Radicalized individuals with a variety of ideological backgrounds also may see this conflict or other geopolitical events as a justification for violence.”

The five-page assessment provided specific operational details on how Iranian proxies could execute attacks across the country, along with guidance for local law enforcement response protocols.

Homeland Security broke protocol by alerting the White House hours before the bulletin’s scheduled release. Trump officials immediately ordered it placed on hold.

The White House did not deny the suppression. Spokeswoman Abigail Jackson offered a defensive explanation: “The White House is coordinating closely with all government agencies to ensure information being disseminated is accurate, up to date, and has been properly vetted — even if that means taking additional time to review to ensure nothing is done in a vacuum.”

A senior DHS official directly contradicted the White House account, stating: “The three [agencies] were going to release a joint intelligence statement that would elevate the threat level and start addressing the Iranian threat on American soil. The White House stopped it, and verbalized down to DHS that any unclassified ‘for official use only’ information going forward concerning Iran has to be reviewed by the White House before any dissemination.”

Our Analysis:

White House Suppresses Joint Intelligence Bulletin on Iran Threats: A Closer Look

The latest maneuver from the Trump administration underscores a troubling pattern of interference with the national security apparatus. According to the Daily Mail, a joint intelligence bulletin warning of elevated terror threats linked to Trump’s military actions against Iran has been blocked from release by White House officials. This decision not only highlights a concerning level of political interference in security matters but also raises serious questions about the administration’s commitment to public safety.

Political Interference over Public Safety

The classified bulletin, a collaborative effort by the FBI, Department of Homeland Security, and the National Counterterrorism Center, was shelved indefinitely by Trump officials, despite its critical insights into potential threats on U.S. soil. It detailed “elevated threats by the government of Iran” targeting various entities within the United States, including military and government personnel, Jewish and Israeli institutions, and Iranian dissidents. Importantly, it also flagged the risk of attacks by “radicalized individuals with a variety of ideological backgrounds” using the U.S.-Iran conflict as a pretext for violence.

The suppression of this bulletin is not just an act of censorship; it’s a dangerous gamble with national security. By prioritizing political optics over the dissemination of vital intelligence, the Trump administration is effectively keeping state and local authorities in the dark about potential security threats, thereby hamstringing their ability to prepare and respond effectively.

Rationale or Excuse?

The White House’s defense, articulated by spokeswoman Abigail Jackson, insists that the delay is to ensure the accuracy and thorough vetting of the information. However, this justification falls flat against the backdrop of a senior DHS official’s statement, which directly contradicts the White House’s account. The official revealed that the directive to hold the bulletin was coupled with an order that any future unclassified information regarding Iran must undergo White House review prior to dissemination.

This directive not only smacks of an attempt to control the narrative but also undermines the autonomy and expertise of the nation’s top security agencies. It’s a clear indication that, for the Trump administration, managing public perception is more critical than addressing and communicating genuine security concerns.

Conclusion: A Disturbing Trend

The decision to block the release of the joint intelligence bulletin is emblematic of a broader and deeply troubling trend within the Trump administration: the politicization of national security. Such actions not only erode trust in the government’s ability to protect its citizens but also jeopardize the very security apparatus designed to safeguard the nation.

The suppression of vital security information for political gain is a reckless strategy that prioritizes the administration’s image over the safety and well-being of the American public. It’s a stark reminder of the need for vigilance and accountability at all levels of government, particularly when the stakes involve national security and public safety.

In the end, the American people deserve transparency, especially when it comes to matters as critical as national security. The Trump administration’s handling of the joint intelligence bulletin is a systemic error that cannot and should not be overlooked.
