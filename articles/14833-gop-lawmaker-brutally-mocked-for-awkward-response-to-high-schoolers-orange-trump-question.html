GOP lawmaker brutally mocked for awkward response to high schooler’s 'orange' Trump question

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosMay 16, 20251 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Image via Screengrab / X.

Rep. Brian Jack (R-Georgia) recently addressed a group of high school students in Washington, DC, and he was caught off guard when one of them asked a question about President Donald Trump's physical appearance.

The student asked Jack, "Why is he so orange?"

The GOP congressman responded, "Uh, you know what? It, it's your perspective. It isn't certainly mine. I just think he has a great tan."

Jack's response is receiving a lot of responses on X, formerly Twitter.

Drummer and progressive X user Richard Angwin tweeted, "Why dodge the question? Trump's orange hue is a public fact, not a perspective. Maybe focus on policy instead of makeup choices?"

X user Maxwell Black joked, "Because he likes to ah peel."

Another X user, David Twerkowitz, posted, "Do they think they don't have eyes? His face looks like where the Mississippi meets the ocean."
