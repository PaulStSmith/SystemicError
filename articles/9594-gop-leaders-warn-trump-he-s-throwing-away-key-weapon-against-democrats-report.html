GOP leaders warn Trump he's throwing away key weapon against Democrats: report

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosOctober 14, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Our Analysis:Analysis: The Strategy and Fallout of Government Shutdown Tactics

U.S. President Donald Trump holds up a signed executive order banning transgender girls and women from participating in women’s sports, in the East Room at the White House in Washington, U.S., February 5, 2025. REUTERS/Leah Millis

As the government shutdown rolls into its second week, some Republicans are not happy that Donald Trump took away one weapon they had hoped to use to get Democrats to bend to their will.

According to a report on Monday from Politico, there is little light at the end of the tunnel as the government shutdown rumbles on, with the GOP leadership now saying it will no longer let Democrats call for their own stopgap spending bill to fund the government until the end of the month — instead allowing only Republican alternatives.

That means, Politico wrote, that Republicans will be sticking to their hardball tactics in the hope that Democrats will be blamed.

But there is also a belief that the president has handcuffed them by taking away what they felt was a major motivator that could get Democrats to budge.

The report noted, “The GOP fear is that if the military pay deadline passes without action, there will be little to stop the shutdown from continuing for several more weeks at least. Some Republicans have privately warned the White House that taking unilateral action to pay service members would deprive the party of a key lever to make Democrats feel overwhelming consequences for their refusal to act on a House-passed spending bill.”

Trump has ordered the Pentagon to use all means necessary to divert money to meet service members’ payroll during the shutdown, while other government workers — those who have not lost their jobs — will be left hanging this week.

According to Sen. Kevin Cramer (R-ND), “It’s a compelling reason to open the damn government ... the troop deadline is the issue — if anything becomes an inflection point, it’s that.”

Politico reported, “The GOP effort to force Democrats to heel comes as talks between the top four congressional leaders remain virtually nonexistent. And there’s no sign that rank-and-file Senate Democrats — just five of whom could quickly end the shutdown — are ready to flip ahead of another scheduled vote on the House-passed stopgap Tuesday night.”

Our Analysis:

Analysis: The Strategy and Fallout of Government Shutdown Tactics

In the intricate chess game of political power plays, the current government shutdown reveals a bold, yet morally ambiguous strategy by the Republican party, underscored by a deep-seated willingness to leverage essential services and workers’ pay as a bargaining chip. The Politico article sheds light on a tension-filled narrative that unwraps layers of strategic maneuvers, blunders, and the potential human cost of political deadlock.

The GOP’s Hardball Approach: A Double-Edged Sword

The Republicans, as depicted in the article, appear to be playing a high-stakes game, opting for what can only be described as hardball tactics. By refusing to allow Democrats to propose their own stopgap spending bill, they not only tighten the vice on negotiations but also underscore a troubling readiness to let the shutdown drag on, hoping the blame falls squarely on the Democratic camp.

However, this approach is fraught with risk and reveals a myopic vision. It operates under the assumption that the public will blame the Democrats for the shutdown, a gamble that neglects the possible blowback against the GOP for perceived intransigence and manipulation of essential services as leverage.

Trump’s Controversial Directive: A Tactical Misstep?

President Trump’s decision to order the Pentagon to divert funds to ensure military payroll continues amidst the shutdown exemplifies a unilateral approach to problem-solving. While on the surface, it may seem like a move to protect service members’ financial stability, it paradoxically undermines the Republican negotiation position. By removing what GOP leaders considered a “major motivator” for Democratic concession, Trump inadvertently may have weakened his party’s bargaining power.

This action, while possibly well-intentioned towards the military, highlights a lack of strategic coherence within the Republican ranks. It raises questions about the efficacy and unity of their approach to the negotiations, potentially emboldening the Democratic position.

The Human Cost and Political Fallout

At the heart of this political brinkmanship are the countless government workers and their families who bear the brunt of the shutdown. The distinction made by Sen. Kevin Cramer (R-ND) about the compelling nature of the “troop deadline” subtly acknowledges the critical impact of the shutdown on individuals’ livelihoods. It inadvertently highlights a troubling hierarchy of value placed on different categories of government workers, with military personnel deemed more worthy of receiving their pay on time than other government employees.

This aspect of the shutdown not only reveals a moral dilemma but also a potential political miscalculation. As the shutdown drags on with “little light at the end of the tunnel,” the public’s patience may wear thin, with both parties suffering damage to their reputations. However, the GOP, by virtue of their hardline stance and tactical errors, may find themselves bearing a disproportionate share of the blame.

Conclusion

The ongoing government shutdown and the strategies employed by the GOP, as reported by Politico, underscore a high-risk political maneuver that could have significant ramifications, both for the party’s standing and the lives of millions of Americans. The decision to prioritize political leverage over the immediate well-being of government workers presents a stark reminder of the often-cynical nature of political negotiations. As this deadlock continues, the need for a resolution becomes ever more urgent, not just for the sake of political expediency, but for the real lives caught in the balance.
