'You lied!' Pam Bondi buried by MAGA after announcing new charge dismissal

A Saturday announcement by Attorney General Pam Bondi that she is dismissing charges against a doctor charged with helping people avoid Covid vaccines did not earn her much in the way of applause from supporters of Donald Trump.

Instead, she was raked over the coals over the Jeffrey Epstein files debacle that has led to a war within the Department of Justice she oversees.

With Bondi facing scrutiny from both sides of the aisle for defending the DOJ for trying to move on from the case of accused pedophile Jeffrey Epstein, her attempt on X to change the subject fell flat on Saturday.

Donald Trump's AG wrote, "At my direction @TheJusticeDept has dismissed charges against Dr. Kirk Moore. Dr. Moore gave his patients a choice when the federal government refused to do so. He did not deserve the years in prison he was facing. It ends today."

Getting Moore off the hot seat was a priority for Rep. Marjorie Taylor Greene (R-GA) who recently wrote, "I am writing a letter to the DOJ asking all charges be dropped against Dr. Kirk Moore. who is facing thirty five years in federal prison for destroying thousands of vials of COVID-19 vaccine, giving his patients vaccine cards without taking the shots, and injecting saline into children whose parents wanted them to believe they got vaccinated without risking the deadly side effects. This man is a hero, not a criminal."

Bondi complied, but her announcement opened the door for MAGA fans furious about the missing Epstein client list that had been promised to them.

Logtime Trump supporter and frequent X-poster Gunther Eagleman suggested, " You only caved because of pressure let’s not forget that. Can you stop opining on social media and stick to official press releases? You have already degraded your position enough."

"You have underestimated the negative impact of your handling of the Epstein case. No “good news” you share is going to distract from this reality. We the Trump base have zero confidence in your ability to fulfill your duties. The honorable thing to do would be to resign," wrote LizaM.

Trump-supporting influencers the Hodgetwins responded with a terse "Now release the damn Epstein list."

Elon Musk fan Mr. Pitbull predicted, "If Pam Bondi remains Attorney General, none of the Deep State traitors like Leticia James, John Brennan, James Comey, Fauci, Adam Schiff, or AOC are ever going to jail, much less prosecuted. Not to mention the Dan Bongino, Kash Patel fallout! She has to go! That look....."

"When are you resigning?!!!!! We can’t lose Bongino and Kash because you’re lying about the Epstein files," accused Jammies.

"I expected good things from you but the Epstein debacle has damaged your credibility immensely. Nobody will let it go," added another Trump fan.

Our Analysis:

The Bondi Debacle: A Masterclass in Misplaced Priorities

In a stunning display of political myopia, Attorney General Pam Bondi has managed to alienate herself from virtually every corner of the political arena. Her recent decision to dismiss charges against Dr. Kirk Moore, a figure controversial for his actions aimed at helping people avoid COVID-19 vaccines, has ignited a firestorm of criticism. Yet, this act, intended as a diversion from her mishandling of the Jeffrey Epstein files, has only served to intensify scrutiny and outrage.

A Failed Attempt at Distraction

Bondi's announcement, made with the intention of shifting focus, fell spectacularly flat. In her words: "At my direction @TheJusticeDept has dismissed charges against Dr. Kirk Moore...He did not deserve the years in prison he was facing. It ends today." This statement, rather than mollifying her critics, only opened the floodgates of condemnation, particularly from the MAGA camp, which had already been vociferously demanding accountability over the Epstein files debacle.

The Epstein Shadow

The Epstein case, a symbol of systemic failure and elite impunity, remains a haunting specter over Bondi's tenure. Attempts to "move on" from accusations against the convicted pedophile and the mystery of the missing client list have not only failed but backfired, drawing the ire of even the staunchest Trump supporters. Critics like Gunther Eagleman and LizaM accuse Bondi of caving to pressure, degrading her position, and fundamentally failing to fulfill her duties. The calls for her resignation underscore a profound disillusionment with her handling of a case that continues to epitomize the deepest flaws in the justice system.

The Moore Controversy: A Disturbing Heroization

The case of Dr. Kirk Moore adds another layer of controversy. Rep. Marjorie Taylor Greene's defense of Moore as a "hero" for his actions, which include destroying thousands of vials of COVID-19 vaccine and deceiving parents and their children about vaccinations, is not only misguided but dangerous. It reflects a troubling trend of heroizing individuals who undermine public health efforts during a global pandemic. Bondi's compliance with Greene's request does not merely reflect a poor judgment call; it signifies a disturbing alignment with reckless anti-science rhetoric.

The Verdict from MAGA Country

The response from MAGA supporters and influencers has been swift and unforgiving. The Hodgetwins' terse demand for the release of the Epstein list, and Mr. Pitbull's bleak prediction regarding Bondi's inability to bring "Deep State traitors" to justice, encapsulate the disillusionment and anger percolating within a base that once saw Bondi as an ally. This sentiment is punctuated by accusations of lies about the Epstein files and demands for Bondi's resignation, highlighting a profound breach of trust.

In Conclusion: A No-Win Situation

Pam Bondi's attempt to navigate the treacherous waters of political scandal has left her stranded in a no-win situation. Her efforts to divert attention from the Epstein debacle have not only failed but have compounded her problems, alienating supporters and critics alike. The Moore decision, far from being a saving grace, has become yet another albatross around her neck. As the chorus for her resignation grows louder, one thing becomes clear: in the court of public opinion, Bondi's credibility has been irreparably damaged. The saga serves as a stark reminder of the consequences of misplaced priorities and the perils of underestimating the public's demand for accountability and justice.
