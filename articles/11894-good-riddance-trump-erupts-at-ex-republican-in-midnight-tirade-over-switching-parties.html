'Good riddance!' Trump erupts at ex-Republican in midnight tirade over switching parties

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosAugust 9, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

U.S. President Donald Trump speaks before departing for Scotland, on the South Lawn at the White House in Washington, D.C., U.S., July 25, 2025. REUTERS/Kent Nishimura

President Donald Trump lashed out at Georgia's former lieutenant governor Geoff Duncan after he switched parties.

Duncan has been an outspoken critic of the former president since he attempted to overturn his 2020 election loss in Georgia and elsewhere, and this week he announced that he was leaving the Republican Party to become a Democrat, and Trump heaped scorn on the former elected official.

"Failed former Lt. Governor Geoff Duncan, of Georgia, is a total loser," Trump posted Friday at 1:08 a.m. EST. "Was never able to get anything done, all he ever did was complain. We didn’t want him in the Republican Party any longer, so I’m told he became a Democrat. Good riddance Geoff. You don’t even have a chance!!!"

Duncan, who served as lieutenant governor from 2019 until 2023, said he's considering a run for higher office after switching parties, and there has been speculation in Georgia that he might launch a campaign for governor to succeed Gov. Brian Kemp, who cannot run for re-election due to term limits.

"My journey to becoming a Democrat started well before Donald Trump tried to steal the 2020 election in Georgia," Duncan wrote in the Atlanta Journal-Constitution announcing his party switch. "There’s no date on a calendar or line in the sand that points to the exact moment in time my political heart changed, but it has. My decision was centered around my daily struggle to love my neighbor, as a Republican."

Our Analysis:

Geoff Duncan's Party Switch: A Political Earthquake or a Desperate Bid for Relevance?

In a political landscape that often feels more like a reality TV show, the latest episode features former Georgia Lieutenant Governor Geoff Duncan and his public break-up with the Republican Party. In what can only be described as a dramatic turn of events, Duncan has decided to switch sides, trading in his GOP badge for a Democratic one. But is this move a genuine political evolution, or merely a desperate grasp at remaining relevant in a rapidly changing political environment?

Donald Trump's Predawn Twitter Tirade: A Sign of Fear or Dismissal?

"Failed former Lt. Governor Geoff Duncan, of Georgia, is a total loser," blasts Trump in a 1:08 a.m. tweet that reeks of both desperation and disdain. This isn't just a casual jibe; it's a calculated move to discredit Duncan before his political rebranding can gain any traction. Trump's nocturnal social media escapades have long been a source of both amusement and alarm, but this particular outburst reveals more than just personal vendetta—it underscores the threat Duncan's defection poses to the Trumpian vision of the GOP.

Duncan's Defection: A Moral Awakening or Political Opportunism?

Duncan's announcement in the Atlanta Journal-Constitution paints his departure from the Republican Party as a journey of personal and political enlightenment. "My journey to becoming a Democrat started well before Donald Trump tried to steal the 2020 election in Georgia," Duncan proclaims, attempting to frame his switch as a moral stand rather than a tactical maneuver. However, one cannot help but question the timing and motivations behind this so-called awakening. Is Duncan genuinely disillusioned with the GOP's trajectory, or is he simply aligning with what he perceives as the winning side in a post-Trump era?

The Political Implications: A Realignment or a Ripple?

Duncan's flirtation with a gubernatorial run as a Democrat is intriguing, to say the least. With Governor Brian Kemp hitting term limits, the race for Georgia's governorship is wide open. Duncan's switch could signal a significant realignment within Georgia's political landscape, or it could merely be a ripple in the state's tumultuous political waters. The question remains: can a former Republican lieutenant governor successfully reinvent himself as a Democratic gubernatorial candidate in a state that has been a battleground for some of the most contentious political fights in recent memory?

Conclusion: A New Chapter or the Same Old Story?

Geoff Duncan's party switch is a bold move, but it's far from clear whether it will be a game-changer or simply another footnote in the saga of American politics. His claim of a gradual drift away from the Republican Party due to its recent actions and his supposed moral epiphany might resonate with some voters. Yet, others will undoubtedly view this move with skepticism, if not outright cynicism.

In the end, Duncan's political future will depend not just on his ability to convince voters of his newfound Democratic credentials, but also on whether he can offer a compelling vision that distinguishes him from both his Republican past and his Democratic competitors. Until then, Duncan's defection is less about the seismic shifts in political allegiance and more about the personal ambitions of a politician navigating the treacherous waters of American politics.
