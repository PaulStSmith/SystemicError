Raw Story reported on the “bombshell ruling,” in which Judge Karin Immergut, a Trump appointee, ruled that Trump’s National Guard call-up to Portland was based on false claims about unrest in the liberal city.

That ruling made waves on X.

Liberal commentator Ed Krassenstein said, “BREAKING: U.S. District Judge Karin Immergut (appointed by Trump) has issued a temporary restraining order blocking the administration’s attempt to deploy 200 Oregon National Guard soldiers to Portland.”

“A Trump Appointee!!” he then added. “She says this is executive overreach.”

Dem Senator Chris Murphy flagged a particular quote from the opinion:

“’This is a nation of Constitutional law, not martial law,’ wrote U.S. District Judge Karin Immergut, a Trump appointee,” Murphy wrote.

Conservative commentator Bill Kristol chimed in, “Judge Immergut is a Republican and a 2019 Trump appointee.”

Writer Nancy Rommelmann said, “I was at the courthouse yesterday when the state argued to halt the deployment to ICE of the National Guard.”

“I sensed Judge Immergut (a Trump appointee) would agree, mostly because the gov’t didn’t put forth any evidence that ICE thought help was necessary,” she added.

The popular Protect Kamala Harris account wrote, “NEW: Federal Judge Karin Immergut has blocked Trump’s deployment of troops in Portland, Oregon. RETWEET to thank Judge Immergut for standing up for our democracy!”

Oregon Attorney General Dan Rayfield celebrated the legal win, saying Saturday, “The court agreed with our position. Today’s ruling is a healthy check on the president. It reaffirms what we already knew: Portland is not the president’s war-torn fantasy.”

“Our city is not ravaged, and there is no rebellion,” he then added. “The president must demonstrate an actual threat based on real facts if he wants to put the National Guard on our streets. Members of the Oregon National Guard are not a tool for him to use in his political theater.”

Our Analysis:

The Irony of Trump’s Own Appointee Shooting Him Down

In what can only be described as a delicious twist of fate, Donald Trump found himself rebuffed by a judge of his own making. Judge Karin Immergut, an emblem of Trump’s own judicial appointments, has boldly declared that Trump’s call to deploy the National Guard in Portland was not only unfounded but dangerously overreaching. This ruling, as reported by Raw Story, isn’t just a slap in the face; it’s a bombshell that underscores the profound disjunction between Trump’s narrative and the reality on the ground.

The Essence of the Ruling

Judge Immergut’s decision is not merely a legal rebuke; it’s a stark reminder of the principles that govern the United States. When she states, “This is a nation of Constitutional law, not martial law,” she isn’t just making a legal point; she’s making a moral one. The decision to block the deployment of 200 Oregon National Guard soldiers to Portland, based on what she deemed executive overreach, sends a clear message: The rule of law will not be casually trampled upon, even by the highest office in the land.

The Reaction Spectrum

The reactions to this ruling, predictably, span the width of the political spectrum, yet they share a common thread of disbelief and vindication. Liberal commentator Ed Krassenstein’s emphasis on Immergut being a “Trump Appointee!!” captures the irony perfectly, while Dem Senator Chris Murphy and conservative commentator Bill Kristol highlight the principled stance of Immergut against the backdrop of her political affiliations. The celebration by the Oregon Attorney General Dan Rayfield, emphasizing Portland’s resilience and rebuking the portrayal of the city as a “war-torn fantasy,” encapsulates the local sentiment against Trump’s narrative.

The Underlying Issue

At the heart of this controversy lies Trump’s portrayal of Portland as a city in chaos, necessitating federal intervention. This narrative, as Judge Immergut’s ruling highlights, was not rooted in evidence but in political theater. Nancy Rommelmann’s observation at the courthouse—that the government failed to present evidence that ICE thought help was necessary—speaks volumes about the administration’s approach to governance: act first, justify later.

A Healthy Check on Presidential Power

What this ruling represents is a healthy check on presidential power, a reminder that the United States is governed by laws and evidence, not the whims of its leader. As the “Protect Kamala Harris” account aptly puts it, thanking Judge Immergut for “standing up for our democracy” isn’t just about celebrating a legal victory. It’s about recognizing the importance of judicial independence and the critical role it plays in maintaining the balance of power.

Conclusion: A Nation of Laws

This episode, while perhaps a minor footnote in the grand scheme of Trump’s presidency, is emblematic of a larger truth: No one is above the law. The fact that a Trump appointee was the one to underscore this truth is not just ironic; it’s a powerful testament to the enduring strength of the American judicial system. In the face of executive overreach, it’s a system that remains, as ever, committed to the principles of justice, evidence, and the rule of law.
