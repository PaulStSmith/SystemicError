Trump's FTC accused of hatching revenge plot to crush media ratings firm

A new lawsuit alleges that President Donald Trump’s Federal Trade Commission is trying to run a media ratings company out of business in retaliation for it giving poor marks to outlets that support the Republican Party.

According to The Washington Post, NewsGuard, founded in 2018 by two former media executives, “researches news sites and assigns them ‘reliability ratings’ based on their journalistic standards, selling these scores to readers, tech platforms and advertisers that want to steer clear of low-quality sources. The company, which insists it is rigorously nonpartisan, has made enemies in recent years by giving low ratings to conservative outlets such as the right-wing cable news channels Newsmax and One America News.”

In their lawsuit, NewsGuard alleges that the FTC has undertaken multiple moves designed to crush the company’s business as an act of political revenge.

“Last May, Trump’s FTC launched an investigation of NewsGuard, ordering the company to hand over the names of all its clients, virtually every document and communication it has ever produced related to its news ratings, and all of its financial reports since the time of its founding, the company alleges,” said the report. “Then, before approving a $13 billion merger that created the world’s largest ad agency, the FTC included an order that effectively blocks the newly formed goliath from ever doing business with companies like NewsGuard.”

These measures, the lawsuit said, are a violation of the First and Fourth Amendments comparable to “the witch hunts of the McCarthy era,” and a reflection of right-wing FTC chair Andrew Ferguson’s crusade to “brazenly [use] its power not for any issue concerning trade or commerce, but rather to censor speech.”

This comes after federal courts sharply rebuked the FTC last year for an investigation into the liberal watchdog group Media Matters for America, allegedly for colluding with advertisers to block tech billionaire Elon Musk’s X platform from receiving ad revenue. Media Matters had triggered several advertiser withdrawals from the site by revealing to the public how frequently the ads were being displayed next to antisemitic and neo-Nazi content.

Our Analysis:

The FTC’s Controversial Crusade: A Deep Dive into Alleged Political Retribution

In what could be described as a chilling narrative straight out of a political thriller, we’re witnessing an audacious move by the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) under the Trump administration that reeks of vindictiveness and a blatant disregard for the foundational principles of free speech and press. The recent lawsuit filed by NewsGuard, a media ratings company known for its commitment to nonpartisan assessment of news outlets, against the FTC is not just a legal battle; it’s a glaring spotlight on the dangerous intersection of politics and the free market’s regulatory mechanisms.

The Allegations: A Closer Look

NewsGuard’s lawsuit paints a damning picture of the FTC’s actions. The demand for exhaustive details about its operations, including client lists, all communications regarding news ratings, and comprehensive financial reports since its inception, is not just overreaching—it’s a draconian attempt to intimidate and possibly dismantle an organization whose only “crime” is its dedication to truth and journalistic integrity.

The inclusion of a clause in a $13 billion merger, explicitly designed to isolate NewsGuard by preventing the world’s largest ad agency from doing business with them, is not just punitive; it’s an economic stranglehold intended to suffocate NewsGuard’s operations. This maneuver is not merely regulatory overreach; it’s a calculated strike against a perceived political adversary.

The Right’s FTC Chair Andrew Ferguson: A Political Pawn?

The actions attributed to FTC chair Andrew Ferguson suggest a disturbing politicization of what should be an impartial federal agency. Describing these moves as reminiscent of “the witch hunts of the McCarthy era” is not hyperbole when you consider the targeted nature of this investigation and its potential implications on free speech and press.

The FTC’s mandate is to protect consumers and promote competition, not to engage in political vendettas. Yet, here we are, witnessing what appears to be an egregious abuse of power aimed at censoring or punishing media outlets based on their political leanings or the content they produce.

Historical Parallels and Warnings Ignored

This isn’t the first time the FTC has been accused of overstepping its bounds for political reasons. The investigation into Media Matters for America, under similar pretenses, was met with sharp rebuke from federal courts. It’s alarming that these prior judicial admonitions seem to have done little to temper the FTC’s enthusiasm for politically motivated investigations.

The Bigger Picture: A Threat to Democracy

At the heart of this lawsuit is a fundamental question about the role of government in regulating media and speech. When regulatory agencies can wield their power to silence or punish media outlets based on their editorial content or political affiliations, we tread on dangerous ground. It’s a direct assault on the principles of free speech, a free press, and the very essence of democracy.

The actions of the FTC, as alleged by NewsGuard, go beyond mere regulatory oversight. They represent a sinister form of political censorship, one that should alarm not just media outlets but every citizen who values the principles of freedom and democracy.

In Conclusion

The lawsuit filed by NewsGuard against the FTC is a critical moment for press freedom and the integrity of our democratic institutions. It’s a stark reminder of the need for vigilance against the abuse of power by those who seek to manipulate the free market and the free exchange of ideas for political gain.

As this legal battle unfolds, it’s essential to remember what’s at stake: the very pillars of democracy that allow for a diverse, vibrant media landscape free from undue political influence. The FTC’s actions, as alleged, are not just an attack on NewsGuard but an affront to the democratic values we hold dear. It’s a wake-up call to all who cherish freedom, liberty, and justice.
