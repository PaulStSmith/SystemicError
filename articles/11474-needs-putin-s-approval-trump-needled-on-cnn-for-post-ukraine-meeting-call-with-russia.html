'Needs Putin's approval': Trump needled on CNN for post-Ukraine meeting call with Russia

U.S. President Donald Trump stands with Russian President Vladimir Putin, as they meet to negotiate for an end to the war in Ukraine, at Joint Base Elmendorf-Richardson in Anchorage, Alaska, U.S., August 15, 2025. REUTERS/Kevin Lamarque

President Donald Trump told reporters in the Oval Office on Monday that, following his meeting with eight European leaders —including Ukraine’s Volodymyr Zelensky — he plans to speak with Russian President Vladimir Putin. As one CNN commentator quipped, the arrangement made Trump appear as if he were seeking Putin’s approval.

CNN host Boris Sanchez brought the matter up to Kimberly Dozier, a global affairs analyst who previously reported on intelligence and counterterrorism for The Associated Press.

"Yeah," Dozier said, drawing out the word and putting her hands on her hips. "The fact that he feels he's got to check in with Putin right away and that — just as this meeting is taking place, we get news that Putin is saying, by the way, no NATO troops on the ground in Ukraine. That is unacceptable."

She noted that it still "feels like Trump needs Putin's approval in a way that's got to be very disturbing to Zelensky sitting there. But of course, Zelensky was a good actor. He's playing it cool. He's not biting on any of the questions he got."

Hopefully, she added, the European officials will tell Trump that it's time to put "troops on the ground inside Ukraine and that it's not Vladimir Putin's business."

Our Analysis:

Trump's Allegiance: A Dance for Putin's Approval

In a recent spectacle that unfolded in the Oval Office, President Donald Trump's declaration of intending to speak with Russian President Vladimir Putin immediately after meeting with eight European leaders, including Ukraine's Volodymyr Zelensky, has sparked more than a few eyebrows to raise in incredulity. The situation, as keenly observed and commented upon by CNN analysts, paints a rather disconcerting picture of Trump’s foreign policy posture—one that seems, at best, to be seeking Putin's nod of approval or, at worst, acting upon his behest.

The Unsettling Subtext

Kimberly Dozier, a seasoned global affairs analyst, didn't mince her words when highlighting the crux of this diplomatic oddity. Her observation—that Trump's rush to confer with Putin, especially juxtaposed against the backdrop of Putin's stark warning against NATO troops' presence in Ukraine, reeks of a peculiar desperation for Putin's endorsement—is both telling and troubling.

Let's dissect this further. The message sent to the international community, and more specifically to Ukraine, is alarmingly clear: Trump appears more invested in placating Putin than standing in solidarity with European allies or supporting Ukraine's sovereignty against Russian aggression. This dynamic is disturbingly reminiscent of a supplicant seeking validation, a stance grossly unbefitting for the leader of the world's foremost democratic superpower.

Zelensky's Diplomatic Tightrope

The article also sheds light on Volodymyr Zelensky's predicament, trapped in a geopolitical tug-of-war. Despite the undercurrents of tension and the potentially enormous implications for his country, Zelensky’s composure was nothing short of commendable. Playing it cool in the face of probing questions, he demonstrated a level of diplomatic finesse and restraint many seasoned politicians might envy. Yet, beneath this calm exterior, one must wonder about the whirlwind of concerns and strategic calculations about Ukraine's precarious position on the global chessboard.

A Call to Action Misdirected?

Dozier's hopes that European officials might coax Trump into a more assertive stance against Russian intimidation tactics, specifically through the deployment of NATO troops within Ukraine, underscores a critical point. The decision-making autonomy in matters of international security and the defense of democratic integrity should not, under any circumstances, be outsourced to the Kremlin's whims. That Trump’s actions might suggest otherwise is not only a profound misstep but a glaring indictment of his administration's foreign policy philosophy.

In Conclusion

The article, through its nuanced critique and analysis, lays bare the uncomfortable realities of international politics under Trump’s administration. It paints a picture of a presidency seemingly caught in a web of Putin's weaving, struggling to assert its independence and protect the interests of its allies. The implications of such a dynamic are far-reaching, affecting not just the immediate stakeholders but the global order at large.

As observers and analysts, our role is to continue scrutinizing these developments with a critical eye, ensuring that the discourse remains grounded in the principles of sovereignty, democracy, and international solidarity. The scene described in the Oval Office is a stark reminder of the complexities and challenges facing global diplomacy today. It's a narrative that demands our attention, our understanding, and, most importantly, our unequivocal critique.
