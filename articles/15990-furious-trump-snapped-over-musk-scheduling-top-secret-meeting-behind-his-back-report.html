Furious Trump snapped over Musk scheduling top secret meeting behind his back: report

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosApril 18, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Donald Trump (Photo via Reuters)

A blindsided Donald Trump angrily swore and demanded White House staffers intercede to keep Elon Musk from being given a briefing on China from top-ranking Pentagon officials reports Axios.

Two weeks ago, the Wall Street Journal reported that the president was blindsided by the news that the billionaire was about to be welcomed by Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth for a meeting on possible China war plans in March.

Now Axios is reporting that Trump killed the meeting himself and then was forced to do damage control after the story was leaked before the meeting which has since led to two aides to Hegseth being put on leave.

ALSO READ:'All hands on deck': Democrats unleash new strategy to derail Trump

The report notes that it was not Hegseth who called off the detailed briefing, but instead Trump after a furious outburst.

"What the f--- is Elon doing there? Make sure he doesn't go," Trump reportedly demanded of his aides, with Axios reporting, "The planned Pentagon briefing, however, got him [Musk] cross with the boss at the Resolute Desk."

The report adds one insider admitted, "POTUS still very much loves Elon, but there are some red lines. Elon has a lot of business in China and he has good relations there, and this briefing just wasn't the right thing."

Our Analysis:

Trump's Tantrum: A Tale of Ego, Elon, and Espionage

In a spectacle that could only unfold in the highest echelons of American power, we find ourselves dissecting a narrative that is as bizarre as it is troubling. Donald Trump, a figure whose presidency was perpetually marred by controversy, apparently threw a fit worthy of a Shakespearean drama upon learning that Elon Musk, the billionaire with as many ambitions as controversies, was slated for a Pentagon briefing on China. This episode, as reported by Axios, sheds light not just on the personalities involved, but on the intricate dance of power, influence, and national security.

The Outburst
Let's set the scene: Trump, upon hearing that Musk was about to receive a briefing from top Pentagon officials on China, reportedly unleashed a tirade of fury. "What the f--- is Elon doing there? Make sure he doesn't go," he demanded, according to Axios. This reaction is emblematic of Trump's tenure; a blend of impulse, insecurity, and the incessant need to micromanage narratives, especially those that intersect with national security and personal relationships.

The Cancellation
It wasn't Pete Hegseth, the Defense Secretary, who called off the meeting. No, it was Trump himself, acting in the aftermath of his own tempest. This decision, while framed as a protective measure for national interests, reeks of personal vendetta and the prioritization of ego over statecraft. The subsequent damage control, leading to aides being put on leave, is a classic move in the Trump playbook—punish the messengers and tighten the circle.

The Elon Factor
Elon Musk, a figure as polarizing as he is influential, sits at the center of this debacle. With vast business interests in China, Musk represents a complex figure in the arena of U.S.-China relations. The insider's remark that "POTUS still very much loves Elon, but there are some red lines," speaks volumes. It hints at the balancing act between personal admiration and the pragmatic considerations of national security and foreign policy. Musk's involvement in this narrative is a stark reminder of how individual corporate interests and personal relationships can complicate the landscape of global diplomacy.

The Underlying Issues
This episode is emblematic of a broader issue within Trump's administration: a chaotic approach to governance where decisions are often made impulsively, without regard to their long-term implications on national security or international relations. It also raises questions about the role of private individuals and business leaders in matters of state, especially when those individuals have significant interests in foreign countries.

Conclusion: Lessons in Governance

This incident is more than just a tale of tempers and titans. It's a cautionary tale that underscores the need for steady, informed, and principled leadership in matters of national security. The whims of an individual, no matter how powerful, should not dictate the strategic interests of a nation. Moreover, it highlights the precarious nature of involving business magnates in diplomatic and military affairs, especially when those magnates have vested interests that could conflict with national objectives.

As we reflect on this bizarre chapter, it's crucial to remember the stakes involved. National security, foreign policy, and the intricate dance of international relations are far too important to be left at the mercy of ego-driven outbursts. It's a lesson in governance, a stark reminder of the need for stability, foresight, and a governance style that transcends personal vendettas and embraces a broader vision for the country and its place in the world.
