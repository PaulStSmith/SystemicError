The Kids Aren’t Just All Right — They’re Doing the Adults’ Job

There’s a familiar lament echoing across generations: “Kids these days…” It’s usually followed by a litany of lazy clichés about attention spans, TikTok brain rot, and how young people supposedly don’t know how the world works. Yet every now and then, reality storms the gates and hands the megaphone not to the pundits, not to the self-appointed moral guardians, but to a kid who slices through political rot with the precision of a scalpel.

Enter Tristan Ryan Perkins — a 13-year-old who just detonated the conservative narrative that today’s youth are apathetic, uninformed, and oblivious to politics. Tristan’s viral commentary isn’t “cute.” It’s a cold indictment of how much the adults in power have failed, how deeply the rot has spread, and how desperately the country needs a generation untainted by the cowardice and corruption that defines the MAGA era.

This isn’t a story about precocity. It’s a story about necessity.

When entire branches of government roll over for an aspiring autocrat, when the political class shrugs at corruption, when millions of adults treat politics like a team sport instead of a fight for their own lives — of course a kid had to stand up and do the job they wouldn’t.

A Thirteen-Year-Old With More Civic Sense Than Half of Congress

Tristan doesn’t ramble. He doesn’t deflect. He doesn’t play the coy “both sides” game that cable news anchors cling to like a security blanket. He goes straight for the jugular: Trump’s threats to invade Mexico, the weaponized bigotry, the border wall delusion, the tariffs, the historical theft of Mexican land — and then juxtaposes it with Mexico’s consistent humanitarian response to U.S. disasters.

While Republican leaders preen about “loving America,” it’s a kid who understands what actual values look like.

It’s a hell of a thing when a middle-schooler exhibits more moral clarity than elected officials who collect six-figure salaries to look the other way.

A Generation Done With Gaslighting

Tristan isn’t having any of the MAGA melodrama. When he calls Trump “the leak in the roof,” he’s doing more than making an analogy: he’s tearing down the bad-faith argument conservatives weaponize against anyone who dares to criticize the country.

Fixing a home isn’t hate. Fixing a country isn’t treason. But abandoning your moral compass to appease a strongman certainly is.

Young voices like Tristan’s aren’t “radical.” They’re simply un-poisoned. They haven’t been marinated in 30 years of right-wing propaganda that teaches people to turn their brains off the moment a demagogue promises vengeance.

When a Child Understands Consent, Power, and Accountability Better Than the GOP

Then there’s the Epstein section — and this is where the kid doesn’t just show clarity. He shows courage. Adults recoil from these topics; Tristan dissects them with a sober, tragic understanding far beyond his years.

He recites a list of Trump’s accusers — a list longer than the number of trans college athletes Republicans claim are an existential threat to the republic. That comparison alone should have shattered the GOP’s entire moral panic machine. Instead, it took a kid to say what major networks are too timid to articulate.

Tristan’s commentary lays bare the grotesque inversion of priorities in American politics:

  • The GOP will wage legislative warfare against a handful of marginalized athletes…

  • …while cheering a man with more assault allegations than they have coherent policies.

When a 13-year-old can see through this hypocrisy, what’s the excuse of the voters who still can’t?

The Harshest Indictment: The Grown-Ups Failed

If you want a grim snapshot of America’s civic decay, here it is:

  • A child perfectly understands that Trump is a menace to democracy…

  • …and yet millions of adults with voting power either can’t or won’t.

Adults who refuse to vote.
Adults who vote out of spite.
Adults who choose ignorance because critical thinking is too much work.
Adults who decided that cruelty is the point.

Tristan sees it clearly because the consequences will land on his generation’s neck — not on the Boomers who torched the future for tax cuts, not on the Gen X cynics who chose nihilism over responsibility, not on the Millennials exhausted from 20 years of political whiplash.

It’s the kids who will have to clean up the mess.

No wonder they’re angry. No wonder they’re loud. They have every right to be.

Project 2025: A Villain’s Blueprint Exposed by a Teenager

While mainstream journalists tiptoe around Project 2025 like it’s Voldemort’s diary, this kid spells it out without hesitation. He lays bare its strategy: manipulate Trump, fracture the nation, then install a puppet (J.D. Vance) to carry out a blueprint for authoritarian government.

A child sees it plainly.
Media executives still debate whether calling it “authoritarian” is too impolite.

This is the real tragedy: the institutions that should be safeguarding democracy are asleep at the wheel, and it falls to a teenager to ring the alarm.

The Backlash: Fragile Adults Losing Their Minds Online

Predictably, the moment Tristan gained visibility, the MAGA trolls descended. Their attacks follow the same tired script: projection, ignorance, rage, and an absolute inability to debate ideas on their merits.

They call it “indoctrination” whenever a young person refuses to regurgitate right-wing talking points. But indoctrination isn’t what we’re seeing. What we’re seeing is education — something MAGA voters abandoned the moment critical thinking became inconvenient.

Their panic over a well-informed teenager says more about them than about him. Authoritarians fear youth because youth isn’t yet conditioned to accept cruelty as normal.

A New Generation Is Watching — And Tallying the Receipts

Tristan’s generation grew up in the shadow of Trumpism. For most of their lives, American politics has been defined by corruption, disinformation, and authoritarian flirtation. They’ve seen rights attacked, institutions eroded, and democracy placed on life support.

Far from being apathetic, they’re taking notes. And they’re furious.

This rising cohort isn’t just “more progressive.” They’re more informed. More globally aware. More diverse. More intolerant of bigotry. More allergic to lies.

And they’re coming.

Gen Z already voted in record numbers. Gen Alpha will follow — and if Tristan is a preview, they won’t be asking permission to lead. They’ll simply take the wheel from adults asleep at it.

The Kids Are Ready. The Question Is Whether Democracy Will Survive Long Enough for Them to Inherit It.

You can only fail a generation for so long before they stop waiting for you to fix the world and start fixing it themselves. Tristan isn’t an anomaly; he’s a signal flare. He represents a cohort raised not by the broken promises of institutions but by the harsh clarity of lived experience.

If American democracy collapses, it won’t be because the kids didn’t care. It’ll be because too many adults preferred fantasy to responsibility.
But if democracy survives, it will be because this next generation refused to let the country sink without a fight.

They are already doing what too many adults refused to do: speak the truth plainly, confront power unapologetically, and refuse to normalize cruelty.

The kids aren’t the future.
They’re the rescue mission.
