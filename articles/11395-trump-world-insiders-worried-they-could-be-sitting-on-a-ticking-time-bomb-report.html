Trump world insiders worried they could be sitting on a 'ticking time bomb': report

There are growing fears within Trump world that the economic path Donald Trump is taking the country down is poised to go south now that the impact of his tariffs is starting to take effect.

According to a report from Wired’s Jake Lahut, the tariffs combined with the roundup of immigrants, which is impacting the labor pool, has created uncertainty that has supporters of the president wary that his “experiment” in driving the economy may not pan out.

Writing, “Trump has put almost all the ingredients in place for slow growth amid high unemployment and inflation, the potent combination known as stagflation,” Lahut added that a coming “tariff hike on Chinese goods, now set to jump from 30 percent to 80 percent by November 10” has some Republicans in Trumpworld’s orbit “bracing for impact.”

“I’m probably surprised that there has not been more concern,” a Republican strategist and former Trump administration official explained. “I think the reality is that we’re at that sort of inflection point, where retailers were reluctant to raise prices because they feared retaliation from the administration.” They added, “Now the reality is setting in that these are not transitory. There are going to be economic consequences.”

Noting that a combination of the tariffs, which are hitting U.S. consumers’ pocketbooks already, and rising unemployment, is creating a “ticking time bomb,” one Trump strategist admitted, “If this experiment fails, it’s gonna fail horribly, and I think we’ll begin to see the impacts of that sooner than later.”

According to James Angel, a finance professor at Georgetown University, “All signs look pretty pessimistic on the inflation front. You don't have to be a rocket scientist to figure out that tariffs will increase the prices we pay for imported goods. No amount of spin will change that.”

He added, “The economic chaos with on-again, off-again tariffs has caused business and consumer expectations to drop. That in itself is likely to cause a recession.”

Our Analysis:

The Tariff Tangle: Trump's Economic Experiment on the Brink

In a recent article from Wired, Jake Lahut dives into the heart of a looming economic crisis, one brewed in the cauldron of Trump's administration. The concoction? A blend of tariffs and immigration crackdowns that's beginning to sour faster than milk in the sun. Lahut's analysis points to a stark reality: Donald Trump's economic strategies might be leading the country straight into an economic downturn, potentially marked by the dreaded combination of stagflation - slow growth, high unemployment, and inflation.

Tariffs: A Double-Edged Sword

At the core of this issue are the tariffs, particularly the impending hike on Chinese goods from 30 percent to a staggering 80 percent by November 10. This move has caused ripples of concern, even among Trump's staunchest supporters. The Republican circle, once unwavering in their confidence, now seems to be "bracing for impact." The troubling aspect here is not just the tariffs themselves but the apparent lack of foresight in their implementation. As one Republican strategist lamented, the reality is now setting in that these measures are not temporary but have long-term economic consequences.

The Labor Pool Drain

Equally troubling is the impact of Trump's immigration policies on the labor pool. The article touches on how the roundup of immigrants is causing uncertainty, further complicating the economic landscape. This isn't just about filling jobs; it's about the broader implications for industries reliant on immigrant labor and the potential ripple effects on the economy at large.

Stagflation: The Looming Threat

The term stagflation is thrown around quite a bit in economic discussions, but here, it's more than just theoretical. Lahut suggests that Trump has "put almost all the ingredients in place" for such a scenario. This isn't a matter of if but when. The combination of tariffs hitting consumers' wallets, coupled with rising unemployment, creates what one Trump strategist described as a "ticking time bomb."

James Angel, a finance professor at Georgetown University, cuts through any attempts at spin, stating plainly that tariffs will increase prices for imported goods, a fact that no amount of political maneuvering can alter. Moreover, the "economic chaos" spurred by on-again, off-again tariffs is setting the stage for a recession, driven by falling business and consumer expectations.

Conclusion: A Harsh Reality Check

The article from Wired, as analyzed here, paints a grim picture of the current state and potential future of the U.S. economy under Trump's guidance. It's a stark reminder that economic policy cannot be treated like a game of chess where you can reset the board after a bad outcome. The consequences are real and felt by every American, from the consumer to the business owner.

The real question now is not whether Trump's economic experiment will fail - but rather, how severe the fallout will be. As the tariffs begin to bite and the labor market tightens, the U.S. stands on the precipice of an economic downturn that could have been avoided. It's a lesson in the importance of thoughtful, informed policy making - and a warning of the dangers of its absence.
