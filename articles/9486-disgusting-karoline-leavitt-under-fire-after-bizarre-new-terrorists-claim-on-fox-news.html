'Disgusting': Karoline Leavitt under fire after bizarre new 'terrorists' claim on Fox News

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosOctober 18, 20255 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

White House press secretary is drawing criticism after alleging that members of the Democratic Party are mainly terrorists, undocumented immigrants and violent criminals.

Speaking to Fox News on Thursday, Karoline Leavitt claimed, “The Democrat Party’s main constituency is made up of Hamas terrorists, illegal aliens, and violent criminals.”

Republicans have spent weeks alleging that the murder of conservative Charlie Kirk was prompted by allegations that Republicans were fascists. They have demanded that Democrats condemn such allegations and stop comparing them to Nazi leader Adolf Hitler.

“But Dems should really tone down their rhetoric…” said publisher of The Bulwark, Sarah Longwell, sarcastically.

“Remember when they pretended to want to ‘turn down the temperature?’” asked Human Rights Campaign press secretary Brandon Wolf.

“This s--t is so f---ing dangerous and everyone on the Republican side just nods along,” said former Barack Obama strategist Dan Pfeiffer.

“This is INCREDIBLY dangerous framing and should make the hair on the back of your [neck] stand, whether you’re a Republican, Democrat, or Independent. And it’s also patently false,” said veteran fighter pilot Carey Lohrenz.

Democratic digital strategist Ally Sammarco told Leavitt, “You’re disgusting and a liar.”

“Now say all that again from the Qatari airbase in Idaho,” quipped filmmaker Jeremy Newberger, referencing the news from Secretary Pete Hegseth that the United States will “build a Qatari Emeri air force facility at the Mountain Home Airbase in Idaho.”

“Disgusting bulls--t,” said public speaker and journalist John Harwood.

Senate Majority PAC’s Lauren French recalled, “Chattering class lost its goddam mind over Clinton’s ‘basket of deplorables’ comment. This - like so much of the White House’s most egregious behavior - will be overlooked as just how they talk, political theatre.”

“They dropped their whole ‘trans terrorist’ thing because it got laughed at in focus groups btw,” said journalist Ari Drennen.

Venture Beat executive editor Carl Franzen suggested the Democratic Party sue her for slander.

“’Deplorables’ was a scandal for years and she wasn’t even in the government yet. This is the White House Press Secretary, whose salary is paid by the American public,” noted American Immigration Council senior fellow Aaron Reichlin-Melnick, recalling Hillary Clinton’s comment about Trump having supporters who are a “basket of deplorables.”

“Really grotesque that @PressSec is trying to distract from the Nazis running parts of her party by lying. ESPECIALLY since her boss is the guy who prioritized releasing hundreds of violent criminals on his first day on the job,” wrote national security expert Marcy Wheeler.

“How is this anything other than deliberate incitement that risks leading to deadly violence?” asked government affairs VP Dylan Williams at the Center for International Policy.

Our Analysis:

A Dangerous Escalation in Political Rhetoric:

In an era where political discourse often seems to have hit rock bottom, White House Press Secretary Karoline Leavitt’s comments to Fox News represent a new nadir. Alleging that the Democratic Party’s main constituency is “made up of Hamas terrorists, illegal aliens, and violent criminals” isn’t just a descent into the absurd; it’s a leap into the perilously irresponsible. This isn’t merely political theater; it’s a dangerous and reckless distortion of reality that has serious implications for the fabric of American society.

Bold Claims, Baseless Foundations:

Leavitt’s comments are not just inflammatory; they are a masterclass in how not to engage in political discourse. To suggest that a major American political party primarily consists of terrorists and criminals is not only patently false but a gross misrepresentation that only serves to deepen divisions. It’s an insult to the intelligence of the American public and a grave disservice to the principles of democracy.

The Hypocrisy of “Turning Down the Temperature”:

The irony of Republicans demanding Democrats to “condemn” comparisons to fascists and then turning around to make such baseless allegations is rich — a stark example of the pot calling the kettle black. The call for Democrats to tone down their rhetoric, coming from the same corners that now host these egregious statements, is hypocrisy of the highest order. It seems the temperature is only a concern when the heat is directed at them.

The Dangerous Consequences of Reckless Rhetoric:

Critics of Leavitt’s comments, ranging from former Obama strategist Dan Pfeiffer to veteran fighter pilot Carey Lohrenz, rightly emphasize the danger such rhetoric poses. This isn’t just about political disagreement; it’s about creating a climate where people are dehumanized and violence becomes more thinkable. When high-ranking officials engage in such blatant misinformation, it doesn’t just stay within the confines of political debate; it seeps into society, potentially inciting violence and widening the chasm between Americans.

The “Deplorables” Double Standard:

The reaction (or lack thereof) to Leavitt’s comments when compared to the uproar over Hillary Clinton’s “basket of deplorables” remark is telling. Clinton’s comment, made during a campaign, faced widespread criticism and became a focal point of the 2016 election. In contrast, Leavitt’s far more derogatory and unfounded statement seems to be brushed off by many as just another day in the office. This double standard exposes a troubling acceptance of extreme rhetoric, provided it comes from certain quarters.

Conclusion: A Call for Accountability and Civility:

The response from various sectors, calling out the dangerous nature of Leavitt’s comments, is a crucial reminder that such rhetoric should not and cannot become normalized. The suggestion by Venture Beat executive editor Carl Franzen that the Democratic Party should sue for slander underscores the gravity of Leavitt’s baseless allegations. This isn’t about stifling free speech; it’s about holding individuals accountable for spreading dangerous falsehoods that threaten the very fabric of our society.

The descent into such baseless and inflammatory rhetoric marks a dangerous pivot in political discourse. It’s incumbent upon all political leaders and their representatives to engage in fact-based, constructive dialogue. Democracy thrives on debate and disagreement, but it is imperiled by lies and unfounded accusations. The path forward requires a return to civility, truth, and a shared commitment to the common good. Anything less is a disservice to the American people and the democratic principles upon which the country was founded.
