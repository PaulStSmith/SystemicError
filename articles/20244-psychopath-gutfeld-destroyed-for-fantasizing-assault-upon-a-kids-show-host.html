'Psychopath’ Gutfeld destroyed for fantasizing assault upon a kid’s show host

Fox News Turns Child Cruelty Into a Punchline

The People With Power

Greg Gutfeld is not a random loudmouth in a bar. He is a Fox News host speaking from one of the most powerful propaganda platforms in American media. The story’s real power center is not Ms. Rachel, a children’s entertainer who advocates for immigrant children and war-traumatized children. It is the network that gives Gutfeld a national microphone and the political culture that rewards him for using it to normalize contempt.

What He Said, What He Meant

The reporting centers on Gutfeld’s attack on Ms. Rachel after she supported Palestinian children, visited immigrant detention centers, and brought children’s letters to Washington. His response was not an argument. It was a fantasy about violence, framed as mockery, aimed at a woman associated with care, children, and empathy.

That matters. This is not “edgy commentary.” It is a public figure converting cruelty into entertainment and dressing it up as political insight.

The Real Target Is Sympathy

Ms. Rachel’s offense, in the logic of this media ecosystem, is that she makes suffering visible. She speaks for immigrant children. She acknowledges Gaza. She refuses the lie that pain only counts when it belongs to the right kind of victim.

That is why the attack lands where it does. The point is not to debate immigration policy or child welfare. The point is to punish empathy itself, especially when it crosses the border of the approved in-group. Gutfeld’s language tries to recast compassion as naïveté, then recast violence as common sense.

Scapegoating As Policy Adjacent Entertainment

Pennacchia’s response gets to the core: this kind of rhetoric makes it easier to justify locking children up by painting undocumented people as predators first and human beings never. The source also notes the Trump administration detains elementary school students and asylum-seeking makeup artists, not the criminal caricatures Fox wants its audience to imagine.

That is the pattern. First come the distortions. Then comes the moral panic. Then comes the cage. The cruelty is not incidental to the politics. It is the mechanism that makes the politics usable.

Institutional Cowardice On Display

Fox News does not accidentally host this stuff. It profits from it. Gutfeld’s segment depends on a broader institution willing to launder sadism as satire and misogyny as personality. The article also points out the irony that Fox itself has a record of dangerous conditions for women, which makes the performative outrage even thinner.

So the issue is not one commentator losing his temper. It is a media machine that trains audiences to laugh at dehumanization while pretending it is just opinion. That is how authoritarian habits spread in plain sight: by making the audience feel clever for accepting what should disgust them.

The Pattern

This story is about what happens when power gets to define empathy as weakness and child suffering as a culture-war prop. It is a familiar conservative move: attack the caregiver, protect the institution that cages children, and call the whole thing honesty.
