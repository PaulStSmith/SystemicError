Head, meet desk: how one Republican posted GOP red-scare idiocy for everyone to see

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosDecember 11, 20254 min read

When Rep. Ann Wagner (R-MO) found out that radical communists from California were trying to meddle with the critical gerrymandering efforts of Missouri Republicans, she sprang into action.

Wagner took to X — platform formerly known as Twitter — to let it be known that she wasn’t about to stand for any of that. Here’s what she posted:

Help fuel the work. Independent analysis doesn’t fund itself:
☕ Buy us a coffee

Now, that’s the kind of bold response we need more of in the country, by God. The last thing Missouri should stand for is to allow California puppets of George Soros to be pushing us around.

There was one small problem, however, with Wagner’s righteous indignation.

Just a detail.

It turns out that she had her Californians a little mixed up.

You see, the offensive post from the California Democrat was referencing a gathering of concerned citizens at the California City Hall Railroad Park right down there on 500 Oak Street in the fine little town of California, Missouri.

Population 4,458, nestled in beautiful Moniteau County, 24 miles west of Jefferson City.

As the great Gilda Radner’s legendary Saturday Night Live character Emily Litella would have said: “Never mind.”

In Wagner’s defense, both the state of California and the town of California, MO are indeed located to our west. The little town is about 140 miles from St. Louis County, conveniently on the way toward the West Coast.

And it turns out that California Democrat is the name of the hometown newspaper, not a political organization in Gavin Newsom’s west coast den of inequity.

That’s easily confused, isn’t it?

And who can blame Wagner for her outrage anyway? If I might really add fuel to the fire, it turns out that the Missouri citizens who gathered at the town railroad park were organized, at least in part, by the American Civil Liberties Union (ACLU).

Now, if I didn’t happen to be a past president of the ACLU chapter, let me tell you, I’d really go off on the danger posed by this sort of thing.

But Mr. Soros won’t permit me to do that.

In any event, kudos to HuffPost reporter Jennifer Bendery, who grabbed a screenshot of Wagner’s tweet.

For some reason, Wagner has since deleted it.

I don’t know why Wagner would memory-hole the thing.

California needs to butt out of our elections. Right, Emily?

Our Analysis:

The Misguided Missile of Missouri’s Political Theater

In an era where political discourse often teeters on the brink of the absurd, Rep. Ann Wagner’s (R-MO) recent escapade stands out as a textbook example of the farcical missteps politicians are prone to in their zeal to appear vigilant against perceived threats. Wagner’s reaction to what she believed was Californian interference in Missouri’s gerrymandering saga is more than just a blunder; it’s a glaring testament to the knee-jerk reactionary politics that plague our system.

Wagner’s Wild West Confusion

At first glance, Wagner’s fervent outcry against the “radical communists from California” meddling in Missouri’s redistricting efforts might evoke a nod of approval from those who view external interference with suspicion. However, Wagner’s bold stance quickly crumbles under the weight of reality when it’s revealed that her Californian bogeymen were none other than residents of California, Missouri—a small town within her own state.

The irony is almost too rich to digest. The “California Democrat” that got Wagner’s gears grinding is not a left-wing cabal from the Golden State but a local newspaper serving a community of 4,458 souls. This mix-up is not just a simple error; it’s a stark illustration of how quickly facts can become casualties in the rush to score political points.

The ACLU: A Convenient Scapegoat

Wagner’s misplaced outrage doesn’t stop with geographic mix-ups. The revelation that the gathering she so vehemently opposed was organized, in part, by the American Civil Liberties Union (ACLU), adds another layer of irony to the tale. The ACLU, often demonized by certain factions as a boogeyman of the left, was merely facilitating citizen engagement in the political process—a fundamental democratic principle.

The mention of George Soros as a puppet master is a tired trope, wielded with reckless abandon to stoke fear and division. It’s a baseless assertion that serves as a smokescreen for the real issue at hand: the manipulation of electoral boundaries for partisan gain, otherwise known as gerrymandering.

A Deleted Tweet but an Indelible Mark

Wagner’s decision to delete her tweet does little to erase the embarrassment of the situation. If anything, it highlights a common tactic in modern politics: when confronted with a mistake, erase it from the digital record and hope it fades from memory. Fortunately, diligent reporting ensures that these moments of folly are captured and preserved, serving as a reminder of the perils of jumping to conclusions without due diligence.

Concluding Thoughts

The saga of Rep. Ann Wagner’s indignation and subsequent revelation serves as a microcosm of the broader issues facing political discourse today. It underscores the need for a more thoughtful, fact-based approach to political engagement, where substance triumphs over sensationalism.

As we navigate these tumultuous political waters, let us remember the importance of accuracy, the value of understanding the issues at hand, and the necessity of keeping our discourse grounded in reality. Only then can we hope to address the systemic errors that undermine our democracy.
