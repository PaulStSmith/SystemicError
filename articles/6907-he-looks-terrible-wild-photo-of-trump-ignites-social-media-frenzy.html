'He looks terrible': Wild photo of Trump ignites social media frenzy

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosJanuary 7, 20262 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

U.S. President Donald Trump gestures as he makes an impression of a transgender weightlifter during his address to House Republicans at their annual issues conference retreat at the Kennedy Center, renamed the Trump-Kennedy Center by the Trump-appointed board of directors, in Washington, D.C., U.S., January 6, 2026. REUTERS/Kevin Lamarque

Internet critics sounded off Tuesday after President Donald Trump was caught in an unflattering press photograph while delivering a speech to House Republicans at the Kennedy Center.

The picture, taken in the middle of a rant about transgender women athletes, caught him pantomiming a woman weightlifter struggling to compete; he is seen flailing his arms, eyes bulging and tongue protruding, as the light unflatteringly captured his face makeup to give him a bronzish-green complexion.

The image quickly went viral on social media, with a number of commentators reacting to it with varying degrees of hilarity, disgust, and general embarrassment for the country.

“This is a real image,” wrote New Yorker staff writer Susan Glasser.

“He looks terrible. Yellow,” wrote former Politico foreign policy reporter Laura Rozen.

“This is a real photo of Donald Trump. A national and global embarrassment,” wrote liberal influencer Harry Sisson.

“Trump is a disgusting human being,” wrote former CNN White House correspondent John Harwood.

“This is a man who cannot lift himself out of a chair without using both hands; alternately, he rarely stands and lately, when he stands, he falls asleep standing,” said writer Larkin Warren. “Betting he can’t lift anything heavier than a Big Mac.”

“The irony being that Trump went out of his way to praise the White House photographers this morning,” wrote The Bulwark’s Sam Stein.
