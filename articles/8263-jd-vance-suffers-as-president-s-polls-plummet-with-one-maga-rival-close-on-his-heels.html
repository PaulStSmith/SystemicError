'JD Vance suffers' as president's polls plummet – with one MAGA rival close on his heels

U.S. Vice President JD Vance attends a Turning Point USA event at the Pavilion at Ole Miss at the University of Mississippi in Oxford, Mississippi, U.S. October 29, 2025. REUTERS/Jonathan Ernst/Pool

New polling shows Vice President JD Vance’s chances for becoming the next Republican presidential nominee have taken a big hit.

The vice president enjoyed a 20-point lead over his chief rival to the MAGA throne – Donald Trump Jr. – in an August poll, but that advantage has been cut in half as the president’s popularity plummets, reported The Independent.

“The issue for Vance is that he’s so closely tied to this administration,” said Mark Shanahan, an associate professor at the University of Sussex. “For the moment, he has to be in lockstep with the president. If he’s not, he simply isn’t doing the job he has been elected to fulfill. So, while Trump’s polling is languishing, Vance suffers.”

Vance held a 36-10 lead over President Donald Trump’s namesake son in a McLaughlin and Associates poll in August and a 38-20 lead over him in October, but the younger Trump narrowed the gap to 34-24 in the most recent poll conducted this month.

The president’s own polling has collapsed in recent months, and every major poll has found he has a negative rating for the first time.

“Even a Fox News poll suggested that 61 percent of respondents think that Trump’s administration has damaged the economy, with just 38 percent approving of his economic strategy,” The Independent reported. “A further 76 percent said that they view the economy negatively, with Fox analysts pointing to the fact that this is ‘worse than the 67 percent who felt that way in July and the 70 percent who said the same at the end of former President Biden’s term.’”

Surveys conducted by YouGov, Gallup, Ipsos, R.M.G, Research, Morning Consult and Quinnipiac found similar negative results for the president, and that unpopularity has dragged down JD Vance but somehow lifted the 79-year-old president’s son.

Our Analysis:

The Political Downfall: Analyzing JD Vance’s Plummeting Polls

In the latest chapter of the Republican saga, Vice President JD Vance finds himself in a precarious position, as recent polling data paints a grim picture of his political future. Once a dominant figure in the race for the Republican presidential nomination, Vance’s lead has been dramatically slashed, illustrating a political landscape in turmoil and a party grappling with its identity.

The Ties That Bind and Break

JD Vance’s Decline: The crux of Vance’s predicament lies in his unwavering alliance with the current administration. As reported by The Independent, Vance’s fortunes are inextricably linked to the president’s waning popularity. Mark Shanahan’s commentary hits the nail on the head: Vance’s role demands loyalty to the administration, yet this very loyalty is the albatross around his neck. In a political twist of fate, Vance’s adherence to duty may very well be his undoing.

The Numbers Tell the Tale

Polling Woes: The stark numbers from McLaughlin and Associates reveal a narrative of decline. From a commanding 36-10 lead over Donald Trump Jr. in August to a narrowed gap of 34-24, Vance’s stronghold on the nomination is slipping through his fingers. The cause? A direct correlation with the president’s plummeting approval ratings. Even a Fox News poll, often seen as sympathetic to the Republican cause, underscores the widespread disillusionment with the administration’s handling of the economy.

The Trump Factor

Donald Trump Jr.’s Unlikely Rise: Amidst the political turmoil, an unlikely beneficiary emerges: Donald Trump Jr. Despite the senior Trump’s own unpopularity, his son appears to be ascending in the Republican ranks, challenging conventional wisdom. This phenomenon signals a complex dynamic within Republican circles, where loyalty to the Trump brand still holds considerable sway despite the administration’s failings.

Economic Discontent and Political Fallout

A Damaged Economy: The consensus across multiple polls is damning. A significant majority view the economy negatively, a sentiment that has only worsened over time. This economic dissatisfaction serves as a critical backdrop to the political narratives unfolding within the Republican Party. As the economic strategy of the administration faces widespread disapproval, figures like Vance find themselves caught in the crossfire, their political fortunes tied to the perceived economic mismanagement.

Conclusion: A Party at a Crossroads

The unfolding drama within the Republican Party is more than a mere political skirmish; it’s a reflection of a party at a crossroads. JD Vance’s faltering campaign is symptomatic of a broader identity crisis, where allegiance to the Trump brand clashes with the realities of governance and public approval. As the party grapples with its future direction, the question remains: Can the Republican Party reconcile its internal divisions and forge a path forward, or will it remain ensnared in the web of its own making?

In the grand chessboard of American politics, figures like JD Vance and Donald Trump Jr. are but pawns in a larger game, their fates intertwined with the shifting allegiances and turbulent currents of their party’s identity crisis. As we observe the spectacle unfold, let’s not lose sight of the systemic issues at play, for they will ultimately define the contours of American political life in the years to come.
