According to a report from ABC News, investigators from the Eastern District of Virginia U.S. attorney’s office interviewed former special counsel John Durham in August seeking dirt on Comey — and he told them in no uncertain terms that he came up with nothing on the former FBI official during his four-year investigation into Russian influence in the 2016 election.

The report noted that Durham, whose appointment Trump supported, told, “federal prosecutors investigating James Comey that he was unable to uncover evidence that would support false statements or obstruction charges against the former FBI director.”

Adding to that, investigators in the Washington, DC office, now headed by former Fox News personality Jeanine Pirro, also had nothing to offer against Comey.

ABC News is reporting Durham’s conclusions raise the prospect that the special counsel, “once elevated by Trump and other Republicans believing he would prosecute high-level officials involved with the investigation of the president’s 2016 campaign -- could now become a key figure aiding Comey’s defense.”

ABC News is also reporting that Halligan was made aware of Durham’s conclusion and nonetheless proceeded before a grand jury, where she got an indictment by a thin margin on two of the three charges she levied against Comey.

According to the report, the combination of multiple offices finding nothing on Comey “could not only bolster the argument that Comey was targeted but also highlight the weaknesses that experienced prosecutors had already identified in the case.

Our Analysis:

The Quagmire of Political Prosecutions: The Case Against Comey

In a twist that reads like a script from a political thriller, the newly minted U.S. Attorney Lindsey Halligan’s case against former FBI Director James Comey appears to be crumbling before it even hits the courtroom. The keystone of this saga is the revelation from ABC News that John Durham, the former special counsel and a figure once heralded by Trump allies for his potential to upend the narrative around Russian influence in the 2016 election, has essentially cleared Comey of the accusations that are currently being leveraged against him.

The Fruitless Pursuit by John Durham

Durham’s investigation, which spanned four years and was tinged with the hope (or rather, the expectation) of Trump and his cohort that it would yield prosecutable evidence against high-ranking officials like Comey, has instead fizzled out. Durham’s unequivocal statement to federal prosecutors that he “was unable to uncover evidence” to support charges of false statements or obstruction against Comey is not just a statement on Comey’s innocence in these matters but a glaring spotlight on the political motivations that seem to underpin this entire endeavor.

The Political Theatre of Jeanine Pirro and Lindsey Halligan

Adding an extra layer of intrigue (or absurdity, depending on one’s perspective) to this scenario is the involvement of Jeanine Pirro, a former Fox News personality now heading the Washington, DC office. The fact that both her office and Durham came up empty-handed in their quests against Comey does not merely suggest a lack of evidence; it screams of a politically motivated witch hunt that has now backfired spectacularly.

Despite Durham’s findings—or perhaps, in spite of them—Halligan proceeded to secure an indictment against Comey on two of the three charges, by a “thin margin.” This action not only raises questions about the integrity of the legal process but also undeniably points to the desperation inherent in pursuing a case so visibly devoid of substance.

The Implications of a Baseless Prosecution

The ramifications of this development extend far beyond Comey’s legal battles. They underscore a disturbing trend of using the judicial system as a tool for political vendettas, a maneuver that undermines the very foundation of justice and impartiality that the system claims to uphold. Durham, once believed to be the harbinger of the downfall of Trump’s perceived enemies, may now inadvertently serve as the linchpin in Comey’s defense, highlighting the precariousness and political bias embedded in these investigations.

The “weaknesses that experienced prosecutors had already identified in the case” against Comey, as noted by ABC News, are not just cracks in the prosecution’s armor; they are gaping holes that expose the lack of a credible case from the start. The persistence in pushing forward with charges, in light of overwhelming evidence to the contrary, is a blatant exhibition of the misuse of legal authority for political ends.

Conclusion: A Systemic Error in the Judicial Machinery

The case against James Comey, as it stands, is a stark emblem of the systemic errors plaguing our judicial and political systems. It’s a cautionary tale of what happens when the lines between justice and political rivalry are not just blurred but obliterated.

This saga serves as a reminder of the importance of upholding the principles of justice, impartiality, and integrity in the face of political pressures. It’s a call to action for citizens and officials alike to critically evaluate and condemn the misuse of the legal system for political gains, ensuring that such baseless prosecutions do not become a staple of our political landscape.
