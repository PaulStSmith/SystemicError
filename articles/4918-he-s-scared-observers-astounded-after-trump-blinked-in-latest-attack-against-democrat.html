President Donald Trump’s latest attack against a top Democrat on Monday night left analysts and observers astounded.

Trump posted on Truth Social that California Gov. Gavin Newsom had dropped out of the 2028 presidential race, a campaign that Newsom has yet to confirm that he is running. The post came after MAGA piled on Newsom for making a comment about his intelligence that they construed to be a negative comment about Black people.

“Wow! Gavin Newscum just dropped out of the Presidential Race!!! President DJT,” Trump wrote on Truth Social.

Analysts and observers reacted to the post on social media.

“Trump just blinked. He’s scared,” journalist Oscar Gonzalez posted on X.

Michael McDonald, a political science professor at the University of Florida, posted on Bluesky that Newsom “can now declare Trump just resigned.”

“Our president’s AI-generated reality continues apace, I guess,” economist Sky Marchini posted on Bluesky.

“He did?!” independent journalist Chris Cilliza posted on X. “Uh....”

Trump’s attacks came as public polling showed the president losing support as the midterms approached. In public remarks on Monday, Trump said polls showing he has an around 40% approval rating are “wrong,” and claimed that his approval is “much higher” without providing evidence.

Our Analysis:

Analyzing Trump’s Latest Political Gambit: The Imaginary Withdrawal of Gavin Newsom

In what can only be described as a bewildering move, President Donald Trump has launched an attack so bizarre, it leaves us questioning the fabric of reality within political discourse. On the battleground of Truth Social, Trump declared that California Governor Gavin Newsom had bowed out of the 2028 presidential race—a race Newsom hasn’t even confirmed he’s running in. This claim is not just unfounded; it’s an outright fabrication, a hallmark of Trump’s political strategy that thrives on chaos and confusion.

The Facts Behind the Fiasco

Trump’s post claims, “Wow! Gavin Newscum just dropped out of the Presidential Race!!! President DJT,” a statement so riddled with inaccuracies and premature conclusions, it’s hard to know where to begin. But let’s dissect this methodically:

  1. Gavin Newsom’s Non-Candidacy: Newsom has not declared any intention to run in the 2028 presidential race. Trump’s announcement of Newsom’s withdrawal from a non-existent campaign is not just premature; it’s a non-sequitur in the political conversation.

  2. The MAGA Reaction: Trump’s attack follows a pattern of seizing on ambiguous statements by political opponents, often twisting them to serve his narrative. The claim that Newsom made disparaging remarks about Black people, taken out of context and weaponized by Trump and his followers, is a tactic we’ve seen repeatedly.

  3. The Response: The reaction from analysts and observers ranges from disbelief to mockery. Oscar Gonzalez’s comment, “Trump just blinked. He’s scared,” suggests Trump’s move is one of desperation rather than strategy. Meanwhile, Michael McDonald’s quip that Newsom can now declare Trump resigned is a sharp retort to the absurdity of Trump’s claim.

The Broader Context

Trump’s delusional post comes at a time when public polling indicates a slip in his support as the midterms approach. His denial of polling data, asserting his approval rating is “much higher” without substantiation, is a classic Trump move—reject reality, substitute your own.

The Implications

This incident is emblematic of a deeper problem in American politics:

  • The Erosion of Truth: When a former president can fabricate political events outright, it underscores the erosion of truth as a shared value in the political arena. Trump’s post isn’t just a lie; it’s an attempt to reshape reality to his advantage.

  • The Power of Social Media: This episode also highlights the power of social media platforms like Truth Social to amplify unfounded claims. The swift spread of Trump’s post and its acceptance by some segments of the population reveal the dangerous potential for disinformation to influence public perception and discourse.

  • The Polarization Effect: Such tactics only serve to deepen the divide in American politics, turning every issue into an us-versus-them battleground. The real casualty here is the possibility of meaningful dialogue and compromise.

Conclusion

Trump’s latest attack on Gavin Newsom is not just a political maneuver; it’s a symptom of a larger disease plaguing American political discourse. The fabrication of Newsom’s presidential race withdrawal is a stark reminder of the challenges facing the nation: an environment where lies are wielded like weapons, truth is a casualty, and the political arena is nothing more than a stage for the theater of the absurd. As observers and participants in this democracy, it’s incumbent upon us to demand better, to sift through the noise for the truth, and to hold our leaders accountable for their words and actions. Only then can we hope to heal the systemic errors eroding the foundations of our political system.
