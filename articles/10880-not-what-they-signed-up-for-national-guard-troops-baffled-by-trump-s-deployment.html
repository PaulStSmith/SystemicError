'Not what they signed up for': National Guard troops baffled by Trump's deployment

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosSeptember 5, 20255 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Members of the National Guard walk near the White House on the National Mall after U.S. President Donald Trump deployed the National Guard and ordered an increased presence of federal law enforcement to assist in crime prevention, in Washington, D.C., U.S., August 21, 2025. REUTERS/Al Drago

With President Donald Trump having deployed the National Guard to patrol Washington, D.C. for weeks as part of his takeover of the district, and lawsuits advancing against it, there is another issue Trump has to contend with, CNN's Jeff Zeleny told anchor Boris Sanchez on Thursday: the rock-bottom morale of guardsmen who were called up only to realize they had little to no purpose.

"Walk us through what you're hearing," said Sanchez.

"Well, Boris ... we have seen these National Guardsmen and airmen around the city, but they are largely spending most of their time in areas where tourists populate on the National Mall, on the Wharf, and other things," said Zeleny. "But we've been talking to a lot of the soldiers and their families, actually, to get an actual sense of what is going on."

"Of course, these members of the National Guard are used to being deployed to emergencies and things across the country, but they're not exactly sure, in some cases, what they're doing here," said Zeleny. "They're spending a lot of time walking around, some time working on beautification projects, but one father of a guardsman in Tennessee told our reporters this. He said, 'I don't know what he thought he would be doing, but I really don't think he thought he would just be walking around for 12 hours every day. When I talked to him, he was just like, yeah, this is crazy.'"

Further, he continued, "one other wife of a guardsman who was deployed here to Washington — again, everyone says that they are willing to serve, but the question is, what exactly is the deployment? And this wife was very interesting as she told our team this: 'It's a little unusual in terms of what the National Guard is usually deployed for, but I mean, I guess I would say I'm open to it. I feel like maybe they would get a little boost in morale if they could just get a little bit more detail.'"

"So that is sort of the question here: what are they doing, necessarily?" he added. "Of course, they're helping to keep the city safe and secure, but they are spending a lot of time walking around and other D.C. National Guard people are cleaning up the parks. And of course, that is not necessarily what they signed up for."

Our Analysis:

The Misuse of the National Guard: A Critical Analysis

In a recent coverage by CNN, a rather disconcerting scenario has unfolded in Washington, D.C., shedding light on President Donald Trump's decision to deploy the National Guard in what appears to be a show of force rather than a necessity. The report by Jeff Zeleny, as discussed with anchor Boris Sanchez, unveils not just a questionable use of military resources but also a concerning disregard for the morale and purpose of the National Guard personnel involved.

Deployed but Not Engaged

At the heart of this issue is the rock-bottom morale among the guardsmen, who were mobilized to patrol Washington, D.C., ostensibly to maintain order and security. However, Zeleny's report paints a picture of disillusionment and confusion among these troops. It's one thing to deploy the National Guard for emergencies and national crises—situations they are trained and prepared for. It's entirely another to have them meandering around tourist hotspots and engaging in beautification projects, activities that hardly require military intervention.

The Voice of the Guardsmen and Their Families

The voices of the guardsmen and their families are particularly telling. They reveal a deep-seated bewilderment at the nature of their deployment. One father lamented the aimlessness of his son's daily routines, highlighting a stark deviation from the guardsman's expectations of duty. Similarly, a wife's commentary underscores the ambiguity surrounding their mission, hinting at a deployment that seems more whimsical than warranted.

These accounts underscore a fundamental disconnect between the administration's decision-making and the actual role and capabilities of the National Guard. It raises a critical question: What is the true purpose of this deployment?

A Misguided Utilization of Resources

This situation points to a broader issue of the misuse of military resources for political posturing. The deployment of the National Guard in such a manner is not just an operational misstep; it's a disservice to the individuals who have committed to serve their country. They are trained for emergencies, for moments when their skills and dedication can genuinely make a difference, not for aimless patrols and park cleanups.

Moreover, this misuse reflects poorly on the administration's respect for military institutions and their purpose. It's a glaring example of how military force can be exploited for appearances rather than substantive security needs.

Conclusion: A Call for Reevaluation

The deployment of the National Guard in Washington, D.C., as described by CNN's report, demands a critical reevaluation. It's imperative to question not just the rationale behind such decisions but also the implications they have on the morale and purpose of our servicemen and women. Military resources are not theatrical props; they are a critical component of our national security and emergency response systems. Their use should be judicious, respectful, and, above all, purposeful.

In this instance, the administration's decision has fallen short on all these counts. It's a systemic error that not only undermines the morale of the National Guard but also erodes public trust in how military resources are utilized. As this situation unfolds, one can only hope for a swift reevaluation of the deployment, ensuring that the National Guard is used in a manner that respects their commitment, skills, and invaluable role in our nation's security infrastructure.
