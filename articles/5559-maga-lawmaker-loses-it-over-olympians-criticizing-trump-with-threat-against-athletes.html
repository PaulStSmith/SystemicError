MAGA lawmaker loses it over Olympians criticizing Trump with threat against athletes

By Paulo Santos

Sen. Rick Scott (R-FL) melted down and threatened Olympians after several athletes representing the United States openly criticized President Donald Trump‘s administration and the current state of America.

Scott delivered a curt response to the Olympic athletes currently competing at the Milan Cortina Winter Games voicing their concerns about their home country in a video message on X posted Sunday night, The Daily Beast reported. It’s unclear which athlete or comment prompted his reaction.

“Representing the United States—a beacon for freedom and democracy—at the Olympics is an honor. Anyone who feels otherwise should be stripped of their USA Olympic uniform,” Scott said.

Several members of Team USA have spoken out against the administration as the country reels from recent violence from ICE agents, including the two public killings of citizens in Minneapolis.

Freestyle skier Hunter Hess, who recently expressed misgivings about ICE killing American citizens, said, “I think, for me, it’s more I’m representing my friends and family back home, the people that represented it before me, all the things that I believe are good about the U.S. If it aligns with my moral values, I feel like I’m representing it. Just because I’m wearing the flag doesn’t mean I represent everything that’s going on in the U.S.”

The statement apparently set off Trump, who took to his Truth Social platform to criticize the athlete.

“U.S. Olympic Skier, Hunter Hess, a real Loser, says he doesn’t represent his Country in the current Winter Olympics,” Trump wrote.

“If that’s the case, he shouldn’t have tried out for the Team, and it’s too bad he’s on it,” Trump added. “Very hard to root for someone like this. MAKE AMERICA GREAT AGAIN!”

Other athletes have denounced the harsh Trump administration policies as the international audience booed Vice President JD Vance during the opening ceremony for the winter games.

“I feel heartbroken about what’s happened in the United States when, you know, I’m pretty sure you’re referencing ICE and some of the protests and things like that,” freestyle skater Chris Lillis told reporters last week. “I think that as a country, we need to focus on respecting everybody’s rights and making sure that we’re treating our citizens as well as anybody with love and respect.

Our Analysis:

The Thin Ice of Political Retribution

In a spectacle that could only unfold in the era of Trumpian politics, Sen. Rick Scott (R-FL) has launched an astonishing attack on Olympians—an attack that reeks not of patriotism, but of petty authoritarianism. The Milan Cortina Winter Games, a stage meant for athletic prowess and international camaraderie, has been tainted by Scott’s chilling warning to athletes who dare criticize the Trump administration or the tumultuous state of affairs in the United States.

The Meltdown

Scott’s meltdown was triggered by a series of athletes expressing their concerns about the current U.S. administration and the recent violent actions by ICE agents, including the public killings of citizens in Minneapolis. His response? A threat to strip any dissenting athlete of their USA Olympic uniform, essentially saying: Silence or be silenced.

The Athletes’ Stand

The athletes, however, stood their ground. Freestyle skier Hunter Hess, for instance, provided a nuanced take on what it means to represent his country. He articulated that wearing the flag does not equate to blind allegiance to everything happening within the U.S. borders. This statement, emblematic of thoughtfulness and moral clarity, was met with disdain by former President Donald Trump, who resorted to his typical playbook of name-calling and MAGA rallying on his Truth Social platform.

The Global Stage

The international stage of the Olympics, where moments of unity and peace are cherished, has instead been used as a battleground for domestic political squabbles. The booing of Vice President JD Vance during the opening ceremony underscores the global audience’s awareness and disapproval of the divisive and harsh policies championed by the Trump administration.

A Missed Opportunity

What Scott and Trump fail to grasp is the power of the Olympics as a moment for reflection and growth. Instead of threatening athletes with punitive measures for speaking out, they could have seized the opportunity to listen and perhaps understand the deep-seated concerns these athletes have about their country—a country they love enough to represent on one of the world’s biggest stages.

The Bigger Picture

The issue at hand goes beyond mere political disagreement. It’s about the right to free speech, the right to critique one’s government, and the fundamental values that the United States purports to stand for: freedom and democracy. By attempting to silence these athletes, Scott and Trump are not defending American values; they are undermining them.

In Conclusion

The chilling effect of Scott’s threats against Olympians cannot be understated. It sends a clear message: allegiance to the administration over allegiance to the principles of democracy and freedom of expression. This is not patriotism; it’s authoritarianism dressed in the guise of national pride. The athletes of Team USA, in their bravery and refusal to be silenced, represent the true spirit of America—one that does not shy away from self-criticism and strives for a more just and inclusive society.
