‘Doesn’t know who he’s talking to’: Newsom scorches Trump over troop deployment

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosJune 11, 20255 min read

California Governor Gavin Newsom looks on as he attends an event, to announce the Golden State Literacy Plan and deployment of literacy coaches statewide, at the Clinton Elementary School in Compton, California, U.S. June 5, 2025. REUTERS/Daniel Cole

California Democratic Governor Gavin Newsom chastised President Donald Trump for claiming he recently spoke with him to discuss his decision to deploy 700 Marines to Los Angeles, in addition to the 4,000 National Guard troops he is sending. A portion of L.A. has been besieged by anti-deportation protests.

Asked by reporters in the Oval Office when the last time was that he spoke to the Governor, Trump paused before replying, “A day ago, called him up to tell him, got to do a better job.”

“He’s doing a bad job, causing a lot of death and a lot of potential death,” Trump alleged (video below). There do not appear to have been any deaths due to the Los Angeles protests.

“If we didn’t send out the National Guard, and last time we gave him a little additional help, you would have Los Angeles would be burning right now. Los Angeles would be not a lot different than what you saw, take place in California, and Los Angeles, just a little while ago.”

But the California Democrat disputed Trump’s claim.

“There was no call. Not even a voicemail,” he wrote on social media.

“Americans should be alarmed that a President deploying Marines onto our streets doesn’t even know who he’s talking to,” said Newsom, leveling charges that appeared to reinforce claims—primarily from the left—that President Trump’s mental competence is in question.

Newsom did speak with Trump, he said over the weekend, but the two did not discuss the National Guard.

“Newsom said he and Trump spoke late on Friday night—about 1.30 a.m. Saturday in D.C.—but Trump never brought up the National Guard,” The Daily Beast reported on Monday.

“We talked for almost 20 minutes and he barely, this issue never came up,” Newsom said on MSNBC. “I tried to talk about L.A., he wanted to talk about all these other issues. We had a very decent conversation.”

But “Newsom slammed Donald Trump as a ‘stone-cold liar’ in an MSNBC interview on Sunday, insisting the president’s angry public posturing doesn’t match the tone he struck during a Friday phone call.”

Others also weighed in.

“This is concerning. Who did Trump think he was speaking to?” asked The Lincoln Project.

“Two very different stories here,” observed NBC News Senior National Political Reporter Natasha Korecki.

Our Analysis:

Analysis of the Article: A Masterclass in Miscommunication or Misdirection?

In a recent showcase of the ongoing political theater, California's Democratic Governor Gavin Newsom and President Donald Trump find themselves embroiled in a clash of narratives that's as bewildering as it is troubling. The discord centers around Trump's claim of a recent conversation with Newsom regarding the deployment of 700 Marines to Los Angeles amidst anti-deportation protests, a claim that Newsom vehemently denies. The situation is rife with contradictions, misstatements, and a glaring lack of evidence, presenting a perfect storm for our analysis.

Trump's Controversial Claims

President Trump, in his characteristic fashion, made bold assertions about the state of Los Angeles, predicting a fiery chaos akin to previous unrest in California, were it not for his intervention. His narrative, however, crumbles under the weight of scrutiny:

  • No Deaths Reported: Trump's alarmist remark about the protests causing "a lot of death and potential death" stands on shaky ground as there appears to be no record of fatalities linked to the Los Angeles protests. This fearmongering tactic is not only misleading but dangerously escalates tensions without basis.

  • The Phantom Phone Call: The centerpiece of this controversy is Trump's claim of speaking to Newsom "a day ago," a claim that Newsom outright denies. This discrepancy raises serious questions about the President's grasp on reality or, perhaps, an intentional obfuscation of the truth. The absence of any record or acknowledgment from Newsom's camp of such a call leads us to question whether this conversation ever took place.

Newsom's Counter-Narrative

Governor Newsom's response to Trump's narrative is a stern rebuke of the President's account. Newsom's insistence that no such call discussing the National Guard or Marines occurred not only contradicts Trump but also paints a picture of a President disconnected from the realities of his own administration's actions.

  • Claims of Mental Incompetence: Newsom's suggestion that Americans should be alarmed at the President's alleged confusion adds a sinister layer to this saga, insinuating that Trump's mental competence might be in question. This is a serious charge that, while grounded in a concerning incident, should be approached with caution and based on a pattern of behavior rather than a single event.

  • A "Stone-Cold Liar": By labeling Trump a "stone-cold liar," Newsom escalates the rhetoric to new heights. This accusation, while reflective of Newsom's frustration, ventures into territory that might detract from the substantive issues at hand, namely the questionable deployment of military forces on American streets and the accuracy of Trump's public statements.

The Wider Implications

This incident isn't just a petty squabble between two high-profile politicians; it's indicative of a deeper malaise affecting American politics. The ease with which misinformation can be spread by those in power, the lack of accountability for false or misleading statements, and the polarization that ensues are symptoms of a systemic failure.

  • Misinformation and Accountability: The stark contrast between Trump and Newsom's accounts highlights a troubling trend of misinformation emanating from the highest levels of government. The absence of a clear, objective truth in this matter undermines public trust in our leaders and institutions.

  • The Role of the Media: The media's reporting on this incident, while necessary, must tread carefully to avoid amplifying unfounded claims. The focus should be on seeking the truth, verifying facts, and holding those in power accountable for their words and actions.

Conclusion: A Call for Clarity and Integrity

As we dissect this bewildering tangle of claims and counterclaims, the need for clarity, truth, and integrity in our political discourse has never been more apparent. The incident serves as a stark reminder of the responsibilities shouldered by our leaders and the media in upholding these values. The American public deserves no less.
