'Even Elon is disgusted': Trump brutally mocked after avalanche of overnight rants

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosJune 5, 20252 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

President Donald Trump on April 14, 2025 (Joey Sussman/Shutterstock.com)

During his first presidency, Donald Trump spent a lot of time posting on Twitter, now the Elon Musk-owned X.com. But during his second term, President Trump is using his own Truth Social as his primary social media outlet. And he used Truth Social to do a lot of ranting late Tuesday night, June 3 and early Wednesday morning, June 4.

In one of his posts, Trump wrote, "With the exception of the RIGGED PRESIDENTIAL ELECTION OF 2020, THE AUTOPEN IS THE BIGGEST POLITICAL SCANDAL IN AMERICAN HISTORY!!!"

In another post, he attacked Sen. Rand Paul (R-Kentucky) for criticizing his "big, beautiful bill," writing, "Rand votes NO on everything, but never has any practical or constructive ideas. His ideas are actually crazy (losers!). The people of Kentucky can't stand him. This is a BIG GROWTH BILL!"
