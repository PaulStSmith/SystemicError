'We had an unbelievable victory today': MAGA host celebrates after MN mass shooting

Pro-MAGA Infowars host Owen Shroyer celebrated what he called an "unbelievable victory" after an alleged transgender person was accused of carrying out a mass shooting that left at least two children dead.

Following the shooting at Annunciation Church in Minneapolis on Wednesday, Shroyer pointed out that the suspect had used the names Robin Westman and Robert Westman.

"I look at this and I'm saying, at this point, obviously, the feds cannot keep this information from the local police, if that's what was going on in the beginning, which I suspect was," Shroyer told Kyle Seraphin, a former FBI agent who had his security clearance revoked during former President Joe Biden's administration. "I don't know how they don't come out in a press conference now and put all this information out, because this is like they've lost, they've lost."

"So, okay, you won the first round. You got a lead-off home run on us because you pretended like you didn't know any of this in the first press conference," he said of law enforcement. "But now we just got up to bat in our half of the inning and we just hit a grand slam... Because, yeah, again, a trans-identified person."

Shroyer noted that mainstream media were reporting the shooter's identity.

"I don't, Kyle, I don't think, I don't think any of this information gets out, A, if it's not for you, quite frankly, B, if it's not for now us on the show today," the host argued. "I don't think any of this information is out. I don't think you're seeing this. Now Fox News is picking it up. Now it is officially mainstream news."

"I think I've probably been on air in Infowars longer than anybody not named Alex Jones," he continued. "I'm telling you, in the last four hours, you just, we just went through a historical moment when it comes to free press, you know, versus institutional cover-up."

Shroyer, who was sentenced to 60 days in jail following the Capitol riot, called his coverage of the shooting "a paramount moment."

"We just went through a paramount moment where the institutional cover-up has been beaten by free independent press," he remarked. "God bless Infowars. Unbelievable victory today. I mean, this is, I, I'm, I'm stunned, Kyle. I can't even believe we just hit back-to-back grand slams in the, in an inning. And now we're going back out onto the mound."

Seraphin, however, was not as jubilant.

"Yeah, it doesn't feel like a win, knowing that there's kids who didn't survive this," he replied.

Our Analysis:

Exploiting Tragedy for Agenda: A Closer Look at Infowars' "Unbelievable Victory"

In a grotesque display of opportunism, Owen Shroyer, a host for the pro-MAGA platform Infowars, declared an "unbelievable victory" in light of a mass shooting carried out by an alleged transgender individual, resulting in the tragic death of at least two children. This incident, which unfolded at Annunciation Church in Minneapolis, is a stark reminder of the lengths to which some media personalities will go to further their own narratives, irrespective of the human cost involved.

The Morbid Celebration

Shroyer's comments, made during a conversation with Kyle Seraphin, a former FBI agent, are not just distasteful; they are indicative of a deeply problematic rhetoric that prioritizes ideological points scoring over empathy and human decency. The shooter's alleged transgender identity was seized upon by Shroyer as a weapon to wield against perceived enemies, namely the mainstream media and, by extension, the broader LGBTQ+ community.

Distortion of Facts for Narrative

The discourse quickly veers into conspiracy territory with implications that there was some form of "institutional cover-up" orchestrated by federal agencies, a claim made without evidence. Shroyer's assertion that this tragedy represents a victory against such a cover-up because the shooter's identity was disclosed is not only baseless but dangerously misleading. It transforms a moment of collective mourning into a spectacle for self-congratulation.

The Irony of Infowars Claiming a 'Historical Moment'

Shroyer's statement that Infowars has triumphed over an "institutional cover-up" through this coverage is laughably ironic, considering the platform's notorious history of spreading misinformation. To claim a "paramount moment" for free press while engaging in such exploitative and sensationalist reporting is a mockery of genuine journalism.

A Sole Voice of Reason

It's worth noting that Seraphin, despite his association with Shroyer in this instance, displayed a moment of clarity and humanity by acknowledging the grim reality: children lost their lives. This acknowledgment stands in stark contrast to Shroyer's ghoulish revelry and serves as a grim reminder of the actual "loss" suffered: the loss of innocent lives, not some contrived battle in a culture war.

Conclusion

The exploitation of tragedy to further political agendas is nothing new, but the brazenness with which Owen Shroyer and Infowars engage in such tactics should alarm us all. At a time when communities should come together to mourn and seek solutions to prevent future tragedies, Infowars has chosen to wallow in the mire of division and conspiracy theories. Their "unbelievable victory" is, in truth, a testament to their moral bankruptcy and a profound disrespect to the victims and their families. This is not journalism; it's opportunism at its most vile.
