Trump ally cashing in on vape firm accused of targeting kids and a brutal junta

Roger Stone’s Real Business Is Impunity

Power, Not Personality

The reporting is straightforward: Roger Stone, a convicted political operative pardoned by Trump, is now collecting millions as a lobbyist for clients that include a vape distributor accused of illegally targeting children, a crypto figure indicted on federal fraud charges, and Myanmar’s military junta. That is the context. The political meaning is sharper: Washington is not being manipulated by Stone from the margins. It is paying him because he still has access to the machinery of power.

The Decision Was the Point

Stone did not drift into this role by accident. Trump’s pardon mattered. So did Trump’s return to the White House. Once political loyalty is rewarded with clemency and access, the market opens for exactly this kind of racket: the convicted become consultants, the disreputable become fixers, and scandal becomes a line item. The source is right to note Stone’s fees, but the deeper story is that the system has made criminality convertible into influence.

Who Benefits From the Blur

The weak framing in stories like this is the temptation to treat Stone as a colorful provocateur, as if his work were somehow detached from the people hiring him. It is not. Ecto World is not paying for vibes. It is paying for protection, normalization, and reach while New York’s attorney general says the company illegally marketed addictive candy- and fruit-flavored nicotine products to children. Stone’s function is to launder intent into process, to move an abuse of the public into the language of lobbying.

The Target Is Always the Vulnerable

This is not abstract corruption. The alleged conduct here is predatory in the most ordinary sense: addictive products, youth targeting, and manufactured demand. The victim is not Washington’s reputation. It is children being turned into customers. When a political operator makes money defending that pipeline, the moral line is not fuzzy. It is drawn by profit.

From Vape Scandal to State Rot

The same pattern appears in the other clients listed in the report: a fraud-indicted crypto promoter and a military junta linked to crimes against humanity. That is not a random client roster. It is a business model built on access to institutions that have stopped enforcing basic democratic and moral boundaries with enough consistency to matter. Stone is not the disease. He is a symptom of a political order that keeps finding room for men whose main skill is selling absolution to the worst bidder.

The Larger Pattern

The real lesson is not that Washington attracts grifters. It is that power without consequence attracts them, funds them, and then asks the public to call it politics. Trump’s pardon culture, the lobbying industry, and the tolerance for organized predation all reinforce the same structure: impunity at the top, harm at the bottom, and a media frame that too often treats the whole arrangement as colorful dysfunction instead of deliberate decay.
