'Art of saying no': Michelle Obama reveals reason for missing Trump’s inauguration

In January, former First Lady Michelle Obama was absent from two major events: President Donald Trump's inauguration on January 20 and the late President Jimmy Carter's funeral on January 9.

Political reporters wondered why she wasn't at either event. But Obama offered some clarification on the April 22 episode of her vodcast.

Obama, according to the Daily Beast's Nandika Chatterjee, would have been sitting next to Trump at Carter's funeral. And she said, on the vodcast, that when she didn't attend those events, she was making the decision that was "right for me." And she addressed claims that she didn't attend because she and former President Barack Obama were having marital problems.

Michelle Obama explained, "People couldn't believe that I was saying no for any other reason; they had to assume that my marriage was falling apart. It took everything in my power to not do the thing that was perceived as right, but do the thing that was right for me. That was a hard thing for me to do."

The former first lady said she had to "basically trick" herself into skipping Trump's inauguration.

“It started with not having anything to wear," Michelle Obama recalled. "I was like, 'If I'm not going to do this thing, I got to tell my team, I don't even want to have a dress ready, right?' Because it’s so easy to just say, 'Let me do the right thing.'"

The former lady also said that she's trying to practice "the art of saying no" — and encourages other women to do the same.

Michelle Obama commented, "I want our daughters, I want the young women out there… I want my girls to start practicing different strategies for saying no. After all that I’ve done in this world, if I am still showing them that I have to keep — I still have to show people that I love my country, that I'm doing the right thing, that I am always setting, going high all the time, even in the face of a lot of hypocrisy and contradiction. All I'm doing is keeping that crazy bar that our mothers and grandmothers set for us.”

Our Analysis:

The Art of Saying "No": Michelle Obama's Masterclass in Personal Integrity

In the intricate dance of political appearances and the unspoken obligations of public figures, Michelle Obama's recent revelations on her vodcast have stirred more than just the surface of political etiquette. The former First Lady's decision to skip two significant events — President Donald Trump's inauguration and the late President Jimmy Carter's funeral — has been a topic of speculation among political circles. However, her candid explanation sheds light on a deeply personal struggle that transcends mere political rivalry or protocol.

The Essence of Choice

Michelle Obama's choice to absent herself from these events, particularly the one where she would have been seated next to Donald Trump, is a powerful assertion of self-respect and personal boundaries. It's a stark reminder that even in the highest echelons of power, the personal autonomy of individuals, especially women, can still be a battleground. Her decision, as she puts it, was "right for me," a simple yet profound statement on doing what aligns with one's values, despite external pressures.

The Myth of Marital Discord The speculation that her absence was due to marital problems with Barack Obama is a classic example of society's eagerness to ascribe personal failings to women who make unconventional choices. It's both laughable and lamentable that her detractors couldn't fathom a woman of her stature making a decision based on personal integrity rather than domestic issues. Michelle Obama's rebuttal to these claims is a scathing indictment of such baseless speculation, emphasizing the need to prioritize one's well-being over public perception.

The Political Tightrope

Michelle Obama's recounting of "tricking" herself to skip Trump's inauguration, starting with not having a dress ready, is a fascinating glimpse into the mental gymnastics often required to navigate the political landscape. It underscores the absurdity of the expectations placed on public figures, where attending events can become a performative act rather than a genuine expression of support or respect.

The Art of Saying "No"

Perhaps the most striking aspect of Michelle Obama's discussion is her emphasis on "the art of saying no" and encouraging women to adopt this practice. In a world where women, especially those in the public eye, are expected to acquiesce to demands and expectations, this is a revolutionary call to arms. It challenges the "crazy bar" set by societal norms and encourages women to assert their agency.

Conclusion: A Lesson in Integrity and Autonomy

Michelle Obama's decision and the rationale behind it transcend political squabbles or celebrity gossip. They touch upon the fundamental right of individuals, particularly women, to navigate their lives according to their principles, free from the tyranny of public expectations. Her candidness is not just refreshing but necessary, serving as a beacon for those struggling to maintain their integrity in the face of societal pressures. In a world quick to judge and slow to understand, Michelle Obama's stance is a powerful testament to the strength of character and the importance of personal autonomy.
