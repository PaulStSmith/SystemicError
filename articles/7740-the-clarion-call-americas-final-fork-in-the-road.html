The Clarion Call: America’s Final Fork in the Road

By Paulo Santos

For decades, the question “Are you better off than you were four years ago?” served as America’s political compass. It wasn’t ideological. It wasn’t partisan. It was a gut check — a simple measure of whether the country was moving forward or backward. And historically, it worked. When times were good, incumbents won. When they weren’t, power changed hands.

Then came 2024 — the year the compass shattered.

Because by every measurable, material, empirical standard, Americans were better off in 2024 than they were in 2020. Not marginally. Dramatically.

But the truth no longer mattered.

The right-wing propaganda apparatus — a monster engineered over decades — convinced millions to doubt their own reality, rewrite their own memories, and vote against their own interests. And the result was catastrophic: the return of a man who entered Election Day already an adjudicated rapist and a 34-count convicted felon.

This wasn’t politics. This was national self-harm.

And now that the consequences have arrived — loudly, rapidly, and predictably — it’s time to lay out exactly how we got here, and why 2026 may be the last exit ramp before authoritarianism slams shut behind us.

I. 2020: The Abyss We Should Never Forget

The early months of 2020 were a national trauma scarred into the country’s collective psyche. Under the disastrous stewardship of the Trump administration, the federal government treated a global pandemic as a PR inconvenience rather than the public health emergency it was. Experts were sidelined. Science was mocked. Supplies were hoarded or withheld for political gain.

And Americans died.

Not quietly, not abstractly, but visibly — so visibly that cities resorted to storing bodies in refrigerated trucks because morgues were overflowing.

This is the baseline against which 2024 should have been measured. This is the “then” in the question “Are you better off now?” And yet, through a perfect storm of disinformation and weaponized resentment, millions were persuaded that the hellscape of 2020 was somehow preferable to the stability and recovery of 2024.

II. 2021–2023: The Recovery Sabotaged

The incoming administration inherited a burning crater where the federal government used to be. And yet — despite obstruction, judicial warfare, and coordinated sabotage — the country clawed its way back.

  • Unemployment plunged.

  • Wages rose.

  • Inflation, a global phenomenon, receded.

  • The stock market stabilized.

  • COVID deaths dropped massively.

  • Small businesses reopened.

  • Supply chains untangled.

For most Americans, life was not just better; it was fundamentally restored.

But propaganda doesn’t care about data. It manufactures emotion. It implants resentment. It distorts memory. And it did so with brutal efficiency.

By 2023, the right-wing media machine had accomplished what seemed impossible: convincing a critical mass of voters that the world they were living in was somehow worse than the nightmare of 2020.

III. 2024: The American Electorate Falls for the Con Again

On paper, 2024 should have been a rout. A convicted felon with multiple civil judgments for sexual assault and defamation should not have been a contender for dogcatcher, let alone President. But Trump was never selling governance — he was selling grievance.

And for a country saturated in resentment, marinated in conspiracy, and trained to distrust its own institutions, grievance won.

Kamala Harris warned the country explicitly — almost prophetically — about what a second Trump term would bring:

  • Autocratic overreach.

  • Attacks on civil liberties.

  • Weaponization of federal agencies.

  • Economic destabilization.

  • Emboldened foreign adversaries.

  • Erosion of democratic norms.

  • Institutional corruption on a scale not seen in modern American history.

Every warning was dismissed, mocked, or drowned in noise.

And now, every warning is coming true.

IV. 2025: The Bill Comes Due

The moment Trump slouched back into the Oval Office, the country was yanked out of the campaign’s fantasyland and dropped straight into the consequences. Nothing that unfolded was unpredictable. It was the logical conclusion of electing a man whose entire political philosophy boils down to vengeance, graft, and personal impunity.

And the wreckage began immediately.

Economic Stability Shattered

  • Markets jolted awake to the reality that chaos was back in charge.

  • Investment retreated, jittery and uncertain.

  • Federal economic policy became erratic, improvisational, and vindictive.

Healthcare Under Assault

  • Protections were gutted.

  • Costs rose.

  • Access shrank.

  • Ideological cruelty once again replaced public health.

Civil Rights Dragged Backward

  • LGBTQ rights attacked.

  • Reproductive freedom throttled.

  • Voting rights targeted with surgical precision to entrench minority rule.

Foreign Policy in Freefall

  • Autocrats emboldened.

  • Allies sidelined.

  • America’s reliability, already scarred, further eroded.

Corruption Normalized

  • Nepotism, kickbacks, and self-dealing weren’t scandals — they were operating procedure.

  • Federal agencies became tools for settling scores, rewarding loyalists, and punishing critics.

The Rule of Law Treated as an Inconvenience

A sitting president openly hostile to accountability has predictable consequences: systemic rot. Institutions can’t withstand a leader who rejects the concept of limits.

And beneath all this chaos lies the most revealing truth:

Even many who voted for Trump now quietly admit they were better off just one year earlier.

The propaganda fog is thinning. Reality is asserting itself. The cult armor is cracking.

2025 is the year the country learned — again — that electing an aspiring autocrat comes with a bill. And payment is extracted from everyone, including his supporters.

V. The Sliver of Hope: The Voters Are Waking Up

Across the country, in every special election, every off-cycle race, every local contest that pundits claimed didn’t matter, Democrats have been outperforming — not by inches, but by miles.

This is not an accident.

It is what happens when a populace recognizes that authoritarianism was not a “warning” but a promise. It is what happens when voters see the chaos, feel the consequences, and decide they’ve had enough.

2026 is shaping up to be the first real referendum on Trump’s second-term wrecking ball — and early indicators suggest voters are preparing to swing back.

VI. The Clarion Call: Vote While Voting Still Exists

Democracy is not a permanent state. It is a practice — one that must be defended repeatedly, especially from internal threats. The United States is now experiencing what political scientists have warned about for decades: a soft coup masquerading as populism.

Trump’s movement is not conservative. It is not patriotic. It is not about policy. It is about power — absolute, unaccountable, authoritarian power.

And authoritarian movements only end one of two ways:

They are defeated at the ballot box, or they destroy the ballot box.

2026 may be the last chance to reverse this trajectory before the machinery of democracy is dismantled piece by piece.

This is not melodrama. This is modern history, repeating on American soil.

The solution is not complicated — it is simple, urgent, and non-negotiable:

Vote Democrat. Up and down the ballot. Everywhere. Without exception.

Vote not out of habit, but out of survival.
Vote not for a party, but for a future where elections still exist.
Vote not because it is easy, but because authoritarianism thrives when democracy sleeps.

The country has seen what happens when a convicted felon and aspiring dictator is handed the keys again.

The consequences are here.
The warnings were real.
The danger is ongoing.

2026 is not just another midterm.
It is America’s next — and possibly final — chance to choose freedom over fascism.

Vote while you still can.
