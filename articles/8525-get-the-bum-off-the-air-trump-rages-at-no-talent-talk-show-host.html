'Get the bum off the air': Trump rages at 'no talent' talk show host

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosNovember 21, 20255 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Donald Trump (Reuters)

Donald Trump has raged at “no talent” talk show host Jimmy Kimmel in a post to Truth Social.

The president has made few allies in the late-night television sphere, previously posting about Stephen Colbert, whose Late Show will end next season. Trump has now targeted Kimmel and the Live! show, with a post to Truth Social showing the president is furious at the entertainer.

Trump wrote, “Why does ABC Fake News keep Jimmy Kimmel, a man with NO TALENT and VERY POOR TELEVISION RATINGS, on the air? Why do the TV Syndicates put up with it? Also, totally biased coverage. Get the bum off the air!!!”

The post comes as Kimmel took a jab at Trump in his monologue following the signing of the Epstein files bill. Kimmel said, “We are carefully following the path of Hurricane Epstein right now. It’s a category five, it’s expected to make landfall sometime very soon, we are now one step closer to answering the question ‘what did the president know?’ and ‘how old were these women when he knew them?’”

Kimmel continued his jabs at the president in the monologue, with the talk show host referring to the “quiet, piggy” comments made by Trump on Air Force One. The president made the comments to a Bloomberg News reporter who had asked about Trump’s ties to Epstein.

Kimmel said, “Trump is not a happy little meal right now. Every time he gets asked about Jeffrey Epstein, he loses his mind. He said ‘Quiet, piggy’ to a reporter and it barely made the news. If a man spoke like that to a female co-worker in a workplace harassment training video, you’d go, ‘Ah, that’s over the top. Nobody would do that.’”

Trump has previously made comments on Kimmel and his show, with the president once saying the 58-year-old comedian is “next to go in the untalented Late Night Sweepstakes”.

Trump wrote, “The word is, and it’s a strong word at that, Jimmy Kimmel is NEXT to go in the untalented Late Night Sweepstakes and, shortly thereafter, Fallon will be gone.

“These are people with absolutely NO TALENT, who were paid Millions of Dollars for, in all cases, destroying what used to be GREAT Television. It’s really good to see them go, and I hope I played a major part in it!”

Our Analysis:

The Art of Deflection: Trump vs. Kimmel

In the latest episode of what could be dubbed The Real Housewives of Political America, Donald Trump, the former president known for his subtlety as much as a bull in a china shop, has taken to his digital pulpit on Truth Social to lambast Jimmy Kimmel, the “no talent” talk show host. The tirade was sparked by Kimmel’s audacity to joke about Trump in the context of the Epstein files bill, a subject Trump evidently finds less amusing and more nerve-wracking.

Trump’s Attack: A Classic Move

Trump’s strategy is as transparent as it is tired: attack those who critique him. His post on Truth Social was a textbook example, questioning why ABC and TV syndicates would “keep Jimmy Kimmel, a man with NO TALENT and VERY POOR TELEVISION RATINGS, on the air.” This move is hardly novel for Trump, who has previously thrown similar tantrums about Stephen Colbert, and now, with his sights set on Kimmel, continues his campaign against late-night television hosts who dare to mock him.

“NO TALENT and VERY POOR TELEVISION RATINGS”?

Trump’s accusation of Kimmel having “no talent and very poor television ratings” is, at best, a gross misrepresentation of reality. Kimmel’s show, while maybe not universally adored, has been a staple of late-night television for years, suggesting there is a significant audience that disagrees with Trump’s assessment. Moreover, the focus on television ratings as a measure of worth feels particularly rich coming from a former reality TV star whose own approval ratings during his presidency often left much to be desired.

Kimmel’s Monologue: Hitting Where It Hurts

Kimmel’s jab at Trump, using the hurricane metaphor to shed light on the Epstein controversy, is a prime example of late-night television fulfilling its role as a source of political satire and commentary. Kimmel’s remarks highlight a critical point about Trump’s relationship with Jeffrey Epstein, a topic that remains a sore spot for the former president. Furthermore, Kimmel’s comparison of Trump’s “Quiet, piggy” comment to a scenario of workplace harassment underscores the often-dismissed or normalized unacceptable behavior of Trump.

The Irony of Calling for Kimmel’s Cancellation

Trump’s call to get “the bum off the air” is an ironic twist of fate for someone who has been banned from major social media platforms for inciting violence. His assertion that Kimmel’s coverage is “totally biased” while demanding his removal from the airwaves is a glaring contradiction that exposes Trump’s thin skin and intolerance for criticism.

Conclusion: The Pot Calling the Kettle Black

Trump’s attack on Kimmel is less about Kimmel’s supposed lack of talent or poor ratings and more a reflection of Trump’s own insecurities and inability to handle criticism. His reaction is a textbook example of his approach to dissent: instead of engaging in constructive dialogue or reflection, Trump opts for bluster and baseless attacks. In the grand scheme of things, this latest tirade is just another chapter in Trump’s ongoing war against anyone who dares to hold him accountable or, heaven forbid, poke fun at him.

The real question isn’t why TV syndicates keep Kimmel on the air; it’s why Trump continues to receive a platform for his unfounded attacks. Perhaps it’s time for the former president to reflect on his own advice and consider that sometimes, silence is golden.
