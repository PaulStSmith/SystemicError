'What are you afraid of?' JD Vance's Epstein interview answer instantly backfires on him

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosAugust 11, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Our Analysis:JD Vance's Dubious Blame Game: A Closer LookThe KickbackThe Reality Check

Vice President JD Vance set off alarm bells with experts on Sunday with his answer to a Fox News host asking about the Jeffrey Epstein scandal.

In his reply during the interview, Vance sought to blame Democrats, saying, "We know that Jeffrey Epstein had a lot connections with left-wing politicians and left-wing billionaires ... Democrat billionaires and Democrat political leaders went to Epstein island all the time. Who knows what they did?"

The kickback online happened instantly.

Former Obama speechwriter Jon Favreau said, "Donald Trump’s name is in the Epstein files and [Vance] has been plotting with the AG and FBI Director to cover it up Release the names! Democrats, Republicans, billionaires, or not. What are you afraid of, [Vance]?"

University of Illinois international relations professor Nicholas Grossman responded with a question: "Why is Donald Trump protecting left-wing politicians and left-wing billionaires who are associated with Jeffrey Epstein from justice?"

"Why is he hiding that from the American people? What else must be in the Epstein files that he so badly wants to hide that he's protecting political opponents to do it?" he then asked.

Historian Kevin M. Kruse said, "The official White House line is now that those dirty Democrats must've been hiding some serious dirty about dirty Democrats in the Epstein files and that's why the much more transparent Republicans won't release them now and uh Trump might pardon Maxwell too?"

"The more I think about it, the more I think JD Vance is right," he then added. "The Epstein files must just have dirt on Democrats, so Republicans have every reason to release them in full. Checkmate, libs."

Dean Obeidallah, a former attorney and host of SiriusXM radio’s daily program, “The Dean Obeidallah Show," also chimed in on social media:

"JD Vance wants you to believe only Democrats hung around with Jeffrey Epstein."

"In reality Trump was on Epstein's Lolita Express plane at least 7 times, was caught on video partying with Epstein and said in 2002 in midst of Epstein child sex ring 'I've known Jeff for fifteen years. Terrific guy,'" he then added.

Our Analysis:

JD Vance's Dubious Blame Game: A Closer Look

In a recent interview with Fox News, Vice President JD Vance took a remarkably selective memory lane down the infamous Jeffrey Epstein scandal. Vance's attempt to skew the narrative by singling out Democrats as Epstein's primary cronies not only sets off alarm bells but also blatantly ignores a more nuanced and troubling reality.

"We know that Jeffrey Epstein had a lot connections with left-wing politicians and left-wing billionaires ... Democrat billionaires and Democrat political leaders went to Epstein island all the time. Who knows what they did?" Vance proclaimed, attempting to paint a picture as if Epstein's notorious activities were a partisan affair. This assertion, however, doesn't just stretch the truth—it snaps it.

The Kickback

The backlash was swift and scathing. Former Obama speechwriter Jon Favreau didn't mince words, pointing out the elephant in the room—Donald Trump's own connections to Epstein, which Vance conveniently omitted. Favreau's call to "Release the names! Democrats, Republicans, billionaires, or not" is a demand for transparency that Vance's narrative sorely lacks.

Nicholas Grossman, a professor at the University of Illinois, flipped the script by asking why Trump would protect these so-called left-wing associates of Epstein if they were indeed the primary wrongdoers. His questions underline the absurdity of Vance's implication that there's some grand conspiracy to shield Democrats at the cost of truth and justice.

Historian Kevin M. Kruse's sarcastic endorsement of Vance's logic further highlights the farcical nature of blaming one side of the aisle. Kruse's ironic "Checkmate, libs" underscores the ridiculousness of the assertion that only Democrats could be implicated in the Epstein files.

Dean Obeidallah, a former attorney and radio show host, brings us back to reality by reminding everyone of Trump's well-documented connections to Epstein, including flights on the infamous "Lolita Express" and Trump's own words from 2002, praising Epstein as a "terrific guy."

The Reality Check

Vance's comments are a masterclass in deflection and denial, a ploy to politicize a scandal that is, at its core, about the exploitation and abuse of minors. It's a dangerous game of smoke and mirrors, where the truth is obscured by political agendas.

The Epstein scandal is not a partisan issue; it is a human issue. Attempting to frame it as a problem of the left or the right does a disservice to the victims and hampers efforts to bring all perpetrators and enablers to justice, regardless of their political affiliations.

Vance's narrative is not just flawed; it's irresponsible. It perpetuates a false dichotomy that shields the guilty by muddying the waters with partisan accusations. The real question we should be asking isn't about Democrats or Republicans but rather: How deep do the ties go, and who, regardless of their political stripes, will be held accountable?

To use the Epstein scandal as a political football is to lose sight of the gravity of the crimes committed. It's time to reject these transparent attempts at obfuscation and demand accountability across the board. After all, the victims deserve justice, not to be used as pawns in a political blame game.
