The Scam of the Century: Trump’s War on Reality and Renewables

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosAugust 20, 20253 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Donald Trump’s latest outburst against renewable energy would be laughable if it weren’t so dangerous. His screed, posted to his own Truth Social echo chamber, was predictably incoherent: “Any State that has built and relied on WINDMILLS and SOLAR for power are seeing RECORD BREAKING INCREASES IN ELECTRICITY AND ENERGY COSTS. THE SCAM OF THE CENTURY! We will not approve wind or farmer destroying Solar. The days of stupidity are over in the USA!!! MAGA.”

Let’s be perfectly clear: Trump’s claims are a toxic mix of lies and projection. Here’s the truth.

Renewables Don’t Drive Costs Up—They Drive Them Down

Every credible analysis shows that solar and wind are now the cheapest forms of new energy on the market. Lazard’s 2025 Levelized Cost of Energy (LCOE) report puts utility-scale solar and onshore wind at $26–40/MWh, a fraction of the cost of coal ($45–80/MWh) or nuclear ($67–141/MWh). Trump is not just wrong; he is deliberately gaslighting the public.

The real culprits for rising bills are the fossil fuels he fetishizes. Oil shocks, gas price manipulation, and coal plant upkeep are expensive and volatile. Renewables, by contrast, run on free inputs—sunlight and wind. If your bill is high, it’s because of Exxon, not solar panels.

The Real Scam: Fossil Fuel Subsidies

Trump calls renewables the “scam of the century.” But the true scam has been the fossil fuel industry’s decades-long stranglehold on U.S. policy. Billions in subsidies continue to flow into oil and gas even as the industry rakes in record profits. Meanwhile, American taxpayers foot the bill for endless wars and military deployments to secure global oil supplies.

The Inflation Reduction Act of the prior administration began shifting the balance, putting money into renewables, EV infrastructure, and energy efficiency. Trump is now trying to reverse that progress in service of his fossil fuel donors. That’s not leadership—that’s corruption.

Farmers Don’t Hate Renewables—They Benefit from Them

Trump paints a cartoon villain picture of “farmer destroying Solar.” The reality? Solar leases and wind turbine hosting agreements are lifelines for farmers. They provide steady revenue that helps family farms stay afloat during bad harvests or price crashes. And unlike drilling or strip-mining, renewables don’t destroy arable land. Farmers grow crops and raise livestock right alongside wind turbines.

What does threaten farming? Climate change. Droughts, floods, and extreme heat waves—all intensified by burning fossil fuels—are the real existential threat to American agriculture. If Trump truly cared about farmers, he’d stop selling them out to Big Oil.

Projection, Thy Name is Trump

As always, Trump’s rhetoric is projection. He cries “scam” because his own career is one long con: Trump University, Trump steaks, Trump casinos—all failures propped up by other people’s money. Now he’s running the same con for fossil fuels, peddling lies that will leave Americans sicker, poorer, and less secure.

“The days of stupidity are over,” Trump bellows. He’s half-right. The stupidity of ignoring science, of pretending fossil fuels are freedom, of selling out farmers to petro-dictators—that stupidity must end. But his administration is doubling down on it, using the official channels of government as propaganda organs.

The Bigger Picture

This moment reveals more than Trump’s hostility to clean energy. It reveals that the U.S. government’s official communications are now indistinguishable from his Truth Social rants. The presidential podium has become a soapbox for fossil-fueled disinformation.

The fight over renewables is not just about kilowatts—it’s about whether the U.S. embraces the future or shackles itself to the past. Trump’s fossil-fueled fantasy is a dead end. Solar panels and wind turbines are not “the scam of the century.” They are the path out of ecological collapse, the path to energy independence, and the path to a livable planet.

And yes, the days of stupidity must be over. But that will only happen when Trump and his fossil-fuel cronies are removed from power.

This isn’t a glitch.
It’s the design.
