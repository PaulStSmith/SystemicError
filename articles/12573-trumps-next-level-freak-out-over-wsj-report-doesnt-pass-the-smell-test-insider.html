Trump’s ‘next-level’ freak-out over WSJ report ‘doesn’t pass the smell test:’ Insider

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosJuly 20, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

U.S. President Donald Trump walks upon his arrival from Pennsylvania, at Joint Base Andrews, Maryland, U.S., July 15, 2025. REUTERS/Nathan Howard

President Donald Trump’s “emotional reaction” to the bombshell report from the Wall Street Journal has reached heights never seen before during either of his two terms, argued former advisor to House Speaker John Boehner, Maura Gillespie, who called the president’s response to his increasingly frustrated base “visceral” and “next level.”

The Wall Street Journal published a bombshell report this week revealing new details about Trump’s relationship with convicted sex offender Jeffrey Epstein, alleged to have operated a blackmail operation targeting powerful individuals. Trump’s response has been fierce, immediately launching a $10 billion lawsuit against the publication, and insulting many of his own supporters as "stupid" who continue to promote theories around Epstein.

“I thought it was interesting with the Wall Street Journal (report), his reaction to it was so visceral; to me, it doesn't pass the smell test,” Gillespie said, speaking on MSNBC.

“Something doesn't feel right here, and his reaction to this, his emotional reaction to this feels next level. Normally, his base just acquiesces and says let's find a common enemy and fight against that, (but) right now, they feel as though they're being lied to, and he does not know how to address it.”

Since the Justice Department closed further investigation into Epstein last week, Trump has employed a number of tactics to move his base past curiosity around the disgraced financier; he’s tried to highlight his administration's accomplishments on immigration, he’s called theories about Epstein a Democratic “hoax,” and he’s insulted his own supporters who won’t drop the matter as “weaklings” and “stupid.”

None have worked, however, with Gillespie noting how Trump’s inability to quell his base’s outrage as “so out of character.”

“I think he's losing the plot a little bit himself,” she said. “I do think it is belittling his supporters' intelligence by pretending that he can just wave a piece of red meat over here and distract them when they're pretty set on getting the transparency he campaigned on and promised them.”

Our Analysis:

Trump's Meltdown: A Visceral Reaction to the Unsavory Truth

In what can only be described as a political spectacle of the highest order, President Donald Trump's reaction to the Wall Street Journal's exposé reveals more than just a visceral response; it exposes a man seemingly cornered by the ghosts of his past associations. Former advisor to House Speaker John Boehner, Maura Gillespie, paints a picture of a president whose emotional volatility has reached unprecedented levels, signaling a crisis point not only for him but for his fervent base as well.

The Bombshell Report

The Wall Street Journal's investigation into Trump's relationship with convicted sex offender Jeffrey Epstein is not just another story; it's a glaring spotlight on the murky depths of power, privilege, and illicit connections. Epstein's alleged operation, targeting powerful individuals for blackmail, is the kind of narrative that political nightmares are made of. Trump's response? A $10 billion lawsuit against the publication and a barrage of insults towards his own supporters who dare to seek the truth.

The Reaction: Beyond the Pale

Trump's tactics in dealing with the fallout are as bewildering as they are telling. From highlighting his administration's accomplishments on immigration to dismissing the Epstein theories as a Democratic "hoax," his strategy seems to be one of diversion rather than address. Yet, the most shocking aspect of his response is his outright insult towards his own base, labeling them as "weaklings" and "stupid" for their persistence in seeking transparency.

The Base's Dilemma

Gillespie's observations underscore a critical juncture for Trump's supporters. Accustomed to rallying behind a common enemy, they find themselves at odds with their leader, feeling betrayed and lied to. Trump's inability to quell the outrage or provide the promised transparency not only marks a departure from his typical modus operandi but also hints at a deeper, more systemic issue.

Losing the Plot

The suggestion that Trump is "losing the plot" is perhaps an understatement. This entire saga reflects a profound disconnect between the president and the reality of his situation. By attempting to distract and belittle his supporters' intelligence, Trump not only underestimates their desire for truth but also exacerbates the very crisis he seeks to avoid.

Conclusion: The Transparency Dilemma

The essence of this debacle lies not just in the salacious details of Trump's past associations but in the inherent promise of transparency and accountability that he has so blatantly disregarded. The expectation of truth, once a cornerstone of his appeal to his base, has become the very weapon that threatens to unravel his constructed narrative.

In the end, this episode is a stark reminder of the fragility of trust and the peril of promises unkept. As Trump navigates this tumultuous chapter, one thing becomes painfully clear: the truth, no matter how deeply buried or vehemently denied, has a way of coming to light. And the cost of its exposure may be more than just political capital; it may well be the very soul of his presidency.
