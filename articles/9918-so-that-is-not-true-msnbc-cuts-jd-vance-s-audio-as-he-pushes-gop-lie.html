'So that is not true': MSNBC cuts JD Vance's audio as he pushes GOP lie

By Paulo Santos

JD Vance, Chris Jansing (MSNBC screenshot)

On Wednesday afternoon, MSNBC cut the audio as Vice President JD Vance spoke from the James Brady Briefing Room to call him out as a liar.

As Vance attempted to place the blame for the government shutdown on Democrats despite Republican control of both chambers of Congress and the White House, he fell back on the GOP talking point that Democrats want to hand out free healthcare to undocumented immigrants, which is against the law.

As Vance harangued the White House reporters, the audio was cut, and MSNBC host Chris Jansing exclaimed, “So that is not true.”

“So let me tell you several points that are true,” she continued, “The Democratic proposed continuing resolution does not provide free health care to undocumented immigrants, nor are Democrats advocating for that.”

“But even beyond that, undocumented immigrants cannot buy health care plans on the ACA exchange,” she elaborated. “Therefore, they cannot benefit from the subsidies that the Democrats are talking about. In addition to that, they cannot enroll in Medicare, Medicaid, or CHIP, the Children’s Health Insurance Program.“

“It is important to note providing free health care to undocumented immigrants is against federal law,” she said, correcting Vance. “For the most part, undocumented immigrants are barred from using federally funded health care programs.”

Our Analysis:

The Art of the Lie: JD Vance and the GOP’s Misinformation Machine

In an era where truth seems malleable in the hands of those with power, the recent actions taken by MSNBC in response to Vice President JD Vance’s statements are a refreshing, albeit rare, stand against political misinformation. Vance, speaking from the James Brady Briefing Room, engaged in what has become a disturbingly common practice among GOP ranks: blatant lying to further political agendas. His attempt to shift the blame for the government shutdown onto Democrats, despite Republican control of both chambers of Congress and the White House, is a bold maneuver that reeks of desperation and deceit.

The GOP’s Favorite Boogeyman: Free Healthcare for Undocumented Immigrants

Vance’s fallback on the oft-repeated and thoroughly debunked GOP talking point that Democrats want to provide free healthcare to undocumented immigrants is not just misinformation; it’s a calculated move to stoke fear and division. This claim is not only factually incorrect—it’s against the law. As MSNBC host Chris Jansing correctly pointed out, the Democratic proposed continuing resolution does not include provisions for free healthcare for undocumented immigrants. Furthermore, undocumented immigrants are legally barred from purchasing health care plans on the ACA exchange, enrolling in Medicare, Medicaid, or CHIP—the Children’s Health Insurance Program. This makes Vance’s assertions not just misleading, but patently false.

MSNBC’s Bold Interruption: A Necessary Correction

MSNBC’s decision to cut the audio as Vance spoke is a notable moment in contemporary political coverage. It’s a stark reminder of the media’s role in combating misinformation. Chris Jansing’s interjection to correct Vance’s falsehoods is not just newsworthy; it’s a crucial defense of truth in a political landscape increasingly characterized by lies. By stating, “So that is not true,” Jansing took a stand not just for factual accuracy but for ethical journalism.

The Larger Picture: The GOP’s Misinformation Strategy

Vance’s attempt to mislead the public is not an isolated incident but a symptom of a broader GOP strategy that exploits misinformation and fear-mongering to achieve political ends. This strategy relies on creating a scapegoat—in this case, undocumented immigrants—to distract from their inability to govern effectively, as evidenced by the government shutdown under their control. It’s a tactic as old as politics itself, but its persistence in modern American discourse is particularly alarming.

Conclusion: The Importance of Fact-Checking and Media Accountability

The incident underscores the vital importance of fact-checking and media accountability. In an age where misinformation can spread faster than ever before, the role of journalists in correcting falsehoods cannot be overstated. MSNBC’s handling of Vance’s speech should not be an outlier but a standard approach for all media outlets. As consumers of news, it’s our responsibility to demand this level of integrity from our news sources. Only through a collective commitment to truth can we hope to navigate the treacherous waters of modern political discourse.

JD Vance’s falsehoods and MSNBC’s response to them are more than just another news story; they are a clear indication of the current state of American politics. As the GOP continues to peddle misinformation, the need for vigilant, fact-based journalism has never been more critical. The truth may be inconvenient for some, but it remains an indispensable pillar of a functioning democracy.
