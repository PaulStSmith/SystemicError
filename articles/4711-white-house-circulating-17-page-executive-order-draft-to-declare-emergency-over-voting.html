White House 'circulating' 17-page executive order draft to declare emergency over voting

President Donald Trump, who has insisted on federalizing voting and who issued an executive order last March to pressure states to require proof of citizenship to register voters, is reportedly now being urged to declare an emergency over voting.

“Pro-Trump activists who say they are in coordination with the White House are circulating a 17-page draft executive order that claims China interfered in the 2020 election as a basis to declare a national emergency that would unlock extraordinary presidential power over voting,” The Washington Post reported in an exclusive.

“President Donald Trump has repeatedly previewed a plan to mandate voter ID and ban mail ballots in November’s midterm elections, and the activists expect their draft will figure into Trump’s promised executive order on the issue.”

According to Florida lawyer Peter Ticktin, who is advocating for a presidential order on voting, “we have a situation where the president is aware that there are foreign interests that are interfering in our election processes.”

“That causes a national emergency where the president has to be able to deal with it,” Ticktin told the Post.

Claiming there is an emergency would allow the president to ban voting by mail and voting machines “as the vectors of foreign interference, Ticktin argued.”

Trump has repeatedly urged Republicans to pass the SAVE Act, which critics say could disenfranchise millions of American citizens who do not currently have a passport or access to their birth certificates. It could also disenfranchise people who have married and changed their names but did not do so on all their legal documents.

“Trump has said that if the bill fails, he will act unilaterally to impose the changes for the midterms,” the Post reported.

Our Analysis:

A Cloak of Emergency: Trump’s Latest Electoral Gambit

In what can only be described as a brazen assault on the democratic process, President Donald Trump is reportedly considering an unprecedented move that could fundamentally alter the landscape of American elections. According to The Washington Post, Trump is being nudged towards declaring a national emergency over voting, based on unfounded claims of foreign interference, specifically pointing fingers at China. This maneuver, if actualized, would not only be a staggering overreach of executive power but also a clear and present danger to the integrity of our electoral system.

The Mechanism of Control

The strategy laid out involves a 17-page draft executive order, a document that serves as a testament to the lengths certain factions are willing to go to secure control. The order posits China’s interference in the 2020 election as the rationale for invoking emergency powers, thereby granting the president extraordinary authority over the voting process. This is not just an alarming proposition; it’s a wholesale fabrication designed to manipulate public perception and justify draconian measures that would otherwise be indefensible.

Voter Suppression Disguised as Security

Under the guise of protecting elections, this plan would mandate voter ID and ban mail ballots for the November midterms. This is not about election security; it’s about voter suppression, plain and simple. The SAVE Act, which Trump has aggressively pushed, is a clear indicator of this intent. Critics of the act are right to point out its potential to disenfranchise millions of American citizens — those without passports, birth certificates, or matching names across all legal documents due to marriage. The implications of such legislation are far-reaching and disproportionately affect marginalized communities.

The Real Emergency

The true emergency here is not foreign interference, but the deliberate undermining of democratic norms by those at the highest echelons of power. Trump’s flirtation with unilateral action to impose these changes is not just a threat to the midterms; it is a threat to the very foundation of representative democracy. The idea that one individual, dissatisfied with legislative processes, could impose such sweeping changes to voting is antithetical to the principles upon which the United States was built.

Conclusion: A Call to Action

The audacity of the proposed executive order, and the rhetoric surrounding it, should serve as a wake-up call. This is not merely a political maneuver; it’s an assault on the democratic process. It represents a clear attempt to manipulate election outcomes under the pretense of addressing non-existent threats. The real danger lies not in hypothetical foreign interference but in the very real attempts by those in power to cling to it by any means necessary.

As citizens, it is incumbent upon us to recognize these tactics for what they are: desperate attempts to subvert the will of the people. The sanctity of the vote is the cornerstone of democracy, and we must not allow it to be eroded by falsehoods and fear-mongering. The time to speak out, to demand accountability and transparency in our electoral process, is now. Failure to do so could result in irreparable damage to the fabric of our nation.
