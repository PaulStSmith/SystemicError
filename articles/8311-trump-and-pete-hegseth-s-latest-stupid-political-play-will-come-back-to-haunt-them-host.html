Trump and Pete Hegseth's latest 'stupid political play' will come back to haunt them: host

Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth’s attempt to please Donald Trump by pursuing Sen. Mark Kelly (D-AZ) with a possible recall and court-martial will not play well with the public, as evidenced by GOP lawmakers siding with the former Navy pilot and astronaut, according to some.

Expanding upon reports that Hegseth wants to make an example of the Arizona Democrat for his part in a public service announcement advising military members they don’t have to follow “illegal“ orders, “Morning Joe” host Joe Scarborough and his panel agreed it is a massive misstep by the administration.

“The political negligence of Republicans for somehow putting these war heroes up front to simply defend the concept that our soldiers and sailors and airmen and Marines and members of the Coast Guard should uphold their oath to god and country, seems to me a very shortsighted and stupid political play by Republicans and members of the Trump administration,” the MS NOW host told his panel.

“Yeah, 100 percent,” MS NOW contributor Sam Stein remarked.

“A couple of things to remember,” he elaborated, “For many of these Cabinet members, including Hegseth, they were asked during their confirmation hearings, would you, you know, execute an illegal order? So this concept that this is some sort of grand, weird conspiracy that Trump might issue these executive orders or orders that were illegal is not far-fetched.”

“I mean, this is a hypothetical that’s been engaged by Hegseth and others already,” he continued.

“Secondarily, yeah, by elevating Mark Kelly, I mean, by doing this, you were just elevating Mark Kelly, who frankly, has been through tragedy, doesn’t deserve to have death — the threat of execution leveled at him by the president of the United States,” he explained. “But also, if we’re being honest, will surely benefit politically from this. All he has to do is put on his bomber jacket, go on TV and say, ‘I’m not going to be intimidated by this guy, I just spoke the truth,’ and I’m sure the fundraising money is going to come pouring in. And frankly, rightfully so.”

Our Analysis:

Analysis: The Misguided Crusade Against Senator Mark Kelly

In a bewildering attempt to curry favor with Donald Trump, Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth’s pursuit of Senator Mark Kelly (D-AZ) for a possible recall and court-martial emerges as a grotesque misadventure in political judgment. The strategy, if one could be so generous as to call it that, is not just flawed; it is a catastrophic miscalculation that underscores a profound misunderstanding of both military ethics and public sentiment.

The Ill-Conceived Plot

At the heart of this debacle is Hegseth’s ambition to make an “example” of Senator Kelly, a decorated former Navy pilot and astronaut, for his participation in a public service announcement. This PSA courageously reminded military personnel of their duty to disobey “illegal” orders, a fundamental principle that safeguards the very essence of military honor and legal obligation. The notion, as peddled by Hegseth, that this constitutes grounds for a recall or court-martial, is not just ludicrous; it’s dangerously authoritarian.

A Unified Chorus of Reason

The reaction from GOP lawmakers, siding with Kelly, signals a rare moment of bipartisan clarity against the Trump administration’s often divisive and erratic policies. “Morning Joe” host Joe Scarborough and his panel rightly lambasted the move as “political negligence.” To question the loyalty and integrity of a war hero defending constitutional values is, as Scarborough puts it, a “shortsighted and stupid political play.”

A Misunderstanding of Military Duty

The commentary by MS NOW contributor Sam Stein brings into sharp relief the absurdity of the administration’s position. Cabinet members, including Hegseth, have previously affirmed during their confirmation hearings their commitment to refuse execution of illegal orders. This acknowledgment underscores the reality that the potential for such orders is not merely hypothetical but a tangible concern that has historically tested the moral compass of military and governmental officials alike.

The Unintended Consequences

Stein’s observation that the administration’s attack on Kelly inadvertently elevates the senator’s profile is spot on. Kelly’s dignified response to these baseless accusations not only solidifies his standing as a principled public servant but also galvanizes political and financial support in his favor. In attempting to undermine him, Hegseth and, by extension, the Trump administration, have inadvertently amplified Kelly’s message of integrity and lawful conduct.

Conclusion: A Political Fiasco

In essence, the attempt to court-martial Senator Mark Kelly for upholding the tenets of lawful military service is a farce that betrays a desperate and misguided attempt to appease authoritarian impulses. This debacle should serve as a clarion call to all who value democratic principles and the rule of law. The defense of ethical conduct in the face of unlawful orders is not a liability; it is the very foundation upon which the United States military prides itself. The true scandal here is not Senator Kelly’s defense of legality and morality but rather the Trump administration’s relentless assault on these core American values.
