Trump rages over 'sick rumor' in overnight threat to investigate: 'I'll be finding out!'

Donald Trump[ (Photo via Reuters)

Not content with having spent over three hours in the middle of the day holding a press conference with his Cabinet, where he weighed in on multiple topics in a seemingly random manner, Donald Trump was up at almost 2 AM posting on Truth Social.

Having been lavished with praise from his subservient inner circle on TV Tuesday afternoon, the president lashed out at NBC News over reports that late-night host Seth Meyers –– a frequent Trump critic –– may have his contract renewed.

On Truth Social he complained, “There is a sick rumor going around that Fake News NBC extended the contract of one of the least talented Late Night television hosts out there, Seth Meyers.”

According to the president, who has seen CBS make plans to cancel the even more popular Stephen Colbert’s late-night show, he will be looking into NBC’s reported plans.

“He has no Ratings, Talent, or Intelligence, and the Personality of an insecure child. So, why would Fake News NBC extend this dope’s contract. I don’t know, but I’ll definitely be finding out!!!” he threatened.

Our Analysis:

The Late-Night Twitter Tirade: A Study in Presidential Priorities

In an astonishing display of misplaced priorities and late-night social media rants, President Donald Trump once again took to Truth Social to air his grievances. Not content with a marathon three-hour press conference, which itself was a spectacle of randomness and self-congratulation, Trump found it necessary to attack late-night host Seth Meyers in the wee hours of the morning. This episode serves as yet another stark reminder of Trump's inability to differentiate between personal vendettas and presidential duties, a theme all too common in his administration.

Trump's Late-Night Obsession: Beyond the Pale of Reason

At almost 2 AM, a time when most would expect the leader of the free world to be asleep or at least pondering matters of national security, Trump was busy lambasting NBC News and Seth Meyers. His post, filled with the usual bravado and baseless insults, decried Meyers as "one of the least talented Late Night television hosts out there," a statement that not only undermines the essence of subjective entertainment but also highlights Trump's insatiable need for attention and adulation.

The Irony of Trump's "Fake News" Tirade

Trump's attack on Meyers and NBC is laced with irony. He accuses NBC of being "Fake News" while simultaneously spreading unverified claims about Meyers' contract and the fate of Stephen Colbert’s show on CBS. This hypocrisy underscores a troubling pattern of behavior where Trump uses the term "Fake News" as a shield against any criticism or reporting that does not conform to his worldview.

The President's Misguided Priorities

What is truly alarming about this late-night escapade is not just the content of Trump's tirade but the timing and the platform. This incident occurred immediately after a press conference that was supposed to address pressing national matters. Instead of focusing on policies or responding to critical issues facing the nation, Trump chose to fixate on the entertainment industry and personal vendettas. It begs the question: where are the president's priorities?

The Dangers of a Distracted Commander-in-Chief

Trump's behavior is not just a trivial matter of a president venting on social media; it's a symptom of a much larger problem. His tendency to lash out, pursue personal feuds, and focus on trivial matters over substantive governance poses a significant risk to the effective functioning of the presidency. When the leader of the free world seems more concerned with late-night talk show hosts than with pressing global issues, it sends a message of instability and distraction.

Conclusion: A Presidency Marred by Pettiness and Distraction

Donald Trump’s late-night rant against Seth Meyers is emblematic of a presidency that has often been mired in pettiness and distraction. Instead of rising above personal slights or media criticisms, Trump has consistently demonstrated a propensity to engage in disputes that are beneath the dignity of his office. This latest episode on Truth Social is not just a critique of a late-night host; it's a glaring spotlight on Trump's skewed priorities and his unsettling approach to the presidency. In the grand scheme of things, the real "sick rumor" is the notion that these late-night tirades are an acceptable norm for presidential behavior.
