Legal experts are having a “collective heart attack” — and no one is more responsible for enabling a radicalized second term for President Donald Trump and “legal emergency” than the U.S. Supreme Court, an analyst said on Tuesday.

Some people have suggested we are in the midst of a constitutional crisis, Salon columnist Heather Digby Parton wrote.

“Legal scholars, former judges and law professors are having a collective heart attack over what the administration, particularly the Justice Department and Supreme Court, are doing to the rule of law and the Constitution. Right now, the only bulwark appears to be the lower courts,” Digby Parton writes.

The writer pointed to a 2024 New York Times interview with “50 highly respected members of the legal establishment,” where those interviewed signaled they were concerned about the second Trump presidency “based on what he had done in the first.” Some argued that the Department of Justice would maintain its integrity and qualified people with experience would continue to lead the agency, but that attitude changed.

“When the Times recently caught up with these former officials, their hair was on fire,” according to the writer, explaining that eight months after Trump took office, those warnings have now prompted the same legal experts to say they are “very, very worried.”

“Trump has taken a wrecking ball to those beliefs. ‘What’s happening is anathema to everything we’ve ever stood for in the Department of Justice,’” a former official who worked with both Democrats and Republicans told The Times.

About half of the people interviewed were Republicans, many who worked for Trump in the first administration, she added.

And “all but one of the respondents rated Trump’s second term as a greater or much greater threat to the rule of law than his first term. They consistently characterized the president’s abuses of power — wielding the law to justify his wishes — as being far worse than they imagined before his re-election.”

Most of them didn’t expect “internet trolls” Kash Patel and Dan Bongino to be appointed to key leadership positions and execute Trump’s demands.

But that’s not the only problem.

The Supreme Court has issued “terse orders on these shadow docket rulings, most of which have overturned their judgments to favor the president’s position — and leaving them vulnerable to threats from right-wing commentators, and even the White House.”

“The court’s immunity decision alone gave Trump the green light to do whatever he wanted and let everyone else pick up the pieces. Coupled with the misuse and abuse of the court’s emergency — or ‘shadow’ — docket, the conservative majority has only reinforced the idea that the president is to be given total latitude without constitutional restraint,” the author explains.

This has created a ripple effect on lower courts and issued more warning signals from legal experts.

“But there can be little doubt we are in the midst of an historic legal emergency — and, so far, the only people who appear to be preventing our system of justice from crumbling entirely are the states and lower federal courts. May they have the fortitude to hold out, or things will get much worse,” Digby Parton writes.

Our Analysis:

The Judiciary’s Juggernaut: A Scathing Indictment of Supreme Court Complicity

In the vortex of American political discourse, the notion that the U.S. Supreme Court has morphed into an enabler of President Donald Trump’s radicalized second term is not just a hypothesis but a distressing reality, according to a recent analysis. This assertion, as Salon columnist Heather Digby Parton posits, paints a grim picture of a judiciary emboldened to dismantle the very fabric of the rule of law and the Constitution. The alarm bells are ringing, and the sound is deafening.

The Collective Legal Heart Attack

It’s not every day that you hear about legal experts having a “collective heart attack,” but here we are. The cause? A cocktail of unprecedented actions by the administration, particularly the Justice Department and, crucially, the Supreme Court. The latter’s role, traditionally seen as the guardian of constitutional sanctity, is now under scrutiny for its “terse orders” and decisions that seemingly prop up presidential power unchecked.

The Shadow of the ‘Shadow Docket’

The misuse of the Supreme Court’s emergency—or “shadow”—docket emerges as a particularly egregious example of judicial overreach. This mechanism, once a procedural footnote, has been weaponized to issue rulings that skew heavily in favor of the president’s position, often with scant explanation or accountability. This not only emboldens executive overreach but also sends a chilling message to lower courts and the broader legal community about the state of judicial independence.

The Trumpian Wrecking Ball

The analysis underscores a palpable shift in the legal landscape since Trump’s re-election, with a former official lamenting that the President has taken a “wrecking ball” to foundational beliefs in justice and rule of law. The appointments of figures like Kash Patel and Dan Bongino, who were previously dismissed as “internet trolls,” to key leadership positions signify a troubling departure from norms and a clear threat to the integrity of the justice system.

Republicans Aghast

It’s worth noting that the concern transcends partisan lines. About half of the legal experts interviewed, many of whom served under Trump during his first term, expressed grave concerns about the trajectory of his second term. Their collective assessment? A presidency that has veered dangerously off the constitutional rails, with abuses of power far exceeding their initial fears.

A Legal Emergency

The phrase “historic legal emergency” doesn’t get thrown around lightly. Yet, here we find ourselves, according to Digby Parton’s analysis, teetering on the brink of judicial and constitutional collapse. The only glimmer of hope, as dim as it may seem, resides within the states and lower federal courts, whose fortitude against this tide of executive and judicial overreach is now more crucial than ever.

In Conclusion: A Call to Arms

The Supreme Court’s trajectory under the Trump administration is not just an academic concern—it’s a blazing inferno threatening the very pillars of American democracy. The “collective heart attack” among legal scholars is a clarion call for vigilance, resistance, and a reinvigorated commitment to the principles of justice and the rule of law. The judiciary, once considered the last bastion against tyranny, must not become its enabler. If the current trends persist, the damage may be irreversible, making the urgency for corrective action not just necessary but existential.
