'Just pardon the salmonella': Trump mocked after golf club is flagged for 18 violations

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosJune 6, 20252 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Donald Trump at the Trump International Golf Club in Bedminster, New Jersey on July 28, 2022 (Image: Shutterstock)

On Wednesday, June 4, Forbes reported that the Trump National Golf Club in Bedminster, New Jersey had been flagged for 18 health violations in an inspection report from the Somerset County Department of Health.

The agency gave the business, which is owned by President Donald Trump, a "conditionally satisfactory" grade of C overall but cited some food-related violations.

Forbes' Zach Everson reported, "The club received a score of 32 out of a 100 possible points, with the inspector documenting 18 violations — nine of which were deemed critical, meaning they 'may result in an unacceptable health risk.' The club was out of compliance with all three requirements in the 'food protected from contamination' category, with violations including expired milk, raw meat stored improperly and a dishwasher that may not reach the required temperature. The first two problems were corrected during the inspection."
