'RIP NYC': MAGA lawmakers in a panic over Dem victory in New York City

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosNovember 6, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Democratic candidate for New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani waves at a campaign rally in the Manhattan borough of New York City, U.S., October 13, 2025. REUTERS/Angelina Katsanis

A number of MAGA-aligned lawmakers went into panic after the decisive victory of Democratic nominee Zohran Mamdani Tuesday night in New York City’s mayoral race.

“Under [New York Gov.] Kathy Hochul’s weak catastrophic leadership, New York City has now fallen to a pro-Hamas, Defund the Police, Tax Hiking, Antisemite Jihadist Communist,” wrote Rep. Elise Stefanik (R-NY) in a social media post late Tuesday night.

“And instead of standing up for hardworking New York families, Kathy Hochul proved once again that she is truly the Worst Governor in America with her desperate endorsement of Commie Mamdani.”

Mamdani won handily in Tuesday’s election, securing more than 50% of the vote according to the unofficial election results, receiving more than 1 million votes, the first such instance since 1969. He ran a progressive campaign pleading to implement rent freezes, build more public housing and increase taxes on the city’s wealthiest residents, some of whom went into full-blown panic after he won the Democratic nomination back in June.

For MAGA loyalists, however, Mamdani’s victory spelled the doom for the United States’ most populated city.

“NYC is so screwed,” wrote retired U.S. Army Lt. Gen. Michael Flynn late Tuesday night in a social media post on X, with Flynn being a staunch supporter of President Donald Trump, having supported his efforts to overturn the results of the 2020 election.

“Never in my lifetime did I ever imagine a communist being elected in any American city,” wrote David Freeman, a self-described “MAGA Christian” and political commentator who’s amassed more than 1.6 million followers on X. “RIP NYC.”

And Rep. Andy Ogles (R-TN), among the most feverishly pro-Trump lawmakers in Congress, declared that the United States “must re-classify the threat of communism in our country to Cold War levels” with Mamdani’s victory. The day before the election, Ogles also shared a video of the 9/11 attacks while demanding New York “wake up,” a likely reference to Mamdani’s Islamic faith.

Other Mamdani critics, such as Rep. Nicole Malliotakis (R-NY), were more subdued with their disdain for Mamdani and his victory.

“New Yorkers will learn the hard way what socialism is,” Malliotakis wrote in asocial media post on Xfollowing the election.

Our Analysis:

The Hysteria Over Mamdani’s Victory: A Closer Look

In what could only be described as a theatrical display of political fear-mongering, a number of MAGA-aligned lawmakers have launched into a frenzied panic following Zohran Mamdani’s victory in New York City’s mayoral race. The responses, ranging from outrageous accusations to dire predictions of doom, reveal more about the state of political discourse in America than the actual implications of Mamdani’s win.

Rep. Elise Stefanik’s Hyperbolic Reaction

Rep. Elise Stefanik’s statement post-election is a masterclass in hyperbole and misinformation. Labeling Mamdani as a “pro-Hamas, Defund the Police, Tax Hiking, Antisemite Jihadist Communist” is not only factually dubious but a blatant attempt to incite fear. Stefanik’s choice of words, especially the use of “Commie Mamdani,” serves to demonize not only the candidate but the democratic process itself.

Fearmongering as a Political Tool

The panic among MAGA loyalists, as seen through social media posts by figures like retired U.S. Army Lt. Gen. Michael Flynn and political commentator David Freeman, showcases a deliberate effort to frame Mamdani’s victory as the end of New York City. Freeman’s remark, “Never in my lifetime did I ever imagine a communist being elected in any American city,” ignores Mamdani’s progressive platform in favor of sensationalist rhetoric designed to equate progressive policies with communism—an age-old tactic that has lost none of its potency among certain circles.

The Misuse of Historical Tragedy

Rep. Andy Ogles’ decision to share a video of the 9/11 attacks the day before the election, in a bid to sway voters against Mamdani due to his Islamic faith, represents a new low in political campaigning. This act of blatant Islamophobia, under the guise of warning against communism, is reprehensible. It not only trivializes a national tragedy but also perpetuates harmful stereotypes about Muslims in America.

A Sober Approach Amidst the Hysteria

In contrast to her colleagues’ apocalyptic visions, Rep. Nicole Malliotakis’ statement, though still critical, at least sticks to political criticism without veering into bigotry or conspiracy theories. Her warning that “New Yorkers will learn the hard way what socialism is” reflects a legitimate political stance, even if it simplifies and misrepresents Mamdani’s policies.

Conclusion

The reaction to Zohran Mamdani’s victory in NYC’s mayoral race from certain MAGA-aligned lawmakers and commentators is a stark reminder of the current polarized political climate. By choosing to engage in fearmongering, misinformation, and outright bigotry, these figures are not only misrepresenting Mamdani’s political platform but are also undermining the democratic process. It’s crucial to critically assess these responses and recognize them for what they are: attempts to manipulate public perception through fear, rather than engage in honest political debate.
