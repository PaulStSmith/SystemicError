Trump is 'botching' key issue — and that's finally 'sunk in' for MAGA: ex-GOP strategist

A former GOP strategist has broken down the reason he believes the MAGA faction of Donald Trump’s voter base is splintering so rapidly.

Rick Wilson, founder of The Lincoln Project, suggested the economy is affecting most Americans, including those in the MAGA movement. The former Republican Party strategist suggested that this is the reason, more than any other, for the president’s approval rating to be in rapid decline.

In an appearance on Fast Politics with Molly Jong-Fast, Wilson said, “We’re seeing it in the polling where, for years and years, it was that 35% that could not be moved, and now it’s the 25% that cannot be moved.

“And I think there’s some stuff happening out there — I think it’s the economy, and his [Trump’s] absolute botching of the economy. I think that’s a huge factor. You can’t unweigh that factor.”

Wilson went on to suggest this is an issue the Biden administration had struggled to tackle also, and that Trump is now parroting the economic excuses of the previous president.

The ex-GOP strategist said, “The Biden administration said the same things to the Republican and Democratic voters that Trump is saying now. The stock market’s doing great, and it was. Prices are going down, and they were. But it didn’t make people feel better because they didn’t experience it in their day-to-day life.

“With Trump now, I think it has sunk in because you’re starting to see these polls like, ‘Who was a better president?’ People are saying Biden. Striking.

“I think they had this weird sense that maybe it had started to turn the corner, and then Trump did all this tariff stuff. That is hitting very much in the face of a lot of conservative voters. They all voted for Donald Trump.”

Wilson has predicted further disasters for the Trump administration are looming too, with Pam Bondi set for a rough time in the near future.

In his Substack, Wilson wrote, “The question is no longer whether the Attorney General of the United States believes that shielding her Master is a defensible exercise of executive authority. She quite evidently does.

“The first Attorney General to go to prison did so because he convinced himself that the ends justified the means and that the law was pliable in his hands. Pam Bondi should take that to heart, if she has a heart.”

Our Analysis:

The Cracks in the MAGA Facade: An Analysis of Rick Wilson’s Observations

In a recent commentary that slices through the fog of political discourse like a knife through butter, Rick Wilson, a former GOP strategist and founder of The Lincoln Project, offers a scathing diagnosis of the MAGA movement’s crumbling foundations. His insights, disclosed during an appearance on Fast Politics with Molly Jong-Fast, pinpoint the economy as the critical ailment afflicting Donald Trump’s once unwavering voter base. Here, we dissect Wilson’s assertions with our characteristic blend of fierce clarity and biting commentary.

The Economic Achilles Heel

Wilson’s argument hinges on the premise that the economy is the primary factor behind the rapid decline in Trump’s approval ratings. This is a poignant observation, underscoring how even the most fervent political loyalties can be eroded by the harsh realities of economic hardship. Wilson notes a significant shift in the MAGA movement’s unshakeable base, from 35% to a dwindling 25%. This reduction not only highlights the tangible impact of economic policies on voter sentiment but also shatters the myth of an unbreakable MAGA allegiance.

However, Wilson doesn’t stop there. He extends his critique to the Biden administration, suggesting that both political camps have failed to address the economic distress felt by many Americans. This bipartisan failure, as Wilson points out, has led to a peculiar situation where Trump’s past economic missteps, particularly his tariff policies, are now being felt by conservative voters who once championed his cause. The irony here is palpable, as those who once rallied behind Trump’s economic nationalism are now its victims.

The Paradox of Prosperity

Wilson’s analysis touches on a critical paradox in American politics: the disconnect between macroeconomic indicators and the lived experiences of ordinary citizens. He rightly criticizes the rhetoric of both Trump and the Biden administration for focusing on broad economic metrics like stock market performance, which fail to capture the economic precarity facing many Americans. This dissonance between political narratives and reality is a festering wound in the body politic, one that Wilson adeptly exposes.

The Forecast of Further Turmoil

Looking ahead, Wilson predicts more trouble for the Trump administration, specifically targeting Pam Bondi, the former Attorney General of Florida. Wilson’s comparison of Bondi’s situation to historical precedents of legal overreach and the potential for imprisonment is a stark warning. It serves as a reminder of the legal and ethical quagmires that can consume those too closely aligned with power. Wilson’s critique is not just a prognostication of individual downfall but a broader condemnation of the moral and legal compromises that have come to define the Trump era.

Conclusion: A Movement at a Crossroads

Rick Wilson’s commentary provides a razor-sharp analysis of the factors leading to the erosion of the MAGA movement’s base. By focusing on the economy’s role in this dynamic, Wilson highlights the precarious nature of political loyalty in the face of material hardship. Moreover, his critique of the bipartisan failure to address the economic concerns of ordinary Americans is a sobering reminder of the disconnect between political leadership and the electorate.

However, Wilson’s insights go beyond mere political analysis. They serve as a cautionary tale about the dangers of economic complacency and the ethical pitfalls awaiting those who navigate the halls of power without regard for the law or the well-being of the public. As the MAGA movement faces these existential challenges, Wilson’s commentary stands as a critical examination of the current political landscape and a warning of the turmoil that may lie ahead.
