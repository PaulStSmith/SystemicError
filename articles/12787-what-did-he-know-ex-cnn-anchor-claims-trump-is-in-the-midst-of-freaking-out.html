'What did he know?' Ex-CNN anchor claims Trump is in the midst of 'freaking out'

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosJuly 14, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Our Analysis:The Art of Distraction: Trump's Chaotic Dance with the Epstein Files

Donald Trump (Photo via Reuters)

Appearing on MSNBC, former CNN anchor and White House correspondent Jim Acosta expressed surprise at how Donald Trump is mishandling the chaos at the Department of Justice due the Jeffrey Epstein files.

Speaking with host Ali Velshi, Acosta claimed he knows Trump well enough to recognize that things are going horribly wrong and that the president now seems to be in a state of panic.

"Now Donald Trump is saying on Truth Social that leading Democrats wrote the Epstein files and that they're a fake," he began. "And so, you know, he's twisted himself into a pretzel on this and it takes me back to that Cabinet meeting that he had last Tuesday where, you know, somebody asked the question of Pam Bondi about the Epstein files and he flipped out."

"You know, I've been around Donald Trump. I've been up close with him. I think I know when he's worried and he is freaking out right now over these Epstein files," Acosta continued. "And it just raises the question, what is going on here? What is going on with Donald Trump and these Epstein files? We just don't know and that's why Steve Bannon and so many others over the last several days have been saying, go ahead and release the Epstein files."

He later pointed out, "What gives? And, Ali, the question that iIhad earlier this week when the Justice Department put out that memo that tried to sweep the whole thing under the rug, is did some kind of directive come out of the white house come from Susie Wiles, Stephen Miller, or donald trump himself to put out that memo?"

"If that's the case, then that ties this whole matter back to the White House and so that's why I think the age-old Watergate question is operative here: what did the president know and when did he know it?" he concluded.

Our Analysis:

The Art of Distraction: Trump's Chaotic Dance with the Epstein Files

In a recent appearance on MSNBC, former CNN anchor and White House correspondent Jim Acosta provided a revealing glimpse into the current tempest swirling around Donald Trump and the Department of Justice concerning the Jeffrey Epstein files. Acosta, with a history of up-close encounters with Trump, expressed his astonishment at the former president's handling—or, more accurately, mishandling—of the situation, a debacle that seems to be spiraling into outright panic.

Trump's Pretzel Logic

Acosta's comments highlight a peculiar twist in the narrative where Trump, on his platform Truth Social, accuses leading Democrats of fabricating the Epstein files. This accusation is not just a stretch; it's a leap into the absurd. Trump, known for his Houdini-like escapes from political quagmires, now resembles more of a magician running out of tricks, desperately trying to redirect the audience's gaze. His claims are not just a deflection but a glaring exhibition of panic, an attempt to tangle the truth into such a knot that discerning fact from fiction becomes a Herculean task for the average observer.

The Cabinet Meeting Meltdown

Referencing a specific cabinet meeting where the subject of the Epstein files triggered Trump's ire, Acosta points to a crack in Trump's usually unflappable exterior. This moment of uncharacteristic vulnerability gives credence to the notion that Trump is indeed deeply troubled by the unfolding Epstein saga. It's not just the question that rattled him but the fear of what answers might come tumbling out if the files are thoroughly examined.

The Bannon Factor

Steve Bannon's call to release the Epstein files adds another layer of intrigue. When figures like Bannon, who have historically been in Trump's corner, start pushing for transparency, it begs the question: What do they hope to gain, or what disaster are they trying to avert? Bannon's motives, always a maze of self-interest and strategy, suggest that the stakes are higher than many might perceive.

The White House Connection

Acosta's speculation about a directive from the White House to bury the Epstein issue under layers of bureaucratic obfuscation is particularly damning. It suggests a level of complicity and direct involvement from the highest levels of Trump's administration, tying the president even closer to the Epstein scandal. The Watergate-style question of "what did the president know and when did he know it?" becomes not just relevant but urgent. This isn't just about the sordid details of Epstein's crimes but about the integrity of the presidency itself.

Conclusion: A Ship in Stormy Seas

In essence, what Acosta lays bare is a narrative of desperation and chaos at the heart of Trump's response to the Epstein files controversy. It's a tale of panic at the highest levels, of a president and his allies twisting in the wind as they try to navigate the stormy seas of scandal. The attempt to shift blame to Democrats, the meltdown in a cabinet meeting, and the calls for transparency from unexpected quarters—all paint a picture of a ship struggling to stay afloat.

As the saga unfolds, one thing is clear: the truth about the Epstein files and Trump's connection to them is a specter that will haunt the former president. The more he twists, the tighter the noose of reality seems to draw. In the end, it may not be the scandal itself but the frenzied attempts to obscure the truth that prove to be Trump's undoing.
