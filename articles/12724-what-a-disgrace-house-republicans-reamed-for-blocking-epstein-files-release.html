Trump recently posted to Truth Social, "Why didn’t these Radical Left Lunatics release the Epstein Files? If there was ANYTHING in there that could have hurt the MAGA Movement, why didn’t they use it? LET PAM BONDI DO HER JOB — SHE’S GREAT!"

Shortly after becoming attorney general, Bondi promised to reveal all she had on the disgraced financier pedophile who committed suicide in a Manhattan jail cell before he could stand trial. Rep @RoKhanna took to social media Tuesday, writing, "Rules voted 5-7 to block the full House from voting on my amendment to have a FULL release of the Epstein file. People are fed up. They are fed up."

Podcaster Brian Tyler Cohen wrote, "There it is. Republicans just BLOCKED the release of the Epstein Files."

"They chose to protect Trump instead of telling the American people the truth. Disgraceful. We all know that if Trump’s name wasn’t in the files, they would’ve voted to release them."

The Epstein Files: A Masterclass in Political Cowardice

In a move as unsurprising as it is disheartening, House Republicans have once again showcased their unyielding allegiance not to the American people, but to the whims of former President Donald Trump. By voting to block the release of the Epstein files, they have effectively chosen to safeguard potential skeletons in Trump’s closet over providing transparency and truth to the public. Trump's directive, as per his usual modus operandi, was clear: drop the issue and protect at all costs.

Trump's Transparent Tactics

Trump’s post on Truth Social is a mere smoke screen, a feeble attempt to redirect the narrative. His question, "Why didn’t these Radical Left Lunatics release the Epstein Files?" is a classic Trump diversion—ignoring the fact that his own party is the one actively obstructing their release. His commendation of Attorney General Pam Bondi as “GREAT” for her silence on the matter is equally transparent. It's a stark reminder of Trump’s pattern: if you're with him, you're great; against him, and you're a lunatic.

Bondi's Broken Promise

Pam Bondi’s tenure as attorney general has been nothing short of a masterclass in evading accountability. Promising to reveal all she had on Epstein and then failing to deliver is not just a broken promise; it’s a betrayal of public trust. The fact that Epstein was a known associate of Trump and that nothing has been released speaks volumes about the priorities at play. It’s not about justice; it’s about protection.

The MAGA Rift Widens

The refusal to release the Epstein files has not gone unnoticed, fueling a growing rift within the MAGA movement. This division, however, seems less about moral outrage and more about the inconvenience of having to defend the indefensible. It’s a testament to how far the movement has strayed from any semblance of ethical grounding, prioritizing loyalty to Trump over the pursuit of truth.

A Disgraceful Defense

Comments from individuals like Rep @RoKhanna and podcaster Brian Tyler Cohen highlight the frustration and disbelief at the Republicans’ decision. The assertion by Republicans Against Trump that "[t]hey chose to protect Trump instead of telling the American people the truth" encapsulates the essence of this debacle. It’s a clear indication that for House Republicans, the truth is merely a commodity to be traded for political security.

The Verdict of the People

The user @jeff_ostach’s declaration that "[t]he losers are the American people" is a succinct summary of the situation. Conclusion: A Call to Memory

As the details of this shameful episode unfold, it’s incumbent upon the electorate to remember who stood for transparency and who chose the path of obfuscation and deceit. The refusal to release the Epstein files is not just a political maneuver; it’s a litmus test for integrity. And in this test, House Republicans have shown their true colors, prioritizing the protection of Trump over the pursuit of truth.
