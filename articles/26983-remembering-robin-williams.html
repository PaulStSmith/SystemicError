Remembering Robin Williams

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosJuly 14, 20261 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Robin Williams reportedly recorded around 16 hours of improvisation while creating the Genie for Disney’s Aladdin. We only got to hear a small fraction of it.

Those recordings aren’t just outtakes—they’re an irreplaceable piece of cinema history and a rare opportunity to witness one of the greatest improvisational performers of our time creating an iconic character.

I’ve started a petition asking Disney, in consultation with Robin Williams’ estate, to release these sessions as a respectful archival collection, documentary, or Disney+ special.

If you love Robin Williams, Aladdin, animation, or simply great artistry, please consider signing and sharing.

👉 https://c.org/hzZ7mhGc4V

“Some masterpieces deserve to be admired. Others deserve to teach us how they came to be.”

It’s the design.
