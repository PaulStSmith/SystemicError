'He's making us weak': Governor has 'heartbreaking' realization about Trump helping China

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosApril 24, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

In an MSNBC appearance, Massachusetts Governor Maura Healey slammed several of President Donald Trump’s policies because of the negative impact they are having on scientific research.

“First, let's talk about Harvard. You actually have this post that you put up, Donald Trump's cuts to universities and hospitals mean scientists are being laid off, and shutting down life-saving research. It's bad for patients, bad for science, and bad for our economy. Give me a sense of your reaction to what is happening at not just Harvard, but beyond,” Host Mika Brzezinski asked.

“Well, this is bigger than Harvard, and I stand with Harvard,” Governor Healey said. “I support what they are doing it is very, very important that Harvard filed that suit and stand up to the bully that Trump is.”

The Governor claimed the consequences of removing funding from certain colleges, NIH, and other outlets is large. “[UMass Medical] had to lay off 200 faculty members. They've had to rescind offers to 80 graduate students. Mika these are scientists, okay? These are people who are discovering and working on life-saving cures for cancer, Alzheimer's, ALS, you name it, and all of that is being shut down.”

“Here's what also is happening, which Americans need to know. The ones benefiting from Donald Trump's misguided attacks on our universities and on research are benefiting China,” Healey said. “China is recruiting right now on our campuses all those scientists and faculty members, and students who've been laid off or had their offers rescinded and say, Come to China, we'll build you that lab. We'll give you staff. So, for a President who is about America first, he's making America last. He's making us weaker. He's making us less competitive, and it breaks my heart. It breaks my heart to know that patients will suffer [and] they're already suffering.”

She also believes, “[Trump] is driving everyone, including American students and faculty members, out of this country because they want to be able to do their research somewhere. So this is a very dangerous situation. Totally destabilizing and it's really pointing America in the wrong direction.”

The pair went on to speak about what the Bay State is doing to help small businesses dealing with tariff issues.

Our Analysis:

Analysis: Dismantling Trump's Assault on Science and the American Dream

In a recent MSNBC appearance, Massachusetts Governor Maura Healey didn't mince words when critiquing President Donald Trump's policies and their deleterious effects on scientific research—a cornerstone for innovation and public health advancement. The discussion, steered by Host Mika Brzezinski, shed light on a grim reality: Trump's budgetary machete is not only slashing through the heart of scientific exploration but also, inadvertently or not, serving America's competitors on a silver platter.

Harvard and Beyond: A Battle for the Soul of Science

Governor Healey's defense of Harvard's lawsuit against the Trump administration is a clarion call to action. It's not just about one prestigious institution; it's a broader fight for the intellectual and ethical backbone of America. The assertion that Trump's cuts are "bad for patients, bad for science, and bad for our economy" is an understatement. When universities like Harvard and UMass Medical are forced to lay off hundreds of faculty members and rescind offers to graduate students, we're not just losing jobs—we're losing the next generation of lifesavers and pioneers.

The Great Irony: America Last

Perhaps the most scathing indictment of Trump's policies comes from their unintended beneficiaries: China. As Healey points out, China is eagerly scooping up the talent cast aside by America's own self-sabotage. For a president who chants "America First," his actions are directly undermining this mantra, propelling America towards a future where it's a follower in science and innovation, not a leader. The notion that Trump's policies are making America "weaker" and "less competitive" is not just a speculative fear; it's a rapidly materializing reality.

The Exodus: A Nation of Forsaken Potential

The heartbreak Healey speaks of is palpable and shared by many. The idea that America, once the beacon of progress and opportunity, is now driving its brightest minds away should be a wake-up call. The notion that "patients will suffer" is not alarmist; it's a fact. Research into diseases like cancer, Alzheimer's, and ALS doesn't just stop without immediate consequences. Every day of halted research is a day lost in the battle against these relentless killers.

The Tariff on Truth

While the focus on scientific research is paramount, Healey's mention of the impact of Trump's tariffs on small businesses is another thread in this tapestry of policy failure. It's a reminder that the assault on science doesn't exist in a vacuum; it's part of a broader narrative of economic and intellectual erosion.

Conclusion: A Call to Arms

Governor Maura Healey's words are not just a critique; they're a call to arms. This isn't about politics; it's about the survival of American innovation, public health, and global leadership. As this analysis shows, the consequences of Trump's policies are far-reaching, affecting not just the scientific community but the very fabric of the American dream. The question now is, will America heed the call before it's too late?
