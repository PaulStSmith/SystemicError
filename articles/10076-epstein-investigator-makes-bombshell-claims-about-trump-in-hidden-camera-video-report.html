Hidden-camera footage released by Project Veritas founder James O’Keefe, who’s been accused of releasing deceptively edited videos in the past, appears to capture former DOJ investigator Glenn Prager speaking about the Epstein files during a flight earlier this month from Phoenix to Washington, D.C., reported The Daily Beast.

“I’ve interviewed all the victims,” Prager says in the video. “There’s never been an instance where Trump was on a plane with these kids and the rapes occurred.”

“People want to tie it to [Trump] and say he’s covering up for himself, but he’s not,” Prager adds.

Prager, whose LinkedIn profile states that he has more than 25 years of experience in oversight and investigations for the DOJ, Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) at the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, claims that Trump is “protecting a lot of people” by refusing to order the release of the Epstein files despite promising to do so during his re-election campaign.

“I don’t know what’s new that I have never seen,” Prager says in the video. “He’s so hesitant to show what’s going on with releasing all his files. I think he’s protecting a lot of other people.”

Trump has long sought to distance himself from the disgraced financier, who died in a Manhattan holding cell in 2019 while awaiting trial on additional sex trafficking charges. The president has attempted to deflect from his possible involvement in Epstein’s crime network by calling the investigation a “hoax” – which Prager strongly disputed.

“I mean, come on,” Prager says in the video. “You know it’s not a hoax... He says a lot of stupid stuff. He’s been on the plane, now, many times. It’s just, he was never on the plane with the kids.”

Prager also claims that Epstein will eventually be unmasked as “a CIA informant,” which has been the basis of many conspiracy theories about the late financier and philanthropist.

The White House referred The Daily Beast to DOJ when asked to comment, but that agency did not follow up on a statement provided to O’Keefe: “This individual [Glenn Prager] worked at the Department of Justice as a program analyst over 15 years ago. He has no understanding of, or access to, the underlying facts in this investigation. His statements should not be considered accurate. It is disgusting that someone would further exploit victims of sexual abuse by fabricating stories for their personal benefit.

Our Analysis:

Unpacking the Epstein-Trump Allegations: A Closer Look at Project Veritas’ Latest Bombshell

In the murky waters of American politics and high-profile criminal cases, the name Jeffrey Epstein remains a dark beacon of controversy and speculation. The latest twist in this ongoing saga comes from an unexpected source: a covertly recorded conversation with a former Department of Justice (DOJ) veteran, Glenn Prager, as released by Project Veritas. The claim? That President Donald Trump is actively covering up for Epstein’s co-conspirators. But before we dive headlong into these murky waters, let’s not forget who’s holding the fishing rod – James O’Keefe and Project Veritas, an organization with a checkered past regarding the veracity and editing of their explosive ‘revelations.’

The Allegations: A Grain of Truth or a Beach of Lies?

Prager, with an impressive resume boasting over 25 years at the DOJ and the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, is caught on camera making some hefty claims. He asserts that he’s interviewed all of Epstein’s victims and found no evidence linking Trump to the crimes on the infamous plane rides. Yet, in the same breath, he suggests that Trump is shielding other, unnamed individuals by refusing to release the Epstein files, a promise made during his re-election campaign but left unfulfilled.

The leap from interviewing victims to insinuating a presidential cover-up is, to put it mildly, astronomical. Prager’s allegations that Trump is “protecting a lot of other people” is a loaded statement, begging for further evidence or context. Without additional proof, these claims hover in the realm of speculation, a far cry from the concrete convictions many are hoping for.

The Source: Credibility in Question

Project Veritas, the platform releasing this hidden-camera footage, is no stranger to controversy. With a history of accusations regarding deceptively edited videos, one must question the integrity of this latest offering. The organization’s modus operandi involves undercover operations that often straddle the fine line between investigative journalism and sensationalist propaganda. This does not inherently discredit the information, but it does necessitate a more skeptical reception and a rigorous cross-examination of the facts.

The White House and DOJ Response: A Dismissive Rebuttal

In a rather dismissive manner, the White House deflected inquiries to the DOJ, which in turn offered a scathing rebuke of Prager’s credibility. According to them, Prager’s association with the DOJ ended over 15 years ago, casting doubt on his access to and understanding of current investigations. The DOJ’s statement, branding Prager’s actions as exploitative and based on fabrications, further muddies the waters of this already convoluted narrative.

The Epstein-Trump Connection: A Conspiracy Theorist’s Dream

At the heart of Prager’s claims is the suggestion that Epstein will eventually be revealed as a CIA informant, a theory that has been fodder for conspiracy theorists for years. This angle, while intriguing, lacks substantive evidence and veers dangerously close to sensationalism rather than solid reporting. It’s essential to tread carefully when navigating these conspiracy-laden territories, lest we lose sight of the quest for truth amid a sea of unfounded claims.

Conclusion: A Call for Rigorous Scrutiny

In the end, the allegations brought forth by Project Veritas and Glenn Prager demand a thorough investigation, free from the biases of political affiliations and the sensationalism of conspiracy theories. The seriousness of the accusations and the potential implications on the victims and the accused warrant a careful, methodical approach to uncovering the truth. As always, the devil is in the details, and in the case of Jeffrey Epstein and the shadowy figures surrounding him, those details are particularly elusive.

The need for rigorous scrutiny has never been more critical. We must demand evidence, question motives, and seek clarity amidst the chaos. Only then can we hope to navigate the complex web of allegations and counter-allegations that continue to surround one of the most notorious figures in recent history.
