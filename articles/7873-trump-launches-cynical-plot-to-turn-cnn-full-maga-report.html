Trump launches 'cynical plot' to turn CNN 'full MAGA': report

A “cynical plot” to turn CNN into a MAGA-friendly channel could be underway as Donald Trump hears out bids from Netflix and Paramount.

Paramount CEO David Ellison has reportedly told insiders he would influence CNN into making changes that would benefit the Trump administration. The Wall Street Journal claims Ellison has “offered assurances to administration officials” that CNN would receive sweeping changes should Paramount win the bidding war for Warner Bros. Discovery.

Netflix has also bid on Warner Bros., but Ellison has played up to Trump’s apparent desire to have CNN changed. The president told people close to him that he wanted new ownership of CNN and changes to its programming, which Ellison appears to have assured he can do.

But the plot to make CNN “full MAGA” relies on Jared Kushner too, who has backed a $108billion takeover bid from Paramount for Warner Bros. Discovery. Ellison made it clear what he wants from the takeover and took a shot at Netflix.

He said, “I’m incredibly grateful for the relationship that I have with the president, and I also believe he believes in competition. And when you fundamentally look at the marketplace, allowing the number one streaming service to combine with the number three streaming service is anti-competitive.”

“We want to build a scaled, new service that is basically, fundamentally in the trust business, that is in the truth business, and that speaks to the 70 percent of Americans that are in the middle, and we believe that by doing so, that is for us, kind of doing well while doing good.”

Ellison was asked about what the president may make of his possible CNN takeover. The 42-year-old Paramount Skydance CEO replied, “We’ve had great conversations with the president about this, but I don’t want to speak for him in any way, shape, or form.”

Trump has made his thoughts on CNN very clear over the course of his two presidential terms. Most recently, Trump posted a furious tirade against the news channel and Caitlin Collins.

He wrote, “Caitlin Collins of Fake News CNN, always Stupid and Nasty, asked me why the new Ballroom was costing more money than originally thought one year ago. FAKE NEWS CNN, and the guy who runs the whole corrupt operation that owns it, is one of the worst in the business. Their ratings are so low that they’re not even counted or relevant anymore. MAGA!!!”

Our Analysis:

The Alleged MAGA Makeover of CNN: Unraveling the Plot

In what reads like a script torn from a satirical political thriller, we find ourselves delving into an article that alleges a “cynical plot” to transform CNN into a MAGA-friendly channel. This narrative isn’t just a casual piece of gossip; it’s a glaring spotlight on the potential corrosion of media integrity and the audacious plans of powerful figures to manipulate the news landscape to their advantage. Let’s dissect the key elements of this story with fierce scrutiny.

Paramount’s Ambitious CEO and Trump’s MAGA Dream

At the heart of this unfolding drama is Paramount CEO David Ellison, who reportedly has made overtures to the Trump administration, promising to steer CNN in a direction that would be more palatable to MAGA enthusiasts. The boldness of this proposition cannot be overstated. Ellison’s alleged “assurances” to administration officials about sweeping changes at CNN should Paramount emerge victorious in the bidding war for Warner Bros. Discovery is nothing short of brazen.

The role of Jared Kushner in this narrative cannot be overlooked. His backing of a $108 billion Paramount bid adds another layer of intrigue and suggests a concerted effort to reshape the media landscape to fit a particular political agenda. Ellison’s shot at Netflix, positioning this move as a battle for competition and truth, is ironic at best, given the context.

The Distortion of Media and Democracy

What stands out in this account is the unabashed willingness to manipulate a major news outlet for political gain. The notion of turning CNN “full MAGA” is not just a radical shift in editorial direction; it’s a direct assault on the principles of journalistic integrity and independence. The implications of such a shift—should it come to pass—are profoundly disturbing for the democratic discourse.

Ellison’s comments about building a service that is “in the truth business” while actively courting political favor to alter a news outlet’s editorial stance is contradictory. This isn’t about providing a platform for the “70 percent of Americans that are in the middle”; it’s about tilting the scales of media influence in favor of a specific political ideology.

Trump’s Vendetta Against CNN

Donald Trump’s disdain for CNN is well-documented. His tirades against the network and journalists like Caitlin Collins underscore a broader strategy to discredit any media that challenge his narrative. Trump’s vision for CNN’s future ownership and the explicit desire for programming changes reveal a disturbing intent to undermine press freedom and reshape media coverage to boost his image and agenda.

Conclusion: The Threat to Media Independence

The article lays bare a chilling scenario: powerful figures conspiring to manipulate a major news network for political and personal gain. This isn’t just about CNN or the Trump administration; it’s a symptom of a larger disease eating away at the foundations of our democracy.

Media outlets must remain bastions of independent thought and inquiry, free from the overreach of political ambitions. As we witness attempts to co-opt media for partisan purposes, the need for vigilant, fearless journalism has never been more critical. Let’s not be spectators to the erosion of media integrity. The stakes are too high, and the consequences too severe.

In the end, it’s not just about CNN or the MAGA agenda; it’s about preserving the sanctity of our information ecosystem. We must resist any efforts to turn our news outlets into propaganda machines. The battle for the soul of our media is a battle for the heart of our democracy.
