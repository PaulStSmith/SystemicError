A senior White House official told CNN that “it was made clear to President Trump” a vote on releasing the Epstein files was “an inevitable reality,” and he spent the morning insisting the scandal was an attempt by Democrats to distract from his successes.

“Cost, and INFLATION, were far higher under the Sleepy Joe Biden Administration, than they are now,” Trump posted at 10:45 a.m. EST. “In fact, costs under the TRUMP ADMINISTRATION are tumbling down, helped greatly by gasoline and ENERGY. Affordability is a lie when used by the Dems. It is a complete CON JOB. Thanksgiving costs are 25% lower this year than last, under Crooked Joe! We are the Party of Affordability!”

Trump made the same claim about Thanksgiving costs last week and was subjected to a community note on X pointing out that the Walmart website he cited offered a highly misleading comparison between prices in 2024 and 2025.

“The 2025 Walmart Thanksgiving meal contains 15 items while the 2024 meal contained 21 items,” fact checkers said. “Additionally, most of the brand name items in the 2024 meal were replaced with Great Value items.”

The community note linked to a pair of posts on Walmart’s corporate website showing that prices for a sample Thanksgiving meal in 2024 for eight people worked out to $7 per person, with 21 menu items listed (although the store claimed 29 items were used), while a sample meal for 10 people in 2025 came out to under $4.00 per person with 15 items on the menu.

The president also posted multiple times about the Epstein case, which has exploded back into public view after the House returned to session and a discharge petition passed, forcing a vote to compel the Department of Justice to release investigation files, and the House Oversight Committee issued thousands of emails obtained through the late sex offender’s estate.

“The Democrats are doing everything in their withering power to push the Epstein Hoax again, despite the DOJ releasing 50,000 pages of documents, in order to deflect from all of their bad policies and losses, especially the SHUTDOWN EMBARRASSMENT, where their party is in total disarray, and has no idea what to do,” Trump posted at 9:33 a.m. EST. “Some Weak Republicans have fallen into their clutches because they are soft and foolish. Epstein was a Democrat, and he is the Democrat’s problem, not the Republican’s problem! Ask Bill Clinton, Reid Hoffman, and Larry Summers about Epstein, they know all about him, don’t waste your time with Trump. I have a Country to run!”

He followed that post up an hour later with an announcement that Attorney General Pam Bondi and the FBI would investigate Epstein’s relationship with Clinton and the others, as well banks that did business with him.

“Now that the Democrats are using the Epstein Hoax, involving Democrats, not Republicans, to try and deflect from their disastrous SHUTDOWN, and all of their other failures,” Trump posted at 10:35 a.m. EST, “I will be asking A.G. Pam Bondi, and the Department of Justice, together with our great patriots at the FBI, to investigate Jeffrey Epstein’s involvement and relationship with Bill Clinton, Larry Summers, Reid Hoffman, J.P. Morgan, Chase, and many other people and institutions, to determine what was going on with them, and him. This is another Russia, Russia, Russia Scam, with all arrows pointing to the Democrats. Records show that these men, and many others, spent large portions of their life with Epstein, and on his “Island.” Stay tuned!!!

Our Analysis:

Analyzing Trump’s Social Media Meltdown Over Epstein Vote and Cost Claims

In a recent spectacle of political deflection and misinformation, President Donald Trump took to social media to voice his frustrations over the impending House vote on the Jeffrey Epstein files and to make dubious claims about the cost of Thanksgiving under his administration compared to that of Joe Biden’s. This article provides a fertile ground for dissecting the layers of misinformation and strategic distraction employed by Trump, illustrating a broader pattern of behavior that has come to characterize his political tactics.

The Epstein Distraction

Trump’s reaction to the news that a vote on releasing the Epstein files was “inevitable” was nothing short of a meltdown, characterized by a barrage of posts aimed at directing the public’s attention away from the Epstein scandal and towards what he claims to be the Democrats’ failures. The invocation of the Epstein case, a highly sensitive and controversial issue, serves as a glaring attempt to shift focus from his own administration’s mishandlings and controversies. Trump’s claim that Epstein was a “Democrat problem” not only oversimplifies but also blatantly ignores the bipartisan nature of the Epstein scandal, demonstrating a clear intent to politicize a deeply systemic issue involving abuse and exploitation at the highest levels of power.

Misleading Claims about Costs and Inflation

Trump’s assertions regarding the cost of living and inflation under his versus Biden’s administration are riddled with inaccuracies and misleading comparisons. The claim that Thanksgiving costs are “25% lower this year than last, under Crooked Joe” is a masterclass in misinformation, leveraging incomplete and cherry-picked data to construct a narrative of economic superiority. Fact-checkers have pointed out the flawed comparison between the 2024 and 2025 Thanksgiving meal costs cited by Trump, highlighting discrepancies in the number of items and the switch to cheaper, store-brand products in the latter. This manipulation of data is emblematic of Trump’s broader strategy of employing misleading statistics to bolster his image as a steward of economic prosperity.

Politicizing the Justice Department

Trump’s announcement that Attorney General Pam Bondi and the FBI would investigate Epstein’s connections with prominent Democrats and financial institutions further exemplifies his tendency to use federal agencies as tools for political gain. Labeling the renewed interest in the Epstein case as “another Russia, Russia, Russia Scam” and directing federal resources towards investigating his political adversaries underlines a dangerous precedent of politicizing justice and law enforcement in America. This move not only diverts attention from the pressing need to address systemic abuse and corruption but also undermines the integrity of federal institutions by casting them as players in a partisan battleground.

Conclusion

Trump’s response to the inevitable vote on the Epstein files, coupled with his misleading claims about economic conditions, paints a picture of a political figure deeply entrenched in the art of distraction, misinformation, and the politicization of federal agencies. Rather than addressing the substantive issues at hand or engaging in constructive policy debate, Trump opts for a strategy of deflection, aiming to muddle the public discourse and shift blame onto his political adversaries. This approach not only erodes public trust in political and judicial processes but also detracts from the urgent need to address the systemic issues laid bare by the Epstein scandal. In this light, Trump’s social media meltdown can be seen not just as a moment of political desperation but as a symptom of a deeper malaise within the current political ecosystem.
