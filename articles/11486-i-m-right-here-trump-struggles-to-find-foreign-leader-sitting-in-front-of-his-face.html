'I'm right here': Trump struggles to find foreign leader sitting in front of his face

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosAugust 19, 20253 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Trump locates Finnish President Alexander Stubb at his table (Photo: Screen capture via Aaron Rupar on Bluesky.)

President Donald Trump flubbed the introduction of Finnish President Alexander Stubb on Monday during the meeting with European leaders who support Ukraine.

Trump was announcing each leader, who then would make a comment or thank him for the invitation. He gave each official a little compliment.

As he announced Stubb, however, he appeared not to realize that the Finnish president was sitting right in front of him.

"President Stubb of Finland. And he's somebody that — we're all here, we're, we're," he looked around the room, seemingly confused.

"I'm right here," said Stubb.

"Oh, you look better than I've ever seen. You look — but you've done a great job, and we wanted to have you here because you're somebody that we all respect," Trump continued.

Journalist Aaron Rupar posted a screen capture of Stubb's position sitting before Trump to illustrate the unusual nature of the moment.

"It seems Trump almost forgot about Finnish President Alexander Stubb, launching into folksy talk about the White House before throwing it to Stubb, who began by justifying Finland’s seat at the table. He implies that Finland’s history of dealing with Moscow will bear fruit in Ukraine," commented Kevin Rothrock, the English-language managing editor for the Russian news site Meduza.

Our Analysis:

A Masterclass in Presidential Ineptitude: Trump's Latest Gaffe

In what can only be described as a farcical moment straight out of a political satire, President Donald Trump managed to flub the introduction of Finnish President Alexander Stubb, showcasing not only a disturbing lack of awareness but also a profound disrespect for the very fabric of international diplomacy.

Trump, in his characteristic manner, turned a standard procedure of introductions into a bewildering spectacle of confusion. As he called upon leaders to make their remarks, thanking them with personalized compliments, he stumbled upon President Stubb — or rather, failed to stumble upon him, despite Stubb being right in front of his eyes. The moment Trump uttered, "President Stubb of Finland. And he's somebody that — we're all here, we're, we're," only to trail off in confusion, was not just a lapse in memory but a glaring display of incompetence.

The sheer absurdity of the situation was punctuated by Stubb's interjection, "I'm right here," which seemed to momentarily ground Trump back to reality. Yet, instead of offering a sincere apology or gracefully recovering, Trump resorted to his all-too-familiar tactic of vague compliments and awkward flattery, telling Stubb, "Oh, you look better than I've ever seen. You look — but you've done a great job, and we wanted to have you here because you're somebody that we all respect."

This incident transcends mere embarrassment; it is a symptom of a larger, more troubling issue at play. Trump's inability to recognize a seated world leader — in a small, presumably well-prepared setting — is indicative of a broader lack of preparation, respect, and perhaps most critically, attention to the details crucial in international relations.

Kevin Rothrock's commentary highlights another angle of Trump's gaffe — the missed opportunity to acknowledge Finland's strategic position and history with Moscow, especially relevant given the context of supporting Ukraine. Trump's failure to engage meaningfully on such topics, choosing instead to plunge into "folksy talk," further illustrates his disinterest in the nuances of global politics.

The incident, as captured and shared by journalist Aaron Rupar, serves not only as a reminder of Trump's frequent departures from presidential norms but also as a stark example of his habitual underestimation of international diplomacy. Such behavior not only undermines the stature of the American presidency but also chips away at the United States' credibility on the world stage.

In conclusion, Trump's blunder with President Stubb is not a mere comedic relief; it's a worrying sign of ineptitude and a cavalier approach to international relations. It's high time for a recalibration of expectations and standards, lest we continue to witness the erosion of diplomatic decorum and international respect under Trump's watch.
