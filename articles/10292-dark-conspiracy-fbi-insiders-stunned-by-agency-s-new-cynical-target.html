'Dark conspiracy': FBI insiders stunned by agency's new 'cynical' target

By Paulo Santos

A person passes by the FBI seal on the wall of the FBI headquarters, days after the Trump administration launched a sweeping round of cuts at the Justice Department, in Washington, U.S., February 3, 2025. REUTERS/Kevin Lamarque

The Trump administration is gearing up to establish a new threat designation for transgender people in a move that left multiple insiders stunned, according to two national security officials who spoke on the condition of anonymity.

According to the officials, who spoke with journalist Ken Klippenstein in a report published Thursday, the FBI is preparing to label transgender suspects under a new threat category dubbed “Nihilistic Violent Extremists,” a replacement designation for the Biden administration’s “Anti-Authority and Anti-Government Violent Extremists" threat category, established in the wake of the Jan. 6 Capitol riot.

The new designation, one of the officials said, was established, in part, due to the killing last week of right-wing influencer Charlie Kirk, with many in the MAGA sphere attempting to link Kirk’s suspected killer with transgenderism.

“They are cynically targeting trans people because the shooter’s lover was trans,” one senior national security official told Klippenstein. “The administration has convinced itself that the Charlie Kirk murder exposes some dark conspiracy.”

The officials went on to say that the new designation would provide the Trump administration with “political cover” for its campaign against transgenderism, given the designation’s ambiguous name that doesn’t refer directly to transgender people, and the White House’s frequent use of more generic terms like “gender ideology extremism.”

While Kirk’s suspected killer has been reported to have been in a romantic relationship with a transgender person, no evidence exists that they were motivated to carry out the killing by any form of transgender ideology.

This hasn’t stopped Trump administration officials, however, from claiming that the suspect had engraved bullets with "transgender and anti-fascist ideology" messages, a claim that had to be walked back after further reporting found no evidence of the suspect having engraved pro-transgender messages on any shell casings.

FBI Director Kash Patel has also suggested transgender ideology may have played a role in Kirk's killing, telling the House Judiciary Committee on Wednesday that his agency was "looking at the entire spider web for any of these attacks" when asked about shooting by transgender people.

Our Analysis:

Analysis: The Trump Administration's New Threat Designation - A Cloak for Transphobia?

In what can only be described as a jaw-dropping escalation of targeting a marginalized community, the Trump administration appears set to weaponize national security labels against transgender individuals under the guise of combating “Nihilistic Violent Extremists.” This report, brought to light by journalist Ken Klippenstein, signals not just a policy shift but a disturbing utilization of power against an already vulnerable group.

The attempt to link transgender individuals to a new category of threat, replacing the Biden administration's “Anti-Authority and Anti-Government Violent Extremists,” in the aftermath of the Jan. 6 Capitol riot, reeks of political opportunism and manipulation. The fact that this move was partially motivated by the killing of Charlie Kirk, a right-wing influencer, and attempts by MAGA proponents to associate his suspected killer with transgenderism, is both sensationalist and baseless.

Cynical Targeting Under the Veil of National Security

It's telling that one senior national security official admitted to Klippenstein that the administration is “cynically targeting trans people” because of a loose association between the shooter and transgenderism. This admission is a glaring indication of how the administration seeks to exploit a tragedy for political gain, engaging in what can only be characterized as a witch-hunt against transgender individuals by linking them to a so-called “dark conspiracy.”

The designation’s ambiguous name, “Nihilistic Violent Extremists,” and the administration’s vague rhetoric around “gender ideology extremism,” are tactics seemingly designed to provide political cover. This strategy allows for broad interpretations and applications, fueling the administration’s campaign against transgenderism without explicitly mentioning the community, thereby attempting to sidestep accusations of direct targeting.

Unfounded Claims and the Spread of Misinformation

The narrative being pushed by the Trump administration—that the suspect in Kirk’s killing was motivated by transgender ideology—is not only unsubstantiated but dangerously misleading. The rush to claim that the suspect had engraved bullets with “transgender and anti-fascist ideology” messages, only for such claims to be walked back upon further scrutiny, exemplifies a reckless disregard for the truth in favor of pushing a political agenda.

FBI Director Kash Patel’s suggestion to the House Judiciary Committee that transgender ideology might have played a role in Kirk's killing, despite a lack of evidence, further illustrates an alarming trend of government officials promoting unfounded theories. This approach not only demonizes the transgender community but also diverts attention from the real and complex motivations behind acts of violence, reducing them to simplistic and baseless explanations.

Conclusion: A Dangerous Precedent

The Trump administration’s move to label transgender suspects under a new threat category is not just an attack on a marginalized community but a dangerous manipulation of national security mechanisms for political ends. This policy, rooted in bias and misinformation, sets a concerning precedent for the use of government power against specific social groups under the guise of combating extremism.

In light of these developments, it is imperative for journalists, legal experts, and the public to remain vigilant, questioning and challenging the administration’s narratives and policies. The targeting of transgender individuals, under any pretext, should not be normalized or accepted. It’s a stark reminder of the lengths to which certain factions will go to marginalize and demonize those they oppose, often under the thinnest veneers of national security and public interest.
