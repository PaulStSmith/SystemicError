'Are you this stupid?' Morning Joe floored by Trump's accidental 'gift' to Democrats

Joe Scarborough (center) speaks on MS NOW’s “Morning Joe,” Feb. 6, 2026. (Screengrab / MS NOW)

President Donald Trump made several moves this week that left Joe Scarborough floored Friday morning at what he described as a series of “self-defeating” mistakes that were nothing more than an unintentional “gift” for Democrats in the upcoming midterm elections.

On Thursday, Trump shifted his explanation as to why National Intelligence Director Tulsi Gabbard was present during the recent FBI raid on a Georgia election center. The same day, Trump also made the stunning demand that the Dulles Airport and Penn Station be named after him in exchange for the unfreezing of $16 billion in federal funds for two major infrastructure projects.

His behavior and startling admissions, Scarborough argued, were not only hurting his entire party’s prospects in the upcoming midterms, but providing a clear runway for Democrats to seize victory this November in an election blowout.

“Look at this, like, seriously? Are you guys kidding me? Tulsi Gabbard down in Georgia, are you this stupid?” Scarborough said in shock.

“Are you this stupid that you think you’re going to get away with it? Are you this stupid? Why are you so stupid? Why do you do things that hurt Republican candidates in the state of Georgia, hurt Republican candidates across the country?”

Sen. Jon Ossoff (D-GA) is up for re-election this year, and currently maintains a “huge fundraising edge” over his GOP opponents. Trump’s ever-changing narrative around the FBI raid on the Georgia election center, Scarborough argued, would provide “a gift for Ossoff” later this year.

Trump’s withholding of $16 billion in funds for a major infrastructure project in New York would similarly hurt Republicans later this year, Scarborough warned.

“All of this is so self-defeating! If you don’t believe me, just look at the poll numbers!” he said. At this stage, all you’re doing is hurting the Republican Party. All you’re doing is hurting your support in the center, all you’re doing is making sure Democrats have a great off-year election.”

Scarborough also railed against Trump over his most recent gaffe – a blatantly racist video he posted on social media depicting Barack and Michelle Obama as non-human primates, yet another move by the president that Scarborough said would ultimately hurt Republicans at the ballot box.

“All you’re doing is making yourself look pathetic – Republicans – for not standing up to this open racism and bigotry,” Scarborough said.

Our Analysis:

Trump’s Political Missteps: A Masterclass in Self-Sabotage

In a week that can only be described as a circus of political folly, President Donald Trump has yet again managed to astonish observers with a series of decisions that beggar belief. His actions, which include a baffling shift in narrative regarding the FBI raid in Georgia and a demand as absurd as renaming major infrastructure after himself, serve as a glaring exhibit of self-defeating behavior. Joe Scarborough’s critique, while charged with emotion, hits the nail squarely on the head. Let’s dissect the layers of ineptitude and self-sabotage that Trump has displayed, essentially wrapping up a gift with a neat bow for the Democrats in the upcoming midterm elections.

The FBI Raid Narrative Shift: A Masterstroke of Confusion

First on the docket is Trump’s ever-changing explanation concerning the presence of National Intelligence Director Tulsi Gabbard during the FBI raid on a Georgia election center. The shiftiness of this narrative not only undermines any remaining credibility but also plays directly into the hands of Democratic narratives. It’s as if Trump is scripting a how-to guide on political self-destruction. Scarborough’s disbelief, peppered with rhetorical questions about the intelligence behind such moves, underscores a broader bewilderment. Why, indeed, would Trump choose to muddy the waters in a state critical to the Republican Party’s fortunes, especially with Sen. Jon Ossoff (D-GA) already holding a “huge fundraising edge”?

The Infrastructure Hostage Crisis

Moving on to the surreal demand to have Dulles Airport and Penn Station renamed in his honor in exchange for releasing $16 billion in federal funds earmarked for major infrastructure projects. This is not just a display of monumental ego; it’s a strategic blunder of epic proportions. Holding vital infrastructure improvements hostage for personal aggrandizement is not only politically tone-deaf but also detrimental to the Republican Party’s image and prospects. It hands the Democrats not just ammunition, but an entire arsenal.

A Gift to Democrats

Scarborough’s exasperation is palpable when he addresses the self-defeating nature of Trump’s actions. The poll numbers he refers to are a testament to the widespread disapproval of such antics. Trump’s behavior alienates not just the moderate center but rational beings in general. It’s a classic case of snatching defeat from the jaws of victory, providing Democrats with a clear path to a significant win in the November elections.

Open Racism and Bigotry: The Final Straw

The icing on this unpalatable cake is Trump’s most recent gaffe: a blatantly racist video depicting Barack and Michelle Obama as non-human primates. This act of vile racism is not just reprehensible on a human level; it represents a catastrophic political miscalculation. Scarborough’s condemnation of Republicans for their silence and failure to stand up to such bigotry is a crucial point. This silence not only implicates them by association but also further erodes the party’s standing with any voter possessing a moral compass.

Conclusion: A Political Albatross

Joe Scarborough’s outrage is more than justified—it’s necessary. In a week of bewildering political missteps by Trump, the former president has proved himself to be an albatross around the neck of the Republican Party. His actions are not merely self-defeating; they are an active impediment to the party’s success. As we edge closer to the midterm elections, one thing becomes abundantly clear: Trump’s greatest gift might just be to the Democrats, wrapped in the foil of his unparalleled capacity for political self-sabotage.
