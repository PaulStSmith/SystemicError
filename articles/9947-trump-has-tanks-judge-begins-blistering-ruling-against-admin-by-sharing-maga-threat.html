'Trump has tanks': Judge begins blistering ruling against admin by sharing MAGA threat

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosOctober 1, 20255 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

United States District Court for the District of Massachusetts

U.S. District Judge William Young found that the Trump administration wrongly ordered the deportation of pro-Palestinian students to “strike fear” and deny free speech rights.

Young began his blistering 161-page ruling on Tuesday by sharing a threat he received in his chambers.

“Trump has pardons and tanks... what do you have?” the threat said.

“Alone, I have nothing but my sense of duty,” the judge wrote in response. “Together, we the people of the United States — you and me — have our magnificent Constitution.”

Young went on to explain why he had ruled against administration officials, including Homeland Security Secretary Kristi Noem and Secretary of State Marco Rubio.

“This Court finds as fact and concludes as matter of law that Secretaries Noem and Rubio and their several agents and subordinates acted in concert to misuse the sweeping powers of their respective offices to target non-citizen pro-Palestinians for deportation primarily on account of their First Amendment protected political speech,” he wrote.

“They did so in order to strike fear into similarly situated non-citizen pro-Palestinian individuals, pro-actively (and effectively) curbing lawful pro-Palestinian speech and intentionally denying such individuals (including the plaintiffs here) the freedom of speech that is their right.”

“Moreover, the effect of these targeted deportation proceedings continues unconstitutionally to chill freedom of speech to this day,” he added.

The judge addressed the “Trump brand” by quoting his wife.

“He seems to be winning. He ignores everything and keeps bullying ahead,” Young quoted.

Agreeing with the sentiment, Young noted that the president often ignored “the Constitution, our civil laws, regulations, mores, customs, practices, courtesies -- all of it; the President simply ignores it all when he takes it into his head to act.”

“Behold President Trump’s successes in limiting free speech -– law firms cower, institutional leaders in higher education meekly appease the President, media outlets from huge conglomerates to small niche magazines mind the bottom line rather than the ethics of journalism,” Young added. “Where things run off the rails for him is his fixation with ‘retribution.’”

“It is at this juncture that the judiciary has robustly rebuffed the President and his administration.”

Young concluded that monetary damages would not suffice because “harm here and the deprivations suffered runs far deeper.” It was not clear, however, what the judge would order in a future “remedy phase.”

“I fear President Trump believes the American people are so divided that today they will not stand up, fight for, and defend our most precious constitutional values so long as they are lulled into thinking their own personal interests are not affected,” he remarked. “Is he correct?”

Some court watchers were shocked by the ruling.

“Omg. I’ve never seen anything like this in a judicial opinion,” lawfare senior editor Anna Bower wrote.

Our Analysis:

The Iron Fist of Censorship: Trump’s Assault on Free Speech

In a judicial system often criticized for its snail-paced proceedings and Byzantine complexity, U.S. District Judge William Young’s ruling emerges not just as a beacon of judicial clarity but as a scorching indictment of the Trump administration’s blatant disregard for the very bedrock of American democracy: the First Amendment. Let’s dissect, with the precision of a surgeon and the indignation this situation warrants, the implications of Young’s 161-page tour de force against what can only be described as a calculated campaign of fear and suppression.

The Misuse of Power

Judge Young’s findings are as clear as they are alarming. Homeland Security Secretary Kristi Noem and Secretary of State Marco Rubio, under the Trump administration’s directive, have been found to weaponize the formidable powers of their offices against non-citizen pro-Palestinian voices. This wasn’t merely an overreach of authority; it was a targeted strike against free speech. “They did so in order to strike fear into similarly situated non-citizen pro-Palestinian individuals, pro-actively (and effectively) curbing lawful pro-Palestinian speech and intentionally denying such individuals (including the plaintiffs here) the freedom of speech that is their right,” Young writes.

This isn’t about policy disagreements or the enforcement of immigration laws within the bounds of reason and justice. This is about silencing dissent, about using the threat of deportation as a gag. In the Trump administration’s America, it seems the First Amendment is but a paper tiger, easily ignored by those wielding power.

The Trump Brand: Ignorance and Bullying

Judge Young insightfully quotes his wife to underscore a chilling reality: “He seems to be winning. He ignores everything and keeps bullying ahead,” encapsulating Trump’s modus operandi. The President’s disregard for constitutional values, civil laws, and even basic human decency is not just troubling; it’s a clear and present danger to the fabric of the nation.

Young’s ruling paints a stark picture of a presidency obsessed with “retribution,” where anyone daring to exercise their right to free speech faces the administration’s wrath. Yet, what’s perhaps most concerning is the ripple effect this has across sectors. Law firms, educational institutions, and media outlets, as per Young’s observations, have all been cowed into submission, prioritizing appeasement and profit over principle and the public good.

The Judiciary’s Stand

In a rare moment of unity and resolve, the judiciary, as per Judge Young, has stood firm against Trump’s onslaught on free speech. It’s a reassuring reminder that the system, though flawed, has checks and balances capable of correcting course. However, Young’s ruling is not just a rebuke but a clarion call. The “harm here and the deprivations suffered runs far deeper” than something monetary damages can address. The question then becomes, what will be the remedy? And more fundamentally, what will it take to heal the wounds inflicted upon the nation’s constitutional values?

A Divided Nation’s Test

Judge Young’s fears are our fears: that Trump believes, perhaps correctly, that a divided America might not stand up for its constitutional values. This ruling should serve not just as a wake-up call but as a rallying cry. The divisions among us are less a cause than a symptom of the erosion of our democratic principles and rights. The real test is whether we can rise above these divisions to defend the core values that define us.

In the end, Judge Young’s ruling is more than a legal document; it’s a historical record of a time when the very essence of American democracy was under threat from within. It’s a stark reminder that the battle for free speech and democratic values is ongoing, and complacency is the enemy. As we move forward, let’s remember the courage it took to pen such a ruling and the vigilance required to ensure that the freedoms we often take for granted are never eroded on our watch.
