'Shocking': Joe Scarborough flags new 'absolutely stunning' anti-Trump polling number

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosFebruary 7, 20264 min read

President Donald Trump continues to sink in the polls on issues related to affordability and the economy, but on issues he’s historically performed best at – namely, immigration – his numbers have sunk dramatically in what Joe Scarborough flagged Friday as a “shocking turnaround from over the past year.”

“I was really struck by the poll [on whether] Donald Trump’s immigration policies make you safer or less safe, and the majority of Americans say [they] make Americans less safe,” Scarborough said Friday while on MS NOW’s “Morning Joe.”

“It’s just an absolute stunning, almost shocking turnaround from over the past year. Obviously, you look at the killings, you look at the brutality that [Homeland Security Secretary] Kristi Noem’s forces are engaging in, the secret police... get the masks off, please!”

Trump’s favorability on issues like the economy – while never historically strong – has cratered since he took office, falling by double digits in January from as recently as last July. On immigration, however, where Trump once held majority support, his support has also sunk, including in private polling conducted by the GOP itself.

The cratering support for Trump’s immigration policy has largely been driven by his administration’s chaotic and violent deportation raids across the nation. More than 328,000 migrants have been arrested as part of the mass operations, and despite Trump’s pledge to target only the “worst of the worst,” more than 73% of arrested migrants had no criminal history.

Amid rising grocery prices that Scarborough noted were “more inflationary in the month of December than any time since 2022,” Trump’s ongoing fixation on matters unrelated to affordability and the economy – including his recent campaign to force officials to rename the Dulles Airport and Penn Station in his honor – was a clear sign that the GOP was in trouble in the upcoming midterms, Scarborough said.

“[Affordability is] what Americans want to talk about!” Scarborough said. “Not renaming crappy airports and lousy railway stations, or racist tweets, or Greenland, or Venezuela, or all this other stuff!”

Our Analysis:

Trump’s Tumultuous Tenure: A Deep Dive into Declining Polls and Policy Pitfalls

In a political landscape that often feels like a fever dream, the recent shifts in public opinion regarding President Donald Trump’s administration offer a fascinating, albeit troubling, snapshot of a presidency in peril. Joe Scarborough’s comments on MS NOW’s “Morning Joe” serve as a stark reminder of the dramatic nosedive Trump’s approval ratings have taken, particularly on issues that were once considered his stronghold: immigration and the economy.

The Economic Erosion

Let’s cut through the fluff. Trump’s economic favorability, “while never historically strong,” has not just declined; it has plummeted. This isn’t a gentle slope; it’s a cliff dive. The fall in approval by double digits since last July is more than a fluctuation; it’s a freefall. And it’s not hard to see why. Amidst rising grocery prices and what Scarborough aptly describes as the most inflationary month since 2022, the Trump administration’s preoccupation with trivial matters such as renaming airports and railway stations is not just out of touch—it’s absurdly disconnected.

Immigration Inconsistencies

On immigration, the issue once considered Trump’s trump card, the numbers have sunk dramatically. This is not just a decline; it’s a collapse of confidence. The “shocking turnaround” noted by Scarborough underscores a significant disillusionment with Trump’s immigration policies. The administration’s chaotic and violent deportation raids, arresting over 328,000 migrants, 73% of whom had no criminal history, reveal a policy not only of inefficiency but of brutality.

The Brutality of Policy

Scarborough’s reference to the “killings” and the “brutality” of Homeland Security Secretary Kristi Noem’s forces is particularly chilling. The mention of “secret police” and a plea to “get the masks off” paints a dystopian picture that should alarm any observer, regardless of political affiliation. This isn’t just bad policy; it’s morally reprehensible action that betrays a fundamental disregard for human dignity and rights.

A Misguided Focus

Trump’s “ongoing fixation” on inconsequential matters while the nation grapples with real issues of affordability and economic stability is not just a failure of leadership—it’s a mockery of it. The attempt to rename Dulles Airport and Penn Station in his honor, amidst economic turmoil and a pandemic of policy failures, is an exercise in vanity unmatched by most modern leaders. It’s not just that the GOP is “in trouble” for the midterms, as Scarborough suggests; it’s that the nation is in trouble if this is the focus of its leadership.

Conclusion: A Leadership in Crisis

The article lays bare a presidency that is not only struggling in the polls but is fundamentally failing to address the critical issues facing the American people. The dramatic decline in support for Trump’s immigration policy and the cratering approval ratings on the economy are symptomatic of a broader crisis of leadership. In a time when Americans crave stability, affordability, and sensible governance, the Trump administration offers distraction, divisiveness, and disorder.

In the end, the question isn’t just whether the GOP can recover for the midterms; it’s whether America can recover from a presidency that prioritizes personal aggrandizement over public good. The answers, much like the policies discussed, seem troublingly elusive.
