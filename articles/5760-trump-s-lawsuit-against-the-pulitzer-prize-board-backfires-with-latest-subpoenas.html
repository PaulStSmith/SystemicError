Trump's lawsuit against the Pulitzer Prize Board backfires with latest subpoenas

In 2022, President Donald Trump sued the Pulitzer Prize Board members, but that is now coming back to haunt him.

Law&Crime reported Monday that the board members’ legal counsel is subpoenaing the full, unredacted copy of former special counsel Robert Mueller’s report to verify claims Trump made were “defamatory.”

Trump has filed several demands for depositions from board members in Okeechobee County, Florida, but the defendants formally sought the report as well as “all documents and communications exchanged between” Trump and Mueller’s team.

The request “includes but is not limited to […] any negotiation” with Mueller about Trump’s records or his “answers to written questions,” the 2016 hack of the DNC by Russians, and the WikiLeaks dump that followed, information on the Trump Tower Moscow project, Trump Jr.’s 2016 Trump Tower meeting, and more, quoted Law&Crime.

According to Trump, the Pulitzer board is responsible for reports by the New York Times and the Washington Post, which won awards for their 2017 coverage of the Russia scandal that the president still disputes. Trump continues to demand that the paper’s prizes be rescinded, even nine years later.

Since the Pulitzer Prize is the “pinnacle of American journalistic achievement,” Trump claimed that the award “carries very important connotations,” and comments from board members “damaged” his “reputation, profession, and business.” They allegedly “wrongfully imply criminal” and “un-American conduct” by Trump around what he calls the “Russia Collusion Hoax.”

“The Pulitzer Prize Board has an established, formal process by which complaints against winning entries are carefully reviewed,” the board told Trump when he first filed the lawsuit.

The board said that it commissioned “two independent reviews of the work submitted by those organizations to our National Reporting competition. Both reviews were conducted by individuals with no connection to the institutions whose work was under examination, nor any connection to each other. The separate reviews converged in their conclusions: that no passages or headlines, contentions or assertions in any of the winning submissions were discredited by facts that emerged subsequent to the conferral of the prizes.”

The Mueller report, made public, never alleges collusion between Trump and Russia. Instead, it highlights “numerous links between the Russian government and the Trump campaign.” So, the members requested access to that full, unredacted report.

Our Analysis:

Trump’s Legal Quagmire: A Tale of Hubris and Subpoenas

In a twist that seems lifted from the annals of poetic justice, former President Donald Trump’s litigious nature might just be his undoing. In a move that can only be described as spectacularly backfiring, Trump’s lawsuit against the Pulitzer Prize Board members has opened a Pandora’s box that could very well unleash a trove of uncomfortable truths for him.

The Lawsuit: A Brief Overview

Trump’s legal battle initiated in 2022 against the Pulitzer Prize Board centers on a grievance that would make lesser mortals balk: he claims that the Pulitzer awards given to The New York Times and The Washington Post in 2017 for their reporting on the Russian interference in the 2016 election were based on what he calls the “Russia Collusion Hoax”. Trump’s assertion that these reports were defamatory forms the crux of his lawsuit. However, in an ironic twist, the board’s legal defense’s latest move—to subpoena the full, unredacted Mueller report—might just spotlight Trump’s own missteps.

The Demand for the Mueller Report: Why It Matters

The request from the Pulitzer Prize Board’s legal counsel for the Mueller report, in its unredacted glory, is not just a mere procedural step. It is, in essence, a strategic maneuver to verify Trump’s claims against the veracity of the reporting he deems defamatory. This is where Trump’s strategy starts to crumble. The Mueller report, while not outright accusing Trump of collusion, documented “numerous links between the Russian government and the Trump campaign,” which hardly paints the picture of innocence Trump wishes to portray.

The Backfire: A Self-Inflicted Wound

Trump’s relentless pursuit to have the Pulitzer prizes rescinded based on his allegations has led him into treacherous legal waters. By demanding depositions from Pulitzer Board members in Okeechobee County, Florida, Trump essentially opened the door for the defendants to request comprehensive details of his interactions with Mueller’s team. This includes negotiations, written question answers, and more, related to various contentious points such as the DNC hack and the infamous Trump Tower Moscow project.

The Irony of Trump’s Claim

Trump’s claim that the Pulitzer Prize carries “very important connotations” and that comments from board members have “damaged” his “reputation, profession, and business” is an assertion rich with irony. The Pulitzer Board’s response to Trump’s initial lawsuit highlighted a rigorous review process that undermines Trump’s accusation of bias or wrongful implication. The board’s defense, armed with the Mueller report, stands as a testament to the journalistic integrity Trump so vehemently questions.

Conclusion: The Unraveling of a Lawsuit

Trump’s lawsuit against the Pulitzer Prize Board is more than a legal skirmish over awards; it’s a litmus test for the truth he so frequently claims to be in his favor. As the board’s legal team delves into the Mueller report, what emerges could either vindicate Trump or validate the reporting he has long disputed. This legal battle, initiated by Trump in an attempt to salvage what he believes to be his tarnished reputation, may instead serve to further underscore the links and actions he has spent years denying.

In the grand scheme of things, this lawsuit illuminates the peril of attempting to rewrite history through the courts. The subpoena for the Mueller report isn’t just a request for documents; it’s a demand for truth in a saga where transparency has often been cloaked in shadow. Trump’s pursuit of the Pulitzer Prize Board, intended to be a demonstration of his power to challenge and reshape narratives, may instead become a stark reminder that the past, especially one as documented and scrutinized as his, cannot be so easily contested or controlled.
