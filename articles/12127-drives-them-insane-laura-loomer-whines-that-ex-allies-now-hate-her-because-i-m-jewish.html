'Drives them insane!' Laura Loomer whines that ex-allies now hate her 'because I'm Jewish'

Conservative extremist and Donald Trump "whisperer" Laura Loomer posted a long lament on social media Saturday, complaining that she is a victim of "Jew hatred" from allies on the right — and that she can no longer ignore it.

Loomer, who has been the guiding force behind multiple firings in the Trump administration by ferreting out apostasy against the president. began by claiming that she has long been criticized for her faith by critics on the left — but now she sees it coming from her own side.

In her confessional post on X, she wrote, "I never realized how bad it was on the right until last year," before elaborating, "I started to realize how bad it was when people I have known for the last 10 years started excluding me from conversations about censorship, debanking, and cancel culture because they didn’t want to have a Jewish example of what has happened to some of them."

Complaining that some of them have used her as their "token" Jewish friend, she added, "Then, I realized it 100% when these same people who would ask me for favors would attack me on their own shows or their chat roots and accuse me of being an Israeli spy or a dual citizen because it was easier to say than to praise me for my hard hitting investigations."

"I don’t really talk to many people in right wing media anymore because I have come to see that the quality of my work doesn’t mean anything to any of them because I am Jewish," she accused and then added, "I used to not notice it as much, but now it’s become very obvious to me."

She continued, "These people will never admit to my struggle combatting cancel culture and censorship over the last 10 years and now hard I have worked to defeat it because it drives them insane that I was subjected to this despite being Jewish," before concluding with, "Maybe human beings would be better off in life being more reclusive and anti-social to protect ourselves from forces of jealousy, resentment and greed which drive all human conflicts.

"I think about that a lot when I remind myself that people operate on primarily resentment for others and animosity toward merit based systems. Maybe the world will never operate on a merit based system after all."

Our Analysis:

Laura Loomer's Lament: A Caustic Chronicle of Right-Wing Betrayal

In a recent social media outburst that could only be described as both astonishing and predictable, Laura Loomer, a figure known for her conservative extremism and close ties with Donald Trump, has aired her grievances in what could be seen as a dramatic act of public self-victimization. Loomer, who prides herself on being a de facto Trump "whisperer" and has been instrumental in orchestrating multiple dismissals within the Trump administration, has shifted her accusatory glare from the left to her own ideological backyard.

Her lament, posted on the platform X, is a long-winded diatribe that cries foul over perceived "Jew hatred" emanating from her allies on the right. It's a striking revelation, considering her history of targeting critics on the left with similar accusations. Loomer's narrative is rife with contradictions and ironies that beg for a closer examination.

The Victim Card Played with a Heavy Hand

Loomer's self-portrayal as a martyr in the battle against "censorship, debanking, and cancel culture" reveals a startling lack of self-awareness. She claims to have been ostracized and belittled by her peers, not for her incendiary views or controversial tactics, but solely because of her Jewish identity. This accusation comes from someone who has, without a hint of irony, utilized and benefited from the very culture of exclusion and cancelation she now decries.

Tokenism or Just Deserts?

Her complaint about being used as a "token" Jewish friend and then accused of being an "Israeli spy" or harboring dual loyalties is a textbook example of the kind of divisive rhetoric that has been a hallmark of her career. It is a potent reminder of the adage, "live by the sword, die by the sword." Loomer's shock at this turn of events is, in itself, shocking. Did she expect immunity from the vitriol and conspiracy theories that are currency in the circles she frequents?

The Quality of Work Defense

Loomer's assertion that the "quality of her work" has been ignored because of her Jewish identity is a deflection of the highest order. It's not her faith that's at issue here; it's the content and methodology of her work. Her career has been marked by sensationalism, misinformation, and outright conspiracy peddling. To claim that her current pariah status is due solely to anti-Semitism within right-wing media is to ignore the very essence of her public persona.

A Misguided Conclusion

Her final musings on human nature and the world's failure to operate on a merit-based system are perhaps the most telling part of her rant. Loomer seems to equate merit with notoriety, conflating her ability to stir controversy with genuine achievements. Her lament is less about the right's betrayal and more about her personal realization that the world of extremist politics is as fickle as it is ruthless.

In Conclusion:

Laura Loomer's recent outburst is a spectacle of irony and self-victimization. It's a vivid illustration of the toxic environment within right-wing extremist circles, where loyalty is fleeting, and allegiance is as shallow as the ideologies that bind them. Loomer's plight might evoke a twinge of sympathy if it weren't so steeped in hypocrisy and if she weren't such an active participant in the very culture she now finds herself ostracized by.

This episode serves as a cautionary tale about the dangers of extremist politics and the inevitable implosion that occurs when ideologues consume their own. Loomer's lament is not a call to action against anti-Semitism; it's a self-serving attempt to salvage her relevance in a movement that has moved on to the next controversy, leaving her in its wake.
