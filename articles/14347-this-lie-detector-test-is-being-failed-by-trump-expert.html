This 'lie detector test' is being failed by Trump: expert

By Paulo Santos

The former chair of the United States Council of Economic Advisers warned Monday that the bond markets are flashing red over Donald Trump’s economic agenda.

In a sharply worded MSNBC op-ed titled, “The Bond Market is an Economic Lie Detector Test,” Jared Bernstein, a Biden administration economist, argued that markets are reacting to “a toxic combination” of MAGA-inspired policies that threaten to both raise interest rates and slow growth.

Trump “is failing” the test, according to Bernstein, and “that’s bad news for your wallet," he added.

EXCLUSIVE: Trump accused of new grift that puts Qatari plane in shade

“The recklessness of both the Trump administration’s economic agenda and congressional Republicans’ deficit-exploding legislation are playing out in real time in ways you need to know about,” Bernstein wrote Monday. He pointed to the $1.8 trillion deficit in 2024 – 6.4% of GDP – as one factor rattling investors.

When the government borrows more, interest rates climb, he told readers. “Actions have consequences, and financial markets don’t subscribe to the administration’s alternate reality where everything’s fine and under control.”

The noted economist ended his op-ed Monday with a stark conclusion. “In fact, the bond market is loudly saying everything is far from fine — and the folks under control are out of control,” he said.

If Trump and his GOP allies continue down the same economic path, Bernstein warned, the economic consequences could show up in mortgage rates, credit cards, and auto loans.

Our Analysis:

The Bond Market's Stark Warning: A Clear-Eyed Look

In an MSNBC op-ed that cuts through the fog like a beacon of harsh truth, Jared Bernstein, a seasoned voice from the Biden administration, delivers a scathing critique of the Trump economic agenda. Through a lens of undeniable fact and economic theory, Bernstein's analysis, titled "The Bond Market is an Economic Lie Detector Test," lays bare the dangerous precipice upon which the Trump administration and its MAGA-driven policies precariously balance. Let's dissect the meat of this argument, layer by layer, to understand the depth of the impending doom they forecast.

Trump's Economic Folly: A Recipe for Disaster

At the core of Bernstein's argument is the assertion that the bond markets, those unyielding arbiters of economic reality, are signaling a storm on the horizon due to Trump's economic policies. This is not mere hyperbole; it's an alarm bell in a fog of political obfuscation. The markets are reacting, and not kindly, to what Bernstein aptly describes as "a toxic combination" of MAGA-inspired policies that are simultaneously primed to hike interest rates and stymie growth. The verdict? Trump is flunking the test, and according to Bernstein, "that's bad news for your wallet."

The Recklessness of Deficit-Exploding Legislation

Bernstein doesn't just throw around accusations without grounding them in hard, cold facts. He zeroes in on the projected $1.8 trillion deficit in 2024, a staggering 6.4% of GDP, as a primary culprit in this economic horror story. The mechanics are simple yet often ignored by those at the helm of Trump's policy-making machine: when the government borrows more, interest rates inevitably rise. This isn't speculative; it's a fundamental economic principle, one that the Trump administration seems to blithely disregard in favor of maintaining an "alternate reality."

The Consequences of Ignoring Economic Realities

Bernstein's piece culminates in a dire warning that cuts through the noise with the precision of a scalpel: the bond market is screaming that all is not well, and those supposed to be in control are anything but. This isn't just an academic exercise; the real-world implications of continuing down this path are profound. Bernstein warns of the tangible impacts on everyday Americans, with potential spikes in mortgage rates, credit card interest, and auto loan costs.

Conclusion: A Harsh Reality Check

In essence, Bernstein's op-ed serves as a necessary reality check in an era where economic policies are often dictated by political whims rather than prudent economic strategy. The bond market, with its unemotional and ruthless efficiency, is signaling a clear warning: the current trajectory of Trump's economic agenda is unsustainable. The implications of ignoring this warning are dire, not just for the abstract economy, but for the financial well-being of every American.

In a world where truth often becomes tangled in political rhetoric, Bernstein's analysis stands out as a beacon of clear, unvarnished reality. It's a reminder that, ultimately, the laws of economics cannot be bent by political will. As the Trump administration continues to peddle its economic fantasies, the bond market's warning grows louder and more urgent. Ignoring it is not just folly; it's a recipe for disaster.
