'I know this sounds wacko': Key signs flagged that Trump will never leave the White House

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosOctober 28, 20252 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

President Donald Trump speaks during the ASEAN-U.S. Summit in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia on Oct. 26, 2025. Vincent Thian/Pool via REUTERS

An analyst pointed to signs that President Donald Trump will never leave the White House, admitting, “I know this sounds crazy.”

Strategist Julie Roginsky tells MSNBC anchor Chris Jansing on Monday that Trump’s response that he’s not ruling out a third term — which is unconstitutional — and that he “would love to do it” signals he will not leave office.

“Nobody’s going to succeed him. He’s going to succeed himself. Let’s just be real,” Roginsky said.

“He’s not building that ballroom because he’s planning on leaving. He’s not going anywhere, and nobody’s going to get him out of there,” she added. “And by the way, the Supreme Court says you can’t run again. He will just ignore the courts. And I’m happy to see Marco Rubio without Steve Witkoff as our Secretary of State. So I’m happy to see that he’s appeared and popped up somewhere, but he’s not going to be the nominee. JD Vance is not going to be the nominee because Donald Trump is not going anywhere. And I understand that sounds crazy and wacko, but think about all the other crazy, wacko things that this president has done that we could not believe he’d ever do. And you see why he’s going to run again in 2028. And he’s not going anywhere unless, you know the ravages of time take him away. He’s not leaving that White House.”

The 22nd Amendment says that no person can be elected to the presidency more than twice. That includes the vice president, as both the president and the vice president are elected to office.
