'He can't win': Insiders cast doubt on White House 'fool's folly' bid to oust GOP governor

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosOctober 4, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

FILE PHOTO: U.S. President Donald Trump looks on, on the day he announces a deal with Pfizer to sell drugs at lower prices, in the Oval office of the White House in Washington, D.C., U.S., September 30, 2025. REUTERS/Ken Cedeno/File Photo

Pressure from Donald Trump’s White House to primary a Republican Party governor for refusing to redistrict her state is running into a wall of skepticism and resistance, reports Politico.

As the report notes, the White House is not happy with Gov. Kelly Ayotte (R-NH) for balking at demands to help Republicans increase their margin in the House and there are reports that part of the pressure campaign includes getting controversial Department of Homeland Security official Corey Lewandowski to run against her.

As Politico’s Lisa Kashinsky, Kelly Garrity and Myah Ward are reporting, there are upsides and downsides to Lewandowski leaving DHS, where he is rumored to be in a personal relationship with Director Kristi Noem, and may have overstayed his welcome, but leaving for a run would hand the White House an “off-ramp” to move him along, some suggest.

The report notes, “Amid the chatter about Lewandowski’s potential interest in running for governor, rumors also swirled this week that he was leaving the agency. One administration official said Lewandowski would only leave if requested by Trump or White House chief of staff Susie Wiles. The official, granted anonymity to discuss personnel matters, added that his exit would ‘dramatically change the atmosphere across DHS.’”

GOP insiders in New Hampshire aren’t so sure that primarying the incumbent is a good idea, with one doubting Lewandowski, who comes with considerable baggage, including accusations of assaulting a woman, could win.

“He’s obviously a smart guy, so he throws his name in there just to get headlines. But in the end, he’s not going to do it because he knows he can’t win,” one insider told Politico.

Influential former state Rep. Gene Chandler (R), described as a “power broker,” also cast doubts about the primary challenge, telling Politico, “It’s a fool’s folly.”

Our Analysis:

The Stark Reality of Political Maneuvering: Trump’s Push Against Gov. Ayotte

In what reads like a twisted plot from a political thriller, the latest maneuver from Donald Trump’s White House to oust Republican Governor Kelly Ayotte of New Hampshire reveals the murky depths to which political machinations can sink. The pressure to primary Gov. Ayotte for her refusal to manipulate redistricting in favor of the Republican Party’s hold in the House is a glaring testament to the lengths some are willing to go to secure power, regardless of the ethical implications.

The Controversial Chess Piece: Corey Lewandowski

At the heart of this political saga is Corey Lewandowski, a controversial figure with a history of accusations, including assaulting a woman. His potential candidacy against Gov. Ayotte is not just a political calculation; it’s a clear sign of desperation. The Politico report sheds light on the inner workings of a White House that seems to be grasping at straws, attempting to leverage Lewandowski’s notoriety for political gain. The notion that his departure from the Department of Homeland Security (DHS) would serve as an “off-ramp” for the White House to rid themselves of a problematic figure while simultaneously attacking a dissenting Republican governor is both cunning and deeply troubling.

The Questionable Strategy

The strategy to unseat Gov. Ayotte underscores a significant issue within the Republican Party: the prioritization of party loyalty over principle. The insistence on redistricting to maintain a House majority at the expense of fair representation is a stark reminder of the party’s willingness to manipulate the democratic process. Furthermore, the consideration of Lewandowski as a viable candidate, despite his considerable baggage, speaks volumes about the state of political discourse and the criteria for public office candidacy.

Skepticism and Resistance: A Glimmer of Hope?

The resistance and skepticism from GOP insiders and influential figures in New Hampshire offer a glimmer of hope in this sordid tale. The acknowledgment of Lewandowski’s unsuitability as a candidate by a GOP insider and the dismissal of the primary challenge as a “fool’s folly” by former state Rep. Gene Chandler suggest that there are still those within the party who prioritize integrity and the well-being of their constituents over blind allegiance to the whims of the White House.

Conclusion: A Reflection on Political Integrity

The attempt to primary Gov. Kelly Ayotte is more than just a political skirmish; it’s a reflection of the broader issues of integrity and ethics in politics. The willingness to employ controversial figures and pressure tactics to achieve partisan goals is a dangerous precedent. It undermines the democratic principles that should guide political action and erodes public trust in their elected officials. As this saga unfolds, one can only hope that reason and integrity will prevail over opportunism and manipulation.
