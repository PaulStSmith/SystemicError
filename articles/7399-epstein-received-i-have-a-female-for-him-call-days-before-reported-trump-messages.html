Epstein received “I have a female for him” call days before reported Trump messages

Jeffrey Epstein received a troubling phone message in 2004 from an unidentified caller stating, “I have a female for him,” according to newly released Justice Department documents. The message was recorded just days before Epstein reportedly received calls from Donald Trump during a high-stakes bidding war over a luxury Florida property.

The timing of the messages was highlighted Friday by retired journalism professor and former prosecutor Seth Abramson, who said their proximity raises unresolved questions about Epstein’s activities during that period.

“As a former investigator, I would have questions,” Abramson wrote on X. “In the same week Epstein and Trump were reportedly playing phone tag and Trump wanted a significant favor, Epstein received a message saying someone had a woman for him.”

The handwritten note containing the unidentified caller’s message was dated Nov. 8, 2004, and addressed to “J.E.,” an apparent reference to Epstein. While the caller’s name was redacted by the Justice Department, the message itself was left unredacted in the latest release of thousands of Epstein-related files.

In July, The Palm Beach Post reported that phone messages documenting calls from Trump to Epstein were discovered during a 2005 police raid on Epstein’s Palm Beach home. Those notes included Trump’s name and phone number and were dated between Nov. 11 and Nov. 20, 2004 — days after the unidentified message referencing a woman.

At the time, Trump and Epstein were competing to purchase a 43,000-square-foot waterfront estate in Palm Beach. Trump ultimately won the bidding war, acquiring the property for more than $41 million.

Trump is not facing criminal charges related to Epstein, nor has he been accused of wrongdoing in connection with the newly released documents.

Our Analysis:

The Tangled Webs of Power: Epstein, Trump, and a Disturbing Phone Message

In an era where the convergence of power, wealth, and moral turpitude seems all too common, the latest revelations from newly released Justice Department documents concerning Jeffrey Epstein provide a grim reminder of the dark underbelly of elite circles. A phone message from 2004, stark in its brevity and disturbing implications, states, “I have a female for him,” a sinister echo of the commodification of women pervasive in Epstein’s world. This message, precariously timed just days before Epstein reportedly juggled calls with none other than Donald Trump, throws a harsh light on their interactions during a luxury property bidding war in Florida.

The Implications of the Message

The handwritten note, undeniably addressed to “J.E.” and shrouded in anonymity due to redactions, points to the systemic and normalized exploitation within these networks. The caller, whose identity remains shielded by the Justice Department, is reduced to a footnote in a saga that reveals much about the transactions that underpin the lives of the super-rich and morally bankrupt.

The Trump-Epstein Connection: A Question of Morality

The proximity of this message to Trump’s attempts to contact Epstein, as detailed by retired journalism professor and former prosecutor Seth Abramson, cannot be overlooked. Their communication, ostensibly about a high-stakes real estate deal, now casts a shadow over Trump, despite no formal accusations or charges against him in relation to Epstein’s criminal activities. The timing is, at the very least, questionable and, at most, indicative of the murky moral waters these figures navigated.

Trump’s victory in the bidding war, securing a Palm Beach estate for over $41 million, is a mere footnote in the broader narrative of power plays and potentially exploitative relationships. The transaction is emblematic of the elite’s competitive displays of wealth, with the underlying dynamics between Trump and Epstein remaining a subject of speculation and concern.

A Systemic Failure to Protect

What is most alarming about this revelation is not just the explicit content of the message but what it represents: a systemic failure to protect the vulnerable from the predatory behaviors of powerful men. The document, part of a larger cache of Epstein-related files, underscores the failure of various institutions to adequately address and dismantle networks of abuse and exploitation.

The Need for Accountability

This episode, while only a single thread in the complex tapestry of Jeffrey Epstein’s crimes, serves as a stark reminder of the cultural and systemic enablers of sexual exploitation. The connections between Epstein and figures like Trump must be scrutinized, not to indulge in a witch hunt, but to reaffirm our collective commitment to justice and accountability, regardless of an individual’s wealth or status.

The revelation of this phone message should not be seen in isolation but as part of a broader, more disturbing narrative that requires unwavering attention and action. As the public sifts through the implications of these communications, the necessity for a thorough and unimpeded investigation into Epstein’s network and his connections remains paramount. Only through such scrutiny can we hope to untangle the web of complicity and ensure that such egregious abuses of power are not repeated.
