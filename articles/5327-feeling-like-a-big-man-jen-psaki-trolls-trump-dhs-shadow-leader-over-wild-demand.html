'Feeling like a big man!' Jen Psaki trolls Trump DHS shadow leader over 'wild' demand

MS NOW’s Jen Psaki ruthlessly mocked Department of Homeland Security official Corey Lewandowski’s efforts to pressure department officials to let him carry a government service gun with him, despite him not in any way meeting the criteria to be armed on duty.

This comes after extensive reporting about the friction caused behind the scenes by Secretary Kristi Noem’s alleged romantic affair with Lewandowski, whom she has effectively let run massive parts of department policy. Among other things, Noem is said to have fired a pilot over a missing blanket and to have made extensive use of a luxury plane with a private cabin in the back for herself and Lewandowski to travel in.

“There’s the incredible saga over Corey Lewandowski’s attempt to play policeman,” said Psaki. “This may be one of my favorite details ... according to the Wall Street Journal, Lewandowski made it known to top ICE officials that he wanted to be issued a law enforcement badge and a federally issued gun, despite the fact, of course, that he was not a law enforcement official and he has not undergone any law enforcement training that we know of whatsoever.”

“Now, Lewandowski tried asking ICE’s legal center to write him a justification for being issued a badge and a gun, and when a top ICE lawyer declined to sign off on the whole thing — I wonder why — he was placed on administrative leave,” said Psaki. “Lewandowski then eventually persuaded other lawyers to sign off on his gun-toting ambitions, feeling like a big man. Corey Lewandowski, I guess. And this is my favorite part. The ICE director’s autopen was used to sign off on the paperwork.”

“Now, of course, DHS denies that all of this happened and claimed that the ICE lawyer was put on administrative leave for other reasons,” Psaki continued. “Okay, but MS NOW has confirmed a large part of that story tonight, also reporting that the ICE lawyer was actually escorted from the building after refusing to sign off on Lewandowski’s request for a gun, according to two current and former officials.”

“The whole incident,” Psaki said, was “wild.”

Our Analysis:

The Farce of Corey Lewandowski’s Gun Quest: A Symptom of a Deeper Rot

In a display that oscillates between the ludicrous and the downright alarming, Corey Lewandowski’s attempt to arm himself with a government service weapon, despite a glaring lack of qualifications, underscores a broader and more disturbing trend within the current administration. Jen Psaki’s commentary on MS NOW, ruthlessly mocking Lewandowski’s farcical efforts, serves as a potent reminder of the absurdity and potential dangers inherent in the politicization of security apparatuses. But let’s dive deeper into the layers of this grotesque onion, shall we?

Firstly, the sheer audacity of Lewandowski’s request—to be issued a law enforcement badge and a federally issued gun without any of the requisite training or official standing—is nothing short of astounding. It’s akin to a child demanding the keys to a sports car without ever having taken a driving lesson. The fact that this request went as far as it did, with Lewandowski even attempting to coerce ICE’s legal center into justifying his whims, speaks volumes about the entitlement and hubris that pervades this administration’s inner circle.

The ICE Lawyer’s Stand and Subsequent Punishment: It’s somewhat heartening to see that there was resistance from at least one top ICE lawyer, a beacon of reason amidst this farce. However, the fact that this lawyer was then placed on administrative leave—ostensibly for “other reasons”—and escorted from the building, reeks of retaliation and suppression of dissent. This is not the hallmark of a healthy democratic institution but rather the modus operandi of authoritarian regimes.

The Bigger Picture: But let’s not lose sight of the forest for the trees. This incident is a symptom of a much larger disease: the weaponization of government for personal gain and vendettas. The alleged affair between Secretary Kristi Noem and Lewandowski, and the resultant influence Lewandowski wields, is cause for serious concern. From firing a pilot over a missing blanket to extravagant travels hinting at a misuse of taxpayer dollars, the Noem-Lewandowski saga paints a disturbing picture of nepotism and corruption.

The Mockery of Process: The use of the ICE director’s autopen to sign off on Lewandowski’s gun-toting dreams is a mockery of bureaucratic process and accountability. It’s a chilling reminder of how far some will go to bypass legal and ethical norms for personal power or protection. DHS’s denial, in light of MS NOW’s confirmation of the story, only adds insult to injury, showcasing a blatant disregard for transparency and truth.

In conclusion, while Psaki’s mockery on MS NOW might bring a momentary chuckle, the underlying issues at play are no laughing matter. They signal a dangerous erosion of standards, a blurring of personal and professional lines, and a cavalier attitude towards authority and governance. Lewandowski’s saga is but a piece in the larger puzzle of systemic dysfunction—a puzzle that, if left unsolved, threatens the very foundations of our democratic institutions.
