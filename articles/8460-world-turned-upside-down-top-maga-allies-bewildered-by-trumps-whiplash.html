President Donald Trump’s move to cast out loyalist Rep. Marjorie Taylor Greene (R-GA) and appear to embrace New York City’s new mayor, Zohran Mamdani, has left his most ardent top allies bewildered, with even former White House aide Steve Bannon remarking it’s a “world turned upside down.”

Trump revoked his endorsement of Greene following public spats over his handling of the Jeffrey Epstein files in a stunning break that led up to Greene’s announcement Friday night that she planned to resign from Congress in January. At the same time, Trump appeared to warm to Mamdani, despite warning New York City would see catastrophic consequences if it voted in the democratic socialist.

Bannon joined fellow MAGA die-hard Laura Loomer in expressing befuddlement, Politico reported Saturday.

“A world turned upside down,” far-right podcaster Bannon told the outlet in a text message.

Our Analysis:

Trump’s Political Acrobatics: A Dance of Disarray

In an almost theatrical turn of events, President Donald Trump’s decision to disavow Rep. Marjorie Taylor Greene (R-GA) and simultaneously cozy up to New York City’s new mayor, Zohran Mamdani, has sent shockwaves through the ranks of his most unwavering confidants. This bewildering shift has rendered even the likes of Steve Bannon, a former White House aide and a pillar of far-right rhetoric, to declare it a “world turned upside down.“

Greene’s Abrupt Ouster

Trump’s withdrawal of support for Greene, following their public disagreements over his handling of the Jeffrey Epstein files, culminated in Greene’s unexpected announcement of her intention to resign from Congress come January. This move is not just stunning; it’s a seismic shift in Trump’s political loyalties. Greene, a staunch loyalist known for her controversial and often extreme viewpoints, represented more than just a vote in Congress; she was a symbol of Trump’s enduring influence over the Republican Party. Her ousting signals a potential recalibration of Trump’s political alliances and priorities, a development that raises more questions than it answers.

An Unlikely Embrace?

Simultaneously, Trump’s apparent warming to Zohran Mamdani, a figure as far removed from Trump’s political ideology as one might imagine, adds layers of confusion and intrigue to this political saga. Mamdani, a democratic socialist whose victory Trump previously warned would spell disaster for New York City, being described as a “very rational person” by Trump is nothing short of bewildering. This unexpected endorsement, or at the very least, acknowledgment of rationality, stands in stark contrast to Trump’s historical disdain for anything remotely left of center.

The MAGA Confusion

Steve Bannon’s and Laura Loomer’s reactions encapsulate the broader sense of disorientation and disbelief among Trump’s base. Bannon’s remark about the situation being a “world turned upside down” is an understatement. For MAGA die-hards, Trump’s actions are not just confusing; they’re a direct challenge to the ideological purity test that has come to define Trumpism. Loomer’s admission of confusion and her quest for clarification from the administration highlight a significant rift. The question she poses, “What’s the purpose of people voting in 2026 if the Democrat policies are ‘rational?’” speaks to a fundamental concern about the direction and coherence of Trump’s political strategy.

Conclusion: A Strategic Miscalculation?

Trump’s latest maneuvers might be a miscalculation of epic proportions. By alienating Greene and, by extension, her followers, Trump risks fracturing his base. The attempt to engage with figures like Mamdani, whether genuine or a calculated act of political theater, is unlikely to endear him to the left or the center. Instead, it might just alienate the very core of his support. In politics, as in physics, every action has an equal and opposite reaction. Trump’s actions have sent ripples through the political landscape, the consequences of which are yet to fully unfold. But one thing is clear: in Trump’s quest to remain politically relevant, he may have just scored an own goal.
