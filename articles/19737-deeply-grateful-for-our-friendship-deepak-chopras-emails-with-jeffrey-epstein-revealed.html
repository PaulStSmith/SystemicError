CNN is reporting the connections between convicted sex trafficker Jeffrey Epstein and new age, wellness author Deepak Chopra.

In a televised report that aired Monday, CNN showed some of the hundreds of emails the two men exchanged over the years after Epstein earned his first conviction.

The documents show that their friendship was far more “intimate” than previously known.

Between the years 2016 and 2019, the two men also met up in person at Epstein’s New York City townhouse, South Florida home and Paris apartment.

They would chat about mutual friends, wellness, spirituality and the universe as a whole. Chopra even went so far as to sign off some of his messages using “Love” or “XO.”

In one message, Chopra said, “I’m deeply grateful for our friendship.”

Chopra appeared to be aware of Epstein’s collection of girls, reporters revealed.

“On at least two occasions, Chopra, who has been married for decades, invited Epstein to bring his ‘girls’ on trips,” the CNN report said.

“If you want use a fake name. Bring your girls,” Chopra wrote in a 2017 email to Epstein on his way to Israel.

Chopra added a note saying: “Your girls would love it as would you.”

Chopera then urged Epstein to attend a workshop he was holding in Switzerland. Again, he told Epstein to come “with your girls.”

Another 2017 email has Chopera referencing Epstein’s “girls” when speaking about former Saudi Princess Ameerah Al-Taweel. Chopra described her as “v sweet – like your girls.”

As the two discussed a speech Chopra was giving in Shanghai in 2017, Chopra wrote to Epstein: “Universe is human construct. … Cute girls are aware when they make noises.”

He later added: “God is a construct Cute girls are real.”

“I was never involved in, nor did I participate in, any criminal or exploitative conduct. Any contact I had was limited and unrelated to abusive activity,” Chopra wrote on X earlier in February.

Chopra added that he was “deeply saddened by the suffering of the victims in this case.”

He also said, “Some past email exchanges have surfaced that reflect poor judgment in tone. I regret that and understand how they read today, given what was publicly known at the time.”

Our Analysis:

The Unsettling Alliance: Deepak Chopra and Jeffrey Epstein

CNN’s latest report unearths the disconcerting depths of the connection between convicted sex trafficker Jeffrey Epstein and new age, wellness author Deepak Chopra. This isn’t just a story of fleeting acquaintanceship; it’s a saga of intimate emails and unsettling endorsements, painting a picture far darker and more complex than previously imagined.

The Emails: A Trail of Questionable Morality

The friendship, as detailed by CNN, wasn’t just a surface-level exchange of pleasantries. Between 2016 and 2019, Chopra and Epstein met up across Epstein’s various residences, engaging in discussions on wellness, spirituality, and the universe. Yet, it’s the content of their communication that sends chills down the spine. Chopra’s sign-offs, teeming with ‘Love’ and ‘XO’, might appear benign if not for the context they’re mired in.

The “Girls”: An Alarming Reference

Chopra’s awareness and even encouragement of Epstein’s notorious company is nothing short of horrifying. His invitations for Epstein to bring his “girls” along on trips, including to Israel and a workshop in Switzerland, are deeply troubling. The casual manner in which Chopra references these individuals—not as victims, but as companions for travel and leisure—reveals a stunning disregard for the gravity of Epstein’s crimes.

The Rhetoric: Disturbing and Dismissive

Chopra’s messages, including one bizarrely juxtaposing the universe as a human construct against the reality of “cute girls,” not only trivialize the experiences of Epstein’s victims but also demean them to mere objects of commentary. The attempt to separate the concept of “God” from the reality of these girls further insinuates a disturbing detachment from the severity of Epstein’s actions.

Chopra’s Defense: Insufficient and Insensitive

Chopra’s response to the revelations, claiming a lack of involvement in any criminal or exploitative conduct, falls flat. His admission of “poor judgment in tone” and expressions of regret do little to alleviate the concerns raised by his documented communications with Epstein. The claim of being “deeply saddened” by the victims’ suffering reads as a hollow, after-the-fact acknowledgment, overshadowed by his previous interactions and encouragements.

Conclusion: A Reflection of Systemic Error

This isn’t just a tale of two individuals but a reflection of a broader systemic failure to recognize and address the enablers of exploitation. Deepak Chopra, once a revered figure in wellness and spirituality, now stands as a testament to how societal pedestals can obscure troubling realities. As we dissect the layers of this alliance, we must confront the uncomfortable truths about the individuals we uplift and the signs we choose to ignore.

The CNN report serves not only as an exposé but as a call to action. It’s a reminder of the importance of vigilance, of the need to scrutinize the moral compasses of those in positions of influence, and of the fundamental obligation to stand with victims over perpetrators, no matter the social or spiritual standing of the latter.
