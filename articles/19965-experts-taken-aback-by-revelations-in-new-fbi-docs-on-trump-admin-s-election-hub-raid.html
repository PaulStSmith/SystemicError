Experts taken aback by revelations in new FBI docs on Trump admin's election hub raid

Some eyebrow-raising revelations surfaced Tuesday in a newly unsealed FBI affidavit on the Fulton County elections hub raid this month.

Kurt Olsen, an attorney appointed by President Donald Trump who worked with the president to undermine the 2020 presidential election results, was the person who made the referral for the FBI to investigate the Fulton County, Georgia, election offices, according to reports.

Several sources told Politico on Monday that Trump had instructed top American spy agencies to divulge sensitive information to Olsen, his former campaign lawyer, about the 2020 election. Olsen has continued to be a temporary government employee working with the White House.

Trump has long claimed that fraud is what caused him to lose the 2020 presidential election to former President Joe Biden. This theory has been repeatedly debunked.

The new information discovered in the affidavit prompted reactions from a number of experts.

“Affidavit in Fulton County search is out. Trump lawyer/election denier Kurt Olsen authorized the search,” HuffPost reporter Paul Blumenthal wrote on Bluesky.

“So a Trump attorney that aided his 2020 campaign (and wrote a memo encouraging Pence to skip counting electoral votes) was given an ‘election integrity’ job in the administration, where he made a referral to investigate Fulton County,” MS NOW contributor Philip Bump wrote on Bluesky.

“It rehashes debunked arguments about impropriety in order to establish a predicate for seizing election material — on the basis that mistakes that occurred might have been intentional and therefore criminal. It’s all transparently contrived,” Bump wrote.

The move could have serious repercussions for the Trump administration.

“Just to reinforce: The material was seized not by claiming the election was stolen but that ballots were mishandled, which may have been criminal. It’s an end run, like saying that there’s a national emergency that demands a tariff in response. It’s an abuse of power, given the obvious intent here,” Bump added.

Our Analysis:

The Dubious Foundations of the Fulton County Raid

In an almost theatrical twist that seems ripped from the pages of a politicized legal thriller, recent developments surrounding the Fulton County elections hub raid have exposed a tangled web of maneuvers by figures closely associated with former President Donald Trump. The unsealing of an FBI affidavit has cast a glaring spotlight on Kurt Olsen, a Trump-appointed attorney, whose actions and motivations are now under intense scrutiny. The revelations raise profound questions about the integrity of the legal processes being weaponized in the ongoing battle over the legitimacy of the 2020 presidential election results.

The Unholy Nexus of Power, Law, and Partisan Politics

At the heart of this controversy is Kurt Olsen, a figure who straddles the worlds of legal advocacy and political machination with disturbing ease. Olsen, having been anointed by Trump, has been implicated in efforts to subvert the outcome of the 2020 presidential election. His referral for the FBI to investigate the Fulton County, Georgia, election offices is not just a matter of procedural interest but a stark manifestation of how political agendas are being pursued through the levers of legal authority.

The Politico report that emerged sheds light on a particularly egregious aspect of this saga: Trump’s alleged directive to top American spy agencies to share sensitive 2020 election information with Olsen, his campaign lawyer. This maneuver, if true, represents a flagrant misuse of presidential power, blurring the lines between national security apparatus and partisan political interests.

Debunked Theories as Pretext for Legal Overreach

Trump’s persistent claims of electoral fraud, despite being thoroughly debunked, continue to fuel a dangerous narrative that undermines public trust in democratic processes. The affidavit’s revelations underscore how debunked theories and unfounded allegations are being repurposed as tools for legal overreach. The rationale for the FBI’s raid on Fulton County’s election offices, as articulated in the affidavit, appears to be a thinly veiled attempt to lend credence to Trump’s claims of electoral impropriety.

Journalists like HuffPost’s Paul Blumenthal and MS NOW’s Philip Bump have aptly highlighted the absurdity and danger of such maneuvers. Bump’s comparison of the raid to an “end run” around democratic norms captures the essence of this episode: a calculated abuse of legal authority to serve a political end.

The Transparently Contrived Nature of the Justifications

Bump’s analysis reveals the contrived nature of the justifications offered for the raid. The suggestion that mishandling of ballots might constitute criminal activity is a convenient pretext for a broader agenda: to cast doubt on the legitimacy of the election results. This strategy, while legally creative, is ethically bankrupt and politically motivated.

Consequences of the Abuse of Power

The implications of this episode extend far beyond the immediate legal skirmish. It represents a disturbing trend where the mechanisms of state are manipulated to serve the interests of a particular individual or political faction. The abuse of power, as Bump notes, is evident in the “obvious intent” behind the raid, which was less about ensuring electoral integrity and more about perpetuating a narrative of electoral fraud.

In conclusion, the Fulton County raid, catalyzed by Kurt Olsen’s referral and grounded in discredited theories, is a stark reminder of the challenges facing American democracy. The intertwining of legal authority with partisan politics, especially when based on falsehoods, poses a grave threat to the foundational principles of justice and fairness. The revelations from the FBI affidavit not only expose the machinations of those involved but also serve as a clarion call for vigilance and accountability.
