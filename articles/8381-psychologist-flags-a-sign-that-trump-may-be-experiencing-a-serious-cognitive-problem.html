A clinical psychologist flagged a sign that President Donald Trump may be experiencing a “serious cognitive problem” during a podcast interview that debuted on Sunday.

Trump has displayed some questionable behavior in recent days, including an instance where he snapped at a reporter on Air Force One and told her, “Be quiet, piggy.” The event renewed conversations about Trump’s cognitive health.

Dr. John Gartner, an author and former professor at Johns Hopkins University, discussed Trump’s mental fitness during an interview with The Daily Beast’s Joana Coles on a new episode of “The Daily Beast Podcast.”

Gartner noted that Trump has admitted to taking multiple cognitive tests since taking office. Trump appears to be divulging the information to address concerns about his mental state, but Gartner said the admissions reveal a much larger problem.

“They said they did advanced imaging,” Gartner said. “Okay, well, Trump said not once, not twice, but three times that he had taken cognitive tests. Plural. Okay, so not just a screening exam...They gave him multiple tests. We do not give people multiple cognitive tests unless we suspect there’s a serious cognitive problem.”

“We also never, ever...give someone an MRI unless we suspect or need to rule out a serious problem,” he continued. “So, we know his doctors gave him multiple cognitive tests and an MRI. They didn’t say explicitly because of the brain, but we can certainly be sure they scanned his brain. If they’re giving him a neuropsychological battery, they’re scanning his brain.”

“If Donald Trump were just an ordinary patient and you saw these kinds of serious signs of dementia, a responsible doctor would give him both a neuropsychological battery and an MRI,” he added. “Of course, they’re not telling us why they gave those tests.
They’re not telling us the results.”

Our Analysis:

Trump’s Cognitive Health: A Deep Dive into the Alarming Signs

In a recent revelation that has sent shockwaves through the political and medical communities alike, Dr. John Gartner, a clinical psychologist with a notable background, including a former professorship at Johns Hopkins University, has raised an alarm over President Donald Trump’s cognitive health. During a podcast interview on “The Daily Beast Podcast” with Joana Coles, Gartner dissected Trump’s recent behavior and the implications of his multiple cognitive tests and MRI.

The Unsettling Incident

Trump’s behavior has always been a subject of scrutiny and controversy, but the situation seems to have escalated. A particularly disturbing incident on Air Force One, where Trump derogatorily addressed a reporter as “Be quiet, piggy,” has reignited concerns over his mental fitness. Such outbursts are not only unbecoming of a president but also indicative of deeper issues.

The Admission That Speaks Volumes

Trump’s own admissions have inadvertently thrown a spotlight on his possible cognitive decline. Gartner points out a critical detail that cannot be ignored: Trump has undergone multiple cognitive tests. In the medical world, such steps are not taken lightly. They are red flags, signaling that healthcare professionals have serious concerns about a patient’s cognitive health.

The Diagnostic Process Unpacked

Gartner’s insights into the diagnostic process are particularly revealing. The fact that Trump has been subjected to not just a single screening exam, but multiple cognitive tests and an MRI, speaks volumes. These are not routine procedures. They are comprehensive evaluations aimed at diagnosing significant cognitive impairments.

The necessity of an MRI further underscores the gravity of the situation. This imaging test is a powerful tool for examining the brain’s anatomy, and its use here suggests an attempt to rule out or confirm serious neurological conditions. The secrecy surrounding the reasons for these tests and their results only adds to the mounting concerns.

The Implications

Gartner’s analysis brings to light a worrying possibility: that the leader of the free world might be grappling with “serious signs of dementia.” If Trump were any ordinary patient, the course of action—based on the evidence at hand—would be clear and unambiguous. Yet, the veil of secrecy that shrouds the health of public figures has left the public in the dark.

Conclusion

The revelations from Dr. John Gartner raise more questions than answers about President Trump’s cognitive health. The fact that a figure of Trump’s stature is undergoing such extensive cognitive evaluations should be a matter of public concern and debate. It’s high time for transparency and accountability, as the implications of cognitive impairment in a sitting president could have far-reaching consequences for national and global affairs.

In a political climate already rife with controversy and division, the issue of Trump’s mental fitness adds another layer of complexity to the narrative. The public deserves clarity on the health and capability of their leaders. The silence and secrecy that currently prevail serve no one, least of all a populace whose safety and well-being hinge on the sound judgment of its highest official.
