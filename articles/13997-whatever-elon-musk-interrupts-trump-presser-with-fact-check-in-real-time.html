'Whatever': Elon Musk interrupts Trump presser with fact check in real time

Elon Musk looks on in the Oval Office as he attends a press conference with U.S. President Donald Trump, at the White House in Washington, D.C., U.S., May 30, 2025. REUTERS/Nathan Howard

As President Donald Trump claimed he was "disappointed" with Elon Musk's criticism of his spending bill during an Oval Office press conference Thursday, the Tesla founder posted his thoughts on the bill to X.

"Slim Beautiful Bill for the win," Musk posted as Trump's press conference continued on.

Musk followed-up with, "Keep the good, remove the bad."

He also posted a "100" emoji in response to a post saying, "The Senate can cut the pork and send it back. We don't have to throw the baby out with the bathwater here."

Musk kept posting as Trump claimed Musk was upset about incentive cuts to electric vehicles.

"Whatever. Keep the EV/solar incentive cuts in the bill, even though no oil & gas subsidies are touched (very unfair!!), but ditch the MOUNTAIN of DISGUSTING PORK in the bill," Musk wrote. "In the entire history of civilization, there has never been legislation that both big and beautiful. Everyone knows this! Either you get a big and ugly bill or a slim and beautiful bill. Slim and beautiful is the way."

Musk added, "False, this bill was never shown to me even once and was passed in the dead of night so fast that almost no one in Congress could even read it!"

Earlier this week, Musk called the bill an "abomination" and urged Congress to "kill the bill."

Our Analysis:

The Great Charade: Trump, Musk, and the "Slim Beautiful Bill"

In a theatrical display of political and corporate drama, President Donald Trump and tech magnate Elon Musk have engaged in a public feud over a controversial spending bill. This spectacle, while entertaining, underscores the deep-seated issues within American politics and the influence of powerful individuals in shaping policy discourse. Let's dissect the farce and the underlying truths it reveals.

Trump's "Disappointment" and Musk's Social Media Barrage

President Trump expressed his "disappointment" in Musk's criticism during a press conference, a move that seems more aligned with high school drama than the governance of a nation. Meanwhile, Musk took to X (formerly Twitter) to unleash a series of posts that ranged from vaguely supportive ("Slim Beautiful Bill for the win") to outright critical ("MOUNTAIN of DISGUSTING PORK in the bill"). This exchange highlights the increasingly theatrical nature of political discourse, where public figures engage in performative disagreements on social media platforms rather than substantive policy discussions.

The Substance (or Lack Thereof) of Musk's Critiques

Musk's commentary on the bill is a masterclass in being vaguely specific. He calls for keeping the "good" and removing the "bad," a statement so devoid of substance it's practically ethereal. He criticizes the "MOUNTAIN of DISGUSTING PORK" without detailing what he considers to be "pork," leaving his followers to fill in the blanks with their biases.

His claim that "In the entire history of civilization, there has never been legislation that both big and beautiful" is a hyperbolic simplification of legislative history, ignoring the complexity and necessity of comprehensive legislation in addressing multifaceted issues.

The Irony of Musk's EV/Solar Incentive Comments

Musk's stance on keeping EV/solar incentive cuts in the bill, despite the continuation of oil & gas subsidies, is a curious one. Given that Tesla, Musk's own company, has benefitted significantly from government subsidies for electric vehicles and solar panels, his willingness to sacrifice these incentives seems either a display of unexpected altruism or a calculated move to maintain a facade of impartiality. The latter seems more plausible, considering the potential for these cuts to harm competitors who are more reliant on such incentives than Tesla might currently be.

The Veracity of Musk's Claims

Musk's assertion that the bill was "passed in the dead of night so fast that almost no one in Congress could even read it" is a serious allegation, suggesting a lack of transparency and due diligence in the legislative process. However, without specific evidence to support this claim, it remains part of Musk's rhetorical flourish designed to undermine confidence in the legislative process and, by extension, in the very institutions that have helped to subsidize and support the growth of his businesses.

Conclusion: The Theatre of the Absurd

The exchange between Trump and Musk over the spending bill is emblematic of a broader issue in American politics: the outsized influence of individual personalities over substantive policy debate. As the public is drawn into these personal dramas, the real issues at stake—the details of the spending bill, its potential impacts on various sectors, and the implications for ordinary Americans—receive less attention than they deserve.

In this theatre of the absurd, the audience is left wondering whether these performances are a distraction from the systemic issues plaguing American democracy. As we navigate this era of personality politics, it becomes increasingly important to cut through the noise and demand clarity, substance, and genuine accountability from our leaders and influencers.
