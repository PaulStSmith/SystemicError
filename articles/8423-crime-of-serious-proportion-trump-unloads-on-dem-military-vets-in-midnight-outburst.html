Donald Trump at around midnight had an “outburst” in which he said Democratic military veterans have committed “a crime of serious proportion.”

Trump took to Truth Social to respond to a video in which several Dem lawmakers with military and intelligence backgrounds reminded troops about their constitutional duty to deny “illegal” orders. An MSNBC host hours later called Trump’s comments an “outburst.”

Our Analysis:

Trump’s Midnight Rant: A Study in Hyperbole and Misinterpretation

In what can only be described as a classic display of Donald Trump’s penchant for dramatics and distortion, the former president took to Truth Social around midnight in a fiery “outburst,” launching an attack on Democratic military veterans. His allegations? That they committed “a crime of serious proportion.” This claim, bold and brash as ever, was sparked by a video where Democratic lawmakers, veterans of military and intelligence services themselves, reminded troops of their constitutional duty to refuse illegal orders.

Let’s dissect the meat of this melodrama:

The Misconstrued Message

Trump and his MAGA cohort leaped to suggest that the video was a call for troops to ignore lawful orders. This interpretation is a stretch at best and a deliberate misreading at worst. The video did exactly what democracy demands – it reinforced the bedrock principle that the military owes its allegiance to the Constitution, not to any one individual, even if he or she is the Commander-in-Chief.

Trump’s Accusation of Sedition

Trump’s response was nothing short of incendiary. “THE TRAITORS THAT TOLD THE MILITARY TO DISOBEY MY ORDERS SHOULD BE IN JAIL RIGHT NOW,” he declared, elevating the situation to “SEDITION AT THE HIGHEST LEVEL.” This accusation is both ironic and alarming. Sedition, a term historically weaponized against dissenters, is here used to vilify those upholding a foundational constitutional principle. It’s a bold move, to accuse others of a “major crime” for doing nothing more than reminding military personnel of their oath.

The Lack of Evidence

In a subsequent post, Trump claimed that “MANY GREAT LEGAL SCHOLARS AGREE” with his interpretation. Yet, he failed to name any of these scholars or provide a shred of evidence to support his serious allegations. This tactic isn’t new to Trump’s playbook. It’s a classic Trump maneuver: make a bold claim, cite unnamed experts, and offer no proof.

The Media’s Reaction

An MSNBC host later labeled Trump’s comments as an “outburst,” a term that feels almost too gentle for the gravity of Trump’s accusations. This wasn’t merely an outburst; it was a calculated attempt to delegitimize and criminalize legitimate political discourse and constitutional fidelity.

Conclusion: A Distortion of Duty

Trump’s late-night tirade is a textbook example of his strategy to weaponize misinformation and stir up controversy. By misinterpreting the video’s message and hurling accusations of sedition, Trump seeks to undermine the very essence of military duty to the Constitution. It’s a dangerous game of twisting truths to fit a narrative, one that seeks to erode trust in democratic institutions and principles.

The real “crime of serious proportion” here isn’t the reminder of constitutional duty by Democratic veterans. Rather, it’s the deliberate distortion of that reminder for political gain. In Trump’s world, upholding the Constitution appears to be sedition, a notion as dangerous as it is absurd.
