We want to inform you that Raw Story will no longer post on our official editorial account on X, the platform formerly known as Twitter.

This decision has been a long time coming. As the U.S. faces the most controversial presidency in our history, it’s clear that X has become a breeding ground for far-right disinformation, hate speech, and conspiracy theories — often amplified by the platform’s owner, Elon Musk.

The risks of continuing to engage there now outweigh the rewards, and we believe our resources are better spent amplifying fearless journalism through platforms that reflect our values.

To be clear: readers can still share our stories on X, and our reporters may occasionally use the platform for news-gathering purposes or to embed relevant content when reporting warrants it.

But Raw Story will no longer participate in promoting our journalism through a platform that increasingly serves as a megaphone for authoritarian propaganda and misinformation.

Raw Story’s independence has always been fueled by our readers — not Big Tech algorithms. Our breaking news reporting is free to read and open to all our readers, and we invite you to support us directly to help keep it that way.

Please consider making a one-time contribution to help us continue our efforts. No amount is too small. Now more than ever, independent journalism matters. Thank you for standing with us — The Raw Story Team.

Our Analysis:

Raw Story Bids Adieu to X: A Bold Move or A Necessary Retreat?

In an era where the digital landscape is as volatile as the political climate it mirrors, Raw Story's recent announcement about severing ties with X (the platform formerly known as Twitter) is a move that deserves more than a passing glance. The platform, under the stewardship of Elon Musk, has been accused of becoming a cesspool for far-right disinformation, hate speech, and conspiracy theories. But is Raw Story's departure an act of valiant defiance, or a strategic withdrawal from a battlefield that's become too perilous? Let's dissect.

The Catalyst for Change

Raw Story's decision didn't emerge from a vacuum. It's a response to what they describe as "the most controversial presidency in our history," coupled with the transformation of X into what they perceive as a "megaphone for authoritarian propaganda and misinformation." This isn't just a change in platform preference; it's a stand against a culture that they believe has strayed far from journalistic integrity and democratic discourse.

Elon Musk: A Polarizing Figure

It's no secret that Musk's acquisition and subsequent management of X have been polarizing, to say the least. The accusation that Musk himself has played a role in amplifying questionable content adds another layer of complexity to the issue. It's a significant claim, suggesting that the problem isn't just the platform's users, but its ownership. This is a bold accusation and one that reflects the gravity of Raw Story's concerns.

The Risks vs. Rewards Equation

Raw Story's assertion that the "risks of continuing to engage there now outweigh the rewards" is a telling statement. It speaks to a broader dilemma facing many media outlets and content creators today: How does one navigate a platform that offers vast reach but is riddled with ethical landmines? The answer, it seems, is not straightforward and depends heavily on an organization's values and the boundaries they are willing to set for themselves.

A Commitment to Independence

One of the more admirable aspects of Raw Story's announcement is their emphasis on independence, fueled by reader support rather than Big Tech algorithms. This isn't just about distancing themselves from a problematic platform; it's a reaffirmation of their commitment to "fearless journalism." The call for direct reader support underscores a desire to remain untethered to the whims of tech giants and their opaque algorithms. It's a challenging path but one that aligns with their values.

The Role of Platforms in Journalism

As Raw Story steps back, it's worth pondering the role social media platforms play in journalism today. While these platforms can amplify important stories, they can also mire those stories in a quagmire of misinformation and toxicity. The decision to engage or disengage with these platforms is not merely tactical; it's deeply ethical. Raw Story's move highlights the ongoing tension between reach and integrity within digital journalism.

In Conclusion: A Call to Action

Raw Story's departure from X is more than a strategic pivot; it's a call to action for independent journalism and its supporters. It's a reminder that in the face of disinformation and discord, the values of truth, integrity, and independence must be upheld, even if it means leaving behind a platform with immense reach. Whether this move will inspire similar actions from others remains to be seen, but one thing is clear: the battle for a truthful discourse is ongoing, and the terrain is ever-changing.
