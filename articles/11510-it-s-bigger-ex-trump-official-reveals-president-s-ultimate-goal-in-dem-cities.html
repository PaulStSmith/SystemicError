'It's bigger': Ex-Trump official reveals president's 'ultimate goal' in Dem cities

Miles Taylor, a former Department of Homeland Security official who served in President Donald Trump's first term, asserted that the commander-in-chief was deploying the military in U.S. cities for "power and leverage."

During a Sunday appearance on MSNBC, Taylor disagreed with pundits who believe that Trump's goal was to crack down on crime and immigration.

"I would say it's even bigger than that," he explained. "And I think immigration is going to be used as the Trojan horse to make all of this happen. I mean, well, it's like a Russian doll of Trojan horses to mix a whole bunch of metaphors."

"But for Donald Trump, it's about neither of those things," he continued. "It's about power and leverage. And that sounds abstract. That sounds conspiratorial. But everything that he does by his own explication is about how do you get the leverage to do the next thing you want."

Taylor pointed out that Trump was using a common authoritarian tactic.

"You control the guys with the guns," he noted. And especially if that's in Democratic cities, then it doesn't matter whether it's immigration or whether it's federal housing law or whether it's student loans or whether it's universities."

"If he controls force in that territory," he added. "He has the leverage to go get all of those things done, to coerce those localities and states to do what he wants. That is ultimately the goal here."

Our Analysis:

Miles Taylor's Scathing Critique of Trump's Use of Military Power

Miles Taylor, a name that resonates with a certain level of insider authority given his former position within the Department of Homeland Security under President Donald Trump, has thrust a sharp and unsettling accusation into the public discourse. In a recent appearance on MSNBC, Taylor peeled back the layers of Trump's intentions behind deploying the military in U.S. cities, suggesting a motive far more sinister than mere concern over crime and immigration. His analysis, while it may sound conspiratorial to some, is grounded in a chilling logic that demands attention.

Power and Leverage: A Dangerous Game

At the heart of Taylor's argument is the assertion that Trump's strategy is not about addressing the issues directly. Instead, it's about wielding power and gaining leverage. This tactic, as Taylor pointedly notes, is not about solving problems but about controlling the chessboard. The deployment of the military, according to him, is less about the safety of American cities and more about a show of force, a demonstration of power to bend local and state authorities to his will.

The Misuse of Military Might

Taylor's comparison of Trump's tactics to that of an authoritarian regime is both bold and alarming. The phrase "You control the guys with the guns" encapsulates the stark reality of what it means to have the military under one’s thumb. The implications of such power, particularly when wielded with the intent to subjugate democratic cities, cannot be overstated. It suggests a dystopian scenario where might makes right, and where the military is used as a tool for political coercion rather than national defense.

Immigration as the Trojan Horse

Taylor's metaphor of immigration being used as a "Trojan horse" is particularly poignant. It suggests a strategy of using a seemingly innocuous issue to mask a broader agenda of expanding control and influence. This "Russian doll of Trojan horses" analogy might mix metaphors, but it effectively conveys the complexity and deceit embedded in Trump's policies. It’s a stark reminder that what is often presented to the public is merely the surface of deeper, more calculated maneuvers.

The Bigger Picture

What Taylor is highlighting is a disturbing vision of governance where power is pursued for its own sake, and where the ends justify the means. This approach not only undermines the democratic principles upon which the United States is built but also sets a dangerous precedent for the use of military force within domestic borders. The idea that any President could use the military as a personal tool to gain leverage over state and local governments is antithetical to democracy.

Conclusion: A Call for Vigilance

Miles Taylor's warnings serve as a stark reminder of the fragility of democratic institutions and the lengths to which some will go to consolidate power. While some may dismiss his analysis as exaggerated or conspiratorial, the underlying logic of his argument is hard to ignore. It’s a call to vigilance for both the public and policymakers to ensure that the military is never used as a pawn in political games. Trump's deployment of the military, as analyzed by Taylor, is not just about the immediate impacts but about setting a precedent that could fundamentally alter the balance of power in the United States.
