The Supreme Coup: From Judicial Power Grab to Justices Who Must Be Removed

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosJune 29, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

We demand impeachment.

In a 6-3 ruling that will reverberate through every courthouse and community in America, the Supreme Court's conservative majority declared war on the nation's ability to defend itself against tyranny. On June 27, 2025, the Court ruled in Trump v. CASA, Inc. that lower courts no longer have the authority to issue nationwide injunctions. With a single decision, the Court stripped federal district judges of their power to halt unconstitutional federal actions across the country—a lifeline often used to protect marginalized communities from sweeping executive overreach.

Make no mistake: this is not judicial restraint. This is a judicial coup.

Amy Coney Barrett, writing for the majority, dressed up the power grab in legalese: universal injunctions, she claimed, "exceed the bounds of judicial equity" under the Judiciary Act of 1789. But this was never about equity. It was about enabling one man—Donald Trump—to enact his extremist policies unchecked. At the center of the case was Executive Order 14160, a grotesque attempt to restrict birthright citizenship. The lower courts halted it with injunctions that protected everyone. Now, those protections are dead.

The majority’s message is clear: unless you’re one of the named plaintiffs in a lawsuit, you can be bulldozed by illegal policies. The Supreme Court has effectively neutered the judiciary as a check on executive abuse, leaving us all to fend for ourselves in the courts, case by agonizing case.

That would be egregious enough on its own. But the rot at the Court's core runs deeper—right into the chambers of Justices Samuel Alito and Clarence Thomas, two men who have not only authored corrosive decisions like this one, but who are also walking ethics violations masquerading as jurists.

Let’s start with Thomas. For years, he has accepted lavish gifts from Republican mega-donor Harlan Crow—private jet flights, luxury vacations, even school tuition payments for a relative. He disclosed none of it. On top of that, his wife, Ginni Thomas, was actively involved in efforts to overturn the 2020 election, including text messages urging White House officials to subvert the results. Clarence Thomas then refused to recuse himself from cases involving January 6 or Trump’s election challenges. That isn’t oversight; it’s complicity.

Samuel Alito, for his part, decided subtlety was optional. Twice in recent years, far-right insurrectionist symbols have flown over his homes—the upside-down American flag, and the "Appeal to Heaven" flag, both now recognized signs of Stop the Steal partisanship. When caught, Alito blamed his wife. Then, he refused to recuse himself from any case related to Trump or January 6.

These men are not impartial arbiters of the law. They are partisans in robes. Their allegiance is not to the Constitution, but to a reactionary movement that views the rule of law as an obstacle, not a foundation.

The consequence of allowing them to remain on the bench is not abstract. It is immediate and real. With the CASA decision, they have kneecapped the judicial system’s ability to act as a national shield against unconstitutional policies. It is the beginning of a new era where only the Supreme Court—dominated by right-wing ideologues—can issue relief for the nation as a whole. Lower courts? Sidelined. People harmed? Tough luck.

And let’s be honest: this isn’t a legal philosophy. This is tactical warfare. The Court is consolidating power at the top, where it can be controlled by six justices who have been groomed, vetted, and installed by billionaires and culture warriors who want America remade in their image.

So what do we do?

We demand impeachment.

Congress has the authority—and the obligation—to impeach Justices who commit high crimes, misdemeanors, or abuses of office. Accepting bribes. Failing to disclose financial entanglements. Participating in cases where they have a clear conflict of interest. Flying seditious symbols and refusing to step aside. That meets the bar. It shatters it.

Impeachment isn’t about punishing political enemies. It’s about preserving the legitimacy of the judiciary. A Supreme Court that acts as an unelected super-legislature, unbound by ethics and accountability, is a threat to democracy itself. We are long past the point where polite rebukes and editorials will suffice.

Alito and Thomas are the symptom. The power grab in Trump v. CASA is the disease. And the cure must start with removal.

We must also demand that Congress reclaim the tools of democratic oversight: legislation mandating enforceable codes of ethics for Justices, restoring the power of district courts to issue national relief when rights are violated, and expanding the bench if necessary to break the Federalist chokehold.

Enough deference. Enough compromise. Enough pretending that this Court is functioning as intended.

We are in the middle of a slow-moving constitutional collapse. The Supreme Court is no longer a backstop. It is a battering ram.

Either we stop it now, or it will demolish what little remains of our democratic guardrails.

Impeach the Justices. Reclaim the judiciary.

Before it’s too late.
