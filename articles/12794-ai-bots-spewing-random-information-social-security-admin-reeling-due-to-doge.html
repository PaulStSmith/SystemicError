'AI bots spewing random information': Social Security Admin reeling due to DOGE

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosJuly 14, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

A woman holds a sign during a protest against cuts made by U.S. President Donald Trump’s administration to the Social Security Administration, in White Plains, New York, U.S., March 22, 2025. REUTERS/Nathan Layne

Elon Musk's Department of Government of Efficiency (DOGE) has left a wide swath of "chaos and dysfunction" within the halls of the Social Security Administration with recipients facing new and, at times, insurmountable obstacles getting help.

According to a report from MSNBC by Zeeshan Aleem, Musk and some of his DOGE employees may be gone, but the damage has been done and watchdogs are stunned by the staffing cuts that have left seniors scrambling when they can get someone to answer the phone.

Noting a Washington Post report detailing "swamped" phone lines, MSNBC is reporting that callers are being forced to wait up to five hours to get through.

According to Jessica LaPointe, of the American Federation of Government Employees (AFGE), transfers and dismissals of staffers have severely damaged the agency's ability to provide support.

“The 1-800 number — they do offer a critical role at the agency, but it’s triage, whereas customer service representatives actually clear work for the agency,” LaPointe explained. “So it’s just going to create a vicious cycle of work not getting cleared, people calling for status on work that’s sitting because the claims specialists now are going to have to pick up the slack of the customer service representatives that are redeployed to the tele-service centers.”

Jen Burdick, a Social Security expert with Community Legal Services added, "We spend a lot of time calling Social Security offices on people’s behalf — sometimes 15 times a day. We’re on hold for hours, then get AI bots spewing random information you never asked for before hanging up.”

Our Analysis:

The DOGE Debacle: A Masterclass in Mismanagement

Elon Musk's foray into government efficiency, through the establishment of the Department of Government of Efficiency (DOGE), has swiftly morphed into a cautionary tale of ambition clashing with reality—leaving the Social Security Administration (SSA) in a state of disarray that would be comedic if it weren't so tragically affecting some of the most vulnerable in society. The MSNBC report by Zeeshan Aleem paints a grim picture of a system in turmoil, where seniors are left to navigate a labyrinth of bureaucratic inefficiency, a situation that seems less about streamlining and more about gutting critical infrastructure.

The Fallout: A System in Crisis

The heart of the issue lies in the staffing cuts implemented under DOGE's watch. These cuts have not only diminished the workforce but have also dismantled the very fabric of the SSA's operational capabilities. It's a classic example of the adage, "If it ain't broke, don't fix it," being flagrantly ignored. The result? Callers now face up to five-hour waits to get through to someone who might be able to assist them—assuming they don't first get lost in an abyss of automated responses.

Jessica LaPointe of the American Federation of Government Employees (AFGE) succinctly captures the absurdity of the situation. The 1-800 number, intended as a lifeline, has become a purgatorial loop for those seeking help. The strategic misstep of redeploying customer service representatives to tele-service centers has not alleviated the workload but compounded it, creating a vicious cycle of unresolved cases and mounting frustration.

Jen Burdick's experience further illustrates the Kafkaesque scenario unfolding within the SSA. The notion that legal professionals are required to call the SSA upwards of 15 times a day on behalf of clients, only to be met with the monotonous drone of AI, is a stark indicator of a system that has not been streamlined but strangled.

The Culprit: A Misguided Vision of Efficiency

At the heart of this chaos is a fundamental misunderstanding of what government efficiency truly means. Efficiency is not merely about cutting costs and reducing staff; it's about enhancing the service provided to the public. It requires a nuanced approach that balances fiscal responsibility with operational effectiveness. The DOGE initiative, under Musk's guidance, seems to have missed this point entirely, opting instead for a scorched-earth policy that prioritizes optics over outcomes.

The repercussions of these actions are far-reaching. Seniors, who depend on the SSA for essential support, are left feeling abandoned and helpless. The additional stress and uncertainty placed on an already vulnerable demographic is not just an operational failure; it's a moral one.

Looking Forward: Rebuilding Trust

The departure of Musk and some DOGE employees does little to mend the fissures that have formed. The task now falls to the remaining staff and the broader governmental oversight bodies to rectify these missteps. This will require more than just reversing staffing cuts; it will necessitate a comprehensive review of the policies enacted under DOGE's tenure and an earnest effort to restore faith in the SSA.

The MSNBC report on the turmoil within the SSA is a sobering reminder of the consequences of reckless reform. It underscores the importance of thoughtful, informed policymaking that prioritizes the welfare of the public over the allure of efficiency for efficiency's sake. The DOGE debacle is not just a failure of execution but a failure of vision—a stark lesson for future endeavors in government reform.
