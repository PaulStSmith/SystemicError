Donald Trump’s decision to turn on Rep. Marjorie Taylor Greene (R-GA), combined with outside influencers like podcaster Joe Rogan falling out of love with the president on a multitude of fronts, is set to cripple his agenda and eventually open him up to a wave of investigations.

A panel convened on MS NOW to discuss the fallout in the Greene--Trump MAGA alliance agreed that no one who has been strongly allied with the volatile president is immune to his wrath if they disagree with him in the slightest.

Speaking with host Chris Jansing, Democratic adviser and MS NOW analyst Michael Hardaway claimed the president is going to be increasingly getting the cold shoulder from lawmakers he has come to depend upon and facing Democrats out for blood.

Former Republican Party campaign adviser Tara Setmayer set the stage for Hardaway when she told the host, “Look, this is a point about loyalty, right? Because she’s not the first one who has been tremendously loyal to him and found out it’s not necessarily a two-way street, right? Which is why I wonder why she’s so shocked that it happened to her.”

“No one is immune from Donald Trump turning on them, no one,” she added. “ He’s done it to every person; he even turned on his loyal mentor Roy Cohn in the ‘80s, who basically built Donald Trump into the monster that he is today and taught him everything he knows. He turned on him. He would turn on his own children.”

With Jansing noting new reports about Republican lawmakers looking for an exit like Greene, she asked, “You know a lot of these folks, what’s happening, Michael?”

‘I think winter is coming for Donald Trump,” he quickly replied. “When Democrats win control of Congress, you’re going to have investigations into the litany of illegal and truly concerning actions.”

“He’s put forth everything from what seems to be a coming attack on Venezuela to this litany of illegal decisions around the trade war and around investing in private companies,” he pointed out. “I think that’s going to be a problem for him. If I’m in Congress and I’m a Republican, why would I stick around for a lame duck president that is not loyal?”

“And by the way, a lame duck president that can’t run again,” he observed.

Our Analysis:

The Unraveling of Trump’s MAGA Alliance: A Critical Analysis

Donald Trump’s political saga has always been more akin to a Shakespearean tragedy than a conventional presidential tenure. The latest chapter, as reported, involves his fallout with Rep. Marjorie Taylor Greene (R-GA) and the dwindling affection from influencers like Joe Rogan. This episode is not just a mere spat within the ranks but a harbinger of a potential political implosion that threatens to cripple Trump’s agenda and expose him to a barrage of investigations.

The Illusion of Loyalty in Trump’s World

The discourse on MS NOW, featuring insights from Democratic adviser Michael Hardaway and former Republican Party campaign adviser Tara Setmayer, illuminates a fundamental truth about Trump’s modus operandi: loyalty is a one-way street. Setmayer’s commentary on Greene’s shock at being ostracized by Trump despite her unwavering loyalty is particularly telling. It underscores a pattern of behavior where Trump’s allies are disposable the moment they cease to serve his interests or dare to dissent.

This revelation is hardly groundbreaking for those who have watched Trump’s career. His history of discarding allies—dating back to his betrayal of Roy Cohn, the notorious lawyer who taught Trump the dark arts of legal and media manipulation—is well documented. Yet, it’s the sheer predictability of these betrayals that astounds. The question is not if Trump will turn on his loyalists but when.

A House of Cards Poised to Collapse

Michael Hardaway’s prognosis of “winter is coming for Donald Trump” is not just a clever turn of phrase but a stark warning. The predicted Democratic takeover of Congress threatens to unleash a storm of investigations into Trump’s myriad questionable actions, from potential warmongering in Venezuela to dubious decisions in the realm of trade wars and private investments.

The metaphorical winter represents a reckoning that has been a long time coming. Trump’s presidency and post-presidency period have been marred by a litany of controversies and alleged illegalities that have somehow been kept at bay. But as loyalty within his ranks wanes and the potential for Democratic control of Congress looms, Trump’s protective shield of unyielding support is showing signs of fracture.

The Republican Dilemma: To Flee or Not to Flee

For Republican lawmakers, the unfolding drama presents a profound dilemma. Aligning with a “lame duck president” who not only faces significant legal and political vulnerabilities but also exhibits a glaring lack of loyalty to his allies, seems like a political suicide mission. The mention of Republicans looking for an exit strategy is not just a signal of dwindling support for Trump; it’s an acknowledgment of the shifting tides and the desire to survive the political purge that may follow.

Conclusion: The Fall of a Titan?

As Trump’s MAGA alliance crumbles and his circle of loyalists diminishes, one must ponder the implications not just for Trump but for the Republican Party and the broader political landscape. The potential for a wave of investigations represents not just a personal challenge for Trump but a moment of reckoning for his brand of politics.

The narrative of unyielding loyalty, once thought to be Trump’s invincible armor, is proving to be his Achilles’ heel. The fallout with Greene and the disillusionment of figures like Joe Rogan are not anomalies but symptoms of a deeper malaise within the Trump camp. As “winter comes,” one wonders whether Trump’s political fortress can withstand the storm or if it will be buried beneath the avalanche of its own making.
