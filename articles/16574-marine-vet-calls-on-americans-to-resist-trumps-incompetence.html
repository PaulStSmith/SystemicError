Memorial Day Misery: How Trump’s Military Maneuvers Betray Veterans

Abuse of Power Under the Guise of National Security

Once again, the Trump administration demonstrates its disregard for both military tradition and veterans’ welfare, as outlined by John Keker, a Vietnam War veteran and infantry platoon commander, in his recent op-ed. Keker criticizes President Trump for his “reckless misuse of the military,” which includes initiating a war in Iran without proper congressional or public discourse. This act reflects a broader pattern of Trump’s governance: unilateral military actions and a troubling approach to both domestic and foreign policy that jeopardizes American lives and undermines democratic norms.

Decisions Without Oversight

Trump’s decision to engage in military actions across multiple countries—including Iran, Yemen, and Syria—without significant congressional oversight or public debate reveals a strategic inclination towards authoritarianism. His actions reflect a disturbing readiness to use military power aggressively and impulsively. The launch of a war in Iran, particularly, underscores a lack of foresight and preparation, as seen when Trump ignored his advisors’ warnings about the potential shutdown of the Strait of Hormuz, a critical maritime chokepoint.

A Pattern of Disregard for Military Ethics

Keker points out that Trump’s military orders often lack clear objectives and exit strategies, exemplifying a misuse of military resources and personnel. The strike on a girls’ school in Iran, which killed over 100 students, is an especially egregious example of how military action can be mismatched with stated humanitarian goals. Furthermore, Trump’s suggestion to use Democratic-run American cities as “training grounds” for the military dangerously blurs the lines between domestic law enforcement and military operations, treating American citizens as combatants.

Politicizing the Military and Undermining Resilience

The Trump administration’s pattern of expanding military operations without adequate oversight is a clear strategy to politicize military power. This tactic not only threatens the constitutional balance but also endangers American lives and global peace. It is an attempt to consolidate power by creating external enemies and internal divisions. Keker’s call for veterans to lead the resistance against these abuses is poignant, emphasizing that those who understand war’s horrors are uniquely positioned to advocate for responsible military use.

The Role of Veteran Advocacy in Upholding Democracy

Keker stresses the importance of veterans in the ongoing struggle to maintain democratic governance and ethical military conduct. His faith in the American public, and particularly in veterans, to resist autocracy underscores a critical avenue for advocacy and resistance. By leveraging their understanding and experiences, veterans can influence public opinion and policy, advocating for a return to principled, lawful military engagement.

Conclusion: The Imperative of Vigilant Citizenship

This Memorial Day, as we remember those who have served and sacrificed, John Keker’s reflections remind us of the need for vigilance against the misuse of military power. It highlights the necessity for active citizenship, especially from those within the veteran community, to ensure that military power is used judiciously and only when absolutely necessary. Trump’s actions are not just a series of policy missteps; they are a concerted effort to reshape American military ethos and democratic norms in his favor. It is a call to action for every citizen to support checks and balances and resist the drift toward autocratic rule.
