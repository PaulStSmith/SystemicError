Tucker Carlson detained in Israel after taking MAGA official up on debate offer: report

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosFebruary 19, 20264 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Our Analysis:Tucker Carlson’s Alleged Detainment: A Closer Look

Tucker Carlson speaking with attendees at the 2020 Student Action Summit. (Gage Skidmore/Flickr)

Former Fox News host turned far-right influencer Tucker Carlson claimed Israeli security forces briefly detained him at the airport after a trip to Tel Aviv for a chat with former Arkansas Gov. and current U.S. Ambassador to Israel Mike Huckabee.

According to the Daily Mail, Carlson “flew into Tel Aviv for a sit-down with Mike Huckabee, who challenged Carlson to speak to him directly following an online spat about the country’s treatment of Christians. Carlson, who also frequently criticizes Israel for its military actions in Gaza, took Huckabee up on his offer.”

After that event, however, Carlson said that at the airport for the return trip, “men who identified themselves as airport security took our passports, hauled our executive producer into a side room and then demanded to know what we spoke to Ambassador Huckabee about.”

“Two sources familiar with the matter ... told the Daily Mail that the Israeli government initially did not want to allow Carlson into the country, prompting a delicate negotiation involving the State Department,” said the report. “In the end, the Israeli government decided to not bar Carlson from entering the country in order to avoid a ‘diplomatic incident,’ according to a Channel 13 report.”

In recent years, Carlson has been caught in a growing divide on the far right, between those who align with the traditional Christian Right’s apocalypticist-motivated political support of Israel, who often legitimize their beliefs as support for the Jewish people, and the more overt neo-Nazi elements with antisemitic motivations against Israel, who often tie their cause to the increasingly dire welfare of the Palestinian people amid the invasion of Gaza.

Carlson has positioned himself on the latter side, having given a platform to overt Nazi apologist and Holocaust denier Nick Fuentes. A publicly aired disagreement about the merits of this engagement caused a schism at the right-wing Heritage Foundation near the end of last year.

Tucker Carlson’s Alleged Detainment: A Closer Look

The Incident at the Airport: A Theatrical Display of Diplomacy? Former Fox News host and current far-right influencer, Tucker Carlson, claims he was briefly detained by Israeli security forces at the airport following a discussion with former Arkansas Governor and current U.S. Ambassador to Israel, Mike Huckabee. This incident, as reported by the Daily Mail, unfolds a narrative rich in irony and political maneuvering. Carlson, who has been a vocal critic of Israel’s military actions in Gaza and has engaged in spats with Huckabee over the treatment of Christians in Israel, found himself in a delicate situation that reeks of high diplomatic stakes and underlying tensions.

The fact that two sources familiar with the matter revealed the Israeli government’s initial reluctance to allow Carlson entry, only to relent to avoid a “diplomatic incident,” speaks volumes about the fragile dance between maintaining public relations and asserting national security protocols. This incident illuminates the complexities of international diplomacy where individuals like Carlson, known for their controversial and polarizing views, become pawns in a larger game of geopolitical chess.

Carlson’s Controversial Stance: Between Apocalyptic Support and Antisemitism Tucker Carlson’s positioning within the far-right spectrum is particularly noteworthy. The article highlights the growing divide among the far-right between those who support Israel from a Christian apocalyptic perspective, believing in the legitimization of their support for the Jewish people, and those who oppose Israel on antisemitic grounds, often expressing solidarity with the Palestinians caught in the conflict.

Carlson’s alignment with the latter, through his platforming of individuals like Nick Fuentes, a known Nazi apologist and Holocaust denier, not only showcases the dangerous waters he navigates but also the broader ideological battles within the far-right movements. The schism at the Heritage Foundation over Carlson’s engagement with such controversial figures underscores the deep divisions and the contentious debates on the direction and the soul of far-right politics in America.

Conclusion: The Intersection of Media, Politics, and Diplomacy This incident, while seemingly a minor hiccup in international relations, underscores the significant role media personalities like Tucker Carlson play in the complex interplay of global politics, diplomacy, and public opinion. The delicate handling of his visit by both the U.S. and Israeli governments indicates the careful balancing act countries must perform in the digital age, where influencers wield power that can sway diplomatic ties and international perceptions.

Moreover, Carlson’s controversial stance and the ideological divide it represents within the far-right movement in the United States highlight the volatile nature of political alignments and the shifting sands of political support for international issues. The engagement of far-right figures with international politics not only affects domestic perceptions but also has the potential to influence diplomatic relations and policy decisions on a global scale.

In essence, the Tucker Carlson airport detainment saga is a microcosm of the broader complexities and challenges facing modern diplomacy, media influence, and the ideological battles shaping politics today. It’s a stark reminder of the power of individuals to affect international relations and the ongoing struggle between different factions of political thought for dominance and influence in the public sphere.
