'This is strange': Trump fans baffled by puny turnout at North Carolina rally

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosDecember 23, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Our Analysis:The Mirage of Prosperity at Trump’s Rocky Mount Rally

President Donald Trump on Friday in Rocky Mount, North Carolina. (Jessica Koscielniak/Reuters)

Donald Trump’s rally in tiny Rocky Mount, North Carolina drew a smaller than expected crowd to the economically distressed region, leaving merchandise vendors and supporters disheartened.

According to interviews conducted by the Washington Post, the presidential visit was marked by a sense of disappointment and disillusionment as Trump touted economic growth claims that contrasted sharply with the visible hardship surrounding the Rocky Mount Events Center.

One Trump merchandise vendor struggled to move inventory, marking MAGA beanies down to $5 without success. When addressing the reduced price, 18-year-old Thomas Schafer responded, “I don’t have $5. We’re really broke. It’s Christmas time, man.”

Post reporter Natalie Allison noted that regions like Rocky Mount face significant economic challenges ahead, as enhanced subsidies for Affordable Care Act plans—available for the past five years—are expected to expire in the coming year.

This economic anxiety likely contributed to lower attendance than Trump typically draws. Guy Harper, who has sold Trump merchandise at rallies nationwide for eight years, was struck by the reduced turnout. He began packing his merchandise hours before the rally commenced, observing, “Look at this. Usually, Trump rallies are like a football tailgate. This is strange.”

Harper attributed the sparse crowd to multiple factors, including a late start and voter skepticism about economic conditions.

Despite the modest attendance, Trump claimed a massive turnout and falsely stated that crowds of people outside were waiting to enter the venue.

Our Analysis:

The Mirage of Prosperity at Trump’s Rocky Mount Rally

In the shadow of grandiose claims and the bedazzled spectacle that often accompanies Donald Trump’s rallies, the event in Rocky Mount, North Carolina, unfolded like a grim parable of disillusionment. Here, the narrative of economic prosperity, so frequently trumpeted by Trump, starkly clashed with the lived realities of the local populace—a community grappling with economic distress. The Washington Post’s coverage of this event paints a vivid picture of disappointment, both in terms of turnout and the economic disillusionment palpable among the attendees and vendors.

Disenchanted Vendors and the Echoes of Economic Hardship

One of the most telling indicators of the rally’s atmosphere comes from the experience of merchandise vendors. The tale of Thomas Schafer, an 18-year-old vendor, struggling to sell MAGA beanies—even at the drastically reduced price of $5—speaks volumes. Schafer’s remark, “I don’t have $5. We’re really broke. It’s Christmas time, man,” is a raw expression of the economic hardship that pervades regions like Rocky Mount. It’s a stark reminder of the disconnect between the rosy economic narratives often promoted at such rallies and the harsh financial realities faced by many Americans.

A Reality Check from Rocky Mount

The event’s diminished crowd size is another glaring aspect that cannot be overlooked. Guy Harper, a veteran Trump merchandise seller, noted the unusual sparsity of the crowd, likening previous rallies to “a football tailgate” in comparison. This observation is not just a commentary on the rally’s ambiance but a profound insight into the waning enthusiasm or perhaps the growing skepticism among Trump’s base. The reasons for this reduced turnout are multifaceted, including skepticism about the economic conditions that Trump touts.

Moreover, Trump’s claim of a massive turnout, contradicting visible evidence and Harper’s observations, underscores a troubling pattern of disconnect with reality. The assertion of crowds waiting outside, while inside, vendors and supporters wrestle with visible economic strain, encapsulates the dissonance between Trump’s narratives and the palpable realities of his supporters.

The Looming Economic Anxiety

The article also touches upon a critical aspect of the economic anxiety looming over regions like Rocky Mount—the expiration of enhanced subsidies for Affordable Care Act plans. This detail is not just a footnote in the broader narrative but a significant factor contributing to the economic uncertainty faced by the community. It’s an essential piece of the puzzle in understanding the economic backdrop against which this rally was set.

Conclusion: The Unraveling of Economic Promises

The Rocky Mount rally, as depicted in the Washington Post’s report, emerges not just as a story of a poorly attended political event but as a microcosm of the broader economic challenges and disillusionments facing many Americans. The event starkly contrasts the image of economic prosperity often painted by Trump with the reality of those who continue to struggle financially.

This episode serves as a potent reminder of the importance of grounding political rhetoric in the realities of those it aims to serve. The dissonance between Trump’s claims and the economic hardship visible at the Rocky Mount rally is a clarion call for a more nuanced and reality-based approach to discussing and addressing America’s economic challenges.
