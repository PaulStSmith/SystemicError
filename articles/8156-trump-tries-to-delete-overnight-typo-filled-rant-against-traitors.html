Trump tries to delete overnight 'typo-filled' rant against 'traitors'

President Donald Trump futilely tried to delete his Sunday night Truth Social rant, but his typo-filled fury was captured by various screen shots, The Daily Beast reports.

“Ther [sic] are laws that effect our nation,” Trump posted, but then quickly deleted, they note.

On Trump’s second attempt at posting, he confused “effect” and “affect,” instead going with what The Daily Beast describes as “the less problematic word” “impact” instead.

Trump’s edited version finally made it to Truth Social, with both ending in the taunt, “DO WHAT HAS TO BE DONE!!!”

“Trump seemed to be quoting former U.S. Navy Commander Kirk Lippold, who now works as an inspirational speaker, as part of a rant against Democratic ‘traitors,’” the Daily Beast reports.

Last week, six Democratic lawmakers with military and intelligence backgrounds released a video urging current U.S. service members to refuse any orders they deem unlawful, an action that aligns with U.S. military law.

Trump called them the “Seditious Six” and posted that the lawmakers, including former Navy captain and astronaut Sen. Mark Kelly (D-AZ) had committed “SEDITIOUS BEHAVIOR, punishable by DEATH!”

This tear comes after another of Trump’s Truth Social tantrums, in which he used an offensive slur against Minnesota Gov. Tim Walz (D) “in a scathing post at Thanksgiving, where he also called out political correctness,” the Daily Beast explains.

“There’s something wrong with him, absolutely, sure,” Trump told reporters Sunday on Air Force One, refusing to apologize.

Our Analysis:

The Unraveling of Decorum: Trump’s Typo-Laden Tirades

In the latest installment of what can only be described as the theatrical absurdity of American politics, former President Donald Trump has once again taken to his social media platform, Truth Social, to unleash a series of typo-riddled rants that have both amused and alarmed observers. The Daily Beast’s recent report on Trump’s failed attempt to erase his grammatical gaffes from the digital world is a testament to the chaotic nature of his communication style, a hallmark of his presidency and post-presidency life.

Trump’s Misguided War on Words

Trump’s struggle with the English language is not just a minor footnote; it’s a glaring spotlight on his inability to convey coherent thoughts. His initial post, rife with errors, attempted to address laws impacting the nation but fell short due to his confusion between “effect” and “affect.” Opting for the “less problematic word” “impact” on his second try does little to salvage the dignity of his statement. It’s not just about the misspellings or grammatical mistakes; it’s about the fundamental incapacity to communicate effectively and accurately, a basic expectation for any public figure, let alone a former President of the United States.

The Seditious Six and the Misuse of Sedition

Trump’s reference to the “Seditious Six,” a moniker he has assigned to six Democratic lawmakers with military and intelligence backgrounds, is as dangerous as it is absurd. These lawmakers, including Sen. Mark Kelly, released a video urging U.S. service members to refuse unlawful orders, a stance that is not only legal but morally incumbent upon military personnel. Trump’s accusation of sedition and the invocation of the death penalty for such actions is not only a gross misinterpretation of the law but a reckless incitement of violence. The term “sedition” has been weaponized by Trump to attack political adversaries, undermining the legal and constitutional frameworks that define it.

The Disregard for Political Correctness

Trump’s tirade against Minnesota Gov. Tim Walz, employing an offensive slur, underscores his continued disdain for political correctness, a concept he frequently derides as an infringement on free speech. However, Trump’s rhetoric transcends the boundaries of political correctness, venturing into the realm of outright disrespect and bigotry. His refusal to apologize, affirmed during a conversation with reporters on Air Force One, is not a display of strength but a stark revelation of his unwillingness to acknowledge or rectify his wrongdoings.

Conclusion: The Unending Tantrum

Trump’s latest outbursts on Truth Social are not isolated incidents but part of a broader pattern of behavior that has characterized his political persona. His disregard for the norms of communication, legal principles, and basic decency is not just a personal failing but a systemic issue that reflects the current state of political discourse in America. As the former leader of the free world continues to spew typo-filled fury, one must ponder the impact of such rhetoric on the fabric of American society and the political landscape. The real question remains: How long can this level of discourse be sustained without irreparable damage? In a world where words wield immense power, the careless, chaotic, and confrontational language of Trump is not just an embarrassment—it’s a ticking time bomb.
