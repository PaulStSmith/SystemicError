Former federal prosecutor Shan Wu weighed in via a video over the weekend. It is called “Trump DOJ SHOCKED by SURPRISE STAR WITNESS in Comey CASE,” Wu explains that, “There is a new star witness” in the James Comey case.

“It’s not who you think it might be. It’s not some FBI informant, or someone close to him, like a buddy of his who is going to tell all,” Wu said. “It’s somebody we all thought had gone quietly into the night.”

The star witness to whom he is referring is Special Counsel John Durham, who investigated the investigators of the Russian voting interference case at the FBI, on behalf of Trump.

According to Wu, Durham will be a “great witness for Comey.”

“As reporting indicates, Durham, who spent all these years examining the origins of the FBI investigation into the Russian interference with the 2016 campaign, told federal prosecutors who were investigating Comey... he could not uncover evidence that would support false statements or obstruction charges against Comey,” he then added.

Wu went on to say Durham actually provided “exculpatory” information that could potentially help Comey.

Our Analysis:

The Unexpected Twist in the Comey Case: Analyzing the Impact of Durham’s Testimony

In a turn of events seemingly ripped from the pages of a political thriller, the Comey case is taking an unexpected detour, courtesy of a “surprise star witness” from the very administration that initiated the investigation. John Durham, a name synonymous with the Trump administration’s quest to uncover alleged misdeeds within the FBI’s investigation into Russian interference, is now poised to play a pivotal role in what appears to be an unraveling narrative against former FBI director James Comey.

The Irony of Durham’s Involvement

The irony of Durham’s involvement cannot be overstated. Tasked by Trump to investigate the investigators, Durham’s emergence as a key figure in Comey’s defense is nothing short of dramatic irony. It’s a plot twist that would be deemed too far-fetched even for fiction, yet here we are, witnessing the unraveling of a case that has been as much about political vendettas as it has been about the pursuit of justice.

Durham: The Unexpected Shield

According to Shan Wu, a former federal prosecutor, Durham is set to undermine the DOJ’s prosecution of Comey, not by wielding new accusations or uncovering hidden misconduct, but by providing exculpatory evidence in Comey’s favor. This revelation is particularly striking given Durham’s original mandate. Wu’s assertion that Durham could not “uncover evidence that would support false statements or obstruction charges against Comey” flips the narrative on its head. Instead of delivering the damning evidence that many in the Trump administration might have hoped for, Durham is poised to dismantle the case against Comey from within.

The Significance of Exculpatory Evidence

The provision of exculpatory evidence by someone as closely tied to the Trump administration’s investigative efforts as Durham is a potent reminder of the complexities inherent in political investigations. It underscores the importance of following the evidence wherever it may lead, even if it leads to conclusions that are inconvenient for those who initiated the investigation. In this context, Durham’s role transcends that of a mere witness; he becomes a symbol of the integrity of the investigative process, a process that should be immune to political pressures and biases.

Conclusion: A Case of Unexpected Alliances and Political Drama

As this case continues to unfold, it’s clear that the political drama surrounding it is far from over. Durham’s expected testimony in favor of Comey is a stark reminder that in the tangled web of political investigations, alliances can be unexpected, and outcomes can defy initial expectations. This development raises significant questions about the motivations behind the DOJ’s prosecution of Comey and suggests that the pursuit of justice is often fraught with twists that challenge our preconceptions. In the end, the Comey case may be remembered not for its conclusions, but for the unexpected journey it took to reach them, with Durham’s surprising role highlighting the unpredictable nature of political legal battles.
