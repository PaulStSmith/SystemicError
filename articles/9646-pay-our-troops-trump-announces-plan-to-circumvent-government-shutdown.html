‘Pay our troops!’ Trump announces plan to circumvent government shutdown

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosOctober 12, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Our Analysis:Trump’s Blame Game: A Masterclass in Deflection

Donald Trump speaks during the signing of executive orders in the Oval Office. REUTERS/Jonathan Ernst

President Donald Trump announced Saturday that he had directed Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth to “use all available funds” to pay military service members to circumvent the ongoing government shutdown, blaming “radical left Democrats” for holding the “security of our nation hostage.”

“If nothing is done, because of ‘Leader’ Chuck Schumer and the Democrats, our Brave Troops will miss the paychecks they are rightfully due on October 15th,” Trump wrote in a social media post on Truth Social.

“That is why I am using my authority, as Commander in Chief, to direct our Secretary of War, Pete Hegseth, to use all available funds to get our Troops PAID on October 15th. We have identified funds to do this, and Secretary Hegseth will use them to PAY OUR TROOPS. I will not allow the Democrats to hold our Military, and the entire Security of our Nation, HOSTAGE, with their dangerous Government Shutdown.”

The government shutdown is in its 11th day as of Saturday, and stems from disagreements among lawmakers on health care policy. Democrats have refused to support any funding measures that don’t include funding an extension to Affordable Care Act subsidies, whereas Republicans have stood firm against Democrats’ demands, and instead have pushed a temporary funding bill.

“The Radical Left Democrats should OPEN THE GOVERNMENT, and then we can work together to address Healthcare, and many other things that they want to destroy,” Trump wrote. “Thank you for your attention to this matter!”

Our Analysis:

Trump’s Blame Game: A Masterclass in Deflection

In a recent post on Truth Social, President Donald Trump has once again brandished his flair for the dramatic, aiming his cannons at the so-called “radical left Democrats” for the ongoing government shutdown. He announces a grandiose plan to direct Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth to “use all available funds” to ensure military service members are paid, framing this maneuver as a heroic intervention against the Democrats’ alleged hostage-taking of national security. However, a closer examination reveals this narrative to be a meticulously crafted facade, designed to deflect blame and manipulate public perception.

The Misuse of Military Pay as a Political Tool

First and foremost, Trump’s decision to circumvent the government shutdown by ensuring military pay is a strategic move that exploits the military as pawns in a larger political game. The idea that military service members would not receive their paychecks due to the shutdown is presented as a dire consequence of Democratic obstinacy. Yet, this framing conveniently ignores the shared responsibility Congress and the President have in preventing such outcomes. It’s a classic Trump maneuver: create a crisis, or exacerbate one, then step in with a supposed solution that paints him as the savior, all while sidestepping the underlying issues.

Misdirection and Blame

Trump’s direct blame of “radical left Democrats” for the shutdown due to their demands for Affordable Care Act (ACA) subsidies is a glaring example of political misdirection. By focusing solely on the Democrats’ insistence on healthcare funding, Trump seeks to obscure the broader context of the budgetary impasse. The shutdown, now in its 11th day, is the result of a fundamental disagreement on national priorities, with healthcare being a significant point of contention. However, to pin the shutdown solely on the Democrats’ healthcare demands is to oversimplify and misrepresent the negotiation dynamics at play.

The Dubious Title of “Secretary of War”

An interesting, and perhaps telling, slip in Trump’s announcement is the reference to Pete Hegseth as the “Secretary of War.” The United States hasn’t had a Secretary of War since the position was rebranded as Secretary of Defense in 1949, following the end of World War II. This archaic slip-up is more than a mere faux pas; it reflects a mindset steeped in bellicosity and conflict. Whether intentional or not, it evokes a bygone era of American militarism and imperialism, which may resonate with certain segments of Trump’s base but is alarmingly out of touch with contemporary governance and diplomacy.

Conclusion: A Dangerous Game of Chicken

Trump’s strategy, as outlined in his social media post, is nothing short of a dangerous game of chicken, using the livelihoods of military personnel as leverage to score political points against his adversaries. Instead of fostering a spirit of cooperation and compromise, Trump’s rhetoric amplifies division and hostility. The real issue at hand—the disagreement over healthcare policy and the resulting government shutdown—demands serious negotiation and dialogue, not theatrical blame games.

As the shutdown drags on, it’s essential to look beyond Trump’s distraction tactics and focus on the substantive policy debates that have led to this impasse. Only through genuine bipartisan effort can the government move beyond this deadlock, ensuring both the well-being of its military service members and the health and security of the nation as a whole.
