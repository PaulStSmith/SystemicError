President Donald Trump threatened NBC’s broadcast license days after the network aired an interview with Sen. Raphael Warnock (D-GA).

“Raphael Warnock was on Meet the Fake Press with a one sided and very biased Kristen Welker as the Host(ess!)” Trump ranted on Wednesday. “Warnock spent the entire show using Religion to try and divide the Country! If a Republican, in particular ME, made those statements, it would be FRONT PAGE NEWS.”

“He ended by saying that he was going to his Church to preach now, and while I think that’s fine, I do say, ‘What ever happened to separation of Church and State?’ That would be the first thing they would use against us but, actually, it’s something that never much bothered me,” he continued. “Anyway, Warnock is a LIGHTWEIGHT whose wife correctly states that he tried to run her over with a car. Fortunately, she was able to get out of the way, and only almost lost her foot. Warnock is a bad guy, and NBC News, which is second only to ABC Fake News for being the worst, should be ashamed of themselves in allowing garbage “interviews” with untalented and uncaring people to take place.”

Trump then suggested that the Federal Communications Commission, or FCC, should investigate NBC.

“The Public airwaves, which these Networks are using at no charge, should not be allowed to get away with this any longer!” he wrote. “They should be properly licensed, and pay significant amounts of money for using this very valuable Public space.”

The president’s remarks came just hours after FCC Chair Brendan Carr told a Senate committee that he had the power to shut down broadcast networks for satirical speech.

Our Analysis:

Trump’s Tirade: A Misguided Missile in the War on Free Speech

In a recent eruption of ire, former President Donald Trump launched a scathing attack on NBC, following an interview the network conducted with Sen. Raphael Warnock (D-GA). Trump’s diatribe, steeped in personal vendetta and blatant disregard for the principles of free speech, not only undermines the essence of democracy but also highlights a dangerous penchant for authoritarianism.

Trump’s Critique: A Blend of Bias and Bile

Trump’s rant began with an assault on the integrity of the interview, dubbing NBC’s ‘Meet the Press’ as “Meet the Fake Press” and lambasting both the network and the host, Kristen Welker, for their alleged bias. His tirade didn’t stop at mere name-calling; Trump accused Warnock of using religion to “divide the country,” a claim both rich in irony and poverty of evidence. This is the same Trump who, during his presidency, rarely hesitated to exploit religious sentiments for political gain. Yet, when a Democrat discusses religion, Trump cynically questions the separation of church and state—a principle he conveniently remembers when it suits his agenda.

The Threat to NBC: An Assault on the First Amendment

The crescendo of Trump’s rant was his call for the Federal Communications Commission (FCC) to investigate NBC, suggesting that the network’s broadcast license be threatened over content he finds disagreeable. This is not merely an attack on a media outlet but an affront to the First Amendment itself. The idea that a president could whimsically challenge a network’s license over unfavorable coverage is antithetical to the very notion of free speech and a free press. Trump’s suggestion that networks should “pay significant amounts of money” for using public airwaves is a thinly veiled attempt to exert governmental control over the media—a hallmark of authoritarian regimes.

FCC’s Role: A Potential Enabler?

Adding fuel to the fire, FCC Chair Brendan Carr’s recent statement to a Senate committee, claiming the power to shut down broadcast networks for satirical speech, is alarming. While Carr’s comments were not directly linked to Trump’s rant, the timing and content of these remarks serve as a stark reminder of the fragility of press freedoms under certain regulatory powers. The notion that the FCC could leverage its authority to silence satire—a vital form of political commentary and dissent—is chilling.

Conclusion: A Call to Vigilance

Trump’s attack on NBC and the broader implications of such threats against the media underscore the ongoing battle for the soul of American democracy. It’s a scenario where the lines between critique and censorship blur, where the power to question the government is under threat by the very figures who should be safeguarding it. As citizens and defenders of democratic principles, we must remain vigilant. We must recognize these attacks for what they are: not mere rants of a disgruntled former leader, but potential precursors to a world where the press is shackled, dissent is silenced, and power goes unchecked.

The essence of democracy rests on the ability to speak truth to power, and any attempt to undermine this foundation must be met with unwavering resistance. Trump’s tirade against NBC is not just an attack on a network but a broadside against the very principles that underpin a free society. It’s a reminder that the battle for free speech and a free press is ongoing, and complacency is the enemy of liberty.
