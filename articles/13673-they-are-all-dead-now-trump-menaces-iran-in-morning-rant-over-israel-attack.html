'They are all dead now': Trump menaces Iran in morning rant over Israel attack

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosJune 14, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Donald Trump (Photo via Jonathan Ernst for Reuters)

After remaining silent late Thursday and all night, Donald Trump jumped into the fray early Friday morning after Israel launched an attack on Iran.

After Israel launched "Operation Rising Lion," targeting Iran's nuclear program and reportedly killing three senior military officials, Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu promised more to come, which led Trump to offer his own warning.

At 6am ET, the president jumped on Truth Social and wrote, "I gave Iran chance after chance to make a deal. I told them, in the strongest of words, to 'just do it,' but no matter how hard they tried, no matter how close they got, they just couldn’t get it done. I told them it would be much worse than anything they know, anticipated, or were told, that the United States makes the best and most lethal military equipment anywhere in the World, BY FAR, and that Israel has a lot of it, with much more to come - And they know how to use it. Certain Iranian hardliner’s spoke bravely, but they didn’t know what was about to happen. They are all DEAD now, and it will only get worse! "

He added, "There has already been great death and destruction, but there is still time to make this slaughter, with the next already planned attacks being even more brutal, come to an end. Iran must make a deal, before there is nothing left, and save what was once known as the Iranian Empire. No more death, no more destruction, JUST DO IT, BEFORE IT IS TOO LATE. God Bless You All!"

Our Analysis:

Analysis: Trump's Rhetoric on Israel-Iran Conflict

In an early morning social media salvo, former President Donald Trump has once again illuminated the complex web of international relations, military brinkmanship, and his own unique brand of diplomacy. Following Israel's "Operation Rising Lion" against Iran, Trump's commentary not only underscores his enduring influence on global political narratives but also his characteristic bluster, which often blurs the lines between diplomatic negotiation and outright intimidation.

Trump's Intervention: Timing and Tone

First and foremost, it's imperative to examine the timing and tone of Trump's intervention. His decision to weigh in after a significant military operation has commenced is particularly telling. This delay suggests a calculated move to gauge the initial fallout before aligning his stance. Moreover, the early morning timing of his post on Truth Social could be seen as an attempt to dominate the day's news cycle, a tactic Trump has effectively used in the past.

Trump's language, laden with bravado and veiled threats, is emblematic of his tenure as president. Phrases like "I gave Iran chance after chance" and "I told them it would be much worse than anything they know" illustrate his preference for a confrontational and paternalistic approach to diplomacy. This rhetoric, while appealing to his base, significantly oversimplifies the intricate dynamics of international negotiations and the grave realities of military conflict.

The Substance of Trump's Claims

Trump's assertion that "Iran just couldn’t get it done" in terms of reaching a deal under his administration is a gross oversimplification of a highly complex situation. It disregards the multifaceted reasons behind the failure of negotiations, including the potential lack of goodwill on both sides and the international community's divided stance on the Iran nuclear deal.

Moreover, his claim that "Certain Iranian hardliner’s spoke bravely, but they didn’t know what was about to happen. They are all DEAD now" not only displays a troubling glee over the loss of life but also seems to dangerously conflate military and diplomatic victories. This rhetoric could potentially escalate tensions further, undermining any efforts towards de-escalation and peaceful resolution.

Implications for U.S. Foreign Policy

Trump’s comments, particularly his emphasis on the lethality of U.S. and Israeli military equipment, may have significant implications for U.S. foreign policy, regardless of whether he holds office. His statements could be construed as an implicit endorsement of preemptive strikes and military aggression, potentially straining U.S. relations with not only Iran but also other global powers wary of increased militarization in the region.

Conclusion: A Perilous Path Forward

Donald Trump's intervention in the Israel-Iran conflict through a social media post is more than just a former leader commenting on international affairs; it is a potent reminder of the influence he wields and the contentious strategies he promotes. His rhetoric, characterized by a mix of ultimatums and boasts about military supremacy, underscores a perilous approach to conflict resolution—one that prioritizes power over diplomacy and might over dialogue.

In the broader scheme of things, Trump's comments represent a significant challenge to the current administration's efforts to navigate the complex geopolitical landscape of the Middle East. As tensions escalate and the potential for further conflict grows, the international community must carefully consider the implications of such rhetoric and the path it charts for global peace and security.
