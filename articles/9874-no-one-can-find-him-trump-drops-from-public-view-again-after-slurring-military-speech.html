'No one can find him': Trump drops from public view again after 'slurring' military speech

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosOctober 4, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Our Analysis:The Disappearing Act of Donald Trump: A Closer Examination

U.S. President Donald Trump walks off the stage after speaking during a meeting of senior military leaders convened by U.S. Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, at Marine Corps Base Quantico in Quantico, Virginia, U.S., September 30, 2025. REUTERS/Kevin Lamarque

President Donald Trump hasn’t made a public appearance since his rambling and authoritarian speech to top military leaders, and people are noticing.

The president told high-ranking military leaders to prepare to deploy in American cities for “a war from within,” saying he told Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth to use “dangerous cities as training grounds,” but he disappeared from view for the rest of the week.

“Donald Trump is in the presidential witness protection program,” House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries told MSNBC’s “Morning Joe.” “No one can find him.”

“Trump has not been seen in public since Tuesday and has no public events on his calendar today,” noted journalist Aaron Rupar.

Many observers noticed that Trump’s energy seemed low the last time he was in public and expressed confusion about his extended riff on taking care not to fall down stairs during the military speech, and they recalled the weekend at the start of September when his extended absence from view sparked rumors about his health and welfare.

“Have we not seen cankles in public since the shutdown started?” asked Democratic strategist Adam Parkhomenko.

“Ladies and gentlemen, the weekend (at Bernie’s),” joked historian Kevin Kruse.

“He had to have another stroke so that his face will droop on both sides,” suggested Bluesky user Bob.

“I’m not interested in conspiracies, we all know about his hand bruising and the bells Palsy episode on 9/11,” said Bluesky user ChicagoDad. “We saw him slurring like crazy. The generals speech wasn’t normal energy wise. If the next appearance is him seated behind his desk, flanked by others while covering his bruised hand...”

“Where’s Trump?” added X user PatriotDaughter. “He hasn’t been seen since that 10% battery life speech to the top brass. He just keeps sending his minions out to bluster.”

“Trump hasn’t been seen in public since Tuesday and has nothing on his schedule today,” noted X user Alison Harrison. “We have only seen offensive memes on his Truth Social that indicate he’s alive and kicking. Trump is unwell — what are they hiding about his health status?”

“The president laying back so other people take blame for the shutdown is an old tactic,” opined Semafor’s Dave Weigel. “But the question is: Are the Republicans on camera winning the argument? So far it’s mostly been sombrero memes.”

Our Analysis:

The Disappearing Act of Donald Trump: A Closer Examination

In what can only be described as a bizarre turn of events, President Donald Trump has seemingly vanished from the public eye following a speech that can generously be termed as “rambling” and “authoritarian.” This speech, delivered to top military leaders, called for the deployment of military forces in American cities to engage in what he described as “a war from within.” The specifics of this plan, as relayed to Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, involved using “dangerous cities as training grounds.” The audacity of such a statement cannot be overstated—it’s a chilling proposition that reeks of authoritarianism and a gross misuse of military power.

Following this alarming spectacle, Trump has retreated into the shadows, sparking a flurry of speculation and concern. House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries quipped on MSNBC’s “Morning Joe” that Trump is in the “presidential witness protection program,” highlighting the bizarre nature of his disappearance. Journalist Aaron Rupar and others have pointed out Trump’s absence from the public eye and his empty public schedule, raising questions about his whereabouts and condition.

Observers have noted Trump’s “low energy” during his last public appearance and his odd fixation on not falling down stairs. This behavior, coupled with his sudden withdrawal from public view, has reignited rumors and concerns about his health. Theories and jokes abound on social media, with references to weekend at Bernie’s scenarios and speculations about health crises. These range from strokes to Bell’s Palsy, highlighted by Trump’s previous instances of hand bruising and slurred speech.

What is particularly troubling is the silence and the shift in focus to offensive memes on Trump’s Truth Social account, which serve as a weak indicator of his “presence.” This absence is not merely a personal health issue—it’s a matter of national security and governance. The president’s role, especially in times of crisis, is to lead and reassure the nation, not to fuel speculation and concern through inaction and opacity.

Dave Weigel, from Semafor, raises an excellent point regarding the strategy (or lack thereof) behind Trump’s disappearance. Is this a calculated attempt to let others take the blame for current crises, such as the government shutdown? If so, it’s a cowardly move that undermines the responsibilities of his office. Moreover, the use of “sombrero memes” as a distraction tactic is not only juvenile but indicative of a leadership that resorts to bigotry and humor to deflect from serious issues.

In conclusion, the situation begs the question: What is really going on with President Trump? His disappearance, following a speech that flirted dangerously close to declaring martial law, is alarming. The lack of transparency and accountability is unacceptable. The American public deserves answers, not memes or silence. The implications of Trump’s propositions and his subsequent disappearance are far-reaching and disturbing. It’s a stark reminder of the fragility of our democratic institutions and the ever-present threat of authoritarianism lurking beneath the surface of our politics.

The people deserve better.
