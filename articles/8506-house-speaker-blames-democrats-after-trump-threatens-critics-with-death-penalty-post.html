House speaker blames Democrats after Trump threatens critics with 'death penalty' post

Melania Trump gestures next to Republican presidential nominee former U.S. President Donald Trump and Mike Johnson, Speaker of the House, as they attend the 79th annual Alfred E. Smith Memorial Foundation Dinner in New York City, U.S., October 17, 2024. REUTERS/Brendan McDermid

President Donald Trump on Thursday raised alarms and prompted calls to Capitol Police when he suggested that Democrats should face the “death penalty” over “seditious behavior.” When pressed on comments by The Independent, House Speaker Mike Johnson was noncommittal, and deflected criticism on to Democrats.

Earlier this week, several Democratic members of congress — Rep. Chris DeLuzio, Rep. Chrissy Houlahan, Rep. Maggie Goodlander, Rep. Jason Crow, Sen. Elissa Slotkin and Sen. Mark Kelly — with backgrounds in the military and intelligence, released a video on social media stating that current military members were allowed to ignore illegal orders from their superiors, and encouraged them to do so.

In a series of posts to Truth Social on Thursday, Trump accused the Democrats of “seditious” conduct over the video and called for them to face trial. He later shared a response to his post which suggested the lawmakers should face the death penalty.

“This is really bad, and Dangerous to our Country. Their words cannot be allowed to stand. SEDITIOUS BEHAVIOR FROM TRAITORS!!! LOCK THEM UP???” Trump’s post read, with another calling the video, “SEDITIOUS BEHAVIOR, punishable by DEATH!”

“HANG THEM GEORGE WASHINGTON WOULD !!” an anonymous account said in reply, which was later shared by the president.

When pressed for a response to the comments by the president, Speaker Johnson quickly changed the subject to the video from his Democratic colleagues without condemning Trump.

“What I read was he was defining the crime of sedition,” Johnson told The Independent. “But obviously attorneys have to parse the language and determine all that. What I’m saying, what I will say unequivocally, that was a wildly inappropriate thing for so called leaders in Congress to do to encourage young troops to disobey orders… This is out of control and is wildly inappropriate,” Johnson said. “And for a senator like Mark Kelly, or any member of Congress in the House or Senate to be engaged in that kind of talk is is, to me, just so beyond the pale.”

Our Analysis:

Trump’s Dangerous Rhetoric: A Dive into the Abyss of Political Discourse

In an alarming display of rhetoric that should have no place in any democratic society, President Donald Trump has once again crossed a line that blurs the boundaries between incendiary speech and outright calls for violence. This time, his target is the Democratic Party, which he accuses of “seditious behavior” over a video encouraging military personnel to refuse illegal orders. Trump’s suggestion that such actions merit the “death penalty” is not just irresponsible; it’s a reckless flirtation with the kind of political violence that democracies around the world fight to suppress.

The House Speaker’s Deflection: A Masterclass in Evasion

House Speaker Mike Johnson’s response, or lack thereof, to Trump’s comments is a textbook example of political deflection. Rather than addressing the dangerous implications of a former president endorsing the execution of political opponents, Johnson chooses instead to criticize the Democrats for their video. This evasion is not just disappointing; it’s a glaring failure of leadership. At a time when the political temperature in the United States is already at a boiling point, Johnson’s refusal to condemn Trump’s words is tantamount to tacit approval.

The Democrats’ Video: Sedition or Safeguarding?

The video that triggered Trump’s wrath featured several Democratic members of Congress with military and intelligence backgrounds - including Rep. Chris DeLuzio, Rep. Chrissy Houlahan, Rep. Maggie Goodlander, Rep. Jason Crow, Sen. Elissa Slotkin, and Sen. Mark Kelly - reminding military personnel of their right and duty to refuse illegal orders. This is not sedition; it’s a reaffirmation of a fundamental principle that safeguards the legality and morality of military actions. The encouragement to adhere to the law should be seen as a patriotic act, not a betrayal of the country.

A Dangerous Precedent

Trump’s reaction to the video, amplified by his social media platform of choice, Truth Social, sets a dangerous precedent. By calling for the trial and potential execution of political opponents over a principled stance, Trump not only undermines the democratic values of free speech and lawful dissent but also emboldens those who see violence as a legitimate tool in political discourse. His actions are a stark reminder of the fragility of democratic institutions and the constant vigilance required to protect them from those who would seek to undermine them for personal or political gain.

Conclusion: The Silence that Speaks Volumes

The most telling aspect of this entire episode is not just the content of Trump’s outbursts or even Johnson’s evasive responses. It’s the silence from large swaths of the political landscape that resonates the loudest. This silence, whether born out of fear, loyalty, or indifference, is complicit in the erosion of democratic norms and the escalation of political rhetoric into dangerous territory. As history has repeatedly shown, when leaders are allowed to call for the punishment or death of their political adversaries without consequence, the path to violence and tyranny becomes all too easy to tread.

The United States finds itself at a crossroads, where the choice is between the defense of democratic principles or the descent into a discourse of violence and retribution. The path chosen will define the character of its democracy for generations to come.
