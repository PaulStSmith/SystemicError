'MAGA is very angry': Conservative strategist says new pope is in far right's 'crosshairs'

Newly elected Pope Leo XIV, Cardinal Robert Prevost of the United States, appears on the balcony of St. Peter’s Basilica at the Vatican, May 8, 2025. REUTERS/Stoyan Nenov TPX IMAGES OF THE DAY

The new pope is a balanced papal leader with a biblical message, but that hasn't stopped MAGA allies from being enraged about him being elected, a conservative political consultant said on Friday.

Rick Wilson, a consistent thorn in the side of Donald Trump, wrote leading up to the weekend that "MAGA hates the woke pope."

"And by 'woke,' I mean a Bergolian cardinal influenced by Pope Francis whose opening remarks were full of a rich warmth, and an overt emphasis on mercy, humility, justice, and a more pastoral message that was almost universally well received, even by Donald Trump and J.D. Vance," he clarified.

The "rest of MAGA," Wilson added, "is very, very angry."

"Pope Leo — lately Cardinal Robert Prevost — was one of the types of cardinals in the crosshairs of a MAGA opposition research and smear campaign to disqualify moderate and progressive cardinals for the Papacy. (I heard about this effort six or seven years ago and counseled a potential vendor to stay far, far away. I heard it was later rebooted after it leaked under another organization.)," Wilson wrote. "They wanted a MAGA Pope, a cardinal in a red baseball cap willing to turn the Church of Rome into one more echo chamber."

Where the pope is from also serves its part, Wilson said.

"The fact that Leo is an American from Chicago makes them more furious," he noted. "He understands our politics and culture in a way no other candidate for the Papacy would have, and this frightens MAGA TradCath types who wanted a Trump ally in the Vatican."

In his conclusion, Wilson wrote, "God forbid the new Pope follow the teachings of Jesus and not follow the teachings of the MAGA Apocrypha."

"Much of their fury has come from Pope Leo’s advocacy for decent treatment for immigrants and refugees, which, of course, is banned in the Book of Homan," he wrote on Friday. "The arrogance of MAGA’s predominantly Protestant base demanding a Catholic Pope who fits their ideological bill was remarkable, as was their anger."

Our Analysis:

The MAGA Conundrum: A Pope Not Made in Their Image

In a recent tirade that could only be described as paradoxical, MAGA allies have found themselves at odds with the election of the new Pope, a figure supposedly embodying the antithesis of their ideological echo chamber. Rick Wilson, a name synonymous with anti-Trump rhetoric, has shed light on this peculiar spectacle, illustrating the depth of MAGA's discontent with the new pontiff's 'woke' demeanor. However, this 'wokeness' is merely a reflection of a commitment to mercy, humility, justice, and a pastoral message—values that, ironically, should resonate rather than repel.

The Irony of MAGA's Papal Preferences

MAGA’s quest for a Pope crafted in their own ideological image is as bewildering as it is audacious. Wilson reveals that there was indeed an effort to sideline moderate and progressive cardinals in favor of a Pope who would wear MAGA's metaphorical red cap, transforming the Vatican into yet another bastion of right-wing echo. This revelation not only exposes MAGA’s misguided ambition but also underscores a fundamental misunderstanding of the Papacy’s role and purpose.

The American Pope Phenomenon

The election of Pope Leo, formerly Cardinal Robert Prevost, an American with deep insights into U.S. politics and culture, has particularly incensed the MAGA camp. Their discontent stems from a fear of the unknown—a Pope who understands America but refuses to conform to the MAGA blueprint. This fear reveals a deeper issue within MAGA ranks: a resistance to global perspectives on justice, mercy, and humility, especially when these perspectives come from one of their own.

The 'MAGA Apocrypha' vs. The Teachings of Jesus

Wilson’s critique hits its zenith with the assertion that MAGA would prefer a Pope who aligns with the 'MAGA Apocrypha' over the actual teachings of Jesus. This preference is not only blasphemous but also telling of MAGA’s ideological rigidity. Pope Leo’s advocacy for immigrants and refugees—principles deeply embedded in Christian doctrine—has been met with fury rather than compassion, revealing a stark contradiction between MAGA’s proclaimed Christian values and their actual political stances.

The Unbearable Lightness of Being 'Woke'

The term 'woke' has been weaponized in political discourse, but in the context of Pope Leo’s papacy, it signifies a return to core Christian values. The MAGA movement’s aversion to these values, under the guise of combating wokeness, highlights a profound detachment from the very teachings they claim to uphold.

Conclusion: The Misguided Crusade

The MAGA movement’s anger towards Pope Leo is not just about religious or ideological differences—it’s a manifestation of a broader cultural and political war against anything that challenges their worldview. Wilson’s analysis uncovers the absurdity of MAGA’s demands for a Pope in their image, a demand that not only misunderstands the essence of the Papacy but also seeks to undermine the universal values it represents. In their quest for a MAGA Pope, they have exposed their own ideological rigidity and intolerance, proving that their crusade is not about faith, but about power and control.

In conclusion, the spectacle of MAGA’s fury towards Pope Leo is a revealing study in contrasts: between the teachings of Jesus and the doctrines of MAGA, between global empathy and insular ideology, and ultimately, between humility and hubris.
