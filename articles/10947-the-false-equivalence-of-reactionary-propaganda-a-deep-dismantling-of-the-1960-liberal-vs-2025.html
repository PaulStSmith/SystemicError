The False Equivalence of Reactionary Propaganda: A Deep Dismantling of the "1960 Liberal vs. 2025 Liberal" Meme

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosSeptember 2, 20256 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

The meme is simple in form, but poisonous in intent. You’ve seen it: “If a liberal from 1960 showed up in 2025, he’d be considered a conservative. If a liberal from 2025 showed up in 1960, he’d be considered a dangerous lunatic.” It circulates widely on Twitter and Facebook, often pushed by accounts that wear MAGA hats as digital halos. At first glance, it reads like a clever observation about shifting social norms. But dig an inch deeper, and you see it for what it is: a crude, manipulative inversion of history designed to smear progressives and launder reactionaries into legitimacy.

Let’s dismantle this aberration piece by piece.

1. The Overton Window Is Not a Pendulum

Yes, the “acceptable” range of political debate shifts over time. In 1960, civil rights legislation was considered radical. Interracial marriage was illegal in many states. Homosexuality was criminalized. Women were openly denied equal pay and workplace opportunities. Environmental protections were almost nonexistent. All of these were contested political battles, framed as “lunatic” positions by the establishment.

Fast-forward to 2025, and these once “radical” positions are enshrined as baseline civil rights. It is true that a 1960 liberal, plucked out of history and dropped into today, might sound more conservative compared to our current progressive causes. But that’s not because conservatives today embody the liberalism of 1960. It’s because society evolves forward, not backward. Yesterday’s radical reforms become today’s common sense. That’s the mechanism of progress.

The meme tries to trick you into thinking the pendulum swings wildly, so that liberals now are beyond reason, while conservatives are simply holding the line of sanity. But history doesn’t work that way. It doesn’t “swing” back and forth between oppression and freedom like a metronome; it marches forward, usually against the screams of conservatives.

2. Conservatives Were Never the Liberals of 1960

The meme implies that today’s right-wingers are just yesterday’s Democrats, unfairly smeared as extremists by a runaway left. This is a lie. In 1960, conservatives opposed integration, civil rights, feminism, environmental protections, and international human rights conventions. In 2025, conservatives still oppose racial justice, women’s autonomy, LGBTQ+ equality, environmental action, and internationalism. The positions haven’t evolved; the reactionary core has remained the same.

What has shifted is the public consensus around them. What was once a radical liberal demand—say, the Civil Rights Act—is now mainstream. Conservatives were on the wrong side then, and they’re on the wrong side now. They don’t inherit liberalism; they recycle yesterday’s reactionary arguments with new targets.

If conservatives want to claim the mantle of 1960 liberals, let them explain why their political ancestors filibustered civil rights bills, why they supported segregationist platforms, and why today’s MAGA faithful still cheer voter suppression laws cut from the same cloth.

3. The "Dangerous Lunatic" Smear Is History Repeating

Every generation of reformers is called insane. Martin Luther King Jr. was branded a communist and a subversive. Feminists were told they were destroying the family. Gay rights activists were vilified as predators and degenerates. Environmentalists were mocked as doomsayers and job-killers. Every movement pushing for equality and justice was described in exactly the same terms the meme uses: dangerous lunatics.

Today’s liberals fighting for trans rights, climate action, prison reform, universal healthcare, or racial equity are not outliers—they are the inheritors of the same tradition of struggle that brought us civil rights, women’s suffrage, labor protections, and the end of Jim Crow. The smear is recycled, the villains the same, only the targets change.

So when this meme says a 2025 liberal would be seen as a lunatic in 1960, the correct response is: yes, and so would MLK, so would Gloria Steinem, so would Harvey Milk, so would Rachel Carson. That’s the price every reformer pays in their own time. History vindicates them later, long after conservatives have finished slandering their names.

4. Gaslighting by Historical Erasure

This meme is a classic example of political gaslighting. It seeks to erase the fact that conservatives have consistently opposed progress, then recast them as victims of liberal overreach. By presenting the liberal tradition as having “gone insane,” it implies that conservatives are merely normal people holding on to common sense. It’s a rhetorical trick that flips the moral polarity of history.

In reality, the conservatives of 1960 were segregationists. The conservatives of 1980 were moral crusaders against gay rights and feminists. The conservatives of 2000 were launching wars under false pretenses and mocking climate scientists. The conservatives of 2025 chant about “wokeism” and wave off democracy itself. There’s nothing noble or sane in that trajectory. It’s the same rot, dressed in new culture-war slogans.

Meanwhile, liberals—whether in 1960 or 2025—continue to fight for expanded freedom, justice, and inclusion. To portray that continuity as “lunacy” is to side with the oppressors of every generation.

5. The Function of the Meme: Whitewashing Fascism

Let’s be clear: this meme is not about history. It’s not a good-faith commentary on ideological drift. It’s propaganda. Its function is to normalize reactionary extremism by recasting it as yesterday’s normality. It’s an attempt to gaslight people into thinking that MAGA’s frothing bigotry is just the same old common sense your grandfather had.

But that’s false. MAGA is not a 1960 liberal; it’s a 1960 segregationist with a smartphone and a Facebook account. The meme doesn’t reveal a truth about shifting liberalism—it conceals the continuity of right-wing cruelty across decades.

6. What This Meme Doesn’t Want You to Remember

  • In 1960, liberals pushed civil rights; conservatives resisted. Today, liberals defend voting rights; conservatives suppress them.

  • In 1960, liberals pushed feminism; conservatives derided women who sought equality. Today, liberals fight for reproductive rights; conservatives try to drag us back to forced births.

  • In 1960, liberals demanded environmental protections; conservatives mocked them. Today, liberals demand climate action; conservatives call it hysteria.

  • In 1960, liberals wanted separation of church and state; conservatives tried to entrench Christianity in public law. Today, liberals still want separation of church and state; conservatives demand Christian nationalism.

The actors have not changed. The fight has not changed. Only the battlegrounds shift with time.

7. Why the Meme Resonates With the Right

The right shares this meme because it flatters them. It allows them to cast themselves as misunderstood moderates, punished for being consistent while liberals run wild into absurdity. It’s a coping mechanism for a movement that has lost every moral battle of the last century. By pretending they are “yesterday’s liberals,” they get to avoid confronting the uncomfortable truth: that history will damn them just as it damned their predecessors.

It also weaponizes nostalgia. For people unsettled by change, the idea that liberals have “gone too far” is comforting. It lets them believe they are the reasonable center, even while they cheer on book bans, voter purges, and authoritarian strongmen. Nostalgia becomes a shield against accountability.

8. The Real Lesson From This Meme

The meme unintentionally reveals a truth: progress is always labeled lunacy in its own time. A 2025 liberal would indeed look extreme in 1960—because fighting for climate justice, trans rights, and universal healthcare would be alien concepts in that era. But that doesn’t mean today’s liberals are wrong. It means they’re ahead of their time, just as the civil rights movement was ahead of its time, just as suffragettes were ahead of theirs.

The people who called MLK a dangerous radical in 1960 were conservatives. The people who call today’s liberals dangerous radicals in 2025 are conservatives. And history will judge them the same way: as obstacles to justice, as footnotes on the wrong side of humanity’s story.

Conclusion

This meme wants you to believe that liberals have lost their minds, that conservatives are the true heirs of common sense, and that progressivism is a march toward lunacy. But the historical record demolishes that narrative. Conservatives were reactionary in 1960, and they remain reactionary in 2025. Liberals were fighting for justice in 1960, and they are still fighting for justice in 2025. The only thing that has changed are the fronts on which the battle is fought.

If a liberal from 1960 showed up in 2025, he might be shocked by how far society has come. But if a liberal from 2025 showed up in 1960, he’d be called a lunatic for the same reason all reformers are: because justice looks insane to those who profit from injustice. And that is not a condemnation of liberalism. It’s a badge of honor.

The meme collapses under its own false equivalence. The real lunacy is thinking that cruelty, repression, and authoritarian nostalgia represent sanity.

This isn’t a glitch.
It’s the design.
