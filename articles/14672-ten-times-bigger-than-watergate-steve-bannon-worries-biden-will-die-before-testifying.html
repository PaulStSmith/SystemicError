'Ten times bigger than Watergate': Steve Bannon worries Biden will die before testifying

Right-wing influencer Steve Bannon argued that former President Joe Biden's prostate cancer diagnosis was 10 times "bigger than Watergate."

During an interview with MAGA ally Laura Loomer, Bannon suggested that aides had revealed Biden's cancer diagnosis to distract from recently released interview tapes from former special counsel Robert Hur.

Bannon, a former strategist for President Donald Trump, worried that Biden might die before the Department of Justice and Congress could investigate him.

"So they reveal the cancer on Sunday after this thing blows up on Saturday, Friday night, and Saturday," he said. "What then is to be done? What is your recommendation to DOJ? What's your recommendation to the House? What's your recommendation to investigative reporters? What should be done here, given you may not have Biden around in six, seven, eight months, ma'am?"

Loomer argued that doctors treating Biden, including his wife Jill, should have their licenses revoked and be sentenced to prison.

"So, you know, there definitely needs to be an investigation into all of the physicians who paid Joe Biden visits," she insisted. "I think that all of his staff need to be subpoenaed, and there needs to be an investigation and charges brought against Dr. Jill, the fake doctor, who has committed elder abuse."

Our Analysis:

A Critical Dissection of Bannon's Prostate Cancer-Watergate Comparison

In a recent spectacle of conspiracy theorizing, Steve Bannon, a right-wing influencer and former strategist for President Donald Trump, made a claim that can only be described as audaciously absurd. He argued that former President Joe Biden's prostate cancer diagnosis is "10 times bigger than Watergate." This statement, made during an interview with MAGA ally Laura Loomer, is a prime example of the lengths to which political figures will go to manipulate facts for their narrative.

The Real Watergate vs. A Medical Diagnosis: An Absurd Comparison

To equate a personal medical diagnosis with one of the most significant political scandals in American history is not only ludicrous but a dangerous conflation of the personal with the political. Watergate, an event that led to the resignation of President Richard Nixon, involved illegal activities and a cover-up at the highest levels of government. Biden's health, on the other hand, is a personal issue and leveraging it for political gain is both morally repugnant and intellectually dishonest.

Unfounded Accusations and Dangerous Rhetoric

Bannon's insinuation that Biden's diagnosis was revealed to distract from interview tapes of former special counsel Robert Hur is speculative at best and nefariously misleading at worst. The leap to suggest that Biden might die "in six, seven, eight months" to preemptively discredit potential investigations is nothing short of fear-mongering. It is a baseless attempt to cast doubt on the legitimacy of investigations and the judicial process.

Furthermore, Laura Loomer's call for the revocation of medical licenses and imprisonment for Biden's medical team, including his wife Jill Biden, whom she disparagingly refers to as a "fake doctor," crosses the line from political commentary into personal attack and legal absurdity. It's essential to note that Jill Biden holds a Ed.D., making the term "doctor" both accurate and deserved, albeit not in a medical sense, which Loomer's comment seems to intentionally misunderstand to mislead.

The Misuse of the Term "Elder Abuse"

The accusation of elder abuse is a serious one, and its misuse here is reprehensible. Elder abuse is a real and pressing issue for many, and to weaponize it as a political tool diminishes the experiences of those truly affected. The suggestion that medical care, presumably provided with the patient's consent, constitutes abuse is a gross misrepresentation of what elder abuse entails.

The Irony of Calling for Investigations

Bannon's and Loomer's calls for investigations into Biden's medical team and the insinuation of illegitimate "auto pen pardons" are ironic, considering their alignment with the Trump administration, which has been the subject of numerous investigations itself. The fixation on investigating Biden over a medical diagnosis, rather than addressing substantive policy issues or allegations of misconduct, reveals a partisan agenda that prioritizes political gain over the well-being of the nation.

Conclusion: A Dangerous Diversion

In conclusion, Bannon's comparison of Biden's cancer diagnosis to Watergate is not only a gross exaggeration but a dangerous diversion from real issues. It represents an alarming trend in political discourse where personal health issues are weaponized, and conspiracy theories are propagated without regard for truth or decency. As political commentators and the general public, it is our responsibility to challenge these baseless claims and demand a higher standard of discourse. The real "Systemic Error" here is the perpetuation of such unfounded and harmful narratives for political ends.
