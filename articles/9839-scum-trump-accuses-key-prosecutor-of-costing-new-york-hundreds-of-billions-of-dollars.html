'Scum': Trump accuses key prosecutor of costing New York 'hundreds of billions of dollars'

Donald Trump (Reuters)

Donald Trump on Saturday accused Attorney General of New York Letitia James of costing the state “hundreds of billions of dollars.”

Trump took to Truth Social to lob the attack at James, who won a civil fraud lawsuit against him.

“Corrupt Letitia James is costing New York State Hundreds of Billions of Dollars in lost business. No company or individual wants to be there, knowing that she’s the ‘Attorney General,’” Trump claimed without providing evidence. “She is a Complete and Total Disaster, who tried running against Kathy Hochul for Governor, and only got 1% of the Vote in Polls, and QUIT.”

Trump continued:

“She then went back to her WITCH HUNT against President Donald J. Trump, and others. Until this ‘SCUM’ is removed from the Attorney General’s Office, no company will move to New York, and few companies will be using the New York Stock Exchange, or NASDAQ, for going Public.”

The president then added, “The new Texas Exchange will be taking ALL of this business away, and we’re talking Hundreds of Billions of Dollars!”

Our Analysis:

Analyzing Trump’s Attack on Attorney General Letitia James

Donald Trump’s recent tirade against New York Attorney General Letitia James on Truth Social is a masterclass in deflection and unsubstantiated claims. Trump accuses James of costing New York “hundreds of billions of dollars” due to her legal actions against him. This claim, as expected, comes without a shred of evidence to support it.

The Unsubstantiated Economic Fallout

Trump’s assertion that James’ actions are driving businesses away from New York and towards a “new Texas Exchange” is a bold one. It’s also entirely baseless. No data or analysis is provided to back up this grandiose claim of economic migration caused by the Attorney General’s legal pursuits. It’s a classic Trump move: make a sweeping, negative statement and hope the audience doesn’t notice the lack of substantiation.

The Personal Attack Masquerading as Economic Concern

Calling James “Corrupt”, “a Complete and Total Disaster”, and “SCUM” shifts the conversation from legal and procedural critique to personal vilification. It’s a tactic designed to discredit the prosecutor, not through legal argumentation or evidence, but through character assassination. This is not a new strategy for Trump, but it’s always disheartening to see political discourse dragged down to such levels.

Misrepresenting Political History

Trump’s mention of James’ gubernatorial bid is misleading. His reduction of her political career to a single failed attempt and an alleged 1% polling is a gross oversimplification. It ignores the complexity of political campaigns, the reasons behind James’ decision to run (or not run), and her eventual decision to continue her work as Attorney General. This reductionist view serves to undermine James’ legitimacy and competence without engaging with her actual performance in office.

The Threat to New York’s Economy

The idea that James’ legal actions against Trump could single-handedly drive businesses from New York to Texas is ludicrous. New York remains a global financial hub; its status is built on more than the whims of a single state official. The suggestion that the New York Stock Exchange and NASDAQ would lose significant business due to this is to misunderstand the complexities of financial markets and the reasons companies choose to go public in New York.

Conclusion

Donald Trump’s attack on Letitia James is a textbook example of his approach to criticism: attack the critic, ignore the criticism. Instead of addressing the substance of James’ lawsuit, Trump opts to attack her character and invent economic consequences. This strategy might rally his base, but it does nothing to address the actual legal and ethical issues at hand. It’s a smokescreen, designed to distract from the real conversation about accountability and justice.
