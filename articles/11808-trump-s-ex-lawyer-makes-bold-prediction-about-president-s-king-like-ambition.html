When Americans stepped into the voting booth in 2024, they brought to power something more like a monarchy that a presidency, according to the president's ex-lawyer and fixer Michael Cohen.

"I’ve never been shy about making bold, public predictions. And to date, as you have seen, I’ve been right almost every time. This one is no different," he wrote on his Substack over the weekend. "We didn’t just elect President Trump to a second term. We gave him power that looks less like a presidency and more like a monarchy. And this King is already using his courts to punish those who stood against him."

Cohen goes on to say, "We’re not guessing. We’re not speculating. We’re watching it happen in real time."

"Every day there’s another headline, another subpoena, another court ruling; and every day Trump is winning," he added. "Not quietly, not in the shadows, but right in front of us. It’s the kind of political retribution that used to be whispered about. Now, it’s a press conference."

Cohen goes on to cite a recent example of exactly what he means.

"Ed Martin is the clearest example of how this is being carried out. A longtime Trump loyalist and vocal supporter of those who stormed the Capitol on January 6, Martin now leads something called the 'Weaponization Working Group' at the Department of Justice," Cohen wrote. "The title alone sounds like a parody, but the mission is deadly serious: target the president’s political opponents under the banner of rooting out 'abuses' in the justice system."

He continued:

"Martin’s first assignments? New York Attorney General Letitia James; the woman who hit Trump with a half-billion-dollar civil fraud judgment, and Senator Adam Schiff, who led the charge on impeachment. Both are facing federal investigations over allegations of mortgage fraud. Both deny wrongdoing. And both now have federal prosecutors digging into their lives because they had the audacity to stand up to the King."

Cohen next makes his prediction about Trump's targets.

"So, here’s my newest prediction: both Letitia James and Adam Schiff will be held 'accountable.' Whether it’s pursuant to some strained interpretation of law or simply Trump’s personal desire, they are both doomed in this system. That’s not because they’re guilty of anything; it’s because they went after the King and failed to take him down," he wrote Sunday. "And in politics, as in history, there’s an old adage: you don’t strike at a King unless you kill him. They missed; and now, he’s coming for them. God bless their political souls. May they find peace in the afterlife!"

Our Analysis:

The Monarchical Ambitions of Donald Trump: A Critical Analysis

Michael Cohen, once the loyal attorney and fixer for Donald Trump, has now taken to his Substack to issue a grave warning about the trajectory of American politics under Trump's influence. Cohen, no stranger to bold claims, asserts that the reelection of Trump heralds not just another presidential term but the rise of a monarchy in disguise. This isn't hyperbole; it's a distress signal.

Cohen's Insight: A Monarchy Masquerading as a Presidency

Cohen's assertions are grounded in his intimate understanding of Trump's ambitions and methodologies. His claim that the U.S. has essentially crowned Trump a king isn't made lightly. It's based on observable, ongoing actions: the wielding of power to target and punish political adversaries, a practice more befitting a tyrannical monarch than a democratically elected president. The formation of the so-called 'Weaponization Working Group' under Ed Martin, tasked with targeting Trump's political foes, epitomizes this monarchial misuse of power. The group's Orwellian title belies its true purpose: not to safeguard justice but to weaponize it against Trump's adversaries.

The Case of Letitia James and Adam Schiff

Cohen points to Letitia James and Adam Schiff as prime examples of Trump's retribution in action. Both have been thorns in Trump's side—James, with her half-billion-dollar civil fraud judgment against him; Schiff, as a leading figure in impeachment proceedings. Now, under the guise of rooting out "abuses" in the justice system, they find themselves the subjects of federal investigations. This isn't justice; it's revenge, served cold and calculated.

Cohen's Prediction: A Foregone Conclusion

Cohen's newest prediction—that James and Schiff will be held "accountable" not for any actual wrongdoing but for their opposition to Trump—underscores a grim reality. In Trump's America, the law is not an instrument of justice but a weapon of political vendetta. Cohen's invocation of the adage about striking at a king and missing speaks volumes about the perilous state of democracy under Trump's rule.

Conclusion: A Wake-Up Call

Cohen's article is more than an exposé; it's a wake-up call. The transformation of the presidency into a monarchy, with Trump at its helm, wielding the justice system as his personal cudgel, is not a future possibility but a present reality. This isn't just about Trump; it's about the soul of American democracy. Cohen, through his firsthand experience and subsequent revelations, paints a picture of a political landscape warped by power and revenge. It's a scenario that should alarm anyone, regardless of political affiliation.

As observers, analysts, and citizens, the onus is on us to heed Cohen's warning. The shift towards autocracy under Trump isn't just a threat; it's unfolding in real time. Dismissing Cohen's insights as mere speculation or hyperbole would be both naive and dangerous. The evidence is clear, the trajectory alarming. The question now is not if this march towards monarchy can be stopped, but whether the will exists to halt it.
