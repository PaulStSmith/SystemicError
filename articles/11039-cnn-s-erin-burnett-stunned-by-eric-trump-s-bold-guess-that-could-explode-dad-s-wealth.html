Eric Trump's eye-popping prediction for Bitcoin floored CNN anchor Erin Burnett on Friday evening, who warned if it comes true, President Donald Trump's wealth will "explode."

Eric Trump predicted Bitcoin would reach $1 million during his speech at the Bitcoin Asia 2025 conference in Hong Kong.

At the conference, he told audience members, "There’s no doubt bitcoin will reach $1 million," meaning one Bitcoin would be worth $1 million. That number currently stands at about $108,000. Eric Trump cited long-term demand, limited quantities of the cryptocurrency and surging institutional interest.

"It's a stunning number," Burnett told viewers of her show, "OutFront," noting that Eric Trump delivered an "effusive sales pitch for Bitcoin while his family is profiting big time from crypto."

She then played a montage of Eric Trump's comments, calling Bitcoin "the greatest asset in the world."

"I really believe in the next several years, Bitcoin hits $1 million. There's no question. Buy right now. Shut your eyes. Hold it for the next five years and you are going to do terrifically well," he promised.

Should his prediction come true, Burnett noted it'd be a 10-times return.

Dan Alexander, senior editor for Forbes, told Burnett that Donald Trump has "conjured up about $1.5 billion from new crypto projects."

"He's also taken his most valuable asset, a $2 billion stake in the Trump Media & Technology Group, and turned that toward crypto. So if you add those two things together right now, you're looking at about $3.5 billion tied to the crypto industry of his roughly $6 billion fortune," he said.

The number astounded Burnett.

"That is incredible," she replied. "I mean, it's actually really stunning. Never mind that he used to say crypto was bunk. Just the amount of money that they're making — and his sons are out there sales and marketing on it — is impressive."

Alexander later said Eric Trump's embrace of crypto has improved his relationship with his father. While Trump put his children in charge of his assets during his first term, they were mostly seen as asset managers.

"They weren't doing a lot of entrepreneurial things," said Alexander.

That's changed in Trump 2.0.

"Not only do they have the keys to the car, but they're allowed to drive it as fast as they want," he said.

And they're steering toward Bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies.

"It's made tons of money not just for their father, but also for them. It's surely given him a lot of confidence, giving him a reason to sort of — it’s kind of a coming-out party. He’s an entrepreneur in his own right now," said Alexander, of Eric Trump.

Our Analysis:

Eric Trump's Bitcoin Bonanza: A Tale of Profit and Prognostication

In a recent spectacle that could only unfold in the world of cryptocurrency and high-stakes finance, Eric Trump took center stage with a prediction so audacious it left CNN anchor Erin Burnett flabbergasted. At the Bitcoin Asia 2025 conference in Hong Kong, Trump Jr. confidently declared that Bitcoin would skyrocket to $1 million. Currently lounging around the $108,000 mark, such a prediction doesn't just raise eyebrows—it practically launches them into orbit.

The Sales Pitch of the Century

The younger Trump's pitch was nothing short of evangelical. Amid his sermon on Bitcoin’s inexorable rise, he cited familiar reasons: long-term demand, scarcity, and burgeoning institutional interest. Yet, his exhortation to "Buy right now. Shut your eyes. Hold it for the next five years," smacks more of a Vegas gamble than a sound investment strategy. It's the financial equivalent of a firework display: brilliant, bold, and potentially explosive.

Trump's Crypto Empire

Erin Burnett's reaction to Trump's crypto crusade was a mix of astonishment and skepticism. The revelation that Donald Trump has leveraged his fortune towards crypto, turning a $2 billion stake into a $3.5 billion crypto behemoth, is a jaw-dropper. The transformation of Trump's portfolio from real estate mogul to crypto kingpin underscores a seismic shift in the family's business strategy.

Profit and Paradox

The irony of the Trumps' newfound crypto enthusiasm cannot be overstated. Once dismissed as "bunk" by the patriarch, cryptocurrency has now become the golden goose for the Trump empire. This about-face is not just a change of heart; it's a full-blown love affair with digital currency. The fact that Eric Trump's Bitcoin zeal has reportedly improved his relationship with his father speaks volumes about the allure of crypto profits.

Entrepreneurial Evolution or Ethical Erosion?

Dan Alexander's comments on the Trumps' entrepreneurial shift hint at a deeper narrative. The Trump children, once mere custodians of their father's assets, have revved the engine and are now speeding down the crypto highway. This "coming-out party" for Eric Trump as an entrepreneur is laced with both opportunity and opacity. The blurring lines between personal profit, public promotion, and political power raise questions about the ethical implications of such a venture.

Conclusion: A Gilded Gamble

Eric Trump's million-dollar Bitcoin bet is more than just financial speculation; it's a symbolic stake in the ground for the Trump family's evolving business empire. As spectators and potential investors watch this crypto carousel spin, the distinction between visionary investment and veiled opportunism becomes increasingly blurred. In the end, whether Bitcoin reaches the stratospheric heights of Trump's prediction or crashes back to earth remains to be seen. What's clear, however, is that the Trumps are all in—and the stakes could not be higher.
