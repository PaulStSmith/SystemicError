Trump knows exactly what he's doing — and that's what makes him dangerous

Misdiagnosing Monsters: The Dangerous Deflection of Donald Trump’s True Nature

The Comfort of Misdirection

In the relentless swirl of discourse surrounding Donald Trump’s public behavior and political maneuvering, a particularly misleading narrative has surfaced: attributing his actions to dementia. This notion doesn’t just misdiagnose the man; it fundamentally obscures the calculated and intentional nature of his toxic politics. By suggesting his behaviors could be the product of a medical condition, such as dementia, commentators and parts of the public are dangerously close to absolving Trump of responsibility.

Power Play and Puppeteering

Trump is not an isolated actor in his political theater. He is deeply embedded within a network of powerful elites who use his visibility and volatility to their advantage. Figures like Elon Musk and Mark Zuckerberg are portrayed as leveraging Trump as a “power tool” to gain unfettered access to economic and political power. This is not a scenario of a lone, confused old man but a strategic alliance of mutual benefit among wealthy and influential players.

Strategic Selections and Supreme Consequences

The careful selection of individuals like Stephen Bannon, Stephen Miller, and particularly Supreme Court Justices Neil Gorsuch, Brett Kavanaugh, and Amy Coney Barrett under Trump’s administration was not random or misguided. These choices were deliberate, loaded with the purpose to reshape American politics and societal norms. The characterization of Trump as potentially senile overlooks the fact that these decisions were made to embed certain ideologies deeply into the judiciary and executive branches of the government.

Misplaced Sympathy and Real Victims

Equating Trump’s actions with the symptoms of dementia not only potentially garners undue sympathy but also insults those who truly suffer from the condition and their families. Dementia is a devastating reality for many, and its comparison to Trump’s intentional actions diminishes the genuine struggles faced by actual patients and their caregivers. This narrative serves as a distraction from the real and present dangers posed by Trump’s clear-minded, strategic decisions and actions.

The Dangers of Underestimation

Labeling Trump’s actions as the result of dementia underestimates the threat he poses. His tactics are not the result of cognitive decline but are choices designed to provoke, manipulate, and consolidate power. His history and behavior suggest a pattern of exploiting systems and people to maintain and grow his influence. This is not dementia; it is, as the source article vividly describes, hate.

Conclusion: Recognizing the Reality

The narrative that Donald Trump may be suffering from dementia is not just incorrect; it is hazardous. It shifts the focus from his agency and the calculated harm he and his enablers continue to inflict on political and social norms. Understanding Trump’s actions as deliberate moves in a game of power and influence is crucial. We must not mistake malicious intent for medical condition. The true diagnosis is not clinical but is deeply political, rooted in a history of manipulative and destructive behavior that has been consistently supported and enabled by powerful figures in American society. The story here isn’t medical; it’s a stark reminder of how power operates in plain sight, with real consequences for democracy.
