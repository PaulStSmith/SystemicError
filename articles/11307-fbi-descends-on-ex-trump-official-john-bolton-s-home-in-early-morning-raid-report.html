FBI descends on ex-Trump official John Bolton's home in early morning raid: report

Former Donald Trump advisor John Bolton is the subject of an early morning raid being conducted by the FBI, The Bulwark is reporting.

Bolstered by live video being streamed by Lawfare’s Ben Wittes which shows the street being blocked off by Montgomery County police, the Bulwark’s Jonathan Last is reporting, “Montgomery County police confirmed that they were on site in support of the FBI.”

Bolton has become a major critic of the Trump administration critic of the Trump administration since leaving under a cloud during the president’s first term, where the longtime advisor to presidents provided guidance on the Middle East.

The raid comes just days after the former Trump appointee criticized his former boss over his meeting with Vladimir Putin in Alaska.

Just over a week ago, Trump lashed out at Bolton and the media, which platforms Bolton, by writing on Truth Social, “Constantly quoting fired losers and really dumb people like John Bolton, who just said that, even though the meeting is on American soil, ‘Putin has already won.’ What’s that all about? We are winning on everything. The fake news is working overtime (no tax on overtime)!”

As the raid began, FBI Director Kash Patel posted on X, "NO ONE is above the law… @FBI agents on mission."

The New York Post is reporting, "Federal agents busted into Bolton’s house in Bethesda, Md., at 7 a.m. in an investigation ordered by FBI Director Kash Patel, a Trump administration official told The Post."

MSNBC is reporting that the FBI is searching for documents related to national security.

As the Bulwark’s Last noted, “It is uncertain what the law enforcement presence pertains to, but Bolton was the target of an investigation at the end of Trump’s first term for allegedly disclosing classified information in his 2020 memoir, The Room Where It Happened.”

Our Analysis:

The Raid on John Bolton: A Spectacle of Political Vendettas?

Early on a morning that will surely be etched in the annals of American political drama, the FBI, with the support of Montgomery County police, descended upon the residence of John Bolton, former advisor to Donald Trump. This raid, reported by The Bulwark and streamed by Lawfare’s Ben Wittes, wasn't just another law enforcement operation. It was a vivid tableau of the ongoing saga of political retribution and the complex dance of power within the echelons of the United States.

Bolton, a figure who has pivoted from being a stalwart advisor within the Trump administration to one of its most vociferous critics, especially regarding policies on the Middle East and Trump’s unsettling coziness with Vladimir Putin, finds himself in the eye of the storm. This raid, following closely on the heels of Bolton's criticism of Trump's meeting with Putin in Alaska, reeks not of justice, but of vengeance, a theme all too common in the current American political landscape.

Let's dissect the modus operandi and the timing. The raid was executed at 7 a.m., a time chosen perhaps for its element of surprise but also indicative of the urgency—or should we say desperation?—in silencing a dissenting voice. Kash Patel, FBI Director and a figure from the Trump administration, ominously posted on X, "NO ONE is above the law… @FBI agents on mission." This statement, while noble at its surface, under this context, drips with irony and the bitter aftertaste of political motivations.

The Allegations and The Undercurrents

The FBI's interest in Bolton purportedly centers around national security documents and allegations of disclosing classified information in his 2020 memoir, The Room Where It Happened. However, the subtext is impossible to ignore. The raid is not just about classified documents; it's a manifestation of the Trump administration's broader strategy to quash dissent and manipulate the levers of justice to serve personal vendettas.

Trump's attack on Bolton via Truth Social, labeling him a "fired loser" and "really dumb," is not just a personal rebuke but a strategic move to discredit Bolton before his supporters. This tactic of discreditation and demonization is emblematic of Trump's modus operandi, wherein he seeks to annihilate the credibility of anyone who dares to criticize him.

The Spectacle and The Response

What's equally concerning is the spectacle this raid has become, with live video streams and public statements from officials. It transforms a serious legal action into a media circus, undermining the gravitas of law enforcement operations and potentially prejudicing public opinion.

Conclusion: A Dangerous Precedent

The raid on John Bolton's house is more than just a search for documents; it's a glaring beacon of the dangerous path the United States is treading, where personal animosities can potentially dictate legal actions. It serves as a stark reminder of the necessity of maintaining a clear demarcation between law enforcement and political vendettas.

In the grand scheme of things, this raid might be a footnote in the tumultuous chronicle of American politics. However, it underscores a systemic error in the political framework of the United States, where the tools of justice can be wielded as weapons in political skirmishes. As this story unfolds, one must ask: Are we witnessing the law in action, or the actions of those who believe they are above the law? The answer to this question is pivotal, not just for John Bolton, but for the very soul of American democracy.
