'Absolutely sick': Kash Patel's 'humiliating' leadership at FBI stuns ex-GOP analyst

Kash Patel via Evelyn Hockstein/Reuters

Some of Kash Patel's leadership decisions at the FBI have been nothing short of "humiliating," according to one former conservative analyst.

Tim Miller, writer at large for The Bulwark, discussed Patel's leadership at the FBI on a recent episode of "The Bulwark Podcast." He recorded the episode before learning that conservative activist Charlie Kirk had been murdered at Utah Valley University on Wednesday. Patel's FBI is now leading the investigation, which has exposed some of the disarray behind the scenes.

Miller pointed out that Patel has made employment decisions based on an agent's political loyalty to President Donald Trump, citing a recent lawsuit filed by multiple former FBI Agents.

"It is sick what these people are doing at the FBI, it is absolutely sick," Miller said. "And it is going to lead to a more dangerous country. I think that both on the merits and on the politics of this, people should be outraged by the way that our federal police force is being run right now."

Miller also criticized Patel's handling of the investigation into Kirk's murder.

"Kash Patel absolutely bungled up publicly posting about having a suspect and then having to post again about how they had released the suspect," Miller said. "Humiliating."

Our Analysis:

Kash Patel's Controversial Tenure: An Analysis of FBI's Political Entanglement

The recent commentary by Tim Miller, a former conservative analyst and writer at large for The Bulwark, on Kash Patel's leadership at the FBI, shines a glaring spotlight on the agency's current turmoil. According to Miller, Patel's leadership has been "humiliating," especially in light of the handling of high-profile cases and employment decisions seemingly based on political allegiance. This critique, as discussed on a recent episode of "The Bulwark Podcast," delves into the heart of a deeply concerning issue: the apparent politicization of the Federal Bureau of Investigation.

Political Loyalty Over Professional Merit

The allegation that Patel has made employment decisions based on an agent's political loyalty to former President Donald Trump is not just alarming; it's profoundly dangerous. This approach undermines the very foundation of unbiased law enforcement and threatens the integrity of federal investigations. The fact that this claim is supported by a lawsuit filed by multiple former FBI agents only adds weight to the accusation. When political loyalty becomes the yardstick for employment or advancement within the FBI, the agency's credibility and the public's trust in its operations are severely compromised.

The Mishandling of High-Profile Investigations

Miller's critique of Patel's handling of the investigation into the murder of conservative activist Charlie Kirk is particularly damning. The public missteps—announcing a suspect had been apprehended only to later admit the suspect had been released—are not just embarrassing on a superficial level. They reflect a deeper disarray and lack of strategic foresight within the FBI's current leadership. Such blunders in high-profile cases not only hinder the investigative process but also potentially compromise the pursuit of justice.

A Dangerous Precedent

Miller's assertion that the current state of the FBI under Patel's leadership could lead to a "more dangerous country" is a stark warning. The implications of a politically compromised federal law enforcement agency are far-reaching. Beyond the immediate impact on specific investigations or employment practices, the perception that the FBI is driven by political biases can erode public trust, embolden criminals, and weaken national security.

The Call for Outrage

Miller believes that both the merits of the FBI's operations and the politics involved should incite public outrage. This is a reasonable stance, given the gravity of the accusations and the potential consequences of continued political interference in law enforcement. The integrity of the FBI is paramount to maintaining law and order, and any actions that compromise its impartiality and effectiveness should be met with significant concern and demand for accountability.

Conclusion

The criticisms leveled by Tim Miller against Kash Patel's leadership of the FBI paint a troubling picture of an agency in turmoil. The alleged prioritization of political loyalty over professional integrity and the mishandling of crucial investigations are issues that demand immediate attention and rectification. The FBI's role as a cornerstone of American law enforcement and national security makes its politicization a matter of grave concern. As the investigation into Charlie Kirk's murder continues, the spotlight on Patel's decision-making and leadership at the FBI will undoubtedly intensify. The credibility of the Bureau—and perhaps the safety of the nation—hangs in the balance.
