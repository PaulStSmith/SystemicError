'Some things never change!': Trump rages at Zelenskyy ahead of Monday meeting

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosAugust 20, 20253 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Our Analysis:Trump's Rhetoric on Ukraine: A Tangled Web of Contradictions

President Donald Trump raged at his Ukrainian counterpart ahead of a high-stakes meeting between the leaders on Monday.

Trump plans to meet with Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy and several European leaders on Monday to discuss ways to end the war in Ukraine. The meeting follows a summit Trump held with Russian President Vladimir Putin on Friday in Alaska.

Ahead of the meeting, Trump announced that there would be "very real consequences" for Russia if it did not accept a ceasefire. That ceasefire was not agreed to, according to media reports.

"President Zelenskyy of Ukraine can end the war with Russia almost immediately, if he wants to, or he can continue to fight," Trump posted on Truth Social on Sunday evening. "Remember how it started."

Trump has flip-flopped in his support for Ukraine since the war began. At times, he parroted Russian talking points about Ukraine starting the war, even though Russia invaded the country in February 2022.

Trump has also seemed to agree with Putin that Ukraine should give up some land in exchange for ending the war. Russia has said it wants to control the Donetsk region in eastern Ukraine. Zelenskyy rejected Russia's offer.

The president seemed to address the land swap issue in his Truth Social post as well.

"No getting back Obama given Crimea (12 years ago, without a shot being fired!), and NO GOING INTO NATO BY UKRAINE," the president wrote. "Some things never change!!!"

Our Analysis:

Trump's Rhetoric on Ukraine: A Tangled Web of Contradictions

President Donald Trump's approach to the ongoing war in Ukraine is a masterclass in inconsistency and confusion. His latest outburst directed at Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy ahead of a crucial meeting is no exception. Trump's statements, as reported, oscillate wildly between threatening Russia with "very real consequences" for not agreeing to a ceasefire and undermining Ukraine's position both territorially and in terms of NATO membership.

The Ceasefire Charade

Trump's threat of "very real consequences" for Russia's non-compliance with a ceasefire, which was unsurprisingly rebuffed, is a typical example of his bluster that often lacks follow-through. Such statements are intended more for media consumption than for effectuating genuine diplomatic solutions. The failure to secure a ceasefire after making such a bold claim only underscores the ineffectiveness of his strategy, if it can be called a strategy at all.

A Tug-of-War with Truth

Trump's narrative on the war's origins is as perplexing as it is misleading. His penchant for echoing Russian propaganda, which falsely attributes the start of the war to Ukraine, flies in the face of the universally acknowledged fact that Russia invaded Ukraine in February 2022. This propagation of falsehoods serves to muddy the waters and obscure Russia's unprovoked aggression against its neighbor.

Land for Peace: A Dangerous Concession

Perhaps most alarming is Trump's flirtation with the idea of Ukraine ceding territory to Russia in exchange for peace. This stance not only undermines Ukraine's sovereignty but also signals a willingness to reward aggressive territorial expansion. The mention of Donetsk and the dismissal of Ukraine's claim to Crimea, coupled with the opposition to Ukraine joining NATO, reveal a disconcerting alignment with Russian objectives in the region.

No Change in Sight

Trump's concluding remark, "Some things never change!!!", is ironically the most accurate observation in his tirade. Indeed, his approach to foreign policy, characterized by inconsistency, contradiction, and a dangerous oversimplification of complex issues, remains unchanged. His statements do little to advance the cause of peace in Ukraine and instead contribute to a narrative that emboldens aggressors and undermines the principles of national sovereignty and territorial integrity.

In summary, Trump's erratic posturing on the Ukraine crisis exemplifies a broader pattern of behavior that is not only unhelpful but potentially hazardous. By playing fast and loose with the facts and sending mixed signals to both allies and adversaries, Trump complicates diplomatic efforts to resolve the conflict and casts doubt on the United States' commitment to upholding international norms. The situation demands a clear, principled stance in support of Ukraine's sovereignty and territorial integrity, not a cacophony of contradictory statements that serve only to confuse and detract from the gravity of the situation.
