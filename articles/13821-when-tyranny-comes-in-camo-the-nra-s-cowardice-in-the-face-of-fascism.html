When Tyranny Comes in Camo: The NRA's Cowardice in the Face of Fascism

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosJune 10, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

The Hypocrisy is the PointGuns for Me, Not for TheeA Paper Tiger with Blood on Its HandsThe Real Patriots Are in the Streets

The NRA has long painted itself as the vanguard of liberty, the last line of defense between the American people and a tyrannical government. Their rhetoric is soaked in revolutionary cosplay, invoking the Founding Fathers, quoting Jefferson out of context, and fetishizing firearms as sacred instruments of freedom. “The tree of liberty must be refreshed with the blood of patriots and tyrants,” they love to repeat, conveniently forgetting the historical nuance that surrounds that quote. In their narrative, the citizen must be ever-ready to rise up, musket or AR-15 in hand, to defend against government overreach.

But now, in June 2025, as Los Angeles burns under the heel of an actual authoritarian crackdown—complete with ICE raids, National Guard occupation, Marines patrolling American streets, and protesters facing tear gas and rubber bullets—the NRA has nothing to say. Not a press release. Not a tweet. Not even a weak “thoughts and prayers.” The silence is not just conspicuous. It’s revealing.

The Hypocrisy is the Point

Let’s be crystal clear: the NRA’s silence isn’t an oversight. It’s policy. Because the fantasy they’ve sold for decades was never about resisting tyranny in general—it was always about resisting liberal governance. When a Democrat proposes common-sense gun control? That’s tyranny. When a Republican president invokes Title 10 to militarize domestic law enforcement against peaceful protesters? That’s “maintaining order.”

The NRA’s entire raison d'être has been a lie. It was never about defending liberty—it was about arming a particular ideology. One that sees immigrants as invaders, Black Lives Matter protesters as threats, and the federal government as tyrannical only when it serves the vulnerable.

When the ATF enforces gun laws under a Democratic administration, the NRA calls for defiance. When ICE snatches people off the street under orders from a convicted felon occupying the Oval Office, they applaud from the shadows. Because this is the kind of tyranny they’ve always supported—the kind that targets the marginalized while protecting the powerful.

Guns for Me, Not for Thee

Their silence also exposes the racial and class dynamics embedded in their so-called principles. When armed white men stormed the Michigan Capitol during COVID lockdowns, the NRA defended them. When Black Panthers lawfully carried weapons into the California Statehouse in the 1960s, the NRA helped Ronald Reagan push for stricter gun laws. When Kyle Rittenhouse crossed state lines to play vigilante, he was lionized. When Philando Castile, a licensed Black gun owner, was shot by police during a traffic stop, the NRA shrugged.

And now, as immigrant neighborhoods in Los Angeles are terrorized by federal agents, as working-class protesters face down riot police and soldiers, as journalists are assaulted and civil liberties trampled—the NRA offers no defense. Because the people under siege aren’t their people. Their vision of the Second Amendment doesn’t include undocumented immigrants, Black and brown communities, or the left-wing working class. Those Americans are the ones the NRA wants guns used against, not the ones they want armed in defiance of state violence.

A Paper Tiger with Blood on Its Hands

This is the organization that has pumped fear and paranoia into American politics for generations. That lobbied to keep guns in the hands of abusers, stalkers, and extremists. That radicalized millions into believing that any regulation was the first step to dictatorship. That profited off school shootings, mass murders, and suicides by pushing more guns as the solution to every problem.

But when actual tyranny arrives—not in the form of legislation or taxes, but in boots on the ground, raids at dawn, and the suppression of dissent—they cower. Because their guns were never meant for that kind of fight. They were meant to intimidate, not resist. To suppress, not defend. To uphold a twisted vision of America where the only people with rights are the ones who look and think like them.

The Real Patriots Are in the Streets

The protesters in Los Angeles—immigrants, students, workers, journalists—are the real defenders of liberty right now. They are standing up against an increasingly fascist regime using their voices, their presence, and yes, sometimes their bodies as shields. They are doing what the NRA claims is the highest American virtue: resisting government tyranny.

And yet, not only does the NRA abandon them—it stands, silently and complicitly, with the boot that crushes them.

It’s time to stop pretending the NRA is a civil rights organization. It is a propaganda arm for right-wing authoritarianism. A grift that wraps fascism in the flag and calls it freedom. And every time they stay silent in moments like these, they prove it.

The next time someone parrots that tired NRA line about needing guns to resist tyranny, ask them where the NRA was when the Marines rolled into Los Angeles.

We already know the answer: polishing their boots, not raising their rifles.
