'Morning Joe' aghast at Trump's bizarre obsession 'with the male physique'

Based on an article by Sarah K. Burris

Paulo SantosOctober 14, 20263 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

What happened

AlterNet reports that President Donald Trump used an Ohio appearance on Monday night to praise Republican Sen. John Husted’s body, saying he examined Husted “up and down” and commenting on his rear end, hands and jawline.

On Tuesday, MSNBC’s “Morning Joe” panel mocked the remarks. Joe Scarborough described Trump as increasingly preoccupied with muscular men, while Mika Brzezinski said he should “keep his private life private.” Scarborough also argued that such commentary was unlikely to help Husted at a midterm campaign event.

The power behind the spectacle

Trump was not merely an eccentric guest saying something embarrassing. He was the president appearing at a political event for a senator whom Scarborough described as potentially vulnerable. Trump held the institutional power in the room: presidential authority, party dominance and the ability to commandeer the purpose of the event.

Husted’s role, by contrast, was to receive the endorsement and absorb whatever spectacle accompanied it. That imbalance matters more than the panel’s jokes. A president turned a senator’s body into rally material, and the political apparatus around them treated the performance as part of the price of presidential support.

Mockery is not analysis

The “Morning Joe” segment focused heavily on insinuation: Trump notices men’s bodies, therefore the panel invited viewers to laugh at what that might imply about his private life. That framing is cheap and politically evasive. Nothing in AlterNet’s account establishes anything about Trump’s sexuality, and speculation about it explains neither his conduct nor his power.

The relevant issue is not that a man complimented another man’s appearance. It is that the president publicly appraised an ally’s rear end during an event ostensibly intended to advance that ally’s political interests. Turning the episode into a Village People joke replaces scrutiny of degrading presidential behavior with adolescent innuendo.

The senator becomes a prop

Trump’s remarks, as reported, reduced Husted’s political value to a collection of physical attributes: hands, jawline, athleticism and body shape. Husted was present as a senator seeking political reinforcement, but the president recast him as an object in Trump’s performance.

That is a familiar structure of personalized power. Allies are not treated as independent officeholders with constituencies and responsibilities; they become scenery for the dominant figure. Their dignity, message and campaign needs remain subordinate to his appetite for attention.

A media system built for diversion

David French joked that while Trump was rambling about physical characteristics, he was not making bad deals with Russia. The line inadvertently exposes the problem. Presidential spectacle consumes airtime precisely because it is easy to clip, ridicule and circulate. Consequential policy becomes the aside.

AlterNet reproduces that distortion rather than correcting it. The story documents the panel’s laughter more thoroughly than the Ohio event’s political purpose, Husted’s electoral position or any substantive issue before voters. The result is coverage about reactions to a provocation, not reporting that clarifies how presidential power was being used.

The politics of permanent distraction

The larger pattern is not hidden sexuality or harmless eccentricity. It is a political and media arrangement in which presidential self-indulgence displaces public substance, allies submit to humiliation for access, and commentators convert the degradation into entertainment.

Trump generated the spectacle. The event’s political organizers enabled it by placing presidential attention at the center of the campaign. “Morning Joe” then turned it into comic content, and AlterNet packaged the comedy as the story. Each institution followed its incentive; none gave readers much reason to understand what power was doing while everyone laughed.
