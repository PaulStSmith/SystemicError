Jimmy Kimmel, MeidasTouch, and the War on Free Speech

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosSeptember 18, 20253 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Disney/ABC just caved. Let’s not mince words: Jimmy Kimmel has been indefinitely suspended, his show effectively canceled, not because of poor ratings, not because of scandal, but because he dared to joke about the wrong sacred cow in the eyes of the Trumpist right. Nexstar Media — that vulture conglomerate operating 32 ABC affiliate stations — set the ball rolling by refusing to carry Jimmy Kimmel Live! over remarks he made following the Charlie Kirk debacle. Disney/ABC followed suit, suspending him entirely. Corporate cowardice dressed up as “business decisions.”

This isn’t “showbiz politics.” This is censorship. This is how authoritarian regimes tighten the noose: they don’t need to jail comedians outright, they simply pull the plug on their platforms. The oligarchs and their corporate media lapdogs do the dirty work, silencing voices that make people laugh and think. And let’s be clear — Kimmel’s comedy has always been a thorn in the side of the reactionary right precisely because it wields truth wrapped in satire.

The fascist creep isn’t theoretical anymore. First it was teachers and librarians. Then it was journalists. Now it’s late-night comedians. Colbert’s under siege. Kimmel’s show is gone. The message is unmistakable: fall in line or face professional ruin. Corporate news has chosen its side, and it isn’t with democracy.

That’s why MeidasTouch’s offer matters. Unlike Disney’s boardroom full of spineless execs who bow at the altar of political intimidation, MeidasTouch is standing up to the regime. With six million subscribers on YouTube and digital reach that eclipses ABC’s dwindling broadcast numbers, they’re building a platform that can’t be bought, bullied, or silenced. Their message to Kimmel — we will never tell you what you can or cannot say — is exactly the kind of radical transparency and solidarity this moment demands.

Let’s not kid ourselves. Meidas doesn’t have Disney’s war chest. They can’t throw Marvel money at late-night television. But what they do have is conviction. They’ve already dethroned Joe Rogan twice from the top of the YouTube podcast charts. They’ve built a digital-first media network that thrives because millions of people are hungry for the truth, not sanitized press releases. That’s more power than any corporate overlord can buy.

Systemic Error may not have Meidas’ six million subscribers — not yet. But we share their DNA. We stand with them in defense of the Free Press, against censorship, against fascism’s creeping hand. When comedians are punished for jokes, when networks are bullied into submission by political thugs, the press itself must rally. And we are here for that fight.

Jimmy Kimmel doesn’t need Disney. What he needs is a platform that won’t betray him at the first sign of pressure. What he needs is what MeidasTouch is offering: a home where free speech isn’t a slogan but a principle. A home where laughter, satire, and truth can breathe without a corporate censor’s red pen hovering overhead.

This is bigger than Kimmel. This is about drawing a line. Either the press, in all its forms, stands up to the regime’s intimidation, or we lose one more voice at a time until silence reigns. Today it’s Kimmel. Tomorrow it will be the rest of us.

At Systemic Error, we may be a smaller outfit, but our commitment is the same: unflinching truth, no matter how bitter it tastes. The free press is the last line of defense against authoritarianism. Every silenced comedian, every censored journalist, every gagged teacher is another step toward dictatorship. And we refuse to let that stand.

So to Jimmy Kimmel: take Meidas up on their offer. Keep the mic hot, the jokes sharp, the truth sharper. Democracy depends on people like you refusing to shut up.

And to Disney and Nexstar: history will remember where you stood. Spoiler alert — it won’t be on the side of freedom.
