'Huge scandal!' New Trump-Epstein photos light up internet

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosJuly 24, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Donald Trump raises a fist to the cameras. REUTERS/Evelyn Hockstein

President Donald Trump received sharp criticism on Tuesday after CNN published a batch of what anchor Erin Burnett called "never-before-seen" photos.

The photos show disgraced financier Jeffrey Epstein and Trump hanging out at some pivotal moments in the two men's lives. For instance, one photo depicts Epstein attending Trump's wedding to his second wife, Marla Maples. Another photo shows Trump and Epstein hanging out at the 1999 Victoria's Secret fashion show.

CNN published the photos at a time when Trump is trying to distance himself from the Epstein saga, which has consumed his presidency for about two weeks.

Social media lit up with reactions to the report.

"The Trump-Epstein friendship should have been a huge scandal in the 2020 election, the year after Epstein was charged and then died in prison on Trump’s watch," former MSNBC host and Zeteo founder Medhi Hasan posted on X. " It should have been a huge scandal in the 2024 election, after audio emerged of Epstein saying he was Trump’s closest friend. And it should be a huge scandal now!"

"Is there more to the story of why they stopped being such close friends?" Jesse Lee, a former senior advisor to Rep. Nancy Pelosi (D-CA), posed on X. "It’s always that they had a 'falling out,' or this 'creep' line. Suspicious repetition."

" Holy S---, Epstein was at Trump's second wedding and there's VIDEO," Keith Olbermann wrote on X.

"Trump is trying to distance himself but this just further destroys his argument," political commentator Harry Sisson posted on X.

"Donald Trump was best friends with one of the most notorious child molesters in American history," wrote the West Chester Democratic Party on Bluesky.

Our Analysis:

The Unveiling of a Controversial Alliance: Trump and Epstein

The recent CNN publication of "never-before-seen" photos showcasing the once-cozy relationship between former President Donald Trump and disgraced financier Jeffrey Epstein has reignited a firestorm of critique and speculation. These photos, which could very well be dubbed as snapshots of complicity, are not merely relics of the past but glaring evidences of a narrative Trump has tirelessly attempted to distance himself from. The visual documentation of Epstein at Trump's wedding and their joint appearance at the 1999 Victoria's Secret fashion show peels back the layers of a connection Trump wishes the public would forget.

The Inconvenient Truth

As Trump endeavors to extricate himself from the Epstein saga, these photos serve as inconvenient truths, undermining his efforts. It's a spectacle of irony and denial, where Trump's past actions starkly contrast his present disavowals. The timing of CNN's publication is particularly poignant, striking at the heart of Trump's political vulnerabilities as he attempts to navigate through the treacherous waters of his presidency’s controversies.

Social Media Erupts

The reaction on social media platforms was swift and merciless. Figures like Medhi Hasan and Jesse Lee didn't just throw shade; they cast a pall over Trump's attempts to rewrite his history with Epstein. Hasan's commentary underscores a significant failure of the political and media landscape to hold Trump accountable, not just in the aftermath of Epstein's charges and death but as an ongoing scandal that should have been at the forefront of electoral debates.

Jesse Lee's probing question about the nature of Trump and Epstein's "falling out" invites a deeper scrutiny into the dynamics of their relationship, suggesting that the oft-repeated narrative of a mere 'falling out' might be a convenient smokescreen.

Video Evidence: The Unraveling of a Denial

Keith Olbermann's revelation of video evidence of Epstein's presence at Trump's wedding not only adds another layer to this saga but also visualizes the depth of their association. This isn't just about being photographed together at events; it's about significant life moments and shared spaces that hint at a deeper, more intimate connection.

A Tarnished Legacy

The closing comment by the West Chester Democratic Party encapsulates the gravity of the situation. Trump's friendship with Epstein is not a mere footnote in his history; it's a glaring indictment of his character and the choices he's made. To be associated with "one of the most notorious child molesters in American history" is a stain no amount of political maneuvering can cleanse.

Conclusion

The unveiling of these photos is more than a revelation; it's a testament to the enduring ties that bind Trump to Epstein. As Trump tries to navigate his political future, these images serve as stark reminders of his past, challenging the narrative he seeks to promote. The discourse on social media following CNN's publication is not just a reaction; it's a demand for accountability and a refusal to let this aspect of Trump's history be swept under the rug. This saga is far from over, and as more information comes to light, one can only hope that it leads to a more informed and critical public discourse.
