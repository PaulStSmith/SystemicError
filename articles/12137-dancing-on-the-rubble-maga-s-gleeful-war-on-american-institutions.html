Dancing on the Rubble: MAGA's Gleeful War on American Institutions

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosAugust 2, 20253 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

From Conservatism to ContemptThe Myth of the Deep StateThe Fantasy of CollapseThe Cost of CrueltyA Cult of Destruction

On August 1, 2025, nearly half of the Department of Education was terminated by direct order of Donald Trump. The mass firing was swiftly condemned by figures like Senator Elizabeth Warren, who rightly described it as an all-out assault on public education and the Americans who rely on federal student aid. But in the far-right echo chambers of MAGA world, the news wasn’t met with concern or debate. It was met with celebration.

One of the loudest cheers came from the troll account "Catturd™," a popular right-wing persona who posted, "Your tears are so delicious, they taste like ice cream." The glee wasn’t just casual internet mockery—it was emblematic of a deeper, darker truth: MAGA is no longer a movement about conserving anything. It is a movement defined by destruction, revanchism, and a fetish for collapse.

From Conservatism to Contempt

Traditional conservatism believed in limited government, yes, but it also believed in functional government. It valued stability, institutions, and order. It viewed the Constitution as a framework to be preserved, not a hurdle to be smashed.

The MAGA movement has mutated beyond that. It has become a vehicle for grievance politics, its ideology less about principles than about enemies. MAGA doesn't want to fix the system. It wants to watch the parts it hates burn. Every federal employee is a suspect, every regulation a tyranny, every social safety net a socialist plot.

They do not want to reform the Department of Education. They want it obliterated. And when Trump swings the axe, they applaud not because they believe in a better way forward, but because they believe in vengeance.

The Myth of the Deep State

How did this transformation happen? Years of right-wing propaganda have laid the groundwork. The notion of the "Deep State"—a shadowy network of unelected bureaucrats supposedly working to undermine the will of the people—has become dogma on the right.

This mythology paints every civil servant as an enemy of freedom. It doesn't matter if they're a data analyst managing Pell Grant distributions or a career scientist advising on education standards. In MAGA world, these people are faceless operatives of a hostile regime. And if you believe that, then of course you'd cheer their removal.

The irony is that these are the very people who keep America functioning. The Department of Education doesn’t indoctrinate children—it administers financial aid, enforces civil rights in schools, and supports education for disabled students. Firing half its staff isn’t heroic. It’s vandalism.

The Fantasy of Collapse

The glee is more than spite; it’s fueled by a nihilistic fantasy. Many MAGA adherents have convinced themselves that America must be torn down to be rebuilt. They fantasize about a return to an idealized 1950s utopia—a world of rigid hierarchy, racial homogeneity, and unchallenged authority. They see today's institutions as corrupted beyond repair, so they cheer their dismantling as a necessary purge.

This is why you see them laugh at mass layoffs, belittle civil servants, and mock public grief. It’s not a bug. It’s the feature. They want to see the machine break.

The Cost of Cruelty

But while the troll accounts and culture warriors cheer, the consequences are real. Public schools across the country rely on federal guidance, funding, and civil rights enforcement. Millions of students depend on federal student loans to access higher education. The chaos created by slashing the Department of Education will not be felt by elites—it will be felt by the most vulnerable: poor students, disabled children, and communities without state-level support structures.

And let’s be clear: this isn’t fiscal conservatism. It’s cruelty wrapped in red, white, and blue. There is no plan, no replacement, no vision for better governance. Only a void where institutions used to be, and a mob cheering as the walls come down.

A Cult of Destruction

What we are witnessing is the political equivalent of a death cult. MAGA has become a movement that thrives on annihilation. It is sustained by the suffering of those it deems unworthy, and by the illusion that destruction is progress.

They don’t want to build. They want to rule the ruins.

And while they laugh, the rest of us are left with the bill: weakened institutions, a demoralized workforce, and a generation of Americans abandoned by their own government.

This is not patriotism. This is not governance. This is sabotage masquerading as leadership.

And if we don’t name it, confront it, and reject it, there won’t be anything left to save.

This isn’t a glitch.
It’s the design.
