Tulsi Gabbard threatens to shred Marco Rubio with scramble to help Trump

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosJuly 23, 20255 min read

Tulsi Gabbard (Reuters)

President Donald Trump's intelligence chief Tulsi Gabbard could wind up roping in Secretary of State Marco Rubio in her half-baked effort to expose a "treasonous conspiracy" against the president, according to a fact-checker.

The director of national intelligence released alleged evidence Friday accusing Barack Obama and his high-ranking administration officials of a criminal conspiracy against Trump related to the Russiagate investigation, and Washington Post fact-checker Glenn Kessler examined her claims – which she says will be referred for prosecution – against previous probes of the matter.

"All told, the previous [investigative] reports add up to about 2,500 pages of dense prose, compared with the thin gruel of emails and meeting agendas released by Gabbard," Kessler wrote. "Her report provides no indication that she has studied the earlier investigations. Yet she asserts there was 'direct intent to cover up the truth about what occurred.'"

"If so, one of the co-conspirators would be current Secretary of State Marco Rubio," he added.

Rubio, then a Republican senator from Florida serving on the Intelligence Committee, signed off on reports that looked into the creation of the Intelligence Community Assessment (ICA) about Russia's meddling in the 2016 election, and one volume of those findings spent nearly 160 pages on the assessment Gabbard dredged up as questionable.

“The Committee found the ICA presents a coherent and well-constructed intelligence basis for the case of unprecedented Russian interference in the 2016 U.S. presidential election,” the Senate report said. “On the analytic lines of the ICA, the Committee concludes that all [redacted] analytic lines are supported with all-source intelligence, although with varying substantiation. The Committee did not discover any significant analytic tradecraft issues in the preparation or final presentation of the ICA.”

Rubio also accepted another volume of the report that concluded Vladimir Putin had personally ordered a hack of Democratic operatives.

“Moscow’s intent was to harm the Clinton Campaign, tarnish an expected Clinton presidential administration, help the Trump Campaign after Trump became the presumptive Republican nominee, and undermine the U.S. democratic process,” the report stated.

The Senate investigation concluded that Trump was personally complicit, but their findings match up with allegations by special counsel Robert Mueller, who determined that Russian operatives successfully hacked Hillary Clinton's campaign and Democratic Party organizations, and those materials were posted online by WikiLeaks and other intermediaries to help the Republican candidate win the election.

"Why did the intelligence community become more convinced that Putin directed the effort to swing the election to Trump, instead of just seeking to inject turmoil in the U.S. elections?" Kessler wrote. "As the news reports misquoted by Gabbard show — and as subsequent investigations confirmed — additional, credible intelligence showed that Putin decided to back Trump over Clinton. That’s not a conspiracy but a natural evolution from careful investigative work."

Our Analysis:

Tulsi Gabbard's Quixotic Crusade: A Fact-Check

In a political landscape that often resembles a Salvador Dalí painting more than a functional democracy, the latest episode involves President Donald Trump’s Director of National Intelligence, Tulsi Gabbard, embarking on what can only be described as a quixotic quest. Gabbard has released what she claims is evidence of a "treasonous conspiracy" against Trump, drawing Secretary of State Marco Rubio into the fray. However, a closer examination by Washington Post fact-checker Glenn Kessler paints a picture of a half-baked effort lacking in both substance and understanding.

The Thin Gruel of Evidence

Gabbard’s allegations pivot on the Russiagate investigation and its implications. Yet, when placed under the microscope, her so-called evidence—primarily emails and meeting agendas—pales in comparison to the exhaustive investigative work previously undertaken. Kessler notes, "All told, the previous [investigative] reports add up to about 2,500 pages of dense prose, compared with the thin gruel of emails and meeting agendas released by Gabbard." This disparity not only underscores the superficiality of Gabbard's efforts but also hints at a lack of due diligence on her part.

Marco Rubio: Unwitting Co-Conspirator?

The narrative takes an intriguing turn with the implication of Marco Rubio. As a then-Republican senator from Florida and member of the Intelligence Committee, Rubio endorsed reports that starkly contrast with the narrative Gabbard attempts to weave. These reports, far from hinting at a conspiracy, confirmed the coherent and well-constructed intelligence basis for Russia's interference in the 2016 U.S. presidential election.

Rubio's acceptance of the Intelligence Community Assessment (ICA) and subsequent findings, including Vladimir Putin’s direct order to hack Democratic operatives, stands in stark contrast to Gabbard’s accusations. It begs the question: How can Rubio be implicated in a conspiracy that his own endorsed findings contradict?

A Misguided Missile

Perhaps the most glaring issue with Gabbard's crusade is not just the lack of substantial evidence but also the misinterpretation and misquoting of news reports. Kessler points out that contrary to Gabbard's assertions, subsequent investigations have corroborated the initial findings regarding Putin's preference for Trump over Clinton. This isn't indicative of a conspiracy but rather the fruition of meticulous investigative work.

Conclusion: A Quest for Truth or a Fool’s Errand?

In the final analysis, Tulsi Gabbard’s endeavor to expose a so-called treasonous conspiracy against President Trump seems more akin to tilting at windmills than a grounded pursuit of justice. The discrepancies between her claims and the established evidence are not just minor quibbles but chasms that undermine the credibility of her effort. Marco Rubio, rather than being a co-conspirator, emerges as an individual caught in the crosshairs of a misguided missile.

The political arena is no stranger to controversy and conspiracy theories, but it's imperative that allegations of this magnitude are grounded in solid evidence and coherent reasoning. As it stands, Gabbard's effort appears to be more sound and fury, signifying nothing than a legitimate exposé of wrongdoing.

In a world where facts are increasingly under siege, the responsibility falls on public figures and officials to uphold a standard of truth and integrity. Unfortunately, in this episode, what we witness is a departure from these principles, resulting in a spectacle that serves neither the public interest nor the cause of justice.
