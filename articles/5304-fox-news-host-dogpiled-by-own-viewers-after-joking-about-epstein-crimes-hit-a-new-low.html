Fox News viewers revolted against the network this weekend after hosts on its political talk show “The Five” erupted in laughter after joking about Jeffrey Epstein’s alleged crimes, and fiercely defended the Trump administration’s botched release of Epstein-related files.

“If you read the Epstein files, Epstein got his money from two Jewish billionaires – Les Wexner and Leon Black – and a little bit of money from the Jewish banking dynasty, the Rothschilds in Europe,” said Fox News’ Jesse Watters, who was among the five panelists.

“It looks like he’s mostly just a fixer! A guy who advises, he helps people with their problems – sometimes those problems are ‘you need a girl.’ If you need it, he’s got it!”

Watters’ fellow panelist, political commentator and comedian Greg Gutfeld, appeared to have thought up on the spot a new title for Epstein.

“He’s a sex rabbi!” Gutfeld exclaimed, sparking the room to erupt in laughter.

“Fox News has hit a new low,” wrote one YouTube user in the comment section of Fox News’ upload of the video.

“So FOX NEWS just [skips] over the fact that The Dear Leader’s name [is] mentioned 1 [million] times in [the Epstein] files,” wrote another, making reference to a claim from Rep. Jamie Raskin (D-MD), who said that Trump’s name appears more than 1 million times in the unredacted Epstein files.

“Holy hell, not you guys down playing it too,” wrote another.

Our Analysis:

Fox News’ Controversial Epstein Segment: A Deep Dive into Viewer Backlash

In a recent episode that could only be described as both baffling and disturbing, Fox News’ “The Five” descended into a realm where the gravity of Jeffrey Epstein’s alleged crimes became fodder for laughter and crass jokes. Panelist Jesse Watters, alongside his co-hosts, opted for a narrative so reductive and offensive, it could serve as a case study in media irresponsibility.

The Troubling Dismissal of Epstein’s Crimes

Watters’ commentary, suggesting Epstein was merely “a fixer” for those needing illicit services, trivializes the profound impact of Epstein’s actions on the lives of countless young women and girls. To frame a man facing serious allegations of sex trafficking as a mere problem-solver for the rich and powerful is not just tone-deaf; it’s a gross misrepresentation of the sinister nature of Epstein’s operations.

“Sex Rabbi”: A Disturbing Moniker

Greg Gutfeld’s addition of the “sex rabbi” title not only adds to the trivialization but also ventures into deeply insensitive territory, mocking both the severity of Epstein’s alleged crimes and the cultural significance of a rabbi in Judaism. Such remarks highlight a concerning lack of empathy and decorum, elements one would hope to find in any serious discussion on a news platform.

Viewer Outrage: A Sign of the Times

The viewer backlash on YouTube underscores a growing public intolerance for media missteps and insensitivities, especially concerning issues as grave as sex trafficking. Comments express dismay not only at the flippant treatment of Epstein’s crimes but also at what is perceived as a broader failure by the Justice Department—and by extension, the media—to adequately address and prosecute related crimes and co-conspirators.

Fox News’ “New Low”

What’s perhaps most telling is the characterization of this segment as Fox News hitting “a new low.” This sentiment reflects a broader disillusionment with the network’s handling of critical issues, with viewers feeling betrayed by a perceived downplaying of serious allegations and a failure to hold powerful figures accountable.

The “Dear Leader” and the Epstein Files

References to Donald Trump’s frequent appearances in the Epstein files, as noted by Rep. Jamie Raskin, only add fuel to the fire. The dismissal of these mentions by “The Five” panelists plays into a narrative of selective reporting and a reluctance to critically examine figures associated with the network’s favored political sphere.

Conclusion: A Call for Accountability

This episode serves as a stark reminder of the media’s powerful role in shaping public discourse and the importance of approaching sensitive topics with the gravity they deserve. Fox News’ viewers are clearly signaling their hunger for a more responsible and fact-based approach to reporting, one that respects the victims of crimes rather than making light of their suffering. It’s high time for networks, Fox News included, to heed this call for accountability and integrity in journalism.
