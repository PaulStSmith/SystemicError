Alabama's Jim Crow Revival: The GOP's Brazen Assault on Black Voters

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosMay 12, 20253 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

There are states that play dirty, and then there’s Alabama—a repeat offender in the long, ugly history of disenfranchising Black Americans. This time, the Republican-dominated state legislature has outdone itself by willfully ignoring multiple federal court orders, including from the Supreme Court, to redraw its racially gerrymandered congressional maps. And now, in an extraordinary decision, a federal panel has ruled what we already knew: Alabama intentionally discriminated against Black voters.

Not “passively.” Not “by accident.” Not as a byproduct of political convenience. No, this was intentional racial discrimination. An act of deliberate, calculated, systemic suppression aimed directly at a historically marginalized population whose votes threaten entrenched Republican power.

Let’s put that in context: a panel of three judges—including two appointed by Donald Trump—found it wasn’t even a close call. They called the state’s strategy to evade compliance a “strategic attempt to checkmate the injunction.” Translation: they knew they were violating the law, and they did it anyway, with full contempt for democracy and the courts. And this isn’t just defiance—it’s sabotage. Sabotage of democratic principles, of constitutional mandates, and of the dignity of every voter whose voice was reduced to background noise in a rigged political theater.

This is not a fluke. This is a feature. Alabama’s Republican lawmakers crafted their maps to box in Black voters, squeezing all that electoral power into one district while preserving their own unearned dominance in others. It’s apartheid by cartography. They ignored court rulings. They ignored the Voting Rights Act. They ignored the Constitution. And they did so not with regret or hesitation, but with smug certainty that they could get away with it. Because they often do.

And for what? Power. Raw, naked, racialized power. The kind of power that doesn’t seek to represent but to dominate. The kind of power that fears an honest vote count because it knows what that would mean: change. Accountability. Justice. And that terrifies them more than any court order.

The kicker? There are still no consequences for the actual people who orchestrated this. We talk about “the legislature” as if it were an amorphous cloud of paperwork. But the reality is that these are men with names, salaries, pensions, and titles—and they just got caught rigging democracy in broad daylight. If you or I tried to cheat a federal process with this kind of malice and planning, we’d be in jail.

But not them. Because in America, apparently, election theft is only a crime if you do it with a fake ballot. Do it with a Sharpie and a district map? Well, then it's just “politics.” White-collar election rigging is still whitewashed by legal technicalities and institutional inertia.

Enough. If this country wants to claim it’s a democracy, then it needs to stop treating civil rights like a game of Calvinball. We need federal oversight of these maps. We need criminal investigations. We need personal consequences. Because if Alabama gets away with this again, other states will follow. They already have. Texas, Georgia, Florida—they’re watching, taking notes, and sharpening their pencils. Every time one of these schemes goes unpunished, it becomes a blueprint.

Gerrymandering is not a policy dispute. It’s not some nerdy debate over drawing lines on a map. It’s a tool of authoritarianism. It is a scalpel in the hands of political surgeons who know exactly where to cut to drain the lifeblood from representative government. And Alabama is holding it with both hands, grinning, daring the rest of the country to stop them.

So what now? Do we wring our hands? Wait for the next election cycle to try again under rules still written by the cheaters? Or do we finally admit what this is—a structural attack on democracy—and fight it with the seriousness it demands?

Every stolen vote is a stolen voice. Every manipulated map is a knife in the back of the republic. And every coward who lets it happen is complicit.

Alabama may be leading this charge, but make no mistake: the battle is national. And the time to fight back is now.

This isn’t a glitch.
It’s the design.

Written and published by Paulo Santos for Systemic Error.
