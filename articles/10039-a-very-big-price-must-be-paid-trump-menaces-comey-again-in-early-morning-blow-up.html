'A very big price must be paid!' Trump menaces Comey again in early morning blow-up

Prior to rushing off for a day of golf at the Ryder Cup, Donald Trump jumped on Truth Social to lash out at former FBI Director James Comey, who was indicted late Thursday by the newly appointed U.S. Attorney Lindsey Halligan on two federal charges.

Trump, who had previously used his social media platform to urge Attorney General Pam Bondi to pursue Comey despite the previous U.S. attorney’s refusal to go before a grand jury, insisted once again that Comey lied to Congress.

On Truth Social, he wrote, “Whether you like Corrupt James Comey or not, and I can’t imagine too many people liking him, HE LIED! It is not a complex lie, it’s a very simple, but IMPORTANT one. There is no way he can explain his way out of it.”

The president also set the stage for future complaints by noting the judge assigned to the case, U.S. District Judge Michael Nachmanoff, was appointed to the bench by President Joe Biden.

“He is a Dirty Cop, and always has been, but he was just assigned a Crooked Joe Biden appointed Judge, so he’s off to a very good start. Nevertheless, words are words, and he wasn’t hedging or in dispute. He was very positive, there was no doubt in his mind about what he said, or meant by saying it. He left himself ZERO margin of error on a big and important answer to a question. He just got unexpectedly caught,” he wrote.

He then added, “’James ‘Dirty Cop’ Comey was a destroyer of lives. He knew exactly what he was saying, and that it was a very serious and far reaching lie for which a very big price must be paid! President DJT.”

Moments later, he posted again, writing, “JAMES COMEY IS A DIRTY COP. MAKE AMERICA GREAT AGAIN!”

Our Analysis:

The Theatre of Political Vendetta: Trump’s Tirade Against Comey

Donald Trump, never one to shy away from the spotlight or controversy, has once again unleashed a barrage of attacks - this time targeting James Comey, the former FBI Director. The spectacle unfolded on Trump’s favored battleground, Truth Social, where the ex-president holds court. His tirade was sparked by the recent indictment of Comey on two federal charges by U.S. Attorney Lindsey Halligan, a move Trump has eagerly championed.

The Charges: A Closer Look

The indictment of Comey is a significant event, warranting a thorough examination. However, Trump’s reaction to it is less about the legal implications and more about settling old scores. It’s crucial to underscore that the push for these charges comes after Trump’s vocal criticisms and demands on social media for legal action against Comey, despite a previous refusal by another U.S. attorney to proceed. This raises questions about the influence of political pressure on legal proceedings and the independence of the judiciary.

Trump’s Social Media Barrage: A Strategic Distraction?

Trump’s posts on Truth Social are a masterclass in distraction and manipulation. By attacking Comey, labeling him a “Dirty Cop,” and preemptively casting doubts on U.S. District Judge Michael Nachmanoff—appointed by Joe Biden—Trump is playing a familiar tune. This strategy serves two purposes: it rallies his base by vilifying a common adversary, and it sows seeds of doubt about the fairness of the judicial process. It’s a classic Trump move to deflect and project, turning the spotlight away from his own controversies.

The Role of Political Appointees

The appointment of Lindsey Halligan by Trump and the assignment of Judge Nachmanoff by Biden highlight the deeply political nature of these legal battles. Trump’s commentary underscores a worrying trend where individuals in the legal system are viewed through a partisan lens, potentially undermining trust in these institutions. The emphasis on the appointing figure, rather than the qualifications or integrity of the appointee, is a dangerous game that threatens the foundational principle of impartial justice.

The Bigger Picture: Democracy Under Fire

Trump’s narrative is more than just a personal vendetta; it’s symptomatic of a broader issue plaguing American politics. The relentless attacks on figures like Comey and the judiciary reflect a disturbing disregard for democratic norms and institutions. By framing Comey’s indictment as a victory and casting aspersions on the judicial process, Trump is not just attacking individuals; he’s undermining the very pillars of democracy.

Conclusion: A Call to Reason

As this saga unfolds, it’s essential to remain vigilant and critical of the motives behind such attacks. Trump’s tirade against Comey, while not unexpected, is a stark reminder of the power of rhetoric and its impact on public trust and democratic institutions. The focus should be on the facts of the case, the integrity of the legal process, and the preservation of democratic norms, rather than the spectacle of political vendettas.
