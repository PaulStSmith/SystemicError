Trump drops panicked attack on GOP lawmakers standing in way of his new power grab

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosDecember 7, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

U.S. President Donald Trump speaks to the media before boarding Marine One upon departure for New York, in Washington, D.C., U.S., September 11, 2025. REUTERS/Evelyn Hockstein

Donald Trump on Saturday posted a panicked plea aimed at fellow Republicans who are standing in the way of his latest power grab.

The president took to Truth Social over the weekend to target GOP lawmakers who might vote against his proposed plan to amend maps in Indiana to give Republicans extra seats. Trump one day earlier wrote, “Congratulations to Indiana’s tremendous Republican State Representatives... who just overwhelmingly voted to pass their new, much fairer, and improved, Congressional Map... this new Map would give the incredible people of Indiana the opportunity to elect TWO additional Republicans in the 2026 Midterm Elections.”

But Trump then took a change in his tone as he mentioned the next step for his power grab.

“The Indiana Senate must now pass this Map, AS IS, and get it to Governor Mike Braun’s desk, ASAP, to deliver a gigantic Victory for Republicans in the ‘Hoosier State,’ and across the Country. I am hearing that these nine Senators, some of whom are up for Re-Election in 2026, and some in 2028, need encouragement to make the right decision: Blake Doriot, Brett Clark, Brian Buchanan, Dan Dernulc, Ed Charbonneau, Greg Goode, Jim Buck, Rick Niemeyer, and Ryan Mishler,” he wrote. “Let your voice be heard loud and clear in support of these Senators doing the right thing. Thank you for your attention to this matter!”

A day after calling them out by name in a massive national pressure campaign, Trump took up the issue in Indiana again, writing, “Why would a REAL Republican vote against this when the Dems have been doing it for years???”

“If they stupidly say no, vote them out of Office - They are not worthy - And I will be there to help! Thank you Indiana!” the president wrote.

Our Analysis:

Trump’s Desperate Plea: A Blatant Power Grab Masquerading as Fair Play

Donald Trump’s recent social media frenzy serves as a glaring exhibit of his relentless pursuit to manipulate the political landscape to his advantage, a tactic as transparent as it is audacious. His call to action for the Indiana Senate to pass a controversial map—under the guise of fairness—unmasks a desperate attempt to cement Republican control by any means necessary.

The Irony of Fairness

Trump’s initial proclamation, hailing the proposed Congressional map in Indiana as “much fairer, and improved,” reeks of irony. Labeling a scheme designed to blatantly increase Republican seats as “fair” is a masterclass in political doublespeak. This isn’t about fairness; it’s about power. The audacity to claim that the manipulation of electoral boundaries—a practice known as gerrymandering—is an act of justice for the people of Indiana is both bold and falsehood-laden.

A Call to Arms for a Power Grab

Trump’s subsequent posts transition from self-congratulations to a thinly-veiled threat against those who might oppose this plan. By naming and shaming specific Indiana Senators, Trump isn’t just applying pressure; he’s wielding his influence as a weapon. This isn’t leadership; it’s intimidation. His message is clear: fall in line, or face the consequences. The implication that those who oppose this gerrymandering effort are somehow betraying Republican values is not just manipulative; it’s dangerously divisive.

The Hypocrisy of “Real Republicans”

Perhaps most egregious is Trump’s attempt to label dissenters as not “REAL Republicans,” suggesting that blind allegiance to party over principle is the yardstick by which one’s political purity should be measured. The hypocrisy of this statement cannot be overstated, especially coming from someone who has, on numerous occasions, deviated from traditional conservative values himself. The call to oust those who oppose this plan under the threat of being “not worthy” is a stark reminder of Trump’s transactional view of loyalty.

A False Equivalence

Trump’s rationale—that Democrats have engaged in similar practices, and thus, Republicans are justified in doing the same—embodies a false equivalence that undermines the very foundations of democratic integrity. The “they did it first” argument is a childish justification for unethical behavior, not a legitimate defense. It’s a distraction, a smokescreen meant to shift focus away from the real issue: the systematic manipulation of electoral boundaries for partisan gain.

Conclusion: A Democracy at Risk

Donald Trump’s latest tirade is more than just a plea for support; it’s a testament to his willingness to undermine democratic principles for personal and party gain. This isn’t just about Indiana; it’s a microcosm of a larger, more sinister strategy to erode the fabric of American democracy. The insistence on loyalty over integrity, the manipulation of electoral processes, and the blatant power grabs are signs of a democracy in distress.

It’s imperative that we see through these tactics for what they are: desperate attempts by a man and a movement to cling to power at any cost. The citizens of Indiana—and indeed, all Americans—deserve better than to be pawns in this dangerous game of political chess. Our democracy should not be for sale to the highest bidder or the loudest voice.
