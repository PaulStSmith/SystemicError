$30 Million for Insurrecting While White: The Blood Price of Hypocrisy

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosMay 6, 20252 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

AI generated illustration

So the Department of Justice—under Trump 2.0—wants to hand the family of Ashli Babbitt $30 million in taxpayer money. Not for her service. Not for any miscarriage of justice. But for dying while committing a violent coup attempt against the U.S. government. That’s not justice—that’s a state-sponsored reward for domestic terrorism, provided you meet the complexion requirements. It's a grotesque inversion of morality: insurrection gets a payout, while obedience gets a bullet.

Meanwhile, names like Tamir Rice, Breonna Taylor, Eric Garner, Aiyana Jones, Philando Castile—they echo in the graveyard of American conscience. Shot, strangled, suffocated, and erased by the state they never even attacked. What did their families get? Demonized. Gaslit. Told they "should’ve complied." Told to grieve in silence while their loved ones were painted as criminals posthumously. Settlements—when they exist—are wrung from civil lawsuits against municipalities who offer hush money without admitting fault, and only after years of grueling court battles.

But Babbitt? She wasn’t complying. She wasn’t peaceful. She was breaking into the Speaker’s Lobby during a violent insurrection. She wasn't some confused bystander. She was radicalized, dangerous, and in the middle of an attempted coup, egged on by lies, grievance, and a cult of personality that traded the Constitution for a red cap. Her death wasn’t the result of overreach or accident—it was the predictable, necessary consequence of trying to overthrow a democratic government by force.

And now she’s a martyr with a $30 million price tag—because the regime wants to turn a traitor into a saint. This is a deliberate political maneuver. It’s a signal flare to the militant fringe that white violence will not only be forgiven—it will be funded. That if you’re white and rageful enough, the government will write your family a check with blood on it.

Let’s be crystal clear: this isn’t the system “failing.” This is the system working exactly as designed—to protect whiteness at all costs, to canonize rebellion when it's cloaked in privilege, and to vilify obedience when it's worn in Black skin.

This isn’t about law and order. It never was. It’s about who the law was written to protect, and who it was designed to crush. It’s about the selective sanctity of life. It's about how one group gets civil settlements and teary tributes, while another gets body bags and hashtags. It’s about white grievance being weaponized into policy, into payout, into political theatre where martyrdom is bought and truth is gagged.

$30 million for insurrecting while white.
Zero for dying while Black and breathing.

If that doesn’t light a fire under the myth of American justice, nothing will. And if we don’t keep that fire burning—fanned by truth, outrage, and relentless resistance—it will be smothered under the weight of state-sponsored revisionism. They will rewrite history to make the traitors look like patriots and the victims look like threats. Our job is to make damn sure they fail.

Because the blood isn’t dry. And the ledger is nowhere near balanced.
