McGraw made the remarks in an interview with The New York Times that was published on Sunday.

"I don't think I'm qualified to talk about politics," he said despite becoming a MAGA mouthpiece in recent months.

After a lucrative career as a TV host, the once-licensed psychologist has baffled friends and acquaintances with his new path.

"Still an advocate, he says, for legal immigration and gay rights, Dr. Phil believes he has nonetheless lost friends from his bygone life in Beverly Hills, Calif., where he keeps a home," the paper noted. "He has failed publicly as never before, after a long talk-show reign at No. 1, for the high privilege of clawing into the living algorithm of ubiquitous news chatter."

"I don't have to be doing this at all," the TV personality quipped. "This is not a got-to job. This is a get-to job."

Over the weekend, HBO host Bill Maher confronted McGraw for partnering with the Trump administration.

"Why are you going on these ICE raids? I don't understand that," Maher said. "You're a guy who, for so many years, has been working to put families together, to bring families who are apart and heal them. And now you're going on raids with people who are literally separating families. Explain that."

"Now, that's bulls—!" McGraw exclaimed. "If you arrest somebody that's a citizen that has committed a crime, or is DUI with the child in the backseat, do you think they don't separate that family right then, right there?"

Our Analysis:

The Paradox of Dr. Phil McGraw: A Commentary

Dr. Phil McGraw, a figure who once nestled comfortably in the heart of American daytime television, has seemingly ventured into territories far beyond his proclaimed expertise—or lack thereof. His recent assertion in The New York Times that he is "not qualified" to discuss politics strikes as a bizarre, if not hypocritical, statement, especially considering his actions that scream otherwise. Let's dissect this utterance and the implications of his recent endeavors, which starkly contrast with his claims of political naivety.

The Inconsistency: Political Actions vs. Political Words

Dr. Phil's involvement in an Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) raid alongside the Trump administration is a glaring contradiction to his self-professed unqualification to discuss politics. This act alone, far from being apolitical, is steeped in the contentious politics of immigration. It is an overt alignment with policies that have been widely criticized for their humanitarian implications. Yet, McGraw's participation in such a politically charged event starkly undermines his subsequent claim of political ignorance.

MAGA Mouthpiece or Misunderstood Advocate?

The article describes Dr. Phil as having become a "MAGA mouthpiece" in recent months, an assertion that paints a vivid picture of his political engagements. Yet, he maintains that he is an advocate for legal immigration and gay rights. This duality is perplexing. How does one reconcile participating in ICE raids, actions that are intrinsically linked to family separations, with advocacy for family unity and rights? This contradiction is not just confusing; it is deeply troubling.

The Critique from Bill Maher: A Reflection of Public Sentiment

Bill Maher's confrontation of Dr. Phil provides a necessary critique. Maher's questioning of McGraw's participation in ICE raids, considering his previous work on family reunification, highlights a jarring inconsistency in McGraw's public persona and actions. Dr. Phil's defensive response, comparing ICE actions to the separation of families during DUI arrests, is a false equivalence. The systemic separation of families at the border, often for seeking asylum (a legal right), cannot be simplistically equated to criminal arrests. This comparison is not only flawed but dangerously misleading.

The Luxury of Choice

Dr. Phil's remark, "This is not a got-to job. This is a get-to job," reveals an unsettling privilege. The choice to engage in political actions, while claiming apolitical stances, is a luxury many do not possess. For the individuals and families affected by ICE raids, there's no "get-to" option. They are thrust into these situations without choice, often resulting from desperate circumstances.

Conclusion: A Call for Accountability

Dr. Phil McGraw's foray into the political arena, masked by a veneer of disqualification, is a disservice to the public discourse. The actions of public figures, especially those with significant platforms, must align with their stated beliefs and values. When these figures engage in activities that have profound political implications, they should be prepared to face scrutiny and be held accountable for their actions.

In essence, Dr. Phil's recent endeavors and statements present a cautionary tale of the complexities and responsibilities that come with public influence. It's not enough to claim ignorance or unqualification; actions speak louder than words, and in the political realm, those actions can have far-reaching consequences.
