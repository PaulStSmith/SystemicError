Republican admits election-rigging plan aims to preserve ‘Christian conservative majority'

A top Missouri GOP state lawmaker says her Republican governor is actively looking into redistricting the state. She offered as his motivation a desire to preserve its “Christian conservative majority.”

Gov. Mike Kehoe “will always consider options that provide congressional districts that best represent Missourians,” his spokesperson, Gabby Picard, said, according to Bloomberg News.

A redistricting plan, spurred by President Donald Trump’s directive to Texas that he wants an additional five Republican seats in the U.S. House of Representatives, would be enacted through a special session of the Missouri state legislature, should Governor Kehoe decide to call lawmakers in.

But Missouri’s Republican Senate president pro tem, State Senator Cindy O’Laughlin (photo), attributed a somewhat different motivation to the Governor.

“He wants to be sure Missouri’s representation matches Missouri’s Christian conservative majority,” O’Laughlin said, according to Bloomberg.

Politico on Wednesday noted that Republicans already control six of the state’s eight House seats. Redistricting the Show Me State would reportedly eliminate Democratic U.S. Rep. Emanuel Cleaver’s seat. Cleaver, a former Chair of the Congressional Black Caucus, has served in the House of Representatives since 2005.

Kehoe spoke about redistricting with a local Fox affiliate this week.

“Is Missouri represented properly in Washington, D.C., and, quite frankly, what can we do to support President Trump’s agenda?” he said. “And Mike Johnson, the Speaker of the House, who’s done a very good job.”

Fox 2 reported that the Trump White House reportedly has “pressured” Missouri Republicans to redraw their state’s maps.

O’Laughlin told KSSZ-93.9 that conversations often focus on “how do we stop progressives from coming into our state and naming a person and then pouring the money into our state to try to pass something.”

She added that seeing what “happens when Democrats are in control at the federal level” is “frightening.”

Her Senate bio says that prior to “being elected to the Missouri Senate, Sen. O’Laughlin served as a school bus driver, an administrator at a local Christian school and as a member of her local school board.”

Our Analysis:

Missouri's Redistricting Ruse: A Masterclass in Partisan Gerrymandering

In what can only be described as a bold move to further entrench partisan politics at the expense of democratic representation, Missouri's GOP leadership, under the guise of preserving a "Christian conservative majority," is actively exploring redistricting strategies. This maneuver, spearheaded by Governor Mike Kehoe and backed by a top state lawmaker, reveals not just a quest to solidify Republican control but a disturbing willingness to warp democratic processes to ideological ends.

Governor Kehoe's Motivations: A Cloak of Representation

Governor Kehoe's office, with a nod to ambiguity, states he will always consider options that "best represent Missourians." Yet, the underlying intention, as revealed by State Senator Cindy O’Laughlin, is unabashedly targeted at ensuring Missouri’s representation mirrors its "Christian conservative majority." This rationale not only blatantly disregards the state's diverse populace but also suggests a troubling conflation of political representation with religious and ideological homogeneity.

The Trump Directive: A Catalyst for Gerrymandering

Inspired by Donald Trump's directive for additional Republican seats in the U.S. House of Representatives, Missouri's contemplated redistricting plan is not a standalone act but part of a broader, more insidious strategy to manipulate electoral boundaries for partisan gain. By potentially eliminating Democratic U.S. Rep. Emanuel Cleaver’s seat, a move that would disproportionately affect a voice representing the Congressional Black Caucus, the plan starkly illustrates how gerrymandering undermines minority representation and the principles of fair competition in a democracy.

The Frightening Rhetoric of Control

Senator O’Laughlin's remarks to KSSZ-93.9 shed light on a deeper, more disturbing aspect of this endeavor. The framing of the issue as a battle against progressives "pouring money" into the state to enact change is a classic example of fearmongering. Furthermore, her assertion that witnessing Democrats in control at the federal level is "frightening" not only vilifies opposition but also promotes a divisive and alarmist narrative that undermines the very fabric of bipartisan cooperation and democratic engagement.

A Legacy of Political Manipulation

Before her tenure in the Senate, O’Laughlin's roles ranged from a school bus driver to a school administrator and local school board member. This diverse background, while commendable for its service to the community, does not insulate her current actions from criticism. The leap from local educational roles to spearheading efforts that could significantly distort democratic representation is both jarring and indicative of how deeply partisan objectives can influence individuals once dedicated to community service.

In Conclusion: A Call for Vigilance

The proposed redistricting efforts in Missouri, under the guise of representing a "Christian conservative majority," are a stark reminder of the lengths to which political actors will go to entrench power. This endeavor, far from being an isolated case, is symptomatic of a larger, more systemic issue plaguing American democracy: the manipulation of electoral boundaries for partisan gain. As observers, critics, and citizens, the response must be one of vigilance and advocacy for fair representation, lest we allow the foundational principles of our democracy to be eroded by the whims of those in power.
