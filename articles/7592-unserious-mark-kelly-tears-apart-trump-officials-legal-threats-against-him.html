Sen. Mark Kelly (D-AZ) publicly refused to back down on CNN amid the Trump administration’s legal threats against him for his joint video with ex-military Democrats reminding active servicemembers they must refuse illegal orders.

This comes after Kelly’s legal team sent a warning to the Secretary of the Navy that they would pursue all avenues to stop an “unconstitutional and extraordinary abuse of power,” like recalling him to active duty to court-martial him.

“I want to ask you, Punchbowl News is reporting that the Defense Department is now escalating its review of your participation in that video ... the Pentagon is now calling this a, quote, ‘official command investigation,’” said anchor Anderson Cooper. “I know last time you and I spoke, you said you’d heard nothing about this investigation beyond a social media post from Secretary Hegseth. What have you heard now? What’s your response to the reporting?”

“Well, first of all, Anderson, I haven’t heard anything. Still nothing,” said Kelly. “And this time to Pentagon correspondents, I guess it’s Matt Gaetz and the pizzagate conspiracy guy put something on social media. That’s how we were notified. That’s how unserious they are. That shows like what this is all about. This is about attention.”

“And they call it a command investigation,” Kelly continued. “Anderson, I spent 25 years in the United States Navy. I was in many commands. I retired from the Navy 14 years ago. I’m not in any command now. I don’t know what they’re talking about.”

“At some point, I imagine they’ll probably notify me of something,” he added. “I’ll be with the Secretary of Defense tomorrow in a briefing ... you know, maybe at that point he’s going to come up to me and say something officially, because I haven’t heard anything officially from DOD.”

Our Analysis:

The Theater of the Absurd: The Pentagon’s “Command Investigation” into Sen. Mark Kelly

In a recent development that seems ripped straight from the pages of a Kafkaesque novel, Sen. Mark Kelly (D-AZ) finds himself at the center of what can only be described as a political farce masquerading as a “command investigation” by the Pentagon. The absurdity of this situation is palpable, and it underscores a disturbing trend of using military authority as a tool for political retaliation.

The Basis of the “Investigation”

Let’s dissect the origins of this so-called investigation. Sen. Kelly, alongside other ex-military Democrats, participated in a video reminding active servicemembers of their duty to refuse illegal orders. This is not only a constitutional safeguard but a moral imperative within the military ethos. In response, Kelly’s legal team preemptively warned against an “unconstitutional and extraordinary abuse of power,” including the ludicrous notion of recalling Kelly to active duty to court-martial him.

The Pentagon’s Response: A Farce Unfolds

According to CNN’s Anderson Cooper, Punchbowl News reported that the Defense Department is escalating its review into Kelly’s participation in the video. The Pentagon, in what appears to be a dramatic overreach, is calling this an “official command investigation.” However, as Kelly astutely points out, the notification of such an investigation came not through official channels but via a social media post from individuals like Secretary Hegseth, Matt Gaetz, and a figure associated with the debunked “pizzagate” conspiracy. This method of communication is not just unprofessional; it’s downright farcical.

Kelly’s Unassailable Logic

Sen. Kelly’s response to this situation is a masterclass in rational thought under absurd circumstances. With 25 years of service in the United States Navy and having retired 14 years ago, Kelly is no longer under any command that the Pentagon could feasibly investigate in this context. His confusion is not just justified; it’s the only reasonable reaction to such a bizarre claim.

Moreover, Kelly’s expectation of possibly being notified about this investigation during a briefing with the Secretary of Defense only adds another layer of absurdity to the mix. It’s as if the Pentagon is playing a game of bureaucratic telephone, where messages are lost, and the outcome is a bewildering mess of miscommunication and misdirection.

Conclusion: The Real “Unserious” Actors

Sen. Mark Kelly’s ordeal highlights a dangerous trend where military protocol and authority are weaponized for political vendettas. The fact that Kelly has to address such a nonsensical issue is a distraction from the serious business of governing and a tarnish on the integrity of the institutions involved.

The real “unserious” actors here are those who initiated this farcical investigation. Their actions are not just an affront to Kelly but to the principles of justice and due process. In their quest for attention and political retribution, they have embarked on a path that undermines the very foundations of our democratic and military institutions.

In a sane world, this investigation would be dismissed as quickly as it was concocted. But in the current political climate, where absurdity often trumps reason, one can only hope that clearer heads will prevail, and this investigation will be seen for what it truly is: a baseless, politically motivated witch hunt.
