Nancy Mace’s lead campaign consultant resigns after she 'turned her back on MAGA'

J. Austin McCubbin, a member of Rep. Nancy Mace’s (R-SC), gubernatorial campaign team, has resigned in a post on X, saying she has “turned her back on MAGA.”

“Nancy Mace has yet again decided to turn her back on MAGA to hug the political cactus that is the Rand Paul + Thomas Massie wing of the Party. I have notified her and her team that I am resigning because of this,” McCubbin writes.

McCubbin, who has been involved in politics for over three decades, starting in 1994, serving as the Trump South Carolina State Director for the 2024 campaign and as a campaign manager and fundraising consultant for various high-profile Republican figures, including Representative Andy Barr and Senator Rand Paul, signed on as lead consultant for Mace’s gubernatorial campaign in August.

“My name has been used publicly, while going back on her word to pay me, to trade on my Team Trump status and to work on her behalf with the White House, and I am 100% breaking with her campaign out of loyalty to the President,” he writes.

Mace has had a fluctuating and complex relationship with President Donald Trump, most recently defying pressure from him to stop her push for the release of all files related to deceased convicted sex trafficker Jeffrey Epstein.

McCubbin says it goes much deeper than that, writing on X, “The 2026 gubernatorial race has always been first and foremost a run for the president’s endorsement. South Carolina is Trump Country. Earlier this year Nancy parted ways with her General Consultant, because he was openly criticizing the President’s Iran strike and siding with Thomas Massie.”

Massie led a successful bipartisan effort in the U.S. House of Representatives to pass the Epstein Files Transparency Act, the law that requires the Department of Justice (DOJ) to release the Epstein files.

McCubbin also points to a PAC that has supported Mace as another reason for his resignation.

“Last month the [Kentucky Republican Senator] Rand Paul-affiliated Protect Freedom PAC began spending money on Nancy’s behalf. I had no prior knowledge of that, and campaigns can’t control outside spending. I chalked it up to nothing but independent activity,” he explains.

“When I talked to Nancy last Wednesday, it became clear she has fully embraced the Rand Paul PAC. She told me she is directing a personal friend to fund a 7-figure check to Rand’s PAC. She distanced herself, and now she’s fully embracing the Rand Paul + Thomas Massie wing for money,” he adds.

“Nancy Mace is wittingly or unwittingly a proxy for Rand Paul’s 2028 presidential campaign. That’s the straw that broke the camel’s back,” McCubbin explains.

Saying he tried to warn Mace about the repercussions associated with this connection, McCubbin says she refused.

“She wouldn’t hear it when I warned her about the political risks and offered alternative solutions. She accused me of mansplaining, a woke term used by people who hate men, who ironically are her best demographic. That dog don’t hunt,” he adds.

“Anyone who knows me knows that I have nothing personally against Rand or Nancy. This is about loyalty. My loyalty lies with President Trump, my colleagues I worked with in the campaign trenches and the MAGA base that I had the privilege to work with as the Team Trump SC State Director,” he says.

McCubbin concluded saying Trump should move away from Mace.

“Governor McMaster has been a great governor. He has been very loyal to the President. South Carolina needs someone cut from the same cloth, where you know their word is their bond. My advice to the President, my friends in the White House, and South Carolina Trump voters: scratch her name from the list. As we say in South Carolina, there’s no lesson in the second kick of a mule,” he concluded.

Analysis: The Resignation of J. Austin McCubbin and the Intricacies of GOP Internal Politics

In a dramatic flourish on the social media platform X, J. Austin McCubbin, a seasoned political operative with deep ties to the MAGA movement, publicly resigned from Rep. Nancy Mace’s (R-SC) gubernatorial campaign. His departure isn’t just a blip on the political radar; it’s a seismic wave that exposes the fractious and splintered landscape of the Republican Party. Let’s dissect the layers of this political drama, shall we?

The MAGA vs. Rand Paul + Thomas Massie Dichotomy

McCubbin accuses Mace of betraying the MAGA camp for the “political cactus” that is the Rand Paul and Thomas Massie wing of the party. This is not just a resignation; it’s an indictment of Mace’s political maneuvering and perceived disloyalty to the MAGA cause. The reference to a “political cactus” is both vivid and telling, suggesting that aligning with Paul and Massie is not only uncomfortable but downright hazardous.

The Alleged Betrayal

McCubbin’s grievances extend beyond ideological differences. He claims that Mace reneged on financial agreements and exploited his “Team Trump status” for her benefit, further alleging that her gubernatorial race was a mere stepping stone for securing Trump’s endorsement—a prized asset in “Trump Country.”

The Rand Paul Connection

The involvement of the Rand Paul-affiliated Protect Freedom PAC in Mace’s campaign is presented as a cardinal sin in McCubbin’s eyes. His revelation that Mace directed significant funding towards this PAC cements his view of her as a political apostate, embracing what he perceives as anathema to the MAGA movement.

Accusations of “Mansplaining” and Woke Culture

In a particularly charged segment of his resignation, McCubbin lambasts Mace for accusing him of “mansplaining,” which he dismisses as a term favored by those who “hate men.” This accusation not only trivializes legitimate concerns about gender dynamics in professional settings but also signals a disdain for so-called “woke” culture, further aligning McCubbin with the anti-PC (politically correct) stance often associated with Trump’s base.

Loyalty Above All

At its core, McCubbin’s departure underscores a fundamental principle that he holds dear: loyalty to Trump. His narrative is steeped in notions of allegiance, portraying himself as a stalwart defender of Trumpian values against the perceived onslaught of betrayal and political opportunism.

Conclusion: The Wider Implications

McCubbin’s resignation is emblematic of the wider schisms within the Republican Party. It’s not just about personal grievances or ideological purity; it’s a stark illustration of the ongoing battle for the soul of the GOP. As the party grapples with its identity in the post-Trump era, figures like McCubbin serve as reminders of the deep currents of loyalty and dissent that continue to shape its future.

Governor McMaster’s endorsement as a figure of continuity, “cut from the same cloth” as Trump, suggests a yearning for a return to what McCubbin views as the halcyon days of undivided loyalty to Trumpism. The advice to “scratch her name from the list” is a stark warning to those within the GOP who dare to deviate from this orthodoxy.

In essence, this saga is not just about a political resignation; it’s a microcosm of the existential struggle within the GOP, between adherence to Trump’s legacy and the exploration of new political frontiers. McCubbin’s scathing departure paints a vivid picture of this tumultuous landscape, where loyalty is the coin of the realm, and deviations are met with swift retribution.
