His new jeopardy is only one facet of the unfolding national disaster instigated by his own actions in October 2016. In those days before a presidential election, he made a fateful decision to disclose a renewed FBI probe of Democratic nominee Hillary Clinton and “her emails” (which ultimately proved to contain no classified information, as the Trump administration officially acknowledged many months later).

It was a choice that violated legal ethics and Justice Department rules, and has permanently damaged the institutions of law he claimed to be protecting.

Yet however dismal Comey’s own conduct may have been, and however culpable he remains in the rise of Donald Trump, the Justice Department’s fraudulent attempt to jail him on direct orders from the president is an historic assault on the liberty of all Americans and must be resisted as such. Although Comey isn’t the first victim of Trump’s drive for authoritarian power and won’t be the last, his case represents a stark departure from American standards of justice and an unmistakable step toward tyranny.

Trump warned the country many times that he would abuse presidential power for “retribution” against his adversaries and critics, and — unlike his admired predecessor Richard Nixon — he made no effort to conceal what he is doing to get Comey and others. When Erik Siebert, the Trump-appointed U.S. attorney in the Eastern District of Virginia refused to prosecute Comey, the president forced him to resign.

Trump instantly replaced Siebert with Lindsey Halligan, a pliant White House attorney with no relevant qualifications for the job. She does display the abject subservience and ideological extremism required by her boss. Within days of her appointment, and just before the statute of limitations expired, Halligan delivered the two-page indictment of Comey.

In that tissue-thin bill of particulars, the Trump Justice Department charges Comey with lying to a Senate committee about a press leak from the FBI’s top echelons. Although the indictment cites no evidence whatsoever, its lynchpin appears to be an alleged contradiction between Comey’s sworn testimony that he never “authorized” such a leak, and the testimony of his former deputy Andrew McCabe that he did. But as several experts have noted, there may be no conflict between their narratives of that incident.

Among the underlying ironies is that McCabe’s 2016 leak to The Wall Street Journal involved an investigation of the Clinton Foundation, which came to nothing as such probes inevitably do. His aim was to dispel rumors, spread by conservative FBI agents seeking to sabotage the Clinton campaign, that the FBI had buried the foundation probe for political reasons.

Subsequent investigations forced McCabe to admit responsibility for that leak, which violated FBI and Justice Department rules, especially in the months before an election. Those extensive probes — by Justice Department Inspector General Michael Horowitz and later by Trump’s own Russia special counsel, John Durham — both found no basis to charge Comey or McCabe with any crime, while casting doubt on McCabe’s credibility. Horowitz and Durham had plenty of criticisms of the former FBI executives, but then again so do I.

Under those circumstances — with all the glaring proof of Trump’s unlawful meddling — the chances that Comey will be convicted, or even go to trial, seem small unless the courts abandon legality and abdicate to fascist rule. Even if the indictment is vacated, this rogue president will have inflicted severe costs not only on his “enemy” but on the country whose Constitution he falsely swore to uphold.

Our Analysis:

The Fraught Case Against James Comey: A Dive into Irony and Injustice

In a move that reeks of political vendettas and a disturbing departure from legal norms, the indictment of James Comey by a federal grand jury in Virginia stands as a testament to the degradation of justice under the current administration. The layers of irony enveloping this case are as thick as they are bitter, painting a complex picture of Comey’s journey from a controversial figure to a target of a politicized prosecution that could only be deemed as fraudulent.

Comey’s Dubious Legacy and the 2016 Election Fiasco

It’s crucial to acknowledge the backdrop against which this indictment occurs. Comey, the former FBI director, made a decision in October 2016 that would forever mar his career and arguably alter the course of American history. By disclosing a renewed FBI investigation into Hillary Clinton’s emails — which, as it turned out, contained no classified information — Comey breached legal ethics and Department of Justice rules. This act, which Comey claimed was in defense of the law’s institutions, did more to undermine them than protect. The irony that Comey, once accused of damaging the very fabric of justice, now finds himself ensnared in a baseless legal battle cannot be overstated.

A Malignant Indictment and the Assault on Liberty

However, acknowledging Comey’s flawed actions does not excuse the historic assault on justice that is his indictment. Directed by a president notorious for his threats of retribution against adversaries, this move marks a chilling step towards authoritarianism. Trump’s disdain for the rule of law is hardly a secret, yet the brazenness with which he seeks to punish Comey through a pliant and unqualified appointee, Lindsey Halligan, is unprecedented.

The indictment itself, based on an alleged contradiction in testimonies regarding an FBI leak, is so devoid of evidence that it borders on the absurd. It highlights not Comey’s guilt but the lengths to which Trump’s administration will go to weaponize the justice system against perceived enemies. The fact that experts and subsequent investigations have found no basis to charge Comey only adds to the indictment’s lack of legitimacy.

The Broader Implications: A Step Toward Tyranny

Trump’s actions against Comey are not merely an isolated vendetta but a symptom of a much larger disease infecting American democracy. By targeting Comey, Trump signals his willingness to subvert justice for personal gains, pushing the country towards a tyranny that it has long prided itself on opposing. The case against Comey, thin as it is, serves as a stark warning: the erosion of legal norms and the politicization of indictments pose grave threats to the liberty of all Americans.

The Mockery of Justice and the Cost to the Nation

Even if this indictment is eventually vacated, the damage inflicted by this rogue president on his “enemy” and the nation at large is profound. The cost of such a brazen assault on the Constitution and the principles of justice cannot be overstated. It’s a cost measured not just in the personal plight of James Comey but in the erosion of trust in American institutions and the principles they are supposed to uphold.

In essence, the indictment of James Comey is not just a legal travesty but a glaring symptom of a justice system being manipulated for political retribution. It’s a case that should concern not just the critics of Comey or Trump but anyone who believes in the rule of law and the importance of safeguarding the institutions that protect our liberty. As we navigate these tumultuous waters, it’s imperative to remember that today’s target may be Comey, but tomorrow’s could be anyone deemed an adversary by those in power. The fight against this kind of tyranny is not just Comey’s but ours as a nation committed to justice and democracy.
