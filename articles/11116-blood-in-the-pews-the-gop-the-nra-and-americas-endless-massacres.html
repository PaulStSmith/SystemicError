Blood in the Pews: The GOP, the NRA, and America’s Endless Massacres

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosAugust 28, 20255 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

A Nation Held Hostage by Gun FetishistsThe NRA’s Bloody TithesThe Anatomy of CowardiceThe Cost of InactionA Call for AccountabilityNo More Silence

On August 27, 2025, in Minneapolis, Minnesota, two children were murdered in a Catholic church during morning Mass. An eight-year-old and a ten-year-old, shot dead where they sat in the pews. Seventeen others were wounded, fourteen of them children. Elderly parishioners were hit. The shooter, 23-year-old Robin Westman, brought a rifle, a shotgun, and a pistol into the house of God and turned the sanctuary into a slaughterhouse before killing himself. The FBI is investigating it as both a hate crime and an act of domestic terrorism.

The details are harrowing. The facts are clear. But the deeper sickness runs through the veins of American politics, where the Republican Party has sold its soul to the National Rifle Association and its gun-manufacturer overlords. And so the ritual repeats: innocent children murdered, politicians flooding the airwaves with hollow condolences, and then—the deafening silence of inaction.

A Nation Held Hostage by Gun Fetishists

The United States is the only country in the world where this happens with such regularity. Schools, churches, synagogues, mosques, shopping malls, theaters, parades—every space where people gather has become a potential killing field. And yet, instead of mobilizing to end the carnage, Republican lawmakers kneel at the altar of the NRA, prostituting themselves for campaign donations while families bury their children.

After every mass shooting, the GOP follows the same script. They offer “thoughts and prayers” with a crocodile tear, then quickly pivot to the well-worn excuse: “Now is not the time to talk about gun reform, emotions are high.” The truth is, there is never a “good time” for them to talk about reform, because talking about reform means confronting their addiction to NRA money. It means confronting the deadly ideology of gun fetishism they’ve stoked for decades. It means admitting that their cowardice has turned schools and churches into free-fire zones.

The NRA’s Bloody Tithes

The NRA is not merely a “gun rights” organization. It is the political arm of the firearms industry, funneling millions into Republican campaigns to ensure that nothing—no massacre, no pile of child-sized coffins, no grieving community—will ever move Congress to act. The GOP doesn’t serve the American people; it serves its corporate masters in the arms trade. For every dead child, there’s profit in another AR-15 sold. For every mass shooting, there’s a surge in ammunition sales. For every massacre, there’s a fundraising email warning that “the government is coming for your guns.”

This is blood money. And Republicans cash the checks with hands still wet from the violence their inaction enables.

The Anatomy of Cowardice

What makes the GOP’s cowardice so obscene is not just their refusal to act, but their cynical attempts to deflect blame. Some will claim the problem is “mental health” while voting down funding for mental health services. Others will claim “violent video games” are to blame, as if Fortnite pulls a trigger. Still others will double down on their fascist fantasies, suggesting the solution to school shootings is to arm teachers—turning classrooms into battlegrounds rather than places of learning.

But the world knows the truth. The UK doesn’t have Fortnite bans, and it doesn’t have school shootings. Japan doesn’t allow mass civilian armories of AR-15s, and it doesn’t have churches turned into killing fields. Germany has mental health crises, but it doesn’t bury schoolchildren by the dozens. The difference is simple: only the United States allows unfettered access to weapons of war, defended by politicians too cowardly and corrupt to lift a finger against the NRA’s stranglehold.

The Cost of Inaction

Two children are dead in Minneapolis. Their names should be remembered, their faces should haunt every Republican Senator and Representative who lines their pockets with NRA cash. But they won’t. Because this is not the first massacre, and under GOP rule, it won’t be the last.

Columbine. Sandy Hook. Parkland. Uvalde. Now Minneapolis. Each name a scar on the national psyche. Each tragedy a preventable atrocity. Each time, Republicans have offered nothing but thoughts, prayers, and lies. Each time, they have chosen power and profit over lives.

This is not “freedom.” This is bondage—to a death cult that worships firearms and feeds its addiction with the bodies of children. This is not democracy. This is a nation held hostage by gun manufacturers, their lobbyists, and the Republican cowards who sell themselves to them.

A Call for Accountability

The GOP will pretend this shooting is an aberration, the act of a lone deranged man. It is not. It is the logical consequence of decades of Republican policies that have ensured guns are easier to buy than healthcare, that background checks remain riddled with loopholes, that weapons of war circulate freely in civilian hands. This shooting is not an accident. It is policy. It is the intended result of deliberate obstruction.

If America ever hopes to break this cycle, it must confront the Republican Party for what it has become: the political arm of the gun lobby. The blood of Minneapolis’s children is not just on the hands of one man with a gun. It is on every cowardly Republican who takes NRA money. It is on every senator who filibusters background checks. It is on every congressman who parrots the “now is not the time” lie. It is on the entire GOP, from the state legislatures writing “permitless carry” laws to the presidential candidates vowing loyalty to the Second Amendment above human life.

No More Silence

We cannot allow this ritual to repeat. We cannot let children’s funerals fade into statistics. We cannot let Republicans get away with laundering their complicity through empty platitudes. Every time a Republican mouths the words “thoughts and prayers,” we must remind them of the blood they’ve enabled. Every time they say “emotions are high,” we must answer: emotions should be high, because children are being murdered while you do nothing.

The Minneapolis Catholic school shooting is not just another tragedy. It is another indictment of a broken system, another moment when America must choose between the death cult of the GOP and the lives of its children.

And if the country does not choose life—if it does not finally break the NRA’s grip and hold Republicans accountable—then we know exactly what comes next. More vigils. More mass graves. More children slaughtered in pews, classrooms, and playgrounds. More “thoughts and prayers.” More lies. Until America itself becomes a graveyard of democracy, buried under the weight of cowardice and guns.

In memory of the children murdered in Minneapolis, and all the others whose lives were stolen by Republican cowardice and NRA corruption.
