Stunning confession proves Trump insiders know the reckoning is coming

Based on an article by Thom Hartmann

Trump Is Not Hiding the Theft

The Crime Is Advertised

The source lays out a simple fact: Trump and his people are not whispering about election manipulation, they are announcing it in advance. He has pushed mail-voting fraud lies, tried to seize state election power, and now his administration is exploring rules that would choke off ballot delivery unless states hand over voter data first. That is the context.

The point is not confusion. The point is preparation. You do not need a fake fraud panic this elaborate unless you are building public permission for suppression before the votes are even cast.

Power, Not Process

This story is about institutional power, not abstract election “integrity.” Trump is the decision-maker at the top. His administration is pushing executive action, Postal Service rules, DHS voter databases, and open talk of using ICE around polling places. Steve Bannon says the quiet part out loud. The White House refuses to rule it out.

That is the chain of command. The machinery is being assembled by men with power over federal agencies, law enforcement, and administrative rules. The targets are obvious: mail voters, Democratic states, and anyone who depends on ordinary access to the ballot.

ICE at the Polls Means Intimidation

If ICE is being floated as a presence near polling places, call it what it is: state intimidation. ICE should be abolished. It is not a neutral public service when deployed as a political threat against voters. It is a coercive arm of the state, and using it this way is an admission that the vote alone is not enough for these people.

The message is not subtle. If you can scare people away from voting, you can distort turnout without having to cancel an election outright. That is cleaner, cheaper, and more cowardly than a coup, which is exactly why authoritarian politics loves it.

The Legal Cover Is the Real Tell

Todd Blanche’s fear is revealing because it is not performative. He says people in this circle are worried about being investigated and indicted if they lose power. That is not paranoia; it is the emotional residue of people who know their conduct cannot survive scrutiny.

The same is true of the legal architecture around Trump. The article names lawyers, judges, and appointees who translate personal loyalty into state action. That is the whole mechanism: not just an authoritarian president, but a legal class willing to launder his impulses into paperwork, appointments, and supposedly lawful orders.

The Weak Link Is Also the Opening

The source is right to say the courts have not fully surrendered. Some of these moves have already been blocked, and a federal judge tossed out the Comey and Letitia James indictments after ruling the appointment behind them unlawful. That matters. So does the fact that Trump’s circle is afraid of what a future administration can uncover.

But that fear cuts both ways. It shows how much they still expect institutions to work if Democrats win enough power to use them. A Democratic House can subpoena, cut funding, and expose the hidden directives. That is not salvation; it is containment. In a system this degraded, containment is still serious power.

What This Pattern Reveals

The larger pattern is familiar: when the right cannot reliably win a fair contest, it does not abandon the contest. It rigs the terrain, floods the zone with fraud propaganda, weaponizes bureaucracy, and counts on legalistic cowards to call it procedure. That is what the mail rule, the voter-roll schemes, and the ICE threats are for.

This is not an argument about one election. It is a model of rule by administrative sabotage. They are trying to convert the state into a machine that decides who gets to vote, who gets watched, and who gets punished after the fact. The only reason they are doing it so openly is that they think they can get away with it.
