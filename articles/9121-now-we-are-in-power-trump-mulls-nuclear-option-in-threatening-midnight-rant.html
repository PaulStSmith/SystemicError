'Now we are in power': Trump mulls 'nuclear option' in threatening midnight rant

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosNovember 1, 20256 min read

Donald Trump (Photo via Reuters)

In a rambling late-night attack, Donald Trump first urged Republicans to use the “nuclear option” to end the government shutdown in a short Truth Social Post and then expanded upon it in a rambling screed where he declared, “We are in power.”

With polling showing that Republicans who control the White House and both chambers in Congress are being hit with the blame for the government shutdown that is two days away from being the longest in history, Trump wrote on Truth Social that it’s time for the GOP to ignore the Democrats and force the issue despite possible fallout.

The president started off by boasting, “I just got back from Asia where I met the Leaders of many Countries, including China, Japan, South Korea, Malaysia, Australia, Canada, New Zealand, Singapore, Thailand, Cambodia, Vietnam, and others. It was a Great Honor to meet them but, more particularly, to see that America is respected again — RESPECTED LIKE NEVER BEFORE! Great Trade Deals were made, Long Term relationships now exist, and money is pouring into our Country because of Tariffs and, frankly, the Landslide Results of the 2024 Presidential Election.”

But he then added, “The one question that kept coming up, however, was how did the Democrats SHUT DOWN the United States of America, and why did the powerful Republicans allow them to do it? The fact is, in flying back, I thought a great deal about that question, WHY?”

“Majority Leader John Thune, and Speaker of the House Mike Johnson, are doing a GREAT job, but the Democrats are Crazed Lunatics that have lost all sense of WISDOM and REALITY. It is a sick form of the now ‘legendary’ Trump Derangement Syndrome (TDS) that only comes from losing too much,” suggested Trump, who lost the 2020 presidential election to President Joe Biden.

He went on to falsely claim, “They want Trillions of Dollars to be taken from our Healthcare System and given to others, who are not deserving — People who have come into our Country illegally, many from prisons and mental institutions.”

“This will hurt American citizens, and Republicans will not let it happen. It is now time for the Republicans to play their ‘TRUMP CARD,’ and go for what is called the Nuclear Option — Get rid of the Filibuster, and get rid of it, NOW!” he insisted. “Just a short while ago, the Democrats, while in power, fought for three years to do this, but were unable to pull it off because of Senators Joe Manchin of West Virginia and Kyrsten Sinema of Arizona. Never have the Democrats fought so hard to do something because they knew the tremendous strength that terminating the Filibuster would give them.”

He added, “They want to substantially expand (PACK!) the United States Supreme Court, make Washington, D.C. and Puerto Rico States (Thereby automatically picking up 4 Senate seats, many House seats, and at least 8 Electoral Votes!), and many other highly destructive things.”

“Well, now WE are in power,” he threatened, “and if we did what we should be doing, it would IMMEDIATELY end this ridiculous, Country destroying ‘SHUT DOWN.’ If the Democrats ever came back into power, which would be made easier for them if the Republicans are not using the Great Strength and Policies made available to us by ending the Filibuster, the Democrats will exercise their rights, and it will be done in the first day they take office, regardless of whether or not we do it. In addition to all of the other things we would get, such as the best Judges, the best U.S. Attorneys, the best of everything, this was a concept from years ago of then President Barack Hussein Obama and former Majority Leader Harry Reid in order to take advantage of the Republicans.”

“Now I want to do it in order to take advantage of the Democrats….’ he tailed off.

Our Analysis:

The Illusion of Power: Trump’s Latest Tirade

In a characteristic late-night outburst, Donald Trump has once again taken to Truth Social to share his musings, which oscillate between self-congratulation and outright misinformation. His latest rant, ostensibly about the government shutdown, quickly devolves into a myriad of unrelated boasts and baseless accusations. Let’s dissect the key points of this rambling escapade and the underlying fallacies within.

The Misuse of “We Are in Power”

Trump’s declaration of “We are in power” amidst a government shutdown is not just ironic; it’s fundamentally misleading. The government shutdown, a direct consequence of legislative impasse, is hardly a testament to effective governance or political strength. It’s a sign of failure, an inability to govern efficiently or to reach across the aisle. Yet, Trump attempts to twist this narrative to serve as a bizarre flex of Republican dominance.

The Blame Game and the “Nuclear Option”

Trump’s suggestion for Republicans to use the “nuclear option” to end the government shutdown is a dangerous proposition. The filibuster, a critical tool for ensuring minority voices in the Senate are heard, is under threat. Trump’s call to “Get rid of the Filibuster, and get rid of it, NOW!” ignores the long-term implications of such a move, favoring short-term gains over the health of the American democratic process.

It’s worth noting the hypocrisy in blaming Democrats for the shutdown. Control of both the White House and Congress ostensibly places the responsibility squarely on the Republicans’ shoulders. Yet, in Trump’s narrative, the fault lies elsewhere. This deflection is a familiar tactic, aiming to shift public perception away from Republican accountability.

Unfounded Claims and Distracting Boasts

Trump’s claim that Democrats want “Trillions of Dollars to be taken from our Healthcare System and given to others, who are not deserving” is not just unfounded; it’s fearmongering. Such statements are designed to inflame and divide, without basis in policy proposals or factual debates. Furthermore, his detour into boasting about trade deals and respect from foreign leaders serves as a distraction, a smokescreen to divert attention from the issue at hand—the government shutdown.

The Irony of Trump’s Loss and Future Threats

Perhaps the most glaring irony in Trump’s post is his reference to the “legendary” Trump Derangement Syndrome (TDS), a term coined to describe his critics’ supposed irrational dislike of him. This comes from a president who lost the 2020 presidential election to Joe Biden, yet continues to wield significant influence over the Republican Party and its base.

Trump’s threats to “take advantage of the Democrats” by eliminating the filibuster and his baseless warnings about Democrats expanding the Supreme Court or granting statehood to Washington, D.C., and Puerto Rico are not just political strategies; they’re divisive tactics meant to rally his base through fear.

Conclusion: A Dangerous Game

Trump’s late-night tirade is more than just a rambling screed; it’s a dangerous game of political brinkmanship. By advocating for the “nuclear option,” spreading misinformation, and threatening to upend democratic norms, Trump is playing with the very fabric of American democracy. The real issue at hand—the government shutdown—is lost in a sea of self-aggrandizement and baseless attacks. This is not the mark of effective leadership or governance; it’s a symptom of a deeper malaise affecting American politics, where power is pursued at all costs, regardless of the consequences for the nation’s democratic institutions or its people.
