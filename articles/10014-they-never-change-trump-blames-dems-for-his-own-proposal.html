'They never change': Trump blames Dems for his own proposal

Trump has ordered House and Senate Republicans not to negotiate with Democrats on legislation to fund the federal government, keeping it open after funds run out at midnight on September 30. He canceled a meeting with Democratic House and Senate Minority Leaders Hakeem Jeffries and Chuck Schumer on ways to fund the government. And he has repeatedly made false and extreme accusations of what Democrats are asking for in exchange for their votes, calling them “unserious and ridiculous demands.”

The president earlier this week wrongly claimed that Democrats are “threatening” to shut down the government “unless they can have over $1 Trillion Dollars in new spending to continue free healthcare for Illegal Aliens.”

He also, wrongly, claimed Democrats want to “force Taxpayers to fund Transgender surgery for minors, have dead people on the Medicaid roles, allow Illegal Alien Criminals to steal Billions of Dollars in American Taxpayer Benefits, try to force our Country to again open our Borders to Criminals and to the World, allow men to play in women’s sports, and essentially create Transgender operations for everybody.”

On Thursday in the Oval Office, Trump was asked why he would fire or lay off federal workers, rather than furlough them, as is the norm during government shutdowns.

“Well, this is all caused by the Democrats,” was the President’s response. “They asked us to do something that’s totally unreasonable. They never change. They want to give money away to illegals, illegally, people that entered our country illegally. They want to give them massive federal money. And we don’t want to do that.”

Trump continued to talk about borders, declaring, “we have absolutely perfect borders, like you haven’t seen in many years,” Democrats, he insisted “want to open up the borders,” “this is what [Chuck] Schumer wants, this is what the Democrats want. They want to have, and they want to take our money,” he added, referring to tariffs.

Our Analysis:

Trump’s Blame Game: A Masterclass in Deflection

In a move that could only be described as brazen, President Donald Trump has once again deflected responsibility for his own administration’s actions onto the Democrats, this time regarding the potential mass firings of federal workers in the event of a government shutdown. Trump’s strategy, it seems, is to build a fortress of falsehoods so high that the truth becomes obscured to casual observers.

The Blame: Trump has ordered House and Senate Republicans not to negotiate with Democrats, effectively holding the federal government hostage as the funding deadline looms. His refusal to meet with Democratic leaders Hakeem Jeffries and Chuck Schumer to discuss funding is not just a political power play; it’s a dereliction of duty. By blaming Democrats for his administration’s plan to conduct mass firings rather than furloughs, Trump is not just twisting the truth—he’s inverting it.

The Claims: Trump’s accusations against Democrats are as wild as they are unfounded. Claims that Democrats want over $1 trillion in new spending for free healthcare for illegal aliens, or that they are pushing for taxpayers to fund transgender surgery for minors, are not just false, they are dangerously misleading. These statements are not simply political hyperbole; they are symptomatic of a larger strategy to demonize the opposition and distract from the real issues at hand.

The Reality: The decision to fire or lay off federal workers, rather than furlough them, is an unprecedented move in response to a government shutdown. Furloughs are the norm because they allow workers to eventually return to their jobs without the government having to navigate the complexities of rehiring. The decision to opt for mass firings reveals a disturbing willingness to sacrifice the livelihoods of federal workers for political leverage.

The Borders: Trump’s assertion that the U.S. has “absolutely perfect borders” is so detached from reality, it borders on delusion. His claims about Democrats wanting to open borders to criminals and force the country into financial ruin are manipulative fearmongering. It’s a classic Trump tactic: exaggerate and distort the positions of your opponents to make your own seem more reasonable by comparison.

Conclusion: The spectacle of Trump’s presidency continues to devolve into a theater of the absurd, where facts are treated as malleable and accountability is an alien concept. His latest maneuver to blame Democrats for a crisis of his own making is nothing short of political sabotage. It’s a strategy aimed at confusing the public and shifting blame away from his administration’s failures. In Trump’s America, it seems the buck stops anywhere but with him.

The sad truth is, the real losers in this political game of chicken will be the federal workers facing job insecurity, and the American people, whose government is being held hostage by a president unwilling to operate within the bounds of reality or reason.
