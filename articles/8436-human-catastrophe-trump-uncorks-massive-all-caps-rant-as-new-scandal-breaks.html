Donald Trump on Sunday morning unleashed a massive rant aimed in part at U.S. allies amid what some have called “one of the biggest foreign policy scandals in history.”

The president over the weekend took to his own social media site, Truth Social, to release the rant totaling more than 200 words. The massive meltdown comes amid disputed reports that Marco Rubio disseminated Russia-written documents to Ukrainian officials and passed them off as a U.S. peace deal.

Trump began by pointing the finger at former President Joe Biden.

“The War between Russia and Ukraine is a violent and terrible one that, with strong and proper U.S. and Ukrainian LEADERSHIP, would have NEVER HAPPENED. It began long before I took office for a Second Term, during the Sleepy Joe Biden Administration, and has only gotten worse,” the president wrote before making unsupported claims about the 2020 election. “If the 2020 Presidential Election was not RIGGED & STOLEN, the only thing the Radical Left Democrats are good at doing, there would be no Ukraine/Russia War, as there wasn’t, not even a mention, during my first Term in Office. Putin would never have attacked!”

Trump then continued:

“It was only when he saw Sleepy Joe in action that he said, ‘Now is my chance!’ The rest is history, and so it continues. I INHERITED A WAR THAT SHOULD HAVE NEVER HAPPENED, A WAR THAT IS A LOSER FOR EVERYONE, ESPECIALLY THE MILLIONS OF PEOPLE THAT HAVE SO NEEDLESSLY DIED. UKRAINE ‘LEADERSHIP’ HAS EXPRESSED ZERO GRATITUDE FOR OUR EFFORTS, AND EUROPE CONTINUES TO BUY OIL FROM RUSSIA.”

Going even further, Trump concluded his rant by saying, “THE USA CONTINUES TO SELL MASSIVE $AMOUNTS OF WEAPONS TO NATO, FOR DISTRIBUTION TO UKRAINE (CROOKED JOE GAVE EVERYTHING, FREE, FREE, FREE, INCLUDING ‘BIG’ MONEY!).”

“GOD BLESS ALL THE LIVES THAT HAVE BEEN LOST IN THE HUMAN CATASTROPHE! President DJT” he then added.

Our Analysis:

Donald Trump’s Unhinged Rant: A Dissection

In a display that can only be described as theatrically unhinged, Donald Trump took to his social media platform, Truth Social, to unload a rant that reeks of desperation and misinformation. The former president’s tirade, ostensibly aimed at U.S. allies and the current administration, is a convoluted mishmash of blame-shifting, historical revisionism, and outright falsehoods. Let’s dissect this piece by piece, shall we?

The Blame Game

Trump begins his rant with an immediate finger-pointing at former President Joe Biden, claiming, “The War between Russia and Ukraine is a violent and terrible one that, with strong and proper U.S. and Ukrainian LEADERSHIP, would have NEVER HAPPENED.” This statement not only ignores the complex geopolitical history leading up to the conflict but also absurdly suggests that his administration’s leadership could have single-handedly prevented it. This is not just an oversimplification; it’s fantasy.

Unsupported Claims and Election Obsession

Trump then leaps to the unsupported claim that the 2020 Presidential Election was “RIGGED & STOLEN,” a narrative thoroughly debunked by numerous court cases, audits, and recounts across the United States. His insistence on linking his election loss to the outbreak of the Ukraine-Russia war is a bewildering leap of logic that serves only to stoke the fires of conspiracy rather than shed light on any factual reality.

Revisionist History

“Putin would never have attacked!” Trump asserts, attributing a level of deterrence to his administration that is laughably naive. This revisionist take conveniently ignores the annexation of Crimea in 2014, a clear act of aggression from Russia towards Ukraine that happened under the watch of Barack Obama, not Joe Biden. The claim that there was “not even a mention” of conflict between Ukraine and Russia during his term is not just false; it’s dangerously misleading.

A War Inherited?

“I INHERITED A WAR THAT SHOULD HAVE NEVER HAPPENED,” Trump proclaims, a statement that is baffling on multiple levels. First, it’s chronologically impossible, given that the full-scale invasion of Ukraine by Russia occurred after his presidency. It’s akin to claiming inheritance of a house that was built after you moved to another city. The logical gymnastics required to follow this train of thought would puzzle even the most ardent of his supporters.

Ungrateful Ukraine and the European Oil Dilemma

Trump’s complaints about Ukrainian “LEADERSHIP” expressing “ZERO GRATITUDE” and Europe’s continued purchase of Russian oil are perhaps the only aspects grounded in a semblance of reality, but they are presented in a context that strips them of nuance. European dependence on Russian energy is a complex issue tied to decades of geopolitical maneuvering, not a simple matter of choice easily undone by presidential fiat or tantrums.

The Arms Sales Accusation

“THE USA CONTINUES TO SELL MASSIVE $AMOUNTS OF WEAPONS TO NATO, FOR DISTRIBUTION TO UKRAINE,” Trump decries, framing U.S. support for Ukraine as a transactional sin rather than a strategic necessity in the face of aggression. His portrayal of Biden’s aid to Ukraine as “FREE, FREE, FREE, INCLUDING ‘BIG’ MONEY!” is an attempt to paint the current administration as wasteful, ignoring the strategic importance of supporting Ukraine against Russian aggression.

A Concluding Prayer Amidst Chaos

Trump’s concluding “GOD BLESS ALL THE LIVES THAT HAVE BEEN LOST IN THE HUMAN CATASTROPHE!” is a jarring end to a rant filled with misinformation, self-aggrandizement, and historical revisionism. It’s as if he momentarily remembers the human cost of the conflict he has so cavalierly used as a prop in his political theatrics.

In summary, Trump’s rant is not just a massive meltdown; it’s a masterclass in misinformation, a deliberate attempt to rewrite history and absolve himself of any responsibility. It’s a reminder that the truth is often the first casualty in the war for political relevance.
