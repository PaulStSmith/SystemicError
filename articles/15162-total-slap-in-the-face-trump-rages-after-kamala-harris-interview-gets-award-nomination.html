In a Wednesday post on his Truth Social platform, Trump argued that the interview amounted to "election interference" and "turned out to be much worse than expected."

The president claimed that Harris's answers were "unlawfully fixed, manipulated, and doctored throughout by CBS, in order to try and make Kamala appear at least somewhat coherent."

According to Trump, the full transcript showed "that the 'interview' was nothing but fake, corrupt, anti-Trump propaganda."

"On top of that, in a total slap in the face to anyone who believes in TRUTH and Honest Journalism, this Fake News Puff Piece has now been nominated for an award by the totally discredited Emmys," he griped.

"Did 60 Minutes and its Corporate Parents apply to get an Emmy for an illegally falsified interview, or did other Fake Outlets nominate them for this dubious 'honor'?" he asked. "These antics are why the American People have no trust in the Press, and demand that the Media, very much including 60 Minutes, CBS, and its owners, be held responsible for their corruption and lies, which is exactly what we are doing in Court!"

Trump has sued CBS for $10 billion over the interview.

Our Analysis:

Trump's Tirade Against "60 Minutes": A Dissection

President Donald Trump's latest outburst on his Truth Social platform targets the "60 Minutes" news program over an award nomination for an interview with former Vice President Kamala Harris. Trump's reaction is a rich tapestry of accusations, ranging from "election interference" to the interview being "unlawfully fixed, manipulated, and doctored." Let's dissect this with the sharp scalpel of scrutiny.

Election Interference or Sour Grapes?

Trump's claim of "election interference" is a bold one, especially considering the timing of the interview and the nomination. To label a news program's interview as such requires a substantial burden of proof, which Trump conveniently fails to provide. His assertion seems less about genuine concern for electoral integrity and more about a personal vendetta against any media portrayal that does not glorify him.

The Art of Manipulation... Allegedly

Trump accuses CBS of manipulating the interview "in order to try and make Kamala appear at least somewhat coherent." This is a classic Trump move - undermining the credibility of his opponents by questioning their coherence and capacity. However, the accusation of manipulation is grave. If true, it would indeed be a scandal. But Trump's track record of making unfounded claims demands that we approach his statements with skepticism. Absent of evidence, this accusation looks more like an attempt to smear than a genuine concern for journalistic integrity.

The Emmy Nomination: A Conspiracy?

Trump's rant against the Emmy nomination of the "60 Minutes" interview as a "Fake News Puff Piece" and a "total slap in the face to anyone who believes in TRUTH and Honest Journalism" is both ironic and revealing. It's ironic because Trump's presidency was not exactly a golden era for truth-telling. Revealing, because it shows how Trump views any form of recognition or validation of his critics as a direct attack on him. The notion that the Emmys are part of a grand conspiracy against him is ludicrous, yet perfectly aligned with Trump's worldview where everything is about him, and any criticism or challenge is part of a larger plot.

The Lawsuit: A $10 Billion Battle Cry

Trump's lawsuit against CBS for $10 billion over the interview is the cherry on top of this convoluted sundae. The amount is astronomical and seems more like a figure designed for headlines rather than a realistic assessment of damages. It's a battle cry, a rallying call to his base to show that he's fighting against the perceived corruption of the media.

Conclusion: Sound and Fury, Signifying Nothing

Trump's tirade against "60 Minutes" and CBS is a textbook example of his strategy: deflect, attack, and inflate. By painting himself as a crusader against a corrupt media empire, he distracts from the substance of the Harris interview and any criticisms therein. It's a familiar play from Trump's playbook, but one that's increasingly transparent.

The real issue at hand is not the Emmy nomination or the alleged manipulation of the interview. It's about a former President who refuses to exit the stage quietly, constantly seeking to undermine the media's credibility to protect his own legacy. Trump's accusations require scrutiny, for without evidence, they are nothing more than the rantings of a man unable to tolerate any narrative that does not worship at his feet.
