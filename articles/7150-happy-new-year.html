Happy New Year!

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosDecember 31, 20251 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

We’re not ringing in the New Year with platitudes.

No “new year, new you.”
No glittery promises.
No collective amnesia about what just happened.

Help fuel the work. Independent analysis doesn’t fund itself:
☕ Buy us a coffee

This past year made one thing brutally clear: the system isn’t glitching.
It’s functioning exactly as designed — concentrating power, laundering accountability, and demanding gratitude from the people it grinds down.

So here’s our New Year’s message:

May this be the year fewer lies survive first contact with reality.
May cruelty finally start costing something.
May “that’s just how things are” lose its grip as an excuse.
May people stop mistaking stability for justice and tradition for virtue.

We don’t promise optimism.
We promise attention.

Systemic Error exists for one reason: to keep looking directly at the machinery — even when it’s uncomfortable, even when it’s ugly, especially when it’s normalized.

If there’s hope this year, it won’t come gift-wrapped.
It’ll come from pressure. From memory. From refusing to look away.

Happy New Year.
Let’s make it harder for the system to lie to us.

— Systemic Error
