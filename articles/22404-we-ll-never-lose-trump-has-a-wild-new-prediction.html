'We'll never lose': Trump has a wild new prediction

Based on an article by Thom Hartmann

Paulo SantosAugust 23, 20263 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

The GOP Is Not “Concerned” About Election Integrity. It Is Running an Anti-Voter State.

Power, Not Confusion

The source lays out a familiar Republican ritual: invent a crime, cite the invention as evidence, then use it to justify new barriers to the ballot. Howard Lutnick’s claim about 24,000 “noncitizens” voting is presented as a fact pattern. It is a lie. But the lie is not the point. The point is to manufacture permission for suppressing married women, racial minorities, city voters, and low-income people.

That is the actual structure here: the people with institutional power are the ones making the rules, degrading the systems, and then pretending they are responding to disorder.

The Decision Makers

This is not an accidental failure of democracy. The decisions are being made by Trump, his administration, Republican governors, GOP members of Congress, and a Supreme Court majority that keeps moving the constitutional goalposts in the same direction. The source names the machinery plainly: election commissioners fired, voter purges encouraged, CISA gutted, DOJ civil rights enforcement inverted, voter files seized, and polling-place intimidation normalized.

That is not oversight. That is governance by sabotage.

Misdirection as Method

The article is right to call out the lie about noncitizen voting, but the deeper trick is how Republicans keep redirecting blame onto the most vulnerable people in the electorate. Married women, Black voters, city residents, and poor voters are treated as suspicious by default, while the actual operators of election interference are treated as serious statesmen with “concerns.”

That framing is propaganda. It turns voter suppression into administration. It turns domination into procedure. And it asks the public to mistake paperwork attacks, ballot obstruction, and legal intimidation for responsible election management.

ICE at the Polls Means the Ballot Is Under Siege

The source mentions ICE agents outside polling places. That should end the conversation about “reform.” ICE is not a neutral public-service agency that occasionally needs better guidance. In this context it is being used, or threatened to be used, as a weapon of political intimidation.

A democracy does not need immigration police hovering around voting sites. It needs ICE abolished. Anything less preserves the threat structure and leaves the coercive tool in place for the next round of suppression.

The Same Playbook, Just Louder

The article puts Trump’s election sabotage in a line with Nixon, Reagan, and the Florida operation that cleared the path for George W. Bush. The pattern matters: when Republicans cannot rely on broad support, they lean on delay, purges, legal manipulation, institutional cowardice, and court protection. The difference now is not that the behavior is new. It is that they are bragging about it while the press still performs surprise.

That is the real decay on display. Not just criminality, but normalization of criminality through repetition and underreaction.

The System Behind the Scandal

The deeper story is not one election. It is a governing class that treats democratic participation as a threat whenever it is likely to dilute its power. Every purge, lawsuit, intimidation tactic, and judicial assist is part of a single project: make the electorate smaller, whiter, older, and easier to control.

That is why the source reads less like a report on isolated abuses than a field guide to authoritarian practice. The lesson is simple. Republicans are not trying to win a fair contest. They are trying to decide who gets counted before the votes are even cast.
