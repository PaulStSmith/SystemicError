'Are you on ketamine?': Critics mock Elon Musk as he denies reports he's out at Tesla

Elon Musk gestures onstage as he attends the Conservative Political Action Conference (CPAC) in National Harbor, Maryland, U.S., February 20, 2025. REUTERS/Nathan Howard

Tech billionaire and close White House advisor Elon Musk took to X, attacking the Wall Street Journal in the early hours of Thursday after it reported the Tesla board contacted recruitment firms to initiate a CEO search at the company.

"This is absolutely false (and this was communicated to the media before the report was published)," Tesla said from its X account. "The CEO of Tesla is Elon Musk and the Board is highly confident in his ability to continue executing on the exciting growth plan ahead."

Longtime Senate veteran Jim Manley asked, "Are you high on ketamine? Also, how many kids do you have?"

CNN reporter Hadas Gold said, "WSJ denies this. Spox tells me 'We stand by our reporting. Tesla was given the opportunity to provide a statement before publication, which they did not do.'"

"Swords of truth are painful when newspaper report them," said Julia Hobsbawm, founder and CEO of Workathon and The World Work Organization.

Tech wizard Aaron Greenspan commented, "If the Tesla Board had any actual concern about Elon Musk, you know, they could have just fired him for demanding a $56 billion pay package and then not showing up to work for three months. Appointing an interim CEO is not an impossible feat. Instead, they are trying to impress a court."

Musk allies attacked the Wall Street Journal for the report, saying they'd lost their journalistic integrity. One hand surgeon named Yevgeny Shuhatovich accused the Journal of going "woke" due to the three female bylines on the story.

Others pointed out that overnight Tesla's stock fell 4%.

Our Analysis:

The Musk Melodrama: Unraveling the Threads of Corporate Theater

In a recent spectacle that feels more at home in a daytime drama than the financial pages, Elon Musk, the tech billionaire and White House whisperer, has taken his grievances to X (formerly Twitter) in a characteristic early-morning tirade. This time, his ire was directed at the Wall Street Journal for its reporting on Tesla's alleged CEO search. The report, which Musk and Tesla vehemently deny, has sparked a flurry of reactions ranging from the incredulous to the downright bizarre.

The Allegations: Fact or Fiction?

The Wall Street Journal, a publication not known for flippant reporting, published a piece suggesting that the Tesla board was on the hunt for a new CEO. Tesla, through its X account, labeled the report as "absolutely false," asserting that Musk remains the CEO and enjoys the full confidence of the board. This denial, however, does little to quash the underlying tensions and questions about Musk's leadership and focus, given his notorious multitasking across several high-profile ventures and occasional forays into controversy.

The Spectacle of Denial

Musk's response to the Wall Street Journal's reporting was predictably theatrical. He not only refuted the claims but was also joined in his rebuttal by a chorus of allies and critics alike, each adding their own flavor to the unfolding drama. Notably, Jim Manley's quip about ketamine and Musk's progeny did little to elucidate the issue but much to highlight the circus-like atmosphere surrounding Tesla's public relations.

The Stand of the Tesla Board

In an age where corporate governance is under more scrutiny than ever, the Tesla board's steadfast support for Musk raises eyebrows. Critics, like Aaron Greenspan, point out the absurdity of the board's unwavering backing, despite Musk's extravagant compensation and intermittent absence. This situation begs the question: Is Tesla's board truly acting in the best interest of its shareholders and the company, or are they merely Musk's cheerleaders?

The "Woke" Accusation and Stock Woes

In an interesting twist, one critic attributed the Wall Street Journal's reporting to an alleged "woke" agenda, spotlighting the three female bylines on the story. This accusation is a red herring, distracting from the real issues at hand and instead, weaving a narrative of culture war into corporate reporting. Meanwhile, Tesla's stock took a 4% hit overnight, a tangible indicator of the real-world impact of such high-profile controversies.

Conclusion: The Musk Conundrum

Elon Musk, for all his brilliance and innovation, remains a polarizing figure at the helm of one of the world's most influential tech companies. This latest episode with the Wall Street Journal underscores a broader dilemma: Can Tesla navigate its ambitious growth plans under the stewardship of a leader as mercurial as Musk? Time will tell, but one thing is certain—the saga of Elon Musk and Tesla is far from over, and the world will be watching, popcorn at the ready.
