“Other than the Rigged Presidential Election of 2020, the Biggest Scandal in American History is the “AUTOPEN!” Whoever used it was usurping the power of the Presidency, and it should be very easy to find out who that person (or persons) is,” Trump wrote Tuesday.

He went on to accuse Biden – whom he suggested is not of “sound mind,” of approving policies he would never have otherwise supported, including “Open Borders, Transgender for everyone, men in women’s sports, and far more.”

“Fear not, however, we will bring America BACK, BIGGER, BETTER, AND STRONGER THAN EVER BEFORE!” the MAGA leader added.

Moments earlier, Trump praised himself for his actions in Alaska while blaming the Biden-Harris administration for dismantling “job-producing” initiatives there.

Our Analysis:

Trump's Tirade: A Masterclass in Misdirection

Former President Donald Trump's latest barrage on Truth Social is a tour de force in the art of diversion and distortion, a spectacle that would be entertaining if it weren't so deeply troubling for the fabric of American democracy.

Old Grievances, New Attacks

Trump's attack on the use of the "autopen" as the "second-biggest scandal in American history" is, to put it mildly, a gross exaggeration. The autopen, a device used for the automatic signing of a signature, has been in use by presidents for decades to sign routine documents. To elevate its use to the status of a monumental scandal is not only factually dubious but a clear attempt to deflect from more substantive issues facing the nation.

Unfounded Accusations

Trump's accusations against Biden, suggesting he is not of "sound mind" and blaming him for policies like "Open Borders, Transgender for everyone, men in women’s sports," are not grounded in reality. These claims are misleading and serve only to stoke division and fear among the populace. It's a classic Trump move: create a boogeyman to rally against, regardless of the facts.

The Return of the MAGA Promise

Trump's promise to bring America "BACK, BIGGER, BETTER, AND STRONGER THAN EVER BEFORE!" is a rehash of his 2016 campaign rhetoric. It's a vague promise that lacks any concrete policy proposal or vision for the future. It's a slogan, not a strategy.

The Alaskan Adventure

Trump's self-praise for his actions in Alaska and the subsequent blame he places on the Biden-Harris administration for dismantling "job-producing" initiatives is another example of his selective memory. The Trump administration's environmental deregulations may have favored certain industries in the short term, but they also posed significant long-term risks to the environment and indigenous populations. The narrative that Biden is the destroyer of jobs ignores the complexities of sustainable development and environmental protection.

The Maine Claim

Trump's claim about restoring fishing rights in Maine "viciously taken away" by the Obama-Biden administration and his declaration of winning the 2024 election in a "LANDSLIDE" are, quite simply, delusional. His insistence on taking credit for everything positive and blaming others for any negatives, coupled with his baseless assertion of a future election victory, is a dangerous attempt to rewrite reality to fit his narrative.

Conclusion

In a series of posts that veer from the misleading to the outright fantastical, Trump continues to demonstrate a disregard for truth and a disturbing penchant for revisionism. His statements are not grounded in a genuine concern for the welfare of the American people but are instead a transparent attempt to sow division and maintain his grip on the political narrative.

The real scandal here is not the use of an autopen but the relentless assault on truth by a former president who refuses to gracefully exit the stage. In this latest performance, Trump has proven once again that his greatest skill lies not in governance but in the art of misdirection and manipulation.
