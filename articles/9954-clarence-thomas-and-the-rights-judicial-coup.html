Clarence Thomas and the Right’s Judicial Coup

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosSeptember 30, 20253 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

The Weaponization of PrecedentRoe Was Only the BeginningA Court CapturedThe StakesWhat Must Be DoneA Final Word

Every time Clarence Thomas opens his mouth, the American project trembles. His latest outburst at the Catholic University of America’s Columbus School of Law wasn’t a lecture in jurisprudence—it was a roadmap to dismantle the last half-century of progress. With smug disdain for precedent, Thomas all but declared war on the very concept of rights outside the narrowest reading of the Constitution. This isn’t just about abortion anymore. It’s about contraception, marriage equality, privacy, and the civil liberties that drag us out of the 17th century into something resembling modernity.

The Weaponization of Precedent

Thomas sneered that legal precedent isn’t “gospel” but “something somebody dreamt up.” Translation: stare decisis—the principle that past decisions should guide future rulings—is just a nuisance when it protects rights conservatives despise. He trotted out an absurd analogy about an orangutan driving a train to mock his colleagues’ reliance on precedent. But let’s be clear: Thomas isn’t against precedent when it shields corporations or entrenches conservative power. He’s against it when it defends your right to contraception, to love whom you choose, to marry across race or gender, or to be free from state intrusion in your bedroom.

Roe Was Only the Beginning

In 2022, Thomas practically drooled over Roe’s demise, using his concurring opinion to call for the Court to “reconsider” every major substantive due process case: Griswold (contraception), Lawrence (same-sex intimacy), Obergefell (marriage equality). Strip those away, and the United States reverts to a country where the government and the church dictate the most intimate aspects of life. Conservatives have hated substantive due process since it was used to strike at Jim Crow and expand civil rights. Thomas is simply saying the quiet part out loud: their plan is to burn it all down.

A Court Captured

This isn’t Thomas acting alone. The Trump-packed supermajority on the Supreme Court—courtesy of Mitch McConnell’s obstruction and the Federalist Society’s billionaire pipeline—has no interest in neutrality. They are political operatives in robes, engaged in demolition under the guise of interpretation. The project is deliberate: delegitimize the very foundation of civil liberties, then impose a rigid, theocratic order under the pretense of “tradition.”

The Stakes

Don’t be fooled: if substantive due process is gutted, it’s not just contraception or marriage equality at risk. Desegregation itself hangs in the balance. Privacy, bodily autonomy, equal protection—cornerstones of modern American life—become optional, subject to the whims of whichever authoritarian clique holds power. The far right has always fantasized about rolling back the clock to a time when women, LGBTQ people, and racial minorities had no recourse. Thomas is simply telling us the destination.

What Must Be Done

We cannot pretend this is normal. We cannot politely wait for “norms” to reassert themselves while rights are stripped away. This Supreme Court is corrupted beyond repair—tainted by Trump, financed by oligarchs, and staffed by zealots who confuse theology with law. If Congress had any backbone, it would move now: investigate the corruption, expand the Court, and yes, impeach Clarence Thomas. Impeach him for his open contempt of precedent, his wife’s entanglement in insurrectionist plots, and his role in weaponizing the judiciary against democracy itself.

A Final Word

The right’s plan is not hidden—it’s broadcast in Thomas’s sneers and smirks. They want to take away rights that generations fought, bled, and died for. They want to erase the hard-won freedoms that define modern America. And unless we meet their assault with equal force, they will succeed. The alarm bells are blaring. We either act—or we watch the train, driven by orangutans in robes, crash into the wreckage of our democracy.
