Idaho’s Tax Cut Disaster and the Voucher Trojan Horse

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosAugust 29, 20256 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

The Idaho Statesman’s Bryan Clark did the state a service with his sharp indictment of the Republican tax-cut carnival. His central point is plain and devastating: tax cuts for the rich never pay for themselves, and the bill always comes due — not for the wealthy, but for everyone else. Idaho’s $80 million revenue shortfall is proof. The projected $187 million hole, once the so-called “One Big Beautiful Bill” is fully incorporated, is a gaping wound. And what do Idahoans get in return? A shredded social safety net, crumbling public services, and a government that refuses to protect them, all while the rich and powerful laugh their way to the bank.

The Big Lie of Trickle-Down

Idaho Republicans swore, as Republicans always do, that these cuts would stimulate growth, fatten wallets, and fill state coffers. Instead, the coffers are running dry. Medicaid reimbursements are being slashed, forcing doctors to refuse low-income patients. This isn’t an abstract budget debate. It means parents can’t get their kids to a doctor until it’s a crisis. It means chronic conditions go untreated. It means the working poor — the backbone of Idaho’s economy — get sicker, poorer, and more desperate while the donor class pockets the gains.

Bryan Clark underscored the cynical predictability of this cycle: Republicans insist tax cuts won’t bust the budget. They do. Republicans insist the cuts will benefit ordinary people. They don’t. And when the deficit explodes, the scalpel comes out — never to carve away at elite tax breaks, always to gut the programs regular Idahoans actually rely on.

Medicaid on the Chopping Block

The immediate victim of this latest round of cuts is Medicaid. Doctors are already warning they will have no choice but to stop seeing Medicaid patients if reimbursements shrink further. And the consequences will ripple across the entire healthcare system. Emergency rooms — the most expensive, least efficient form of care — will become the only option for the poor. The cost to hospitals will rise, and so will the hidden costs borne by everyone else through higher premiums and taxes. The supposed savings are a mirage. The suffering is very real.

And while Republicans butcher Medicaid, they make sure their sacred cows — tax credits for the rich, loopholes for corporations, and handouts for private schools — are insulated from sacrifice.

The Voucher Scam

The $50 million private school voucher scheme is the perfect example. Already costing more than projected, this program remains untouchable because it’s structured as a “tax credit” rather than a spending program. That’s not fiscal prudence; it’s accounting trickery. Everyone knows it. Legislators designed it that way to shield it from the cuts hammering programs like Medicaid.

Let’s be clear: vouchers are not about “school choice.” They are about laundering taxpayer dollars into religious schools that exist not to educate, but to indoctrinate. The prohibition on directly funding religious institutions is clear in both Idaho’s constitution and in bedrock federal law. Vouchers are the loophole, a Trojan horse to funnel public money into private Christian nationalist academies where children are taught dogma instead of science, ideology instead of history, obedience instead of critical thinking.

Who Really Benefits?

The poor will not see a dime of this voucher money. The barriers are deliberate: parents must front tuition before credits arrive, and private schools can — and will — tack on additional fees, uniforms, and requirements that place them out of reach for working-class families. The vouchers are subsidies for the wealthy, enabling them to shift the cost of private schooling onto the public ledger. Meanwhile, public schools, already starved for funds, will suffer even deeper cuts.

Worse still, the voucher approval process will never be neutral. The boards that determine eligible schools will be stacked with theocrats, ensuring that Christian institutions qualify while Islamic schools, secular alternatives, or anything deemed “unacceptable” to conservative culture warriors are excluded. The money flows one way: into the coffers of fundamentalist institutions determined to churn out the next generation of Republican loyalists.

Crapo’s Hypocrisy

At the federal level, Idaho’s senior senator Mike Crapo provides the blueprint for this hypocrisy. A deficit hawk in rhetoric only, Crapo has spent his career voting for — and championing — tax cuts that have ballooned the federal debt from $3.9 trillion when he entered office to nearly $40 trillion today. His fingerprints are all over the “One Big Beautiful Bill.” He now hides behind accounting tricks, claiming to have cut the deficit when in reality he greased the skids for its explosion.

Crapo even flaunts a “debt clock” on his website, as though the spiraling number is someone else’s fault. Idahoans should see it for what it is: a monument to his own duplicity. He piled up debt without delivering lasting improvements for his state. No new infrastructure boom. No groundbreaking research investment. No expanded social programs. Just more giveaways to the rich, more loopholes for corporations, and more austerity for everyone else.

The Populist Masquerade

The most galling part of this entire fiasco is the GOP’s continuing masquerade as a “populist” party. They drape themselves in the language of the working man while systematically gutting the very programs working families rely on. They rail against “elites” even as they shovel taxpayer dollars into elite pockets. They posture as defenders of the people while rigging the system to entrench wealth and power in fewer and fewer hands.

The result? Idahoans are left with less healthcare, less education, and fewer opportunities, while the wealthy enjoy new tax shelters and subsidized private schooling. Public education erodes, healthcare collapses, infrastructure stagnates — all sacrificed on the altar of an economic ideology that has never worked, except for those it was designed to serve: the donor class.

Theocratic Rent-Seeking

The voucher program also reveals the longer game: power. By defunding public schools and bankrolling private religious ones, Republicans are building an infrastructure of indoctrination. These schools are not accountable to the public. They do not need to meet curriculum standards. They will not teach evolution, climate science, or honest history. They will churn out students marinated in Christian nationalist dogma, who grow into voters primed to view democracy as secondary to “God’s will.”

This is not just fiscal recklessness. It is theocratic rent-seeking, using taxpayer money to underwrite the slow-motion demolition of secular, pluralist democracy. And Idaho is ground zero.

Who Pays the Price?

The price is paid by ordinary Idahoans. By the family whose child can’t see a doctor. By the neighbor who can’t afford medicine. By the public school teacher watching her classroom crumble. By the taxpayer footing the bill for someone else’s private indoctrination academy.

Idahoans are being fleeced in broad daylight. The perpetrators — Crapo in Washington, Moyle in Boise, and the rest of the Republican establishment — will keep telling them that this is “populism.” But populism that makes the rich richer and the poor sicker is nothing but another name for looting.

Conclusion: Looting in the Name of Reform

The Idaho Statesman’s Bryan Clark was right to sound the alarm. Idaho’s fiscal collapse is not an accident. It is the deliberate outcome of Republican policy, designed to protect the wealthy while dismantling the public institutions that sustain ordinary people. The Medicaid cuts, the voucher scheme, the debt explosion — they are not failures of governance. They are the system working as intended.

If Idahoans do not recognize this and act accordingly, they will be trapped in a cycle of austerity and indoctrination, footing the bill for their own dispossession. The “One Big Beautiful Bill” is nothing more than a Trojan horse: beautiful on the outside, rotten and destructive within. And behind it stand men like Mike Crapo, smiling as the debt clock ticks upward and the state burns.

Idaho is the cautionary tale. The rest of America should take heed. This is what happens when you let the fox guard the henhouse: the rich get their tax cuts, the theocrats get their vouchers, and everyone else gets the shaft.
