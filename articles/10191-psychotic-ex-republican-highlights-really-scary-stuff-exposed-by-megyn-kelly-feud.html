'Psychotic': Ex-Republican highlights 'really scary stuff' exposed by Megyn Kelly feud

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosSeptember 23, 20254 min read

Megyn Kelly speaking with attendees at the 2023 Turning Point Action Conference at the Palm Beach County Convention Center in West Palm Beach, Florida. (Photo credit: Gage Skidmore)

A former GOP strategist on Sunday slammed "extremely alarming" claims made by former Fox News host Megyn Kelly over the weekend.

Tim Miller, host of the conservative news outlet The Bulwark's eponymous podcast, and Kelly have been feuding over the details of an MSNBC investigation that revealed President Donald Trump's border czar, Tom Homan, was caught up in a bribery scandal in which he allegedly accepted a $50,000 bribe from an undercover FBI agent in exchange for helping the agent win government contracts.

"I know that hearing about the details of a Twitter feud is kind of like hearing about somebody's fantasy football team's loss," Miller said. "In this case, it's a little bit of an exception, Not because I think you care about the blow-by-blow of my feud with Megan Kelly, but because the arguments that she is making are extremely alarming and they're psychotic, but they're also alarming."

Kelly and Miller engaged in a back-and-forth on X on Saturday night. In one of her responses to Miller, Kelly said, "We don’t care what you say about Tom Homan. We do not trust you. We only care about defeating you."

Miller characterized Kelly's response to the MSNBC report as "we do not care" and that "corruption doesn't matter." He added that Kelly's argument is "chilling," and that it reveals that some "really scary stuff" lies ahead.

"Megyn Kelly thinks you can act with impunity, and she's just extremely blunt about that," Miller said.

"Is there like anything that someone in the Trump administration could do that Megan Kelly would object to?" Miller added. "And I think the answer is basically no as long as it advances their mission."

Our Analysis:

Analysis: The Feud Illuminates Deeper Issues

The recent feud between Tim Miller, a former GOP strategist and host of The Bulwark's podcast, and Megyn Kelly, former Fox News host, transcends personal squabbles and shines a glaring light on the unsettling dynamics within conservative circles. Miller's critique of Kelly's dismissal of serious corruption allegations against Tom Homan, former President Donald Trump's border czar, is not merely a spat but a window into a deeply troubling mindset that prioritizes political victories over ethical considerations.

The Underlying Scandal

At the heart of this controversy is an MSNBC investigation that implicated Tom Homan in a bribery scandal. Allegations that Homan accepted a $50,000 bribe from an undercover FBI agent in exchange for assistance in securing government contracts should alarm anyone, regardless of political affiliation. Yet, Kelly's response, according to Miller, essentially boils down to a chilling indifference to corruption.

Kelly's Alarming Stance

Megyn Kelly's reported remarks, "We don’t care what you say about Tom Homan. We do not trust you. We only care about defeating you," encapsulate a dangerous philosophy. This attitude suggests that the end justifies the means, a perspective that erodes the very foundation of democratic principles and accountability.

Miller's characterization of Kelly's stance as "we do not care" and that "corruption doesn't matter" is indeed "chilling." It signals a willingness to overlook ethical breaches if they serve a political purpose. This normalization of corruption for political gain is not only unethical but also a threat to the integrity of public office and democracy itself.

The Bigger Picture

The feud between Miller and Kelly, while perhaps seeming petty on the surface, actually reveals a larger, more sinister issue at play: a segment of the political right's apparent willingness to excuse and even defend corruption among its ranks. Miller's rhetorical question, "Is there like anything that someone in the Trump administration could do that Megan Kelly would object to?" underscores this point. It suggests a blind loyalty to political figures that could undermine the rule of law and democratic norms.

Conclusion: A Path to "Really Scary Stuff"

Miller's warning of "really scary stuff" ahead is not hyperbole but a logical conclusion if the mindset Kelly allegedly represents becomes more widespread. When political victories are prioritized over ethical standards and the rule of law, democracy is in jeopardy.

The real issue is not the personal feud between Tim Miller and Megyn Kelly, but what it signifies: a disturbing trend where some are willing to dismiss serious allegations of corruption simply because it doesn't align with their political objectives. This should concern anyone who values democracy and the rule of law, irrespective of political affiliation.
