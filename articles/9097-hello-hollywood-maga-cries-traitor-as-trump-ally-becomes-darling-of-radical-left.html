'Hello Hollywood': MAGA cries traitor as Trump ally becomes 'darling of radical left'

By Paulo Santos

Paulo SantosNovember 2, 20254 min read

IN THIS ARTICLE

Donald Trump supporters (Photo via Reuters)

A self-identified MAGA lawmaker is feeling the heat from the base of Donald Trump’s supporters after appearing on a non-conservative’s talk show.

Rep. Marjorie Taylor Greene (R-GA), who recently suggested that “forgotten” Americans should “overthrow” the government, appeared on Real Time With Bill Maher on Friday, Oct. 31. The appearance generated applause by Trump’s critics, who saw Greene being critical of her Republican colleagues and the administration’s actions.

But MAGA’s most deeply entrenched supporters were furious, and didn’t hesitate to make that known on X.

On Saturday, Greene posted, “Halloween was a blast starting with going on Real Time with Bill Maher and then going to the best Halloween party in Beverly Hills,” on X. That resulted in fiery responses.

Don Draven, a self-identified military veteran who supports MAGA, replied with, “Real Time with Bill Maher, then a Beverly Hills bash with reality stars. Meanwhile, Congress is on fire, and she’s out here trick-or-treating for airtime. When you’re more booked than the House calendar, maybe you’re in the wrong House.”

@LizAnn34, a user with “MAGA” in their bio, asked Greene, “When is your reveal that you’re the latest squad member?”

Another purported MAGA veteran, MAG1775, wrote, “Marjorie out here cosplaying as the new Liz Cheney, rubbing elbows in Beverly Hills while pretending she’s still ‘one of the people.’”

“Keep it up, Marge,” the account added. “At this rate, you’ll have a CNN contract before the tan fades.”

Popular MAGA influencer Vince Langman wrote, “Oh, look the new darling of the radical left Marjorie Taylor Greene was invited on Bill Maher last night.”

“Shocking,” Langman added.

Popular conservative influencer Lionel added, “If you would have told me that MTG would have lost so much favor with so many conservatives so quickly I would’ve thought you were so out of your mind.”

Our Analysis:

The Fall from MAGA Grace: Greene’s Tightrope Walk

In a spectacle that could rival the twists and turns of a prime-time drama, Rep. Marjorie Taylor Greene (R-GA) finds herself in the eye of a political storm, courtesy of her own making. Greene, who has never shied away from controversy, has seemingly taken a leap too far for some of her staunchest supporters. Her recent appearance on Real Time With Bill Maher and subsequent celebration in Beverly Hills have ignited a firestorm among MAGA hardliners, revealing a glaring rift within the movement.

A Dance with the Devil?

Greene’s decision to appear on Maher’s show, a platform known for its liberal leanings and critical take on conservative politics, was akin to walking into the lion’s den wearing a steak suit. While some might argue that engaging with opposing viewpoints is healthy for democracy, Greene’s critics within the MAGA base see this as nothing short of betrayal. The backlash was swift and brutal, with figures like Don Draven and MAG1775 taking to X to express their disdain, painting Greene as a political chameleon more interested in airtime and high society than the conservative cause.

Trick or Treating for Airtime?

The juxtaposition of Greene’s actions — discussing politics on a liberal talk show one moment and rubbing elbows with Beverly Hills elites the next — paints a picture of a lawmaker more interested in personal brand building than in addressing the pressing issues facing Congress and her constituents. Don Draven’s scathing critique hits the nail on the head: “When you’re more booked than the House calendar, maybe you’re in the wrong House.“ This sentiment echoes a growing frustration among MAGA supporters who feel abandoned by those they propelled into power, only to watch them seemingly revel in the very institutions they promised to dismantle.

The New “Radical Left Darling”?

The irony of Greene’s newfound infamy among her base is palpable. Once hailed as a fearless crusader for MAGA values, she now stands accused of cosplaying as the new Liz Cheney, a comparison that, in this context, is tantamount to political apostasy. The comment from MAG1775, “Marjorie out here cosplaying as the new Liz Cheney,“ underscores a perceived shift from populist champion to establishment pawn. The suggestion that Greene’s tan from the bright lights of the left-leaning media circuit might only last until she secures a CNN contract is both a biting indictment and a stark warning of the consequences of political inconstancy.

A Mirror to the Movement’s Soul

What this debacle highlights is not just the precarious position of Marjorie Taylor Greene but a deeper existential crisis within the MAGA movement itself. The swift backlash from her base reflects a broader struggle over the soul of conservatism in America, where purity tests and ideological conformity often trump pragmatic political discourse. Lionel’s surprise at Greene’s rapid fall from grace encapsulates the volatile nature of political capital within MAGA circles, where yesterday’s hero can easily become today’s pariah.

In essence, Greene’s foray into the world of Bill Maher and Beverly Hills has opened a Pandora’s box of political backlash that could have lasting implications for her career. The MAGA base, known for its unwavering loyalty to Trump and his acolytes, has shown that its support is not unconditional. As Greene attempts to navigate these treacherous waters, one thing is clear: the path to redemption within her own ranks may be as elusive as a bipartisan consensus in Congress.
