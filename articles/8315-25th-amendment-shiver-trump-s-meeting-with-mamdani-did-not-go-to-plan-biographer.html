'25th Amendment shiver': Trump's meeting with Mamdani did not go to plan: biographer

The meeting between New York City mayor elect Zohran Mamdani and Donald Trump did not go according to plan, according to a biographer.

Michael Wolff suggested the president’s plan had been to be tough but instead Trump looked as though he was won over by the Democratic socialist during their Oval Office press conference. The slip up from Trump, who had reportedly been set to be tough on Mamdani only to say he and the NYC mayoral elect agreed on several issues, has made some insiders “shiver”, The Daily Beast reported.

Wolff quoted a source during an appearance on the Inside Trump’s Head podcast, claiming an insider had been brief to expect quite the opposite from Trump during his meeting with Mamdani.

Wolff, quoting the unnamed source, said, “’Nobody expected that. This was not the script. It was completely out of the blue... and concerning. This wasn’t just losing the plot. This was like a different guy. The look in his eyes was crazy.’”

The biographer has gone on to suggest this will set alarm bells off for some members of the Trump administration, with Wolff suggesting the 25th Amendment could come into play.

The source told Wolff, “The only explanation was that the guy forgot who he is—so weird. It gives you a 25th Amendment shiver.” The 25th Amendment was written to ensure the vice president takes over in the event the president dies, resigns, or is removed from office by impeachment.

The amendment also provides the temporary transfer of the president’s power to the vice president, and this, crucially, can be on the initiative of the president or the vice president. The latter would need a majority of the president’s cabinet to agree to make the change of power, which would have the vice president step in as acting president.

White House Communications Director Steven Cheung has since hit out at Wolff for his comments. He said, “Michael Wolff is a lying sack of s**t and has been proven to be a fraud. He routinely fabricates stories originating from his sick and warped imagination, only possible because he has a severe and debilitating case of Trump Derangement Syndrome that has rotted his peanut-sized brain.”

Our Analysis:

The Unexpected Trump-Mamdani Tango: A Dance of Political Irony or A Prelude to Constitutional Crisis?

In an episode that reads more like a political thriller than a standard meeting between the president and a mayor-elect, the recent rendezvous between Donald Trump and New York City mayor-elect Zohran Mamdani has set the stage for an unexpected plot twist in the ever-dramatic saga of American politics. According to Michael Wolff, a biographer known for his controversial takes on Trump’s administration, what was supposed to be a display of presidential toughness turned into a scene where Trump appeared almost captivated by Mamdani, a Democratic socialist, during their Oval Office press conference.

The Script Flips

Trump, a figure synonymous with hardline conservatism and brash rhetoric, supposedly had his sights set on taking a tough stance. However, the outcome, as Wolff suggests, left some insiders trembling with concerns of unpredictability. The statement that Trump and Mamdani “agreed on several issues” not only confounds the expected political narrative but also sends a chilling signal to those accustomed to Trump’s usual modus operandi.

Insider Shivers and 25th Amendment Whispers

The reaction to this bewildering performance was aptly captured by an unnamed source, quoted by Wolff, expressing that the encounter was “not the script” and labeling it “concerning” and indicative of Trump being “like a different guy.” The mention of the 25th Amendment in this context is nothing short of bombastic. The amendment, a constitutional safety net for scenarios where a president is unable to fulfill their duties, being hinted at suggests a level of dysfunction or incapacity that is alarming, to say the least.

A Clash of Communications

In the wake of Wolff’s explosive revelations, White House Communications Director Steven Cheung launched a scathing counterattack, branding Wolff a “lying sack of s**t” and decrying his allegations as fabrications born from a “severe and debilitating case of Trump Derangement Syndrome.” Cheung’s response, while unsurprisingly vitriolic, underscores the deep divide between Trump’s camp and his critics. The mention of Trump Derangement Syndrome, a term often used to dismiss criticisms of Trump’s presidency as irrational or biased, adds an extra layer of intrigue to the whole scenario.

Analysis: Between the Lines of Political Theatre

What we are witnessing here could very well be an instance of political theatre, with each actor playing to their audience. However, the implications of such a meeting and its aftermath cannot be understated. If Trump, indeed, veered off the anticipated course to find common ground with Mamdani, it challenges the binary narrative of American politics. Yet, the invocation of the 25th Amendment, whether in earnest concern or dramatic flair, paints a picture of a presidency under siege from within.

Wolff’s credibility and Cheung’s rebuttal aside, the core issue remains: a moment of unexpected accord (or at least the appearance of it) between two ostensibly polar opposites. This incident, whether a fleeting anomaly or a symptom of deeper systemic unpredictability, highlights the fragility and the spectacle of political discourse in today’s America.

In dissecting these developments, one must tread carefully between fact and fiction, acknowledging the performative elements of politics while remaining vigilant to the very real consequences of these high-stakes encounters. As the Trump administration navigates these turbulent waters, the specter of the 25th Amendment looms not just as a constitutional provision but as a symbol of the ultimate accountability measure in American democracy.

In conclusion, the Trump-Mamdani meeting serves as a stark reminder of the unpredictability inherent in political leadership and the ever-present tension between image and reality. Whether this episode will be remembered as a curious footnote in history or a turning point in the Trump presidency remains to be seen, but it certainly has provided ample fodder for political analysts and commentators alike.
