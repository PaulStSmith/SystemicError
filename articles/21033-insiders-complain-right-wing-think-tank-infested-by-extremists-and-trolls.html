Insiders complain right-wing think tank infested by 'extremists and trolls'

Heritage Was Not Divided by Ideas. It Was Captured by a Machine.

The Real Story

The New York Times account is about the Heritage Foundation, but the deeper subject is not a think tank in distress. It is a Republican power structure deciding which version of right-wing politics gets institutional legitimacy. Heritage, once a professionalized pipeline into GOP administrations, has been shoved aside, then remade from the inside by the post-Trump right.

Who Holds Power

The relevant power is not “conservative thought” in the abstract. It is Kevin Roberts, the leadership he empowered, and the MAGA-adjacent network that now treats institutions as terrain to seize rather than organizations to steward. When Roberts defended Tucker Carlson after Carlson hosted Nick Fuentes, that was not a rhetorical misstep. It was a loyalty test. Heritage’s old guard understood the message immediately: the institution was no longer being governed for its own credibility, but for its usefulness to a harder, more conspiratorial faction.

The Purge Was the Point

Dozens of staff left for Mike Pence’s Advancing American Freedom, which says everything about the split. This was not a dispute between equally reasonable conservative camps. It was a break between a traditional Republican establishment and a movement willing to normalize white-nationalist-adjacent figures, online radicalism, and open contempt for the old gatekeepers. The “Heritage Hunger Games” description is ugly, but apt: firings, resignations, device-monitoring paranoia, and a steady replacement of experienced staff with openly MAGA recruits is how an institution gets converted into an instrument.

The Misdirection

The usual press-language for stories like this is dysfunction, factionalism, or confusion. That framing is too soft. The source itself includes a former senior analyst saying it was “planned” and “a coup,” and that is closer to the mark. This was not chaos accidentally produced by personality clashes. It was deliberate displacement. Heritage was not merely exposed to extremist pressure; it was overrun by it. That distinction matters because pretending this is a generic internal rift lets the architects off the hook.

The New Hiring Logic

Look at what replaced the old guard: a senior adviser with a murky résumé, a former DOJ official indicted for trying to overturn the 2020 election, and people openly promoting racism, misogyny, and Holocaust denialism. That is not institutional drift. That is a selection process. The standard is no longer competence, credibility, or even conservative seriousness. The standard is whether a person can serve the movement’s appetite for grievance, provocation, and authoritarian loyalty.

The Broader Pattern

Heritage’s collapse into a MAGA holding pen shows how the right’s governing class has changed its definition of strength. It no longer wants expertise unless expertise can be bent to the cause. It no longer wants institutions unless they can be used to launder extremism into respectability. The old conservative establishment is not being defeated by better arguments; it is being replaced by a faction that understands institutions as trophies. That is the pattern: not debate, but capture. Not persuasion, but takeover.
