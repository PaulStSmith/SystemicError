Trump ally trying to sabotage White House official's 2028 presidential run: report

Far-right influencer and ally to President Donald Trump Laura Loomer is actively working to sabotage a top White House official’s alleged bid for the White House in 2028, Politico reported Wednesday.

Loomer, who despite not being a government officials continues to wield “breathtaking influence” over the Trump administration, launching several successful campaigns to oust government officials she views as not adequately loyal to the administration, now has her sights set on Health and Human Services Secretary RFK Jr., who she says has “clear intention” to run for president in 2028.

“I’m not naive enough to think that the president is going to get rid of RFK, but I will say that… there are concerns about some of the staffing decisions over at HHS,” Loomer told Politico Wednesday.

Loomer admitted that despite her heavy influence on the administration, she was unlikely to convince the Trump to fire RFK Jr., but that his principal deputy chief of staff and senior counselor, Stefanie Spear, was a more realistic target.

“I think that there’s a clear intention by Stefanie Spear to utilize her position to try to lay the groundwork for a 2028 RFK presidential run,” Loomer said, citing “sources in HHS” as to her knowledge of the alleged White House bid.

A senior HHS official, speaking with Politico on the condition of anonymity, did not deny that RFK Jr. was mulling a presidential run in 2028, and comments from an anonymous Trump administration official support that claim as well, telling Politico that they “would not be surprised if [Kennedy is] thinking about” a 2028 White House bid.

A self-proclaimed “proud Islamaphobe,” Loomer has been tied to a number of high-profile firings in the Trump administration, including the firing last month of April Doss, the National Security Agency’s top lawyer, and the resignations of Vinay Prasad from the Food and Drug Administration and Adam Schleifer at the Justice Department.

Loomer has also been tied to the firings of more than half a dozen NSA officials in April, and continues to meet regularly with top White House officials, including a recentone-on-one meetingwith Vice PresidentJD Vancein April.

Our Analysis:

The Puppeteer Behind the Curtain: Laura Loomer's Disturbing Influence

In a world where political influence is often cloaked in layers of bureaucracy and diplomacy, the unabashed reach of Laura Loomer, a far-right influencer and a self-described "proud Islamaphobe," into the inner workings of the Trump administration is both breathtaking and deeply concerning. This isn't a tale of political strategy within the conventional boundaries of advisors and elected officials; it's a stark illustration of how external actors with extremist views can shape the highest levels of government.

The Saboteur at Work

Politico's recent report sheds light on Loomer's latest crusade: derailing the potential 2028 presidential ambitions of Health and Human Services Secretary RFK Jr. Despite her lack of an official government role, Loomer's words carry an alarming weight, with her past campaigns having resulted in the ousting of numerous government officials deemed insufficiently loyal to Trump. This isn't about political discourse; it's a witch hunt for ideological purity.

Loomer's focus on Stefanie Spear, RFK Jr.'s principal deputy chief of staff and senior counselor, as a stepping stone to thwarting Kennedy's alleged ambitions, is particularly insidious. Loomer's tactics and her reliance on "sources in HHS" to justify her actions reveal a troubling playbook: use innuendo and internal discord to target and remove those perceived as obstacles.

The Echoes of Approval

What's perhaps more alarming than Loomer's vendetta is the silent acquiescence and, at times, active support she receives from within the administration. The fact that a senior HHS official and an anonymous Trump administration official lent credence to the speculation about RFK Jr.'s potential run—without outright denial—signals a tacit endorsement of Loomer's influence peddling.

The Cost of Extremist Influence

The ramifications of Loomer's unchecked influence are far-reaching. Her successful campaigns against government officials—notably, the firing of the National Security Agency’s top lawyer and the resignations of key figures within the FDA and the Justice Department—speak to a disturbing trend of governance by intimidation and loyalty tests. This isn't just about the individuals who have been ousted; it's about the erosion of institutional integrity and the emboldening of extremist voices.

Conclusion: A Call to Vigilance

The Politico report is more than a story about political maneuvering; it's a wake-up call to the dangers of allowing ideologues like Laura Loomer to operate unchecked within the corridors of power. Her actions, and the administration's apparent willingness to entertain them, underscore a grave systemic flaw: the infiltration of extremist ideologies into the fabric of our government.

As observers and participants in the democratic process, we must remain ever vigilant against the influence of those who seek to undermine our institutions from within. The story of Laura Loomer's influence is a cautionary tale of what happens when we allow the politics of hate and division to overshadow the principles of governance and public service.
